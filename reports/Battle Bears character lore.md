# SkyVu's Eleven Bears Carry Personality, Not Plot

SkyVu's official canon gives all eleven Battle Bears Gold (BBG) classes a stable, joke-voiced personality but almost no biography. The plot lives in three short story games from 2009–2012 (Battle Bears -1, Battle Bears Zero and Battle Bears Zombies), and nearly every scene-level detail of those games survives only on the fan-written Fandom wiki. The six original bears, Oliver, Riggs, Wil, Astoria, Graham and Tillman, are by far the best documented: they have official character cards, a canon 2012 voice-credit list (Ben Vu, Brian Bell, Marc Cohen, Brooke Fencl, Bryan North), and, for Oliver, Riggs and Wil, officially described cutscenes. The other five are defined mainly by BBG item flavour text and wiki-transcribed voice barks, with one exception: SkyVu calls Sanchez "resurrected and re-engineered from near death" after a crash that the wiki places in BB Zombies. The surrounding world is consistent across games. Robotic teddy-bear soldiers from the flagship *Ursa Major* fight pink Huggables that hug enemies to death, a shadowy Huggable Instrumentality Project (H.I.P.) fields robots and turncoats, and Tillman's "Delta Bear" trio begins as the enemy. The November 4, 2026 BBG re-release keeps the eleven classes and adds a new map and skins but no new lore, so the 2011–2014 characterisations remain the reference. For an animator the working rule is: anchor each character's "soul" in SkyVu's own lines, take speech rhythm from the barks after checking them by ear, and present anything the sources leave open (Oliver's crush on Astoria, Botch's past, who rebuilt Sanchez) as fan invention.

## Labels matter because SkyVu wrote personalities, not plots

The research notes behind this report sorted every claim by where it came from, and this report keeps those labels because the gap between them is the central finding. **[Canon]** means SkyVu's own words, live or archived. That covers battlebears.com, the 2011–2014 SkyVu blog, store copy on Steam, the App Store, Google Play and the Microsoft Store, official video titles and descriptions, and the archived 2012 Battle Bears Royale credits page. **[Canon card]** means an official SkyVu character-card image from about 2012–2013 that survives only as an upload on the fan wiki. Researchers viewed and transcribed each card. The cards are very likely the bio images that the 2012 battlebears.com Characters page showed, but that link is unconfirmed ([archived Characters page](https://web.archive.org/web/20121009232023/http://www.battlebears.com/characters)). **[Canon, comic page via wiki]** means text from Chapter 1 of SkyVu's comic, which carries SkyVu's story credits but survives only as page scans on the fan wiki (see the comics paragraph below). **[Game text via wiki]** means in-game strings, such as class blurbs and weapon and skin descriptions, that the wiki labels "Official Battle Bears Gold description" but that nobody has checked against the game files. **[Fan-wiki]** means the Fandom wiki's own plot transcriptions, voice-line transcriptions, trivia and analysis. **[Press]** is third-party coverage. **[Observed]** is a researcher's description of an official image or video frame: the art is official, the reading is not. **[Inference]** is reasoning from the notes and is never lore. **[Unverified]** flags claims that could not be confirmed anywhere.

Four provenance facts shape everything below. First, the most current official description of each class is battlebears.com/pages/characters. Its hero icons are named "…_BBH_…", so it describes Battle Bears Heroes (BBH) kits, not BBG [Inference] ([characters](https://battlebears.com/pages/characters)). Second, the voice cast of the six original bears is canon. The archived BBR credits list "Ben Vu - Papa Bear + Executive Producer + Voice (Oliver)", "Brian Bell - Voice (Riggs)", "Marc Cohen - Voice (Wil & Tillman)", "Brooke Fencl - Voice (Astoria)", "Bryan North - Voice (Graham)" and "Leah Smith - Concept Artist + Voice (ABBi)" ([BBR credits](https://web.archive.org/web/20121130022922/http://www.battlebears.com/games/battle-bears-royale/credits)). That page confirms wiki voice claims that some research threads had recorded as wiki-only. It gives no credit for the Huggable, and it predates B-1000, Botch, Saberi and Sanchez, none of whom has a credited voice anywhere. Third, the BB-1 cutscene series is on the @BattleBearsGame YouTube channel. One research thread treated that channel as official. Another could not tie it to SkyVu, because its description is empty and SkyVu's site links only to youtube.com/battlebears. Quotes from it are labelled **[Canon, channel unconfirmed]** ([@BattleBearsGame videos](https://www.youtube.com/@BattleBearsGame/videos)). Fourth, no researcher could play any YouTube audio. Every BBG voice line quoted here is therefore a fan transcription. The "All Voice Lines" rips that the channel Slinkster posted in February 2026 cover all eleven classes and are the quickest way to check them ([example: Riggs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=osBma0Ce8hk)).

Game abbreviations used below: **BB Zombies** (Battle Bears 1: Zombies, 2009), **BB-1** (Battle Bears -1, 2010), **BLAST** (Battle Bears Blast), **BBR** (Battle Bears Royale, 2011), **BBG** (Battle Bears Gold, relaunched from BBR in 2013 and revived in 2026), **BB Zero** (Battle Bears Zero: Wil's Finest Hour), **BBF** (Battle Bears Fortress), **BBO** (Battle Bears Ultimate, later Overclock) and **BBH** (Battle Bears Heroes, 2023–2024).

## A cyborg crew fights love-crazed Huggables across a joke cosmos

Battle Bears is "an award-winning game series created & produced by Coraline animator Ben Vu" and "the first mobile game with fully animated cutscenes" [Canon] ([about](https://battlebears.com/pages/about)). Vu came out of CalArts to work under Henry Selick at LAIKA on *Coraline* and *Boxtrolls* [Canon] ([team](https://battlebears.com/pages/team)). In 2009, SkyVu Pictures was an Omaha, Nebraska animation studio founded by Benjamin Vu and his brother Hoa, and Ben was "directing BATTLE BEARS, an animated feature film he wrote while at CalArts" [Canon] ([archived 2009 about page](https://web.archive.org/web/20090904235948/http://www.battlebears.com/about/)). Vu described the first game as "Care Bears meets Black Hawk Down" [Press] ([Silicon Prairie News](https://siliconprairienews.com/?p=361)). The heroes are machines in teddy form. The 2009 site called Oliver a "hardheaded robotic teddy bear" ([archived](https://web.archive.org/web/20090923050407/http://www.battlebears.com/oliver)), the Tillman reveal says "Mostly Panda, ALL ROBOT" ([2011 blog](https://web.archive.org/web/20111026075100/http://www.battlebears.com/blog/2011/10/7/stop-tillman-time.html)), and an official-styled cutscene description mocks Riggs as "the robotic and in no way biological bear" [Canon, channel unconfirmed] ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kjvO6XLxgxc)). The wiki adds that Battle Bears bleed **oil** while Huggables bleed **rainbow** [Fan-wiki] ([Huggable](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Huggable)).

The enemy is the Huggable: "Pink Bears wishing to HUG HIM TO DEATH" [Canon] ([App Store: BB Zombies AR](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/battle-bears-zombies-ar/id326979430)), creatures with vocabularies "limited to 'Hug me!'" who are "paradoxically feared for their deadly hugs" [Canon] ([characters](https://battlebears.com/pages/characters)). The official plush's detachable head reveals "a secret rainbow beam, the source of the Huggable's power" and comes with a "Kills with Kindness tag" [Canon] ([plushie](https://battlebears.com/products/huggable-plushie-with-in-game-bonus)). SkyVu's Fortress roster says the plague "all started as a valentines day gift" [Canon] ([archived BBF enemies](https://web.archive.org/web/20130908135751/http://battlebears.com/games/battle-bears-fortress/enemies/)). The species' god-figure is the Necromancer, "the iconic villain from 2009s Battle Bears Zombies… an ancient huggable… considered by some to be a god of the huggable species" [Canon] ([characters](https://battlebears.com/pages/characters)). The wiki fills in biology that SkyVu never states. Huggables are black until rainbow power turns them pink, and they come in pink, blue, red, orange, yellow, green and ghost variants [Fan-wiki] ([Huggable](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Huggable)). The 2013 Fortress roster corroborates the pink, blue, red, orange, green and ghost variants [Canon]. They live on the Huggable Planet, which is the wiki's name for it; SkyVu's video copy calls it the "Pink Planet" [Canon] ([Wil's Finest Hour](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YCltYBxBq20); [Wil's Finest Introduction](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kn5C3Exwm1o)), and the comic calls it "THAT PINK PLANET" [Canon, comic page via wiki] ([File:Comics_9.jpg](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:Comics_9.jpg)). The wiki describes its surface as "big green plains, forests" and says the Huggables there "seem to eat grass" [Fan-wiki] ([Huggable Planet](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Huggable_Planet)). From orbit it is drawn as a blue world inside a pink ring [Observed], which the wiki calls "a group of orbiting Huggables" [Fan-wiki] ([File:The_Huggable_Planet.jpg](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:The_Huggable_Planet.jpg); [File:Comics_3.jpg](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:Comics_3.jpg)). In BB Zombies, the Bearbershop Quartet boss sings the species' motive: "We care too much, we do not see you're dead until we're done… you lost to the first boss" [Fan-wiki lyrics; the song's existence is Canon] ([Bearbershop Quartet](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Bearbershop_Quartet); [official karaoke upload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ouZSsdil0wg)).

The plot comes from three short campaign games, which the wiki orders as BB-1, then BB Zero, then BB Zombies [Fan-wiki] ([Battle Bears Zero](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Bears_Zero)). SkyVu called BB-1 "the prequel" and BB Zero "the encore to the BATTLE BEARS story" [Canon] ([archived BB-1 page](https://web.archive.org/web/20121129222409/http://www.battlebears.com/games/battle-bears-1); [archived BB Zero page](https://web.archive.org/web/20121129223007/http://www.battlebears.com/games/battle-bears-zero)). TouchArcade reported in 2010 that Zero "takes place in-between the first two games" [Press] ([TouchArcade via Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20110425110141/http://toucharcade.com/2010/12/21/battle-bears-1-updated-with-new-campaign/)). SkyVu refers to "the original trilogy" without naming its members, so reading it as these three games is [Inference] ([Epic BBH FAQ via Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20250322132818/https://store.epicgames.com/en-US/p/battle-bears-heroes-faq-253b57)). The wiki transcribes BB-1's opening crawl as: "In the struggle to pay off their ship and return home, the BATTLE BEARS have accepted a mission to a mysterious planet to recover mystical treasures belonging to their newest client" [Fan-wiki] ([Campaign](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Campaign)). Huggables then overrun the *Ursa Major* (Steam: "Defend the flagship Ursa Major") in three campaigns for Oliver, Riggs and Wil [Canon] ([Steam: BB-1](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1482340)). In BB Zero, H.I.P. craft seize the ship and bring it down to the Huggable Planet, and Wil and ABBi fight through "the H.I.P. complex" [Fan-wiki plot; "H.I.P. complex" is Canon]. In BB Zombies, Oliver hauls "Mystical Treasures" across the Huggable Planet, Sanchez's rescue flight crashes, a cloaked figure raises a "zombocalypse", and Riggs arrives to shoot it [Fan-wiki plot; the treasure premise is Canon] ([Battle Bears: Zombies](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Bears:_Zombies); [Steam: BB1 Zombies](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1487460)). A wiki-transcribed BB Zero line has H.I.P. agent Nyx mention "the artifacts" and "the dig site", which suggests both sides are after the same relics. That link rests on wiki transcription only [Inference] ([Nyx](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Nyx)).

Three factions frame the cast. **H.I.P.** has been official since 2024, when SkyVu's BBH heroes page described Nyx as "A defector from the Battle Bears team, nobody knows why Nyx joined the HIP (Huggable Instrumentality Project)… This traitor is a master of deception and a knife throwing specialist" [Canon] ([archived heroes page, May 2024](https://web.archive.org/web/20240522073436/https://battlebears.com/pages/heroes)). The rest of the H.I.P. picture is wiki-only: a Vitruvian-Man Huggable logo worn by B-1000 and by Rho, the robot villain of BB Zero; a map renamed "H.I.P. Test Lab" in BBG (originally "Huggable Factory"), whose H.I.P. name the wiki says suggests H.I.P. "could be involved in the creation of the Huggables"; an ABBi line in BBF saying H.I.P. controls the resurrected Necromancer; and masked human employees who bleed red in BB Zero [Fan-wiki] ([H.I.P.](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/H.I.P.); [H.I.P. Test Lab](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/H.I.P._Test_Lab)). On screen, that map does keep Huggables floating in tubes [Observed] ([map tile](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:H.I.P._Test_Lab.png); [2026 playtest frame](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/2F2Jzrgz-xs/maxresdefault.jpg)). The **Delta Bears** are official. SkyVu's 2011 blog calls Tillman "exactly 1/3rd of the Delta Bear team" and later says "The DELTA BEARS are as surprised to see you as YOU ARE to see them! Yeah, they're in BB0" [Canon] ([Oct 21, 2011 blog](https://web.archive.org/web/20111118171332/http://www.battlebears.com/blog/2011/10/21/back-to-the-ol-grind.html)). SkyVu's 2024 copy, however, makes Swanny "A member of rival group, the Alpha Bears" [Canon], and no source reconciles the two names. The **Ursa Major crew** includes the ship AI ABBi, officially Wil's "mocking robot backpack" [Canon] and, per the wiki, the "Artificial Battle Bears Intelligence" [Fan-wiki] ([ABBi](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/ABBi)). It also includes Jackson, hugged to death in BB-1 [Fan-wiki] and officially "Revived after his 'accident'… ready to take revenge on the huggables that put him in his chair" in BBH [Canon]. Malcolm, the ship's pilot, is named in SkyVu's own comic (see below): in Chapter 1, "No Re-Entry", a speech balloon reads "MALCOLM AND THE OTHERS CAN TAKE CARE OF THEMSELVES." [Canon, comic page via wiki] ([File:Comics_12.jpg](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:Comics_12.jpg)). A wiki-hosted cutscene frame, which the wiki places in BB-1, shows him as a brown bear in a brown long coat with gold epaulettes, flying the ship with twin yokes [Observed] ([File:Malcum_from_battle_bears.png](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:Malcum_from_battle_bears.png)). Only his rank of "Captain" and his role as leader of the Battle Bears are wiki-only [Fan-wiki] ([Captain Malcolm](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Captain_Malcolm)). SkyVu spells the squad's dropship two ways. Its 2010 BB-1 soundtrack titles a track "Get To the Pelican'ts!" [Canon] ([Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/get-to-the-pelicants/383419699?i=383420725)), while its comic drops the apostrophe. In Chapter 1 a speech balloon asks "DO YOU THINK THE OTHER PELICANTS WERE ABLE TO STICK TOGETHER DURING THE EVACUATION?" [Canon, comic page via wiki] ([File:Comics_11.jpg](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:Comics_11.jpg)), and Chapter 2's first page has Riggs calling "RIGGS TO ALL PELICANT SHIPS." [Observed] ([File:Page1.jpg](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:Page1.jpg)). The wiki spells it "Pelican't" and reads the name as a pun on Halo's Pelican [Fan-wiki] ([Vehicles](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Vehicles)).

The tone is the one constant across every source. Vu thanked fans in 2012 for backing "cool appealing characters with core action and slightly edgy comedy. We didn't play it safe" [Canon] ([archived TV announcement](https://web.archive.org/web/20151130045305/http://battlebears.com/battle-bears-coming-to-tv/)). The 2026 Steam page opens "An award-winning 4v4 class shooter with more personality than sense!" [Canon] ([Steam: BBG](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4408320)). SkyVu's map blurbs use a sarcastic, fourth-wall-breaking narrator ("War. What is it good for?"; "In space, EVERYONE can hear you scream") [Canon] ([No Bear's Land](https://web.archive.org/web/20140507102800/http://battlebears.com/games/battle-bears-royale/maps/no-bears-land/); [Shortest Parsec](https://web.archive.org/web/20130623100805/http://battlebears.com/games/battle-bears-royale/maps/shortest-parsec/)), and the recurring catchphrase is "Don't get Hugged to Death!" [Canon] ([Steam: BB-1](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1482340)). A 2012 deal with W!LDBRAIN promised "a full season of 30 minute episodes featuring OLIVER, RIGGS, WIL, ASTORIA, TILLMAN, GRAHAM and the whole cast of enemies" [Canon]. No evidence shows that it aired, and the wiki calls it "presumably cancelled" [Fan-wiki] ([Battle Bears TV](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Bears_TV)). Page scans of two Battle Bears comic chapters survive on the wiki, and Chapter 1's carry SkyVu's own credits. The cover of Chapter 1, "No Re-Entry", reads "BATTLE BEARS THE COMIC", and its credits page reads "STORY: BENJAMIN VU, JOSH BLAYLOCK" above the SkyVu pictures and POPCULT logos [Observed] ([cover](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:Comics_1.jpg); [credits](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:Comics_2.jpg)), so this report treats Chapter 1's text as SkyVu's [Canon, comic page via wiki]. A scan of Chapter 2's first page opens "THIS STORY TAKES PLACE AFTER BATTLE BEARS -1 THE GAME & BEFORE BATTLE BEARS ZERO." [Observed] ([File:Page1.jpg](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:Page1.jpg)). The count of four comics, the publisher name Devil's Due Digital and the aim of filling story gaps "from the perspective of Oliver and Riggs" are still wiki-only [Fan-wiki] ([Battle Bears Comics](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Bears_Comics)).

## Battle Bears Gold grew from five classes to eleven, and 2026 adds no lore

BBG is a matured version of Battle Bears Royale. SkyVu announced BBR's worldwide launch on November 30, 2011 with "5 Distinct classes to choose from" [Canon] ([2011 blog](https://web.archive.org/web/20111206041413/http://www.battlebears.com/blog/2011/11/30/bbr-is-live.html)). According to the wiki, the Sniper arrived in BBR v1.1, "Wil the 'Chub Scout'" in v1.3 (May 3, 2012) and the Engineer in v1.4 (dated both November 28 and December 2012) [Fan-wiki] ([Battle Bears Royale](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Bears_Royale); [Updates](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Updates)). The 2013 BBG trailer offered "Choose from 8 classes… New Assault Class: The B-1000" [Canon] ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rfAyqVJrli4)). Botch, Saberi and Sanchez followed in BBG updates 2.1, 2.4 and 2.6 in July, October and December 2013; SkyVu's own store listings and blog posts confirm all three [Canon]. SkyVu's list dates Battle Bears Gold to "2012", while the wiki and Wikipedia date the relaunch from BBR to May 24, 2013 [Canon vs Fan-wiki/Press] ([about](https://battlebears.com/pages/about); [Battle Bears Gold](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Bears_Gold)). SkyVu later shelved the game "Due to outdated code and deprecated server technologies" [Canon] ([Epic BBH FAQ via Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20250322132818/https://store.epicgames.com/en-US/p/battle-bears-heroes-faq-253b57)).

The 2026 revival, which SkyVu's list calls "Battle Bears Gold Revival", launches November 4, 2026 on Steam (Windows), iOS and Google Play; battlebears.com says "Steam PC, iOS and Google Play". A Steam demo runs October 19–26 [Canon] ([battlebears.com](https://battlebears.com/); [sign-up page](https://play.battlebears.com/)). Steam lists Windows as the only platform for both the game and the demo. The game's store data carries a macOS requirements block, but Mac is not a listed platform [Canon, checked October 18, 2026] ([Steam: BBG](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4408320)). Steam lists the same eleven classes in the order this report follows: "SOLDIER: Oliver, HEAVY: Riggs, DEMOLITION: Tillman, HUGGABLE: Huggable, SNIPER: Astoria, CHUB SCOUT: Wil, ENGINEER: Graham, ASSAULT: B-1000, ASSASSIN: Botch, COMBAT TECH: Saberi, ARBITER: Sanchez" [Canon] ([Steam: BBG](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4408320)). One teaser frame reads "SAME / ICONIC / GAMEPLAY", and another puts "NEW MAP!" over a gold, swirl-engraved Oliver [Observed] ([hq1](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/d2ByJWrsoec/hq1.jpg); [hq2](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/d2ByJWrsoec/hq2.jpg)). The "SAME" panel's red stencil and star on grey stone match the Abusement Park red base, and the "NEW MAP!" backdrop (rust-orange low-poly canopies, a white cottage with a red-brown roof, a wooden fence) is probably Sky Pirates [Inference]. What is new is mechanical, not narrative: a map that playtest notes call "Sky World" and a video calls "Sky Pirates" (an official Short titled "Sky Pirates in BBG!" describes "highlights from our Sky World playtest", so they are probably one map [Inference]), a "Limited Zombie Skin" for sign-ups, a "Legacy Skin" for old accounts, controller support, and the note "All assets in this game are created by human artists" [Canon] ([Steam news](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4408320); [Sky Pirates reveal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r91WkR10W2A); [Short](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/CwxpBZg6Fi4)). None of the 2026 store copy, playtest posts or video descriptions mentions any character, kit, voice or lore change, and nobody could transcribe the October 12 "Q&A with the Creator" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pmE2jKQLSms)).

## Oliver (Soldier) causes the disaster, then saves the day he endangered

Oliver is the franchise lead and the class "Everyone starts with" [Canon] ([characters](https://battlebears.com/pages/characters)). His story begins in **BB Zombies (2009)**, which "stars OLIVER, a teddy bear stuck behind enemy lines" and which Steam calls "the old wacky story of Oliver and the Mystical Treasures" [Canon] ([Steam: BB1 Zombies](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1487460)). SkyVu's first cutscene description sets the scene: "OLIVER has just escaped the Huggable Fortress with the mystical treasures with two Unicorns. Chaos ensues when the pink Huggables catch up to him and are about to hug him to death" [Canon] ([YouTube: Cut Scene #1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9LEbWq27ThQ)). The wiki continues the plot. The wagon axle snaps and the unicorns die, Oliver stacks the treasure bags as sandbags and calls for backup, Sanchez's rescue Pelican't crashes, a zombocalypse rises, and Riggs's shot saves him; along the way Oliver shouts "They're gonna hug me to death!" and greets the Infinite Ammo Shotgun with "Say hello to my new friend!" [Fan-wiki] ([Battle Bears: Zombies](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Bears:_Zombies); [Oliver](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Oliver)).

**BB-1 (2010)**, the prequel, is where his signature moments live. The BB-1 cutscene descriptions read like a gag reel. Oliver is "forced to MacGyver a katana from loose ship components". He rides an elevator "with a James Dean-esque nonchalant attitude… like a boss". He tricks the Mecha-Bearzerker out of an airlock and back "down to the pink planet managing only to guarantee yourself a headache in Battle Bears: ZOMBIES!". Then, as Over Clocked Oliver, "Letting his already enormous pride grow out of control, O.C.O. inadvertently and indirectly causes the demise of everything he has ever, or will ever, hold dear" [Canon, channel unconfirmed] ([katana](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P6z5l7toC3w); [elevator](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJZuwYEq1y8); [Cosmic Litter Bug](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qGBZxOPD53M); [part 8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AA6tYQ81aiQ)). Per the wiki, O.C.O. is born when Oliver is "accidentally caught in the destruction of energy cylinders in the powerhouse". The accident turns him "blue with white eyes" with "white lines of circuitry", and converts his speech to Japanese "poorly translated by the subtitles"; in BBG he speaks English [Fan-wiki] ([Over Clocked Oliver](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Over_Clocked_Oliver)). The wiki also records the beat that best defines his friendship with Wil. ## Riggs (Heavy) leads like Picard and treats a massacre as his day off

Riggs is the squad's father figure. His card reads: "Captain and leader. Brave, serious and is the responsible one. He takes a paternal role and looks after his team. He's part Captain America, part Jean Luc Picard. He never flinches from battle, but knows how to best use his resources – even when that means running to fight another day" [Canon card] ([File:RiggsDescription2.jpg](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:RiggsDescription2.jpg)). Today's site keeps that pairing: he "combines the valor of Captain America and the wisdom of Jean Luc Picard as a brave and strategic leader… always prioritizing his team's safety and success" [Canon] ([characters](https://battlebears.com/pages/characters)). SkyVu's other copy undercuts the nobility. BBR says he "is built like a tank, and moves like a stone… Your melee attack is devastating!", and the BBF page says "If death itself was found violently murdered, the police would think about questioning Riggs and then no do it. Dude's crazy!" [Canon] ([archived BBR classes](https://web.archive.org/web/20121129222414/http://www.battlebears.com/games/battle-bears-royale/classes); [archived BBF heroes](https://web.archive.org/web/20130908135756/http://battlebears.com/games/battle-bears-fortress/heroes/)). Writers should hold both halves together: the protective commander and the cheerful brute.

His story moments come mostly from **BB-1**. The cutscene titles say it all. In "Riggs' Day Off (From NOT Killing Everything)", "After spending a curiously long time in an even curiouser bathroom, Riggs… is just finishing up when tragedy strikes", and he warms up for what textbooks would call "'mass murder' but Riggs… knows it only as 'good, clean fun'". "Firin' the Laser!" adds "A huggable's rainbow soul can power a laser gun!", and "Massacre of the innocents" adds "Huggable viscera is pretty!" [Canon, channel unconfirmed] ([part 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kjvO6XLxgxc); [part 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=esHWLlXDv-0); [part 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vfhSVc-t6pI)). The wiki fills in the beats. He is brushing his teeth when the ship shakes, slams a Huggable's face into a table and grenades it, recharges a laser pistol by smashing its hilt on a green Huggable's head, falls down an elevator shaft and builds a chainsaw from machinery, kills the radioactive mutant Tentacleese and names its detached udder "Udder Chaos". He also yells at Captain Malcolm to fly straight and escapes on the Pelican't with Oliver [Fan-wiki] ([Campaign](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Campaign); [Tentacleese](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Tentacleese)). In **BB Zombies**, his first appearance per the wiki, he is only a radio voice. He introduces Oliver to the Bearzooka, advises Unicorn Blood, and arrives with the other Battle Bears to shoot the zombocalypse's caster. The wiki contradicts itself on whether his target is "the Zombocalypse" or "the cloaked figure" [Fan-wiki] ([Riggs](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Riggs)). In **BB Zero** he appears only in a cutscene infiltrating the Huggable base, and in **BBF** he is the third hero, with a Minigun and Dual Lasers [Fan-wiki].

The "captain" title is the one open seam. The card says "Captain and leader", but Malcolm is the one flying the ship in the cutscene frame the wiki places in BB-1 [Observed] (see above), and the wiki says that in BB-1 he "appears to be in charge" and gives him the rank of Captain [Fan-wiki]. Treating Riggs as squad leader and Malcolm as ship captain is the notes' own reconciliation, not canon text [Inference]. The wiki also calls him "a very skilled engineer" for the chainsaw and laser improvisations [Fan-wiki].

On the card his fur is pale blue-grey to near-white. He is muscular and broad-shouldered, with a square muzzle, a heavy brow, a stern half-lidded glare, a dark diagonal bandolier and a green-and-black ribbed pack over one shoulder [Observed] ([card](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:RiggsDescription2.jpg)). BB-1 frames match this, adding a single dark-grey pauldron and a limp, X-eyed Huggable dangling from his fist. The 2024 portrait is steel blue-grey and snarling. The BBG trailer card is a red, faceted, cracked-armour skin crushing a golden figure [Observed] ([BB-1 frame](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/kjvO6XLxgxc/hqdefault.jpg); [BBH icon](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0370/2469/4408/files/Riggs_BBH_HeroIcon_1.png)). The wiki says his fur is "white" and cites a "Polar Opposites" achievement [Fan-wiki]. Brian Bell voices him [Canon]. Bell also sings on the franchise's rap track "I'm a Battle Bear" and is credited for the 2026 OST's bonus vocals [Canon] ([Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/battle-bears-original-game-soundtrack/349812901); [Steam soundtrack](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5321720)). His transcribed barks are clipped, military and masochistic: "Target neutralized.", "War, it's fantastic!", "Thank you, may I have another?", "Killing me will only make... me... stronger!", "Why aren't there any... medics?", "Good work, team.", "Gotta train harder!", "Fall in!" [Fan-wiki] ([Heavy (BBG)](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_%28BBG%29)). His taunts are "Club Dance" (chest-pumping), "Head Shake" (arms crossed, shaking his head) and "Sumo" [Fan-wiki]. His BBG defaults are the Beebeegun, Hivey League and Barbed Gloves, with Electric Honey, Zombee Gun and Buzz Kill among the extras; reading these as a bee and honey theme is [Inference]. SkyVu also released a "Talking Riggs" app [Canon] ([about](https://battlebears.com/pages/about)).

## Tillman (Demolition) is a Delta Bear robot panda who joined the team the hard way

SkyVu introduced Tillman as an outsider. The 2011 reveal reads: "I give to you TILLMAN THE DELTA BEAR! Tillman, exactly 1/3rd of the Delta Bear team. Exactly 1/1 of the Delta Bears' explosives division. Mostly Panda, ALL ROBOT. There isn't anything in this ludicrously expansive universe that Tillman enjoys more than FIGHTIN'… A baby looks at him funny? Kiss that soft spot goodbye, IT'S FIGHTIN' TIME… when you're nigh indestructable, why take the time?" [Canon] ([2011 blog](https://web.archive.org/web/20111026075100/http://www.battlebears.com/blog/2011/10/7/stop-tillman-time.html)). The same post gives his BBR loadout, which still forms his BBG defaults: the Big Shot "(Chest Cannon)", lock-on Fireworks and the Buzzard "Double Buzzsaw Extension" [Canon]. SkyVu's sources disagree about his allegiance. He is a Delta Bear "maverick-rogue" in 2011. On his card he "Loves to follow orders and yell 'sir yes sir!'" and is "always begging the Captain to charge in guns-a-blazing" [Canon card] ([File:Tillman_description.jpg](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:Tillman_description.jpg)). In BBF he is "Reckless bounty hunter extraordinaire, Tillman is the Michael Bay of space pirates" [Canon] ([archived BBF heroes](https://web.archive.org/web/20130908135756/http://battlebears.com/games/battle-bears-fortress/heroes/)). Today he is "once a powerful enemy of the Battle Bears, Tillman has learned the power of this team the hard way" [Canon] ([characters](https://battlebears.com/pages/characters)). That last line is the bridge between the versions. The notes read it as a canon-safe arc: enemy in BB Zero, beaten by Wil, now on the team [Inference].

The defeat itself happens in **BB Zero**, and only the wiki describes it. The Delta Bears (Tillman plus Skiles, "possibly the leader", and Swanny) wait in a H.I.P. base intending to kill Wil, which the wiki says suggests an alliance. Skiles and Swanny ditch Tillman, and Wil beats him with ABBi's help [Fan-wiki] ([Skiles](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Skiles); [Tillman](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Tillman)). His card adds a contrast with Wil: "unlike Wil, he knows exactly what he's getting into" [Canon card]. Calling Wil his "rival" or giving him a grudge is [Inference]; no source uses either word. Astoria's card names him, with Oliver, as one of the bears she makes "feel incompetent" [Canon card]. Per the wiki, a comic places him with Astoria "on another ship", and he does not appear in the BBF story although he is playable there [Fan-wiki]. The wiki also notes the original BBR app icon featured Tillman and that BB TV art shows him fighting beside Wil and Oliver "but he is not technically a Battle Bear" [Fan-wiki] ([Battle Bears TV](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Bears_TV)). The "Captain" he begs is never named, so the notes advise against naming him on screen [Inference].

His personality pillars are all official: "Gung ho master of heavy artillery", "no subtlety or grace", "foul-mouthed and avoids anything 'fancy'", near-death as "a badge of honor", and "If it can explode, it will. If it cant explode, it will" [Canon card] ([File:Tillman_BBF.jpg](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:Tillman_BBF.jpg)). For a general audience, the notes suggest keeping him foul-mouthed in spirit without on-screen swearing [Inference]. His look on the cards [Observed]: a stocky, round-bellied panda with a white head and torso, black ears and eye patches, glowing white eyes under an angry brow, segmented black robotic limbs with ball joints, and a cylindrical teal backpack on a diagonal chest strap, posed hands on hips. BB Zero concept art shows him shorter and wider than the lanky Skiles and Swanny, rolling on wheel-like feet with a crack on his chest ([File:Delta_bears.png](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:Delta_bears.png)). The BBG trailer card shows a red-and-black masked skin with glowing eyes and a huge olive artillery shell [Observed]. The wiki says he is "completely animatronic", moves on skates or wheels in BBG and BB Zero, has armour cracks at the top right of his head and bottom left of his body, and uses his backpack as a jet pack in BB Zero. It contradicts itself by noting that his BBG gibs show "pink and red flesh". The Cyborg skin jokes that "Tillman can't tell if he looks more like a robot or a bear now" [Fan-wiki; Game text via wiki] ([Demo (BBG)](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Demo_%28BBG%29); [Cyborg](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Cyborg)).

[Fan-wiki] ([Demo (BBG)](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Demo_%28BBG%29)). His taunts are "Robot", "Flex" and "Running Man" [Fan-wiki]. ## The Huggable turns the villain species into a stitched, radio-wired playable brawler

The Huggable is both the franchise's enemy and one of its eleven classes, and SkyVu explains the switch with one adjective. BBR's 2012 class page reads: "The Modified Huggable is a safer version of the standard Huggable. Its quickness and uncanny melee strength makes it a serious threat up close and its unorthodox abilities make it unpredictable in combat" [Canon] ([archived BBR classes](https://web.archive.org/web/20121129222414/http://www.battlebears.com/games/battle-bears-royale/classes)). BBG lists the class only as "HUGGABLE: Huggable", with no name [Canon]. BBH names its version "Huggy", calls it "he", and describes "A nimble navigator and a melee specialist" whose "attack boosts his healing with each punch" and whose Ultimate "So Fly" lets him "soar across large parts of the map" [Canon] ([characters](https://battlebears.com/pages/characters)). The wiki calls it "likely" but "unconfirmed" that Huggy is the BBG Huggable [Fan-wiki] ([Huggy](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Huggy)). Who modified it is a joke, not a fact. The Seesaw weapon text says "The Battle Bear who fashioned the first Huggable with chainsaw hands should not have been faulted for his scientific pursuits, but he was" [Game text via wiki] ([Seesaw](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Seesaw)). Together with "Modified", the notes read this as implying a Battle Bears-side modifier, which remains joke text, not stated canon [Inference].

The species' story role runs through every story game. Huggables invade the *Ursa Major* in BB-1, swarm Oliver and Sanchez in BB Zombies and populate the H.I.P. complex in BB Zero [Fan-wiki]. Their few recorded words are cutscene gags. During the BB Zombies Marecraft Carrier scene: "Unicorn A-45 ready for landing. Get that crap off the deck, sailor!" / "Aye aye, captain!". After the Mecha-Bearzerker falls: "Grass!" [Fan-wiki] ([Huggable](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Huggable)). BBG item text gives the playable one its personality: love as a weapon and cute as a threat. "The Huggable's ability to love will make time seem like it comes to a halt. When actually it's just your feet and you're about to die" (Phantom Hug). "Just when you thought the huggable could not be any more dangerous, it takes it's head off and tries to kill you... to death" (Losing Your Head). Headlights tells the player to "Remove your head and fire out a cutting death beam of rainbow essence" [Game text via wiki] ([Phantom Hug](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Phantom_Hug); [Losing Your Head](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Losing_Your_Head); [Headlights](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Headlights)). Skin lines hint at a dark past ("Failed restraints and a mask betray a past of violence", Hannible) and at fragility ("Good thing Huggables don't have thumbs", Bearettas) [Game text via wiki]. The notes suggest playing it as a near-mute physical comedian whose radio pack and team banner imply it was conscripted or retrofitted by someone unnamed [Inference].

Its BBG look is deliberately unlike a normal Huggable. The wiki says the default "looks nothing like an ordinary Huggable" because of its "bandanges, scars and implants". It describes "one small black, beady eye, while the other appears to be a button", stuffing bulging from wounds, a heavily bandaged head side, a team-coloured banner with three white symbols carried in place of a backpack, and bear-trap hands [Fan-wiki] ([Pink Huggable](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Pink_Huggable)). A researcher's look at the wiki-hosted BBG image matched this: a stitched pink body, a radio with an antenna on a bandaged head, one red button-like eye, a wide grin and a torn belly showing metal [Observed] ([image](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:PinkHuggableCR.jpg)). The BBH icon shows a hot-pink stitched face with two dark round eyes, a single tear and a green radio pack with an antenna [Observed] ([icon](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0370/2469/4408/files/Huggy_BBH_HeroIcon256_1.png)). The wiki notes that the BBH 3D model's right eye is ruby-red, unlike that illustration [Fan-wiki]. The BBG trailer card shows a hot-pink plush with a huge white-toothed grin and an antenna, standing over a toppled teddy, with dripping slime lettering [Observed].

No official source credits a Huggable voice. The wiki says "Huggables have been voiced by Benjamin Vu and Rob Ruiz" and credits Vu for BBH Huggy; Ruiz's official credits are for audio, sound design and music, never voice [Fan-wiki, partly corroborated] ([Huggable (BBH)](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Huggable_%28BBH%29)). The BBG class's quote is "Hug me!" [Game text via wiki]. The wiki adds that it "has improved its vocabulary in Battle Bears Gold, possibly due to the radio attached to its head", yet no transcription of its BBG lines exists across 302 page revisions [Fan-wiki] ([Huggable (BBG)](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Huggable_%28BBG%29)). The fan audio rip is the only way to hear what it says ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PLqiq1cIhwI)). In BBG it is the fastest class, with only 90 health. It fights with Double Rainbow, Tough Love and Bite Me by default, and its taunts are "Sloppy Swish", "Rump Shake" and "Moonwalk" [Fan-wiki].

## Astoria (Sniper) is the first female Battle Bear, aloof outside and loyal inside

Astoria's card is the cleanest character brief in the franchise: "No nonsense, special ops. Always a step ahead of the enemy because she thinks things through. Methodical, calculating and seemingly cold at first glance. She wants to be part of the gang but the others have a hard time including her because she's wicked smart and totally put together; it makes some of the others (Oliver and Tillman mostly) feel incompetent" [Canon card] ([File:AstoriaDescription.jpg](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:AstoriaDescription.jpg)). Today's site softens the coldness: she "spares little time for Oliver and Wil's childish antics. But don't let her cold remarks and serious personality fool you, she really does care for the Battle Bears" [Canon] ([characters](https://battlebears.com/pages/characters)). The two texts fit together as an outsider who wants in but will not ask [Inference]. The 2011 BBR trailer billed her as "The first female BATTLE BEAR ever!" [Canon] ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0gYEZCMQfmA)). Her earliest dated official mention is SkyVu's September 23, 2011 blog of BBR character "Orthographics" [Canon] ([2011 blog](https://web.archive.org/web/20110928111612/http://www.battlebears.com/blog/2011/9/23/if-youre-reading-this-then-its-already-too-late.html)). Official copy places her at the edges of a fight: "lurking around the deathzone edge whilst Riggs (Heavy) is getting stuck into the main action" (BBH), and using the Marecraft map's "tall towers" to "cover most of the battlefield at once" (BBR) [Canon] ([games](https://battlebears.com/pages/games); [archived Marecraft page](https://web.archive.org/web/2013/http://battlebears.com/games/battle-bears-royale/maps/marecraft/)).

She has essentially no story. The wiki calls her "a mysterious sniper with no official known past" who "has never appeared in the Battle Bears storyline" except on a file in BB Zero [Fan-wiki] ([Astoria](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Astoria)). The comic "No Re-Entry" does mention her. In Chapter 1, Oliver asks "BUT WHAT ABOUT ASTORIA AND THE-" and is told "RELAX OLIVER. WE'RE STICKING TO THE PLAN." [Canon, comic page via wiki] ([File:Comics_13.jpg](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:Comics_13.jpg)). The wiki says Chapter 1 gives "the first ever reference of Astoria" ([Battle Bears Comics](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Bears_Comics)) and reads this line as Oliver saying that she and Tillman were "on another ship, not aware of the Huggable threat" [Fan-wiki]; the scanned line breaks off before naming anyone else. Because the wiki places Chapter 2 at San Diego Comic-Con 2011 [Fan-wiki], this mention probably predates the September 2011 blog [Inference]. The wiki also says she began as a male sniper concept with "a harpoon rifle" before SkyVu "quickly decided they needed a female character", and that she was planned as a BBF hero but never released [Fan-wiki]. The wiki's development timeline conflicts with the 2011 orthos, so the notes treat it as unreliable [Inference]. Her canon relationships are that Oliver and Tillman feel incompetent around her and that she is impatient with Oliver and Wil. Any romance with Oliver comes from fan belief and three wiki claims (the BBF line, the wiki's reading of an in-game tree carving as "O+A" for Oliver and Astoria, and the Aztec Attack temples). If a short uses it at all, the notes' safest reading is a one-sided crush by Oliver; her reciprocation is never shown [Fan-wiki; Inference].

On the card she has pale yellow-cream fur with a white muzzle, throat and belly, teal-green eyes in a heavy-lidded, unimpressed glare, small round ears, a grey-blue backpack on dark straps, a black woven utility belt and a hand-on-hip pose [Observed] ([card](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:AstoriaDescription.jpg)). The wiki gives her BBG default "an ammo belt, a backpack and gloves with the colour of the team colour". It calls her "the only Battle Bear with a definite eye color, which is green" [Fan-wiki]. ## Wil (Chub Scout) is fearless only because he never notices the danger

Wil's card defines him precisely: "Naïve computer savant. Just ask Tillman" [Canon] ([characters](https://battlebears.com/pages/characters)). Sources disagree on his temperament. The wiki says he seems "scared of everything all the time", and TouchArcade called him "lovable, timid, nub-handed, and slightly overweight" [Fan-wiki; Press], against the card's "absolutely fearless". The card is the canon anchor.

He debuts in **BB-1**, whose third campaign arrived in the v1.5 update in December 2010 [Fan-wiki; Press]. TouchArcade's contemporary report confirms the core beats. The campaign "takes place after the other Battle Bears, Oliver and Riggs, have already safely escaped the mothership Ursa Major". He is armed with "an arrow that he pulled from his leg" and "a shotgun which he lovingly named Boomstick McShootington", crawls through "a twisting maze of claustrophobic air vents", and the story ends on a "cliffhanger" [Press] ([TouchArcade via Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20110425110141/http://toucharcade.com/2010/12/21/battle-bears-1-updated-with-new-campaign/)). The ship AI ABBi torments him and tricks him into pressing the self-destruct button through a "MULTICHROMATIC MEMORY GAME", which he loves. Then a Huggable chews the wires and the screen reads "To Be Continued" [Fan-wiki] ([Campaign](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Campaign); [Boomstick McShootington](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Boomstick_McShootington)). In BBF he appears as a hologram mentioning "super manly BFF toe rings" [Fan-wiki, Unverified] ([Wil](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Wil)). ## B-1000 (Assault) is a half-ton robot harvesting the bears' data for unnamed villains

B-1000 is the first class not drawn from the story games. The wiki calls it "the first class that is not based on any characters from the Battle Bears series" [Fan-wiki] ([Assault (BBG)](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Assault_%28BBG%29)). SkyVu announced it as "NEW Character Class- the B-1000! A robot made to kill" [Game text via wiki, BBR 1.4.3 notes] and launched BBG in 2013 with "New Assault Class: The B-1000" [Canon] ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rfAyqVJrli4)). Its BBG description gives it a mission: "A mysterious foe who has taken a keen interest in the combat prowess of the Battle Bears. Hobbies include: annihilating all Battle Bears and transmitting their data to the bad guys" [Game text via wiki] ([Template](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Template:Class:Assault_%28BBG%29)). Today's site repeats it: "A mysterious enemy fixated on the Battle Bears' skills, aiming to annihilate them and share their data with villains" [Canon] ([characters](https://battlebears.com/pages/characters)). The official Shortest Parsec map blurb hints at a backstory without naming anyone: "Kinda like a robotic someone I've seen before exploded a few miles away… like some kind of private arms dealer uses it to transfer power to its facilities" [Canon] ([archived](https://web.archive.org/web/20130623100805/http://battlebears.com/games/battle-bears-royale/maps/shortest-parsec/)).

Its link to H.I.P. is wiki analysis. The wiki says its chest emblem is the same symbol Rho wears in BB Zero, which it identifies as the H.I.P. symbol "suggesting subordination to the organisation". It adds that Rho was rumoured as a class in early 2013 and that the name echoes Terminator 2's T-1000 [Fan-wiki] ([B-1000 (Skin)](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/B-1000_%28Skin%29); [Battle Damaged](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Damaged)). Official art confirms a round chest emblem but not what it means. The notes therefore recommend using B-1000 as a recurring spy or hunter antagonist while leaving his masters unnamed, or labelling "H.I.P." as a fan link [Inference]. Official texts use "he/his", while the wiki's own prose mostly says "it" [Canon; Fan-wiki].

His personality lives in item text, which pairs menace with self-aware jokes. He is "a half-ton murderous robot" with "3 razor sharp claws, you're welcome" (Shock N Claw). Under the Iron Bear skin "you'll find no billionaire playboy… only a series of wires and servos bent on the annihilation of the Battle Bears". "Everything about B1000 is loud and explosive". Smoke Screen is "his own version of hide and seek… Hide in smoke and then kill everyone!". His satellite drone is "a B1000's best friend!", and "Who knew B-1000 ran on sugar cookies?" (Sugah Cookehs) [Game text via wiki] ([Shock N Claw](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Shock_N_Claw); [Iron Bear](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Iron_Bear); [Satellite](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Satellite)). His transcribed barks are computer-error puns delivered with sudden glee: "Life-form disintegrated!", "Control, Alt, Deleted!", "Initiate brag sequence!", "Consider your bit flipped!", "Hundreds of tiny metal errors detected!", "Your PC will now power down!", "Logic demolishes your squishy flesh!", "Outcome unacceptable!", "Satisfactory response, tee-hee" [Fan-wiki] ([Assault (BBG)](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Assault_%28BBG%29)). No voice actor is credited anywhere. The notes suggest a deadpan synthetic voice with bursts of cheer [Inference].

The BBH icon shows a glossy red-and-black armoured robot bear head with pointed, plated ears, narrow glowing white-blue eyes, a black snout tip and heavy riveted shoulder armour [Observed] ([icon](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0370/2469/4408/files/B1000_BBH_HeroIconReed.png)). SkyVu's grey T-shirt shows a hulking armoured robot bear with a round chest emblem, a gun-arm on its right and long claw-blades on its left hand [Observed] ([T-shirt](https://battlebears.com/products/b-1000-t-shirt-grey)). In BBG its armour takes the team colour, "maroon" on red and dark blue on blue, with glowing blue eyes. A wiki-hosted screenshot shows a cyan-eyed robot with an orange-and-white gun-arm, a blue energy blade and clawed feet [Fan-wiki; Observed]. Skins swap the emblem: Iron Bear's gold armour carries a power core shaped like the BBG icon, and Rusty Devil has "blank, vacant white eyes" and no H.I.P. symbol [Fan-wiki]. It is slow and tanky (180 health, with long reloads) and "the only class that displays all of its weapons all the time" [Fan-wiki]. Its taunts are the best physical-comedy brief for a robot: "Growth Spurt", "Falling Apart" (a heroic pose until "his right arm… suddenly snaps off", and he reattaches it) and "Big Robo" (an awkward, glitching waddle) [Fan-wiki] ([Taunts](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Taunts)). Its name is "B-1000" in BBG and on Steam but "B1000" in BBH. The site's BBH attack name "Slice n Bite" is probably a copy error, since the wiki gives that attack to Huggy [Canon; Inference].

## Botch (Assassin) is a radioactive loner whose past no SkyVu source explains

Botch arrived in BBG 2.1 in July 2013 ("New in 2.1: - New Character Class! ASSASSIN: Botch") [Canon] ([archived Google Play](https://web.archive.org/web/20130815012436/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.skyvu.battlebearsgold)). Everything official about him is a deliberate blank: "Botch, a bear of very few words with a mysterious past, boasts powerful radioactive powers. Known for his fearsome ranged abilities alongside swift movement speed, he's a formidable foe. Yet, his significant weakness lies in his low health" [Canon] ([characters](https://battlebears.com/pages/characters)). BBH store copy calls him "Botch the Toxic Assassin" [Canon]. The wiki transcribes BBH text saying "very little is known about how he acquired his powers or how he cut his ear, one thing is for certain: a significance amount of radiation was involved" [Fan-wiki]. His BBG class blurb is mechanical: "Botch is what you call a glass cannon… Dipping in and out of battle is Botch's strength", with the quote "Ahh, destruction!" [Game text via wiki] ([Template](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Template:Class:Assassin_%28BBG%29)).

Every origin clue is fan reading. An Easter egg on the Marecraft map (BBR 1.4.3, April 2013) shows "two mysterious outlines of the same bear with a chewed-off ear… pink fur and yellow skin", which "was considered a hint to the character" [Fan-wiki] ([Marecraft (Map)](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Marecraft_%28Map%29)). The wiki's Assassin page calls it "the first known instance of a class being intentionally leaked in an Easter Egg" [Fan-wiki] ([Assassin (BBG)](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Assassin_%28BBG%29)). The wiki also says the radioactive sewer map Toxic Terror "might be a hint to Botch's origins" and puts tally marks and a drawing of him on its walls [Fan-wiki, speculation] ([Toxic Terror](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Toxic_Terror)). The tally marks are real, and they sit beside a rack of Ninja Turtle weapons [Observed] ([2013 gameplay tour](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/WfTBPWZAQCs/maxresdefault.jpg)), which reads as a Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles sewer-lair gag rather than a Botch clue [Inference]. No image researchers opened shows an identifiable drawing of Botch; the visible wall drawings are three chalk bears [Observed] ([map tile](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:Toxic-Terror-Preview.png)). The Botch origin stays fan speculation. The wiki's stub "He was friends with Pish and Posh" was added in 2020 by a wiki admin with no source [Fan-wiki, Unverified] ([Botch history](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Botch?action=history)). Pish and Posh are real SkyVu characters. A 2019 SkyVu video calls them "part of the Battle Cubs team in the movie screenplay" [Canon] ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uHeNNaNs3eg)), and a wiki account named "Benvu" listed "Pish & Posh" among heroes in development in 2023 [Fan-wiki]. Nothing official links them to Botch, and the notes treat the friendship as fan canon at best [Inference]. No relationship with any other character is established officially.

Item text sketches a personality the narrator mocks: lonely, odd, serious and indifferent. "Sometimes when a person gets lonely they talk to themselves. Botch, however, created two friends that rapidly fire deadly lasers... friends are the best!" (Orbs). "Botch is always serious" (Ball N Pain). "a bit clumsy and nonchalant… can't be bothered" (Cosmic Bomb). "Botch tried to look like a normal bear once. He found it awful, but doesn't he look cute?" (Brown Botch). "We chose purple, he was not amused" (Purple Botch). "Botch liked everything before you did. Whatever!" (Emo). "Orange is Botch's favorite color and fruit" (Orange Botch). "Messing with all this radiation is bound to make a person sick" (Green Botch) [Game text via wiki] ([Orbs](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Orbs); [Brown Botch](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Brown_Botch); [Emo](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Emo)). The wiki says he "only talks in grainy one-or-two word quotes", and the transcriptions bear that out with a mix of childlike wonder and weary shrugs: "Pretty!", "Delightful!", "Ooh!", "Wee!", "Sleep!", "It's time!", "Cozy!", "Finally..." (on dying), "Eh. Whatever." (on defeat), "Safety!", "Come!", "Kill!", "Leave!" [Fan-wiki] ([Assassin (BBG)](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Assassin_%28BBG%29)). The notes read him as an eerie, innocent-seeming loner, and say any tragic origin must be presented as fan invention [Inference]. No voice actor is credited.

His fur colour depends on the source. The BBH icon reads near-black or charcoal, a 2013 BBG screenshot reads dark plum or maroon-black, and the wiki says "very dark maroon" on one page and "navy" on another [Observed; Fan-wiki] ([icon](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0370/2469/4408/files/Botch_BBH_icon_reed_1.png)). The constants are huge glowing lime-green eyes without pupils, a notched ear, a grey muzzle with a small dark nose, and a flat line of a mouth [Observed]. The wiki adds an "abnormally large head", small silver-and-red arm guards, "a tiny silver backpack with circular red markings", and movement "slightly levitated from the ground", gliding with green particles [Fan-wiki] ([Botch (Skin)](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Botch_%28Skin%29)). His taunts are the eeriest in the game: "Almighty" (he curls into the foetal position, levitates while spinning, then throws his arms to the sky), "Head Spin" (his head spins two full rounds until he grabs it) and "Air Guitar" [Fan-wiki]. His powers are radioactive and "cosmic": poison clouds, the lasering "Doom Cloud" ("it sure is adorable, huh?"), orbiting poison orbs, gravity wells and BBH's boomerang "Doom Star". He is a glass cannon with 90 health [Canon; Game text via wiki]. The Steam trailer has no splash card for him [Observed].

## Saberi (Combat Tech) built her first gun as a baby and now mothers the squad

Saberi debuted in BBG update 2.4 on October 24, 2013: "New Combat Tech Class: Saberi / 10 Skins Available / 3 Taunts Available / 2 Primary Weapons (Energy Rifle, Plasma Machine Gun) / 2 Secondary Weapons (Missiles, Minigun) / 2 Melee Weapons (Mallet, Sledgehammer)" [Canon] ([archived 2.4 notes](https://web.archive.org/web/20131101183026/http://battlebears.com/battle-bears-gold-update-2-4-now-available-release-notes/)). SkyVu's 2.9 notes give her house-style jokes: Plasma Shotgun, "You know a great way to exfoliate your skin, white hot plasma! Caution may cause severe salt", and Gamma Burst, "Saberi Smash! Creates a deadly radiation area around the player" [Canon] ([archived 2.9 notes](https://web.archive.org/web/20140512024150/http://battlebears.com/battle-bears-gold-update-2-9-now-available/)). Today's site recasts her: "SABERI - Medic. Saberi, the powerful healer and momma bear, commands respect and excels in support roles, making her a cherished asset to any team", with a BBH "Huggable Splicer" that sprays "huggable bits" and a "Healing Turret" [Canon] ([characters](https://battlebears.com/pages/characters)). BBH store copy says "Saberi the Healer", and Steam 2026 reverts to "COMBAT TECH" [Canon]. The notes describe her role drifting from offensive gadgeteer to healer, and suggest writing her as a tinkerer who also patches up her team [Inference].

Her backstory exists only as BBG item text, which the wiki quotes as official. "Other babies have toys, Saberi had this Energy Rifle. She patched it and rebuilt it hundreds of times, but this trusty rapid firing gun has been with her forever." "When others were learning to ride bikes, Saberi was building combustible engines." "Because she couldn't quite figure out where to attach his missiles to her rifle, Saberi decided to create herself a third arm and put them there." Her default skin reads "A weapon expert who doesn't take nothing from nobody! Saberi is a momma bear who knows how to lay down the law", and her class blurb opens "Saberi is something of a prodigy" [Game text via wiki] ([Energy Rifle](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Energy_Rifle); [Missiles](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Missiles); [Saberi (Skin)](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Saberi_%28Skin%29)). Other lines supply hobbies and texture: "Sometimes in order to solve a problem you just need to hit it with a hammer" (Mallet), "Saberi likes to practice her taunt dances in her off time" (Rockabilly), "Fighting on the streets is one of Saberi's specialities!" (Chun B), and "Working with explosions and other things that go boom tends to cover one in a bit of grime" (Charcoal) [Game text via wiki]. The wiki calls her "the second known female Battle Bear, after Astoria" and claims Vine appearances "based on the Battle Bears TV show", which nothing corroborates [Fan-wiki] ([Saberi](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Saberi)). No official text links her to any character, and "momma bear" describes her personality; no source says she has cubs. The notes offer pairings with Graham (fellow tinkerer) and Astoria (the other female bear), and a non-canon premise in which she rebuilt Sanchez's arm [Inference].

Two official looks exist. The 2014 BBG art shows dark brown fur, a tan muzzle, bright green eyes with heavy lashes and eyeliner, and a confident half-smile. She wears a red bandana with white polka dots tied in a big bow on top of her head and a red short-sleeved top, holds a chunky double-barrelled gun with a glowing purple core, and has a mechanical third arm rising behind her with a purple-glowing disc [Observed] ([2014 art](https://web.archive.org/web/20140507101154/https://battlebears.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/2.9_saberi_art_big.png)). Identifying the gun and disc as the Plasma Shotgun and Gamma Burst is [Inference]. A wiki-hosted BBG screenshot matches this look and adds crossed arms and a smug half-smile [Observed]. The 2024 BBH redesign has light tan fur, blonde bangs, teal eyes with one winking, a cream knotted bandana, a dark navy suit with grey harness straps and a red backpack [Observed] ([BBH icon](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0370/2469/4408/files/Saberi_BBH_HeroIcon_1.png)). The notes favour the BBG-era look for 2026-lineage shorts [Inference]. A pale bear with blonde 1940s pin curls in the 2.4 Halloween banner is probably her "Undead" skin ("The 40's were a scary time.") [Inference].

No voice actor is credited. Her transcribed barks make her an upbeat fixer who is mildly surprised when things work: "Ooh, that worked?", "It's not your day!", "Smmmash!", "Hammers fix anything!", "Imma pound some sense into you!", "That's going to leave a mark!", "Oh, another job well done!", "Now why didn't that work?", "Alright, follow me!" [Fan-wiki] ([Combat Tech (BBG)](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Combat_Tech_%28BBG%29)). Her taunts are athletic and confrontational: "River Dance" (step dancing into an airborne 360), "Knee Thing" and "Come At Me, Bro" (a foot-stomp and a paw-beckon) [Fan-wiki]. Mechanically she is "the only class that cannot switch weapons at all". Her backpack secondaries run for 10 seconds, then reload for 30 [Fan-wiki]. The Steam trailer has no splash card for her [Observed].

## Sanchez (Arbiter) crashed rescuing Oliver and came back rebuilt "from the arm up"

Sanchez is the only post-launch class with a story past, and the wiki is the only source for it. In **BB Zombies (2009)** he flies a Pelican't to extract Oliver and radios, "Sanchez here, we'll getcha home. What the—Huggables on the ship! AHHH! Mayday, mayday, we're going down!" Huggables swarm the ship and it crashes. Oliver later finds a still-working machine gun in the wreckage, and his reaction "closely resembles the Rage Face Internet meme" [Fan-wiki] ([Sanchez](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Sanchez)). The wiki cannot decide whether the crash left him "almost killed" (his own page) or killed (the BB Zombies plot page) [Fan-wiki] ([Battle Bears: Zombies](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Bears:_Zombies)). Wiki-hosted cutscene stills show a brown-furred pilot in an olive-green flight helmet, orange goggles and white harness straps, and a crash frame reading "THE SYSTEM IS DOWN!" [Observed] ([still](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:Sanchez.jpg)). SkyVu settled the question in later copy: "Resurrected and re-engineered from near death, Sanchez is now an agility maestro. He effortlessly circles his enemies, mastering the battlefield with unmatched movement" [Canon] ([characters](https://battlebears.com/pages/characters)).

He returned in BBG 2.6 on December 20, 2013. SkyVu's preview two days earlier had promised "Sanchez, the all new Arbiter class!". His kit was the Bind Gun, Glue Bomb, Cloak Field, Speed Field, Blade Augment and Harvester [Canon] ([archived 2.6 notes](https://web.archive.org/web/20131224204516/http://battlebears.com/battle-bears-gold-update-2-6-now-available-full-release-notes/); [teaser](https://web.archive.org/web/20131224203032/http://battlebears.com/battle-bears-gold-update-2-6-new-class-announcement/)). The Microsoft Store pitched him as "ready to control the battlefield! Using his unique slowing weapons" [Canon] ([archived](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330042343/http://apps.microsoft.com/windows/en-us/app/battle-bears-gold/b59fe74f-8081-4bfc-8738-446619cf9c7a)). His BBG class blurb is the line every short should use: "Back from the brink of death, Sanchez has been rebuilt from the arm up!" [Game text via wiki] ([BBG Versions](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Bears_Gold/Versions)). The wiki's BBH table, whose in-game origin is unconfirmed, adds motive: "Sanchez has survived a near-hugged to death experience, and he's made sure no Huggable will sneak up behind him again… Adios, enemigos" [Fan-wiki]. No source names who rebuilt him. The wiki infers rescue by the Battle Bears from a logo on his chestplate, which researchers could not see in the images [Fan-wiki, Unverified]. His kit has changed over time. The Bind Gun was reworked into the Adrenaline Gun, which speeds enemies up, on June 19, 2021, and his health rose from 100 to 150 [Fan-wiki] ([Adrenaline Gun](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Adrenaline_Gun)). The stable elements across versions are the fields, the arm blade and claws, and the rebuilt arm [Inference].

His personality comes through in transcribed BBG barks: solemn, theatrical and fatalistic, with comic slips. "That's where you belong. In the dirt!", "Inadequate. This word describes you.", "My weapon, she is ready!", "For you, I give pain.", "Your blood is my honor.", "I'm not even right-handed!", "Ay, dios mio!", "D'oh!", "Señor muertos... I see your face...", "Every sun must set.", "This victory, it was meant to be.", "If he learns from defeat, he can never lose.", "Come to Sanchez.", "Magnificent!" [Fan-wiki] ([Arbiter (BBG)](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Arbiter_%28BBG%29)). Item text runs a gag about the crash: "So he has a few gray hairs, he just came back from almost dying!" and "So we tried to describe to Sanchez what he looked like after the crash, he has not returned from the bathroom..."; his arm blade is something "Sanchez did not ask for… but he still thinks it is really really cool" [Game text via wiki] ([Gray Sanchez](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Gray_Sanchez); [Blade Augment](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Blade_Augment)). His nationality is unresolved. The wiki says he "seems American" in BB Zombies but "Spanish" in BBG and that he "speaks mainly in Spanish". Its own transcriptions are mostly English with Spanish phrases, and no official source states his nationality [Fan-wiki; Inference]. The wiki also quotes the handedness joke both as "right-handed" and "left handed" [Fan-wiki]. The notes sum him up as speaking "like a doomed hero in a telenovela" while hating Huggables personally and secretly loving his new arm [Inference].

SkyVu's December 2013 art defines his BBG look [Observed] ([weapon card](https://web.archive.org/web/2013/http://battlebears.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/12/sanchez_weapon_ad2-900x691.jpg)). He wears a dark maroon armoured helmet with bear-ear bumps and a wide glowing red visor, and has a light tan face with a small dark chin tuft. He has a grey-khaki chest plate over a camo shirt, olive camo trousers and dark boots. His right arm is a bulky red-and-black mechanical prosthetic, and he carries a glossy blue Bind Gun, a Blade Augment that "Attaches to Arm", three-clawed Harvester talons, and dome fields coloured yellow for speed and purple for cloak. The 2.6 banner adds a gritted-teeth grimace against a desert, and the 2024 BBH icon keeps the helmet, visor, goatee and camo [Observed] ([BBH icon](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0370/2469/4408/files/Sanchez_BBH_HeroIcon512_1.png)). No voice actor is credited, and the Steam trailer has no splash card for him [Observed]. His taunts are "Creepin" (a Thriller zombie stagger), "The Fifth Ruby" (awkward flailing) and "Fox Say Whaaaa!?" (a hop-and-nod parody of "What Does the Fox Say") [Fan-wiki]. The obvious relationship anchor is Oliver, whom he died, or nearly died, trying to rescue [Fan-wiki; Inference].

## Three official visual registers and a teen rating set the animation rules

SkyVu has used three visual registers, and only one of them for narrative [Inference]. The 2009–2012 story cutscenes are 2D. BB Zombies is soft and painterly, with sketchy dark outlines and flat green-hill backgrounds. BB-1 and BLAST are crisp digital cel animation with uniform black outlines, flat fills, simple dot eyes, and textured or near-photographic backgrounds such as a concrete wall or a tiled bathroom [Observed] ([BB-1 frame](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/E6lLj4HfAsc/hq3.jpg)). The BBG splash cards, reused in the trailer on the 2026 Steam page, are outlined renders of the chunky low-poly 3D models over grungy 2D backgrounds with glitch scanlines, paint splatter and neon magenta, cyan and orange. Each card has a per-character font (serif for Astoria, dripping slime for the Huggable, stencil serif for Riggs), and cards exist only for the eight 2013 launch classes [Observed] ([Steam: BBG](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4408320)). That trailer, "Battle Bears Gold Premium - Trailer" [Canon], has class cards pixel-identical to the 2013 BBG trailer [Observed] ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rfAyqVJrli4)), so its gameplay is most likely 2013 footage, not the 2026 build [Inference]. The 2024 BBH portraits are painted busts with thick outlines and swirled backgrounds [Observed] ([characters](https://battlebears.com/pages/characters)). The notes conclude that a BBG short looks most "official" by taking character looks from the BBG register and comedic staging and timing from the cutscene register [Inference]. No official model sheets, colour keys, turnarounds or scale charts were found. The comedy follows a repeatable grammar, documented in SkyVu's own copy. Characters get "Hugged to DEATH!" and the short ends abruptly on "You Lose!" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7IBdqMm5nKg)). Gore is cartoonish and rainbow-coloured ("Huggable viscera is pretty!"). Unicorns turn up everywhere: as wagon teams, as the "Unicorn Horn Crossbow", and as a map set "INSIDE A GIANT UNICORN??!!!!" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SSwlTVbW8Wg)). Bear puns are constant ("The Right to Bear Arms", "Bear With Me"), and a self-aware narrator mocks the plot "for the sake of convenience for the story" [Canon] ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P6z5l7toC3w)). Swearing is played for laughs through bleeps: SkyVu's "WARNING! FCC Censorship!" teaser and the BB Zombies stage track "The Swear-a-phone" [Canon] ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4TeHykdlMew)). The notes add that a faithful short pairs cute enemies with deadpan violence, lets the bears be competent but petty, puts deadpan Oliver or Riggs reactions against Wil's panic, and uses pop-culture parody in moderation [Inference]. The content ceiling is a teen rating. The 2026 BBG is rated USK 12 in Germany, DEJUS 14 in Brazil and IGRS 13 in Indonesia, all for fantasy violence [Canon] ([Steam: BBG](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4408320)). Some fan-transcribed lines and taunts, such as Astoria's "Tea-Bagging", sit above that ceiling [Inference].

For casting, the 2012 credits give a sound-alike brief for six bears and ABBi. One actor, Marc Cohen, voiced both Wil and Tillman, so a small production can double-cast those two "accurate to the original production" [Canon; Inference]. Every other class needs a fresh voice, and fan-uploaded audio rips are the only reference. A fan showcase of the 2026 closed playtest, "BBG All Classes Showcase [Skins, Taunts, Weapons, and Melee]" (JustNubz, September 20, 2026), has chapters for all eleven classes. It is the best available look at the 2026 models and taunts, though no researcher viewed it ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-lAtv-j9Sms)). On fan work, SkyVu runs a fan-art showcase that takes submissions by email or through its Discord "Creative" channel. It also invites "ALL creators" to a Creator Program that was written for BBH [Canon] ([fan art](https://battlebears.com/pages/fan-art); [creators](https://battlebears.com/pages/careers)). Its site Terms, however, prohibit "reproducing, modifying, distributing" the site's own content [Canon] ([terms](https://battlebears.com/pages/terms)), and no official policy on fan films, game audio, monetisation or AI-generated fan work was found. The notes read this as favouring redrawn characters over ripped assets, and they say generative-AI films may be received less warmly given Steam's "created by human artists" note. This is a reading of tone, not legal advice [Inference].

## Conclusion

The documentation splits the roster into a "story six" and an "arena five". Oliver, Riggs, Wil, Astoria, Graham and Tillman have SkyVu-written character briefs, credited voices and, for the first three, officially described story moments. The Huggable, B-1000, Botch, Saberi and Sanchez exist mostly as narrator jokes in item descriptions. For a short-film series, that gap is an opening. SkyVu itself rewrites roles freely: Tillman has four allegiances across its texts, Saberi moves between Combat Tech, Medic and Healer, and Sanchez goes from dead pilot to agility maestro. SkyVu's BBH page also says "Every Character in BB Heroes deserves their own Campaign!" ([games](https://battlebears.com/pages/games)). The notes' canon-safe framing is BBG's own arena conceit. Matches staged as training, rivalry or exhibition bouts contradict nothing official, and former enemies can share a team as long as the film does not claim an official reason [Inference].

The practical sequence for the user's "souls" follows from this. Build each one from the canon card or site line first. Add game-labelled story beats from the wiki with their labels intact. Verify every bark against the Slinkster rips before it goes into a script. Hold the open-question items above as deliberate choices rather than facts. Two pending sources could move these answers: the 2026 "Q&A with the Creator" and the October 19–26 demo. The demo will show whether the 2013 kits, voice lines and skins survive into the version most viewers will know.
