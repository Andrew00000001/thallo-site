# Tillman, the Demolition

> Tillman is a gung-ho, foul-mouthed robot panda, once an enemy of the Battle Bears, who now begs permission to charge in and wears every near-miss explosion like a medal.

## Canon anchor

1. "I give to you TILLMAN THE DELTA BEAR! Tillman, exactly 1/3rd of the Delta Bear team. Exactly 1/1 of the Delta Bears' explosives division. Mostly Panda, ALL ROBOT." [Canon] ([2011 blog][blog])
2. "Gung ho master of heavy artillery. He'll charge into battle without a second glance (unlike Wil, he knows exactly what he's getting into). Loves to follow orders and yell "sir yes sir!" Tillman has no subtlety or grace. He's foul-mouthed and avoids anything "fancy". He's always begging the Captain to charge in guns-a-blazing. For Tillman, every time that he almost gets blown up on every mission is a badge of honor." [Canon card] ([card][card])
3. "Reckless bounty hunter extraordinaire, Tillman is the Michael Bay of space pirates. If it can explode, it will. If it cant explode, it will." [Canon card] ([BBF card][bbfcard]; first sentence also on the [BBF heroes page][bbf])
4. "An explosive personality with an even more explosive moveset, once a powerful enemy of the Battle Bears, Tillman has learned the power of this team the hard way." [Canon] ([characters][chars])
5. "Many have made the mistake of underestimating Wil, many have faced the consequences. Just ask Tillman." [Canon] (Wil's entry, [characters][chars])

## Soul

- **Wants:** a fight, now, ideally with permission (canon: "IT'S FIGHTIN' TIME", [blog][blog]; "begging the Captain to charge in").  **Fears / flaw:** no subtlety; his own splash damage (canon: "be very careful to avoid hitting yourself!", [BBR classes][bbr]).  **Contradiction that makes them funny:** "sir yes sir!" order-lover *and* "maverick-rogue" (canon: card; blog); he obeys perfectly when the order is "charge" [BBG News invention].
- **Core values:** the team he joined "the hard way", orders, near-death as honour (canon: site; card).  **What they'd never do:** turn down a fight or do "fancy" (canon: "never turns down a fight", [blog][blog]; card); sneak (from "no subtlety") [Inference]; admit Wil won fair [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy engine:** chaos agent who thinks he's the straight man [BBG News invention]: literal obedience, overkill, self-blast, pride in surviving (canon: card; BBR warning); third-person brags ([Fan-wiki] "Tillman gonna give it to ya").

## Voice

- **Delivery:** loud, shouty bursts; manic laugh on kills [Inference; audio unchecked]; a raspy texture is a BBG News choice [BBG News invention]. Drops g's ("Waitin'", "yer dyin'") and quotes movies [Fan-wiki]; yells "sir yes sir!" [Canon card].
- **Swearing without swearing:** canonically "foul-mouthed", so swears are bleeped, drowned by a blast or swapped for "FIGHTIN'!" [BBG News invention], like SkyVu's "WARNING! FCC Censorship!" gag [Canon] ([YouTube][fcc]).
- **Known lines** [Fan-wiki; verify by ear against the [Slinkster rip][rip]] ([Demo (BBG)][wd]; [Tillman][wt]): "You look surprised. I MEAN DEAD! *Maniacal laughter*" / "Yippi-KI-yay!" / "You call that PAIN?!" / "Rose...bud..." / "The sun was in my eyes!" / "Tillman gonna give it to ya, we gonna give it to ya!" (also "…he gonna…") / "Waitin' on you Chub Scout!" (BB Zero) / "You ain't as stupid as you look!" (BB Zero, to Wil). Never use "Walk it off, ya nancy!" (slur) or "Was it good for you, too?" (innuendo).
- **New lines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. "Sir yes sir! Quick question, sir: which part do I blow up? ...All of it? SIR YES SIR!"
  2. "Tillman don't do 'fancy.' Tillman does 'crater.'"
  3. "Almost got blown up AGAIN! That's three badges before breakfast!"
  4. "Fetch, Mole! Good boy! No, not toward ME, not toward—" *BOOM*
  5. "Plan? Oh, I got a plan. Step one: FIGHTIN'. Step two: see step one."
- **Casting:** credited voice Marc Cohen, who also voiced Wil [Canon] ([BBR credits][credits]), for reference only; never clone or imitate a real person's voice. Cast an original raspy, chesty shouter with a drill-recruit bark [BBG News invention]. One performer for Wil and Tillman mirrors the original production [Inference].

## Relationships

- **Wil:** "unlike Wil…" [Canon card]; "Just ask Tillman" [Canon]. Wil and ABBi beat him in BB Zero after Skiles and Swanny ditched him [Fan-wiki] ([Tillman][wt]). Tillman's grudge is [Inference]; Wil has forgotten the fight and never notices Tillman's rematch demands [BBG News invention].
- **Oliver:** Astoria makes both "feel incompetent" [Canon card] ([Astoria card][acard]); rival boom-makers with bigger-explosion contests [BBG News invention].
- **Riggs:** card says "Captain and leader" [Canon card] ([Riggs card][rcard]), but Tillman's Captain is unnamed; don't equate them [Inference]. Riggs brakes; Tillman begs to charge [BBG News invention].
- **Huggable:** no canon link; the species is "paradoxically feared for their deadly hugs" [Canon] ([characters][chars]). He can't decide whether the playable one is a teammate or a target [BBG News invention].
- **Astoria:** makes him feel incompetent [Canon card]; the wiki says a comic puts them "on another ship" [Fan-wiki, Unverified] ([Astoria][wa]). He tries to impress her with bigger booms; she corrects his blast math. No romance [BBG News invention].
- **Graham:** towers keep bears alive "Usually" [Canon] ([BBF][bbf]); that they cover Tillman's charges is [Inference]; endless rebuilds [BBG News invention].
- **B-1000 / Botch / Saberi:** no canon links. B-1000 can't file him as robot or bear; Botch is his quiet fan ("Pretty!"); Saberi, the "momma bear" [Canon] ([characters][chars]), patches him up, scolds him and confiscates his fireworks [BBG News invention].
- **Sanchez:** no canon link. Tillman envies Sanchez's real return "from near death" [Canon] ([characters][chars]); Sanchez finds Tillman's badge-of-honor near-misses amateur [BBG News invention].
- **Skiles, Swanny & H.I.P.:** "Delta Bear team" [Canon] ([blog][blog]); its members, the ditching and an H.I.P. base "suggesting an alliance" [Fan-wiki] ([Skiles][wsk]); Swanny later of "the Alpha Bears" [Canon] ([heroes][heroes]). Unreconciled.
- **ABBi:** helped beat him [Fan-wiki]; Wil's "mocking robot backpack" [Canon] ([characters][chars]) never lets it go [BBG News invention].
- **Nyx / Captain Malcolm:** Nyx defected to H.I.P. [Canon] ([heroes][heroes]), while Tillman went from "enemy" to the team [Canon] ([characters][chars]); mirror banter [BBG News invention]. Malcolm, BB-1 pilot [Fan-wiki] ([Malcolm][wm]), is not confirmed as "the Captain" [Inference].

## Look (for animation)

- **Default era: BBG 2013,** in the shared BBG News house style (see [README](README.md)), dressed per SkyVu's c. 2012–13 cards [Observed], used as the BBG default skin ("Plain old vanilla Tillman!" [Game text via wiki] ([skin][wskin])); that the cards show that skin is [Inference], and nobody examined the in-game model. Alternatives [Observed]: masked red-and-black trailer-card skin with an olive artillery shell ([Steam trailer][trailer]); 2024 BBH portrait ([icon][icon]); BB Zero concept art ([art][delta]).
- **Silhouette:** stocky, round-bellied, hands on hips ([card][card]); shorter and wider than Skiles and Swanny ([art][delta]) [Observed]. No official scale; on the shared BBG News scale chart he is ~0.9× Oliver's height (level with Wil) but as wide as Riggs, the widest of the short bears [BBG News invention].
- **Colours** (researcher's estimates from the 2024 portrait, not official) [Inference]: white #DDDEE2, shade #BDBEC7, dark #162D32, gunmetal #4B555F; a tiny #B6420A swatch may be rim or background, so keep it off the body. Teal pack #3F7F86 is an unsampled placeholder [BBG News invention].
- **Body:** white head and torso, black ears and eye patches, segmented black robotic limbs with ball joints, glowing white eyes under an angry brow [Observed]; cracks top-right of head and bottom-left of body [Fan-wiki] ([Tillman][wt]). Wiki: "completely animatronic" ([Tillman][wt]), yet gibs show "pink and red flesh" ([Demo (BBG)][wd]) [Fan-wiki]; show only metal and sparks [BBG News invention].
- **Feet:** skates/wheels [Fan-wiki] ([Tillman][wt]); wheel-like in concept art, blocky on the BBF card [Observed]; BBG News uses wheels [BBG News invention].
- **Gear:** cylindrical teal backpack on a diagonal teal chest strap [Observed]; a jet pack in BB Zero [Fan-wiki] ([Tillman][wt]).
- **Weapons:** Big Shot "(Chest Cannon)", Fireworks ("lock on feature"), Buzzard ("Double Buzzsaw Extension") [Canon] ([blog][blog]), still BBG defaults [Fan-wiki]; Blast-O-Mole, "Tillman's pet mole" [Game text via wiki] ([mole][wmole]); BBH "Firework Flurry" [Canon] ([characters][chars]). Cannon drawn as a chest hatch [BBG News invention].
- **Skins:** Cyborg ("Tillman can't tell if he looks more like a robot or a bear now." [Game text via wiki] ([Cyborg][wcy])); Gold Tillman, Old Timey, Horseless Headsman, Bearmax [Fan-wiki] ([Demo (BBG)][wd]).

## Movement & acting

- **Posture & idle:** hands on hips [Observed] ([card][card]); chest out, revving his wheels awaiting orders [BBG News invention].
- **Walk:** slow class, 160 health [Fan-wiki] ([Demo (BBG)][wd]); top-heavy roll, bad brakes [BBG News invention].
- **Fighting:** "jump bazooka first into a firefight" [Canon] ([blog][blog]); everything does "splash damage" [Canon] ([BBR][bbr]).
- **Pain & victory:** "I've had worse!", "Aw, too easy!" [Fan-wiki] ([Demo (BBG)][wd]); near-death is "a badge of honor" [Canon card].
- **Taunts** [Fan-wiki] ([Taunts][wtaunt]): Robot ("jerky, robotic movement"), Flex (flex, curl down, flex again), Running Man (marching on the spot).
- **Comedy beats** [BBG News invention]: (1) salutes so hard the chest cannon fires; (2) the Blast-O-Mole fetches the bomb back to his wheels; (3) blown to scrap, he reassembles mid-Flex and pins on his scorch marks as medals.

## Story engine

- **Best fit:** 15–30 s single-gag escalation (order, overkill, self-blast, proud survival) ending on an abrupt "You Lose!" card, staged as a training bout [Inference].
- **Loglines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. **Rematch:** Tillman demands a rematch to settle BB Zero; Wil, busy with a snack, wins again without noticing while ABBi narrates.
  2. **Badge Envy:** jealous of Sanchez's comeback from near death, Tillman tries to almost-die harder; Saberi runs out of bandages.
  3. **Nothing Fancy:** Astoria orders a silent extraction; Tillman's idea of "quiet" is one very large firework.

## Prompt pack

- **Character reference prompt:** Fan-art character model sheet (front, side, back and three-quarter views) of Tillman, a squat, round-bellied robot panda soldier; stylized 3D mobile-game character, chunky low-poly early-2010s style, simple textures, saturated colours, bold readable silhouette. White head and torso, black ears and eye patches, glowing white eyes under an angry brow, segmented black robotic limbs with ball joints, wheels for feet, cylindrical teal backpack on a diagonal teal chest strap, small armour cracks at top right of head and bottom left of body; hands on hips, eager and grumpy. Neutral grey background, even lighting, no text.
- **Video prompt starter:** "A squat white-and-black robot panda with glowing white eyes and a teal backpack rolls in on wheel-feet, salutes, and gleefully launches fireworks as explosions rattle him. Chunky low-poly early-2010s mobile-game 3D, saturated colours, snappy cartoon timing."
- **Negative prompt:** realistic fur, real panda, cute pupils, Kung Fu Panda styling, brown or pink fur, no backpack, human hands, flesh, blood, gore, cigars, sexual content, readable profanity, logos, watermarks, real-person likeness.
- **Short name for tools:** `tillman-demolition`

## Canon vs invention ledger

- **Canon you can state as fact:** Delta Bear and its explosives division; "Mostly Panda, ALL ROBOT"; "maverick-rogue"; gung-ho, order-loving, foul-mouthed, unsubtle; near-death a "badge of honor"; "Michael Bay of space pirates"; former enemy now with the team; "Just ask Tillman"; Big Shot, Fireworks, Buzzard, Firework Flurry; Marc Cohen voice (BBR); Steam class "DEMOLITION".
