# Tillman, the Demolition

> Tillman is a gung-ho, foul-mouthed robot panda, once an enemy of the Battle Bears, who now begs permission to charge in and wears every near-miss explosion like a medal.

## Canon anchor

1. "I give to you TILLMAN THE DELTA BEAR! Tillman, exactly 1/3rd of the Delta Bear team. Exactly 1/1 of the Delta Bears' explosives division. Mostly Panda, ALL ROBOT." [Canon] ([2011 blog][blog])
2. "Gung ho master of heavy artillery. He'll charge into battle without a second glance (unlike Wil, he knows exactly what he's getting into). Loves to follow orders and yell "sir yes sir!" Tillman has no subtlety or grace. He's foul-mouthed and avoids anything "fancy". He's always begging the Captain to charge in guns-a-blazing. For Tillman, every time that he almost gets blown up on every mission is a badge of honor." [Canon card] ([card][card])
3. "Reckless bounty hunter extraordinaire, Tillman is the Michael Bay of space pirates. If it can explode, it will. If it cant explode, it will." [Canon card] ([BBF card][bbfcard]; first sentence also on the [BBF heroes page][bbf])
4. "An explosive personality with an even more explosive moveset, once a powerful enemy of the Battle Bears, Tillman has learned the power of this team the hard way." [Canon] ([characters][chars])
5. "Many have made the mistake of underestimating Wil, many have faced the consequences. Just ask Tillman." [Canon] (Wil's entry, [characters][chars])

## Soul

- **Wants:** a fight, now, ideally with permission (canon: "IT'S FIGHTIN' TIME", [blog][blog]; "begging the Captain to charge in").  **Fears / flaw:** no subtlety; his own splash damage (canon: "be very careful to avoid hitting yourself!", [BBR classes][bbr]).  **Contradiction that makes them funny:** "sir yes sir!" order-lover *and* "maverick-rogue" (canon: card; blog); he obeys perfectly when the order is "charge" [BBG News invention].
- **Core values:** the team he joined "the hard way", orders, near-death as honour (canon: site; card).  **What they'd never do:** turn down a fight or do "fancy" (canon: "never turns down a fight", [blog][blog]; card); sneak (from "no subtlety") [Inference]; admit Wil won fair [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy engine:** chaos agent who thinks he's the straight man [BBG News invention]: literal obedience, overkill, self-blast, pride in surviving (canon: card; BBR warning); third-person brags ([Fan-wiki] "Tillman gonna give it to ya").

**Default loadout**

The default loadout is Big Shot / Fireworks / Buzzard: the BBR launch loadout (2011), carried into BBG [Fan-wiki]. The 2011 blog lists Big Shot "(Chest Cannon)", Fireworks ("lock on feature") and Buzzard ("Double Buzzsaw Extension") [Canon] ([blog][blog]). That wording could not be re-opened on 2026-10-16/17 (web.archive.org was unreachable), but all three names are independently official in the archived BBG 2.6 change log of Dec 2013 [Canon].

- **Big Shot** (primary, default; in the 2013 trailer footage; equipped in fan build 3.0.2 [Observed]) ([Big Shot][wbs]). Confidence: partial.
  - **Look:** a squat drum launcher braced two-handed at the belly, muzzle forward [Observed] ([loadout][wbsimg]; Steam trailer about 21.7–22.2 s).
    - Parts: a ribbed olive-grey drum (#73776A–#818473) in a dark-purple bracket frame (#391641–#502151) with grey edges, and a notched yellow muzzle disc (#D9B50F) [Observed, sampled].
    - Size: about 0.45× Tillman's height wide [Inference].
    - It is hand-held, not a hatch in the chest. The 2011 "(Chest Cannon)" most likely means "a cannon braced at the chest" [Inference].
  - **Firing / projectile:**
    - The game text says "Launches a giant missile out at a straight trajectory." [Game text via wiki]; the wiki says it "fires damaging missiles in an arc" [Fan-wiki].
    - As of Dec 2013 it held 6 rounds with a 1 s cooldown [Canon].
    - The round is a dark navy/black rounded bullet with white eye-like markings, trailing a pale smoke streak [Observed, BBR-era image: File:8 battle bears royale.jpg].
  - **Impact:** it explodes on contact (AoE 100/200) [Fan-wiki]. In the trailer (about 18.5–22 s; the fireball itself about 19.9–20.4 s) an orange-yellow fireball engulfs Wil and flings him, intact, while Tillman aims the Big Shot [Observed per the Tillman, 2026 and combat-VFX dossiers; attribution Inference, moderate]. The Wil dossier reads the same seconds as Wil's own Batterito fire breath [Observed there] ([wil.md](wil.md)), so the event is [Open]: never caption or stage "Tillman blasts Wil" as trailer fact, and re-check the frames before quoting the trailer in a short.
- **Fireworks** (secondary, default) ([Fireworks][wfw]). Confidence: partial.
  - **Look:** four slim red firecracker rockets (#D02C34–#E23126) with orange-red cone tips (#DA4122), upright on the black-and-grey back bar, two per side, with gold fuse sticks [Observed, sampled].
    - Each rocket carries a grinning pink-violet Huggable face: round ears, big dark eyes and a wide white toothy grin (#C0598E–#CF4770) [Observed].
    - Size: each about 0.25× Tillman's height [Inference].
  - **Lock-on:** official into Dec 2013 ("Lock radius increased from 300 to 350 / Lock time increased from 0.8 to 1") [Canon]. The wiki now says "It cannot lock on to targets" [Fan-wiki].
  - **Firing / projectile:**
    - The fuses light and the rockets leap straight up in pairs on long yellow-white flame plumes [Observed, fan footage 2016].
    - They come down "in a spiralling cascade" onto the reticle spot [Fan-wiki]. A falling rocket reads as an orange nose on a white-yellow streak [Observed; Inference].
    - 0.5 s rate of fire [Fan-wiki].
  - **Impact:** a scattered cluster of chunky orange fire puffs (#E7912A) with yellow and white-hot cores and red, pink and white spark flecks, not one big blast [Observed, fan footage; attribution Inference, moderate]. The wiki: "a flurry of damaging, orange sparks", AoE 125/250 [Fan-wiki].
- **Buzzard** (melee, default) ([Buzzard][wbuz]). Confidence: observed.
  - **Look:** two curved white robotic arms on black ribbed hose joints arch over his head. Each holds a big circular sawblade in a black-and-white housing [Observed] ([Buzzards loadout][wbuzzimg]). The saw is about 0.6× Tillman's height across [Inference].
  - **Swing:** a slow (about 1 s) overhead swing with long reach. Per the wiki it "can hit targets directly behind and a significant distance above the player… they cannot hit targets directly in front of the player" [Fan-wiki].
  - **Impact:**
    - Melee damage is 70 in the wiki text [Fan-wiki] and 25 in the official Dec 2013 log [Canon].

    - BBG melee contact shows 4–7 yellow five-point cartoon stars and a one-frame flash [Observed].

## Movement & acting

- **Timing (house style):** snappy feature-film timing: strong key poses, quick moves, clear holds, and squash and stretch; no slow, floaty drifting and no wandering camera ([STYLE.md](STYLE.md)). Tillman's holds are his medals: after every blast, a smoking, chest-out proud hold of about a second before the next move [BBG News invention].
- **Posture & idle:** hands on hips [Observed] ([card][card]); chest out, revving his wheels while he waits for orders [BBG News invention]. Between shots he tucks the Big Shot drum against his round belly under one arm, which frees a hand to salute [BBG News invention].
- **Walk:**
  - He is a slow class with 160 health [Fan-wiki] ([Demo (BBG)][wd]). The official 2.6 log (Dec 2013) reads: "Tillman Base Speed increased from 235 to 240. Tillman Base Health increased from 150 to 170." [Canon] The wiki's 2015 revision also said 170 [Fan-wiki].
  - A top-heavy roll with bad brakes [BBG News invention]: in the house style he leans into the roll, skids to a stop with a squash through the belly, and wobbles into a hold.
- **Fighting:**
  - He likes to "jump bazooka first into a firefight" [Canon] ([blog][blog]).
  - Everything he has does "splash damage" [Canon] ([BBR][bbr]). The wiki also quotes this blurb as the BBG class description: "Tillman does not have a weapon on hand that doesn't have splash damage! … be very careful to avoid hitting yourself!" [Game text via wiki].
  - In practice the Reverse Fountain is a non-explosive "Secondary - DPS" spark spray, and the melees are saws, plush heads and maces [Fan-wiki/Observed].
- **Weapon acting** [BBG News invention, built on the Observed carry and the Fan-wiki mechanics]:
  - **Primaries:** braced two-handed at the belly (except the Twin Bearzookas, on the shoulders), so each shot is a small squash through the belly and a crisp hold, never a big recoil.
  - **Secondaries:** they leave the back bar. He hunches, the fuses fizz, and the rockets clear his head.
  - **Lock-ons:** green crescent "LOCKED" brackets snap onto the target [Observed, SkyVu 2014 footage]. Add the green crescent lock brackets in the edit as an original-art overlay; never prompt them (STYLE.md).
  - **Melee:** the arms swing over his head and behind him, and they cannot hit anything straight in front of him [Fan-wiki]. His melee gag is chopping everything except the enemy in his face.
- **Pain & victory:** "I've had worse!", "Aw, too easy!" [Fan-wiki] ([Demo (BBG)][wd]); near-death is "a badge of honor" [Canon card].
- **Taunts** [Fan-wiki] ([Taunts][wtaunt]): Robot ("jerky, robotic movement"), Flex (flex, curl down, flex again), Running Man (marching on the spot).
- **Comedy beats** [BBG News invention]:
  1. He salutes so hard the Big Shot drum at his belly goes off.
  2. He throws the Blast-O-Mole like a fetch toy, and it "brings back" its marching line of flame pillars to his wheels.
  3. Blown to scrap, he reassembles mid-Flex and pins on his scorch marks as medals.
