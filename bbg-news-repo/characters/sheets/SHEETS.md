# Character reference sheets (house style)

These are the Element sheets: one 4K model sheet per character, in the feature-film 3D house style. Each one is attached as the character's reference in every image and video, to keep the look on-model.

- **Model:** Higgsfield Nano Banana Pro, 16:9, 4K (5504×3072), 4 credits a sheet.
- **Prompts:** taken word for word from each soul's Prompt pack. Full-resolution files stay in Higgsfield under the job IDs below. The JPGs here are 1600 px previews.
- **QA:** each sheet is checked against its soul's Look section before it becomes the Element. "Fix" means the sheet needs a targeted edit pass before use.

## QA notes

- **Riggs: pass.** The pale blue-grey plush, red segmented helmet with grey ear tips, harness, yellow-green-ringed medallion, forearm and shin bands and round red back-shell all match the soul.
- **Huggable: fix.**
  - Matches the soul: the button eye with the X stitch on its right, the scar, the grin, the chest seam with ticks, the torn stuffing, the rainbow-smeared jaws and the banner with circle, triangle and square.
  - Off-model: the paws inside the jaws, and the inset paw, are peach skin. They should be hot-pink plush paws.
  - Off-model: the bandage and radio sit on its left side. The soul puts them on its right, over its right ear, on the same side as the button eye.
- **Wil: pass, minor.** The upright ears, red padded helmet with vents and chin strap, ice-pop tank top with drip and stick, glowing red knee pads and rear-facing lens backpack all match. The elbow bands are missing their glowing red pads; fix this in the edit pass if a shot shows his elbows.
- **Graham: fix.**
  - Matches the soul: the hard hat, cyan goggles, brows, moustache, gloves and belt.
  - Off-model: the sash runs from his left shoulder to his right hip. The soul says right shoulder to left hip.
  - Missing: the three thin white stripes on each upper arm.
  - Off-model: the radio pack sits low. Its top should be level with his hat brim.
  - The fur reads as smooth vinyl rather than plush.
- **B-1000: pass, minor.** The maroon armour, low head with cyan eye-slits, cyan claws on the left forearm and the white-and-orange jump-pack all match.
  - The rail-gun looks held rather than built into the forearm.
  - The small chest disc and the claw gauntlet's looping hose are not visible.
  - Usable as is. Tighten the rail-gun mount in the edit pass if a close-up needs it.
