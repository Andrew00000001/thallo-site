# B-1000, the Assault

> B-1000 is a half-ton killer robot who studies the Battle Bears so he can annihilate them and send their data to unnamed villains, and who reports every kill, wound and malfunction like a cheerful error log.

## Soul

- **Wants:** to annihilate the Battle Bears and send "the bad guys" their data (game text [Game text via wiki]: class description), with applause ([Fan-wiki] "Initiate brag sequence!").  **Fears / flaw:** slow, with "frustratingly long reload times" and a detachable arm ([Fan-wiki] stats; "Falling Apart" taunt); dreads a corrupted upload more than scrap [BBG News invention].  **Contradiction that makes them funny:** the "bringer of doom and destruction" has an orb "best friend" ("A small orb that fires missiles whenever he fires, the satellite is a B1000's best friend!"), plays "hide and seek", runs "on sugar cookies" (game text [Game text via wiki]: default skin, Satellite, Smoke Screen, Sugah Cookehs; the Satellite line is also the 2026 in-game text [Observed]) and giggles "tee-hee" ([Fan-wiki]).
- **Core values:** logic ([Fan-wiki] "Logic demolishes your squishy flesh!"); complete data, so he logs even his humiliations [BBG News invention].  **What they'd never do:** whisper, delete a file, or admit an error was his [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy engine:** deadpan menace undercut by tech-support jargon and failing hardware: the galaxy's loudest spy (game text [Game text via wiki]: "Everything about B1000 is loud and explosive"), whose status reports state the obvious while his body betrays his dignity [BBG News invention].

### Weapons

He has 14 BBG weapons [Fan-wiki] ([Assault (BBG)][assault]). Thirteen are listed in the 2026 Closed Playtest #4 Gear Up screens [Observed: fan storyboard frames] ([showcase][showcase], 140–260 s): primaries Bass Cannon, Bearail Gun, Grenader, Energy Overflow, SRCG, Lightning Bolt; secondaries Metal Crush, Energy Barrier, Smoke Screen, Satellite; melees Primus Sword, Shock N Claw, Axecutioner. Last Resort is not among the visible secondaries; the list scrolls, so a hidden fifth entry cannot be ruled out [Observed; Inference]. Whether each ships on Nov 4 is [Unverified]. SkyVu has announced no new, removed or reworked weapon for 2026 [Inference], but its final playtest lists "LIGHTING, EFFECT & TEXTURE IMPROVEMENTS" [Canon: image text in the Oct 14, 2026 Steam post], so 2026 effects may differ from the older footage described below [Inference].

**Loadouts**

- **Kit rule:** he carries one primary (right forearm), one secondary (back) and one melee (left forearm) [Observed: mounting], so the axe and the claws never appear together; the axe never glows, and the cyan belongs to the claws [Observed]. The game itself mixes kits freely: SkyVu's "Stormtrooper Aim" pairs a Grenader-shaped gun-arm with the Metal Crush back unit and a sliver of cyan blade that is probably the Shock N Claw [Observed; IDs Inference], and fan clips are titled "Grenader + Satellite" and "bearail gun+energy barrier" [Observed: fan video titles]. For consistent generations BBG News keeps each short on one of the named kits below, each with its own model-sheet Element, and gives any other mix its own sheet [BBG News invention].
- **2026 default (wiki): Grenader + Smoke Screen + Axecutioner.** The wiki's default primary and melee are the Grenader and the Axecutioner, and it has listed Smoke Screen as the default secondary since the 2020-11-09 edit [Fan-wiki]. SkyVu's Aug 21, 2026 livestream shows the loadout screen with an orange-and-white gun-arm and an axe, and an in-match HUD (Sky Pirates map) with the Grenader icon at 4/4, a Smoke Screen mini-icon and B-1000 health "/180" [Observed] ([livestream][live26]). Reading these as one loadout is [Inference]. BBG News uses this kit as its default for shorts set now [BBG News invention].
- **2013 launch default ("classic 2013 look" only): Grenader + Last Resort + Axecutioner** [Fan-wiki] ([Grenader][gren]; [Last Resort][last]; [Axecutioner][axe]). Since 2020-11-09 the wiki heads Last Resort "NOTE: THIS WEAPON IS REMOVED", though the Assault (BBG) page still lists it [Fan-wiki]; no SkyVu date, version or reason is known, nor what replaced it [Unverified]. Label it a retired 2013 weapon.
- **Signature promo kit (hero shots, title cards): Bearail Gun + Shock N Claw + Metal Crush.** It is the kit in the Steam trailer (2013 footage; Steam-trailer times 1.5–7.0 s, including the B-1000 title card at 5.8–7.0 s) ([Steam][steam]), on the T-shirt and in most store renders [Observed]. In launch week (2013-05-20 to 06-05) the wiki called the Bearail Gun "the default weapon for the B-1000 class" [Fan-wiki old revision], which fits these renders, so the claws were probably part of the pre-launch default kit [Fan-wiki old revision; Inference]. The default melee is still the Axecutioner [Fan-wiki].
- **The default-skin screenshot shows two of the signature weapons:** its orange-and-white gun-arm is the Bearail Gun and its "blue energy blade" is the Shock N Claw, gun on the right arm, claws on the left [Observed comparison; ID Inference] ([screenshot][shot]); the dossier identifies only these two. The earlier reading of that gun-arm as the Grenader was wrong. The default-loadout gun-arm in SkyVu's own 2026 media does have the Grenader's shape (the Aug 21 livestream, and "Stormtrooper Aim", where it is worn with the Metal Crush back unit and a cyan blade sliver on the left) [Observed; ID Inference by shape match].

## Movement & acting

- **Timing (house style):** snappy feature-film timing: strong key poses, quick moves and clear holds, with squash and stretch; no slow, floaty drift and no wandering camera ([STYLE.md](STYLE.md)). For B-1000 that means robot staccato: every move is a fast mechanical snap that lands in a dead-still hold, heavy parts (shoulder plates, the gun-arm) overshoot and settle, and a servo whine rides each snap. The squash lives in stomps and impacts, never in rubbery armour [BBG News invention].
- **Walk:** heavy, slow stomp (tied slowest base speed [Fan-wiki]; speed 215 vs. the Huggable's 330 [Game text via wiki] ([Assault template][tmpl]; [Huggable template][htmpl])); servo whine per step [BBG News invention]. Each step is a plant, a small squash through the legs, a beat of hold, then the next step [BBG News invention].
- **Idle:** slow scanning sweeps, broken into head-snaps and holds [BBG News invention]. The Satellite circling him like a pet is [BBG News invention]: every source shows the orb docked on his upper back in its orange octagonal cradle with cyan pads (#14C8F3), even while he fires [Observed], so draw it docked by default and keep any circling orb a gag labelled as invention.
- **Fighting:** plants and fires heavy area weapons, then stalls on reloads ("Ammunition repopulated!")
