# B-1000, the Assault

> B-1000 is a half-ton killer robot who studies the Battle Bears so he can annihilate them and send their data to unnamed villains, and who reports every kill, wound and malfunction like a cheerful error log.

## Canon anchor

1. "A mysterious enemy fixated on the Battle Bears' skills, aiming to annihilate them and share their data with villains." [Canon] ([characters][chars])
2. "A mysterious foe who has taken a keen interest in the combat prowess of the Battle Bears. Hobbies include: annihilating all Battle Bears and transmitting their data to the bad guys." [Game text via wiki] ([class template][tmpl])
3. "NEW Character Class- the B-1000! A robot made to kill." [Game text via wiki, BBR 1.4.3 notes] ([Updates][updates])
4. "As if a half-ton murderous robot wasn't dangerous enough on its own, we've gone and added 3 razor sharp claws, you're welcome." [Game text via wiki] ([Shock N Claw][shock])
5. "Everything about B1000 is loud and explosive, so why not give him a chain gun that fires explosive bullets?" [Game text via wiki] ([Super Rapid Chain Gun][chain])

## Soul

- **Wants:** to annihilate the Battle Bears and send "the bad guys" their data (canon [Game text via wiki]: class description), with applause ([Fan-wiki] "Initiate brag sequence!").  **Fears / flaw:** slow, with "frustratingly long reload times" and a detachable arm ([Fan-wiki] stats; "Falling Apart" taunt); dreads a corrupted upload more than scrap [BBG News invention].  **Contradiction that makes them funny:** the "bringer of doom and destruction" has a drone "best friend", plays "hide and seek", runs "on sugar cookies" (canon [Game text via wiki]: default skin, Satellite, Smoke Screen, Sugah Cookehs) and giggles "tee-hee" ([Fan-wiki]).
- **Core values:** logic ([Fan-wiki] "Logic demolishes your squishy flesh!"); complete data, so he logs even his humiliations [BBG News invention].  **What they'd never do:** whisper, delete a file, or admit an error was his [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy engine:** deadpan menace undercut by tech-support jargon and failing hardware: the galaxy's loudest spy (canon [Game text via wiki]: "Everything about B1000 is loud and explosive"), whose status reports state the obvious while his body betrays his dignity [BBG News invention].

## Voice

- **Delivery:** deadpan synthetic voice with bursts of cheer [Inference; audio unchecked]. BBG News spec: mid-low, evenly clipped, light digital grit, slightly too loud, then a bright "tee-hee"; error-message vocabulary; narrates his own actions ("Initiate…") [BBG News invention].
- **Swearing without swearing:** errors as curses ("FATAL EXCEPTION!") or a dial-up screech over the word [BBG News invention], like SkyVu's "WARNING! FCC Censorship!" gag [Canon] ([YouTube][fcc]).
- **Known lines** [Fan-wiki; verify by ear against the [Slinkster rip][rip]] ([Assault (BBG)][assault]): "Control, Alt, Deleted!" / "Logic demolishes your squishy flesh!" / "Consider your bit flipped!" / "Initiate brag sequence!" / "Hundreds of tiny metal errors detected!" / "Your PC will now power down!" / "Outcome unacceptable!" / "Satisfactory response, tee-hee". Class quote: "Control. Alt. Deleted!" [Game text via wiki] ([template][tmpl]).
- **New lines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. "Scanning Battle Bear. Threat level: fluffy."
  2. "Error 404: your survival not found."
  3. "Have you tried turning yourself off and on again? Allow me."
  4. "Uploading your embarrassing defeat to the bad guys. Tee-hee."
  5. "Brag sequence buffering… buffering… still buffering… I am extremely impressive."
- **Casting:** no source credits a voice for B-1000; the BBR credits predate him ([credits][credits]). Cast an original voice (human or AI): intelligible robotic flatness, sharp timing on the giggle, clean shouts, bitcrush in post. Never clone or imitate a real person's voice, including famous film-robot performances.

## Movement & acting

- **Walk:** heavy, slow stomp (tied slowest base speed [Fan-wiki]; speed 215 vs. the Huggable's 330 [Game text via wiki] ([Assault template][tmpl]; [Huggable template][htmpl])); servo whine per step [BBG News invention].
- **Idle:** slow scanning sweeps; the Satellite circles him like a pet [BBG News invention].
- **Fighting:** plants and fires heavy area weapons, then stalls on reloads ("Ammunition repopulated!") [Fan-wiki]; "Hide in smoke and then kill everyone!" [Game text via wiki] ([Smoke Screen][smoke]).
- **Pain & victory:** "Processing...pain..."; "Logic demolishes your squishy flesh!" [Fan-wiki]. Sparks and loose bolts, never blood [BBG News invention].
- **Taunts** [Fan-wiki] ([Taunts][taunts]): Growth Spurt ("inexplicably expands in size for a second before shrinking back"), Falling Apart ("his right arm… suddenly snaps off"; he reattaches it), Big Robo (waddling "as if he were glitching").
- **Comedy beats** [BBG News invention]: (1) he reattaches his arm backwards and shoots behind himself; (2) an indoor Smoke Screen trips the sprinklers and he rusts mid-pose; (3) Growth Spurt glitches in a doorway and wedges him.

## Story engine

- **Best fit:** a 20–40 s single gag seen through his HUD (scan labels, progress bars) that ends in malfunction, staged as an arena bout, which contradicts nothing official [Inference]; never name his makers [BBG News invention].
- **Loglines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. **Free Tune-Up:** Graham offers a free tune-up; B-1000 refuses on principle, sheds parts all match, and ends up running on a plate of sugar cookies.
  2. **Profile Pending:** ordered to profile the mysterious Botch, B-1000 runs a full interrogation protocol and transmits a one-line file to the bad guys: "Eh. Whatever." (a [Fan-wiki] Botch bark).
  3. **Brag Sequence:** B-1000 wins and starts his victory brag, but it loads so slowly that the next match begins around him.
