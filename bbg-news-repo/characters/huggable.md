# Huggable, the Huggable

> A stitched, bandaged, radio-wired pink teddy from the bears' enemy species, fielded as "a safer version" of a creature that kills with kindness, who says little beyond "Hug me!" and means it with lethal sincerity.

## Canon anchor

1. "The Modified Huggable is a safer version of the standard Huggable. Its quickness and uncanny melee strength makes it a serious threat up close and its unorthodox abilities make it unpredictable in combat." [Canon] ([BBR classes, 2012][bbr])
2. "Huggables seem to have extremely limited vocabularies, limited to "Hug me!", these colorful creatures are paradoxically feared for their deadly hugs." [Canon] ([characters][chars])
3. "The Huggable's ability to love will make time seem like it comes to a halt. When actually it's just your feet and you're about to die." [Game text via wiki] ([Phantom Hug][phantom])
4. "a unique detachable head that uncovers a secret rainbow beam, the source of the Huggable's power! Each Huggable comes with an official Kills with Kindness tag" [Canon] ([plush][plush])

## Soul

- **Wants:** to hug everyone, friend or foe (canon: class quote "Hug me!" [Game text via wiki], [template][tmpl]).  **Fears / flaw:** fragile (90 health [Fan-wiki]) and thumbless (canon: "Good thing Huggables don't have thumbs" [Game text via wiki], [Bearettas][bearettas]).  **Contradiction that makes them funny:** pure affection that works as a weapon (canon: "paradoxically feared for their deadly hugs", [characters][chars]).
- **Core values:** love at full strength, all the time (canon [Game text via wiki]: Phantom Hug's "ability to love", [Phantom Hug][phantom]).  **What they'd never do:** hold back a hug or say a mean word [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy engine:** a near-mute physical comedian (the research notes' [Inference]). Its grin stays fixed while the bears around it panic [BBG News invention]. Body horror stays cute: "it takes it's head off and tries to kill you... to death" ([Losing Your Head][lyh]), and "Slight battle damage poses no long term threat for this huggable" ([default skin][wpink]) (canon: [Game text via wiki]).

## Voice

- **Delivery:** official text gives it only "Hug me!", and no BBG lines are transcribed, though the wiki says it "has improved its vocabulary in Battle Bears Gold, possibly due to the radio attached to its head" [Fan-wiki] ([class page][wbbg]). BBG News plays it as a small, sing-song plush-toy voice, sweet to the point of eerie, with extra words crackling through the head radio [BBG News invention]. Check the [fan audio rip][rip] by ear for what it actually says before writing more lines; take words from it, never the performance.
- **Swearing without swearing:** it never swears. Frustration comes out as a burst of radio static or a bleep [BBG News invention], after SkyVu's "WARNING! FCC Censorship!" gag [Canon] ([YouTube][fcc]).
- **Known lines:**
  - "Hug me!" (the BBG class quote) [Game text via wiki] ([template][tmpl])
  - Other Huggables, not this class [Fan-wiki] ([Huggable][wspecies]; [Quartet][quartet]):
    - "Aye aye, captain!" (BB Zombies cutscene)
    - "Grass!"
    - "We care too much, we do not see you're dead until we're done" (a boss song)
- **New lines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. "Hug me! ...Hug me? ...HUG ME!"
  2. "Don't worry! I'm the safer version!"
  3. "Group hug!"
  4. "Hug me... over." *kssht*
  5. "No thumbs. Still hugs."
- **Casting note:** no Huggable voice is credited [Canon] ([credits][credits]). The wiki names Benjamin Vu and Rob Ruiz [Fan-wiki] ([Huggable][wspecies]; it credits Vu alone for BBH Huggy, [BBH][wbbh]), for reference only; never clone or imitate a real person. Cast an original voice: light, childlike and too-smiley, with a giggle and a sudden flat, low "hug me" [BBG News invention].

## Relationships

- **Oliver:** "the pink Huggables catch up to him and are about to hug him to death" [Canon] ([Cut Scene #1][cut1]). He flinches whenever his hug-happy teammate opens its arms [BBG News invention].
- **Riggs:** "A huggable's rainbow soul can power a laser gun!" [Canon, channel unconfirmed] ([cutscene][laser]); the BB-1 Riggs copy says "Huggable viscera is pretty!" [Canon, channel unconfirmed] ([Riggs 3/10][bb1-3]). Wary truce: he eyes it like a spare battery and his hand twitches toward a grenade at every "Hug me!", while it thinks he is the ship's best hug [BBG News invention].
- **Tillman:** no canon link. He can't decide whether the playable one is a teammate or a target [BBG News invention].
- **Astoria:** "Astoria always has a smile on her face after blowing off a huggable's head!" [Game text via wiki] ([Old-Timey][oldtimey]). It wants her hug most; she keeps it at scope distance [BBG News invention].
- **Wil:** "WIL forgets to clear the holodeck of HUGGABLES" [Canon] ([BLAST][blast]). He is "always eager to please a friend" [Canon card] ([card][wilcard]), so he hugs back and offers it a sandwich for another [BBG News invention].
- **Graham:** no canon link; Seesaw text credits an unnamed "Battle Bear" with the first chainsaw-handed Huggable [Game text via wiki] ([Seesaw][seesaw]). "The Modified Huggable" [Canon] ([BBR classes][bbr]) has no named modifier. Graham grumpily re-stitches it but is not its maker [BBG News invention].
- **B-1000:** no canon link. B-1000 is "transmitting their data to the bad guys" [Game text via wiki] ([template][b1000]); the wiki reads his chest emblem as H.I.P.'s Huggable logo [Fan-wiki] ([H.I.P.][whip]). The Huggable tries to hug his signal [BBG News invention].
- **Botch:** no canon link. Two near-mute oddballs; Botch never flinches from a hug [BBG News invention].
- **Saberi:** her BBH "Huggable Splicer" sprays "huggable bits" [Canon] ([characters][chars]). It keeps trying to hug the gun [BBG News invention].
- **Sanchez:** Huggables swarmed and crashed his Pelican't in BB Zombies [Fan-wiki] ([Sanchez][wsanchez]). He keeps the playable one in front of him [BBG News invention].
- **Necromancer:** "considered by some to be a god of the huggable species" [Canon] ([characters][chars]). The Huggable is starstruck [BBG News invention].
- **H.I.P.:** the name "Huggable Instrumentality Project" is [Canon] ([heroes page][heroes]). That H.I.P. made Huggables is wiki speculation [Fan-wiki] ([H.I.P.][whip]); never say it built this one.
- **Jackson:** "ready to take revenge on the huggables that put him in his chair" [Canon] ([heroes page][heroes]). He glares; it waves [BBG News invention].
- **ABBi, Nyx, Captain Malcolm:** no source ties them to the playable Huggable.

## Look (for animation)

- **Default era: BBG 2013,** in the shared BBG News house style (see [README](README.md)): the default "Pink Huggable" [Observed], from the wiki-hosted [BBG image][pinkimg] and the Huggable card in the BBG trailer on [Steam][steam]. Alternatives are the 2024 BBH "Huggy" ([icon][icon]) and the unmodified story-game enemy, which the BBG design "looks nothing like" [Fan-wiki] ([Pink Huggable][wpink]).
- **Silhouette & build:** a plush teddy with a head shape unlike an ordinary Huggable's [Fan-wiki] ([Pink Huggable][wpink]). No official scale chart exists; on the shared BBG News scale chart it is the shortest of the eleven, ~0.65× Oliver's height, a head below Wil [BBG News invention].
- **Colours:** the pinks are estimates sampled from the 2024 BBH portrait, not official codes [Inference]: #E34987 (main), #CF3678, #F25C94 (highlight) and #B32566 (shadow). The rest are unsampled placeholders [BBG News invention]: radio green #4B5A35, bandage #E9E1CC, metal #8C9198, button eye #C8102E.
- **Body & eyes:** "covered in tears, scars, bandages and implants", with "one small black, beady eye, while the other appears to be a button" [Fan-wiki] ([Pink Huggable][wpink]). The BBG image shows a red button-like eye, a bandaged head, a wide grin and a torn belly showing metal [Observed]. Possibly official 2012 art adds metal wristbands with blue gems and a knee brace [Observed] ([art][art2012]).
- **Gear:** a green radio with an antenna strapped to its head [Observed]. Instead of a backpack, a team-coloured banner "with three white symbols" appears in matches [Fan-wiki] ([Pink Huggable][wpink]; [class page][wbbg]); BBG News shows it red, the house default team [BBG News invention].
- **Weapons** [Fan-wiki kit list] ([class page][wbbg]): defaults Bite Me (bear-trap hands, per the wiki), Double Rainbow! and Tough Love; extras include Headlights, a head-off "cutting death beam of rainbow essence" [Game text via wiki] ([Headlights][headlights]), So Fly, Mini Me, Super Size Me!, Seesaw chainsaw hands and the Bearettas.
- **Skins** [Game text via wiki]: Black Huggable ("Black is the new black?", [Black Huggable][wblack]), Hugly Sweater and Gray Matter ("Behold the terror.", [Gray Matter][wgray]). Keep Hannible ("a past of violence", [Hannible][whannible]) out of shorts [BBG News invention].
- **Pronoun:** BBG text says "it" and BBH says "he". BBG News uses "it".

## Movement & acting

- **Posture & walk:** the fastest class [Fan-wiki] ([class page][wbbg]), with "quickness and uncanny melee strength" [Canon] ([BBR classes][bbr]). A bouncy scamper with arms half-open; its idle is a fixed-grin sway [BBG News invention].
- **Fighting:** it dives "into large groups of hapless victims" ([Double Rainbow!][wdouble]), pops its head off for [Headlights][headlights], and launches with So Fly ("Aim up fly up!", [So Fly][wsofly]) [Game text via wiki].
- **Pain & victory:** damage barely registers. It pushes escaping stuffing back in and celebrates by hugging the camera [BBG News invention].
- **Taunts** [Fan-wiki] ([Taunts][taunts]): Sloppy Swish (based on an SNL skit), Rump Shake ("wiggles its bottom") and Moonwalk ("shuffles backwards in a circle before jumping up and spinning around in the air").
- **Physical comedy beats** [BBG News invention]:
  1. It opens its arms, every bear dives for cover, and it hugs a crate, which crumples like a can.
  2. After firing Headlights, it gropes blindly for its own head and screws it on backwards.
  3. The Bearettas squirt out of its thumbless paws like soap, so it hugs them instead.

## Story engine

- **Best fit:** a 15–30 second pantomime with one line ("Hug me!"), escalating cute against lethal and cutting hard on the "Hugged to DEATH!" beat [Canon, channel unconfirmed] ([YouTube][hugged]). Hugs end in a crunch sound effect and rainbow sparkles, never injuries [BBG News invention].
- **Loglines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. **Hug Me, Over:** the Huggable's radio picks up B-1000 uploading the bears' data. It hugs the robot until the upload crashes, and B-1000 answers "Outcome unacceptable!" (a [Fan-wiki] bark, [Assault (BBG)][wassault]).
  2. **The Safer Version:** Graham runs a safety inspection on the "safer version". Every checklist item ends with him hugged flat.
  3. **Brave Little Hug:** oblivious Wil accepts a hug and walks off fine. Oliver, laughing at him, catches the Double Rainbow.

## Canon vs invention ledger

- **Canon you can state as fact:** the class "HUGGABLE: Huggable" ([Steam][steam]); "a safer version of the standard Huggable", quick and strong in melee; "Hug me!" and "deadly hugs"; the rainbow beam as "the source of the Huggable's power"; "Kills with Kindness"; BBH's "Huggy" is "he"; the Necromancer, "considered by some to be a god" of the species; H.I.P.'s full name; no credited Huggable voice. [Game text via wiki] lines are probably SkyVu's wording but unchecked against the game, so quote them with the label.
- **Fan-wiki / invented — present as fan material:** stats, kit, taunts, banner, bear traps, "improved vocabulary", Huggy = BBG Huggable, voice names, H.I.P. making Huggables, cutscene lines [Fan-wiki]; a Battle Bears-side modifier [Inference]; everything marked [BBG News invention].
