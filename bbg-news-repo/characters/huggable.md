# Huggable, the Huggable

> A stitched, bandaged, radio-wired pink teddy from the bears' enemy species, fielded as "a safer version" of a creature that kills with kindness, who says little beyond "Hug me!" and means it with lethal sincerity.

## Canon anchor

1. "The Modified Huggable is a safer version of the standard Huggable. Its quickness and uncanny melee strength makes it a serious threat up close and its unorthodox abilities make it unpredictable in combat." [Canon] ([BBR classes, 2012][bbr])
2. "Huggables seem to have extremely limited vocabularies, limited to "Hug me!", these colorful creatures are paradoxically feared for their deadly hugs." [Canon] ([characters][chars])
3. "The Huggable's ability to love will make time seem like it comes to a halt. When actually it's just your feet and you're about to die." [Game text via wiki] ([Phantom Hug][phantom])
4. "a unique detachable head that uncovers a secret rainbow beam, the source of the Huggable's power! Each Huggable comes with an official Kills with Kindness tag" [Canon] ([plush][plush])

## Soul

- **Wants:** to hug everyone, friend or foe (game text: class quote "Hug me!" [Game text via wiki], [template][tmpl]).  **Fears / flaw:** fragile (90 health [Fan-wiki]; still 90 in 2026 playtest thumbnails [Observed, fan thumbnails]) and thumbless (game text: "Good thing Huggables don't have thumbs" [Game text via wiki], [Bearettas][bearettas]).  **Contradiction that makes them funny:** pure affection that works as a weapon (canon: "paradoxically feared for their deadly hugs", [characters][chars]).
- **Core values:** love at full strength, all the time (game text [Game text via wiki]: Phantom Hug's "ability to love", [Phantom Hug][phantom]).  **What they'd never do:** hold back a hug or say a mean word [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy engine:** a near-mute physical comedian (the research notes' [Inference]). Its grin stays fixed while the bears around it panic [BBG News invention]. Body horror stays cute: "it takes it's head off and tries to kill you... to death" ([Losing Your Head][lyh]), and "Slight battle damage poses no long term threat for this huggable" ([default skin][wpink]) (both [Game text via wiki]).

## Look (for animation)

- **Default era: BBG 2013,** rendered in the BBG News feature-film 3D house style ([STYLE.md](STYLE.md)): the default "Pink Huggable" [Observed], from the wiki-hosted [BBG image][pinkimg], the 2013 Gear Up renders [LYH.jpg][lyhimg] and [ChargedRunStance.jpg][crstance], and the Huggable card in the Steam trailer [Observed], which is SkyVu's 2013 launch footage re-edited for the 2026 store page [Inference, strong]. 2026 fan playtest thumbnails still show a "PINK HUGGABLE" skin row and a pink Huggable with 90 health [Observed, fan thumbnails]. The 2026 Pink Huggable has been seen only in a low-resolution Closed Playtest #4 Gear Up frame, holding dark-green Whack-Tus clubs (about #2F422D) [Observed: [JustNubz showcase][nubz] auto-thumbnail hq3; fan source]; its cuffs, antenna and belly in 2026 are unrecorded [Open]. Alternatives are the 2024 BBH "Huggy" ([icon][icon]) and the unmodified story-game enemy, which the BBG design "looks nothing like" [Fan-wiki] ([Pink Huggable][wpink]); use them only for marked flashbacks or skin gags (see [README](README.md)).
- **Rendering:** fuzzy plush with visible stitched seams, the bandage as soft cream gauze and the button eye as glossy plastic; the radio, antenna, cuffs and bear-trap jaws as clean painted metal or plastic; the banner as cloth on a pole [BBG News invention, applying STYLE.md]. The grin stays fixed in every shot, and the bead eye, head tilt and body do the acting [BBG News invention]. (The 2D Gear Up cards vary its mouth [Observed]; shorts don't.)
- **Silhouette & build:** a small, round plush teddy with a head shape unlike an ordinary Huggable's [Fan-wiki] ([Pink Huggable][wpink]) and a tall banner pole rising above its head [Observed]. No official scale chart exists; on the shared BBG News scale chart it is the shortest of the eleven, ~0.65× Oliver's height, a head below Wil, not counting the banner [BBG News invention].
- **Colours:** in-game pinks sampled from 2013 footage, estimates rather than official codes: #FE7BAC (lit), #E24981 (mid-tone) and #A03559 (shadow) [Observed, sampled: Steam trailer 32.7 s], with a #EEA1CD highlight [Observed, sampled: LYH.jpg]. The earlier samples from the 2024 BBH portrait (#E34987 main, #CF3678, #F25C94 highlight, #B32566 shadow) stay as a cross-check [Inference]. Bite Me jaws: black, #0D0F0E to #212021 [Observed, sampled]. The [Observed, sampled] label is carried over from the dossier; the README files sampled hex colours under [Inference], so treat every sampled value here as an estimate of the same weight as the BBH cross-check, not as a firmer spec. The rest are unsampled placeholders [BBG News invention]: radio dark olive-green #4B5A35, bandage cream #E9E1CC, button eye, cuff lights and antenna ball #C8102E, antenna grey #8C9198, cuff dark grey #3A3D42, stuffing white #F2F0EA and grey #A7A9AC.
- **Face:** a glossy red button eye with a stitched scar running up to the cream head bandage, a small black bead eye and a huge white grin [Observed]. The wiki: "one small black, beady eye, while the other appears to be a button" [Fan-wiki] ([Pink Huggable][wpink]). Which side the button eye sits on is not recorded [Open].
- **Body:** "covered in tears, scars, bandages and implants" [Fan-wiki] ([Pink Huggable][wpink]). A zip-stitched chest scar and a torn belly showing grey-and-white crumpled stuffing [Observed: LYH.jpg, the Gear Up cards, Steam trailer 32.7 s]. The wiki calls it a "metalic jagged wound" with "what appears to be stuffing bulging out", so "metal" is [Fan-wiki], not [Observed].
- **Head and radio:** one side of its head is heavily bandaged [Fan-wiki; Observed]. The bandage holds a boxy green radio with a thin grey antenna tipped with a red ball (2013 renders and the Steam trailer's 2013 footage); 2012–13 in-game fan footage and the 2D cards show a blue tip [Observed]. Skins vary: the red Seesaw render has a green tip [Observed]. BBG News uses the red tip and paints the radio dark olive-green, to match its placeholder hex [BBG News invention].
- **Wrists:** for the 3D BBG look, dark grey wrist cuffs, each set with a round glossy red light, and no knee brace [Observed: LYH.jpg and ChargedRunStance.jpg, uploaded 2013-12-21; Steam trailer 32.6–32.7 s]. Possibly official 2012 art adds metal wristbands with blue gems and a knee brace [Observed] ([art][art2012]); these are 2D-art only (they also appear on the Gear Up cards, the knee brace on the Headlights card) [Observed]. The 3D Pink model wears dark grey cuffs with red lights (or the melee weapon's mount), and the orange skin in-game wears blue bands [Observed]. On the 2013 melee renders the weapon's mount covers the wrist [Observed], so cuffs and jaws never share a wrist in a BBG News shot: the main model sheet shows the jaws on and one bare cuffed paw as an inset, and a second bare-paw sheet carries the cuffs alone [BBG News invention].
- **Team banner:** instead of a backpack, a team-coloured banner "with three white symbols" appears in matches [Fan-wiki] ([Pink Huggable][wpink]; [class page][wbbg]). In footage it is a tall, narrow red team banner on a pole rising above its head, three white symbols stacked vertically [Observed: Steam trailer 3.0–3.9 s; [Dancing_Huggable.jpg][dancing], 2013], not "lined up horizontally" as the wiki says. Blue-team Huggables carry blue banners, with varying markings (orange shapes, a teal swirl) [Observed]; some Huggables in the trailer's 2013 footage carry a white flag with rainbow dots [Observed], perhaps a skin flag [Open]. BBG News shows it red, the house default team ([README](README.md)), with the pole strapped to its back [Inference from the wiki's "instead of a backpack"]. The symbols' exact shapes are unrecorded, so BBG News draws them as three plain white geometric shapes, never letters or logos [BBG News invention].
- **Default melee on the paws:** two black bear-trap jaws (open C-shaped loops on round black wrist discs) clamped over both paws, zigzag teeth smeared with iridescent rainbow, each about 0.28× Oliver's height [Observed; size Inference]. In the trailer's 2013 footage melee weapons show only during swings, and idle arms show bare cuffs [Observed; as a rule, Inference]. BBG News keeps the jaws on in model sheets and most shots so the silhouette never drifts, and uses the bare-paw sheet for Phantom Hug, Headlights and other weapon-free shots [BBG News invention].
- **Skins** [Game text via wiki]: Black Huggable ("Black is the new black?", [Black Huggable][wblack]), Hugly Sweater and Gray Matter ("Behold the terror.", [Gray Matter][wgray]). **Gummable:** a glossy red (#FC0025) or blue (#00AFEC) gummy-bear body with a pink-and-lime "CANDY!" gum box in place of the radio [Observed; Fan-wiki: "a box of gum in the place of a walkie-talkie on its head"] ([Gummable][wgum]). Keep Hannible ("a past of violence", [Hannible][whannible]) out of shorts [BBG News invention]. - **Pronoun:** BBG text says "it" [Game text via wiki] and BBH says "he" [Canon] ([characters][chars]). BBG News uses "it".

### Weapons

All 18 Huggable weapons are abilities that come out of its own body, or paired melees worn over both paws; it fires no guns [Fan-wiki; Observed]. Hex values are sampled estimates, and sizes "× Oliver" scale Gear Up proportions by the 0.65× chart height [Inference]. The Steam trailer cited below is SkyVu's 2013 launch footage [Inference, strong]. SkyVu has announced no weapon changes for 2026 [Inference].

- **Full kit (18)** [Fan-wiki] ([class page][wbbg]):
  - Primaries: Double Rainbow! (default; two flat six-stripe rainbow ribbons, red outside to violet inside, orbiting at hip height [Observed]), Headlights (a head-off "cutting death beam of rainbow essence" [Game text via wiki] ([Headlights][headlights]); head removal while firing is also seen in BBR footage [Observed]), So Fly, Static Shuffle (magenta static, a short cyan-white zap, white floor sparks that linger [Observed]; that the magenta crackle is its charge is [Inference]), Losing Your Head.
  - Secondaries: Tough Love (default), Mini Me, Peek A Boo, Super Size Me! (BBG 2.20, May 2015: the Huggable grows giant, slowed under a yellow "Z", and its stomps send out a flat glowing yellow-orange ground ring [Fan-wiki; Observed: official Loud & Proud bundle art and a 2.25 fan screenshot]), Phantom Hug (no gadget: an open paw throws a glowing pink ghost-Huggable; frozen foes get a yellow Z with two white orbiting arrows and a pink ring around the waist [Observed, 2013 fan footage]), Charged Run.
  - Melees: Bite Me (default; black bear-trap jaws with rainbow-smeared teeth [Observed]), Seesaw chainsaw hands, Whack-Tus, Presidentures, Punk Bands, Bearettas, Beaver Feaver.
  - The seven Soldier weapons some lists file under the Huggable come from a pasted Soldier changelog and are not its weapons [Fan-wiki].
- **In 2026 sources:** the Closed Playtest #4 Gear Up menu ([JustNubz showcase][nubz], Sep 20, 2026) names 15 of the 18 on screen; Losing Your Head, Phantom Hug and Charged Run were not seen in the sampled frames, and absence there is not proof of removal [Observed, low-res storyboard frames; Inference]. Two are seen on a Huggable: the Seesaw on a red Huggable on SkyVu's Jul 6 "CLOSED TESTING" Steam banner [Observed, match to wiki images], and Whack-Tus cactus clubs (about #2F422D) on a Pink Huggable in a Playtest #4 Gear Up frame [Observed, fan thumbnail]. How any of them looks in a 2026 match is unseen [Open].

- **Double Rainbow!** (primary; confidence: partial)

The orbit spans about 2.4× its height in the trailer's Graham segment [Observed, measured] (caption it "classic 2013 look"; whether that segment was captured in 2013 or in 2018 or later is [Open], see graham.md), and up to about 3× in top-down fan frames [Inference]; that is about 1.5–2× Oliver [Inference]. The neon-green rims are on the Gear Up card only [Observed]; the in-game curve is built from straight segments [Observed], which BBG News smooths into a clean ribbon [BBG News invention].
  - *Fires:* one tap, centred on itself: "two spinning rainbows that rotate around the user" for about 4 s, shrinking to nothing at the end; melee cancels it; no projectile [Fan-wiki].
  - *Hits:* continuous explosive-type damage to anyone in range [Fan-wiki; continuous reading Inference]. The contact effect is unseen [Open]. In one fan frame a brown bear killed inside the arcs lies intact and face-down beside a small yellow-white sparkle squiggle on the ribbon [Observed]; the killing weapon in that frame is unverified [Open].

- **Bite Me** (melee, paired; confidence: observed)

**Signature weapons for shorts:**

- **Headlights** (primary beam, "the Huggable's only ranged attack" [Game text via wiki]; confidence: partial)
  - *Look:* bowed forward, its head pulled off and set low in front, a white sparkle burst at the neck and a straight, head-thick beam (about 0.2× Oliver) out of the neck hole, banded violet on top through magenta, red, yellow, green and cyan to blue at the bottom [Observed: Gear Up card; size Inference]. In BBR footage the head is gone while it fires and the beam reads warm white with pink-orange edges [Observed, low-res]. The plush's "secret rainbow beam" is [Canon].
  - *Fires:* one tap, then "a long, continuous rainbow beam lasting a few seconds" that must be kept on target, range 1250 units; no melee while firing [Fan-wiki].
  - *Hits:* sustained damage while on target [Fan-wiki]; a yellow-white starburst (#FFF298) at the beam's end [Observed, BBR footage].
- **So Fly** (primary movement attack; BBH Huggy's Ultimate [Canon]; confidence: partial)
  - *Look:* a head-first Superman flight trailing a wavy seven-band rainbow (violet, magenta, red, yellow, green, cyan, blue) with white sparkles at its feet [Observed: Gear Up card]. In the trailer's 2013 footage it rises leaving a broad translucent ribbon (red-orange, yellow, green), then flies belly-down with bare cuffs [Observed; So Fly by Inference].
  - *Fires:* one tap; it flies "forward a short distance in a stream of rainbows", steerable in any direction ("Aim up fly up!" [Game text via wiki]), about 3 s at 575 units/s [Fan-wiki].
  - *Hits:* contact damage, 60 to 80 by proximity [Fan-wiki]; no hit effect seen [Open]. Never add a magenta crystal pillar: that is Tillman's Happy Hour [Inference].
- **Phantom Hug** (secondary saboteur; confidence: observed, low-res 2013 fan footage; not seen in the 2026 menu frames [Observed absence])
  - *Look:* no launcher and no launch pose distinct from its general idle: it fires with one empty paw raised [Observed, low-res]. The shot is a small pale pink-white Huggable-shaped ghost trailed by two or three hollow pink rings (#F87B99 to #F99AF6), a white-hot silhouette with pink outlines seen head-on [Observed]. SkyVu's promo art shows a translucent glowing pink ghost with a yellow-white rim wrapped over a screaming bear's face [Observed; official by Inference].
  - *Fires:* a single shot, reload 5 s: "a cluster of pink energy in the shape of a Huggable" [Fan-wiki].
  - *Hits:* a soft pink-white cloud flares on the target [Observed], which cannot move or turn for about 2.5 s but can still attack, jump and use Specials; the hold itself deals no damage [Fan-wiki]. The target shows a yellow block "Z" (#FDFC04) between two white crescent arrows, a glowing pink ring around its waist and pale pink ghost arms [Observed]. Held targets can still be meleed, and a few violet rings drift off afterwards [Observed; as residue, Inference].
- **Losing Your Head** (primary explosive; confidence: partial; not seen in the 2026 menu frames [Observed absence])
  - *Look:* no model; the head is the bomb [Fan-wiki]. Its text is in the Soul section ([Losing Your Head][lyh]).
  - *Fires:* one tap: it "will shake off its head, which will land at its feet and explode almost instantly", even in mid-air [Fan-wiki].
  - *Hits:* "a bright blue blast", "large and spherical", that envelops the Huggable without hurting it [Fan-wiki]; three fan clips titled for it show the screen flooded cyan (#2FFDF9 to #78FDFC) with a curved sphere edge [Observed; attribution Inference, strong]. ## Movement & acting

- **Posture & walk:** the fastest class ("the highest base speed of any class" [Fan-wiki], [class page][wbbg]), with "quickness and uncanny melee strength" [Canon] ([BBR classes][bbr]). A quick, bouncy scamper with arms half-open, a squash on every footfall and the banner whipping behind; its idle is a fixed-grin sway [BBG News invention]. In 2013–14 fan footage it usually stands with one arm raised [Observed; that this is its general idle is Inference].
- **Timing** [BBG News invention, per STYLE.md]: anticipation, snap, hold. A tiny crouch before every launch, a one-beat hold on the arms-wide "Hug me!" pose before the lunge, a frozen-grin hold after each hit and a dead stop at the end of each scamper. Nothing floats or drifts: even So Fly is a fast streak between two held poses, and Double Rainbow! is a held T pose with the ribbons doing the moving.
- **Fighting:** it dives "into large groups of hapless victims" ([Double Rainbow!][wdouble]), pops its head off for [Headlights][headlights], and launches with So Fly ("Aim up fly up!", [So Fly][wsofly]) [Game text via wiki]. Key poses: arms out in a T for Double Rainbow! [Observed: Steam trailer 7.2–8.5 s]; bowed forward with the head set down in front for Headlights, and head-first Superman for So Fly [Observed: Gear Up cards]; for Phantom Hug, one empty paw raised, the same as its general idle [Observed, low-res fan footage], which BBG News flings out on the shot [BBG News invention]; a two-armed clap for the paired melees [Fan-wiki].
- **Pain & victory:** damage barely registers. It pushes escaping stuffing back in and celebrates by hugging the camera [BBG News invention]. If a short knocks it out, pick one look and keep it for the whole short:
  - **SkyVu's merch look:** the head pops off, white stuffing puffs from the neck and a straight vertical rainbow column shoots up [Canon, 2023 "Huggable Rainbow Explosion" merch art, not gameplay] ([merch][merch]).
  - **The game's two death looks** [Observed]: an intact, floppy face-down collapse (a yellow Huggable at 0/80 in a fan death-camera frame, [hqQgvnMIj4E maxres2][hqq2]), or a pop into a few clean plush pieces with gold sparkles, seen clearly once, on an irradiated orange Huggable (Steam trailer 9.0 s, inside the Graham segment whose capture date, 2013 or 2018 and later, is [Open] (see graham.md); caption it "classic 2013 look"; radiation as the cause is [Inference]). What decides intact versus pieces is unknown [Open]. BBG News keeps the pieces for explosion and radiation KOs, following the research's [Inference] that those break bodies apart, and uses the collapse for everything else [BBG News invention].
  - **The README's rainbow confetti,** as a labelled gag only: confetti is [BBG News invention]; no source shows it, so never caption it as the game's look.
  - Never a rainbow "blood" spray.
- **Taunts** [Fan-wiki] ([Taunts][taunts]): Sloppy Swish (based on an SNL skit), Rump Shake ("wiggles its bottom") and Moonwalk ("shuffles backwards in a circle before jumping up and spinning around in the air").
- **Physical comedy beats** [BBG News invention]:
  1. It opens its arms, every bear dives for cover, and it hugs a crate, which crumples like a can.
  2. After firing Headlights, it gropes blindly for its own head and screws it on backwards.
  3. It aims the strapped-on Bearettas, has no thumbs to pull the triggers, nothing fires, so it clubs with the spiked guards instead. (The strapped-on pistol gauntlets are [Observed]; "Good thing Huggables don't have thumbs" is [Game text via wiki] ([Bearettas][bearettas]).)

## Story engine

- **Best fit:** a 15–30 second pantomime with one line ("Hug me!"), escalating cute against lethal and cutting hard on the "Hugged to DEATH!" beat [Canon, channel unconfirmed] ([YouTube][hugged]). Hugs end in a crunch sound effect and rainbow sparkles, never injuries [BBG News invention]. A bear it KOs flops intact and face-down, as BBG's ragdoll deaths do, after a white flash and yellow stars [Observed]; three blue lightning-bolt Joule tokens can pop out where it falls [Observed; as a KO marker, Inference].
- **Loglines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. **Hug Me, Over:** the Huggable's radio picks up B-1000 uploading the bears' data. It hugs the robot until the upload crashes, and B-1000 answers "Outcome unacceptable!" (a [Fan-wiki] bark, [Assault (BBG)][wassault]).
  2. **The Safer Version:** Graham runs a safety inspection on the "safer version". Every checklist item ends with him hugged flat.
  3. **Brave Little Hug:** oblivious Wil accepts a hug and walks off fine. Oliver, laughing at him, gets clipped by the whirling hip-high rainbow ribbons and flops face-down.
