# Huggable, the Huggable

> A stitched, bandaged, radio-wired pink teddy from the bears' enemy species, fielded as "a safer version" of a creature that kills with kindness, who says little beyond "Hug me!" and means it with lethal sincerity.

## Canon anchor

1. "The Modified Huggable is a safer version of the standard Huggable. Its quickness and uncanny melee strength makes it a serious threat up close and its unorthodox abilities make it unpredictable in combat." [Canon] ([BBR classes, 2012][bbr])
2. "Huggables seem to have extremely limited vocabularies, limited to "Hug me!", these colorful creatures are paradoxically feared for their deadly hugs." [Canon] ([characters][chars])
3. "The Huggable's ability to love will make time seem like it comes to a halt. When actually it's just your feet and you're about to die." [Game text via wiki] ([Phantom Hug][phantom])
4. "a unique detachable head that uncovers a secret rainbow beam, the source of the Huggable's power! Each Huggable comes with an official Kills with Kindness tag" [Canon] ([plush][plush])

## Soul

- **Wants:** to hug everyone, friend or foe (game text: class quote "Hug me!" [Game text via wiki], [template][tmpl]).  **Fears / flaw:** fragile (90 health [Fan-wiki]; still 90 in 2026 playtest thumbnails [Observed, fan thumbnails]) and thumbless (game text: "Good thing Huggables don't have thumbs" [Game text via wiki], [Bearettas][bearettas]).  **Contradiction that makes them funny:** pure affection that works as a weapon (canon: "paradoxically feared for their deadly hugs", [characters][chars]).
- **Core values:** love at full strength, all the time (game text [Game text via wiki]: Phantom Hug's "ability to love", [Phantom Hug][phantom]).  **What they'd never do:** hold back a hug or say a mean word [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy engine:** a near-mute physical comedian (the research notes' [Inference]). Its grin stays fixed while the bears around it panic [BBG News invention]. Body horror stays cute: "it takes it's head off and tries to kill you... to death" ([Losing Your Head][lyh]), and "Slight battle damage poses no long term threat for this huggable" ([default skin][wpink]) (both [Game text via wiki]).

## Voice

- **Delivery:** official text gives it only "Hug me!", and no BBG lines are transcribed, though the wiki says it "has improved its vocabulary in Battle Bears Gold, possibly due to the radio attached to its head" [Fan-wiki] ([class page][wbbg]). BBG News plays it as a small, sing-song plush-toy voice, sweet to the point of eerie, with extra words crackling through the head radio [BBG News invention]. Cadence [BBG News invention]: "Hug me!" at most twice per short (new line 1's run of three is the one exception), first bright and sing-song on the setup hold (two quick beats, pitch rising), then the flat, low "hug me" right before the lunge; giggles fill the pauses, and any other line comes through a crackle of radio static. On screen its mouth never moves (see Rendering); the line is dubbed in the edit. Check the [fan audio rip][rip] by ear for what it actually says before writing more lines; take words from it, never the performance.
- **Swearing without swearing:** it never swears. Frustration comes out as a burst of radio static or a bleep [BBG News invention], after SkyVu's "WARNING! FCC Censorship!" gag [Canon] ([YouTube][fcc]).

### Weapons

All 18 Huggable weapons are abilities that come out of its own body, or paired melees worn over both paws; it fires no guns [Fan-wiki; Observed]. Hex values are sampled estimates, and sizes "× Oliver" scale Gear Up proportions by the 0.65× chart height [Inference]. The Steam trailer SkyVu put on the 2026 Steam page and all five Steam screenshots are 2013 footage SkyVu re-used [Inference, strong]: the trailer's class cards are pixel-identical to SkyVu's May 30, 2013 trailer, and its gameplay matches that trailer's 160×90 storyboard (45 of 55 frames, the Graham segment and the Space Oddity clip included) [Observed]. The opening Gold Digger shot (about 1.49–3.85 s, with the red banner and the 3.27–3.85 s rainbow burst) is in that storyboard too, at 2013 t=6–7 s, framed a little tighter [Observed, storyboard size: weapons shared.addendum.md], though its two samples miss the MSE match threshold in the 2026 dossier's frame table while scoring best at the trailer's offset [Observed, storyboard size]. So use them as a 2013 look reference only: call them "the Steam trailer" or "Steam screenshot screen_0N", caption them "classic 2013 look" where the era matters, never "2026 gameplay", and never rest a 2026-build claim on them alone [Inference]. That includes the Space Oddity clip (about 30.0–32.8 s; Steam screenshot screen_01 is a frame of it), which holds the flight read as So Fly [Inference], the first Punk Bands swing and kill, and the 32.6–32.7 s samples: the same moment, bloom included, is in the 2013 storyboard [Observed], so it is 2013 footage too [Inference, strong]; only why its pillar top blooms is [Open] (see [README](README.md)). ## Movement & acting

- **Posture & walk:** the fastest class ("the highest base speed of any class" [Fan-wiki], [class page][wbbg]), with "quickness and uncanny melee strength" [Canon] ([BBR classes][bbr]). A quick, bouncy scamper with arms half-open, a squash on every footfall and the banner whipping behind [BBG News invention]. Idle: one paw raised beside its head, with a small fixed-grin sway on top. In 2013–14 fan footage it usually stands with one arm raised [Observed; that this is its general idle is Inference], and ChargedRunStance.jpg raises the left paw [Observed, editor's reading]; BBG News always raises the left paw and adds the sway [BBG News invention]. Phantom Hug fires from that same raised paw.
- **Timing** [BBG News invention, per STYLE.md]: anticipation, snap, hold. A tiny crouch before every launch, a one-beat hold on the arms-wide "Hug me!" pose before the lunge, a frozen-grin hold after each hit and a dead stop at the end of each scamper. Nothing floats or drifts: even So Fly is a fast streak between two held poses, and Double Rainbow! is a held T pose with the ribbons doing the moving. Keep the two poses apart: the hug pose leans in on its toes, arms forward and wide, elbows soft; the Double Rainbow! T pose is upright and stiff, arms locked straight out to the sides [T pose Observed; both definitions BBG News invention].
- **Fighting:** it dives "into large groups of hapless victims" ([Double Rainbow!][wdouble]), pops its head off for [Headlights][headlights], and launches with So Fly ("Aim up fly up!", [So Fly][wsofly]) [Game text via wiki].
