# Botch, the Assassin

> Botch is a radioactive bear of very few words with a past nobody explains, who treats destruction as something pretty and shrugs off everything else.

## Canon anchor

1. "Botch, a bear of very few words with a mysterious past, boasts powerful radioactive powers. Known for his fearsome ranged abilities alongside swift movement speed, he's a formidable foe. Yet, his significant weakness lies in his low health." [Canon] ([characters][chars])
2. "Botch is what you call a glass cannon. He has some crazy damage output with poison and his speed, but his weakness however is his lack of life. Dipping in and out of battle is Botch's strength." Class quote: "Ahh, destruction!" [Game text via wiki] ([class template][tmpl])
3. "Sometimes when a person gets lonely they talk to themselves. Botch, however, created two friends that rapidly fire deadly lasers... friends are the best!" [Game text via wiki] ([Orbs][orbs])
4. "Botch tried to look like a normal bear once. He found it awful, but doesn't he look cute?" [Game text via wiki] ([Brown Botch][brown])

## Soul

- **Wants:** to watch things blow up ("Ahh, destruction!") and, quietly, company: he "created two friends" (canon [Game text via wiki]: template, Orbs).  **Fears / flaw:** a glass cannon, "clumsy and nonchalant… can't be bothered", whose Pain Field "increases damage on enemies and teammates alike" (canon [Game text via wiki]: [template][tmpl], [Cosmic Bomb][cbomb], [Pain Field][pain]); he dreads questions about his past [BBG News invention].  **Contradiction that makes them funny:** "Botch is always serious", yet his Doom Cloud is "adorable, huh?" and kills get "Pretty!" (canon [Game text via wiki]: [Ball N Pain][ball], [Doom Cloud][dcloud]; bark [Fan-wiki]).
- **Core values:** pretty explosions (the "Pretty!" bark [Fan-wiki]), his Orbs, orange (his "favorite color and fruit") and cool indifference ("Whatever!") (canon [Game text via wiki]: [Orbs][orbs], [Orange Botch][orange], [Emo][emo]); ranking these as values is [BBG News invention].  **What they'd never do:** explain himself in a full sentence, or try to look "normal" again (canon: "a bear of very few words" [Canon], [characters][chars]; Brown Botch [Game text via wiki], [Brown Botch][brown]); the framing is [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy engine:** the narrator talks about Botch; Botch answers in one word. The item text already does this: "We chose purple, he was not amused" (canon [Game text via wiki]: [Purple Botch][purple]). He is the eerie calm at the centre of the chaos, and his one word is the punchline [BBG News invention].

## Voice

- **Delivery:** "only talks in grainy one-or-two word quotes" [Fan-wiki] ([Assassin (BBG)][assassin]); "tiny, childlike monosyllables with a creepy-delighted tone" [Inference; audio unchecked]. BBG News spec: soft, small and breathy, with static grit; a pause before each word; happy words lift, shrugs drop flat [BBG News invention].
- **Swearing without swearing:** a Geiger-counter crackle over the word, or just "Eh." [BBG News invention], echoing SkyVu's "WARNING! FCC Censorship!" gag [Canon] ([YouTube][fcc]).
- **Known lines** [Fan-wiki; verify by ear against the [Slinkster rip][rip]] ([Assassin (BBG)][assassin]): "Pretty!" / "Delightful!" / "Wee!" / "Sleep!" / "It's time!" / "Cozy!" / "Finally..." / "Eh. Whatever."
- **New lines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. "Ooh. Glowy."
  2. "Normal? Ew."
  3. "Orange. Mine."
  4. "Boom... later."
  5. "Past? ...Pass."
- **Casting:** no voice is credited for Botch; the BBR credits predate him ([credits][credits]). Cast an original voice, human or AI: small, dry, slightly raspy and ageless, with a childlike lift on happy words and light radio grain in post. Never clone or imitate a real person's voice.

- **Pish & Posh:** "friends with Pish and Posh" is an unsourced 2020 wiki stub [Fan-wiki, Unverified] ([Botch][stub]; [history][hist]); SkyVu calls them "part of the Battle Cubs team in the movie screenplay" [Canon] ([YouTube][pish]), not BBG classes.
- **Jackson / Captain Malcolm:** no link in any source.

## Look (for animation)

- **Default era: BBG 2013,** in the shared BBG News house style (see [README](README.md)): the in-game default skin ([screenshot][shot]) [Observed]. He has no splash card in the 2026 Steam trailer [Observed] and postdates the 2013 trailer's eight launch classes [Canon]; no official model sheet was found. Alternative: the 2024 BBH portrait, charcoal on a purple swirl ([icon][icon]) [Observed]. The 2026 build is unviewed.
- **Silhouette:** a compact body under an "abnormally large head"; round teddy ears, one with "a small chunk missing" [Fan-wiki] ([skin][skin]), a notch also seen on the icon [Observed]. He hovers [Fan-wiki]. No official scale; on the shared BBG News scale chart his head-top is level with Oliver's (1.0×) while hovering, with a slighter body, much smaller than B-1000 [BBG News invention].
- **Colours** (estimates from observed official art, not official codes): BBG fur reads "dark plum/maroon-black" [Observed]; the wiki says "very dark maroon" and, elsewhere, "navy" [Fan-wiki]. BBH portrait samples: fur #343746 / #292D34, eyes #CAF946 [Inference]. Unsampled estimates: plum-black fur #2D1F2A, muzzle #8C8A90, arm guards silver #B9BEC4 and red #B3262E, toxic effects #6CF542 [BBG News invention].
- **Face & gear:** huge glowing lime-green eyes without pupils, grey muzzle, small dark nose, flat line mouth, a blue-lit band on the arm or wrist [Observed: icon]; "small silver and red arm guards" and "a tiny silver backpack with circular red markings" [Fan-wiki] ([skin][skin]); green flame in one hand [Observed: screenshot].
- **Weapons:** defaults Doom Cloud (lasers "create a cloud of doom" [Game text via wiki] ([Doom Cloud][dcloud])), Pain Field and Crass Knuckles; extras include Orbs, Fear Orbit, Cosmic Bomb, Gravity Well and Toxic Plague [Fan-wiki] ([Assassin (BBG)][assassin]). BBH adds the boomerang "Doom Star" [Canon] ([characters][chars]).
- **Skins:** Emo, Dry Bones, Plague Doctor, Jester, Orca Treat and colourways [Fan-wiki]; new Halloween and Christmas skins in 2013 [Canon] ([2.4][n24]; [2.6][n26]). Skip Green Justice, a Green Lantern parody [BBG News invention].

## Movement & acting

- **Travel:** he "moves while slightly levitated from the ground" and glides down with "green particle-like effects" [Fan-wiki] ([Assassin (BBG)][assassin]). Feet dangle, the heavy head leads, no footsteps [BBG News invention].
- **Idle:** a hover-bob with the Fear Orbit's "orbs of poison spinning around" him [Game text via wiki] ([Fear Orbit][fear]); using it as his idle, and the twitching notched ear, are [BBG News invention].
- **Fighting:** "Dipping in and out of battle" [Game text via wiki] ([template][tmpl]); fast and ranged [Canon]; spare energy goes "to the sky and it'll come down later" [Game text via wiki] ([Cosmic Bomb][cbomb]).
- **Pain & victory:** "Oof!"; deaths "Aaaaah!", "Cozy!", "Finally..."; victory "Ahh, destruction!" [Fan-wiki]. He fizzles green and sparks, never bleeds [BBG News invention].
- **Taunts** [Fan-wiki] ([Taunts][taunts]): Almighty (curls up "in the foetal position while levitating gradually higher", then throws his arms up), Head Spin (his head "spins for two whole rounds" until he clutches it), Air Guitar.
- **Comedy beats** [BBG News invention]: (1) a Head Spin double take stops off-centre, so he nudges his face straight; (2) he hovers in a sitting pose an inch above the couch: "Cozy!"; (3) a forgotten Cosmic Bomb lands at the worst moment.

## Story engine

- **Best fit:** a 15–30 s near-silent gag in an arena or training bout, which contradicts nothing official [Inference]. Captions mock him in the item-text narrator's voice, and his one word ends it. Never explain his origin or his ear [BBG News invention].
- **Loglines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. **Friends Are the Best:** Botch throws a birthday party for his two laser Orbs, and Wil, the only guest, dodges "friendly" fire all the way to the cake.
  2. **Normal Bear:** Saberi gives Botch a "normal bear" makeover for the team photo. He hates the brown fur, and his glow ruins the picture anyway.
  3. **Boom... Later:** Botch lobs spare energy skyward before a training bout and forgets it. It comes down in a soot-only cartoon blast on Oliver's victory pose.
