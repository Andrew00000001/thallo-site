# Botch, the Assassin

> Botch is a radioactive bear of very few words with a past nobody explains, who treats destruction as something pretty and shrugs off everything else.

## Canon anchor

1. "Botch, a bear of very few words with a mysterious past, boasts powerful radioactive powers. Known for his fearsome ranged abilities alongside swift movement speed, he's a formidable foe. Yet, his significant weakness lies in his low health." [Canon] ([characters][chars])
2. "Botch is what you call a glass cannon. He has some crazy damage output with poison and his speed, but his weakness however is his lack of life. Dipping in and out of battle is Botch's strength." Class quote: "Ahh, destruction!" [Game text via wiki] ([class template][tmpl])
3. "Sometimes when a person gets lonely they talk to themselves. Botch, however, created two friends that rapidly fire deadly lasers... friends are the best!" [Game text via wiki] ([Orbs][orbs]) [Observed: in-game 2.7 store popup, Jan 2014, [store frame][store27]]
4. "Botch tried to look like a normal bear once. He found it awful, but doesn't he look cute?" [Game text via wiki] ([Brown Botch][brown])

## Soul

- **Wants:** to watch things blow up ("Ahh, destruction!") and, quietly, company: he "created two friends" (game text [Game text via wiki]: template, Orbs).  **Fears / flaw:** a glass cannon, "clumsy and nonchalant… can't be bothered", whose Pain Field "increases damage on enemies and teammates alike" (game text [Game text via wiki]: [template][tmpl], [Cosmic Bomb][cbomb], [Pain Field][pain]); he dreads questions about his past [BBG News invention].  **Contradiction that makes them funny:** "Botch is always serious", yet his Doom Cloud is "adorable, huh?" and kills get "Pretty!" (game text [Game text via wiki]: [Ball N Pain][ball], [Doom Cloud][dcloud]; bark [Fan-wiki]).
- **Core values:** pretty explosions (the "Pretty!" bark [Fan-wiki]), his Orbs, orange (his "favorite color and fruit") and cool indifference ("Whatever!") (game text [Game text via wiki]: [Orbs][orbs], [Orange Botch][orange], [Emo][emo]); ranking these as values is [BBG News invention].  **What they'd never do:** explain himself in a full sentence, or try to look "normal" again ("a bear of very few words" [Canon], [characters][chars]; Brown Botch [Game text via wiki], [Brown Botch][brown]); the framing is [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy engine:** the narrator talks about Botch; Botch answers in one word. The item text already does this: "We chose purple, he was not amused" (game text [Game text via wiki]: [Purple Botch][purple]). He is the eerie calm at the centre of the chaos, and his one word is the punchline [BBG News invention].

## Voice

- **Delivery:** "only talks in grainy one-or-two word quotes" [Fan-wiki] ([Assassin (BBG)][assassin]); "tiny, childlike monosyllables with a creepy-delighted tone" [Inference; audio unchecked]. BBG News spec: small, soft and slightly raspy, with light static grit; a pause before each word; a breathy lift on happy words, dry and flat on shrugs [BBG News invention].
- **Swearing without swearing:** a Geiger-counter crackle over the word, or just "Eh." [BBG News invention], echoing SkyVu's "WARNING! FCC Censorship!" gag [Canon] ([YouTube][fcc]).
- **Known lines** [Fan-wiki; verify by ear against the [Slinkster rip][rip]] ([Assassin (BBG)][assassin]): "Pretty!" / "Delightful!" / "Wee!" / "Sleep!" / "It's time!" / "Cozy!" / "Finally..." / "Eh. Whatever."

  2. "Normal? Ew."
  3. "Orange. Mine."
  4. "Boom... later."

- **Casting:** no voice is credited for Botch; the BBR credits predate him ([credits][credits]). Cast an original voice, human or AI, to the Delivery spec above: small, soft, slightly raspy and ageless, with a childlike lift on happy words and light radio grain in post. Never clone or imitate a real person's voice.

### Weapons

Almost all his weapons are floating, translucent, glowing lime energy constructs that hover off one paw and are never gripped [Observed]. Two are held: the Cosmic Bomb charge, at his chest in both paws, and the Tonguechuks, a physical stick in his paw [Observed]. The Cosmic Fire flame sits cupped in his upturned palm without being gripped [Observed], so "gripped energy weapons" does not cover it, which is why the negative also carries "flame in his paw". The research records no side for any one-sided construct [Open]: keep each on the side its first approved take shows (for the Doom Cloud, the approved model sheet), the same in every shot of a short [BBG News invention]. Sizes are in Botch-heads (≈ 0.45× Oliver each). ## Movement & acting

- **Timing** [BBG News invention]: he moves in snaps and holds. A quick glide, a dead-still hover, one small gesture, a held pose. Squash and stretch lives in the big head, the ears and the Doom Cloud; his body stays compact. Even though he levitates, nothing drifts: the hover-bob is small and steady, and every move lands on a clear hold. Holds run 12–16 frames at 24 fps, and a punchline hold runs 24 frames or more. His signature hold is a deadpan palms-up shrug, used on "Eh. Whatever." and as the end-card pose.
- **Travel:** he "moves while slightly levitated from the ground" and glides down with "green particle-like effects" [Fan-wiki] ([Assassin (BBG)][assassin]). Feet dangle, the heavy head leads, no footsteps; each stop gets a tiny overshoot and a hold [BBG News invention].
- **Idle:** a hover-bob with his default Doom Cloud bobbing at his shoulder, as in the 2.1 and 2026 menus [Observed]; the twitching notched ear is [BBG News invention]. Alternative idle: the Fear Orbit's "orbs of poison spinning around" him [Game text via wiki] ([Fear Orbit][fear]), drawn as two lime lattice spheres drifting passively; they "orbit the Assassin violently" only when activated [Fan-wiki]. Using it as an idle is [BBG News invention].
- **Weapon acting:** his weapons float beside him and are never gripped [Observed], so he gestures (a flick, a raised paw, a palm thrust) and the construct does the work [BBG News invention]. Exceptions: the Cosmic Bomb charge is held at his chest in both paws, and the Tonguechuks is a physical stick in his paw [Observed].
- **Fighting:** "Dipping in and out of battle" [Game text via wiki] ([template][tmpl]); fast and ranged [Canon]; spare energy goes "to the sky and it'll come down later" [Game text via wiki] ([Cosmic Bomb][cbomb]). Staging: snap in, one clean strike pose, a two-beat hold, snap out [BBG News invention].
- **Pain & victory:** "Oof!"; deaths "Aaaaah!", "Cozy!", "Finally..."; victory "Ahh, destruction!"; defeat "Eh. Whatever." [Fan-wiki]. He fizzles green and sparks, never bleeds [BBG News invention].
- **Speaking shots** [BBG News invention]: the flat line mouth parts only slightly; the read comes from the eye glow and a head tilt. Sync dialogue in the edit, and keep "open mouth, teeth" in the negative unless a co-star who grins or talks is in frame (Negative prompt, Shots with other characters); then check his mouth in every take by eye.
- **Taunts** [Fan-wiki] ([Taunts][taunts]): Almighty (curls up "in the foetal position while levitating gradually higher", then throws his arms up), Head Spin (his head "spins for two whole rounds" until he clutches it), Air Guitar. Each taunt ends on a held pose [BBG News invention].
