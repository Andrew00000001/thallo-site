# Botch, the Assassin

> Botch is a radioactive bear of very few words with a past nobody explains, who treats destruction as something pretty and shrugs off everything else.

## Canon anchor

1. "Botch, a bear of very few words with a mysterious past, boasts powerful radioactive powers. Known for his fearsome ranged abilities alongside swift movement speed, he's a formidable foe. Yet, his significant weakness lies in his low health." [Canon] ([characters][chars])
2. "Botch is what you call a glass cannon. He has some crazy damage output with poison and his speed, but his weakness however is his lack of life. Dipping in and out of battle is Botch's strength." Class quote: "Ahh, destruction!" [Game text via wiki] ([class template][tmpl])
3. "Sometimes when a person gets lonely they talk to themselves. Botch, however, created two friends that rapidly fire deadly lasers... friends are the best!" [Game text via wiki] ([Orbs][orbs]) [Observed: in-game 2.7 store popup, Jan 2014, [store frame][store27]]
4. "Botch tried to look like a normal bear once. He found it awful, but doesn't he look cute?" [Game text via wiki] ([Brown Botch][brown])

## Soul

- **Wants:** to watch things blow up ("Ahh, destruction!") and, quietly, company: he "created two friends" (canon [Game text via wiki]: template, Orbs).  **Fears / flaw:** a glass cannon, "clumsy and nonchalant… can't be bothered", whose Pain Field "increases damage on enemies and teammates alike" (canon [Game text via wiki]: [template][tmpl], [Cosmic Bomb][cbomb], [Pain Field][pain]); he dreads questions about his past [BBG News invention].  **Contradiction that makes them funny:** "Botch is always serious", yet his Doom Cloud is "adorable, huh?" and kills get "Pretty!" (canon [Game text via wiki]: [Ball N Pain][ball], [Doom Cloud][dcloud]; bark [Fan-wiki]).
- **Core values:** pretty explosions (the "Pretty!" bark [Fan-wiki]), his Orbs, orange (his "favorite color and fruit") and cool indifference ("Whatever!") (canon [Game text via wiki]: [Orbs][orbs], [Orange Botch][orange], [Emo][emo]); ranking these as values is [BBG News invention].  **What they'd never do:** explain himself in a full sentence, or try to look "normal" again (canon: "a bear of very few words" [Canon], [characters][chars]; Brown Botch [Game text via wiki], [Brown Botch][brown]); the framing is [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy engine:** the narrator talks about Botch; Botch answers in one word. The item text already does this: "We chose purple, he was not amused" (canon [Game text via wiki]: [Purple Botch][purple]). He is the eerie calm at the centre of the chaos, and his one word is the punchline [BBG News invention].

## Voice

- **Delivery:** "only talks in grainy one-or-two word quotes" [Fan-wiki] ([Assassin (BBG)][assassin]); "tiny, childlike monosyllables with a creepy-delighted tone" [Inference; audio unchecked]. BBG News spec: soft, small and breathy, with static grit; a pause before each word; happy words lift, shrugs drop flat [BBG News invention].
- **Swearing without swearing:** a Geiger-counter crackle over the word, or just "Eh." [BBG News invention], echoing SkyVu's "WARNING! FCC Censorship!" gag [Canon] ([YouTube][fcc]).
- **Known lines** [Fan-wiki; verify by ear against the [Slinkster rip][rip]] ([Assassin (BBG)][assassin]): "Pretty!" / "Delightful!" / "Wee!" / "Sleep!" / "It's time!" / "Cozy!" / "Finally..." / "Eh. Whatever."
- **New lines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. "Ooh. Glowy."
  2. "Normal? Ew."
  3. "Orange. Mine."
  4. "Boom... later."
  5. "Past? ...Pass."
- **Casting:** no voice is credited for Botch; the BBR credits predate him ([credits][credits]). Cast an original voice, human or AI: small, dry, slightly raspy and ageless, with a childlike lift on happy words and light radio grain in post. Never clone or imitate a real person's voice.

### Weapons

Almost all his weapons are floating, translucent, glowing lime energy constructs that hover off one paw and are never gripped [Observed]. Two are held: the Cosmic Bomb charge, at his chest in both paws, and the Tonguechuks, a physical stick in his paw [Observed]. Sizes are in Botch-heads (≈ 0.45× Oliver each). Where the dossier says [Unverified], this file writes [Open], and its "[BBG News house rule]" becomes [BBG News invention], because the README legend has neither label. Wherever a short shows an [Open] item, the depiction is a fan stand-in: never caption it as how the game looks (weapons overview 3.2). Hex codes are sampled approximations (see the Colours label note).

**Default BBG loadout, since 2.1 (July 2013):** Doom Cloud / Pain Field / Crass Knuckles [Observed: 2.1 loadout screens BotchSkin.jpg and 3LlUZgQWmWQ; Fan-wiki].

- **Doom Cloud** (primary; default; seen in 2026 playtests [Observed]; returns as his "Doom Cloud" Super [Canon], in BB Heroes by [Inference]). "This cute little thing is hiding a deadly secret. Once it's out a myriad of lasers create a cloud of doom around itself, but it sure is adorable, huh?" [Game text via wiki] ([Doom Cloud][dcloud])
  - *Look:* a translucent lime jelly-starfish blob (about #B0FF23) with about five drooping, outlined points, bell-shaped from the side, 0.6–0.8 Botch-heads, floating at his shoulder (menus) or low in front of him (play) [Observed].
  - *Fires:* it spins and rises overhead, then rains long tapering lime needles that turn yellow at the tips; the higher it goes, the wider the spread. It lasts 4 s, and he cannot melee or switch weapons meanwhile [Observed: rain; Fan-wiki: spin, rise, height, timing]. That the needles are its bullets is [Inference]: the Doom Cloud icon reads 0/1 in the same frames.
  - *Hits:* damage only, with no status or explosion, and it still hurts players under roofs [Fan-wiki]. Small yellow V-shaped splashes appear where the needles land [Observed; that they are its bullets is Inference]; killed enemies fly apart [Observed]. At the end it sinks and peters out in a burst of green light; it self-destructs if Botch dies or if it touches a solid surface [Fan-wiki].
  - *Confidence:* observed (idle, icon, rain); documented (spin, rise, end flash).
- **Pain Field** (secondary; default; named in the Sep 20, 2026 menu [Observed]; a pink second-slot icon in an Oct 16, 2026 HUD is probably it [Observed; Inference]). "This field speeds projectiles to cause pain faster. Anyone inside takes 50% more hurting from dangerous things flying around. And we mean anyone, increases damage on enemies and teammates alike." [Game text via wiki] ([Pain Field][pain])
  - *Look:* idle, a small pink-red honeycomb orb (about #E25572), about 0.3 Botch-heads, floating at his side; his only magenta weapon [Observed].
  - *Fires:* deployed in place, it balloons into a huge translucent magenta-violet sphere of faceted hex plates (about #B557D5) that lasts 15 s [Observed; Fan-wiki].
  - *Hits:* no damage of its own. Everyone inside, allies included, takes 50% more damage; no overhead icon and no slow [Fan-wiki]. Don't use the Steam trailer's magenta field as reference: Botch is not in that shot [Observed; attribution Open].
  - *Confidence:* observed.
- **Crass Knuckles** (melee; default; named in the Sep 20, 2026 menu [Observed]). "Sometimes when it comes down to brass tax, you need to go with the basics. Creating a fist and just pummeling the enemy is about as basic as you can get." [Game text via wiki]
  - *Look:* an oversized translucent lime crystal fist bristling with spikes (about #ADFE24), 1.0–1.2 Botch-heads, floating over his paw [Observed].
  - *Fires:* a punch; the giant crystal fist thrusts forward [Observed].
  - *Hits:* 70 melee damage, no status [Fan-wiki]. BBG melee contact pops 4–7 yellow five-point cartoon stars with a one-frame flash [Observed: shared melee convention]; crystal-shard sparks on top are [BBG News invention].
  - *Confidence:* observed.

  - *Look:* two orbs, one hovering beside each raised paw at head height: a swirled lime core in a faceted yellow-white halo (about #E1FD4F), about 0.25 Botch-heads each. They read bright yellow in play. **They never circle him** [Observed; Fan-wiki: "Botch holds one floating orb each in his hands"].
  - *Fires:* a continuous rapid laser (0.05 s, 3 damage per shot) until the clip is empty [Fan-wiki]; a thin pale-yellow line, seen only at low resolution [Observed, low resolution].

"Taking bits of time and space and compressing them into a solid object... Botch has a very strange way of playing frisbee." [Game text via wiki] ([Gravity Well][gwell])
  - *Look:* a glowing lime frisbee disc with a bright centre and a segmented ring (about #B3FD0C), about 0.5 Botch-heads, hovering tilted beside his paw, never gripped [Observed].
  - *Fires:* a fast straight throw (650 u/s), 2 per clip [Fan-wiki]; in flight a flat lime ellipse streak [Observed].
  - *Hits:* it explodes on impact with any surface [Fan-wiki] as a translucent, faceted lime half-dome about bear-sized, sometimes paler or with a cyan inner shell [Observed; that the dome is its blast is Inference]; an enemy flies apart beside it [Observed]. - **Chaos Toss** (primary; 100,000 Joules; at launch in 2.1; seen in 2026 playtests [Observed]). "One would say creating a hand that throws poison clouds is a waste of a really cool superpower, but those people are no longer with us." [Game text via wiki] ([Chaos Toss][chaos])
  - *Look:* a large translucent lime skeletal hand of faceted crystal bone segments, curled into an open claw (about #A5FD23), about 1 Botch-head, floating beside his raised paw [Observed].
  - *Fires:* it lobs a "gleaming blob" on a slight arc (1,250 u/s), about one per second [Fan-wiki]; yellow-green motes and green rays appear near targets [Observed]; the blob itself has not been clearly seen [Open].
  - *Hits:* no direct-hit damage [Fan-wiki]. It bursts into a low, rolling carpet of olive-green puffballs (about #477828), knee to waist high on a bear [Observed], that poisons for 12/s over 6 s and stacks; Botch can poison himself [Fan-wiki].   - *Confidence:* observed (hand, icon, cloud); partial (blob in flight).
- **Fear Orbit** (secondary; at launch in 2.1; seen in 2024; named in the Sep 20, 2026 menu [Observed]; 2026 look unseen [Open]). "Most people think having orbs of poison spinning around you is a bad idea. Botch is not most people." [Game text via wiki] ([Fear Orbit][fear])
  - *Look:* two lime soccer-ball-lattice spheres, about 0.25 Botch-heads each, drifting around him and trailing yellow-green dots [Observed]. This, not the Orbs, is his orbiting weapon.

  - *Fires:* his palm thrusts forward and the flame becomes a broad, medium-range, pale yellow-lime flamethrower jet that passes through enemies [Fan-wiki; Observed]. The BBG-branded "Update 2.3 Botch Weapon Concept Art" shows a long, wavy lime stream [Observed; that SkyVu published it is Inference].

  - *Confidence:* observed.

## Movement & acting

- **Timing** [BBG News invention]: he moves in snaps and holds. A quick glide, a dead-still hover, one small gesture, a held pose. Squash and stretch lives in the big head, the ears and the Doom Cloud; his body stays compact. Even though he levitates, nothing drifts: the hover-bob is small and steady, and every move lands on a clear hold.
- **Travel:** he "moves while slightly levitated from the ground" and glides down with "green particle-like effects" [Fan-wiki] ([Assassin (BBG)][assassin]). Feet dangle, the heavy head leads, no footsteps; each stop gets a tiny overshoot and a hold [BBG News invention].
- **Idle:** a hover-bob with his default Doom Cloud bobbing at his shoulder, as in the 2.1 and 2026 menus [Observed]; the twitching notched ear is [BBG News invention]. Alternative idle: the Fear Orbit's "orbs of poison spinning around" him [Game text via wiki] ([Fear Orbit][fear]), drawn as two lime lattice spheres drifting passively; they "orbit the Assassin violently" only when activated [Fan-wiki]. Using it as an idle is [BBG News invention].
- **Weapon acting:** his weapons float beside him and are never gripped [Observed], so he gestures (a flick, a raised paw, a palm thrust) and the construct does the work [BBG News invention]. Exceptions: the Cosmic Bomb charge is held at his chest in both paws, and the Tonguechuks is a physical stick in his paw [Observed].
- **Fighting:** "Dipping in and out of battle" [Game text via wiki] ([template][tmpl]); fast and ranged [Canon]; spare energy goes "to the sky and it'll come down later" [Game text via wiki] ([Cosmic Bomb][cbomb]). Staging: snap in, one clean strike pose, a two-beat hold, snap out [BBG News invention].
- **Pain & victory:** "Oof!"; deaths "Aaaaah!", "Cozy!", "Finally..."; victory "Ahh, destruction!"; defeat "Eh. Whatever." [Fan-wiki]. He fizzles green and sparks, never bleeds [BBG News invention].
- **Taunts** [Fan-wiki] ([Taunts][taunts]): Almighty (curls up "in the foetal position while levitating gradually higher", then throws his arms up), Head Spin (his head "spins for two whole rounds" until he clutches it), Air Guitar. Each taunt ends on a held pose [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy beats** [BBG News invention]: (1) a Head Spin double take stops off-centre, so he nudges his face straight and holds; (2) he hovers in a sitting pose an inch above the couch: "Cozy!"; (3) a forgotten Cosmic Bomb lands at the worst moment.
