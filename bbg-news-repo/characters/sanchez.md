# Sanchez, the Arbiter

> Brought back from near death and "rebuilt from the arm up", Sanchez is a solemn, theatrical cyborg duelist who slows his enemies, speeds up his friends, never lets a Huggable get behind him, and secretly thinks his new arm is really, really cool.

## Soul

- **Wants:** to finish the job. In BB Zombies he radioed Oliver "we'll getcha home", then crashed [Fan-wiki] ([Sanchez][wsanchez]); he still means to [BBG News invention]. **Fears / flaw:** anything at his back ("he's made sure no Huggable will sneak up behind him again" [Fan-wiki, BBH table; in-game origin unconfirmed] ([BBH][bbh])); melodrama, since every skirmish is destiny ("Every sun must set." [Fan-wiki] ([Arbiter (BBG)][arb])). **Contradiction that makes them funny:** a death-haunted duelist who loves his robot arm ("did not ask for this, but he still thinks it is really really cool" [Game text via wiki] ([Blade Augment][blade])), whose grand speeches collapse into "D'oh!" [Fan-wiki] ([Arbiter (BBG)][arb]).
- **Core values:** honour, fate and the squad ("Help, my friends!" [Fan-wiki] ([Arbiter (BBG)][arb])). **What they'd never do:** turn his back on a Huggable or leave a teammate behind [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy engine:** a straight man who thinks he is in a tragic telenovela while everyone else is in a slapstick shooter; his glue and fields turn scenes into slow-motion or fast-forward gags [BBG News invention, from the notes' "doomed hero in a telenovela" [Inference]].

## Movement & acting

- **Timing** [BBG News invention, per [STYLE.md](STYLE.md)]: strong poses, quick moves and clear holds. His rhythm is a long solemn hold on the speech pose, one snap action, then a hold on the result. The heavy arm leads every turn with a small overshoot and settle. Fields pop open in one beat (squash on the orb, stretch into the dome) and then stay still. One clear action per shot; no floaty drift.
- **Posture & idle:** upright and formal, heavy arm hanging forward; he admires it when nobody is looking [BBG News invention].
- **Walk:** light and circling; he "effortlessly circles his enemies" [Canon] ([characters][chars]). BBG speed is 275, back 300 [Fan-wiki] ([Arbiter (BBG)][arb]); he retreats faster than he advances [BBG News invention].
- **Fighting:** "slow down opponents and finish them off with his devastating melee attacks" [Game text via wiki] ([BBG Versions][vers]). By era: 2013–Jun 2021, slow them with the Bind Gun's crackling hot-pink beam (target shows a yellow Z with swirl arrows) [Observed], then finish them in melee. After Jun 19 2021 the same gun (renamed Adrenaline Gun) speeds targets up instead [Fan-wiki]; no image of that is known [Unverified]. A Sep 2026 playtest frame shows a magenta beam with the yellow Z, so 2026 may still slow [Observed; Inference]. The Speed Field fires from a button on his arm [Fan-wiki] ([Speed Field][speed]); the Drain Field is thrown and the Carpet Bomb is a lobbed explosive [Fan-wiki] ([Drain Field][drain]; [Carpet Bomb][carpet]).
- **Carry:** the gun two-handed across the chest or braced at the hip, the prosthetic hand gripping its rear and, on the card, the gloved left hand under the front; the Glue Bomb, the Drain Field leech and the spinner sit in the prosthetic hand; the blade appears only when the melee is out [Observed].
- **Pain / victory** [Fan-wiki] ([Arbiter (BBG)][arb]): "Such pain!"; on death, "Now I wake from this dream."; on a win, "This victory, it was meant to be." A KO follows the game: a floppy, intact pratfall or a pop into clean toy-like pieces [Observed], with sparks [BBG News invention].
- **Taunts** [Fan-wiki] ([Taunts][taunts]): "Creepin" (Thriller-style zombie stagger), "The Fifth Ruby" (awkward flailing), "Fox Say Whaaaa!?" (hop, bent knees, head nods). Evoke, don't copy, the choreography, and never use the songs [BBG News invention]. Land each taunt on a crisp key pose with a hold [BBG News invention].
- **Physical comedy** [BBG News invention]:
  1. He ends a grand speech and turns to leave; the heavy arm swings him an extra half-turn, so he exits backwards.
  2. He steps into his own Speed Field for a dramatic entrance and delivers the line to nobody, three maps away.
  3. He spins every few steps to check behind him; a Huggable mirrors each spin and stays behind him.
  4. He hurls the Carpet Bomb with a battle cry; it rolls back to his boots and flops open, three fire-puffs ripple across it, and he emerges sooty and sparking (built on the wiki's self-kill note [Fan-wiki]).

## Story engine

- **Best fit:** 20–45 s mock-epics (solemn setup, swelling music, slapstick deflation) and 15 s field gags; crash jokes cut to a 2D BB Zombies-style flashback [BBG News invention].
- **Era rule:** pick one era per short and say which: the classic Bind Gun slow (2013–2021, the default) or a post-2021 Adrenaline speed-up gag. Greased Lightning is captioned as a classic weapon and never implied to be in 2026 [BBG News invention, following the verified weapons research's "Pick one era per short and say which"].
- **Loglines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. **We'll Getcha Home:** Sanchez decides to finally finish rescuing Oliver, who is only at the snack machine; the "extraction" escalates through glue, cloak and speed fields.
  2. **Hide and Seek Champion:** riffing on "Sanchez is really good at hide and seek" [Game text via wiki] ([Cloak Field][cloak]), he cloaks for a game with Wil, who wanders off for a sandwich. Three days later Sanchez is still hiding.
  3. **Every Sun Must Set:** his death monologue runs so long the match timer expires; his team wins on time, and he declares, "This victory, it was meant to be."
