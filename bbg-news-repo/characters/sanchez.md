# Sanchez, the Arbiter

> Brought back from near death and "rebuilt from the arm up", Sanchez is a solemn, theatrical cyborg duelist who slows his enemies, speeds up his friends, never lets a Huggable get behind him, and secretly thinks his new arm is really, really cool.

## Soul

- **Wants:** to finish the job. In BB Zombies he radioed Oliver "we'll getcha home", then crashed [Fan-wiki] ([Sanchez][wsanchez]); he still means to [BBG News invention]. **Fears / flaw:** anything at his back ("he's made sure no Huggable will sneak up behind him again" [Fan-wiki, BBH table; in-game origin unconfirmed] ([BBH][bbh])); melodrama, since every skirmish is destiny ("Every sun must set." [Fan-wiki] ([Arbiter (BBG)][arb])). **Contradiction that makes them funny:** a death-haunted duelist who loves his robot arm ("did not ask for this, but he still thinks it is really really cool" [Game text via wiki] ([Blade Augment][blade])), whose grand speeches collapse into "D'oh!" [Fan-wiki] ([Arbiter (BBG)][arb]).
- **Core values:** honour, fate and the squad ("Help, my friends!" [Fan-wiki] ([Arbiter (BBG)][arb])). **What they'd never do:** turn his back on a Huggable or leave a teammate behind [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy engine:** a straight man who thinks he is in a tragic telenovela while everyone else is in a slapstick shooter; his glue and fields turn scenes into slow-motion or fast-forward gags [BBG News invention, from the notes' "doomed hero in a telenovela" [Inference]].

## Movement & acting

- **Timing** [BBG News invention, per [STYLE.md](STYLE.md)]: strong poses, quick moves and clear holds. Slow words, fast body: his rhythm is a hold on the Verdict pose (below), one snap action, then a hold on the result. The heavy arm leads every turn with a small overshoot and settle. Fields pop open in one beat (squash on the orb, stretch into the dome) and then stay still. One clear action per shot; no floaty drift. Budget at 24 fps: the speech-pose hold runs 12–18 frames, a snap action 3–6 frames and the result hold 12–24 frames; each spoken line runs under about 3 s, with at most one pause (about half a second, before the last word); "D'oh!" lands within about 4 frames of the deflation, with no pause. Never describe his acting as "slow" or "solemn" in a video prompt; write "holds still" instead.
- **Posture & idle:** upright and formal, heavy arm hanging forward; he admires it when nobody is looking (the Admiration, below) [BBG News invention].
- **Signature poses** [BBG News invention]:
  1. **The Verdict** (his speech pose): feet planted wide, chest out, chin up, gloved left hand flat on his chest plate, mechanical right arm raised and pointing at his opponent; the visor's glow band narrows; hold.
  2. **The Admiration** (only when nobody is watching): he lifts the mechanical arm to visor height and turns the wrist once; the glow band widens and a small closed-mouth smile lifts the goatee. If anyone looks, he snaps back into the Verdict. Built on the Blade Augment text [Game text via wiki] ([Blade Augment][blade]).
  3. **The Back Check:** an arm-first whip-turn of 180°, an overshoot and settle, then a hold; built on "no Huggable will sneak up behind him" [Fan-wiki, BBH table] ([BBH][bbh]).
- **Walk:** light and circling; he "effortlessly circles his enemies" [Canon] ([characters][chars]). BBG speed is 275, back 300 [Fan-wiki] ([Arbiter (BBG)][arb]); he retreats faster than he advances [BBG News invention].
- **Fighting:** "slow down opponents and finish them off with his devastating melee attacks" [Game text via wiki] ([BBG Versions][vers]). By era: 2013–Jun 2021, slow them with the Bind Gun's crackling hot-pink beam (target shows a yellow Z with swirl arrows) [Observed], then finish them in melee. After Jun 19 2021 the same gun (renamed Adrenaline Gun) speeds targets up instead [Fan-wiki]; no image of that is known [Unverified]. A Sep 2026 playtest frame shows a magenta beam with the yellow Z, so 2026 may still slow [Observed; Inference]; the beam in that frame ends at a distant bear, not at the bear wearing the Z (`2026.addendum.md`). The Speed Field fires from a button on his arm [Fan-wiki] ([Speed Field][speed]); the Drain Field is thrown and the Carpet Bomb is a lobbed explosive [Fan-wiki] ([Drain Field][drain]; [Carpet Bomb][carpet]).
- **Carry:** the gun two-handed across the chest or braced at the right hip, the prosthetic hand gripping its rear and, on the card, the gloved left hand under the front; the Glue Bomb, the Drain Field leech and the spinner sit in the prosthetic hand; the Carpet Bomb goes diagonally across the chest or upright in the right hand; the blade appears only when the melee is out [Observed].

A KO follows the game: a floppy, intact pratfall or a pop into clean toy-like pieces [Observed], with sparks [BBG News invention]; which look a Huggable gets depends on the weapon (Status icons and KOs, under Weapons).

Evoke, don't copy, the choreography, and never use the songs [BBG News invention]. Land each taunt on a crisp key pose with a hold [BBG News invention].
- **Physical comedy** [BBG News invention]:
  1. He ends a grand speech and turns to leave; the heavy arm swings him an extra half-turn, so he exits backwards.
