# Sanchez, the Arbiter

> Brought back from near death and "rebuilt from the arm up", Sanchez is a solemn, theatrical cyborg duelist who slows his enemies, speeds up his friends, never lets a Huggable get behind him, and secretly thinks his new arm is really, really cool.

## Canon anchor

1. "Resurrected and re-engineered from near death, Sanchez is now an agility maestro. He effortlessly circles his enemies" [Canon, BBH-era site] ([characters][chars])
2. "New Arbiter Class: Sanchez / Primary Weapon: Bind Gun / Primary Weapon 2: Glue Bomb / Secondary Weapon: Cloak Field / Secondary Weapon 2: Speed Field / Melee: Blade Augment / Melee 2: Harvester" [Canon, BBG 2.6 notes, Dec 20, 2013] ([archived][n26])
3. "He's ready to control the battlefield! Using his unique slowing weapons, Sanchez can change the tide of a match!" [Canon, Microsoft Store, 2014] ([archived][ms])
4. "Back from the brink of death, Sanchez has been rebuilt from the arm up!" [Game text via wiki, BBG 2.6 class blurb] ([BBG Versions][vers])
5. "Sanchez did not ask for this, but he still thinks it is really really cool." [Game text via wiki, Blade Augment] ([Blade Augment][blade])

## Soul

- **Wants:** to finish the job. In BB Zombies he radioed Oliver "we'll getcha home", then crashed [Fan-wiki] ([Sanchez][wsanchez]); he still means to [BBG News invention]. **Fears / flaw:** anything at his back ("he's made sure no Huggable will sneak up behind him again" [Fan-wiki, BBH table; in-game origin unconfirmed] ([BBH][bbh])); melodrama, since every skirmish is destiny ("Every sun must set." [Fan-wiki] ([Arbiter (BBG)][arb])). **Contradiction that makes them funny:** a death-haunted duelist who loves his robot arm ("did not ask for this, but he still thinks it is really really cool" [Game text via wiki] ([Blade Augment][blade])), whose grand speeches collapse into "D'oh!" [Fan-wiki] ([Arbiter (BBG)][arb]).
- **Core values:** honour, fate and the squad ("Help, my friends!" [Fan-wiki] ([Arbiter (BBG)][arb])). **What they'd never do:** turn his back on a Huggable or leave a teammate behind [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy engine:** a straight man who thinks he is in a tragic telenovela while everyone else is in a slapstick shooter; his glue and fields turn scenes into slow-motion or fast-forward gags [BBG News invention, from the notes' "doomed hero in a telenovela" [Inference]].

## Voice

- **Delivery:** slow, solemn and grand: dramatic pauses, inverted syntax ("My weapon, she is ready!"), third person ("Come to Sanchez.") [Inference from Fan-wiki transcriptions]. The wiki gives him a Spanish accent in BBG [Fan-wiki] ([Arbiter (BBG)][arb]); keep Spanish to short phrases [Inference]. The joke is his melodrama, never his accent [BBG News invention].
- **Swearing without swearing:** "Ay, dios mio!" [Fan-wiki] ([Arbiter (BBG)][arb]), oaths like "Rust and ruin!" [BBG News invention], and bleeps, as in SkyVu's "WARNING! FCC Censorship!" teaser [Canon] ([YouTube][fcc]).
- **Known lines** [Fan-wiki, unverified by ear; check the [fan rip][rip]] ([Sanchez][wsanchez]; [Arbiter (BBG)][arb]):
  1. "Sanchez here, we'll getcha home. What the—Huggables on the ship! AHHH! Mayday, mayday, we're going down!" (BB Zombies)
  2. "My weapon, she is ready!"
  3. "Inadequate. This word describes you."
  4. "For you, I give pain."
  5. "Come to Sanchez."
  6. "Ay, dios mio!" / "D'oh!"
  7. "I'm not even right-handed!" (also quoted as "I'm not even left handed" ([Harvester][harv]))
  8. "This victory, it was meant to be."
- **New lines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. "This arm, I did not ask for. But look at it. LOOK at it."
  2. "Oliver! I came to take you home. I am still coming. Please hold."

- **Casting note:** no voice is credited. Cast an original warm, slightly gravelly baritone with operatic gravitas that can drop into a squeaky "D'oh!"; any accent fluent, never a caricature. Do not clone or imitate a real person [BBG News invention].

## Look (for animation)

- **Default era: BBG 2013,** in the shared BBG News house style (see [README](README.md)): chunky low-poly 3D [Observed] ([Steam][steam]), per SkyVu's Dec 2013 weapon card and 2.6 banner and a wiki-hosted BBG screenshot [Observed] ([card][card]; [banner][banner]; [screenshot][shot]). Alternatives: the 2024 BBH icon (same helmet, visor, goatee, camo; red shoulder armour) [Observed] ([icon][icon]); the BB Zombies pilot (brown fur, olive flight helmet, orange goggles) for 2D flashbacks [Observed] ([still][still]). The 2026 model is unconfirmed.
- **Silhouette & build:** a helmeted bear made lopsided by one bulky red-and-black mechanical right arm [Observed], a prosthetic per the wiki [Fan-wiki] ([Arbiter (BBG)][arb]). No official scale; on the shared BBG News scale chart he is Oliver's height (1.0×), with a lean frame and the right arm about 1.5× the left [BBG News invention].
- **Colours** (estimates from observed official art, not official): BBG default, matched by eye to researchers' colour words [BBG News invention]: helmet #5A2A26, visor #E0202E, face #D2B48C, goatee #2B1B14, chest plate #A8A48E, camo #8C8F87 / #D9D9D2, trousers #5C6638 / #A3956A, arm #A3141E / #1B1A1C, Bind Gun #1E6FD9. BBH icon, k-means sampled [Inference]: browns #53342B / #24110A; visor #CC1228 / #710204.
- **Face & outfit:** glowing red visor over the eyes; light tan face, small dark goatee, gritted-teeth grimace; maroon helmet with bear-ear bumps, grey-khaki chest plate over camo shirt, olive-and-tan camo trousers, dark boots [Observed].
- **Weapons/props:** glossy blue Bind Gun; "Blade Augment – Attaches to Arm"; "HARVESTER – Similar to Clover Claws" (three claws); yellow Speed Field, purple Cloak Field and dark purple Glue Bomb orbs [Observed] ([card][card]). Fields come from "a command gauntlet on his arm" [Fan-wiki] ([Speed Field][speed]). BBH has an "Adrenaline Gun" [Canon] ([characters][chars]); separately, the wiki says BBG's Bind Gun was reworked into an Adrenaline Gun that speeds enemies up in June 2021 [Fan-wiki] ([Adrenaline Gun][adren]). Anchor films on the arm and fields [Inference].
- **Signature skins** [Game text via wiki]: Gray ("So he has a few gray hairs, he just came back from almost dying!") ([Gray Sanchez][gray]), Judge Sanchez ("Are you ready? You do not look ready!") ([Judge Sanchez][judge]). Redraw parody skins loosely [BBG News invention].

## Movement & acting

- **Posture & idle:** upright and formal, heavy arm hanging forward; he admires it when nobody is looking [BBG News invention].
- **Walk:** light and circling; he "effortlessly circles his enemies" [Canon] ([characters][chars]). BBG speed is 275, back 300 [Fan-wiki] ([Arbiter (BBG)][arb]); he retreats faster than he advances [BBG News invention].
- **Fighting:** slow them, then "finish them off with his devastating melee attacks" [Game text via wiki] ([BBG Versions][vers]); fields fire from a button on his arm [Fan-wiki] ([Speed Field][speed]).
- **Pain / victory** [Fan-wiki] ([Arbiter (BBG)][arb]): "Such pain!"; on death, "Now I wake from this dream."; on a win, "This victory, it was meant to be."
- **Taunts** [Fan-wiki] ([Taunts][taunts]): "Creepin" (Thriller-style zombie stagger), "The Fifth Ruby" (awkward flailing), "Fox Say Whaaaa!?" (hop, bent knees, head nods). Evoke, don't copy, the choreography [BBG News invention].
- **Physical comedy** [BBG News invention]:
  1. He ends a grand speech and turns to leave; the heavy arm swings him an extra half-turn, so he exits backwards.
  2. He steps into his own Speed Field for a dramatic entrance and delivers the line to nobody, three maps away.
  3. He spins every few steps to check behind him; a Huggable mirrors each spin and stays behind him.

## Story engine

- **Best fit:** 20–45 s mock-epics (solemn setup, swelling music, slapstick deflation) and 15 s field gags; crash jokes cut to a 2D BB Zombies-style flashback [BBG News invention].
- **Loglines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. **We'll Getcha Home:** Sanchez decides to finally finish rescuing Oliver, who is only at the snack machine; the "extraction" escalates through glue, cloak and speed fields.
  2. **Hide and Seek Champion:** riffing on "Sanchez is really good at hide and seek" [Game text via wiki] ([Cloak Field][cloak]), he cloaks for a game with Wil, who wanders off for a sandwich. Three days later Sanchez is still hiding.
  3. **Every Sun Must Set:** his death monologue runs so long the match timer expires; his team wins on time, and he declares, "This victory, it was meant to be."

**Canon you can state as fact:**
- BBG 2.6 (Dec 20, 2013) added him as the Arbiter with the kit above [Canon] ([2.6][n26]; [teaser][tease]); the Microsoft Store spelled it "ARBITOR" [Canon] ([archived][ms]).
- "ARBITER: Sanchez" is in the 2026 release [Canon] ([Steam][steam]). BBH: "Sanchez the Arbiter", "Resurrected and re-engineered from near death", Adrenaline Gun, Speed Field [Canon] ([characters][chars]; [App Store][appstore]).
- His look is official art; the descriptions are researchers' readings [Observed].

**Game text via wiki — attribute it, don't state it as fact:**
- The class blurb ("rebuilt from the arm up") and item and skin text are quoted by the wiki as official BBG description but have not been checked against the game files; say "the in-game text, as the wiki quotes it" [Game text via wiki].

**Fan-wiki / invented — present as fan material:**
- BB Zombies pilot role, crash and line; killed or almost killed; accent; voice lines; taunts; stats; 2021 rework; gauntlet; prosthetic; the BBH-table "no Huggable will sneak up behind him" text [Fan-wiki].
- The Necromancer as the BB Zombies caster; BBH hex values [Inference].
- BBG hex values, height, the unfinished-rescue motive, new lines, beats, loglines, dynamics, Saberi's arm upgrades [BBG News invention]. BBG News never names his rebuilder.
