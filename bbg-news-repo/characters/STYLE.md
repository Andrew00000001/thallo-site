# BBG News house style: feature-film 3D

Chosen by Bryce on 2026-10-17 from the 9-style board (style #4). The rebuilt character souls, model sheets and every short use this style. It replaces the earlier "chunky low-poly early-2010s mobile-game 3D" house style.

The style #4 tile sets the render finish only: surfaces, light and proportions. Its character designs came before the verified research and are wrong (for example Riggs's green coiled pack and diagonal bandolier, Oliver's green harness and green launcher, and the Huggable's white antenna with no banner). Never attach the tile, or any tile on the board, as a character, Element or start-frame reference; build those from the BBG News model sheets made from the rebuilt souls [BBG News invention].

Research cited here lives in the Thallo site repo, not in this one: the weapons overview is `research_notes/BBG weapons (verified)/00_OVERVIEW.md` and the maps overview is `research_notes/BBG maps (verified)/00_OVERVIEW.md`. Their section numbers differ, so always name which overview.

## The look

- **Render:** polished, stylized 3D animation with a feature-film finish. Soft, rounded shapes, appealing cartoon proportions and bold, readable silhouettes.
- **Outlines:** none. Characters and sets get no ink outline. The thick black character outline in the Steam trailer (2013 footage [Inference, strong]) and in 2026 captures [Observed] is a game render trait (weapons overview §2.1; maps overview §3), so even shots matched to those looks leave it out. Only a 2D flashback (see Prompt wording) draws outlines [BBG News invention].
- **Faces:** expressive, within each soul's face rules. Most bears act with brows and eyes (Oliver's heavy brows and smirk, Riggs's half-lidded glare). Where a soul hides, fixes or replaces a feature, that rule wins in every take: Sanchez's opaque visor always hides his eyes (the visor glow, his mouth and head tilts act); Graham's goggles stay over his eyes (his brows and moustache act); B-1000 acts with his brow plate and eye-slits, and his jaw plate drops a little when he shouts; Botch's pupil-less glow changes shape; the Huggable's toothy grin never moves (its bead eye, head tilt and body act). Reject any take that breaks these [BBG News invention, taken from the souls].
- **Surfaces:**
  - **Furred bears:** short, soft plush fur wherever the soul gives fur. B-1000 has none, and Tillman's covers only his head and torso, over a hard shell.
  - **Robot parts** (joints, plates, gear): clean painted metal or plastic.
  - **Cloth** (headbands, bandanas, tops, sashes, straps, pouches, banners, camo): soft matte fabric with simple folds [BBG News invention].
  - **Glows** (eyes, visors, goggle lenses, lights, energy cores, beams): clean colour as soft emissive light, with a gentle bloom that lights nearby fur and floor [BBG News invention].
  - **The Huggable:** fuzzy plush with visible stitched seams, a bandage and a button eye.
  - **Creatures and food props** (for example the Gernobyl gerbil, the hawk, the pie and the potato cannon): the cast's soft, rounded finish. Creatures get short plush fur; Huggable heads, toppers and mitts on other characters' gear get the Huggable's stitched plush [BBG News invention].
  - **Weapons:** colours and shapes as summarised in each soul's Weapons section, from the verified weapons research.
- **Lighting:** soft global illumination, a warm key light, a cool fill and a gentle rim light, with cinematic depth of field. On a named map, the map's own practical light is the key (see Sets and light).
- **Colour:** saturated but natural. Red team is the default for the lead; opponents default to the blue team (see Teams in frame).
- **Frame:** shorts and start frames are 9:16 vertical, set in the tool's aspect-ratio setting as well as in the framing line, and composed for Reels: keep faces, sprites, on-screen text and the end card out of the bottom fifth and the right edge, where the Reels interface sits, and leave headroom over any character who will carry a status sprite. Model sheets are 16:9 landscape, four views in a row [BBG News invention].

## Sets and light

Basis: the verified maps research (maps overview §3 and §5). The rules are [BBG News invention] unless labelled.

- **One place per short:** name one map or a plain set. Keep a map's layout, landmark silhouettes, material identity, palette family and practical lights, re-rendered in the house finish, with one landmark per shot (maps overview §5.3).
- **Time of day** [Observed, per map]: No Bears Land noon; Gold Digger, Aztec Attack, Haunted Castle, Abusement Park and Toxic Terror night; Sky View and Steamyard Blues dusk; Desert Airmine, Skate or Die and Sky Pirates day; interiors (Space Oddity, H.I.P. Test Lab, Marecraft, Frozen Pundra) no sky (maps overview §5.1 rule 4).
- **Key light:** on a map, its own practical light is the key (lamp and gold glow, lava, moonlight, ooze, neon), with the cool fill and rim kept (maps overview §5.3 rule 11). The warm-key rig under The look is the default for plain sets.
- **Signs and team colour:** signs keep their shape, colour and place but are blank or carry abstract marks (maps overview §5.2 rule 5). Scenery shows team colour only as accents (maps overview §3).
- **Scale:** doors about 1.5–2× a bear's height; rails and sandbags at knee to waist height [Inference] (maps overview §3).
- **Effect light:** weapon and effect light may flash and tint characters within a shot. Habit 2 fixes the base setup, not these flashes.
- **Organic interiors:** Marecraft is a stylised warm-orange organic cavern with soft cartoon ribs and glowing green pools, never gore (maps overview §5.5 rule 15).

## Habits that keep it from looking generic

1. **Same references every shot:** use each character's Element or model sheet in every image and video (directly, or through a start frame made with it), so faces and gear never drift.
2. **One lighting setup per scene:** keep it fixed across shots, with no mid-scene changes of time of day or base colour. Weapon and effect flashes are allowed (see Sets and light).
3. **Snappy timing:** strong poses, quick moves and clear holds (squash and stretch). Avoid slow, floaty, drifting motion and constant camera wander.
4. **Simple staging:** one clear action per shot and at most two characters with Elements on screen (several copies of one generic target, such as a wave of Huggables, count as one). Keep backgrounds uncluttered.
5. **Check every take:** reject any take with morphing, extra limbs (Saberi's third arm and Tillman's backpack saw-arms are correct), melted props, gear that changes within the take or differs from the sheet the take used, a broken face rule, or anything the README's teen rating bans (blood, gore, organs, slurs, sexual content or innuendo, crude taunts, tobacco, alcohol, drugs). Never post a take that breaks the model sheet.
6. **Finish in the edit (no generation credits):** a light film grain, one consistent colour grade per short, and the README end card, held about 2 s inside the safe area.
7. **Label it:** every post's caption labels the short an unofficial AI fan film, and every short ends on the README end card (see the README's end card and caption rules).
8. **Clean references:** build Elements and start frames only from BBG News model sheets. Official SkyVu art and frames are look references for the eye. If one must be attached (as tillman.md's alternate looks do), crop it to the character, remove HUD, text, logos and card backgrounds (maps overview §5.2 rule 9), and never post it or build a sheet from it unchanged [BBG News invention; basis: SkyVu's Terms of Use, and the lore notes' reading that films which redraw the characters are closer to the fan art SkyVu invites than films that rip official assets, Inference].

## Prompt wording

- **Element:** the saved character reference in the generator (a Higgsfield Element, which a prompt can call by its placeholder), built from the soul's model sheet and named with the soul's "Short name for tools" (for example `riggs-heavy`). Its hands must match the shot: use the soul's empty-handed sheet (Oliver's, Riggs's and Astoria's unarmed Elements are the pattern) or the sheet made for that loadout. Where a soul has several sheets, it says which one is the Element for which shot [BBG News invention].
- **Model sheet:** "Fan-art character model sheet (front, side, back and three-quarter views) of *Name*, *description*; polished stylized feature-film 3D animation character, soft rounded shapes, expressive face, soft plush fur, clean painted metal robot parts, bold readable silhouette. Neutral grey background, even studio lighting, no text."
- **Video suffix:** "Polished feature-film 3D animation, soft rounded shapes, expressive faces, soft plush fur, soft global illumination, cinematic lighting, snappy cartoon timing with strong poses and holds."
- **Framing line:** "Vertical 9:16 framing.", placed right before the video suffix in every video starter and shot.
- **Shared negative list:** photorealistic fur, realistic anatomy, blood, gore, sexual content, real-person likeness, official logos, text, watermarks, morphing, extra limbs, low-poly, flat 2D.
  - **Sanctioned variants** (the only changes to the shared strings, apart from the per-shot rules below):
    - **Saberi:** "extra paws, extra legs, a second mechanical arm" replaces "extra limbs", because her third arm is a defining feature. Whenever Saberi is in frame, every soul's negative uses this replacement, without "a second mechanical arm" when Sanchez, Tillman or B-1000 is also in frame (Co-stars in frame; saberi.md co-star note).
    - **B-1000:** in his model sheets and his solo shots, "glossy painted armour" replaces "soft plush fur" in the model-sheet wording and the video suffix, because he has no fur. In any shot shared with a furred character or the Huggable, the standard strings stay and his solo-only negative line is dropped [BBG News invention].
    - **Object sheets** (a prop with no face or fur, such as graham.md's deployed Turret): "Fan-art prop model sheet (front, side, back and three-quarter views) of *description*; polished stylized feature-film 3D animation prop, soft rounded shapes, clean painted metal robot parts, bold readable silhouette. Neutral grey background, even studio lighting, no text." [BBG News invention].
- **Negative lists and tools:** Higgsfield's Seedance 2.0 and Nano Banana Pro showed no negative-prompt field when the first shorts were planned (Oct 2026); re-check each model before use and record any change here. Where a tool has a negative field, paste the list there. Where it has none, never paste a negative list into the prompt text, because a noun named in a prompt can appear and words like "gore" can trip moderation. Write the wanted look positively instead, add at most a short "Avoid:" sentence of generic drift terms (for example photorealistic fur, realistic anatomy, real-person likeness, official logos, text, watermarks) just before the framing line, and use the full lists as the checklist for habit 5 [BBG News invention].
- **Shot formula** (every shot, weapon shot or not): attach the Element of every character in frame (not pasted), then paste the acting character's line from its soul's video starter (none for a beat with no character in frame) + one action (a weapon snippet replaces the starter's own action, never adds to it) + the scene line + the framing line "Vertical 9:16 framing." + the video suffix, which always comes last. Dialogue, overlays and sound go in a separate line marked as not for pasting [BBG News invention].
  - **Scene line:** one sentence naming the place, the time of day, the key light and the camera, pasted word for word into every shot of a scene, for example "A narrow stone-edged dirt path between two bottomless black mine shafts at night, warm lamp and gold glow with a cool moonlit fill. Locked-off medium shot." For a plain set it is "Simple, uncluttered background.", optionally followed by one camera sentence chosen once for the scene and pasted into each of its shots (astoria.md and saberi.md give one for scenes that are theirs alone) [BBG News invention].
  - **Everyone in frame:** any other character in frame is described in the action in its own soul's words and backed by its Element. A generic opponent is a Huggable, described as "a small round hot-pink stitched plush teddy enemy with a big white toothy grin" (riggs.md's wave-Huggable wording) with the Huggable's blue-team Element (see Teams in frame), or, in a solo shot, "a plain grey padded training dummy"; in a Tutorial short, the Tutorial's orange lattice (wireframe-mesh) bear dummies [Observed: maps tutorial.md]. Never write an unnamed bear ("a rival", "a nearby bear"), which invites an off-model lookalike of the cast [BBG News invention].
- **Colours in prompts:** write colours as words in every prompt; keep sampled or placeholder hex codes in a separate "Colour key (outside the prompt)" line, for tools that accept a palette and for checking takes. Generators paint hex codes as swatches or text.
- **Weapon names:** keep in-game weapon names out of image and video prompt text, because tools read them literally ("Doom Cloud" becomes a storm cloud, "Bearzooka" invites lettering or a generic green bazooka). Describe each weapon by its look, and keep the name in the snippet title, labels and notes.
- **Character and map names:** the same rule covers character and map names. In video starters, snippets, scene lines and every other image or video prompt, refer to each character by its character line (or by its Element placeholder) and each map by its look, never by plain name: a name can print as lettering ("B-1000") or cue a real person or a stereotype. The model-sheet template keeps *Name*, because each sheet is made once and checked, its "no text" clause guards against lettering, and a sheet that prints the name is rejected [BBG News invention].
- **Paste-ready text:** keep notes, sheet choices, overlay reminders and per-shot negative changes out of the text to be pasted: put them after a closing quote or in a separate line marked as not for pasting, so no note or status-sprite name reaches a tool.
- **Co-stars in frame:** a negative applies to the whole frame, so in shots with other characters drop any negative term that describes a co-star (its fur, eyes, build, clothing or gear) and rely on each character's Element. A soul may also keep such terms in a "solo shots only" line. In a shared shot, the negative is the shared list once, plus each soul's own terms that survive every co-star's drop list. Give relative heights in words from the README scale chart (for example "about two-thirds as tall as a teddy-bear soldier", as the Huggable's character line does) [BBG News invention].
- **Teams in frame:** opponents default to the blue team. In Free-For-All the game shows the player's own bear as Red [Fan-wiki] and every other player as Blue [Fan-wiki; Observed 2026] (maps overview §3), so a red lead faces blue opponents. Make a blue-team sheet and Element per character as needed, changing only the team-coloured parts in the soul's blue-team note (the parts listed in weapons overview §2.7); a character whose blue-team note changes no words (botch.md, saberi.md, sanchez.md) reuses its red sheet and Element as they are; weapons and projectiles keep their own colours (README, Team colour). A Huggable opponent carries a blue banner, or the white rainbow-dot flag the README notes [Observed]; what that flag marks is [Open]. A red-on-red hit is only for a deliberate friendly-fire gag [BBG News invention].
- **Faceted effects:** where the research shows a hard-edged faceted blast, dome, cube cluster, shard burst or ice chunk (weapons overview §2.1 and §2.4), render it as crisp cut-crystal facets with smooth bevelled edges inside an otherwise smooth shot. Faceted smoke puffs and goo become soft rounded puffs and glossy blobs, and soft particle fire stays soft. In any shot that prompts a faceted effect, swap "low-poly" in the shared negative for "low-poly character models".
- **Organic interiors and break-apart KOs:** in an organic-interior set (Marecraft's cavern) and in any break-apart KO shot, add "organs, viscera, wet flesh" to that shot's negative or take checklist, after the README's "No blood, gore or organs"; terms the soul's list already carries need not repeat (b-1000.md, huggable.md). A break-apart KO is any pop into pieces (a bear's toy pieces, B-1000's plates and parts, a Huggable's plush pieces with gold sparkles) or SkyVu's merch-art Huggable head-pop with stuffing and a rainbow column; an intact, floppy collapse is not one, and for the Huggable that is every KO that is neither an explosion nor radiation (huggable.md, Pain & victory) [BBG News invention].
- **2D flashbacks:** a clearly marked BB-1, BB Zombies or BLAST flashback may use that game's own 2D look [Observed: the lore report's section on SkyVu's three visual registers, and its animation_reference notes]: crisp digital cel (uniform black outlines, flat fills, minimal shading, simple dot eyes) for BB-1 and BLAST, and soft, painterly 2D with sketchy dark outlines and simple flat backgrounds for BB Zombies. In those shots only, drop "flat 2D" (and any outline term a soul adds) from the negative, and replace the 3D video suffix with the matching 2D suffix [BBG News invention, after the Observed registers]:
  - BB-1 or BLAST: "Crisp digital 2D cel animation, clean uniform black outlines, flat colour fills, minimal shading, simple dot eyes, snappy cartoon timing with strong poses and holds."
  - BB Zombies: "Soft painterly 2D cartoon animation, sketchy dark outlines, simple flat backgrounds, snappy cartoon timing with strong poses and holds."
  - A 3D Element pulls the shot back to 3D, so a flashback uses a 2D reference sheet: the model-sheet wording with its 3D clause ("polished stylized feature-film 3D animation character, soft rounded shapes, expressive face, soft plush fur, clean painted metal robot parts") replaced by the matching 2D suffix's look words. Mark every flashback in the edit, never in the prompt: an edit-added transition in and out and an original-art era tag such as "BB-1, 2010". A BBH-labelled short carries its "BB Heroes" tag from its first frame (README, Era). Every other alternate look is rendered in the house 3D style [BBG News invention].
- **Status sprites and HUD graphics** (Irradiated disc, Slowed Z, Poisoned skull, Controls Reversed "?", Stunned bird, reticles, lock brackets and the sniper crosshair, HEAD SHOT and kill-feed cards) are never written into an image or video prompt. Generate without them, keep the full negative list, and add them in the edit as original-art overlays, with no game UI or logos. Each status below is a flat 2D sprite floating over the head [Observed] (a status that weapons overview §2.6 lists with no icon, such as Frozen, gets none), so frame a shot that will carry one with headroom above the target. The specs, from weapons overview §2.1, §2.2 and §2.6:
  - **Irradiated:** a yellow disc (about #F0C30C–#FBFE13) cut into six wedges that drop out in stages until a yellow trefoil on a black disc remains [Observed; Fan-wiki].
  - **Slowed:** a chunky lemon-yellow block "Z" with a dark outline between two white crescent orbit arrows [Observed].
  - **Poisoned:** a round skull, dark purple (about #410C3A) unless the weapon's dossier shows it green [Observed; the default is BBG News invention].
  - **Controls Reversed:** a glossy lime "?" [Observed].
  - **Stunned:** a blue Nugg bird with a white crest between white spin arcs [Observed].
  - **Lock-on and scope:** green crescent "LOCKED" brackets on the target [Observed: tillman.md]; a red crosshair over the head during a sniper lock [Fan-wiki].
  - **Kill feed and HEAD SHOT:** "[killer] KILLS [victim]" with team-outlined portraits and a red X on the loser; HEAD SHOT puts a red crosshair on the victim's portrait [Observed]. Never use the game's "SUICIDE" wording (maps overview §5.5 rule 16).

## Sound

- **Voices:** follow the README's Voices rule: original voices only, never a clone or imitation of a real person. Whether a voice the video tool generates counts as an "original performer" is [Open]: b-1000.md and botch.md say "human or AI", the README says "original performers", and the call is Bryce's.
- **Mouths:** follow each soul's face rules. The Huggable never lip-syncs (its grin is fixed and it talks through its radio), and Botch's flat mouth barely moves.
- **Music:** original only; no licensed music or lyrics (weapons overview §3.5).
- **Effects:** only a few game sounds are documented [Fan-wiki] (weapons overview §3.6): the Gamma Ray's Geiger ticks that speed up, the Bass Cannon's dubstep loop, the Propbearganda Bearbershop tune, the music cut-out and echo under Controls Reversed, the Wrench's "metal noises" and the class barks; the weapons class dossiers add a few more, such as the Mini Me's laugh-like sound (the weapons research's huggable.md, not the soul) [Fan-wiki]. Every other sound effect is [BBG News invention].
- **Swearing:** bleeped, cut off or swapped for a minced oath (README, Teen rating).
