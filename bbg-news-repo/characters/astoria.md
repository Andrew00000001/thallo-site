# Astoria, the Sniper

> The coolest, most capable bear on the team, who covers everyone from the edge of the fight while quietly wishing they'd let her in.

## Canon anchor

1. "No nonsense, special ops. Always a step ahead of the enemy because she thinks things through. Methodical, calculating and seemingly cold at first glance. She wants to be part of the gang but the others have a hard time including her because she's wicked smart and totally put together; it makes some of the others (Oliver and Tillman mostly) feel incompetent." [Canon card, c. 2012–13] ([File:AstoriaDescription.jpg][card])
2. "No nonsense, Astoria spares little time for Oliver and Wil's childish antics. But don't let her cold remarks and serious personality fool you, she really does care for the Battle Bears." [Canon, BBH-era site] ([characters][chars])
3. "The first female BATTLE BEAR ever!" [Canon, BBR trailer 2011] ([YouTube][trailer])
4. "The tall towers enable Astoria to cover most of the battlefield at once. Beware, though – enemies can easily surprise you while you focus on another target." [Canon, archived BBR map page] ([Marecraft][marecraft])

## Soul

- **Wants:** to belong (canon: "She wants to be part of the gang"). **Fears / flaw:** her competence and cool front keep the others at a distance (canon: "the others have a hard time including her because she's wicked smart and totally put together"; "seemingly cold at first glance"). **Contradiction that makes them funny:** she's icy with friends, but a clean shot makes her grin (game text: "Astoria always has a smile on her face after blowing off a huggable's head!" [Game text via wiki] ([Old-Timey][oldtimey])).
- **Core values:** preparation, precision and the team (canon: "thinks things through"; "she really does care"). **What they'd never do:** charge in without a plan, or ask out loud to be included [BBG News invention, from the notes' Inference "wants in but won't ask"].
- **Comedy engine:** the deadpan straight woman. Others make the chaos; she answers with understatement, and sometimes her own perfect plan backfires (canon: "Oliver and Tillman mostly" feel incompetent; "straight-woman" [Inference]; backfires [BBG News invention]).

## Voice

She insults with icy politeness rather than swearing, and in a bleep gag she is bleeped just once and looks mortified [BBG News invention]; the gag is a nod to SkyVu's "WARNING! FCC Censorship!" teaser [Canon] ([YouTube][fcc]).

You got closer to me than most corpses." · "Watch it, swinging a club here!" · "Not the most difficult battle I've been through." · "Don't get too excited over a series of lucky shots." · "Oh no, I hope no one shoots me." (read as deadpan [Inference])
- **New lines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. "I'm not cold. I'm calibrated."
  2. "I ran the numbers twice. You're still the problem."
  3. "That's not a plan, Wil. That's a snack with a fuse."

- **Casting note:** a cool, crisp, low-mid adult female voice: clipped diction, a sardonic lift at line ends, room for a startled yelp [BBG News invention]. Cast an original performer to this brief; never clone, sample or imitate any real person's voice, including the original actor's. ## Relationships

The romance is NOT canon, and no source found shows her reciprocating [Inference]. At most, show his one-sided crush as showing off, which she ignores [BBG News invention].

## Look (for animation)

- **Default era: BBG 2013,** in the shared BBG News house style (see [README](README.md)): chunky low-poly 3D in saturated colour [Observed] ([Steam][steam]). The outfit comes from the c. 2012–13 card [Observed] ([card][card]) plus the wiki's BBG default: "an ammo belt, a backpack and gloves with the colour of the team colour" [Fan-wiki] ([skin][skin]). Alternatives [Observed]: the BBG splash card (blue eyes, red-and-white top, arms crossed; possibly a skin [Inference]; [Steam trailer][steam]); the 2024 BBH portrait (blue eyes, red-and-white cap, red scarf; [icon][icon]); a teal-masked sniper in a 2026 Steam screenshot, which is Astoria only by [Inference] ([screenshot][ss]).
- **Silhouette & build:** a teddy-bear soldier with small round ears and a hand on her hip [Observed], carrying a long rifle; the class is "Agile and deadly" [Canon] ([BBR classes][classes]). No official scale or build; on the shared BBG News scale chart she is compact, at Oliver's height (1.0×) but slimmer [BBG News invention].
- **Colours** (estimates, not official):
  - Sampled from the 2024 portrait [Inference]: fur #DABF84, shade #937135, muzzle #E1D6BE, red #CC1818 (only 1–2% of the sample). Reusing the muzzle swatch for the belly is a BBG News choice [BBG News invention].
  - Unsampled placeholders [BBG News invention]: eyes #2E9C8A, backpack #6F8296, belt/straps #1F1F24, gloves #CC1818 (red team).
- **Eyes:** teal-green, heavy-lidded and unimpressed [Observed]. The wiki says "a definite eye color, which is green" [Fan-wiki] ([Astoria][wastoria]); later art shows blue [Observed].
- **Gear:** grey-blue backpack on dark straps, black woven utility belt [Observed].
- **Weapons/props:** a sniper rifle [Canon] (BBG default: the Viperifle [Fan-wiki] ([Sniper (BBG)][sniper])); the Tomahawk, "Unleash a terrified and angry hawk that temporarily blinds any player it hits!" [Game text via wiki] ([link][tomahawk]); the Battering Ram ram-skull club, "a weapon of last resort" [Game text via wiki] ([link][ram]). Draw the hawk as an angry-bird gag, with no cultural styling [BBG News invention].
- **Skins** [Game text via wiki]: Old-Timey; Battle Damaged ("Come from the future…" [link][bd]); She Fu ("Crouching bear, hidden sniper." [link][shefu]); The Seer ("3. There is no 3, no one makes it past 2." [link][seer]).

## Movement & acting

- **Posture, walk, idle:** upright and still, hand on hip or arms crossed [Observed]. She walks light and silent. Idle, she clicks her scope one notch, glances at the team and looks away [BBG News invention].
- **Fighting:** from towers and edges [Canon], with tunnel vision as her weak spot (Marecraft line above). "Up close, she's vulnerable but compensates with her 'Explosive Retreat' Ultimate, diving away from the action" [Canon] ([characters][chars]). Most of her rifles need distance to lock on; a headshot kills outright [Fan-wiki] ([Sniper (BBG)][sniper]).
- **Pain, victory, defeat:** she yelps when hit, shrugs off a win and denies a loss [Inference from Fan-wiki barks] ([Sniper (BBG)][sniper]).
- **Taunts** [Fan-wiki] ([Taunts][taunts]): "Kick-the-Moon" (sideways somersault); "Cut-Throat" (thumb across the neck; use it sparingly). "Tea-Bagging" is above the teen ceiling; never use it [Inference].
- **Physical comedy** [BBG News invention]:
  1. She tests the wind and clicks the scope three times, and Tillman blows up the target. She holds, then clicks back three times.
  2. She launches the hawk. It looks at the enemy, looks at her, and blinds Oliver.
  3. Her Explosive Retreat overshoots into a Huggable pile. She freezes, then tiptoes backward.

## Story engine

- **Best fit:** 15–30 s gags built around one long-lens shot: a patient setup, someone else's chaos, a deadpan button. Use 45–60 s for small "she wants in" arcs [BBG News invention].
- **Loglines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. **Clean Shot:** Oliver and Wil keep wandering into Astoria's scope to wave. She fires anyway, and the Huggable bursts into rainbow fluff an inch from Wil, who takes it as a high-five.
  2. **RSVP:** Astoria plans how to ask to join game night like a covert op, with exit routes and fallback lines. Then Saberi just drags her to the couch.
  3. **Overwatch:** Tillman "guards" her sniper tower by blowing up every threat, including each floor beneath her. Her Explosive Retreat lands her right back beside him.
