# Graham, the Engineer

> Graham is the gruff but lovable old mechanic who keeps the Battle Bears' machines running, forever fixing what the younger bears (mostly Oliver) take apart, and far sharper than his creaky physique suggests.

## Canon anchor

1. "Gruff but lovable mechanic, armorer and machines expert. Largely responsible for repairing the ship but always seems to find something new to fix that keeps them grounded – especially when Oliver keeps poaching parts for his newest contraption." [Canon card] ([card][card])
2. "Don't be fooled by his physique. This class relies more on brains than brawn. This wise whisker-bear specializes in defending areas of the map and controlling enemy traffic. You got to wake up pretty early in the morning to sneak one past this old timer." [Canon] ([BBR classes, 2012][bbr])
3. "Master inventor and lawn enthusiast, he keeps the bears supplied with towers that are usually able to keep them alive. Usually." [Canon] ([BBF heroes][bbf]; same text on the [Canon card] [BBF card][bbfcard])
4. "Graham, a lovable mechanic and machines expert, leverages his brains for area control and supporting his team, as well as the occasional crossword." [Canon] ([characters][chars])
5. "One of the oldest of the Battle Bears in existence, Graham is an accomplished inventor that knows any problem can be fixed with a little bit of ingenuity and some elbow grease." [Game text via wiki] ([Graham (Skin)][wskin])

## Soul

- **Wants:** a ship that stays fixed; peace for his lawn and crossword (canon: card; "lawn enthusiast"; "crossword").  **Fears / flaw:** always finds "something new to fix", so they stay "grounded" (canon: card); cranky about "kids these days" ([Fan-wiki] barks).  **Contradiction that makes them funny:** the creakiest bear on the field, yet nobody can "sneak one past this old timer" (canon: BBR).
- **Core values:** "ingenuity and some elbow grease", brains over brawn, "supporting his team" (game text via wiki: skin text; canon: BBR; site).  **What they'd never do:** take off his "belt, backpack or his construction hat" ([Fan-wiki][wg]); hand Oliver a part on purpose [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy engine:** the grumpy straight man who turns out to be right: the kids cause chaos, Graham grumbles, builds something and wins sitting down. Running gags: his back, his lawn, his missing parts, "Usually." [BBG News invention, from canon "Usually." and [Fan-wiki] back barks]

## Voice

- **Delivery:** gravelly, unhurried muttering with sudden bursts ("Eureka!") [BBG News invention; no researcher heard the audio]; old-timey slang (whippersnapper, crumbum, britches, doo-dad), back and age complaints, losing things [Fan-wiki] ([Engineer (BBG)][we]).
- **Swearing without swearing:** minced oaths ("Jumping Jehoshaphat!" [Fan-wiki]); anything worse is bleeped, like SkyVu's "WARNING! FCC Censorship!" [Canon] ([YouTube][fcc]).
- **Known lines** [Fan-wiki; verify by ear against the [Slinkster rip][rip]] ([Engineer (BBG)][we]): "Kids these days. Always blowing up." / "Whippersnapper!" / "Shouldn't you be in school?" / "Stay off of my lawn!" / "Ooh! My back!" / "Jumping Jehoshaphat!" / "Snap. His. BRITCHES!" / "Eureka!"
- **New lines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. "Somebody pulled the stabilizer off my turret. Three guesses, and two of 'em are Oliver."
  2. "Eight across: 'Bear who keeps stealing my parts.' Six letters. Starts with O."
  3. "This turret'll keep you alive. Usually. Don't make me say 'usually' twice."
  4. "Back in my day we fought Huggables uphill both ways, in the snow, with a T-square!"
  5. "It ain't held together with duct tape, sonny. It's held together with experience. ...And duct tape."
- **Casting note:** older male; gravelly baritone, dry crackle, folksy cadence, warm not frail, jumps a register for "Eureka!" [BBG News invention]. "Bryan North - Voice (Graham)" [Canon] ([BBR credits][credits]) is credit history only, not a voice target: cast a new actor with their own take, and never clone or imitate a real person's voice.

- **Riggs:** no canon link. Two veterans; Graham grades Riggs's field repairs harshly [BBG News invention].
- **Tillman:** his towers keep bears alive "Usually" [Canon] ([BBF heroes][bbf]); that they cover Tillman's charges is [Inference]; endless rebuilds [BBG News invention].
- **Huggable:** "The Modified Huggable" [Canon] ([BBR][bbr]) has no named modifier. Graham re-stitches it but didn't make it [BBG News invention].
- **Astoria:** no canon link. He is the one bear allowed to touch her rifle [BBG News invention].

- **B-1000 / Botch:** no canon links. Graham itches to dismantle B-1000, and he waves a Geiger counter at Botch [BBG News invention].
- **Saberi:** fellow tinkerers [Inference]. Workshop rivalry: her "Hammers fix anything!" [Fan-wiki] ([Combat Tech][wct]) versus his elbow grease and T-square [BBG News invention].
- **Sanchez:** "re-engineered from near death" [Canon] ([characters][chars]) by an unnamed rebuilder, so never say Graham (or Saberi) did it; he nags about arm upkeep [BBG News invention].
- **ABBi:** Wil's "mocking robot backpack" [Canon] ([characters][chars]) heckles his repairs [BBG News invention].
- **Huggables / Necromancer:** they "seem to eat grass" [Fan-wiki] ([Huggable][wh]): lawn war [BBG News invention]. The wiki says only Graham "can (barely) communicate with the Necromancer" in BBF [Fan-wiki, Unverified] ([Graham][wg]).
- **H.I.P. / Nyx / Jackson / Captain Malcolm:** no source links them to Graham; tuning Jackson's chair or patching the ship Malcolm (BB-1 pilot [Fan-wiki] ([Malcolm][wm])) flies is [BBG News invention].

- **Silhouette & build:** top-heavy; the wiki gives him "the biggest backpack of all the Battle Bears" [Fan-wiki] ([Graham][wg]). ## Movement & acting

- **Posture, walk & idle:** stooped, paw on lower back, a slow plod with pipes puffing; idles over a crossword or a bolt that doesn't need tightening [BBG News invention]. The Engineer is one of BBG's slowest classes [Fan-wiki] ([Engineer (BBG)][we]).
- **Fighting:** he plants a turret and holds ground, "controlling enemy traffic" [Canon] ([BBR][bbr]); T-square golf swings up close [BBG News invention].
- **Pain & victory:** "Yowch, that smarts!" / "Now THAT's a classic whoopin'!" [Fan-wiki] ([Engineer (BBG)][we]).
- **Taunts** [Fan-wiki] ([Taunts][wtaunt]): Bernie (a lazy shuffle, then "complaining about his hurt back"), Na-Na-Na (flicks his moustache "like a child"), Disco (hand on hip, arm swinging diagonally).
- **Comedy beats** [BBG News invention]: (1) his back cracks like a ratchet and the turret swivels with each click; (2) he follows his missing bolts to Oliver's contraption and unscrews them until it collapses; (3) he measures an enemy with the T-square, squints, adjusts... and the turret fires first.

## Story engine

- **Best fit:** 15–40 s reversal gags where the "slow old man" was three steps ahead: workshop cold opens, one-turret defences, parts chases [BBG News invention].
- **Loglines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. **Spare Parts:** Graham's turret won't fire because Oliver "borrowed" its trigger for a contraption, so Graham takes his parts back one bolt at a time, mid-launch.
  2. **Usually:** Tillman charges a Huggable swarm, bellowing that Graham's turret has his back. It does. Usually.
  3. **Eight Across:** Huggables invade Graham's lawn, and he defends it with exactly one turret without getting up from his crossword.
