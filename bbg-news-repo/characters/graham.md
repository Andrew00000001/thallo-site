# Graham, the Engineer

> Graham is the gruff but lovable old mechanic who keeps the Battle Bears' machines running, forever fixing what the younger bears (mostly Oliver) take apart, and far sharper than his creaky physique suggests.

## Canon anchor

1. "Gruff but lovable mechanic, armorer and machines expert. Largely responsible for repairing the ship but always seems to find something new to fix that keeps them grounded – especially when Oliver keeps poaching parts for his newest contraption." [Canon card] ([card][card])
2. "Don't be fooled by his physique. This class relies more on brains than brawn. This wise whisker-bear specializes in defending areas of the map and controlling enemy traffic. You got to wake up pretty early in the morning to sneak one past this old timer." [Canon] ([BBR classes, 2012][bbr])
3. "Master inventor and lawn enthusiast, he keeps the bears supplied with towers that are usually able to keep them alive. Usually." [Canon] ([BBF heroes][bbf]; same text on the [Canon card] [BBF card][bbfcard])
4. "Graham, a lovable mechanic and machines expert, leverages his brains for area control and supporting his team, as well as the occasional crossword." [Canon] ([characters][chars])
5. "One of the oldest of the Battle Bears in existence, Graham is an accomplished inventor that knows any problem can be fixed with a little bit of ingenuity and some elbow grease." [Game text via wiki] ([Graham (Skin)][wskin])

## Soul

- **Wants:** a ship that stays fixed; peace for his lawn and crossword (canon: card; "lawn enthusiast"; "crossword").  **Fears / flaw:** always finds "something new to fix", so they stay "grounded" (canon: card); cranky about "kids these days" ([Fan-wiki] barks).  **Contradiction that makes them funny:** the creakiest bear on the field, yet nobody can "sneak one past this old timer" (canon: BBR).
- **Core values:** "ingenuity and some elbow grease", brains over brawn, "supporting his team" (game text via wiki: skin text; canon: BBR; site).  **What they'd never do:** take off his "belt, backpack or his construction hat" ([Fan-wiki][wg]); hand Oliver a part on purpose [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy engine:** the grumpy straight man who turns out to be right: the kids cause chaos, Graham grumbles, builds something and wins sitting down. Running gags: his back, his lawn, his missing parts, "Usually." [BBG News invention, from canon "Usually." and [Fan-wiki] back barks]

- **Catchphrase rule** [BBG News invention, on the Fan-wiki bark contexts]: at most one signature line per short. "Usually." [Canon] is his button: when it ends a short it is the last word before the hard cut, said flat after a held beat. "Eureka!" marks only the turn, with the lens flare and his one fast move. "Whippersnapper!" and "Delinquent!" come only on a swing, and "Shouldn't you be in school?" and "Stay off of my lawn!" only on a melee KO, as in game [Fan-wiki]. Shouts build word by word like "Snap. His. BRITCHES!": a short gap between words, the last one loud.
- **Casting note:** older male; gravelly baritone, dry crackle, warm not frail, jumps a register for "Eureka!"; a folksy cadence that mutters in short clauses, trails off mid-complaint and leaves a half-second silence before every comeback [BBG News invention]. "Bryan North - Voice (Graham)" [Canon] ([BBR credits][credits]) is credit history only, not a voice target: cast a new actor with their own take, and never clone or imitate a real person's voice.
