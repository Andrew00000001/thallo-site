# Riggs, the Heavy

> Riggs is the Battle Bears' brave, fatherly leader, part Captain America and part Picard, who guards his team in earnest and treats wiping out Huggables as "good, clean fun."

## Canon anchor

1. "Captain and leader. Brave, serious and is the responsible one. He takes a paternal role and looks after his team. He's part Captain America, part Jean Luc Picard. He never flinches from battle, but knows how to best use his resources – even when that means running to fight another day" [Canon card] ([card][card])
2. "a brave and strategic leader. He's protective, knowing when to stand firm or strategically withdraw, always prioritizing his team's safety and success." [Canon] ([characters][chars])
3. "Riggs the heavy is built like a tank, and moves like a stone… Your melee attack is devastating!" [Canon] ([BBR classes][bbr])
4. "If death itself was found violently murdered, the police would think about questioning Riggs and then no do it. Dude's crazy!" [Canon] ([BBF heroes][bbf])
5. "Riggs warms himself up for what most text books would call 'mass murder' but Riggs, us and everyone reading this knows it only as 'good, clean fun'" [Canon, channel unconfirmed] ([BB-1 Riggs 1/10][bb1-1])

## Soul

- **Wants:** his team safe and one uninterrupted day off (canon card: "looks after his team"; canon, channel unconfirmed: "Riggs' Day Off (From NOT Killing Everything)").  **Fears / flaw:** loves combat far too much (canon, channel unconfirmed: "'good, clean fun'"); fears losing a bear on his watch [BBG News invention].  **Contradiction that makes them funny:** the responsible dad is the scariest bear aboard (canon: "paternal role"; "Dude's crazy!").
- **Core values:** duty, team first, any resource including retreat (canon: "running to fight another day").  **What they'd never do:** flinch in front of his team (canon: "never flinches from battle"), or admit he escaped the *Ursa Major* before Wil [BBG News invention, built on [Press]: Wil's campaign "takes place after… Oliver and Riggs, have already safely escaped" ([TouchArcade][ta])].
- **Comedy engine:** deadpan straight man who is secretly the punchline: horrifying orders given as routine, gratitude for pain, calm amid rainbow confetti (canon, channel unconfirmed: "good, clean fun"; [Fan-wiki] "Thank you, may I have another?").

## Voice

- **Delivery:** low, gruff, clipped military commands with dry, macho humour [Inference; no researcher heard the audio]. Drill jargon ("Hostile detected.") [Fan-wiki] ([Heavy (BBG)][wh]); may call Oliver "Soldier" [Fan-wiki] ([Riggs][wr]). Answers insults with "Noted." [BBG News invention].
- **Swearing without swearing:** bleeps, as in SkyVu's "WARNING! FCC Censorship!" gag [Canon] ([YouTube][fcc]), or "Sweet mother of ordnance!" [BBG News invention].
- **Known lines** [Fan-wiki; check by ear against the [Slinkster rip][rip]] ([Heavy (BBG)][wh]): "Target neutralized." / "War, it's fantastic!" / "Thank you, may I have another?" / "Killing me will only make... me... stronger!" / "Why aren't there any... medics?" / "Good work, team." / "Gotta train harder!" / "Fall in!"
- **New lines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. "We're surrounded. Excellent. Saves us the walk."
  2. "This is a strategic withdrawal. Strategically, we run. Fall in!"
  3. "Soldier, put the Bearzooka down. Slowly. ...Now point it at them."
  4. "Day off: cancelled. Again. Somebody hold my toothbrush."
  5. "Rainbows everywhere. Somebody's cleaning that up, and it's not me. Wil!"
- **Casting:** credited voice Brian Bell [Canon] ([BBR credits][credits]), for reference only; never clone or imitate a real person's voice. Cast an original gravelly low baritone with parade-ground projection and bone-dry timing.

## Look (for animation)

- **Default era: BBG 2013,** in the shared BBG News house style (see [README](README.md)): the default Riggs, taken from his official card (uploaded 2013) ([card][card]) and built as a chunky low-poly BBG model [Observed; Inference]; the 2026 trailer card shows a red faceted skin instead [Observed] ([Steam][steam]). Alternatives: BB-1 2D cel look ([frame][bb1f]); 2024 Battle Bears Heroes (BBH) portrait ([icon][icon]).
- **Silhouette and build:** large, muscular, broad-shouldered; square blocky muzzle, heavy brow, small rounded ears [Observed]; "built like a tank" [Canon]. No official scale [Open]; on the shared BBG News scale chart he is ~1.2× Oliver's height and the broadest bear; only B-1000 (~1.5×) is bigger [BBG News invention].
- **Colours (estimates from observed official art):** fur #C2D4D8 / #9EB1B7 / #75838D, shadow #353A48, researcher-sampled from the 2024 portrait, not official codes [Inference] ([icon][icon]). The card reads pale blue-grey to near-white [Observed]; the wiki says "white" [Fan-wiki] ([Riggs][wr]). Unsampled gear picks: bandolier #2E3035, pack #4F6B35 / #1C1D20 [BBG News invention].
- **Eyes and gear:** stern half-lidded glare, eye colour unrecorded [Observed; Open]. Dark diagonal bandolier; green-and-black ribbed pack over one shoulder [Observed] ([card][card]); BB-1 adds a dark-grey pauldron [Observed] ([frame][bb1f]).
- **Weapons:** BBG defaults Beebeegun, Hivey League, Barbed Gloves [Fan-wiki] ([Heavy (BBG)][wh]); "Beebee gun" is official in BBH [Canon] ([characters][chars]); bee theme [Inference]. Their look is undocumented, so designs, like the toothbrush prop, are [BBG News invention].
- **Skins** [Fan-wiki] ([Heavy (BBG)][wh]): Riggs, Green, Purple, Brown, Orange, Olive; Gold Riggs, Old Timey, No More Mr Ice Guy, Nature's Folly, Teen Wolf, The Incredible Riggs, Law Man, On A Roll, B and J, Rocky Horror. The red card skin is unnamed [Open].

## Movement & acting

- **Posture and walk:** upright, chest out, slow heavy steps ("moves like a stone" [Canon]); tied for slowest base speed [Fan-wiki] ([Heavy (BBG)][wh]). Idle: arms folded, cracking gloved knuckles [BBG News invention].
- **Fighting:** "excels in close combat"; "astonishing leap and ground-smashing ability" [Canon] ([characters][chars]). Improvises: grenades a table-slammed Huggable, builds a chainsaw from elevator parts [Fan-wiki] ([Campaign][wc]). Retreats without shame [Canon card].
- **Pain / victory / defeat:** "Thank you, may I have another?" / "Good work, team." / "Gotta train harder!" [Fan-wiki].
- **Taunts** [Fan-wiki] ([Taunts][wt]): "Club Dance" (jumps, pumps his chest), "Head Shake" (arms crossed, shakes head), "Sumo" (rocks like a sumo wrestler).
- **Physical comedy** [BBG News invention]:
  1. He fights a whole Huggable wave with a toothbrush in his mouth, then rinses and spits.
  2. Oliver pitches a plan; Riggs does the slow Head Shake. It works anyway; one grudging Club Dance pump.
  3. He bellows "Charge!" and lumbers so slowly the fight ends before he arrives. "Good work, team."

## Story engine

- **Best format:** "routine interrupted" (15–45 s): mundane task, chaos, deadpan fix, back to the task, hard cut [Inference from the BB-1 cutscene copy]. Huggable "gore" stays rainbow confetti.
- **Loglines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. **Day Off, Take 47:** Riggs tries to finish brushing his teeth while Huggables flood the bathroom. He wins, rinses, and the alarm sounds again.
  2. **Medic!** (with Saberi): Riggs keeps collapsing with "Why aren't there any... medics?" until Saberi arrives with a lecture and her Sledgehammer. He regrets asking.
  3. **Strategic Withdrawal** (with Tillman): Riggs orders a retreat; Tillman obeys by charging backwards, guns blazing. It works, and Riggs logs it as a retreat.
