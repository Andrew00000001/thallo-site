# Riggs, the Heavy

> Riggs is the Battle Bears' brave, fatherly leader, part Captain America and part Picard, who guards his team in earnest and treats wiping out Huggables as "good, clean fun."

## Canon anchor

1. "Captain and leader. Brave, serious and is the responsible one. He takes a paternal role and looks after his team. He's part Captain America, part Jean Luc Picard. He never flinches from battle, but knows how to best use his resources – even when that means running to fight another day" [Canon card] ([card][card])
2. "a brave and strategic leader. He's protective, knowing when to stand firm or strategically withdraw, always prioritizing his team's safety and success." [Canon] ([characters][chars])
3. "Riggs the heavy is built like a tank, and moves like a stone… Your melee attack is devastating!" [Canon] ([BBR classes][bbr])
4. "If death itself was found violently murdered, the police would think about questioning Riggs and then no do it. Dude's crazy!" [Canon] ([BBF heroes][bbf])
5. "Riggs warms himself up for what most text books would call 'mass murder' but Riggs, us and everyone reading this knows it only as 'good, clean fun'" [Canon, channel unconfirmed] ([BB-1 Riggs 1/10][bb1-1])

## Soul

- **Wants:** his team safe and one uninterrupted day off (canon card: "looks after his team"; canon, channel unconfirmed: "Riggs' Day Off (From NOT Killing Everything)").  **Fears / flaw:** loves combat far too much (canon, channel unconfirmed: "'good, clean fun'"); fears losing a bear on his watch [BBG News invention].  **Contradiction that makes them funny:** the responsible dad is the scariest bear aboard (canon: "paternal role"; "Dude's crazy!").
- **Core values:** duty, team first, any resource including retreat (canon: "running to fight another day").  **What they'd never do:** flinch in front of his team (canon: "never flinches from battle"), or admit he escaped the *Ursa Major* before Wil [BBG News invention, built on [Press]: Wil's campaign "takes place after… Oliver and Riggs, have already safely escaped" ([TouchArcade][ta])].
- **Comedy engine:** deadpan straight man who is secretly the punchline: horrifying orders given as routine, gratitude for pain, calm amid rainbow confetti [BBG News invention] (canon, channel unconfirmed: "good, clean fun"; [Fan-wiki] "Thank you, may I have another?").

### Weapons

The BBG Heavy has 20 items: 8 primaries, 6 secondaries, 6 melees [Fan-wiki] ([Heavy (BBG)][wh]). The Sep 2026 Playtest #4 menu names all 20 [Observed, low-res storyboard frames; weapons overview §5.2]; which ones ship on Nov 4 is [Open]. The bee theme is in the item text: "angry bees" (Beebeegun), "a spreading cone of angry bees" (Hivey League), "a queen bee" (Buzz Kill) [Game text via wiki]. Heavy guns are oversized boxy miniguns, shotguns and launchers held low at the hip, most with a themed topper (a hive, a cheese lump, a Huggable or a Huggable head, a plasma globe, a red can) [Observed]. Every glove melee is a two-glove punch with one shared swing [Fan-wiki]. Sizes are fractions of his standing height [Observed, approximate], converted with the BBG News scale chart [BBG News invention]. Looks below are [Observed] unless noted; hex codes are samples, estimates rather than official codes [Inference]. Pick one era per short for weapon effects and captions, and say which (weapons overview §3.1); the house default design above combines two observed looks on purpose. Never use the BB Heroes behaviour (the "Beebee gun" shell burst, the leap-smash) in a BBG short.

## Movement & acting

- **Timing (house style):** snappy feature-film timing: strong key poses, quick moves, clear holds, squash and stretch; no slow, floaty drifting or wandering camera ([STYLE.md](STYLE.md)). His comedy lives in the holds: after chaos, a dead-still deadpan hold of about a second before he speaks [BBG News invention].
- **Posture and walk:** upright, chest out, slow heavy steps ("moves like a stone" [Canon]); tied for slowest base speed [Fan-wiki] ([Heavy (BBG)][wh]). Animate the walk slow overall but crisp on every contact: plant, a small squash through the legs and a ripple through the fur, a beat of hold, then the next step [BBG News invention]. Idle: arms folded, cracking gloved knuckles [BBG News invention].
- **Fighting:** "excels in close combat" and "astonishing leap and ground-smashing ability" [Canon] ([characters][chars]) come from the battlebears.com characters page, which describes the "Beebee gun" burst attack and an "Ultimate"; the page never names BB Heroes, so tying it to BB Heroes (soft launch May 2023, global Oct 2024) is [Inference], a strong one, since the wiki's BBH table lists Riggs's attack "Beebee Gun" and Ultimate "He can fly now?" [Fan-wiki]. BBG has no leap-smash ability [Inference: no BBG item or class text describes one]. In a BBG short his close-combat identity is the minigun or shotgun plus the two-glove melee punch [Fan-wiki]. The class text adds: "Keep clear of wide open spaces as his primary weapons have poor accuracy! Your melee attack is devastating!" [Game text via wiki]. Improvises: grenades a table-slammed Huggable, builds a chainsaw from elevator parts [Fan-wiki] ([Campaign][wc]). Retreats without shame [Canon card].
- **Weapon acting** [BBG News invention, on Fan-wiki and Observed mechanics]:
  1. **Minigun:** the wind-up is his anticipation: he plants his feet, lowers his brow and holds while the barrels spin up (the wind-up is [Fan-wiki]); then a locked-down firing pose with a steady recoil shudder; then the wind-down and a settle.
  2. **Shotgun and launchers:** one hard kick per shot, held from the hip (the hip carry is [Observed]), snapping back to the pose between shots.
  3. **Two-glove punch:** a one-two with a 2–3 frame freeze on contact as the stars pop, then a heavy follow-through.
- **Pain / victory / defeat:** "Thank you, may I have another?" / "Good work, team." / "Gotta train harder!" [Fan-wiki].
- **Taunts** [Fan-wiki] ([Taunts][wt]): "Club Dance" (jumps, pumps his chest), "Head Shake" (arms crossed, shakes head), "Sumo" (rocks like a sumo wrestler).
- **Physical comedy** [BBG News invention]:
  1. He fights a whole Huggable wave with a toothbrush in his mouth, then rinses and spits.
  2. Oliver pitches a plan; Riggs does the slow Head Shake. It works anyway; one grudging Club Dance pump.
  3. He bellows "Charge!" and lumbers so slowly the fight ends before he arrives. "Good work, team."
