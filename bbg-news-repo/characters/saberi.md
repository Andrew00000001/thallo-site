# Saberi, the Combat Tech

> Saberi is the squad's no-nonsense momma bear and born gadgeteer: raised on an Energy Rifle instead of toys, she built herself a third arm for extra firepower and fixes any problem, or enemy, with a hammer before patching up her team.

## Canon anchor

1. "SABERI - Medic. Saberi, the powerful healer and momma bear, commands respect and excels in support roles, making her a cherished asset to any team." [Canon, BBH-era site] ([characters][chars])
2. "New Combat Tech Class: Saberi / 10 Skins Available / 3 Taunts Available" [Canon, BBG 2.4 notes, Oct 24, 2013] ([archived][n24])
3. "Gamma Burst: Saberi Smash! Creates a deadly radiation area around the player." [Canon, BBG 2.9 notes, May 2014] ([archived][n29])
4. "A weapon expert who doesn't take nothing from nobody! Saberi is a momma bear who knows how to lay down the law." [Game text via wiki, default skin] ([Saberi (Skin)][skin])
5. "Other babies have toys, Saberi had this Energy Rifle. She patched it and rebuilt it hundreds of times, but this trusty rapid firing gun has been with her forever." [Game text via wiki] ([Energy Rifle][erifle])

## Soul

- **Wants:** a squad that's alive and gear that works [Inference] (from "excels in support roles" [Canon] ([characters][chars]) and the Energy Rifle text [Game text via wiki] ([Energy Rifle][erifle])). **Fears / flaw:** escalation; a failed fix gets a bigger hammer [Inference] (from "Sometimes a bigger hammer is necessary for bigger problems." [Game text via wiki] ([Sledgehammer][sledge])). **Contradiction that makes them funny:** "something of a prodigy" [Game text via wiki] ([Combat Tech (BBG)][ct]) who is surprised when her gear works ("Ooh, that worked?" [Fan-wiki] ([Combat Tech (BBG)][ct])), and a healer [Canon, BBH] ([characters][chars]) who "knows how to lay down the law" [Game text via wiki, default skin] ([Saberi (Skin)][skin]).
- **Core values:** respect, self-reliance, her people [Inference] (from "commands respect" and "momma bear" [Canon] ([characters][chars])); play her as a tinkerer who also patches up the team [Inference]. **What they'd never do:** scrap anything, or anyone, still fixable, or back down [BBG News invention], riffing on "doesn't take nothing from nobody" [Game text via wiki, default skin] ([Saberi (Skin)][skin]).
- **Comedy engine:** the warm, bossy fixer whose solutions outgrow the problem: the adult in the room until her fix becomes the chaos [BBG News invention, from "upbeat hands-on fixer with hammer puns" [Inference]].

## Voice

- **Delivery:** warm, brassy, plain-spoken and quick; contractions ("Imma", "outta"), stretched words ("Smmmash!"), hammer puns [Inference from transcriptions]. Mom-swears ("Oh, for Pete's sake!", "Sweet sprockets!") [BBG News invention]; real swears only as bleeps, per SkyVu's "WARNING! FCC Censorship!" teaser [Canon] ([YouTube][fcc]).
- **Known lines** [Fan-wiki, unverified by ear; check the [fan rip][rip]] ([Combat Tech (BBG)][ct]): "Ooh, that worked?" · "Hammers fix anything!" · "Imma pound some sense into you!" · "Smmmash!" · "That's going to leave a mark!" · "Oh, another job well done!" · "Now why didn't that work?" · "Alright, follow me!"
- **New lines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. "Hold still, sweetie. This'll only hurt the Huggable."
  2. "I didn't fix your blaster. I improved it. You're welcome."
  3. "Third arm's for missiles. The other two are for wagging at you."
  4. "Don't make me get the big hammer."
  5. "Ooh, it worked! …Everybody act like I meant that."
- **Casting note:** no voice actor is credited for Saberi anywhere. Casting direction [BBG News invention]: use an original warm, confident adult female voice, mid-low and a little brassy, that snaps from cosy mom to drill sergeant mid-line. Never clone or imitate a real person; use the fan rip only to check wording.

## Relationships

No official text links Saberi to any specific character; each dynamic is labelled.

- **Oliver:** "a specialist in collateral damage" [Canon] ([characters][chars]); she patches up Oliver and whatever he breaks, Bearzooka first [BBG News invention].
- **Riggs:** his "paternal role" [Canon card] ([card][rcard]) beside her "momma bear" [Canon] ([characters][chars]). Mismatched squad parents: his bark "Why aren't there any... medics?" [Fan-wiki] ([Heavy (BBG)][heavy]) summons her, lecture included [BBG News invention].
- **Tillman:** "If it can explode, it will." [Canon card] ([card][tcard]); she patches him up, scolds him and confiscates his fireworks [BBG News invention].
- **Huggable:** the playable one is "Modified" [Canon] ([BBR classes][classes]); her BBH "Huggable Splicer" sprays "huggable bits" [Canon] ([characters][chars]). It keeps trying to hug the gun [BBG News invention].
- **Astoria:** "the second known female Battle Bear, after Astoria"; both say "Woo hoo!" [Fan-wiki] ([Combat Tech (BBG)][ct]). Saberi drags her into the gang [BBG News invention].
- **Wil:** "It's easy to talk Wil into doing just about anything" [Canon card] ([card][wcard]); she mothers him and patches him up [BBG News invention].
- **Graham:** fellow tinkerers [Inference]. Workshop rivalry: her "Hammers fix anything!" [Fan-wiki] ([Combat Tech (BBG)][ct]) versus his elbow grease and T-square [BBG News invention].
- **B-1000:** he wants to "share their data with villains" [Canon] ([characters][chars]); she wants to open him up, and he logs her as an "unauthorised technician" [BBG News invention].
- **Botch:** "a bear of very few words" [Canon] ([characters][chars]); her Gamma Burst "Creates a deadly radiation area" [Canon] ([2.9 notes][n29]). She mothers him, checking his temperature and feeding him, and he answers "Cozy!" (a wiki-transcribed Botch bark [Fan-wiki] ([Assassin (BBG)][assassin]); the scene is [BBG News invention]).
- **Sanchez:** joined two months after her [Canon] ([2.4 notes][n24]; [2.6 notes][n26]); Epic lists a "sanchez-plus-saberi-mega-deal" [Canon], probably a bundle [Inference] ([Epic FAQ][epic]). No source names his rebuilder, so never say Saberi (or Graham) rebuilt him; in openly non-canon "what if" shorts she upgrades his arm, which he protests and loves [BBG News invention].
- **ABBi:** Wil's "mocking robot backpack" [Canon] ([characters][chars]); she heckles Saberi's third arm [BBG News invention].
- **Huggables / Necromancer:** "paradoxically feared for their deadly hugs" [Canon] ([characters][chars]); she hugs back, with a mallet [BBG News invention].
- **Nyx / H.I.P.:** "A defector from the Battle Bears team" [Canon, 2024] ([heroes page][heroes]); she takes it personally [BBG News invention].
- **Jackson:** "revenge on the huggables that put him in his chair" [Canon, 2024] ([heroes page][heroes]); she upgrades the chair [BBG News invention].
- **Captain Malcolm:** wiki-only [Fan-wiki] ([link][malcolm]).

## Look (for animation)

- **Default era: BBG 2013,** in the shared BBG News house style (see [README](README.md)): chunky low-poly 3D, saturated colour [Observed] ([Steam][steam]). Exception: she debuted in BBG 2.4 (Oct 2013) and her clearest official art is from 2014, so she is dressed per that 2014 art and a matching BBG screenshot [Observed] ([art][art]; [screenshot][shot]). Alternative: the 2024 BBH redesign (light tan fur, blonde bangs, cream bandana, winking teal eyes, navy suit, red backpack) [Observed] ([icon][icon]). Her 2026 model is unconfirmed; neither the 2013 BBG trailer nor the 2026 Steam trailer shows a splash card for her, and whether one exists elsewhere is unknown [Observed].
- **Silhouette & build:** big polka-dot bow on top, mechanical third arm rising behind her [Observed] ([art][art]); she "decided to create herself a third arm" [Game text via wiki] ([Missiles][missiles]), backpack-mounted per the wiki [Fan-wiki] ([Missiles][missiles]; [Minigun][minigun]). No official build or scale; on the shared BBG News scale chart she is compact and sturdy, at Oliver's height (1.0×), with the raised arm reaching about 1.4× [BBG News invention].
- **Colours** (estimates, not official): BBG default, matched by eye to researchers' colour words [BBG News invention]: fur #5B3A26, muzzle #C9A47E, red #C8102E, dots #F4F4F4, eyes #3DBA4E, tech glow #9B4DFF, arm metal #7A7F86. BBH portrait, k-means sampled [Inference]: fur #9C8564 / #796247, light #EEE4C7.
- **Face & outfit:** bright green eyes with heavy lashes and eyeliner, tan muzzle, confident half-smile; red bandana with white polka dots tied in a big top bow; red short-sleeved top [Observed] ([art][art]).
- **Weapons/props:** Energy Rifle, Plasma Machine Gun, Missiles, Minigun, Mallet, Sledgehammer; later Plasma Shotgun and Gamma Burst [Canon] ([2.4 notes][n24]; [2.9 notes][n29]), probably the 2014 art's purple-core double-barrel gun and glowing disc [Inference]. BBH: Huggable Splicer, Healing Turret [Canon] ([characters][chars]).
- **Signature skins** [Game text via wiki] ([Charcoal][charcoal]; [Derby][derby]; [Undead][undead]): Charcoal ("Working with explosions and other things that go boom tends to cover one in a bit of grime."), Derby ("Don't mess with a roller girl!"), Undead ("The 40's were a scary time."; probably the pin-curl bear in the 2.4 banner [Inference] ([banner][banner])).

## Movement & acting

- **Posture & idle:** arms crossed, smug half-smile [Observed] ([screenshot][shot]); the third arm twirls a wrench [BBG News invention].
- **Walk:** planted and unhurried [Inference] (BBG Speed 240, Sanchez 275 [Fan-wiki] ([Combat Tech (BBG)][ct])); the arm bobs like a scorpion tail [BBG News invention].
- **Fighting:** a gun in her paws [Observed] ([art][art]); the backpack weapon runs 10 s, reloads 30 s, and she is "the only class that cannot switch weapons at all" [Fan-wiki] ([Combat Tech (BBG)][ct]). Hammers up close: "Slam!" [Fan-wiki] ([Combat Tech (BBG)][ct]).
- **Pain / death / victory:** "Stop it!", "I'll get you next time!", "Oh, another job well done!" [Fan-wiki] ([Combat Tech (BBG)][ct]).
- **Taunts** [Fan-wiki] ([Taunts][taunts]): "River Dance" (step dance, airborne 360), "Knee Thing", "Come At Me, Bro" (stomp, paw-beckon). She practises them "in her off time" [Game text via wiki] ([Rockabilly][rock]).
- **Physical comedy** [BBG News invention]:
  1. The third arm dies mid-brag; one mallet whack revives it. "Hammers fix anything!"
  2. She hugs a sniffling Wil while, behind her back, the arm calmly loads missiles.
  3. She nails the River Dance 360, but the arm doesn't turn with her and bonks her head.

## Story engine

- **Best fit:** 15–30 s fix-it gags (something breaks, she escalates, it works too well or not at all); 45–60 s "momma bear" squad-care pieces [BBG News invention].
- **Loglines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. **Bigger Problems:** Oliver's Bearzooka jams. Saberi works up from Mallet to Sledgehammer to something absurd, and the fix launches Oliver across the map.
  2. **Did Not Ask For This:** In an openly non-canon "what if", Saberi upgrades Sanchez's rebuilt arm (minigun, toaster, bottle opener). He solemnly protests and loves each one ("Sanchez did not ask for this, but he still thinks it is really really cool" [Game text via wiki] ([Blade Augment][blade])).
  3. **Medic!** (with Riggs): Riggs keeps collapsing with "Why aren't there any... medics?" until Saberi arrives with a lecture and her Sledgehammer. He regrets asking.

**Canon you can state as fact:**
- BBG 2.4 debut (Oct 24, 2013) as the Combat Tech with the six launch weapons; Plasma Shotgun and Gamma Burst in 2.9; "COMBAT TECH: Saberi" in the 2026 release ([Steam][steam]); BBH "powerful healer and momma bear" with Huggable Splicer and Healing Turret [Canon].
- Both looks are official art; the descriptions are researchers' readings [Observed].

**Game text via wiki — attribute it, don't state it as fact:**
- Item, skin and class-blurb text is quoted by the wiki as official BBG description but has not been checked against the game files; say "the in-game text, as the wiki quotes it" [Game text via wiki].

**Fan-wiki / invented — present as fan material:**
- Voice lines, taunts, stats, the backpack mount, "second known female Battle Bear", Vine appearances [Fan-wiki].
- Gun and disc names, Undead as the banner bear, BBH hex values [Inference].
