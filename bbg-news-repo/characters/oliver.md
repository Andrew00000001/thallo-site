# Oliver, the Soldier

> The crafty show-off who turns whatever is lying around into a weapon, causes the disaster, then saves the day he endangered, as long as someone is watching.

Collateral damage is his worst enemy. Oliver is known for making up crazy weapons on the spot – like a freestyle rapper who blows things up." [Canon card, 2012] ([File:Oliver_description.jpg](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:Oliver_description.jpg))

## Soul

- **Wants:** an audience and the credit (canon: "the boy requires an audience"). **Fears / flaw:** he jumps first and lets his pride run wild (canon: "Leaps before he looks"; O.C.O.'s "already enormous pride" [Canon, channel unconfirmed] ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AA6tYQ81aiQ))). **Contradiction that makes them funny:** he causes the emergency, then loudly rescues everyone from it (canon: "save the very day he has endangered").
- **Core values:** improvising and style over planning (canon: "Great at improvising"). **What they'd never do:** solve a problem quietly (canon: "absolutely most action packed way"), or hand the credit to someone else [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy engine:** a chaos agent with a cool-guy veneer. He builds the joke, sets it off, then plays the fallout "like a boss" [Canon, channel unconfirmed] ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJZuwYEq1y8)). He works best opposite a straight man (Riggs, Astoria, Graham) or a willing victim (Wil) [Inference] (canon: Astoria vs "Oliver and Wil's childish antics").
