# Oliver, the Soldier

> The crafty show-off who turns whatever is lying around into a weapon, causes the disaster, then saves the day he endangered, as long as someone is watching.

## Canon anchor

1. "Crafty, fun-loving and always getting into trouble. Leaps before he looks. Great at improvising, he can manage to get himself out of almost any fix he puts himself in. Collateral damage is his worst enemy. Oliver is known for making up crazy weapons on the spot – like a freestyle rapper who blows things up." [Canon card, 2012] ([File:Oliver_description.jpg](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:Oliver_description.jpg))
2. "He never passes up an opportunity to show everyone how brave he is, operative word being 'show', the boy requires an audience. Oliver would never solve a problem, big or small, without first considering the absolutely most action packed way he could do so." [Canon card, 2013 redesign] ([File:Oliver.jpg](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/File:Oliver.jpg))
3. "Self proclaimed hero, it doesn't matter if everything went wrong because of him, he's here to save the very day he has endangered." [Canon] ([archived BB Fortress heroes](https://web.archive.org/web/20130908135756/http://battlebears.com/games/battle-bears-fortress/heroes/))

## Soul

- **Wants:** an audience and the credit (canon: "the boy requires an audience"). **Fears / flaw:** he jumps first and lets his pride run wild (canon: "Leaps before he looks"; O.C.O.'s "already enormous pride" [Canon, channel unconfirmed] ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AA6tYQ81aiQ))). **Contradiction that makes them funny:** he causes the emergency, then loudly rescues everyone from it (canon: "save the very day he has endangered").
- **Core values:** improvising and style over planning (canon: "Great at improvising"). **What they'd never do:** solve a problem quietly (canon: "absolutely most action packed way"), or hand the credit to someone else [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy engine:** a chaos agent with a cool-guy veneer. He builds the joke, sets it off, then plays the fallout "like a boss" [Canon, channel unconfirmed] ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJZuwYEq1y8)). He works best opposite a straight man (Riggs, Astoria, Graham) or a willing victim (Wil) [Inference] (canon: Astoria vs "Oliver and Wil's childish antics").

## Movement & acting

- **Posture and idle:** a loose, chest-out swagger; he struts when watched and tinkers at rest [BBG News invention].
- **Fighting:** a "jack of all trades" [Canon] ([characters](https://battlebears.com/pages/characters)) whose Bearzooka is "easy to kill yourself with" [Game text via wiki] ([Template](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Template:Class:Soldier_(BBG))).
- **Pain and victory:** a hug leaves spiral "dizzy" eyes [Observed] ([frame](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/9LEbWq27ThQ/hq1.jpg)). Show sparks, never blood [BBG News invention]. On a win: "Woohoo! Way to go, me!" [Fan-wiki].
- **Taunts** [Fan-wiki] ([Taunts](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Taunts)): "Gang Style" (riding an imaginary horse), "Cheer" (jumping fist pumps) and "Worm" (wiggling on the floor).
- **Comedy beats** [BBG News invention]:
  1. Bearzooka recoil blasts him through a wall. He comes back sooty: "Meant to do that."
  2. A stolen-parts gadget works once, then explodes, leaving only his eyes blinking in the smoke.
  3. He poses for a camera mid-fight and gets hugged from behind. Spiral eyes, hard cut.

## Story engine

- **Best format:** he causes the problem, saves the day and claims the credit, then the short cuts out abruptly [Inference], like SkyVu's "Hugged to DEATH!" endings [Canon] ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7IBdqMm5nKg)). Arena matches contradict nothing official [Inference].
- **Loglines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. **Bearzooka Safety Week:** Oliver films a recruit safety video and demonstrates every rule by breaking it. Graham's freshly repaired wall does not survive.
  2. **Hey Wil:** Oliver dares Wil to sneak into a Huggable nest "and see what happens" (a riff on Wil's canon card). Wil strolls out with snacks and a medal, and Oliver gets hugged.
  3. **Highlight Reel:** Thrilled that B-1000 is recording combat data, Oliver keeps redoing takedowns for better angles until the robot's memory is full.
