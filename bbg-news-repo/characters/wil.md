# Wil, the Chub Scout

> A naive computer whiz so busy obsessing over something else that he strolls through danger without noticing it, is easily talked into Oliver's dares, and keeps beating whoever underestimates him while ABBi, his mocking robot backpack, heckles him.

## Canon anchor

1. "Naïve computer savant. Wil is absolutely fearless – but not by being brave – he's simply oblivious to the danger because he's too busy obsessing over something else at the time. It's easy to talk Wil into doing just about anything, he's always eager to please a friend. 'Hey Wil – go sneak into that Grubfang Den and see what happens,' is something you hear a lot (usually from Oliver)." [Canon card, c. 2012] ([File:Wil_description.jpg][card])
2. "The Chub Scout, known as the masticator of mayhem, thrives on balancing the battlefield with his insatiable appetite for destruction. Many have made the mistake of underestimating Wil, many have faced the consequences. Just ask Tillman. … His Ultimate, 'Sarcastic Air Strike,' features A.B.B.I., his mocking robot backpack, executing a devastating airstrike." [Canon, BBH-era site text] ([characters][chars])
3. "BB0 is a maddening and hilarious adventure of a hapless bear named Wil burdened with saving the universe." [Canon, 2012] ([archived BB Zero page][zero])
4. "…should not be let within ten yards of heavy machinery or high explosives… He's talking to us with crayon drawings…" [Canon] ([Wil's Finest Introduction][intro])

## Soul

- **Wants:** to please friends (canon: "always eager to please a friend"); snacks and games (fan-wiki: "loves to eat food of all sorts"; loves ABBi's "MULTICHROMATIC MEMORY GAME" ([Wil][ww]; [Campaign][wcamp]); food is only implied officially [Inference]).  **Fears / flaw:** oblivious and easily talked into anything (canon card). The wiki ("scared of everything") and press ("timid" [Press] ([TouchArcade][ta])) disagree, so he is fearless until he looks up, then panics [BBG News invention].  **Contradiction that makes them funny:** a "hapless" bumbler who is a "computer savant" and beat Tillman (canon: card; BB Zero; "Just ask Tillman").
- **Core values:** pleasing his friends (canon card: "always eager to please a friend").  **What they'd never do:** curse (fan-wiki: "the only Battle Bear not to have been heard cursing" ([Wil][ww])), refuse a friend's dare (from the card's "easy to talk Wil into doing just about anything" [Inference]), or a snack [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy engine:** the oblivious chaos agent: dared in, he wins or wrecks things by accident and screams late while ABBi, Oliver, Riggs or Astoria play straight (canon: "ten yards of heavy machinery"; staging [Inference]).

## Voice

- **Delivery:** a childlike kid gamer: giddy wins, whiny pain, tattletale, sore loser; gamer slang, eating noises, nub-hand excuses, rapid-repeat panic [Inference from fan-transcribed barks; audio unheard]. Never swears [Fan-wiki] ([Wil][ww]); SkyVu plays censored swearing for laughs ("WARNING! FCC Censorship!" [Canon] ([FCC Censorship][fcc]); audio unheard), so where others would swear, Wil says "fudge-muffins!" [BBG News invention].
- **Known lines** [Fan-wiki, fan-transcribed; verify against the [Slinkster rip][rip]] ([Chub Scout (BBG)][ws]; [Boomstick McShootington][wboom]): "I did it? I did it! HAHA!" · "Hurts, don't it? ... Mmmm... donut." · "I'm telling!" · "But I'm... Still... Hungry..." · "Stupid, nub hands!" · "This game CHEATS!" · "Woooah, helpmehelpmehelpme!" · "OH, HEY, A SHOTGUN!" (BB-1)
- **New lines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. "Oliver says this button makes nachos. Oliver's never wrong. ...Why is it counting down?"
  2. "Aw, fudge-muffins! ...Now I want a fudge muffin."
  3. "Wait, there were Huggables? Sorry, guys. I was in the snack zone."
  4. "Boomstick McShootington, say hi to the nice pink bears!"
  5. "High score! ...ABBi, why do you keep calling it 'property damage'?"
- **Casting note:** an original light, youthful, slightly nasal male voice; bouncy, eager, rising inflections; squeaks in panic [BBG News invention]. Credited voices Marc Cohen (Wil, Tillman) and Leah Smith (ABBi) [Canon] ([BBR credits][credits]) are listed for credit only, not as a casting or sound-alike target; never clone or imitate a real person's voice, and do not feed their recordings to a voice tool. One performer for Wil and Tillman mirrors the original [Inference].

## Relationships

- **Oliver:** dares him ("usually from Oliver") [Canon card] ([card][card]); saves the day Wil endangered in BLAST [Canon] ([BLAST intro][blast]). Best friends, roommates [Fan-wiki] ([Wil][ww]).
- **Riggs:** "looks after his team" [Canon card] ([card][rcard]); Wil's BB-1 campaign begins after Oliver and Riggs "have already safely escaped" [Press] ([TouchArcade][ta]). Protective drill sergeant, guilty conscience [BBG News invention].
- **Tillman:** "Just ask Tillman" [Canon] ([characters][chars]); "unlike Wil, he knows exactly what he's getting into" [Canon card] ([card][tcard]). Beaten by Wil in BB Zero [Fan-wiki] ([Tillman][wt]). Tillman's grudge is [Inference]; Wil has forgotten the fight and never notices Tillman's rematch demands [BBG News invention].
- **Huggable:** "WIL forgets to clear the holodeck of HUGGABLES" [Canon] ([BLAST intro][blast]). He hugs the playable one back and offers it a sandwich for another [BBG News invention].
- **Astoria:** "spares little time for Oliver and Wil's childish antics" but "really does care for the Battle Bears" [Canon] ([characters][chars]). He never notices her coldness, which disarms her [BBG News invention].
- **Graham:** no official link, but SkyVu says Wil "should not be let within ten yards of heavy machinery" [Canon] ([Wil's Finest Introduction][intro]). Graham paints the line and keeps Wil behind it [BBG News invention].
- **B-1000:** wants to "share their data with villains" [Canon] ([characters][chars]); his scans of Wil return only snack data [BBG News invention].
- **Botch:** no official link. Wil chatters, Botch answers in one word, and Wil thinks the Orbs are pets [BBG News invention].
- **Saberi:** no official link. The "momma bear" [Canon] ([characters][chars]) mothers him and patches him up [BBG News invention].
- **Sanchez:** no official link. Wil keeps begging for the crash story, which grows each telling [BBG News invention].
- **ABBi:** "the unwilling back-pack companion" [Canon] ([BB Zero page][zero]); in BB-1 she torments him and tricks him into pressing self-destruct [Fan-wiki] ([ABBi][wabbi]; [Campaign][wcamp]).
- **H.I.P.:** he fights "through the H.I.P. complex" [Canon] ([BB Zero page][zero]) and traps H.I.P.'s robot Rho in a toy console [Fan-wiki] ([Rho][wrho]).
- **Jackson / Captain Malcolm:** in BB-1 he sees Jackson hugged to death; Malcolm escorts him and Oliver to safety [Fan-wiki] ([Campaign][wcamp]).
- **Nyx / Necromancer:** no documented link to Wil.

## Look (for animation)

- **Default era: BBG 2013,** in the shared BBG News house style (see [README](README.md)). Exception: no researcher has described his BBG 2013 in-game default, so until it is checked the model uses the closest observed proxies. Body from SkyVu's c. 2012 card [Observed] ([card][card]); outfit from the March 2012 BBR animation-test frame, "a brown bear in a red cap, white shirt and a grey backpack" [Observed, low-res] ([frame][movesframe]); the wiki puts ABBi on his BBG default skin as the backpack [Fan-wiki] ([ABBi][wabbi]). The research never describes his BBG trailer card ([Steam][steam]) or the 2026 playtest showcase ([fan upload][showcase]); check both before locking the model. **Alternatives** [Observed]: wide-eyed BLAST 2D ([frame][blastframe]); the scarred Zero teaser bear (Wil by [Inference]) ([frame][zeroframe]); the 2024 BBH portrait, big round eyes, tiny "o" mouth ([icon][icon]).
- **Silhouette & height:** pear-shaped, big belly, small rounded ears, tiny round "nub" hands [Observed]; "hands(nubs)" [Canon] ([Wil's Finest Hour][hour]). No official scale; on the shared BBG News scale chart he is ~0.9× Oliver's height and rounder than Oliver (Tillman, at the same height, is wider), a head above the Huggable [BBG News invention].
- **Colours:** researcher's estimates from the 2024 portrait, not official [Inference]: fur #D8AC57, mid #B88A46, shadow #754F26, light #DFCA95. Unsampled picks [BBG News invention]: cap #C8352E, shirt #F2EFE6, ABBi grey #8B9299, eyes #141414, ice pop #3FA7E0.
- **Eyes & body:** small beady black eyes [Observed], wide for takes [BBG News invention]; wiring shows when he is hurt [Fan-wiki] ([Wil][ww]), gag use only.
- **ABBi:** a single camera eye [Fan-wiki] ([ABBi][wabbi]); probably the Zero teaser's one-eyed, minigun-armed unit [Observed; Inference].
- **Weapons & props:** default Boomstick [Fan-wiki] ([Chub Scout (BBG)][ws]), named in BBH [Canon] ([characters][chars]); BB-1's "arrow that he pulled from his leg" and "Boomstick McShootington" [Press] ([TouchArcade][ta]); Senor Potato, Pie Bomb, Traffic Light [Fan-wiki]; blue ice pop [Observed].
- **Signature skins** [Fan-wiki] ([Chub Scout (BBG)][ws]): Gold Wil, Old Timey, Monster Hoodie, Lunchador, Sleepover, Chub Scout.

## Movement & acting

- **Posture & idle:** belly forward, absorbed in a snack (card [Observed]; "obsessing over something else" [Canon card]).
- **Walk:** "Wil's lower body animates independently from his upper body so that weird puttin' he's got going with his nub-legs … is COMPLETELY NATURAL" [Canon] ([Wil's Got MOVES][moves]); play it as fast legs under a calm torso [Inference].
- **Fighting:** "a highly situational class" with "a wide variety of explosive weapons" [Fan-wiki] ([Chub Scout (BBG)][ws]); ABBi's "Sarcastic Air Strike" [Canon] ([characters][chars]).
- **Pain / win / loss** [Fan-wiki] ([Chub Scout (BBG)][ws]): "I'm telling!" / "Yeah, I was pretty good." / "Woah, I wasn't even trying anyway."
- **Taunts** [Fan-wiki] ([Taunts][wtaunt]): Truffle Shuffle (wiggle, belly slap), Laughing (hysterical, belly slap, point), Planking (a 360° spin flat on his belly).
- **Comedy beats** [BBG News invention]: (1) **Delayed panic:** he munches through a firefight, every blast missing, reaches cover, looks back, then screams. (2) **Nub button:** one nub presses four buttons; ABBi sighs; something explodes. (3) **Split body:** his torso calmly finishes an ice pop while his legs sprint in a blur.

## Story engine

- **Best fit:** 15–30 s single-gag shorts: dare, oblivious calm, accidental win or wreck, delayed scream, ABBi undercutting; stage arena fights as training bouts [Inference].
- **Loglines** [BBG News invention]:
  1. **Ten Yards:** Graham paints a ten-yard line around his workshop. Wil obeys it perfectly, "helps" from his tablet, and turns the Turret into a popcorn cannon.
  2. **Data Harvest:** B-1000 corners Wil to scan the Battle Bears' secrets and downloads 4,000 snack rankings, which crash his uplink mid-brag.
  3. **Multichromatic:** a blinking colour-memory terminal in H.I.P. Test Lab calls to Wil. ABBi begs him not to play. He wins, and the whole lab powers down.
