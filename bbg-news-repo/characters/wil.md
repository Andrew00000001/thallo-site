# Wil, the Chub Scout

> A naive computer whiz so busy obsessing over something else that he strolls through danger without noticing it, is easily talked into Oliver's dares, and keeps beating whoever underestimates him while ABBi, his mocking robot backpack, heckles him.

## Canon anchor

1. "Naïve computer savant. Wil is absolutely fearless – but not by being brave – he's simply oblivious to the danger because he's too busy obsessing over something else at the time. It's easy to talk Wil into doing just about anything, he's always eager to please a friend. 'Hey Wil – go sneak into that Grubfang Den and see what happens,' is something you hear a lot (usually from Oliver)." [Canon card, c. 2012] ([File:Wil_description.jpg][card])
2. "The Chub Scout, known as the masticator of mayhem, thrives on balancing the battlefield with his insatiable appetite for destruction. Many have made the mistake of underestimating Wil, many have faced the consequences. Just ask Tillman. … His Ultimate, 'Sarcastic Air Strike,' features A.B.B.I., his mocking robot backpack, executing a devastating airstrike, raining destruction across the battlefield." [Canon, battlebears.com site text] ([characters][chars]; live text fetched by the researcher on 2026-10-18, and the saved copy re-read for this rebuild).
   - **Era tag:** the kit sentences are BBH (2023–) only. The page names no game, so the BBH tie is Inference from its "Ultimate" wording. The elided sentence, "Wil's 'Boomstick' attack launches explosive pellets that burst on impact, covering a wide area.", describes the BBH-only Boomstick; the BBG Boomstick never explodes (see Weapons).
   - **Character lines:** "masticator of mayhem" and "Just ask Tillman" describe Wil himself and are not tied to one game [Inference]. "The masticator of mayhem" also opens his BBG class blurb [Game text via wiki].
3. "BB0 is a maddening and hilarious adventure of a hapless bear named Wil burdened with saving the universe." [Canon, 2012] ([archived BB Zero page][zero])
4. "…should not be let within ten yards of heavy machinery or high explosives… He's talking to us with crayon drawings…" [Canon] ([Wil's Finest Introduction][intro])

## Soul

- **Wants:** to please friends (canon: "always eager to please a friend"); snacks and games (fan-wiki: "loves to eat food of all sorts"; loves ABBi's "MULTICHROMATIC MEMORY GAME" ([Wil][ww]; [Campaign][wcamp]); food is only implied officially [Inference]).  **Fears / flaw:** oblivious and easily talked into anything (canon card). The wiki ("scared of everything") and press ("timid" [Press] ([TouchArcade][ta])) disagree, so he is fearless until he looks up, then panics [BBG News invention].  **Contradiction that makes them funny:** a "hapless" bumbler who is a "computer savant" and beat Tillman (canon: card; BB Zero; "Just ask Tillman").
- **Core values:** pleasing his friends (canon card: "always eager to please a friend").  **What they'd never do:** curse (fan-wiki: "the only Battle Bear not to have been heard cursing" ([Wil][ww])), refuse a friend's dare (from the card's "easy to talk Wil into doing just about anything" [Inference]), or a snack [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy engine:** the oblivious chaos agent: dared in, he wins or wrecks things by accident and screams late while ABBi, Oliver, Riggs or Astoria play straight (canon: "ten yards of heavy machinery"; staging [Inference]).

## Movement & acting

- **Timing:** feature-film snappy, per STYLE.md: strong key poses, quick moves and clear holds with squash and stretch; no floaty drift or wandering camera. Wil's best holds are the oblivious munch before the danger lands, the frozen wide-eyed look-back, the "too hot" tear-streaming face, and the fully inflated beach-ball pose before a pop [BBG News invention].
- **Posture & idle:** belly forward, absorbed in a snack (card [Observed]; "obsessing over something else" [Canon card]).
- **Walk:** "Wil's lower body animates independently from his upper body so that weird puttin' he's got going with his nub-legs … is COMPLETELY NATURAL" [Canon] ([Wil's Got MOVES][moves]); play it as fast legs under a calm torso [Inference].
- **Carry poses** [Observed]: the Boomstick two-handed across the belly; the Batterito and Sole's Bane hugged at the belly; the Killdoba hugged to the chest; the EZ Chee-Z and Whipped Pain cans and the Pop Tart held up in one hand; the Pie Bomb flat on one nub hand like a waiter's plate; the Balance Beam level at the waist; the Señor Potato at the hip; the Bow upright at his side; the Traffic Light shouldered like a mace.

  - **Class:** "a highly situational class" with "a wide variety of explosive weapons" [Fan-wiki] ([Chub Scout (BBG)][ws]).
