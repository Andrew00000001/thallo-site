# Wil, the Chub Scout

> A naive computer whiz so busy obsessing over something else that he strolls through danger without noticing it, is easily talked into Oliver's dares, and keeps beating whoever underestimates him while ABBi, his mocking robot backpack, heckles him.

## Canon anchor

1. "Naïve computer savant. Wil is absolutely fearless – but not by being brave – he's simply oblivious to the danger because he's too busy obsessing over something else at the time. It's easy to talk Wil into doing just about anything, he's always eager to please a friend. 'Hey Wil – go sneak into that Grubfang Den and see what happens,' is something you hear a lot (usually from Oliver)." [Canon card, c. 2012] ([File:Wil_description.jpg][card])
2. "The Chub Scout, known as the masticator of mayhem, thrives on balancing the battlefield with his insatiable appetite for destruction. Just ask Tillman. … His Ultimate, 'Sarcastic Air Strike,' features A.B.B.I., his mocking robot backpack, executing a devastating airstrike, raining destruction across the battlefield." [Canon, battlebears.com site text] ([characters][chars]; live text fetched by the researcher on 2026-10-18, and the saved copy re-read for this rebuild).

## Soul

- **Wants:** to please friends ("always eager to please a friend" [Canon card]); snacks and games ("loves to eat food of all sorts" [Fan-wiki] ([Wil][ww]); he loves ABBi's "MULTICHROMATIC MEMORY GAME" [Fan-wiki] ([Campaign][wcamp]); food is only implied officially [Inference]).  **Fears / flaw:** oblivious and easily talked into anything [Canon card]. The wiki ("scared of everything" [Fan-wiki]) and press ("timid" [Press] ([TouchArcade][ta])) disagree, so he is fearless until he looks up, then panics [BBG News invention].  **Contradiction that makes them funny:** a "hapless" bumbler [Canon, 2012] who is a "computer savant" [Canon card] and beat Tillman ("Just ask Tillman" [Canon]; the BB Zero win [Fan-wiki]).
- **Core values:** pleasing his friends ("always eager to please a friend" [Canon card]).  **What they'd never do:** curse ("the only Battle Bear not to have been heard cursing" [Fan-wiki] ([Wil][ww])), refuse a friend's dare (from the card's "easy to talk Wil into doing just about anything" [Inference]), or a snack [BBG News invention].
- **Comedy engine:** the oblivious chaos agent: dared in, he wins or wrecks things by accident and screams late while ABBi, Oliver, Riggs or Astoria play straight ("ten yards of heavy machinery" [Canon]; the staging [Inference]).
