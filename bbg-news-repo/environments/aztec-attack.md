Built for BBG News shorts in the feature-film 3D house style (`characters/STYLE.md`). ## 1. What it is

- **Name and spelling:** "Aztec Attack", as written in OST track 13 "Aztec Attack", 1:20 [Canon]. It was added as "Facing Temples" in BBR v1.3.1 (June 28, 2012) [Game text via wiki] and renamed at the 2013 BBG relaunch [Fan-wiki] (Dossier: Era and status).
- **Setting:** a night-time arena. Two stepped sandstone temples face each other across a cracked orange plateau island in a lava sea, with a lava pool at the island's waist [Observed]. The wiki calls it an "island-like field surrounded by lava" [Fan-wiki].
- **2026 status:** SkyVu's store text does not name the map [Observed]. It ran in all four 2026 playtest modes: KC, PTB and FFA were observed, and TB is inferred [Observed / Inference] (Addendum §2). Inclusion at launch is very likely [Inference, strong]. The launch modes are [Unverified]. The Steam trailer's Aztec shots are 2013 footage [Inference, strong], so caption them "classic 2013 look".
  - **Official 2026 sources with Aztec footage** [Canon: titles and descriptions; Observed: frames] (Addendum §2; Overview §2.3): the Official Playtest Highlights video (C7uFxvSZ10c, about 1:48–1:54); the Sep 20 livestream (ejjsoKQ-VXU); "BBG Maps Ranked part 1" (jkv6FHPghx8, about 0:59–1:04, lava and sandstone under a magenta sky). These are the best official exterior references, but all predate the Oct 2026 lighting pass, so none proves the 2026 look (Overview §5.1 item 3).

## 2. Layout for staging

- **Shape:** a peanut- or hourglass-shaped island. Its long axis runs from temple to temple, and the island narrows at the waist where the pool sits [Observed: top-down; trailer aerial]. The layout is **point-symmetric (a 180° turn), not mirrored** [Observed] (Dossier: Layout, Shape).
- **Spawns:**
  - "Each team's base is on the inside of each temple" [Fan-wiki].
  - The top-down render has team markers at the side-stair ends, the stair landings and the front stairs [Observed]. Reading them as spawns is [Inference].
  - The 2026 spawn rooms are enclosed chambers inside the temples [Observed] (Addendum §3).
  - In FFA a player spawns at either team's spawn point [Fan-wiki] (Conventions §4).
  - Respawn shield: 4.5 s [Fan-wiki]. Pale-green circling shields are seen in a 2013 Aztec frame, but whether that is the respawn shield or the Shield pickup is [Unverified] (Conventions §4). The respawn look is observed in a 2026 storyboard on another map (Overview §3, 1MP1rFi4t-Q 39:50–40:40) [Observed].
- **Teams:** the red team takes the temple with red-glowing gem panels and the gold floor disc. The blue team takes the temple with teal and multicolour gems and the silver disc [Inference, well supported] (Addendum §3). A red lead's home temple is therefore the red-gem one. Team colour appears only as accents (Overview §3).
- **Lanes** [Observed: top-down, with Inference] (Dossier: Layout, Routes):
  1. The left flank, past the left ruin.
  2. The right flank, past the right ruin.
  3. Four corner pads at the pool's corners, which launch lengthwise toward the temples.
  4. The stepping-stone causeway, which runs from ruin to ruin across the central rock. The rock's two pads launch toward the temples [Observed; Fan-wiki].
  5. The vertical route: a chain of four pads up each temple's back terraces to the summit [Observed: top-down; Fan-wiki: "a series of jump pads"].
- **Vertical spots:**
  - The **summit**, a square grey platform that overlooks the whole island [Observed: top-down]. A sniper is seen on a high grey platform looking across the pool [Observed]; that this is the summit is [Inference].
  - The **mid-level landing** at the temple doorway [Observed].
  - The **obsidian rock** in the pool [Observed].
  - The **ruin roofs**, "usually only accessible by using So Fly or Metal Crush" [Fan-wiki].
- **Sightlines:**
  - Temple to temple down the long axis, across the pool. This is the original "Facing Temples" idea [Observed].
  - Summit to the far temple and the lava sea [Observed / Inference as above].
  - From a spawn-room doorway out over the lava (2026) [Observed: PT2 3:20 frame] (Addendum §3).
  - The ruins sit beside the pool as cover [Observed].
- **Where the camera can sit:**
  - **High aerial** over the island, as in the trailer at about 43.1–45.5 s: Steam trailer (2013 footage [Inference, strong]); shot content [Observed].
  - **Low angle at the foot of the grand staircase**, looking up, as in the trailer at about 12.25–16.4 s: Steam trailer (2013 footage [Inference, strong]); shot content [Observed].
  - **Ground level near one temple**, looking across the pool at the other, much like the May 2013 capture [Observed; composition BBG News invention].
  - **Summit perch**, looking down over the pool [Observed / Inference].
  - **Eye level on the stepping stones**, with lava all round [BBG News invention].
  - **Inside a spawn room**, looking out of the doorway. This is 2026-era only; see section 7 [Observed].
- **Framing:** STYLE.md (Frame): in 9:16, "keep faces, sprites and the end card out of the bottom fifth and the right edge, where the Reels interface sits, and leave headroom over any character who will carry a status sprite" [house rule]. Extending this to the key landmark and the staging ground is [BBG News invention]. Use one landmark per shot (STYLE.md: Sets and light; Overview §5.3 item 13); the establishing shot is the one sanctioned multi-landmark shot.
- **Scale:**
  - Doors are about 1.5–2× a bear's height, and rails sit at knee to waist height [Inference] (Overview §3).
  - Making each stepping stone about one bear wide, so a bear can stand on it, is [BBG News invention]. Their true size is [Open].

## 3. Look in the house style

**Shapes and materials (re-rendered per STYLE.md: soft rounded forms, bold readable silhouettes, no outlines, never "low-poly")** (Dossier: Look; Overview §5.3 items 10–12)
- **Temples:**
  - Warm sandstone blocks with softly bevelled edges.
  - Swirl and scroll reliefs carved deep and rounded.
  - Chunky raised stepped-fret bands along the stair rails and slopes.
  - Smooth matte grey carved stone with dark swirl relief on every flat surface: terrace tops, the summit and the ruin roofs [Observed].
  - Rounded grey cobbles on the side-stair wings [Observed].
- **Mask faces** (on the cubes, in the wall panels): grimacing carved masks with fangs and ear-spools [Observed]. Render them as chunky, appealing cartoon carving, not horror [BBG News invention].
- **Gems:** rounded, glossy cabochon gems set in scroll frames [Observed]. Glow is observed only on the red temple's panels and corner lights and on the teal corner gems [Observed]. A soft inner glow on the other panel gems (teal, pink, blue) is [BBG News invention], allowed under Overview §5.3 item 11 (gem glows as identity anchors). Render every glow with a gentle bloom, following STYLE.md's "Glows" rule.
- **Obsidian rock:** faceted, near-black, with dark-red veins [Observed]. Render it as glossy cut facets with smooth bevelled edges [BBG News invention, by analogy with STYLE.md's faceted-effects rule].
- **Stepping stones:** smaller dark stones [Observed]; making them faceted and veined like the rock is [BBG News invention].
- **Lava:**
  - A glossy molten surface, yellow-hot at the core and orange toward the edges, with red marbling [Observed].
  - It is the brightest thing in frame, with a soft emissive bloom.
  - No smoke or embers appear in any still [Observed absence]. Faint heat shimmer is optional [BBG News invention].
- **Sand:** packed burnt-orange ground with soft, rounded cracks [Observed].
- **Jump pads:**
  - Clean painted dark-metal octagons with a yellow-and-black hazard rim and a glowing cyan crystalline star core, under faint stacked cyan octagon rings [Observed] (Conventions §7).
  - From a distance they read as glowing cyan discs [Observed].
- **Spawn rooms (2026):**
  - Cool slate-grey carved stone with brick courses at the wall bases [Observed].
  - A large pale silver (or gold) disc set into the grey flagstones, engraved with stars, cloud swirls and a clock-tick ring (silver) or sun rays (gold) [Observed, low-res: 320–429 px storyboards] (Addendum §3). A polished metal finish is [BBG News invention].

**Lighting**
- **Classic 2013 look (the default):**
  - Night in every image, with stars and no sun or moon [Observed].
  - The light reads as coming from the lava. Sand and walls glow saturated orange, and shading is soft and low-contrast [Observed].
  - House rig: lava underglow is the key (Overview §5.3 item 11), with a cool fill from the starry sky and a gentle rim (STYLE.md: Sets and light). Naming the sky as the fill source is [BBG News invention]. Never write a moonlit fill.
  - Keep one rig per scene. Weapon flashes may tint characters within a shot.
- **2026 builds before the pass (Jul–Sep playtests)** [Observed, low-res]:
  - Exteriors: the same lava-lit family under a magenta sky.
  - Spawn rooms: dim, cool walls and a brightly lit floor disc, with warm lava light spilling in at the doorways. There are no torches or lamps.
  - What lights the discs is [Unverified]. A skylight is [Inference].
  - Interior rig: the bright pool of light on the disc and the doorway lava spill as the key, a cool grey fill and a gentle rim [BBG News invention].
- **Oct 2026 lighting pass** ("Lighting improvements" [Canon]; banner "LIGHTING, EFFECT & TEXTURE IMPROVEMENTS" [Observed]):
  - No before/after was measured for Aztec. The Conv. addendum §5 covers only No Bear's Land, Desert Airmine, Space Oddity and Frozen Pundra.
  - Oct 15 frames show a magenta sky through the doorways [Observed, low-res]. One PT5 lava sample reads yellower (#F6BC22) than one PT3 sample (#DD780F) [Inference, from hexes across different captures]; no before/after change is claimed.
  - They also show a warm brown-sandstone floor around the red spawn's gold disc, where earlier frames read grey [Observed]. Whether that comes from the pass or from capture differences is [Unverified].
  - So the post-pass look is [Open].

## 5. Hazards, gags and easter eggs

**Verified**
- **Lava:** "If a player falls into the lava, it will be counted as a suicide" [Fan-wiki]. Both the pool and the sea are lava [Observed]. That the sea also kills is [Inference, likely].
  - In a short, a lava fall ends in an intact cartoon pop or pratfall, and the game's "SUICIDE" wording stays off screen (Overview §5.5 item 16).
- **Jump pads:** moving in mid-air "affects the landing point" [Fan-wiki]. A bug stalls players mid-flight on this map [Fan-wiki] (Conventions §7).
  - The pad count is 12 per the wiki [Fan-wiki] but 14 markers on the top-down [Observed]. The true count is [Open].
- **Plant the Bomb:**
  - The carrier "cannot attack nor use special equipment" but can use jump pads [Fan-wiki].
  - If the carrier falls into a suicide pit, the bomb explodes at once [Fan-wiki] (Conventions §8).
  - "In Plant the Bomb the bombs are in the small buildings to the sides of the map" [Fan-wiki, added 2014], so each ruin holds a bomb as well as a pickup (Dossier: Official text; Era and status). Where the tubes sit on Aztec is undocumented [Unverified]. The 2026 red-plinth device inside a temple room is only a tube candidate [Inference].
  - **Blast:** a huge pale-grey faceted cloud [Observed: Plant_the_bombbbbbb_b.jpeg, Aztec Attack, May 2013] (Conventions §8). It deals "huge, but survivable, damage", and the deaths count as suicides [Fan-wiki]. Render it per STYLE.md's faceted-effects rule as crisp cut-crystal facets with smooth bevelled edges inside an otherwise smooth shot, swap "low-poly" for "low-poly character models" in any shot that has it, and drop "smoke plumes" in that shot so the negative does not block it. The kill feed's "SUICIDE" lines stay off screen.
  - 2026 PTB rounds on this map run 5 minutes [Observed] (Addendum §2).
- **Glitches** [Fan-wiki] (Dossier: Hazards):
  - A former glitch (fixed by BBR 1.4.2 [Fan-wiki]) let Mini Me squeeze under a pyramid through the back pillars.
  - A gap at the third level, by the third pad.
  - An invisible barrier on the right steps.
  - A mid-air stall that reaches the ruin roofs.
- **No switches, traps or moving parts** are documented [Unverified]. Do not invent one and present it as real (Overview §5.1 item 2).
- **No fire set dressing.** Flames on a temple top in one fan thumbnail are a weapon glitch (Killdoba) [Inference]. Add no braziers or torches.

**Easter eggs**
- **Over Clocked Oliver and Astoria temple symbols** "at each team's spawn points" [Fan-wiki; Unverified].
  - The claim was added on 2013-07-06. No image from 2012 to 2026 shows them [Observed absence].
  - The floor discs are the likeliest candidates [Inference].
  - Never show, carve or caption them as fact (Overview §5.1 item 1).
- **"Facing Temples" as a nod to Unreal Tournament's Facing Worlds:** fan speculation added in 2025 [Fan-wiki].
- **The pyramids resemble the Zombocalypse pyramid** [Fan-wiki]. This is a shape echo only [Inference].

**Gag ideas** (all [BBG News invention], built on the facts above; cartoon violence only)
1. **The floor is lava:** two bears race across the stepping stones for the sandwich on the rock.
2. **Over-steer:** a bear rides the back pad chain, steers in mid-air (a documented mechanic) and lands in a heroic pose on the summit, facing the wrong way.
3. **The pulsing bomb:** a carrier grabs the bomb in a ruin (the wiki's bomb spot [Fan-wiki]) and, unable to fight back, pad-hops over the lava with it pulsing. A slip ends in the map's observed huge pale-grey faceted blast cloud (see Plant the Bomb above) and an intact cartoon pratfall, never injury.
4. **Ambush at the ruins:** a "who's behind the pillar" beat at a ruined pavilion.
5. **Spawn-room beats (2026 shorts only):** a respawn on the glowing disc inside the circling green shields; a silhouette in the doorway against the lava.
6. **The temple is watching:** an insert reaction shot to the giant gold mask face. Don't place it on the map, because its location is [Unverified].
