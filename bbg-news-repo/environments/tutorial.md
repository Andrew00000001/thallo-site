# Tutorial: house-style environment sheet

This sheet is for BBG News shorts in the feature-film 3D house style (`characters/STYLE.md`). Facts come from the verified maps research in `research_notes/BBG maps (verified)/`, and each keeps its source label. Short names used below: **Dossier** = `tutorial.md` (there is no Tutorial addendum); **Lineup addendum** = `2026-lineup.addendum.md`, gap pass 2, §5 "Tutorial in 2026" (the Oct 15, 2026 build); **Overview** = `00_OVERVIEW.md`; **Conventions** = `environment-conventions.md`; **Story** = `story-locations.md`. Hex values are approximate (about ±15%), sampled from compressed images [Inference] (Overview §0 item 4). The 3.0.7 values (#41D02C, #0B5406, #031602, #C98941, #A13CA6) come from 320×180 ZM storyboard stills [Observed samples] (Lineup addendum §5). The other values tagged "2026" come from Jul–Sep 2026 fan thumbnails (pre-Oct-15 builds such as 2.54.0), before the Oct 15 pass, so they are not 2026-look values.

**What this set is not:** a PvP arena. It is a single-player game mode with one player, Oliver, on a linear course [Fan-wiki] (Dossier: Era and status, "Mode and loadout"). Any second bear or Huggable in a Tutorial short is a [BBG News invention] and must be staged as one.

---

## 1. What it is

- **Name and spelling:** "the Tutorial". SkyVu's 2026 soundtrack lists track 18 as "Tutorial" (0:32), filed among the menu tracks [Canon]. The mode card reads "TUTORIAL" [Observed], and so does the Options button in the 2026 final-playtest build [Observed] (Dossier: Official text; Lineup addendum §5). It is a game mode, not one of the maps: do not count it toward Steam's "16 wacky maps" [Inference] (Overview §2.3, "Not counted").
- **Setting:** a short, linear "virtual" training course [Fan-wiki] in an endless black void. Columns of green glyphs fill the void, and the course is built from neon-green walkways, floating circuit-etched blocks, amber instruction signs, lattice practice dummies and red laser gates. It ends at a row of standard jump pads before a green line-lit hologram of the Pelican't drop ship [Observed]. There is no sky and no time of day [Observed] (Dossier: Setting; Look).
- **Era and 2026 status:** it dates from BBR. Its card is on SkyVu's Aug 31, 2011 Sneak Peek menu [Observed; Canon video], and the arena is first seen in a fan video of Apr 18, 2012 [Observed]. It carried into BBG in 2013 [Fan-wiki]: "The transition from Battle Bears Royale marked several minor changes to the Tutorial" [Fan-wiki] (Conventions §2), and "Over time, certain signs also changed position" [Fan-wiki, added Sep 4, 2013] (Dossier: Era and status, "Changes the wiki records"). The environment and the start view look the same in captures from 2012 to Sep 2026 [Observed], but station and sign placement can differ by era, so match each frame to the era captioned. It is still in the Oct 15, 2026 final-playtest build (3.0.7): an Options "TUTORIAL" button and an end-of-test "PLAY TUTORIAL" prompt are [Observed]; that these, not the PICK LEVEL carousel, are the route in is [Inference] (Lineup addendum §5; the Overview §2.3 labels the route [Observed]). Whether the Oct 15 lighting pass changed its look is [Unverified] (section 3). Whether it is in the Nov 4 launch build is [Unverified], though likely [Inference] (Dossier: Era and status, "2026 status"). It is **not** in the Steam trailer or the Steam screenshots [Observed by measurement] (Dossier: Images viewed).

## 2. Layout for staging

(Dossier: Layout; Hazards and interactive elements)

From outside the barriers (a Jul 17, 2012 out-of-bounds capture) it reads as a chain of zig-zag, neon-edged walkways and platforms floating in the void, with the hologram ship nearby [Observed, low-res].
- **Start (the spawn):** one start point, with the same view in 2012, 2013, 2025 and Sep 2026 [Observed]. The player stands on the open dark-grid floor, facing an amber "MOVE" panel between two bracket-shaped neon-green posts. A big floating "MOVING" label hangs upper left on a yellow leader line, and the hologram ship hangs far ahead in its rings. The labels "SHOOTING" and "MELEE/ WEAPON SWITCH" float to the left, "PICKUPS" and "USE SPECIAL ITEM" to the right, and "OPTIONS" high right beside a raised walkway that ramps up across the upper right [Observed]. ## 3. Look in the house style

**Shapes and materials.** Keep the layout, landmark silhouettes, material identity, palette family and light sources. Re-render everything soft, rounded and feature-film, with no ink outlines, and never write "low-poly" in a prompt (Overview §5.3 items 10–12). The game's materials are wireframe and line-lit holograms, translucent glowing planes, circuit-board line patterns, chamfered neon frames and lattice-mesh dummies [Observed] (Dossier: Look, "Materials"). The finishes below are [BBG News invention], following STYLE.md's Glows and "clean painted metal or plastic" rules.

| Element (game look [Observed]) | House-style finish [BBG News invention] |
|---|---|
| Endless black void with vertical columns of small green capital letters (Latin, not katakana) | Deep green-black space with soft vertical streams of tiny green light specks and short dashes, falling like rain and fading at the top. **No letters**: glyph-free green rain (Overview §5.2 item 8) |
| Near-black green floor with faint green line-work in uneven rectangles, like circuit traces | Smooth, dark, satin-lacquer floor with softly glowing inlaid trace lines. A faint glow pooling on it is fine; never mirror-polished |
| Neon-green walkways with outlined arrow markings | Thick slabs with rounded, bevelled edges and a soft inner glow; the arrows are inset outlined chevrons |
| Floating circuit-etched blocks | Softly rounded translucent emerald blocks with glowing trace patterns etched inside |
| Amber sign panels on bracket-shaped neon posts | Rounded-corner amber glass panels with a glowing lemon-yellow rim, between chunky rounded bracket posts. Blank, or a simple pictogram only (see section 4) |
| Floating 3D labels with yellow leader lines | Leave them out, or show thin pale-yellow leader lines ending in small blank glowing bars [blank bars are BBG News invention, per the Dossier's prompt pack] |
| Lattice-mesh bear dummies | Rounded bear-shaped figures woven from smooth glowing wire lattice, hollow and see-through; orange by default, red or blue at the team-colours station. STYLE.md names these as the Tutorial's generic opponents (Shot formula, "Everyone in frame") |
| Red laser bars | Thin, clean, glowing red light bars stacked across the walkway, with soft bloom. Their emitters are undocumented [Unverified]; show none, or tiny rounded end caps |
| Line-lit hologram ship in concentric rings | Translucent emerald-green hologram with crisp glowing edge lines and slightly darker green faces, inside thin, partly broken glowing rings. Keep the ship generic (Overview §5.2 item 8) |
| Standard jump pad | Clean painted charcoal octagon with soft bevels, a satin yellow-and-black striped rim, a glowing cyan star core and faint translucent stacked octagonal rings (Conventions §16 B) |
| Pickup panel row | Tall dark translucent green glass panels in bright glowing green frames |
| Ice-cream sandwich pickup | The cast's soft rounded food-prop finish: a bear-head ice-cream sandwich with an angry face (STYLE.md: Surfaces; Conventions §16 C) |

**Palette (words, classic look).**
- **Void:** near-pure black with the faintest green cast. **Floor:** deep green-black with dim green trace lines.
- **Rain:** mostly mid and dim forest green, a few bright lime flecks and a few pale grey specks.
- **Walkways and blocks:** bright lime-to-emerald neon with pale mint highlights. **Hologram ship:** emerald body, darker green faces, bright green edges.
- **Signs:** dark umber-amber glass with a lemon-yellow rim. **Leader lines and bear-head outlines:** lemon yellow.
- **Dummies:** tangerine orange; scarlet red; a navy cap; a blue lattice for the distant blue dummies (no hex sampled). **Laser bars:** red-orange to pure red.
- **Pickup panels:** very dark green faces in bright green frames. **Jump pads:** aqua-cyan core, hazard yellow with black stripes, charcoal base.
- **2026 look (3.0.7, low-res samples):** void a deep green-black rather than near-pure black; signs a lighter tan-amber [Observed samples; whether that is the lighting pass or compression is Unverified]. Optional extra: a soft violet-magenta column by the "POWER UP" spot [Observed in 3.0.7; its use in a short is BBG News invention].
- Strength: "saturated but natural". Treat the samples as families, not exact neon (Overview §5.3 item 12; STYLE.md: Colour).

**Reference colours (do not paste):** void #000100–#000501; floor #011400 (p90 #002400), grid up to #083109; rain #1C5D23 / dim #17461C / brightest #3FDE44; walkways #2DC115–#3EFB22, highlights #99FF7C (2014), #33F61C–#53FF37 (Jul 2026); blocks #00CC00–#31FD20; hologram ship body #00D201, faces #0B7B0D, edges #13F610; amber signs interior #4A2C00, rim #F7DB21–#FFFF39, 2026 sign #82590A with highlights #EBDA64; label text and leader lines #E7E74A–#FFFF76, #FBFE4F (2026); bear-head targets #EEFF18–#F1FF33; orange dummies #CD6003–#E58515 (2014), lattice #C4672E–#FF9122 (2026); red dummies #FA0900, blue cap #0C3668; laser bars #D44B14–#EE5F11 (2014), #E70507 (2026); pickup panels face #023400, frame #24ED0B–#36FF31; jump pad cyan #41D7E8–#5FF3FF, hazard yellow #FBD103, base #303032, Tutorial pads #5EE0D4 (Dossier: Look, palette table) [Observed samples]. The Conventions' general "Tutorial green" is ≈ #20B010–#31E419 (Conventions §12). The values tagged 2026 above (walkways, lattice, label lines, laser bars, the 2026 sign, Tutorial pads) come from Jul–Sep 2026 fan thumbnails (pre-Oct-15 builds such as 2.54.0), so they are not 2026-look values. **Lighting: classic 2013 look** (the 2013–14 captures; Overview §5.1 item 3)
- **In the game:** there is no sky, sun, shadow or weather. Every surface lights itself: neon green with bloom, amber signs, red laser bars and cyan jump pads [Observed]. The only depth cue is glow fading into the black [Observed] (Dossier: Look, "Lighting and time of day").
- **House rig** [BBG News invention, after STYLE.md: Sets and light]. This course has no time of day, and its practical light, the neon, is the key:
  - **Key:** soft neon-green glow from the walkways and blocks, broad and low.
  - **Warm accent:** amber glow on faces at sign stations.
  - **Fill:** a cool teal.
  - **Rim:** a gentle green rim from the rain behind.
  - **Local glows:** red bars and cyan pads light only their own spots.
  - **Shadows:** soft contact shading under feet only, with no directional cast shadows.
- **Keep the cast on model:** the green may tint fur edges and undersides only. Fur, bandana and team colours must still match the Element (STYLE.md habit 5).
- Hold one setup for the whole scene (STYLE.md habit 2). Weapon flashes and the power-up aura may tint characters within a shot (STYLE.md: Effect light).

**Lighting: Oct 2026 lighting pass ("2026 look", 3.0.7 final playtest)**

- **Signage:** the one documented 2026 change is PC key prompts on the signs ("MOVE W A S D / JUMP SPACE", "SHOOTING" with a mouse icon, "TEAM COLORS", "JUMP PADS", "OPTIONS ESC") [Observed] (Lineup addendum §5). Keep the text off (section 6, Plate B notes).
- **Purple beam:** a purple vertical beam seen beside a "POWER UP" label in 3.0.7 (ZM 1:27:40) [Observed]; whether it is new in 2026, and what it marks, are [Open]. A purple-magenta power-up aura is already observed in a Jul 2012 capture (Dossier: Look, "Particles").

## 5. Hazards, gags and easter eggs

**Verified hazards and interactive elements** (Dossier: Hazards and interactive elements)
- **No damage:** "the character's damage intake is set to 0%"; "At no point through the Tutorial can the player die" [Fan-wiki].
- **Laser walls** "halt progression until fulfilling certain interactive tasks" [Fan-wiki], for example "shooting the moving targets" [Fan-wiki]. On screen they are stacked thin red beams [Observed]. They are gates, not damage.
- **Special-item platform:** "JUMP ONTO PLATFORM USING SPECIAL ITEM TO PROCEED" (BBR prompt) [Observed].
- **The sandwich** "doubles the damage of all the player's weapons for 20 seconds" and normally "respawns every 90 seconds" [Fan-wiki, Sandwich page; the Pick-Ups page says triples, so the pages conflict] (Conventions §6). Here it "does not respawn" [Fan-wiki]. A crackling purple-magenta aura around the player appears in captures, once with a "POWER UP" HUD tag [Observed]; that the aura is the sandwich's buff is [Inference].
- **Jump pads** at the end [Fan-wiki; Observed].
- **Out of bounds** [Fan-wiki]:
  - **BBR:** players could get round the air barriers and "fall *endlessly* with no way of returning" (patched).
  - **BBG:** a Pro-Mode double jump onto a sign, then onto a laser wall, let the vanishing wall launch players "high above the map" (patched).
  - **As of 2014:** "it is still possible to barely vault over the end line of jump pads by double jumping just before stepping on them" and walk off the end of the path.
- **No lava, fire, water, pits or kill zones** are documented, and nobody can die here [Fan-wiki]. Never invent one as real (Overview §5.1 item 2).

**Easter eggs and references** (Dossier: Easter eggs and references)
- **Secret achievement:** a Jun 7, 2013 fan glitch video shows an achievement panel, "Stop trying to break our…" / "Hey, stop trying to break our game man…", worth 10 points [Observed, low-res]. The wiki once said it was earned by "falling forever" [Fan-wiki]. Its exact title and platform are [Unverified]. The game's matchmaking screen says "THERE IS A SECRET IN THIS GAME" [Game text via wiki].
- **The rain:** two columns read "CONSECTETUR" (Lorem ipsum) [Observed, partial legibility]. That the whole rain is Lorem ipsum is [Inference]. The likeness to The Matrix's code rain is [Inference], and no source states a homage [Unverified]. Show it only as glyph-free green rain (Overview §5.2 item 8).
- **The ship's lineage:** the wiki calls the name a pun on Halo's Pelican and the design "based of" the UD-4 Cheyenne from Aliens [Fan-wiki]. Keep the ship generic (Overview §5.2 item 8).
- **Overstuffed kit:** the Tutorial rifle holds 70 rounds, against the standard 25 ("pre-v1.0" stats) [Fan-wiki], and the HUD reads 70/70 from 2012 to 2026 [Observed]. The shoulder rocket launcher holds 1/1 [Observed].
- **Rocket gag fuel:** the Soldier blurb warns "Use his starting Bearzooka carefully as it is easy to kill yourself with it!" [Game text via wiki, per the lore report], while damage intake here is 0% [Fan-wiki].

**Gag ideas** (all [BBG News invention], built on the facts above; cartoon violence only; the "SUICIDE" wording stays off screen, Overview §5.5 item 16)
1. **Too literal:** at the start panel, the bear copies the curved-arrow pictogram exactly and spins in place until dizzy (from the Dossier's staging spot 1).
2. **Zero damage:** the bear fires his rocket launcher at his own feet. Smoke clears; he is untouched, fur barely ruffled, and shrugs. Use oliver.md's Bearzooka snippet (1), replacing everything from "streaks away, trailing white smoke puffs, at a small round hot-pink" to the end with "streaks straight down at the walkway by his own feet, trailing white smoke puffs. It lands in a big yellow burst of crisp cut-crystal facets with smooth bevelled edges, with round grey cartoon smoke; as it clears he stands unharmed, fur barely ruffled, and shrugs." This is a solo shot: no Huggable, no Huggable Element and no break-apart KO (Dossier: staging spot 2).
3. **The longest fall** (a fan take; caption it so): the bear double-jumps just before stepping onto the end line of pads, vaults it, walks off the end of the path and drops into the rain. Cut away, then back: still falling, now bored, arms folded. Basis: the 2014 end-line vault [Fan-wiki], plus the endless fall that BBR patched [Fan-wiki]; what happens after the 2014 vault, and whether either works in 2026, are [Unverified]. Any achievement-style toast is the short's own invention, in original art, never game UI (Dossier: staging spot 3).
4. **Ninety seconds:** the bear eats the sandwich, glows purple, then sits by the empty spot waiting for it to come back. It never does.
5. **Patience test:** the red bars block the walkway; the bear taps his foot. One lattice dummy wobbles its target-practice wobble; he finally shoots it, the bars blink out, and he strolls on.
6. **Whose side?** At the team-colours station the bear aims at a red dummy wearing a blue cap and hesitates, looking between his own red bandana and the cap. A red-on-red hit is allowed only as this deliberate friendly-fire gag (STYLE.md: Teams in frame). What the station really teaches is [Open].
7. **Launch glitch, played straight:** a double jump onto a panel, then onto a red bar that vanishes underfoot, flings the bear high above the course. Caption it as the patched 2013 glitch, not current behaviour.
8. **First-day montage** (Overview §4 pitch): quick cuts through the stations, ending on the jump-pad runway and the ship.
