# Environments

One house-style environment sheet per Battle Bears Gold map, for staging and generating BBG News shorts. There are 16 maps from the verified maps research, plus the new 2026 map Sky Pirates / Sky World.

Each sheet has seven sections:

1. What the map is.
2. Layout for staging.
3. Look in the feature-film 3D house style.
4. Landmarks and props.
5. Hazards, gags and easter eggs.
6. A paste-ready prompt pack.
7. Era and continuity notes.

Facts and labels come from `research_notes/BBG maps (verified)/` in the thallo-site repo and are never upgraded. Staging, camera and gag ideas are marked `[BBG News invention]`. The house rules are in `characters/STYLE.md`, and the labels follow the legend in `characters/README.md`.

Era rule: the Steam trailer and screenshots are re-used 2013 footage, so caption anything drawn from them "classic 2013 look" and never "2026 gameplay". Some evidence paths in the sheets point to the research scratchpad where the frames were checked.

Each sheet was drafted from the dossier and then checked twice: once for fidelity to the research and once for prompt quality against STYLE.md. Every confirmed issue was fixed.
