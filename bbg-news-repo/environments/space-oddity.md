# Space Oddity: house-style environment sheet

This sheet is for BBG News shorts in the feature-film 3D house style (`characters/STYLE.md`). Facts come from the verified maps research in `research_notes/BBG maps (verified)/`, and each keeps its source label. Short names used below: **Dossier** = `space-oddity.md`; **Addendum** = `space-oddity.addendum.md` (the 2026 final-build look and modes); **Overview** = `00_OVERVIEW.md`; **Conventions** = `environment-conventions.md`; **Conv. addendum** = `environment-conventions.addendum.md`; **Lineup** = `2026-lineup.md` and its addendum; **Story** = `story-locations.md`. Hex values are approximate (about ±15%), sampled from compressed frames [Inference] (Overview §0 item 4); the 2026 values come from 320–429 px storyboards (±10–15%) [Observed] (Addendum: Look, "Final-build palette"). The one high-resolution 3.0.7 frame of this map, boAnKP4zIlg maxres2 (1280×720), has not been sampled for the hall palette (Conv. addendum §2).

**Orientation used throughout:** which end is red and which is blue is undocumented [Unverified] (Dossier: Layout, "Red vs blue"; Addendum: Layout). This sheet says **near base** and **far base**. For a red lead, treat the near base as home [BBG News invention]; opponents default to blue (STYLE.md: Teams in frame).

## 1. What it is

- **Name and spelling:** "Space Oddity" in captions, as on Steam ("classics such as No Bears Land, Gold Digger and Space Oddity") and as soundtrack track 6, "Space Oddity", 0:54 [Canon]. It launched in Battle Bears Royale 1.0 (wiki date Oct 8, 2011) as **"Spacecraft"** and was renamed with the BBG relaunch (iOS, May 24, 2013) [Fan-wiki] (Dossier: Era and status). The artist renders are captioned "Spaceship Level" [Observed]. Not to be confused with Shortest Parsec, the red-and-white station in screen_02 (exterior) and screen_04 (interior) (Dossier: Corrections 1 and 4; Images viewed, Steam).
- **Setting:** a small, enclosed spaceship interior, "symmetrical in every way, even with pick-ups" [Fan-wiki]: a long hall between two two-level team bases, with amber-tinted window walls onto stars [Observed]. Interior, no sky, no time of day [Observed; Inference] (Overview §5.1 item 4; Dossier: Setting).
- **Era and 2026:** returning, named by SkyVu as an "Iconic Map" [Canon]. It appears in five captures of the Oct 15, 2026 final playtest (build 3.0.7), including SkyVu's own livestream, and ran FFA, Team Battle/TDM and Plant the Bomb in that build [Observed] (Addendum: Era and status). A sixth 3.0.7 view, [LA]Huggable's Oct 17 upload boAnKP4zIlg maxres2 (1280×720, build label "3.0.7_2026.11.19_b7 411483"), is the best-resolution 2026 frame of this map on record [Observed] (Conv. addendum §2). The Oct 2026 lighting pass ("Lighting improvements" [Canon]) visibly reached it, moving it toward the warm BBR 2012 look [Observed measurement; Inference, strong] (Addendum: Look). Its Steam trailer clip (about 30.0–32.8 s) and Steam screenshot screen_01 are 2013 footage [Inference, strong]: caption them "classic 2013 look", never 2026 (Overview §0 item 1).

## 3. Look in the house style

**Shapes and materials.** Keep the layout, landmark silhouettes, material identity, palette family and practical lights; re-render everything soft, rounded and feature-film, with no ink outlines and never "low-poly" in a prompt (Overview §5.3 items 10–12). The game's own look is a matte, mid-saturation sci-fi interior, not glossy [Observed] (Dossier: Look). Finishes below are [BBG News invention] after STYLE.md's "clean painted metal or plastic" and Glows rules.
- **Panels:** seamed, riveted metal in cream, olive and grey-green [Observed] → clean painted metal with softly bevelled seams, rounded rivet heads and a satin finish.
- **Octagons everywhere:** pillar, ceiling unit, doorways, windows, alcoves, floor ring and drums [Observed]. Keep the motif crisp but round every corner slightly.
- **Window walls:** orange-tinted, star-specked glass, hex-mesh in the base windows [Observed] → thick, softly glowing tinted panes with a faint hex pattern; stars as small painterly specks, never a photo.
- **Catwalks:** grey-green steel with grated edges [Observed] → chunky rounded rails and soft-cornered perforated grating.
- **Trim and tubes:** glowing yellow edge strips; cyan light tubes in ringed segments [Observed] → soft emissive strips and plump segmented glowing tubes, with gentle bloom that lights nearby fur and floor.
- **Pillar:** squat, near-black olive, with yellow-rimmed portholes, stacked mustard up-chevrons and a dark hatch [Observed] → chunky, softly bevelled, with rounded bezel portholes and raised painted chevrons.
- **Light cone:** a translucent cyan-green cone from the ceiling ring onto the pillar [Observed] → a soft volumetric cone.
- **Crates:** charcoal cargo crates with round pink grinning bear faces [Observed] → chunky matte boxes, softly bevelled, with a clean painted face decal (as the H.I.P. Test Lab sheet renders them).
- **Consoles and screens:** twin cyan screens; dark schematic screens with cyan line drawings [Observed] → rounded console bodies with screens of simple glowing shapes, never text.

**Palette (words)** (strength "saturated but natural" [house style])
- **Classic 2013 look** (Steam trailer grade, the default) [Observed] (Dossier: Look, "Palette"): window glass deep burnt orange to honey amber, flecked with white stars; a golden-orange glow behind the octagonal window; burnt-orange bulkheads with umber shadow; grey-green to sage catwalk steel; deep forest-teal ceiling beams and olive-green back-wall panels; a soft sage ceiling unit; cyan running from deep teal-green in the cone to bright aqua-cyan at the pads; a near-white glowing pillar top over a near-black olive body with mustard chevrons; warm tan floor with ochre inlays, grey-green seams and deep brown shadow; charcoal green-black crates with dusky rose-pink faces. Overall: warm amber-orange and olive cut with cool cyan and glowing yellow trim.
- **2026 lighting pass** (build 3.0.7) [Observed, low-res] (Addendum: Look, "Final-build palette"): golden-amber to honey glass; cream-olive and golden walls and catwalk faces (a second reading, Conv. addendum §5: amber-ochre window walls with olive-grey bulkheads [Observed]); bright lemon-gold glowing trim; an olive-gold ceiling unit lit amber; olive-tan floor with a dark ochre ring around the pillar; near-black olive pillar with dull mustard chevrons and a near-white top tinted mint-cyan; a pale mint-cyan ceiling ring; seafoam-to-aqua pipes, cryo tubes and console screens; deep green shadow on the back wall; near-black green crates (their faces unresolved at storyboard resolution [Unverified]); a yellow-green paw-print triangle; warm brown-orange alcoves. Overall: warmer, brighter and less grey, with a faint yellow-olive (slightly green) cast; cyan practicals unchanged.
- **Other grades (eye reference only):** the BBG 2013 map tile reads bright creamy yellow-olive with lemon trim [Observed], but it is render art, not a capture; 2021 fan captures sit between tile and trailer [Observed]; the BBR renders range from dark grey-orange to hazy pale cyan-grey [Observed]. A short picks the classic 2013 look (trailer, screen_01 and 2013–14 captures, which also sit between tile and trailer) or the 2026 look (3.0.7 frames) (Overview §5.1 item 3), one grade per short (STYLE.md habit 6). The only exception is plate B and its basement plate (section 6), which have no in-game frame. If a BBR-render or 2021 grade is ever used, caption it "BBR-era render look" or "2021 fan-capture look", never "classic 2013 look" or "2026 look" [BBG News invention]. Never use the cooler grey-teal Aug–Sep 2026 grade (3.0.2 / 3.0.5) [Observed], which the pass replaced.

## 5. Hazards, gags and easter eggs

**Verified hazards and interactive elements** (Dossier: Hazards and interactive elements; Addendum: Hazards)
- **No environmental hazard is documented** [Fan-wiki, checked by absence], and none was seen in 2026 frames [Observed, by absence]. The 2012 infobox says "Suicide = No" [Fan-wiki], so there are no kill pits [Inference]. No lava, fire, laser, chute or fall-out: the "deadly laser" and "garbage chute" paragraph was about Shortest Parsec and was removed [Fan-wiki].
- **Jump pads:** four around the centre and three in each base [Fan-wiki]; visible [Observed].
- **Explosive bug at the pillar:** "if an explosive weapon is used above the central pillar the damage is crazy!" [Fan-wiki, added 2016].
- **Basement breach:** the basement "can be shot through with explosives to breach into the main level" [Fan-wiki].
- **Old glitches** [Fan-wiki]: a Huggable would "instantly suicide right when the game began" (BBR 1.4, fixed); the Mini Me glitch (fixed in 1.4.2); Huggables could "So Fly up and get behind the giant window" (2012); So Fly plus Mini Me "exit from the arena and see the spaceship".

**Easter eggs and references** (Dossier: Easter eggs and references)
- **The name:** "likely a reference to the famous song of the same name by English singer David Bowie" [Fan-wiki]; SkyVu has not said so [Unverified]. Caption-only; no likeness, lyrics or melody (STYLE.md: Sound; real-person rule).
- **"Death Star" hologram:** a cyan wireframe sphere with a concave dish [Observed]; a Star Wars nod [Inference]. Show it only as a generic wireframe globe (Overview §5.2 item 8).
- **Boss hologram:** the wiki says Mecha-Bearzerker on two pages and Spaceboss on a third [Fan-wiki, conflicting]; no image of either exists [Unverified]. The schematic screens' bear-shaped mech may or may not be it [Unverified]. A 2012 edit put the centre pickup "on some kind of Hologram Machine it seems" [Fan-wiki, revisions 18800/19717], so a hologram may play above the pillar [Inference]. Build no plot on it (Overview §5.1 item 1).
- **Huggable cargo:** the face crates, the paw-print cryo pods and the paw-print sign [Observed].
- **The ship from outside,** reachable only by glitches [Fan-wiki]; yellow-rimmed octagonal openings and a planet [Observed, low-res 2012].
- **The trailer beat:** "Huggable ambushes a sniper by the central podium" (Steam trailer 30.0–32.5 s) [Observed; 2013 footage, Inference, strong] (Lineup §6).

**Gag ideas** (all [BBG News invention], built on the facts above; cartoon violence only; KOs end in intact cartoon pops, and the "SUICIDE" wording stays off screen, Overview §5.5 item 16)
1. **King of the pillar:** a bear rides a centre pad up into the cyan cone, lands on the glowing top and reaches for the sandwich just as a blue rival lands opposite. Hold the standoff (Dossier: Staging spot 1).
