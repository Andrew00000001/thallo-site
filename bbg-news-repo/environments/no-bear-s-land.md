## 1. What it is

- **Name.** Caption it **"No Bears Land"**, with no apostrophe, as on Steam and as soundtrack track 2, "No Bears Land", 0:44 [Canon] (overview §5.4 item 14).
  - The BBG map-select tile reads "NO BEAR'S LAND" [Observed; Game text via wiki], and the wiki spells it "No Bear's Land" [Fan-wiki].
  - It was "Battlefield Airpad" until the BBR→BBG switch [Fan-wiki], and the rename came with BBG's launch on May 24, 2013 [Inference].
- **Setting.** A big, sunny, open grass plateau: two olive concrete keeps face each other across zig-zag plank trenches, with two crashed Pelican't dropships on the other two sides [Observed; Fan-wiki]. The name puns on WWI's "No Man's Land" [Fan-wiki]. The time of day is noon [Observed] (STYLE.md, Sets and light).
- **Era.** It was a BBR 1.0 launch map (Oct 8, 2011) [Fan-wiki]. It got winter, summer and spring reskins in 2017–2018 [Fan-wiki] (the topic dossiers tag the same patch lines [Game text via wiki]).
- **2026.** It is in the game [Canon]. Steam names it first among the returning "classics" [Canon].
  - SkyVu's Oct 14 "Closed Testing 5" banner shows it [Observed]. It is probably the post-pass build [Inference] (lineup §5), and it is this map's best 2026 reference (overview §4 and §5.1 item 3).
  - It appears in the Jul–Oct 2026 playtest builds [Observed].
  - It is **not** in the Steam trailer or the five Steam screenshots [Observed]. Those are 2013 footage [Inference, strong]: caption them "classic 2013 look", and never use them as reference for this map.

## 2. Layout for staging

**Plan** [Observed: wiki top-down; fan overheads] (dossier, Layout):
- A square grass plateau with cut (chamfered) corners. A pale olive ledge with a bright yellow stripe rims it, and small red or blue light domes stud the rim.
- A dark, angular, fin-shaped pylon stands on each cut corner, with three red lights on the north corners and three blue on the south.
- The wiki adds "small hills and some jagged terrain" [Fan-wiki].
- **Symmetry is rotational, not mirrored.** The Blue half is the Red half turned 180° [Observed]. Never stage it as a mirror image.

**Team ends and orientation** [Observed: top-down; fits the 2026 frame lFr8-yHbbzQ 38:34]:
| | Red (north) | Blue (south) |
|---|---|---|
| Keep's battlement wing | West side | East side |
| Food stand | North-east (Red's left, facing the centre) | South-west (Blue's left) |
| Nearest wreck | The **west** wreck has red bands, and its furrow trails north toward Red | The **east** wreck has blue bands, and its furrow trails south toward Blue |

Each wreck slid toward the opposite team's end [Inference].

**Spawns.**
- Each team spawns "near or behind this wall" of its keep [Fan-wiki]. The overhead shows markers scattered along each keep end [Observed] (conventions §4).
- In FFA "the player will randomly spawn from either team's spawn point" [Fan-wiki].
- A respawned bear is ringed by pale-green shields with a plus emblem for 4.5 s [Fan-wiki; Observed] (conventions §4).

**Lanes** [BBG News invention, on the Observed plan]:
- The **trench zig-zag** is the covered centre lane. It runs from each keep in an S to the centre pillar [Observed; Fan-wiki].
- The **two wreck flanks** are the open lanes, over the hills where the wrecks lie.
- The **open grass** between them is the sniper's field: "This stage works very well with long range classes like the Sniper and Demo… short range classes stay in the corners of the map or in the trenches" [Fan-wiki].

**Vertical spots** (dossier, Layout, "Verticality") [Fan-wiki + Observed]:
- **Trench floors** sit below grade. Grey slatted decks and ramps with yellow edge stripes bridge or roof some sections [Observed].
- **Rolling mounds.** The keeps sit on rises and the wrecks on hills [Observed].
- **Keep roofs**, reached by the jump pad in the trapezoid alcove at the back of each keep [Fan-wiki; Observed].
- **Wreck tops**, reached by climbing "the port wing" [Fan-wiki]. The super pads up there fling players "across the field at a extremely high altitude" [sic] to land "right next to the opposite spacecraft" [Fan-wiki]; that these are the highest arcs on the map is [Inference] (dossier, Layout, "Verticality"). No pad lands a bear on a wreck top.
- **The centre pillar**, reached by its twin pads or a double jump [Fan-wiki].

**Sightlines** [Observed unless labelled]:
- Keep roof to the enemy battlement, across the whole field. This is SkyVu's own 2026 thumbnail vantage (C7uFxvSZ10c).
- From the field toward the Blue keep: bands, doorway, turret, corner pylon, the food-stand parasol, sandbags, traps and decks (6ghQ4IUBqEY maxres1).
- North past the red-banded wreck to the Red keep (lFr8-yHbbzQ 38:34).
- Down a trench, along its plank floor.
- To the horizon: misty blue-grey wooded hills beyond the rim.

**Scale** [Inference] (overview §3; conventions §11):
- Doors are about 1.5–2× a bear's height. The keep's recessed doorway is tall [Observed].
- Sandbags reach knee to waist height.

**Camera positions** [BBG News invention]. The map is open-air, so the camera may fly anywhere above it. Stay inside the rim: overhead glitch views show a pale void beyond it [Observed].
1. **Keep roof.** Over the olive parapet, looking across the field at the enemy battlement. Use it for the hero shot or a sniper beat.
2. **Trench floor.** Low, one-point perspective along the plank floor, with the striped soil walls rising on both sides.
3. **Centre junction.** Low angle from the trench floor, looking up at the grey pillar and its twin pads. Use it for the Sandwich standoff.
4. **Food stand.** Eye-level medium beside the Red keep: parasol, cart and the glowing basket on the grass.
5. **Crash furrow.** Low in the ploughed trough, looking uphill at a wreck perched on its hilltop (seen in 2026 frames [Observed]).
6. **High three-quarter aerial** down the long axis. Use it as an opener only (overview §5.3 item 13).

## 3. Look in the house style

Follow STYLE.md (The look; Sets and light) and overview §5.3:
- Keep the layout, the landmark silhouettes, what each thing is made of, the palette family and the light sources.
- Re-render every surface in the feature-film finish.
- No ink outlines (environments never had one [Observed]), and never write "low-poly".

**Shapes.** The game uses chunky, hard-edged low-poly geometry with hand-painted textures [Observed] (conventions §12). Re-render it as:
- octagonal concrete tower keeps with softly bevelled edges and chunky rounded parapets (the research calls them "towers" and gives no squat proportion; "squat" is kept only for the turret housing and the cart);
- gently rolling, pillowy grass mounds;
- trenches with soft, rounded cut edges and a grass cap that droops over the lip;
- plump, slightly squashed sandbags;
- chunky, rounded cartoon wrecks with fat cylindrical engine pods;
- bold, readable steel tank traps, each three thick beams crossed at the middle like a giant toy jack (in prompts, never "X-shaped crosses", which can read as flat signs or grave crosses, and never "hedgehog" or "tank").

**Materials** [Observed] (dossier, Look, "Materials and props"), re-described:
- **Ground.** Yellow-green turf flecked with small white daisies with yellow centres. Render it as short, soft, stylised grass with tiny rounded daisies, never realistic blades.
- **Keeps.** Olive-drab weathered concrete towers with chipped pilasters and star-shaped bullet cracks. Render soft matte concrete with rounded chamfered edges. The chips become soft rounded nicks and the cracks shallow, soft-edged star dents; nothing reads as damage that hurts. The team-colour double bands are clean painted stripes.
- **Keep roofs.** Cobblestones around a pale round pad, inside a chunky olive parapet. Render them as plump rounded cobbles.
- **Trench floors.** Golden planks. Render warm honey-gold boards with soft rounded edges and gentle grain.
- **Trench walls.** Cartoon soil strata: a grass cap with a dripping edge, then an orange band, brown, and near-black at the bottom. Render soft, layer-cake bands of matte earth.
- **Shoring posts.** Wooden posts with splintered tops. Render rounded timber with soft, chunky cartoon splinter tips.
- **Decks.** Grey slatted decks and ramps with yellow edge stripes. Render clean painted grey slats with crisp yellow painted edges.
- **Sandbags.** Tan, laid in curved arcs and C-shapes. Render soft matte canvas with simple folds (STYLE.md, Cloth).
- **Tank traps.** Black steel X-shaped hedgehogs. Render clean painted dark metal with rounded edges (STYLE.md, Robot parts, extended to set metal).
- **Wrecks.** Dark grey hulls with an olive cast, four fat engine pods on stub wings, team bands, and a cockpit window and twin round headlamps on the nose (Airpad3 render). Render clean painted metal with soft rounded panels and soft dents, and no scorch or smoke.
- **Furrows.** Long orange-edged dirt troughs. Render them as soft, ploughed matte earth with slate-blue barrels and a grass-topped crate.
- **Food stand.** A red-and-yellow pyramid parasol on a single dark pole over a squat grey cart. Render soft matte fabric on the parasol and clean painted metal on the cart.
- **Glows.** These are the jump-pad cyan, the red and blue pylon lights and rim domes, the glowing basket beside the food stand, and the green pods on the hovering craft. Render clean emissive colour with a gentle bloom on nearby grass and concrete (STYLE.md, Glows).
- **Food props** (Sandwich, basket): the cast's soft, rounded finish (STYLE.md, Creatures and food props).

**Palette, in words:**
- **Sky:** saturated mid-blue, paler toward the horizon, with large soft white cumulus clouds.
- **Horizon:** misty blue-grey wooded hills.
- **Turf:** bright yellow-green to lime-olive, with white daisy specks.
- **Trenches:** honey-gold planks, an orange earth band, warm brown, and near-black at the bottom.
- **Keeps:** olive-drab concrete with deep olive shadows, a khaki-olive cobbled roof and a pale grey-green centre pad.
- **Team bands:** a warm tomato red on the Red keep and wreck; a saturated royal blue on the Blue keep and wreck.
- **Wrecks:** dark gunmetal grey with an olive cast.
- **Props:** mustard-tan sandbags, black steel traps and slate-blue barrels.
- **Rim:** a bright lemon-yellow stripe on a pale olive ledge.
- **Accents:** icy cyan pads, and the food stand's red-and-yellow canopy.
- Team colour appears only as accents: the bands, stripes, pylon lights and rim domes (overview §3).

**Lighting, classic 2013 look (the default)** [Observed] (dossier, Look, "Lighting and sky"; conventions §10):
- **What the captures show.** Bright, high sun with crisp, short shadows, under a saturated mid-blue sky that pales toward the horizon, with big soft white cumulus. The infobox says "Day, Sunlight" [Fan-wiki].
  - The classic 2013 look rests on 2013–14 captures (BRVP0IZEw-k, Oct 2, 2013) (overview §5.1 item 3); the Steam trailer does not show this map.
  - The 2012 BBR screenshot and 2026 frames show the same sky type but are other eras.
- **Avoid the haze.** The 2012 "Fields Level" renders are hazier and fade to near-white [Observed], but they are portfolio renders [Inference]. Don't match their haze.
- **No dominant practical light.** The sun is the light source. Emissive accents are identity anchors only: pads, pylon lights, the basket glow and the craft's pods (overview §5.3 item 11).
- **House rig.**
  - The key is a high noon sun from almost overhead, slightly warm, casting crisp, short shadows (every era of this map is observed with crisp shadows; dossier, Look).
  - The fill is cool, from the blue sky dome. Green bounce from the turf goes into soft global illumination.
  - Keep a gentle rim on the characters, and cinematic depth of field that softens the far hills.
  - Colour is saturated but natural.
  - Use one setup per scene (STYLE.md habit 2).

**Lighting, Oct 2026 lighting pass** (overview §3; conventions addendum §5; lineup §5):

## 5. Hazards, gags and easter eggs

**Verified** (dossier, Hazards and interactive elements; conventions §6–§9):
- **The plateau edge.** The infobox says "Suicide: Yes" [Fan-wiki]. Here that most likely means falling off the plateau edge [Inference]. A Shield does not save you: players "can still die by falling off a stage" [Fan-wiki].
- **Jump pads, three kinds** [Fan-wiki; positions Observed]:
  1. behind each keep, up to the roof;
  2. two "on each site of pillar" [sic];
  3. on each wreck, "allowing the players to fly across the field at a extremely high altitude" [sic], to land "right next to the opposite spacecraft".
- **Pickups** [Fan-wiki]:
  - **Sandwich ×1**, on the pillar. It "doubles the damage of all the player's weapons for 20 seconds" and "respawns every 90 seconds". Another wiki page says it triples damage [Fan-wiki, conflicting], so never caption a number.
  - **Imperial Basket Grande ×2**, behind each food stand. It heals 50% of max health, stacks to 200%, and respawns in 60 s.
  - **Health Pack ×2**, on the wrecks, reached by "the port wing". It respawns in 45 s.
  - **Shield ×2**, "on the top of each base tower". It gives 10 s of immunity.
- **Spawn shield.** 4.5 s "to discourage spawn killing" [Fan-wiki].
- **Plant the Bomb.** The bombs sit "in the middle of the trenches near the pillar" and the bomb tubes "on top of the each of the base towers" [Fan-wiki]. A team-coloured round bomb in a square slab was photographed in one of this map's trenches in 2012 [Observed].
- **Not interactive.** No record says the turrets or the corner pylons do anything, so treat them as set dressing [Inference].
- **Not documented:** weather, particles, water, lava or fire on the base map [Unverified: none documented]. Water and sand belong to the summer reskin only [Fan-wiki].
- **Glitches.** Fan titles mention glitches and getting "into the tower" [Unverified]. Never build on them.

**Easter eggs and trivia** (dossier, Easter eggs and references; Era and status):
- **The name** puns on "No Man's Land" [Fan-wiki].
- **The Pelican't** puns on Halo's Pelican, and its design is said to follow the UD-4 "Cheyenne" from *Aliens* [Fan-wiki]. Keep the wrecks generic and chunky, never a recognisable copy of either (overview §5.2 item 8).
- **The keep stencil** reads "XEL-PMOC 20-11" [Observed], which is "11-02 COMP-LEX" backwards [Inference]. Its meaning is [Unverified]. Always render the strip blank.
- **The cartoon bone.** A big white cartoon bone is set in the dark lower soil of a trench wall in the 2012 renders [Observed]. Whether it is in the shipped game is [Unverified]. Use it only as a deliberate fan easter egg.
- **The food stand.** The mascot holds a drumstick right beside the Grande, which BB-1 advertised as "3 pounds of deep fried pain" [Observed; Fan-wiki quoting the BB-1 gag ad]. The pairing looks deliberate [Inference].
- **Reskins.** It was "the first map to feature a reskinned version" [Fan-wiki]. In the winter reskin a frosted tree with red and yellow baubles and a gold star stands on the centre pillar, with snowmen near the food stands and behind the keeps [Fan-wiki; Observed].
- **King of the Windmill** ran here in 2014: the windmill and its green ring stand on a grass knoll among tank traps [Observed thumbnails] (conventions addendum, Corrections to our notes, item 3). The mode is retired [Game text via wiki].
- **The official blurb.** "…probably really nice before you came along… quaint old folk's home… War. What is it good for?" [Canon per our notes; Unverified this session]. The song echo is [Inference]. Don't build a plot on the blurb (overview §5.1 item 1), and never use the song (STYLE.md, Sound).

**Gag ideas [BBG News invention]** (none may appear in a "what's in BBG 2026" post):
- **Sandwich standoff.** Two bears peek over the sandbag lip at the pillar, hit the twin pads at the same instant and bonk heads in mid-air over the Sandwich (dossier, Prompt pack, staging spot 1).
- **Behind the shielding parasol.** A bear under fire ducks behind the food-stand parasol as if it were armour, then helps himself to the basket. This plays the wiki's own wording, "behind a shielding parasol".
- **Wreck-to-wreck express.** Two bears launch off opposite wrecks at once and pass each other at the top of the arc, each landing right next to the other wreck, never on its top (dossier, staging spot 3; the documented landing is "right next to the opposite spacecraft" [Fan-wiki]).
- **The turret that isn't.** A bear swings himself onto the battlement turret to open fire, and it just creaks. Caption it as a fan take: the turret being scenery is [Inference] (overview §5.1 item 2).
- **"We're back."** A roof-top hero pose from SkyVu's own vantage, for launch-day openers (overview §4 pitch).
- **Mind the yellow stripe.** A bear backs toward the rim, windmills his arms and drops out of frame with a cartoon whistle, then pops back in at his keep inside the green spawn shields.

**Teen rating.** Cartoon violence only (overview §5.5 item 16):
- An edge fall is a whistle-drop and a cut away, or an intact pop back at the spawn. The "SUICIDE" feed wording stays off screen.
- The wrecks are empty, abandoned props with no crew, casualties or fire.
- The WWI pun stays a cartoon: no barbed wire, mud, craters or real-war imagery.

## 6. Prompt pack
