## 1. What it is

- **Toxic Terror**, two words, as on the 2026 soundtrack ("14 Toxic Terror 1:18") [Canon] and in the 2.3 notes ("New Map: Toxic Terror") [Fan-wiki]. The 2026 in-game display name is [Unverified] (lineup addendum gap pass 2). The beta name "X Marks The Pain" comes from a fan copy of the notes [Unverified]; never use it as official.
- **Setting.** A dark, damp, "double-floored cavernous sewer system with radioactive waste flowing through" [Fan-wiki]. Green ooze glows under the floor grates, and "a large climbable tree" grows in the middle [Fan-wiki][Observed]. It is underground at night: a shaft shows the night sky [Observed] (dossier, Setting; Look).
- **Era.** BBG only. Previewed Sep 13, 2013 [Canon], released in 2.3 on Sep 27, 2013 (iOS) [Fan-wiki]. No reworks are recorded [Fan-wiki] (dossier, Era and status).
- **2026.** No SkyVu text names it [Canon by absence]. It is in the 2026 builds: SkyVu's final-playtest thumbnail is stamped "3.0.7_2026.11.19_b7" [Observed]; its tile is in PICK LEVEL on Sep 20 and Oct 15 [Observed match]; a team match ran Sep 20, PTB ran Oct 15 ("DELIVER THE BOMB TO SCORE POINTS") and FFA ran Oct 15 [Observed] (overview §2.3). Launch verdict: "Very likely" [Inference, strong]. **It is not in the Steam trailer or the five screenshots** [Observed], which are 2013 footage [Inference, strong] from before the map existed. Its "classic 2013 look" comes from the Sep 2013 preview and 2013–14 captures (overview §5.1 item 3).

## 3. Look in the house style

Follow STYLE.md (The look; Sets and light) and overview §5.3: keep the layout, landmark silhouettes, materials, palette family and light sources; re-render them in the feature-film finish; add no ink outlines; never write "low-poly". Keep one landmark per shot (overview §5.3 item 13).

**Shapes.**
- Softly rounded brick courses and smooth bevelled concrete ledges.
- Plump, rounded pipes with soft elbows and flanges; soft, sagging cable bundles.
- Square grates with clean, rounded bars.
- A chunky, twisting trunk with deep, rounded bark ridges and plump branches.
- Caged lamps with neat, rounded cages; simple, clear sign silhouettes.

**Materials** [Observed] (dossier, Look, Materials), re-described:
- **Brick walls.** Dark brick becomes matte brick with soft edges and a faint damp sheen (the sheen is [BBG News invention], lineup addendum gap pass 2 §4).
- **Concrete.** Poured ledges and floors become smooth matte concrete.
- **Grates.** Square steel grates become clean, dark painted metal grids lit from below.
- **Ooze.** It glows from under the grates, in channels and in shallow puddles [Observed]. It becomes glossy, slightly translucent emissive goo with a gentle bloom (STYLE.md, Glows; Faceted effects: "goo become... glossy blobs"). It never bubbles over the floor.
- **Pipes and cables.** Thick tan or olive pipes become smooth painted pipes; black cable bundles become soft rubbery cables.
- **Bark.** Rough bark becomes deep, matte, rounded ridges.
- **Railings and steps.** Wooden and metal railings [Observed], plain wooden steps and ramps [Observed, low-res]. All are smooth and simple. Railings glow green from below in the 2013 tour (0:18; 1:34–1:46) and lime in the Sep 20, 2026 build (KkDhuNbHn3I ~10:30) [Observed], so this is not a 2026 trait: classic plates may light them green from below.
- **Signs and poster.** Keep each shape, colour and place, render them blank (overview §5.2 item 5).
- **Chalk marks.** Tallies and bear doodles become soft, simple chalk strokes on the brick.
- **Weapon rack.** A plain wooden wall rack, never the Turtle weapons (overview §5.2 item 8).

## 6. Prompt pack

**How these prompts are built:**
- **Closer.** STYLE.md has no environment wording, so each image prompt ends with the framing line plus the closer the other map sheets use, built from STYLE.md's Look and Lighting words. The video line ends with STYLE.md's framing line and video suffix.
- **Era.** The establishing shot, the classic plates and the classic scene lines use the classic 2013 lighting. Written-out 2026 plates and a 2026 scene line follow them; there is no word swap.
- **Aspect ratio.** Set 9:16 in the tool's aspect-ratio setting as well as pasting "Vertical 9:16 framing." (STYLE.md, The look: Frame).
- **Names.** No map, weapon, pickup or character name appears in any prompt (STYLE.md, Character and map names).

**Establishing shot (image)**

In the centre a thick, twisting brown tree grows up through the floor, its bark in deep, soft, rounded ridges, its plump bare branches spreading overhead beneath big rounded olive-tan pipes and soft sagging black cables, the undersides of the branches glowing soft red. The ground floor is a field of square dark steel grates glowing saturated neon green from glossy ooze beneath. Railed balconies with wooden and painted metal railings lit green from below line both sides, joined to the glowing floor by a narrow wooden ramp. Green underglow from the grates as the key, bouncing up onto every surface, warm amber lamp accents, a cool blue-violet fill and rim light along the pipes, deep soft shadows, very low ambient light.
