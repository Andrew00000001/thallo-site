Sources: `research_notes/BBG maps (verified)/desert-airmine.md` (the dossier; there is no `desert-airmine.addendum.md`, and the map's 2026 gap pass is section 2 of `2026-lineup.addendum.md`), `00_OVERVIEW.md` (maps overview), `2026-lineup.md`, `environment-conventions.md` and its addendum (section 5 holds the Oct 2026 lighting measurements). ## 1. What it is

- **Desert Airmine**: spelled this way on the 2026 soundtrack, track 12 [Canon] and in SkyVu's OST track-list graphic [Observed]. The name probably puns on "air" (floating) and "mine" [Inference]. Sand-topped mesa islands float at several heights inside a deep red-rock canyon, rigged as a mine with steel lattice towers, cables and girders around a central drill tower [Observed]; the wiki groups them in three tiers [Fan-wiki]; "desert-like surroundings", "Open-air", "Day, Sunlight" [Fan-wiki] (dossier, Setting).
- **Era:** a BBR 1.0 launch map (Oct 8, 2011) [Fan-wiki]. It was dropped at the BBG relaunch, about May 2013 [Fan-wiki; date Inference], came back in BBG v2.7 (Jan 23, 2014) [Fan-wiki], and was relit game-wide in BBG 3.0 (2021) [Game text via wiki]. **2026:** SkyVu's launch copy does not name it [Canon, by absence]. It appears in SkyVu's own July "Official Playtest Highlights" and in Oct 15 final-playtest frames, build 3.0.7 [Observed]. Launch: "Very likely" [Inference, strong] (overview §2.3; the dossier's own verdict is "likely"). It is **not** in the Steam trailer or screenshots [Observed] (dossier, Era and status).
- **2026 modes seen on this map:** Team Battle / TDM (team kills) [Observed]; FFA [fan label plus frames] (lineup addendum §2 and §7; overview §2.3). Plant the Bomb is not recorded on this map in any 2026 build, so a 2026-look short stages TB or FFA here, not PTB.
- **SkyVu's "BBG Maps Ranked" short is not 2026-status evidence.** It ranks the classic maps: it shows a Desert Airmine clip (0:54–0:59) and places it in the "9-12" tier [Observed; tier read from low-res frames] (lineup addendum §2 and §6). The grid is level-select art, not a 2026 roster and not proof of a 2026 look (overview §0 item 3). The dossier reads the same short the other way: "Part 1's visible bottom tier shows other maps" (dossier, Open questions), so the tier placement is unsettled.

## 2. Layout for staging

The map is **asymmetric**, in three height tiers [Fan-wiki]. The 2012 overhead is a top-down plan, so islands at different heights overlap in it, and the compass orientation is unknown (dossier, Layout). The 2026 frames show islands "on several levels" [Observed]; the wiki's three-level plan and the hidden fourth island were not checkable in 2026 footage [Unverified] (lineup addendum §2). Stage this plan as the legacy layout.

- **Level 1, lowest: the Red base (default home for the lead).** It has "three large islands" [Fan-wiki]:
  - the Red spawn, with a small hut that "holds a small Jump Pad" [Fan-wiki]. The overhead shows a corrugated-roof shed with a blue-framed jump pad in its corner, and barrel clusters [Observed]; that this shed is the wiki's hut is the dossier's reading [Inference];
  - an island with a cactus (a "sombrero cactus" in the 2012–13 text) and a jump pad up to level 2 [Fan-wiki];
  - a third island "positioned beneath the second level", with another pad up [Fan-wiki].
- **Level 2, the largest: the Blue base.** It is "one big, though weirdly shaped island" plus a smaller island reached by a bridge [Fan-wiki]. It is "most recognizable by the big helicopter landing pad" [Fan-wiki]. The overhead shows 7 blue markers round the pad and 2 on a small lower-right island [Observed]. The bases are "very close to each other, only separated by a Jump Pad and a high level-difference" [Fan-wiki].
- **Level 3, the top:** "2 smaller islands only connected by a very long jump using a Jump Pad which involves crossing the oil drill" [Fan-wiki]. The second one holds "a small windmill with a small platform" [Fan-wiki].
- **Hidden fourth island:** under "a small hole in the floor" of the Blue base, which opens with the drill (the wiki says "up" now and said "down" in Nov 2012). It holds the Bearscream Sandwich behind a single jump pad that launches you "up and to the Red Team's base" [Fan-wiki].
- **Centre:** the drill tower, "in the very center of the map" [Fan-wiki; Observed overhead].
- **Lanes and routes:**
  - Jump pads are the main way around; players drop down by free fall [Fan-wiki].
  - "Several small gaps" can be jumped with enough speed [Fan-wiki].
  - Grey X-braced truss bridges and pale-green mesh grate platforms link the islands [Observed: overhead]. The 2026 frames show metal grating walkways and plank bridges [Observed].
  - A long tan wooden beam ("the plank across the big gaping canyon") runs from a grey machine toward the centre [Fan-wiki; Observed].
- **Vertical spots:**
  - the windmill platform (the sniper perch);
  - the jump-pad arcs, including the long top-tier launch over the drill;
  - the tier edges and cliff faces;
  - a "small container" with a tiny jump pad on level 2 [Fan-wiki];
  - the drop through the Blue-base hole.
- **Sightlines:** the windmill platform has an "overview of almost the entire stage"; "The Blue team even has the ability to watch the entire Red team's base from the windmill platform" [Fan-wiki]. The drill tower anchors the middle of most views. Stepped canyon walls and buttes ring everything, and loose rock chunks float in the distant sky in the renders [Observed].
- **Scale:** rails at knee to waist height [Inference] (overview §3). Here they are wooden posts carrying rope or chain-link railings [Observed]. Doors are about 1.5–2× a bear's height (STYLE.md, Sets and light).
- **Camera positions** [BBG News invention]:
  1. Low on the Red-base island, by the hut, looking up the cliff to the helipad tier with the drill tower beyond. The underdog view: blue looks down on red.
  2. Out over the canyon, level with an island's edge, looking back at the stack. This shows the tapered undersides that give the map its silhouette. It is an open-air map, so the camera may hang in the canyon air. Keep the canyon floor lost in haze.
  3. High on the windmill platform, looking down over the Red base. This is the sniper's view the wiki describes.
  4. Locked-off medium at the near end of the plank, looking out over the drop.
  5. Low angle at the foot of the drill tower, looking up at the head and the hanging bit.
  6. Eye level on the helipad tier, looking across the gap to the drill.

## 3. Look in the house style

Per STYLE.md (The look; Sets and light) and overview §5.3: keep the layout, the landmark silhouettes, what each thing is made of, the palette family and the light sources, and re-render every surface in the feature-film finish. No ink outlines, and never write "low-poly".

- **Shapes:** the game is built from chunky, hard-edged, low-poly geometry with hand-painted textures [Observed] (conventions §12). Re-render as soft, bevelled forms with bold silhouettes:
  - plump, flat-topped mesa slabs whose cliffs taper to a rounded point underneath;
  - chunky lattice towers and girders with oversized rounded rivets;
  - rounded planks and posts;
  - plump saguaros with soft ribbed arms;
  - soft-edged corrugated roofs.
- **Materials** [Observed] (dossier, Look, Materials), re-described:
  - **Sand tops:** smooth sand. Render smooth, soft-matte sand.
  - **Cliffs:** striated red rock tapering underneath "like upside-down mesas". Render broad, rounded strata bands with near-black undersides.
  - **Metal:** riveted steel lattice towers and girders, corrugated metal roofs, chain-link fencing, X-braced truss bridges and mesh grates. Render clean painted metal with a soft sheen (STYLE.md, Robot parts, extended to set metal).
  - **Wood:** planks, beams and posts, with rope or chain-link railings. Render rounded wood with soft grain and soft matte rope.
  - **Fabric:** the sombrero is straw with a dark band. Render soft matte woven straw (STYLE.md, Cloth).
  - **Glows:** the jump-pad cyan centres, and a cyan hexagonal ring near the satellite dish in 2026 (what it is is [Unverified]). Render clean soft emissive light with a gentle bloom (STYLE.md, Glows).
- **Palette, in words:**
  - **Sky:** powder blue, with soft pale haze at the horizon.
  - **Sand:** classic look, saturated golden-yellow brightening to lemon-yellow in full sun; Oct 2026 look, a paler, softer butter-yellow (conventions addendum §5). ## 5. Hazards, gags and easter eggs

**Verified** (dossier, Hazards and interactive elements):
- **Falling off.** "Falling off the stage or hitting the drill is very likely to happen on this particular map" [Fan-wiki]. Falls count against the player [Fan-wiki], and a Shield does not stop them [Fan-wiki].
- **The drill.** Hitting it in mid-air kills; "To safely cross this, the player must use the Jump Pads while the drill is down" [Fan-wiki].
  - The map page says it "constantly alternates in going up and down"; BBR 1.4.3 made it "much more erratic" [Fan-wiki].
  - The Graham page calls it "The spinning drill" [Fan-wiki]. The exact motion is unresolved.
  - The drill was not seen moving, or clearly at all, in the 2026 storyboards [Observed absence; Unverified] (lineup addendum §2).
- **The windmill.** "Hitting the windmill itself will kill any player" [Fan-wiki]. Whether the rotor turns is not documented.
- **The Blue-base hole.** "If you fall in that particular hole, do not worry". But if an Engineer mines its only jump pad "and you have no explosive weapons, it could trap you here" [Fan-wiki].
- **Mined jump pads.** These are prime spots for Engineer mines, especially "the Jump Pad at the bottom island below the oil drill", which is "the only exit from that island" [Fan-wiki].
- **Wiki tips:** "Snipers standing on the windmill should be wary of ambushes"; "Sixth Sense Goggles are extremely recommended" [Fan-wiki].
- **Not on this map:** no lava, water, weather or particles are documented [absence across sources]; the renders show only sun bloom and haze [Observed].

**Easter eggs** (dossier, Easter eggs and references):
- **Graham's emblem on the drill.** The wiki calls it "the first clue of the addition of Graham" [Fan-wiki]. It can at most be his first in-game appearance, since SkyVu's 2011 Sneak Peek key art already shows him [Observed; Inference]. Overview §4 pitches Graham-centred shorts here.
- **"A small toilet and three tool sheds"** (the wiki's reading: Graham "built this course" [Fan-wiki, speculation]). The blue portable toilet is seen [Observed]. At least two sheds are seen; three is unconfirmed.
- **Unexplained details:** the bear-face barrels and the pumpjack beam are seen [Observed] but explained by no source.
- **The sombrero cactus** is documented only in the wiki's 2012–13 text [Fan-wiki].

**Gag ideas [BBG News invention]** (none may appear in a "what's in BBG 2026" post):
- **Windmill sniper.** A sniper on the windmill platform is so busy watching the Red base that someone ambushes them, or they back into the windmill and get bonked off in a cartoon fling. This is the wiki's own warning, played as slapstick.
- **Drill-timing jump.** In three beats: launch off the top-tier pad, the drill rises, cartoon pancake bonk. This is the dossier's staging spot 2.
- **"Too good to be true."** A bear drops through the Blue-base hole to the Sandwich, then finds a mine on the only exit pad. The hidden island was not checkable in 2026 footage [Unverified], so keep this gag out of 2026-look shorts, or caption it as the legacy map.
- **Portable toilet.** A bear hides in the toilet while the pumpjack and drill thunder nearby. The door opens on the worst possible moment.
- **Badge cutaway.** A cutaway to the drill's plain yellow badge as a "boss is watching" beat in a Graham short.

**Teen rating:** cartoon violence only. Falls, drill hits and the windmill end in intact cartoon pratfalls, bonks or pops, never cutting or injury. The game's "SUICIDE" wording stays off screen (overview §5.5 item 16).
