## 1. What it is

- **Sky View**, two words, as in SkyVu's 2013 trailer description ("3 New Maps: -Gold Digger -Skate or Die -Sky View") and soundtrack track 3, "Sky View", 1:03 [Canon]. It is a construction site on the open top floor of an unfinished skyscraper, high above a city of office towers, at dusk turning to night [Fan-wiki; Observed] (dossier, Setting; Look).
- **Era.** It is a BBG launch map from late May to June 2013 and not a BBR map [Canon; Fan-wiki] (dossier, Era and status).
- **2026.** The Steam text does not name it [Canon]. It is seen in 2026 builds: the Aug 21 recording EQwloJnrOko (0:40–3:20) and the Oct 15 final playtest (1MP1rFi4t-Q, 0:40–4:30, a crane and crates) [Observed] (overview §2.3; lineup addendum gap pass 1, §6 and §7). Kill Confirmed is observed at 1MP1rFi4t-Q 3:00 (lineup addendum §7, jn_open.jpg) [Observed, low-res]; see §7 for the conflict over KC on this map. The launch verdict is "Very likely" [Inference, strong]. Its Steam trailer overhead (about 47.4–49.6 s) is 2013 footage [Inference, strong], so caption it **"classic 2013 look"**. **It is not Sky World / Sky Pirates** (overview §2.3 and §5.4).

## 2. Layout for staging

(Dossier, Layout; conventions §4, §6 and §8.)
- **Shape.** It is "the smallest map in Battle Bears Gold so far", with a "single-level design" [Fan-wiki]. It is one roughly square deck on tall red columns, with a few plank decks and tilted plates raised slightly above it [Observed].
- **Floor.** Red bolt-holed I-beams divide the deck into square bays. Most bays are filled with black ribbed grating, and some are open, so you see the red frame drop away to a brown rooftop far below [Observed]. "There are no walls around this map" [Fan-wiki].
- **Routes.** Pale plank walkways and plank decks, three to five boards wide, run across the beams. Grey studded steel plates lie flat or form short low ramps [Observed].
- **Cover.** Plywood boards stand on grey square posts, some in V-shaped pairs, and glossy blue tarps are stretched between posts, in a staggered maze [Observed].
  - The dossier reads the whole maze, boards and tarps alike, as waist-to-chest high [Observed]. Conventions §11 reads the trailer's tarps as about a bear's height or taller [Observed, approximate], and overview §3 gives full cover only as a rule of thumb [Inference from single frames].
  - [BBG News invention, choosing between conflicting readings]: stage boards waist-to-chest and tarps about bear height. Keep that choice fixed across every shot of a short until the demo footage settles it (§7, Open).
- **Centre.** "The large, light blue tarp in the middle of the map" [Fan-wiki]. Several tarps run across the middle in the overhead [Observed]. That it splits the team halves is [Inference].
- **Spawns.** The spawns sit close together on either side of the central tarp [Inference, from the PtB text]. "Some spawn points directly face the edge of the map" [Fan-wiki]. Which side is Red is [Unverified]; a red lead faces blue opponents by default (STYLE.md, Teams in frame).
- **Plant the Bomb.** Each bomb sits "on the opposing team's side of the tarp" [Fan-wiki]. Whether the carrier goes to "the right corner of their own side" or to the enemy tube is [Unverified].
  - General PtB hardware (overview §3): a team-coloured round bomb on a same-colour stand, carried to the enemy tube [Fan-wiki; Observed 2012]; a 2-minute fuse that pulses in the last 30 s; the carrier cannot attack; falling with the bomb detonates it [Fan-wiki]. The 2026 tube model is [Unverified]. Stage the carrier unarmed.
- **Vertical spots.** There are no jump pads [Fan-wiki]. Height comes only from the slightly raised plank decks and the low studded ramps [Observed]. The crane and the towers are scenery, not climbable [Unverified].
- **Sightlines.**
  - Across the deck between the staggered screens.
  - Down through an open bay or over the edge, past the bare red frame, to a pale neighbouring facade and the flat brown roof with its pale balustrade far below [Observed]. Any streets are [BBG News invention].
  - Up at the crane jib and hook against the sky.
  - Out to the sunset skyline on one horizon.
- **Camera spots** [BBG News invention, on the Observed plan]:
  - low behind a tarp, with the blue sheen in the foreground (dossier, Prompt pack, spot 1);
  - at the deck edge, looking down past the red columns (spot 2);
  - on a plank walkway under the hook, looking up (spot 3);
  - outside the deck in mid-air, showing the thin platform on its red columns (the wiki's SV4 view [Observed]);
  - high overhead, as in the trailer (classic 2013 look; see the trailer-match note in §6).

## 3. Look in the house style

Follow STYLE.md (The look; Sets and light) and overview §5.3: keep the layout, landmark silhouettes, materials, palette family and light sources, re-render in the feature-film finish, add no ink outlines, and never write "low-poly". Keep one landmark per shot (overview §5.3 item 13).

**Shapes.**
- Sturdy, softly bevelled I-beams with neat, rounded bolt holes.
- Plump plank walkways with rounded board ends.
- Smooth plywood boards on rounded square posts.
- Taut, softly curved tarps.
- A crisp, readable crane lattice with rounded joints.
- Simplified tower blocks with clean panel grids.

**Materials** [Observed] (dossier, Look, Materials), re-described:
- **Beams.** Riveted red-oxide steel becomes clean matte-painted steel with soft rounded rivets and a slight sheen on the lit edges (STYLE.md, Robot parts, extended to set metal).
- **Grating.** Black ribbed grating stays black, with soft, even ribs that read as a dark pattern.
- **Wood.** Pale blond plywood and planks with visible grain become smooth matte wood with soft, simple grain.
- **Plates.** Grey steel with raised round studs becomes clean painted metal with plump rounded studs. They are not see-through.
- **Tarps.** Glossy blue plastic with streaky cyan-white highlights and a wave-like sheen becomes smooth, glossy, taut plastic with soft specular streaks. The tarps never flap.
- **Crane.** Yellow cross-braced lattice with an A-frame apex, black pendant cables, a grey counterweight block and a grey hook block becomes cleanly painted yellow metal with rounded struts and a matte grey block.
- **Towers.** Warm grey-white panel-and-glass grids with flat dark-brown roofs and lighter rims become clean simplified facades with soft glass that reflects teal and sky tones, and pale glowing windows.
- **Grid panels.** Orange-brown with a square grid pattern [Observed]. What they are is [Open]; render them as matte orange-brown squares that keep the square grid pattern; do not make them pallets or tiles.

**Palette, in words:**
- **Steel:** deep red-oxide, warming to coral-rose on lit edges.
- **Grating:** near-black warm brown.
- **Wood:** honey-blond to warm tan.
- **Plates and posts:** warm mid-grey.
- **Tarps:** glossy sky-cyan blue, with cyan-white highlights and deep cobalt folds.
- **Crane:** bright golden yellow, with amber-brown shade.
- **Towers:** warm ivory to blush-white facades, cocoa-brown roofs.
- **Sky:** deep navy with small white stars, purple, magenta-rose, then a rose-peach horizon behind a hazy pale-gold skyline.
- **Team colour:** no team-colour accents are documented in Sky View's scenery. Scenery team colour stays an accent (overview §3, §5.6 item 18); the PtB bomb and stand are team-coloured (§2).

**Lighting, classic 2013 look (the default)** [Observed] (dossier, Look, Lighting and time of day):
- It is dusk turning to night. The light is low and warm: amber on the plank tops and salmon on the tower faces, with tower windows glowing pale.
- Shading is soft and baked, with no crisp sun shadows. There is no weather and there are no particles; the trailer's white puffs are gameplay smoke.
- No lamps or floodlights are seen [Observed absence]. The map's own light is the sunset glow.
- **House rig.** The low warm sunset glow from the horizon side is the key (overview §5.3 item 11). Add a soft cool fill from the navy sky and a gentle rim, with soft global illumination, cinematic depth of field and one setup per scene (STYLE.md habit 2).

**Lighting, between 2013 and 2026:** BBG 3.0 (Nov 15, 2021) listed "New Map Lighting" game-wide [Game text via wiki]. Its effect on Sky View is unknown. ## 5. Hazards, gags and easter eggs

**Verified** (dossier, Hazards and interactive elements):
- **Falling.** "If you fall into the city, you will suicide," because of "the numerous gaps and the fact that there are no walls" [Fan-wiki]. "The smallest misstep at the beginning of the match could result in a Suicide" [Fan-wiki].
- **Shield.** Shielded players "can still die by falling off a stage" [Fan-wiki] (conventions §6 and §9).
- **Bomb carrier.** A carrier who falls sets the bomb off at once [Fan-wiki, a general PtB rule]. That it applies here is [Inference].
- **Exposure.** The teams are close together and cover is thin; "surviving on this map without deaths is very difficult" [Fan-wiki].
- **No jump pads** [Fan-wiki]. **The crane is not interactive** [Unverified]; treat it as scenery.
- **Old ramp bug.** "There used to be a glitch where would get stuck in the ramp" [Fan-wiki].
- **Not on this map:** lava, water, fire, weather or particles [Observed].

**Easter eggs** (dossier, Easter eggs and references; Setting):
- **The name** is "most likely a pun on 'SkyVu'" [Fan-wiki]. No SkyVu text confirms it [Unverified].
- **The Gold Digger link.** Glitch out of Gold Digger and "the background is the same as [[Sky View]]"; the wiki also says the two maps share a skybox [Fan-wiki]. The 2013 frames differ (dusk here; Gold Digger's starry, moonlit sky in the Steam trailer, 2013 footage) [Observed] (conventions §10). Whether the shared-skybox claim is wrong is [Open]: the sky samples may differ by build or grade, and the wiki's "same red urban backdrop" and "with all of its buildings" point to the city backdrop (dossier, Sky and backdrop; Easter eggs).
- **The joke.** The city is a "nondescript city" [Canon per our notes; Unverified this session]. The city is never named [Unverified].
- **A first.** It was "the first map to be on top of a building or in any urban area" [Fan-wiki].

**Gag ideas [BBG News invention]** (none may appear in a "what's in BBG 2026" post):
- **The First Step.** A bear spawns facing the edge, takes one confident step, and the camera tilts down past the red columns to the flat brown roof far below (dossier, Prompt pack, spot 2; the hazard is [Fan-wiki]). Any streets in the shot are [BBG News invention].
- **Invincible, briefly.** A bear grabs the Shield beside its spawn, struts in its green shields and walks off the edge. The rule is [Fan-wiki]; the strut is invention.
- **Tarp-line bomb grab.** The bomb waits on the enemy side of the big tarp, and the carrier tiptoes past a whole enemy team "extremely close by" [Fan-wiki] (spot 1). The bomb is a team-coloured round bomb on a same-colour stand, carried to the enemy tube [Fan-wiki; Observed 2012]; its 2-minute fuse pulses in the last 30 s, the carrier cannot attack, and falling with the bomb detonates it [Fan-wiki]. The 2026 tube model is [Unverified]. Stage the carrier unarmed.
- **Under the Hook.** A hero pose under the dangling hook against the sunset (spot 3). Any crane movement is a fan take (overview §5.1 item 2).
- **Stuck on the ramp.** A bear wedged on a studded ramp, legs cycling: the old bug, captioned as a fan-wiki report of an old bug [Fan-wiki], never as official (overview §5.1 item 1).

**Teen rating.** Cartoon violence only.
- A fall is a cartoon whistle-drop and a cut away, or an intact pop back at the spawn. Never show an impact.
- The "SUICIDE" wording stays off screen (overview §5.5 item 16).
