## 1. What it is

- **Gold Digger**, spelled that way on Steam ("classics such as No Bears Land, Gold Digger and Space Oddity") [Canon] and as soundtrack track 4, "Gold Digger", 0:38 [Canon].
  - **Setting:** a night-time open-pit gold mine [Inference]. An oval pit open to a navy starry sky is walled by tiers of stacked rock blocks, with timber-roofed rail tunnels leading off it [Observed]. The wiki says "a mining cave" [Fan-wiki], but only the side tunnels have a roof (dossier, Setting).
- **Era.** It is not a BBR map [Inference]. It was one of the "3 New Maps" for the BBG launch in May–June 2013 [Canon: 2013 trailer description; launch date Fan-wiki].
- **2026.** Steam names it among the returning "Iconic Maps" [Canon].
  - Its tile appears in the PICK LEVEL carousel of SkyVu's Oct 15 stream [Observed].
  - It appears in Oct 15 final-playtest frames, build 3.0.7, with a red-vs-blue HUD [Observed]. The fan chapter calls that match Team Battle [Unverified]. These are InstableGR's frames (HKbwq7PolNc), the dossier's only sampled 2026 source.
  - It also appears in JustNubz's final-playtest recording (1MP1rFi4t-Q), JN 23:10–25:00 [Observed per lineup addendum §6], a second Oct 15 source. Neither the dossier nor this sheet has colour-sampled it.
  - It appears in SkyVu's Sep 20 stream (ejjsoKQ-VXU, EJ 18:20–20:30) as a rock pit with a "BOMB HOLE WARNING" sign [Observed] (lineup addendum §6). That is before the Oct 2026 pass, and no frame of it has been colour-sampled.
- **Steam footage.** The Steam trailer's Gold Digger shots (1.5–5.0 s, 7.0–10.0 s and 51.9–54.0 s) and Steam screenshot screen_03 are 2013 footage [Inference, strong]. Caption them **"classic 2013 look"**, never "2026 gameplay" (overview §0 item 1).

## 2. Layout for staging

**Plan.** The map is an oval bowl, mostly on one level, with deadly shafts below and raised ledges at the walls [Observed]. It looks mirror-symmetric [Inference]. Which side is red and which is blue, and which way the map faces, are not documented [Unverified] (dossier, Layout).

- **Centre: the twin shafts.**
  - Two deep shafts sit on a diagonal, one kidney-shaped and one a rounded teardrop. From above they read as a lopsided figure 8 [Observed].
  - Grey slab stones ring the rims. The walls are sheer and streaked olive-brown, and they drop into pure black [Observed].
- **The "bridge".**
  - It is a narrow dirt path edged with stones, at floor level, running between the shafts [Observed]. The wiki calls it "a bridge in the middle of the map" [Fan-wiki].
  - It looks about one to two bears wide [Inference].
  - The Bearscream Sandwich sits "at the middle of the bridge" [Fan-wiki].
  - It is not raised and not made of planks.
- **The floor ring.**
  - Packed ochre dirt runs between the shafts and the walls [Observed].
  - Big rounded grey boulders at the shaft lips give cover [Observed].
  - Soft oval pools of light lie on the dirt [Observed].
- **The foot of the wall.**
  - Curving stone-curbed paths and ledges run along it [Observed].
  - Two gold carts sit on opposite ledges, each on a pale stone-paved patch [Observed].
  - Diagonal timber braces lean against the walls [Observed].
- **Openings.** Four kinds appear [Observed]:
  - open timber-framed tunnel mouths with lit cobble interiors;
  - a timber mouth closed low by a plank board;
  - wide openings closed by a post-and-rail fence, with mine track behind;
  - one long, straight, roofed rail tunnel (the map icon).
- **Spawns.**
  - Team-coloured gems mark the spawn sides [Fan-wiki; Observed].
  - Two fenced openings are seen: a red glow at the right post with a gold cart on the ledge to its left (`Gold_Digger_2.jpg`), and a blue gem at the right post with a cart to its left (trailer 52.1 s) [Observed]. One per team side, as a mirror pair, is [Inference].
  - The spawns are probably the tunnel areas behind the gem-marked openings [Inference]. Health Packs are "Inside the spawn area near the central cave" [Fan-wiki].
- **Lanes** [BBG News invention, on the Observed plan]:
  - the bridge is the risky centre lane;
  - the dirt ring on each side of the shafts gives two flanks.
- **Vertical spots.**
  - **Jump pads:** two sit in the dirt ring on opposite sides of the shafts [Observed]. Where they send players is [Unverified].
  - **Raised ledge:** a pickup floats on a raised ledge left of the blue fenced opening [Observed]. That it is the Imperial Basket Grande is [Inference].
  - **Upper tiers:** nothing documents players standing on them, so treat them as backdrop.
- **Sightlines.**
  - Across the shafts from one gem side to the other.
  - Straight down the rail tunnel under its lamps [Observed tunnel].
  - Up the stacked wall to the moon [Observed: trailer 4.0 s].
  - The high overhead [Observed: trailer 52.0–53.8 s].
- **Scale.**
  - A mine cart is about as long as a bear is tall [Observed, approximate] (conventions §11).
  - Doors are about 1.5–2× a bear's height, and railings and fences (the post-and-rail fences here) stand at knee to waist height [Inference] (overview §3, where "rails" means railings). Mine rails lie on sleepers at floor level [Observed].
- **Camera positions** [BBG News invention]:
  1. **Bridge end.** Low and centred, looking along the path, with both shafts falling to black on either side and boulders framing the edges. This is the standoff shot.
  2. **Shaft lip.** Over the shoulder, looking down into the black. Never show a bottom.
  3. **Gem gate.** Eye-level medium on a fenced opening: gem at the right post, cart on the ledge to the left.
  4. **Lamp tunnel.** Locked-off, one-point perspective down the rails to the cart at the end, with lamp cones overhead.
  5. **Moon rim.** Low on the pit floor, looking up the stacked wall to the moon. Use it for the classic look only.
  6. **High three-quarter over the bowl,** the trailer's overhead. Use it as an opener only.

  The pit is open to the sky, so the camera may hang above it. The tunnels have a roof, so inside them keep the camera at bear height.

## 3. Look in the house style

Follow STYLE.md (The look; Sets and light) and overview §5.3:
- Keep the layout, the landmark silhouettes, what each thing is made of, the palette family and the light sources.
- Re-render every surface in the feature-film finish.
- No ink outlines, and never write "low-poly".
- The gameplay frames are very dark. Raise the exposure for shorts but keep the hue relationships [Inference] (dossier, Look).

**Shapes.** The game uses chunky low-poly shapes with simple painted textures [Observed]. Re-render them as:
- plump, softly bevelled rock blocks in uneven tiers;
- big rounded boulders;
- rounded timber posts and beams;
- small cartoon mine carts on little round wheels;
- squat rounded octagonal lamps;
- bold readable shaft outlines seen from above.

**Materials** [Observed] (dossier, Look, Materials), re-described:
- **Pit walls.** Cube-like rock blocks with cobblestone-pattern faces and dark grout lines become soft matte stone blocks with gently rounded edges and soft dark seams.
- **Shaft walls.** Sheer olive-brown faces become broad, smooth, softly bevelled planes that fade into pure black.
- **Tunnel walls.** Bigger, lighter, rounded cobbles. Render them as plump, pale, rounded cobblestones.
- **Tunnel ceiling.** Earthen orange-brown. Render it as soft matte packed earth.
- **Timber.** Plain brown wood. Render it rounded, with soft grain.
- **Rails.** Dark riveted metal on wooden sleepers. Render clean painted metal with soft rounded rivets (STYLE.md, Robot parts, extended to set metal).
- **Gold load.** A flat glowing slab that fills each cart, with no separate nuggets. Render it as a smooth surface of soft emissive gold with a gentle bloom that lights the cart rim and the nearby ground (STYLE.md, Glows).
- **Team gems.** Faceted. Render crisp cut-crystal facets with smooth bevelled edges and a soft inner glow (STYLE.md, Faceted effects wording, borrowed for a set prop).
- **Gold flecks.** Small yellow flecks on the tunnel floor. Render them as tiny soft glints.

**Palette, in words:**
- **Rock:** warm charcoal with a faint plum cast, never cool grey.
- **Boulders and rim slabs:** warm grey with a plum tint.
- **Floor:** tan-ochre packed dirt.
- **Shafts:** olive-brown walls into pure black.
- **Tunnels:** a warm amber-brown floor, warm grey-brown cobbles, an earthen orange-brown ceiling and dark brown timber.
- **Glows:** a golden-yellow load that brightens to lemon-gold, pale warm yellow-white lamp lenses and icy cyan pad centres.
- **Gems:** a vivid royal-blue gem and a deep crimson-ruby gem.
- **Sky and moon:** a deep navy sky with small white stars, and a pale blue-white moon with a soft halo.
- **Team colour** appears only as the gem accents (overview §3).

**Lighting, classic 2013 look (the default)** [Observed] (dossier, Look, Lighting and time of day):
- It is night. The key is low and warm. It comes from the glowing gold loads, the tunnel lamps (whose light cone shows in the map icon) and soft oval pools of light on the dirt.
- The only cool tones are the navy sky and the moon. No moonlight shows on the walls; the rock stays warm dark brown right up to its crests. This corrects the earlier drafts.
- No weather or particles are seen. The only glows are the gold, the gems, the lamps and the pads.
- **House rig.** Warm gold and lamp glow is the key (overview §5.3 item 11). Add a soft cool fill from the night sky, which lifts the shadows without painting the rock crests blue. The moon sits in the sky as a visible source, not as a rim on the stone. Keep a gentle rim on the characters, soft global illumination and cinematic depth of field, with one setup per scene (STYLE.md habit 2).
- **Conflict to flag.** Overview §5.3 item 11 and STYLE.md's example scene line say "cool moon fill" / "cool moonlit fill". This sheet reads the fill as sky ambience, because the dossier sees no moonlight on the rock [Observed]. Flag it for the overview.

**Lighting, 2026 build (3.0.7) look** [Observed, low resolution] (dossier, Era and status, "Lighting"; Look, "2026 comparison"; Corrections item 8):
- **How the 3.0.7 frames differ from the 2013 footage [Observed, low-res].** The 2013 scheme stays: night sky, rock walls, carts, gems and the timber rail tunnel. The frames read slightly brighter, greyer and less orange. The sky is a deep teal-navy a shade lighter than classic navy (#0E2B40 is still dark), the rock a greyer warm brown, the floor a duller ochre, and the gold samples duller. HUD, characters and video encoding inflate these numbers, so they are only an indication (dossier).
- **The moon.** No moon is in view in the one sky frame sampled [Observed]. JustNubz's JN 23:10–25:00 frames have not been checked for it.
- **The cause.** The 2021 BBG 3.0 "New Map Lighting", the Oct 2026 pass, and/or capture and encoding [Inference]. The dossier compares only 2013 Steam footage with 3.0.7 frames. The pre-pass Sep 20 frames (EJ 18:20–20:30) were never sampled as a baseline. The fan claim that the pass restores the old look does not name this map [Unverified]. Conventions addendum §5 measures No Bear's Land, Desert Airmine and Space Oddity (Frozen Pundra incidentally); overview §3 summarises six maps; none is Gold Digger. The addendum's open question names Gold Digger as unmeasured.
- **House rig.** Use the classic rig with a slightly lifted, more neutral night ambience, greyer stone, a deep teal-navy sky a shade lighter than classic navy and no moon in frame. The 3.0.7 frames count as the 2026 look (overview §5.1 item 3; §7 below), but they are low-res, so this rig is provisional. Before the first 2026-look short, re-check it against the JN 23:10–25:00 frames, with the Sep 20 EJ 18:20–20:30 frames as a pre-pass baseline, and against full-resolution Next Fest demo captures once they exist.

## 5. Hazards, gags and easter eggs

**Verified** (dossier, Hazards and interactive elements):
- **The shafts.** "If you fall off the bridge, and into a black pit, you will suicide." [Fan-wiki] They render as bottomless black [Observed].
  - A Shield does not save you: shielded players "can still die by falling off a stage" [Fan-wiki].
  - In Plant the Bomb, a carrier who falls into a pit sets off the bomb at once [Fan-wiki; this is a general rule, not one specific to Gold Digger].
- **Jump pads.** They launch players "either vertically or at an angle" [Fan-wiki]. This map's trajectories are [Unverified].
- **Spawn shield.** It lasts 4.5 s "to discourage spawn killing" [Fan-wiki].
- **No set pieces documented.** SkyVu's blurb mentions "rickety old wooden beams between you and a horrible, horrible death" [Canon per our notes; Unverified this session]. No collapsing beams, moving carts or other interactive set pieces are documented [Unverified].
- **Not on this map:** lava, water, fire, weather or particles. None is documented [Observed].

**Easter eggs and trivia** (dossier, Easter eggs and references; Era and status):
- **Sky View outside.** Glitch out of the map and "the background is the same as [[Sky View]], with all of its buildings"; the Sky View page also says the two maps share a skybox [Fan-wiki]. The Gold Digger frames show no magenta dusk glow, so the fit is only partial [Observed; Inference] (conventions §10).
- **The "Red advantage" trivia.** It says only Red had an Imperial Basket Grande [Fan-wiki]. The wiki's own history contradicts it, and the Sep 2026 edit puts one on both sides [Fan-wiki].
- **The 2.6.1 spawn bug.** A class "will no longer spawn with Bearscream Sandwich on Gold Digger" (Jan 4, 2014) [Game text via wiki]. Which class is [Open]: Arbiter is likelier than Assault [Inference].
- **The name.** "Gold digger" is an idiom for chasing money, and the blurb's "equally avaricious opponents" plays on it [Inference]. Use it for greed gags only.
- **Music.** The map's own track is soundtrack track 4 [Canon]. Nobody has heard it [Observed absence], and shorts use original music only (STYLE.md, Sound).

**Gag ideas [BBG News invention]** (none may appear in a "what's in BBG 2026" post):
- **Bridge standoff.** Two bears inch toward the Sandwich from opposite ends while a pebble drops into the black and never lands (dossier, Prompt pack, staging spot 1).
- **The cart won't budge.** A greedy bear heaves at a glowing cart to wheel the gold home, and it does not move an inch. This plays the documented static prop as the joke, instead of inventing a runaway cart.
- **Born lucky.** A bear respawns already holding the Sandwich, blinks at it, then at camera. This plays the 2.6.1 bug; caption the bug as patch-note history, not a 2026 feature.
- **Spotlight reveal.** A bear steps into a lamp cone in the rail tunnel (dossier, staging spot 2).
- **Gem-lit huddle.** A pre-match huddle at the gem gate, with the team gem as a coloured accent light on faces (dossier, staging spot 3). Keep the scene's base lighting fixed.
- **Moon-rim hero pose.** A silhouette against the full moon from the pit floor. Use it for classic-look shorts only (dossier, staging spot 4).

**Teen rating.** Cartoon violence only.
- A fall into a shaft is a cartoon whistle-drop and a cut away, or an intact pop back at the spawn. No impact or injury is ever shown.
- The game's "SUICIDE" wording stays off screen (overview §5.5 item 16).
- The idiom stays about greed, never about a person.
