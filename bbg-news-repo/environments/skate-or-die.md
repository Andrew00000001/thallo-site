## 1. What it is

- **Skate or Die.** SkyVu spells it "Skate or Die" in the 2013 trailer and "Skate Or Die" on the 2026 soundtrack (track 7) [Canon], and "SKATE OR DIE" on the map icon and in the 2025 short [Observed]. It is a sunny, open-air, walled concrete skatepark under two crossing elevated highway decks, with Battle Bears graffiti on its walls, piers and floor [Fan-wiki; Observed]. It reads as an Earth-like urban underpass [Inference]; where it sits in the Battle Bears world is [Unverified] (dossier, Setting).
- **Era:** one of the "3 New Maps" of the 2013 BBG launch [Canon], not a BBR map (dossier, Era and status). **2026:** the Steam text does not name it [Canon], but it is soundtrack track 7 [Canon]. It appears in SkyVu's 2025 short "Dangerous Skate Parks" [Canon date; Observed]. SkyVu's own 2026 videos show it (the Sep 20 livestream, the Aug 16 "Official Playtest Highlights" C7uFxvSZ10c and the Short "Playtest Highlights Part 1") [Observed; map Inference] (overview §2.3; lineup §2.2). It is seen in fan 2026 playtest thumbnails (Jul 29, Aug 21) [Observed]; the modes, Team Battle and FFA, come from fan chapter text [Unverified] (overview label table). That it ships on Nov 4 is [Inference, strong]; overview §2.3 rates it "Very likely".
- **Official footage:** its Steam trailer shot (≈46.0–47.2 s) is 2013 footage [Inference, strong]; caption it "classic 2013 look". None of the five Steam screenshots shows this map [Observed] (dossier, Era and status, "The Steam trailer's Skate or Die shot"; overview §0 item 1).

## 2. Layout for staging

No top-down plan exists. The layout comes from the trailer's overhead and from ground-level shots, and overview §7 rates it "partial" (dossier, Layout).

- **Overall:** a large, roughly rectangular walled plaza, mostly on one level, with sunken bowls and raised platforms, banks and ramps [Observed]. The wiki calls it "very wide and open" [Fan-wiki].
- **Three working levels:** sunken bowls, the main floor, and raised platforms, banks and the half-pipe [Observed]. The highway decks are scenery overhead, and no source shows players on them [Unverified], so never stage action on top of them.
- **Spawns:** "The two team's spawn points are very large on this map, extending almost the width of the map" [Fan-wiki]. FFA uses the same points [Fan-wiki]. No spawn rooms or doors are documented [Unverified], and where the spawns sit on the ground is [Unverified]. A respawn reads as a bear appearing in the open plaza inside the pale-green spawn shield (conventions §4).
- **Team sides** (dossier, Layout, "Teams and spawns") [Fan-wiki]:
  - **Red:** "the large "pool"" holds a Shield; a Bearscream Sandwich sits "on a large raised half-pipe at the corner of the map, on the Red team's left side"; an Imperial Basket Grande sits "on the large raised platform, closer to the Red team's side".
  - **Blue:** a Shield "behind a huge column"; an Imperial Basket Grande "next to a curved ramp"; a Bearscream Sandwich "behind a ramp ... leading up to the large raised platform".
  - The pickup pages place them differently [Fan-wiki]. The halves are not mirror images [Inference], and which on-screen landmark faces which team is [Unverified].
- **Centre:** an overpass pier stands mid-plaza with a black box against it. The box has steel edges and a ramp sloping off one side, and a small white box with orange marks sits on top under a hovering orange glow [Observed]. That box is the Health Pack [Inference, strong]. The wiki puts a Health Pack on "a large hill near the center", "the large spire directly in the middle of the map, facing the Red team's side" and "On top of the central ramp" [Fan-wiki]; that this hill or spire is the observed central pier and black box is [Inference].
- **Lanes, from the trailer overhead** [Observed]:
  - **The long painted pool:** a long, irregular, river-shaped dry bowl running diagonally down the left side, with steel coping and a wooden-faced bank along its far edge. Characters move in it [Observed], so it is a walkable bowl [Inference].
  - **The small painted pool:** a smaller, angular bowl right of the central pier, in front of the rainbow block.
  - **The street-course props:** a honey-coloured plywood fun-box/kicker with a rust-red rail, a long black open-topped steel ledge (a trough), short orange flat rails, and three short black ledges in a row by the back wall. Their exact positions are unplaced, apart from the three ledges by the back wall.
- **The far wall, left to right** [Observed]: the meadow mural behind the wooden-faced bank; a black-and-white graffito; two wooden A-frame wedge ramps; the giant camera eye with its "ABBi" tag and black cable; a raised concrete block painted with a rainbow ending in a skull; the pier tagged "Oops, Killed ya!"; then, on the right-hand wall, the large pink bone-limbed Huggable. The meadow is left of the central pier and the lens right of it [Observed: 2026 grid tile and 2013 flythrough].
- **Pieces seen at ground level but not placed on a plan** [Observed]:
  - a tall wooden quarter-pipe or half-pipe with orange coping in a corner, near the orange kite graffito and the TV mural;
  - a wide raised wooden platform on chunky wooden box legs that players can walk under;
  - concrete stairs with steel handrails rising to a raised level in front of the poster wall;
  - concrete banks, hips and humps, one with a drain grate;
  - an octagonal bolted steel pipe mouth with a rusty orange interior, set in a hump at the edge of the painted pool. It is presumably one of the wiki's "tunnels" [Inference].
- **Jump pads:** none. "Skate or Die, Sky View, and Toxic Terror are the only maps that contain no Jump Pads" [Fan-wiki] (conventions §7).
- **Sightlines and cover:** "Due to its large size Snipers can be very effective, although cover is very abundant and useful for breaking locks" [Fan-wiki]. Above the walls there is only sky, a billboard on a pole and a second billboard on posts [Observed].
- **Scale:** rails at knee to waist height [Inference] (overview §3, a general rule of thumb); ledge heights are not measured. This map has no measured ratios. The piers are massive and the half-pipe tall [Observed].
- **Camera positions** [BBG News invention]. Use one landmark per shot (overview §5.3 item 13):
  1. **Low at the rim of the long painted pool,** looking across the street course to the central pier and the far wall. This is the dossier's establishing set-up.
  2. **High over the plaza,** matching the trailer's overhead: the pool's diagonal, the street course and the far wall in one frame, with the decks cutting across the top. The map is open-air, so the camera can hang above the plaza.
  3. **Locked-off medium at eye level facing the camera eye.** This is the punchline spot.
  4. **Low angle at the foot of a hammerhead pier,** looking up at the deck's underside against the sky.
  5. **Inside the dry bowl at floor level,** looking up the curved wall to the coping, with painted foam underfoot.
  6. **On the half-pipe deck,** looking down into the transition.
  7. **Between the box legs under the raised wooden platform:** a hiding or ambush shot.
  - Keep the lens, faces and pickups out of the bottom fifth and the right edge (STYLE.md, Frame).

## 3. Look in the house style

Per STYLE.md (The look; Sets and light) and overview §5.3, keep the layout, the landmark silhouettes, the materials, the palette family and the light sources, and re-render every surface in the feature-film finish. Use no ink outlines, and never write "low-poly".

- **Shapes:** the game uses chunky, hard-edged, low-poly geometry with hand-painted textures [Observed] (conventions §12). Re-render these as soft, bevelled forms with bold silhouettes:
  - tall board-formed walls as smooth panels with gently rounded seams and neat rows of soft tie-hole dimples;
  - massive hammerhead piers with plump, rounded T-heads, carrying thick, softly rounded deck slabs;
  - bowls with smooth, rounded transitions and chunky, rounded steel coping;
  - plywood ramps and fun-boxes with rounded edges and steel kick-plates;
  - ledges and boxes as chunky black blocks with rounded, bright steel edges, and rails as chunky rounded tubes;
  - the camera eye as a plump round bezel, and its cable as a thick, softly sagging rubber tube;
  - graffiti as clean, flat spray-paint artwork with soft overspray edges that follows the surfaces, with no letters.
- **Materials** [Observed] (dossier, Look, Materials), re-described:
  - **Concrete:** soft-matte and gently mottled; large square paving slabs with scuffs and spray stains as soft painted marks, never gritty, photoreal grime. The overpass concrete is charcoal and matte.
  - **Bowl floors:** smooth satin paint over concrete, with flat painted foam swirls. It is paint, not water.
  - **Plywood:** warm, rounded wood with soft visible grain.
  - **Metal:** black-painted steel with bright, clean edges; rust-orange rails as a smooth painted patina; the pipe ring as bolted steel with soft painted rust. This is clean painted metal (STYLE.md, Robot parts, extended to set metal).
  - **Cable:** thick, soft black rubber.
  - **Glows:** the lens as soft emissive blue around a near-white core, with a gentle bloom on the wall; the centre pickup's soft orange glow (STYLE.md, Glows).
  - **Weather and particles:** none [Observed]. The only clouds are painted ones in the murals [Observed].
- **Palette, in words:**
  - **Sky:** cloudless cornflower blue at the zenith, fading to a pale peach or lavender-grey haze at the horizon. One 2013 screenshot shows a deeper blue.
  - **Walls:** warm cream-beige in sun, cool dark grey-mauve in shade.
  - **Floor:** warm taupe-brown slabs in sun, deep charcoal-plum in shadow.
  - **Decks and piers:** charcoal.
  - **Plywood:** bright honey-orange in the trailer's sun; darker mid-brown in the wiki screenshots.
  - **Rails:** rust-orange to rust-brown. **Ledges:** near-black.
  - **Painted water:** pale aqua with white foam in the wiki screenshots and 2026 thumbnails; deep slate-blue with pale-cyan highlights in the trailer. The goldfish is orange.
  - **Graffiti accents:** grass-green meadow; pink teddy (dusty magenta in the wiki, bubblegum pink in the trailer); an orange rainbow band; a green skull outline; a teal-blue tag; a brick-red tag; a teal and magenta TV mural.
  - **Camera eye:** a deep blue lens with a near-white cyan core, and a warm grey bezel and shutter.
  - **Team colour:** no team-coloured scenery is documented on this map. Keep team colour on the characters. Any scenery accent is a [BBG News invention] and stays an accent (overview §3).

These are palette families, about ±15% [Inference] (overview §5.3 item 12; dossier, Look, Palette). - **Lighting, classic 2013 look (the default)** [Observed] (dossier, Look):
  - Clear, cloudless daylight. Sunlit concrete reads warm cream-beige, and the decks and piers cast long, hard shadows. It reads as late afternoon [Inference]; STYLE.md files the map under "day".
  - The trailer is warmer and more saturated than the wiki screenshots, with bright orange plywood, a warmer floor and deeper-blue water [Observed]. This is probably the "HD Graphics" mode or the trailer's grade [Inference].
  - **House rig:** the sun is the map's practical light, so it is the warm key (STYLE.md, Key light). Set it fairly low so the decks throw long, crisp shadows across the plaza. Add a cool sky-blue fill and a gentle warm rim on the coping, pier edges and rails. The lens glow and the centre pickup's orange glow are the emissive anchors (overview §5.3 item 11). Finish with soft global illumination and cinematic depth of field. Never grade it as golden hour or sunset, and keep one setup per scene (STYLE.md, habit 2).
  - **One grade per short:** the default is a hybrid of two 2013 sources, taken from the dossier's prompt pack: the wiki screenshots' pale-aqua water with the trailer's honey-orange plywood (in the wiki screenshots the plywood is dark mid-brown) [BBG News invention]. A trailer-matched short swaps in deep slate-blue water with pale-cyan highlights and warmer surfaces, keeping the honey plywood. Pick one of these two grades per short and never switch between them within it.
- **Lighting, Oct 2026 lighting pass: not in the research for this map.**
  - The pass is official ("Lighting improvements", Oct 15) [Canon]. It was measured only on No Bear's Land, Desert Airmine, Space Oddity, Haunted Castle, Toxic Terror and Frozen Pundra (overview §3; conventions addendum §5).
  - An earlier pass came first: v3.0 (Nov 15, 2021) lists "New Map Lighting" for the whole game [Game text via wiki] (dossier, Era and status; overview §0 item 5). The Jul–Sep 2026 builds presumably carried that 2021 lighting [Inference] (conventions addendum §5). So the pre-pass 2026 thumbnails are most likely a third look, neither "classic 2013" nor "2026".
  - Pre-pass 2026 playtest thumbnails show this map in the same daylight with a bright blue sky and muted aqua paint [Observed, low-res]. Finer changes cannot be judged [Unverified], and pre-Oct-15 footage is not proof of the 2026 look (overview §5.1 item 3). The final lighting may differ [Inference].
  - Lineup §2.2 lists a fan final-playtest (Oct 15) image of this map, ECARJ0b3T-0. It has not been analysed for lighting; check it before any 2026-look claim, and use no "2026 look" caption until it is.
  - So there is **no 2026-pass prompt variant**. Do not borrow another map's measured shift. This stays [Open] until a UI-free capture from the demo (overview §7, gap 2).

## 5. Hazards, gags and easter eggs

**From the verified dossier, labels as given** (dossier, Hazards and interactive elements):
- **No environmental hazards** are documented [Unverified], and none are visible: no pits, liquid, traps or moving vehicles [Observed]. **The "water" is paint** [Observed] (overview §5.1 item 2).
- **No jump pads** [Fan-wiki].
- **Skating:** the ramps, rails and ledges are terrain and cover. No skate or grind mechanic is documented [Unverified].
- **The Bearscream Sandwich** "doubles the damage of all the player's weapons for 20 seconds" and respawns every 90 s [Fan-wiki]; another wiki page says triple [Fan-wiki, conflicting] (conventions §6). On this map the two Sandwiches "are placed at opposite ends of the map with a huge open space in between" [Fan-wiki].
- **Unidentified glow:** a white double-line octagonal ring crosses the trailer overhead at ≈46.0–46.2 s. It is probably a weapon or effect ring, not map geometry [Inference; Unverified]. Never build it into the set.
- **Weapon effects in the trailer shot:** the yellow tracer dots and the golden half-dome blast at the base of the black box by the central pier (≈46.8–47.2 s) [Observed] are weapon effects (dossier, Corrections item 1). Never build them into the set either.
- **Plant the Bomb and King of the Windmill** positions are [Unverified].

**Easter eggs** (dossier, Easter eggs and references):
- **The Battle Bears TV mural:** a graffiti copy of the 2012 TV key art [Observed comparison]. SkyVu's Ben Vu announced the series in a letter dated Oct 4, 2012 [Canon, quoted from the wiki's mirror of the letter]; the wiki says it was "presumably cancelled" [Fan-wiki].
- **"Oops, Killed ya!":** the wiki gives Oliver the quote "Oops, killed ya!" [Fan-wiki]. Its appearance as in-game graffiti [Observed] means SkyVu used the phrase in BBG's own art [Inference].
- **ABBi:** the "ABBi" tag plus the camera eye, which the wiki captions "ABBi in Battle Bears Gold" [Observed; Fan-wiki].
- **"B-1000"** is tagged in red-pink under a red heart on a pier [Observed]. The B-1000 class launched with BBG and this map [Canon].
- **Characters on the walls,** per the wiki: "Oliver, Wil, Tillman, Riggs, ABBi, and Huggable" [Fan-wiki]. Oliver, Wil, Tillman, ABBi and Huggables are confirmed [Observed]; Riggs is not identified in any image [Unverified].
- **The rainbow ending in a skull:** linking it to the wiki's Huggable "rainbow blood" is speculation [Inference]. Never use that link (teen rating).
- **Tillman on wheels:** the wiki calls Tillman "the only Battle Bear to use skates/wheels as a main form of locomotion" [Fan-wiki], a natural tie-in [Inference].
- **SkyVu's own framing:** "Dangerous Skate Parks" lists it among the "TOP 3 DEADLIEST SKATE PARKS" [Observed]. That it ranked #1 is [Inference; Unverified].
- **Music:** "Skate Or Die", 1:29, track 7 [Canon], the longest of tracks 1–15 [Inference from Canon durations]. Nobody has heard it [Unverified].

**Gag ideas [BBG News invention]** (none may appear in a "what's in BBG 2026" post):
- **The painted-pool dive** (dossier, Staging spot 1). A bear dives into the "water" and boings off painted concrete in an intact cartoon face-plant. If a gag ever needs real water, its description must say it is a fan take (overview §5.1 item 2).
- **"Someone's watching"** (dossier, Staging spot 2; overview §4). Cut to the lens as a beat. Any glint, swivel or zoom is invention, because the lens never moves in any source.
- **Under his own catchphrase.** Oliver lands a hit right under the red tag, which stays abstract. If the line is spoken, keep it a playful quip, never a crude taunt (STYLE.md, habit 5).
- **Home turf for the bear on wheels** (dossier, Staging spot 3). He carves the half-pipe, or there is a scramble for the Health Pack on the black box. The skating itself is invention.
- **Lock-breaker.** A sniper's lock snaps off as the target ducks behind a fun-box ("cover ... useful for breaking locks" [Fan-wiki]). Add the lock brackets in the edit, never in the prompt (STYLE.md, Status sprites).
- **No pad here.** A bear stamps the floor, waiting for a launch that never comes, because this map has none [Fan-wiki].
- **The Sandwich sprint.** Two bears race for opposite Sandwiches across "a huge open space" [Fan-wiki]. The winner flares with the crackling magenta ring [Observed effect] (section 4, Pickups).

**Teen rating:** cartoon violence only. KOs and falls end in intact cartoon pratfalls or pops. The bone-limbed teddy stays a flat cartoon decal with no blood, and the game's "SUICIDE" wording stays off screen (overview §5.5 item 16).

**Establishing shot (image)**
> "Establishing shot from a low angle at the rim of a dry concrete skate bowl on a clear sunny day. In the foreground the smooth bowl floor is painted pale aqua with flat white foam swirls to look like rippling water, a painted orange goldfish leaps from a painted white splash, and chunky rounded steel coping runs along the rim. Beyond it stretches a skate plaza of large square warm taupe paving slabs, with a honey-coloured plywood fun-box carrying a rust-orange grind rail, and a black box with a ramp sloping off one side at the foot of a massive rounded charcoal concrete pier with a broad T-shaped cap; a small white case with an orange cross sits on the box under a soft orange glow. The pier and the black box stand in the centre of the frame; the bowl fills the lower third.
