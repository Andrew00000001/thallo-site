## 1. What it is

- **Name.** "Steamyard Blues", one word, is the spelling on the 2026 Steam store and the soundtrack; "Steam Yard Blues" is the spelling in SkyVu's 2014 preview title. Both are official [Canon]. Captions use "Steamyard Blues" (overview §5.4).
- **Setting.** An airship yard high in a dusk sky, open at the edges above a near-black maroon void [Observed; "steampunk airship yard" is the dossier's summary, Inference]. It has caged purple airships, roped-down shark-mouth fighter planes, gold gear platforms, dark plank decks and green tunnels [Observed] (dossier, intro; Setting).
- **Era.** Added in BBG 2.8. The wiki dates it Mar 4, 2014 [Fan-wiki]; SkyVu's preview is dated Feb 21, 2014 [Canon]. It launched with King of the Windmill, which was retired in 2.29 (Jan 26, 2018) [Fan-wiki]. It is not a BBR map.
- **2026.** It is named on Steam ("that yearly match on Steamyard Blues, most likely just for the soundtrack") and is soundtrack track 15, 0:51 [Canon]. It is seen in builds 3.0.2 and 3.0.7 [Observed]. The overview's verdict is "In" [Canon]; the dossier reads launch membership as [Inference], very likely (overview §2.3; dossier, Era and status).
- **Not in the Steam trailer or screenshots** [Observed]. Its classic reference is the 2014 preview.

## 2. Layout for staging

(Dossier, Layout; lineup addendum §1, Layout. No overhead exists; overview §7.)
- **Three levels** [Fan-wiki]. The wiki numbers them by importance, not height. The likely order from bottom to top is main ground, tunnels, deck [Inference].
- **Deck (the top).** It is reached by "four jump pads on the ground" [Fan-wiki].
  - The Sandwich sits on "a lead off on the deck at the highest part of the map" [Fan-wiki]. The dossier reads a "lead off" as a projecting ledge [Inference].
  - "On the deck, you can jump on the gear-like wall and go behind it" [Fan-wiki; logged as glitch trivia].
  - The likely gear wall is the cream-and-pink panelled wall studded with gold gears. Characters stand on the gold rail along its top in 2026 [Observed]. That it is the wiki's deck and gear wall is [Inference].
- **Main ground** ("the largest area") [Fan-wiki]:
  - It holds the IBG, two pads up to the deck and one Health Pack.
  - There is one plane on each side, each with a wing pad up into the tunnels.
  - Two slopes at the map's edge hold the two Shields and the last two deck pads, with a tunnel entrance just behind them.
- **Tunnels.** "A tunnel like structure with multiple paths", with the second Health Pack at the intersection [Fan-wiki]. A green tunnel with a roughly octagonal mouth looks out over an airship hull and a plane [Observed].
- **Centre** [Observed]:
  - a two-tier gold gear platform on the plank deck: a raised gear disc over a gear-shaped base whose arms hold two pads, with an orange cog set in;
  - just beyond it, two orange checker-woven drums with white sacks on the rims, gold gear lids and a pad on top.
  - These may be the pads up to the deck, and the centre is the wiki's "middle of the map" windmill spot [Inference].
- **Symmetry.** "One plane on each side" suggests mirrored halves [Inference]. The planes face each other across the deck, noses to the middle [Observed]. The pickups are not mirrored [Fan-wiki].
- **Jump pads.** There are at least six [Inference, from Fan-wiki text], on the plane wings, the drum lids and the gear platform [Observed].
- **Spawns and team sides** are undocumented [Unverified]. A red lead faces blue opponents by default (STYLE.md, Teams in frame). Spawn shield: pale-green shields with a plus emblem for 4.5 s [Fan-wiki; Observed] (conventions §4).
- **Edges.** Open, above the void [Observed]. "There is a ledge beneath the edge on an entire side of the map" [Fan-wiki].
- **Vertical spots:**
  - a wing pad flinging a bear up into a tunnel mouth (a 2014 fan frame looks from a tunnel mouth down onto a wing pad [Observed; route fit Inference]);
  - the gold rail on top of the gear wall [Observed];
  - the deck's lead-off [Fan-wiki];
  - the drum lids and the raised gear disc [Observed].
- **Sightlines:**
  - across the deck, nose to nose between the two planes;
  - from a tunnel mouth down to a wing;
  - up at the caged airship and the copper-column balloon;
  - out past the open edge to the yellow horizon glow and the mountains, and down into the void.
- **Scale.** Rails are knee to waist height and doors about 1.5–2× a bear's height [Inference] (overview §3).
- **Camera spots** [BBG News invention, on the Observed set]:
  - low on the planks between the two planes, their noses framing the gear platform (wide plate);
  - on a wing beside the shark mouth (dossier spot 1; lineup addendum spot 1);
  - inside a tunnel mouth, looking out and down;
  - on the gear-wall rail, or square-on to the wall (medium plate);
  - at the deck edge, a bear silhouetted against the yellow horizon (lineup addendum spot 3);
  - low angle up from a wing pad at the tunnel mouth (low-angle plate).

## 3. Look in the house style

Follow STYLE.md (The look; Sets and light) and overview §5.3: keep the layout, landmark silhouettes, materials, palette family and light sources; re-render them in the feature-film finish; add no ink outlines; never write "low-poly". Keep one landmark per shot (overview §5.3 item 13).

**Shapes:**
- plump brass ribs and lattice with soft rounded rivets;
- chunky gears with rounded teeth;
- taut, gently rounded envelopes;
- rounded, appealing propeller fighters with soft bevels;
- dark planks with rounded board ends;
- soft-bevelled polygonal tunnel mouths;
- squat woven drums.

**Materials** [Observed] (dossier, Look, Materials), re-described:
- **Brass and gold** (ribs, frames, gears, the nose spike): polished warm brass with a soft specular sheen and rounded rivets.
- **Copper column:** burnished copper, octagonal and softly riveted.
- **Envelopes:** soft matte purple fabric with a faint sheen, in taut panels (STYLE.md, Cloth).
- **Planes:** riveted slate-blue painted metal with fine white speckle becomes clean painted metal with rounded rivets (STYLE.md, Robot parts, extended to set metal).
- **Planks:** smooth matte dark wood with soft, simple grain.
- **Ropes:** thick, soft twisted tan rope.
- **Drums:** soft wicker in a chunky orange checker weave.
- **Panels:** smooth matte cream, tan and pink panels with mauve diamonds and yellow square plates.
- **Tunnels:** smooth matte olive-green walls.
- **Porthole:** glossy green glass ringed with rivet studs.
- **Glows** (STYLE.md, Glows): the cyan pad cores and their rising cyan light rings; the balloon's amber diamond panel with sunburst rays.
- The game's textures are painted and toon-like [Observed]. Keep them stylized, never photographic.

**Palette, in words:**
- **Sky, top to bottom:** dusty peach-pink, then a butter-yellow glow along the mountain line, then hazy taupe-grey to mauve mountains darkening at the base, then a plum haze band, then a near-black maroon void. Grey-mauve clouds over the pale yellow glow appear in the 2.8 promo art and one 2014 fan capture [Observed].
- **Metal:** warm honey-gold brass with olive-brass shade; acid yellow-gold gear plates; burnt-orange copper.
- **Airship:** deep aubergine-purple envelopes; a tan-brass band with a dark-purple stencil.
- **Glows:** a glowing amber diamond; bright cyan pads with black-and-yellow hazard rims.
- **Planes:** slate blue; a dark-red shark mouth with white teeth; a white painted eye; yellow-gold canopy frames.
- **Wood and set dressing:** dark chocolate-brown planks; rusty orange-brown drums; cream, tan and dusty-pink panels (loose plates have red edges).
- **Tunnels and glass:** sage-to-olive tunnels; spring-green porthole glass.
- **Shadows:** deep brown and plum.
- **Team colour:** no team-colour accents are documented in this map's scenery [Unverified] (dossier, Team bases and spawns: undocumented). If added, keep them to accents, never a repaint (overview §3; §5.6 item 18).

## 6. Prompt pack

**How these prompts are built:**
- **Closer.** STYLE.md has no fixed environment wording, so each image prompt ends with STYLE.md's framing line and the Look-and-Lighting closer the other map sheets use. The video line ends with the framing line and STYLE.md's video suffix.
- **Era.** All prompts use the classic lighting. The 2026 swaps are under the video line.
- **Names.** No map, weapon, pickup or character name appears in any prompt (STYLE.md, Character and map names).
- **No "steam".** The words "steam" and "steampunk" are kept out of the prompts so that no steam clouds or generic Victorian clutter appear. Each prompt writes "clear air" or "clear, still air" instead.
- **Frame.** Set 9:16 in the tool's aspect-ratio setting as well as in the framing line, and keep faces, sprites and the end card out of the bottom fifth and the right edge (STYLE.md, Frame).
