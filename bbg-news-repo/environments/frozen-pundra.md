# Frozen Pundra: house-style environment sheet

Built for BBG News shorts in the feature-film 3D house style (`characters/STYLE.md`). Facts come from the verified maps research in `research_notes/BBG maps (verified)/`, and each keeps its source label. Short names used below: **Dossier** = `frozen-pundra.md`; **Addendum** = `frozen-pundra.addendum.md`; **Overview** = `00_OVERVIEW.md`; **Conventions** = `environment-conventions.md`; **Conv. addendum** = `environment-conventions.addendum.md`; **Lineup** = `2026-lineup.md`; **Lineup addendum** = `2026-lineup.addendum.md`. **Era names used here.** This map was added in January 2014, so it is **not** in the Steam trailer or the five Steam screenshots (2013 footage [Inference, strong]) [Observed] (Dossier: Era and status). ## 1. What it is

- **Name and spelling:** "Frozen Pundra". This is the spelling in SkyVu's preview title, "BATTLE BEARS GOLD Update 2.7 "Frozen Pundra" Map Preview" [Canon], and in OST track 10, "Frozen Pundra", 0:54 [Canon]. The name probably puns on "tundra" [Inference] (Dossier: Official text; Setting).
- **Setting:** a fully enclosed ice cave, floored with snow, under an icicle-fringed ceiling, with no sky [Fan-wiki + Observed]. A raised snow ring "highway" on rock supports circles a snow-capped central rock dome [Fan-wiki + Observed]. No story location is documented [Unverified] (Dossier: Setting, Layout; Addendum: Setting).
- **Era and 2026 status:**
  - Added in BBG v2.7 [Canon: video title]. SkyVu posted the preview on Jan 22, 2014 [Canon metadata], and the wiki dates 2.7 to Jan 23, 2014 [Fan-wiki]. It was never a BBR map [Inference].
  - SkyVu's 2026 text never names it [Canon, checked]. It was played in every 2026 playtest sampled: TB on Jul 29, FFA on Aug 21 and PTB on Oct 15 [Observed] (Overview §2.3; Addendum: Era and status).
  - 2014 modes: fan frames show a team mode (red-vs-blue scores such as "5 : 2" and "YOU'RE ON TEAM RED") and an FFA-style HUD with no team score [Observed], so both ran on the map in 2014 [Inference] (Dossier: Era and status, 2014 modes). King of the Windmill is observed on the map in the 2014 fan video SrGGNd7V7Xs [Observed] (Conv. addendum, Corrections 4); the mode ran from 2.8 (Mar 2014) to 2.29 (Jan 2018) [Fan-wiki; Game text via wiki] (Conventions §14).
  - Its inclusion at launch is very likely [Inference, strong]. The launch modes are [Unverified].

## 2. Layout for staging

- **Plan:** a ring around a central dome. Pickups are mirrored on "opposite sides" [Fan-wiki], so the map is probably a two-sided, symmetric layout [Inference]. No overhead or top-down image has been found (Overview §7); the 2026 HUD has a radar [Observed] (Dossier: Era and status), but no map plan has been read from it. The ring's diameter and the number of bridges and ramps are [Unverified] (Addendum: Ring-track synthesis; Overview §7).
- **Three levels** [Fan-wiki + Observed] (Dossier: Layout, Verticality):
  1. **The cave floor:** smooth snow with round, bowl-shaped dips, pointed ice cones and tall snow mounds [Observed; mounds Fan-wiki].
  2. **The raised ring track:** a broad, flat-topped snow "highway" with rounded edges, crossing above head height when seen from the floor [Observed]. It rests on squat, snow-capped dark-rock supports with open gaps beneath [Observed]. Its width varies: it has wide, banked sweeps with a rounded raised snow lip (preview 0:04–0:06) and thin snow bridges between snow-capped rock tops (preview 0:11–0:13; HK 25:40 and 26:00 in 2026) [Observed]. 2026 frames show curving ice ramps and slides (HK 22:00, 24:40) [Observed] (Lineup addendum §4); that these are stretches of the ring track is [Inference].
  3. **Inside the dome, near its roof:** reached by a jump pad below, and home to the double-damage sandwich [Fan-wiki]. The interior has never been seen [Unverified].
- **Spawns:** undocumented [Unverified] (Addendum: Spawn sides).
  - Red, 2014: a "YOU'RE ON TEAM RED" frame is at floor level, with the raised track crossing at about head height and the dome beyond [Observed]. So the red spawn is probably on the floor outside the ring, facing inward [Inference].
  - Blue, 2026: the frames are at floor level among cobalt rock and cyan snow, with no landmark that fixes the position [Observed].
  - The spawns probably sit on opposite sides of the ring [Inference]. Which compass side is red is [Unverified].
  - In FFA, a player spawns at either team's spawn point [Fan-wiki], and every opponent shows as Blue [Fan-wiki; Observed 2026] (Conventions §4; Overview §3).
  - A respawning bear is circled by translucent pale-green shields with a plus emblem for 4.5 s [Fan-wiki; Observed] (Overview §3).
- **Routes:**
  - Along the ring top [Fan-wiki + Observed track]. Whether it is the main route is undocumented.
  - Under the ring, through floor-level snow passages between the supports, with jump pads on the floor (preview 0:17–0:18) [Observed]. ## 3. Look in the house style

**Shapes and materials (re-rendered per STYLE.md: soft rounded forms, bold readable silhouettes, no outlines, never "low-poly")** (Dossier: Look; Overview §5.3 items 10–12)
- **Overall mood:** a bright, cool, candy-blue ice cave. It is not dark or scary [Observed].
- **Rock:** the game's rock is faceted, with painted cracks [Observed]. Render it as chunky, rounded rock masses with broad, softly bevelled facet planes and soft, shallow crack grooves [BBG News invention, after STYLE.md's faceted-effects rule]. Horizontal banding is not a 2026 tell: SkyVu's 2014 preview already shows banded indigo-and-cobalt rock in a deep-blue stretch (0:08–0:10), and 2026 frames show banded rock walls too [Observed] (Addendum: Layout; Look). The era difference is the overall shift: 2014 rock reads slate-indigo and purple with lavender washes, 2026 rock reads cobalt, with no lavender in the Oct 15 frames [Observed].
- **Central dome:** a squat, blocky mass of that rock under a thick, rounded snow cap that overhangs its edges, like a frosted cake [Observed]. A dark archway runs through its base.
- **Snow:** smooth and rounded, with soft blue streaks [Observed]. Render it as soft satin-matte drifts with gentle rounded edges and a faint inner glow [BBG News invention]. It never looks like fur.
- **Ice:**
  - The game's ice is translucent with sharp facets [Observed]. Render it as glossy, clear candy-glass with crisp cut facets, smooth bevelled edges and a soft inner glow (STYLE.md: Faceted effects, by analogy).
  - Glassy ice masses and white crystal clusters stand at the edges [Observed].
- **Ceiling:** a rippled sheet of ice, periwinkle to cyan, hung with dense fringes of long, translucent blue icicles [Observed]. Keep the icicles tapered, the documented landmark silhouette (Overview §5.3 item 10), with crisp but gently bevelled, glossy tips [BBG News invention]. The teen rating allows cartoon violence; the take check in section 5 (no piercing, pinning or impaling) is the guard.
- **Big icicles:** a few larger near-white icicles. One shows a small blue starburst crack and another a wavy blue-and-white marbling (preview hq2, hq3, 0:13) [Observed].
- **Hourglass columns:** a hanging icicle tip that almost meets a dark post set in a ring-shaped snow mound (0:15–0:16), or a floor stalagmite (the wiki tile) [Observed].
- **Jump pads:** clean, painted dark-metal octagons with a yellow-and-black hazard rim and a glowing cyan star core, under faint stacked cyan rings [Observed] (Conventions §7). At a distance in 2026 they read as a gold ring with a teal centre [Observed] (Addendum: Ring-track synthesis).
- **Sparkle:** one white four-point twinkle glint on a background rock [Observed: preview hq2]. Using it as an occasional glint is [BBG News invention].

**Lighting**
- **What the game shows** in every era: flat, bright, even light with no visible source and no hard cast shadows [Observed]. There is no sky, no time of day, no falling snow and no weather particles [Observed; Unverified for undocumented areas] (Dossier: Look; Addendum: Look). STYLE.md lists Frozen Pundra as an interior with no sky.
- **No practical key light.** Unlike most maps, this one has no lamp, lava or neon large enough to key from (STYLE.md: Sets and light). The main emissive identity anchors are the jump pads' cyan glow and, in 2026 Plant the Bomb, the near-white light column. Small local glows: the Health Pack's orange-lit base and orange glow bar, a blue-lit base with a white post (preview 0:06), and the hatch's yellow-green lights [Observed] (Dossier: Look, Pickup props; Addendum: Layout; Look).
- **House rig, classic 2013 look (the default)** [BBG News invention, keeping the observed flat, sourceless light]:
  - **Override, needs Bryce's sign-off:** this rig departs from STYLE.md's warm key (The look; Sets and light) and Overview §5.3 item 10, because the map has no practical light and the observed light is flat and sourceless [Observed]. It treats the sourceless glow of the whole ice ceiling as the map's own light. Once signed off, record the exception in STYLE.md's Sets and light section; until then it is [BBG News invention].
  - Key: soft, bright, even, non-directional neutral-white light that seems to come from the whole ice ceiling, so it never reads as a light source. Neutral rather than cool keeps contrast between the cyan snow and the indigo rock.
  - Fill: cool cyan bounce from the snow.
  - Rim: gentle and pale.
  - Accents: pad cyan as local glow on the snow.
  - Shadows: soft contact shadows only.
  - No warm key. The map has no warm source large enough to key from (the Health Pack's orange glow is a small local one), and a warm key would turn the snow cream.
  - If a scene is staged in a lavender zone, keep that soft lavender-pink wash for every shot of the scene. Never switch zones mid-scene (STYLE.md habit 2).
- **Oct 2026 lighting pass** ("Lighting improvements" [Canon]; banner "LIGHTING, EFFECT & TEXTURE IMPROVEMENTS" [Observed]):
  - The Oct 15 frames keep the same cobalt-and-cyan scheme as the July and August builds. No change shows at storyboard resolution [Observed / Inference] (Addendum: Era and status; Conv. addendum §5). The overview measures "saturated cyan and cobalt, with purple under 3% of pixels" (Overview §3).
  - What the pass did to this map cannot be isolated [Inference].
  - BBG 3.0 (Nov 15, 2021) listed "New Map Lighting" game-wide [Game text via wiki] (Dossier: Era and status; Overview §0 item 5), so the map was probably relit at least once between the 2014 look and 2026 [Inference]. The indigo-to-cobalt shift cannot be credited to the Oct 2026 pass.
  - **House rig for the 2026 look:** the same sourceless neutral-white rig (and the same sign-off) over saturated cyan and cobalt, with no lavender wash. In PTB shots, the hatch's near-white light column is a local practical [Observed prop; its use as a light is BBG News invention].
- Weapon and effect flashes may tint characters within a shot (STYLE.md: Effect light).

## 5. Hazards, gags and easter eggs

**Verified**
- **Falling icicles** [Fan-wiki] (Dossier: Hazards; Addendum: icicle behaviour):
  - "some large lightly-coloured icicles can drop from the ceiling and deal damage to players below if they are dealt enough damage… they can take several seconds to drop, do not deal significant damage and each have a small area of effect".
  - "Firing at the icicles causes them to fall off and disappear on contact with the floor."
  - It is the game's third interactive map, after Haunted Castle and Shortest Parsec [Fan-wiki].
  - No frame shows an icicle falling [Observed absence; the sampling is coarse]. The count, the respawn and whether the mechanic exists in 2026 are [Unverified].
- **Jump pads:** one leads to the sandwich in the dome [Fan-wiki]. Pads also sit on the floor under the ring [Observed]. Players "can maneuver to a certain degree" in flight [Fan-wiki] (Conventions §7).
- **Pickups** [Fan-wiki] (Dossier: Layout, Pickups; Conventions §6):
  - The sandwich "doubles the damage of all the player's weapons for 20 seconds" and "respawns every 90 seconds". Another wiki page says "Triples" [Fan-wiki, conflicting].
  - The Imperial Basket Grande heals 50% and overheals to 200%. Its two locations conflict between wiki pages ("below the track" versus "top and bottom corners").
- **Enclosure:** "Turkish SW Boots users often finding themselves hitting obstacles" [Fan-wiki].
- **Plant the Bomb, 2026:** the feed reads "DEPOSIT ⚡ +225" [Observed], matching the wiki's 225-Joule deposit [Fan-wiki]. Staging rules [all Fan-wiki] (Conventions §8; Overview §3):
  - Each team carries its own team-colour bomb, which starts on a same-colour platform, to the enemy tube: "red players have to carry their red bomb to the blue team's tube and vice versa".
  - Lifting the bomb starts a 2-minute fuse; the bomb pulses with increasing intensity in the last 30 s.
  - The player is invincible while picking it up.
  - The carrier "cannot attack nor use special equipment" but can use "Jump Pads and power-ups".
  - In the observed Oct 15 match the recorder was on team blue ("YOU'RE ON TEAM BLUE"), a blue bomb was carried overhead, and "BLUE TEAM WINS" closed it [Observed] (Addendum: Official text; Look). That the blue team carried the blue bomb fits the wiki rule [Inference]. No red bomb has been seen on this map in 2026 [Unverified].
- **No other hazards.** No slippery ice, cold damage or suicide pit is documented or seen [Unverified / Observed absence] (Addendum: Hazards). Never invent one and present it as real (Overview §5.1 item 2).

**Gag ideas** (all [BBG News invention], built on the facts above; cartoon violence only)
1. **The slow icicle:** a bear shoots a big near-white icicle, waits, shrugs and walks off smug. Seconds later it drops on a passer-by for a small, comic bonk and vanishes on the floor in a soft snow puff. The delay, the low damage and the vanishing are [Fan-wiki]; the puff is invented.
2. **Dome launch:** a bear steps on the pad under the arch, rockets up into the dome and comes back glowing with the sandwich's magenta ring. The interior stays off-screen.
3. **Bridge standoff:** two bears meet in the middle of the thin snow bridge to the health perch, under the icicles.
4. **Over-jump bonk:** a bear launches too hard in the cramped cave and taps the ice ceiling, sending a fringe of icicles tinkling. This is built on the wiki's "hitting obstacles"; show it with a pad launch, not named gear.
5. **The snowman watches:** a cutaway to the little snowman outside the track reacting to the chaos. Its look is invented: a small rounded snowman with two ear-like bumps, after the grid-tile figure [Observed shape; identity Inference]. Never caption it as a fact.
