## 1. What it is

- **Abusement Park**: spelled this way in the 2026 soundtrack [Canon] and the 1.3.2 notes [Game text via wiki]. The name is a pun on "Amusement Park" [Fan-wiki]. It is a run-down carnival with a horror streak, at dusk turning to night in every capture, sealed inside cracked stone walls with nothing but void beyond them [Observed]; shorts use night (overview §5.1 item 4). The wiki calls it "a horror-themed carnival" [Fan-wiki] (dossier, Setting).
- **Era:** added in BBR 1.3.2 (Aug 24, 2012) [Game text via wiki]; skybox fixed in BBR 1.3.4 (Oct 20, 2012: "Fixed Abusement Park Skybox") [Game text via wiki]; in BBG from the 2013 relaunch [Fan-wiki]. BBG 3.0 (Nov 15, 2021) shipped game-wide "New Map Lighting" [Game text via wiki]; that it relit this map is [Inference]. **2026:** no SkyVu text names it [Canon]. It does appear in 2026 builds and in SkyVu's own Oct 15 stream [Observed] (2026-lineup.addendum.md, second part, §3), in the teaser's "SAME" panel [Observed; map ID Inference, strong], on the map tile in the thumbnail of SkyVu's Short "BBG Maps Ranked part 1" [Observed] (level-select art, not proof of a 2026 look; overview §0 item 3), and in fan mobile-playtest thumbnails of Sep 20 (5szTpCKfl-c: a carnival booth and a "[RED]"-style wall) [Observed; map Inference] (2026-lineup.md §2.2). **No Steam screenshot shows this map** [Observed]; its only Steam footage is the trailer's red base. The soundtrack opens with its theme [Canon]. Launch is very likely [Inference, strong] (overview §2.3).
- **Modes:** Team Battle and Plant the Bomb [Fan-wiki]; Free-For-All from BBG 2.5 (Nov 27, 2013) [Game text via wiki], on the same spawn points [Fan-wiki]. 2026 fan chapter lists name TDM and KC matches here (Aug 2026) [Unverified fan text]; Kill Confirmed is not among the Steam page's three modes [Canon].

## 2. Layout for staging

Orientation is arbitrary and positions are approximate (dossier, Layout, Schematic). The arena is **asymmetric** [Fan-wiki].

- **Shape:** a roughly square arena walled in tall stone [Observed]. A closed roller-coaster loop circles the edge at roughly roof height on striped pylons [Observed].
- **Red spawn (default home for the lead):** one corner. About 11 spawn positions sit on raised wooden plank decks and on asphalt next to them [Observed]. The deck has chunky yellow U-rails and backs onto the stencilled "[RED]" wall [Observed]. A ramp leads out of the red spawn, and a tunnel runs under the red base [Fan-wiki]. The base has a red pad with a blue nub [Observed], probably the red bomb tube [Inference].
- **Blue spawn:** the diagonally opposite corner. It is a paler, cool-lit plank deck beside wooden stairs, with blue graffiti on the walls [Observed]. Next to it are a blue-and-white striped structure and a wide grey walkway raised on square pillars [Observed], which is probably the wiki's "bridge" [Inference]. The base has a blue pad with a red nub [Observed], probably the blue bomb tube [Inference].
- **FFA** uses the same spawn points [Fan-wiki].
- **Plant the Bomb:** each team's bomb is a team-coloured round bomb on a same-colour stand, carried to the enemy tube [Fan-wiki; Observed 2012] (overview §3). Both bombs start in front of the Mirrored House (below); the probable tubes are the coloured pads in each base [Inference]. The tube model on this map is unknown [Unverified].
- **Lanes:**
  - a narrow corridor between the two spawns [Fan-wiki]. The overhead shows a dark corridor through the south-centre with a rainbow-striped stretch of floor [Observed];
  - a narrow alley next to the Bearscream "gazebo" [Fan-wiki], probably the octagonal striped tent [Inference];
  - the tunnel under the red base [Fan-wiki];
  - "the raised walkway opposite the Red team's spawn points" and "the hole at the corner of the arena" [Fan-wiki];
  - a "[NO ENTRY]" corridor with red curtains and a glowing jump pad [Observed];
  - Red can go round through the back of Blue's base or straight through the platform; Blue can go "through the jump pads and go straight forward" [Fan-wiki].
- **Landmarks for orientation:**
  - the big maroon-and-white square tent sits near the red side [Observed];
  - the octagonal gazebo sits near the blue deck [Observed];
  - the Plant the Bomb bombs stand "in front of the House of Mirrors" [Fan-wiki], on a central yellow booth [Observed; Inference];
  - the EXIT stairs are near the red base [Inference].
- **Schematic features** (dossier, Schematic: traced from the top-down image, orientation arbitrary, positions approximate [Observed]):
  - red side: a long wooden deck or walkway beside the three green peaked tents; a hazard-striped shutter and a ramp leading to a pink-lit room along the red-side edge;
  - the corner beyond the big tent: an orange pit/rubble patch;
  - near the octagonal "gazebo": crates;
  - beside the blue spawn deck: lane-marked concrete;
  - centre: the yellow booth with the blue and red bomb icons on its edge, a box, and the dark corridor with its rainbow strip.
- **Vertical spots:**
  - raised decks and walkways, the stairs, the tunnel and the jump pads [Fan-wiki; Observed];
  - the coaster track overhead is probably not normal play space [Inference]; players are seen on the track only in glitch content (standing on the track, beside a car) [Observed].
- **Sightlines:**
  - the yellow EXIT sign can be seen from the red deck [Observed];
  - the coaster rail crosses the sky over the red base [Observed: Steam trailer];
  - the walls close off every view, so any shot aimed outward ends on a wall top and the plum sky.
- **Scale:** the U-rails come to about a third of a bear's height, knee to thigh [Observed, approximate] (conventions §11). Doors are about 1.5–2× a bear's height [Inference] (STYLE.md, Sets and light).
- **Camera positions** [BBG News invention]:
  1. eye level on the red deck, looking along the U-rails at the striped tent wall with the coaster overhead. This is where the Steam trailer shows Wil and Tillman (2013 footage [Inference, strong]);
  2. low, at the foot of the EXIT stairs, looking up at the sign;
  3. high three-quarter view over the arena from above the coaster loop. The wiki's overview and top-down renders look down on the whole arena [Observed]; that they are SkyVu's is [Inference];
  4. locked-off medium in front of the Mirrored House doors;
  5. down the length of the dark corridor toward the far spawn;
  6. from the grey walkway, looking down onto the blue deck.

  Keep the camera inside the walls or above them, never outside, because outside there is only void.

## 3. Look in the house style

STYLE.md (The look; Sets and light) and overview §5.3: keep the layout, the landmark silhouettes, what each thing is made of, the palette family and the light sources. Re-render every surface in the feature-film finish. No ink outlines, and never the word "low-poly".

- **Shapes:** the game is built from chunky, hard-edged, low-poly geometry with hand-painted textures [Observed] (conventions §12). Re-render as soft, rounded, bevelled forms with bold silhouettes: plump tent peaks, rounded booth roofs, chunky rounded rails, soft-edged stone blocks.
- **Materials:**
  - **Walls:** cracked grey-mauve stone blocks [Observed]. Render them as softly bevelled blocks with broad, rounded cracks.
  - **Decks:** wooden planks with bolt holes and orange-red paint or blood splats [Observed]. Render rounded planks with visible bolt holes and soft orange *paint* splats only (teen rating; overview §5.5).
  - **Asphalt:** grimy grey [Observed]. Render it smooth, with soft wear.
  - **Tents:** striped canvas [Observed]. Render soft matte fabric with gentle sag and simple folds (STYLE.md, Cloth).
  - **Arch signs:** riveted metal [Observed]. Render clean painted metal with rounded rivets.
  - **Hazard surfaces:** yellow-and-black hazard-striped pillars, shutters, ramps and barriers [Observed]. Render glossy painted stripes.
  - **Glows:** marquee bulbs, the backlit lightning-bolt sign box, the glowing yellow doorway and the jump-pad cores [Observed]. Render clean soft emissive light with a gentle bloom (STYLE.md, Glows).
- **Palette, in words:**
  - **Sky:** deep plum night, a dark blue-violet cloud band and a dull maroon glow on the horizon.
  - **Base surfaces:** grey-mauve stone and grimy grey asphalt.
  - **Decks:** the same warm wooden planks on both sides; the difference is light, not material: salmon-orange-lit on the red side, cool blue-grey-lit (reading paler) on the blue side [Observed]. #ffa67f is a lit value only; don't also tint the planks salmon.
  - **Team marks:** vivid red stencils and stars; deep navy-blue graffiti.
  - **Tents:** maroon and off-white stripes; orange and golden-yellow stripes; muted sage-green peaks; yellow-green canvas inside the Candy Stash tent.
  - **Rails and bunting:** golden-yellow U-rails; red-and-teal pennant bunting.
  - **Coaster:** a dark oxblood rail with cream edges, lime-green cars and magenta-pink riders.
  - **Jump pads:** a cyan glow.
  - Team colour appears **only as accents** (overview §3).
