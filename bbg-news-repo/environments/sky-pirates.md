## 1. What it is

- **Name.** SkyVu uses both names: "New Map: Sky World" (Steam news, Aug 19, 2026), and "Sky Pirates Map Reveal" and "Sky Pirates in BBG!" ("Enjoy the highlights from our Sky World playtest!") [Canon]. That they are one map is [Inference, strong]. Write **"Sky Pirates (Sky World)"** on first mention (overview §5.4 item 14). It is not Sky View.
- **Setting.** A small floating autumn island in a pale sky, with a blue expanse below, read as sea [Observed, new-maps addendum; Unverified in lineup §8]. It has a half-timbered village with red roofs, a white clock tower on stone stilts, orange-crowned birches, a limestone mesa and pirate galleons [Observed] (lineup §8, Setting; new-maps addendum, Setting). Lineup §8 has the wooden sailing galleons "moored or hovering alongside" the island (Setting) and sitting "beside the island at deck height" (Layout) [Observed]. The wiki calls it "Island/Autumn themed" [Fan-wiki].
- **Era and 2026 status.** It began as a Battle Bears Ultimate launch map, carried into Overclock [Fan-wiki]. The wiki dates BBU's launch to Jun 25, 2014 [Fan-wiki]; SkyVu dates BBU 2015 and BBO 2016 [Canon] (overview, Eras table). Keep the year out of captions, or give both. It was never in BBG 2013–2021 [Fan-wiki, by absence]. It joined BBG at playtest 3 (Aug 21, 2026) [Canon] and is in the Oct 15 build 3.0.7 [Observed] (new-maps addendum, Era and status). SkyVu calls it "the new Sky Pirates map" [Canon]. It is in the game per overview §2.3 and lineup §8 [Canon]; Nov 4 launch inclusion is [Inference, strong] per the new-maps addendum. **It has no classic 2013 look.**
- **Modes seen here.** Team Battle [Observed: HKbwq7PolNc (HK) 48:00, 52:10] and Kill Confirmed [Observed: ZMhLVXWT6OY (ZM) 12:30; HK 56:20, 58:50], from the on-screen "YOU'RE ON TEAM RED" banners (new-maps addendum, Era and status). Free-for-All appears in fan chapters and titles only [Unverified]. Plant the Bomb was not seen [Unverified]. Whether Kill Confirmed launches is [Unverified] (Steam lists TDM, FFA and PTB [Canon]).

## 2. Layout for staging

(Lineup §8, Layout; new-maps addendum, Layout; conventions §4 and §11.) The full plan shape is not documented [Unverified], so keep staging simple (overview §4).

- **Overall.** One main island top, open and grassy and gently rolling. Pale flagstone slabs are scattered over olive grass [Observed]. A village and a paved square sit around the clock tower [Observed].
- **Centre: the clock-tower square.** The tower stands on four short, squat grey stone legs over a slab-paved plaza, and players run between the legs [Observed]. The Bearscream Sandwich floats at the tower's foot [Observed] (lineup §8, Layout).
- **Village ring.** Half-timbered cottages, orange-and-cream striped market stalls, a hand-cart, crates and barrels stand around the square [Observed]. Treat the cottages as solid scenery and cover; whether any can be entered is [Open].
- **Second tower.** A tall, narrow half-timbered watchtower with a red roof and an arched door [Observed: CwxpBZg6Fi4 oar2].
- **High ground: the limestone mesa.** A steep, pale limestone mesa with a grassy top and birches stands beside the village [Observed]. How players get up it is [Open].
- **Tunnel.** A dark rock tunnel interior shows in SkyVu's Oct 15 stream [Observed: ZMhLVXWT6OY 14:40]. That it runs through the mesa is [Inference].
- **Edges: docks and galleons.** Plank docks and fences line the edge [Observed]. The galleons sit "beside the island at deck height", "moored or hovering alongside" (lineup §8, Layout and Setting) [Observed], so they hang in open sky level with the island top, not on the sea below [Inference]. Plank docks and stair-ramps lead up to the galleons, whose decks are playable: fights happen at the yellow rails beside round portholes [Observed] (new-maps addendum, Layout). A player stands on a steep yellow-trimmed hull with rope rigging [Observed: CwxpBZg6Fi4 hq1]. That the ships add a raised level is [Inference].
- **Water channel.** A narrow blue water channel crosses the grass in places [Observed, low-res].
- **Underside.** The 2026 underside is not documented [Unverified]. In 2015 it was pale faceted rock hanging over turquoise water [Observed: LYITNmvoCZI 4:06–4:08] (new-maps addendum, Setting). Frame shots so the cliff faces read and the underside falls into haze or out of frame; any underside drawn is [BBG News invention].
- **Teal pads.** Small round teal pads [Observed: CwxpBZg6Fi4 oar2]; jump pad or pickup spawn [Unverified]. At Short resolution even Aztec's jump pads read as round teal discs (conventions §7), and the 2013 Sandwich stood on a teal disc (conventions §6). Never drawing the standard octagonal jump pad here is a [BBG News invention] caution, not a documented difference.
- **Spawns and team sides.** Not documented [Unverified]. In FFA, players spawn at random from either team's spawn points [Fan-wiki] (conventions §4), but FFA on this map is [Unverified] (see Modes seen here). The spawn shield is four translucent pale-green heater shields circling the bear [Fan-wiki: "four green shields"; Observed effect], one with a faint plus emblem (render it as an abstract mark), for about 4.5 s [Fan-wiki]. It is seen in 2013 and 2026 [Observed] (overview §3). Whether the 2013 wiki frame shows the respawn shield or the Shield pickup is [Unverified] (conventions §4). It can frame any respawn here. A red lead faces blue opponents (STYLE.md, Teams in frame).
- **Scale.** Doors about 1.5–2× a bear's height, rails at knee-to-waist height [Inference] (conventions §11). Apply the rule to the deck rails and cottage doors.
- **Sightlines.** Across the open lawn and the square in every direction, between the tower legs, from the deck rail back across the village, and from the village up to the mesa. A view from the mesa top down over the village holds only if the mesa top proves playable [Open]; prefer views of the mesa from the village. All of these are read from the observed layout and are [Inference].
- **Camera positions** [BBG News invention]:
  - at bear height on the square, framed by two tower legs;
  - low on the slabs just outside the tower's base, two squat legs framing the bottom of the shot, looking up the tower to the clock face;
  - on a galleon deck at the rail, looking back across the gangway to the village, or out over the drop;
  - at the dock end looking along the hull;
  - high three-quarter, from the air just above the island's edge, for the establishing shot only, framed so the cliff faces read and the underside falls into haze or out of frame.

## 3. Look in the house style

Follow STYLE.md (The look; Sets and light) and overview §5.3: keep the layout, landmark silhouettes, materials, palette family and light sources, and re-render everything in the feature-film finish. Add no ink outlines, never write "low-poly", and keep one landmark per shot (overview §5.3 items 10–13).

**Shapes** [BBG News invention, re-described from the Observed sets]:
- **Cottages.** Plump, softly bevelled cottages with gently sagging steep roofs.
- **Clock tower.** A slim, rounded-edged tower on squat, chunky legs.
- **Birch crowns.** Full, rounded crowns built from soft clustered leaf masses. These replace the game's faceted clumps.
- **Mesa and cliffs.** Smooth, rounded limestone with soft ledges.
- **Galleons.** Fat, curved hulls with chunky rounded rails.
- **Props.** Rounded crates, barrels and stepping-stones.

**Materials**, re-described [Observed] (lineup §8, Look; new-maps addendum, Look):
- **Cottages.** White plaster with dark-brown timber framing, steep red clay tile roofs, dormers and diamond-lattice windows. In the house finish: smooth matte plaster, soft rounded timbers and chunky glossy-matte tiles.
- **Clock tower.** White stone and plaster with a steep red roof, a red skirt roof below the clock and small red-roofed hoods. The clock face is round and grey-white, with 12 knob markers and black arrow-tipped hands, at about 8:10 in the reveal thumbnail [Observed] (new-maps addendum, Landmarks 1). Keep that hand position across the shots of a scene, and draw no numerals on the face. The legs are grey stone. In the house finish: clean, smooth stonework and a crisp, readable clock face.
- **Birches.** White trunks with dark bands. In the house finish: smooth bark with soft painted bands.
- **Ground.** Olive grass and pale paving and stepping-stone slabs. In the house finish: soft, short stylised grass and smooth, rounded slabs.
- **Cliffs and mesa.** Pale limestone, cream-white in the light and cool grey in shade. In the house finish: smooth stone with soft weathering.
- **Galleons.** Dark-brown plank hulls, gold-yellow rails and trim, round brass portholes, black cannon ports, cream sails, rope rigging and a crow's-nest mast. Lineup §8 reads the sails as furled; the new-maps addendum reads them as billowing [both Observed; conflict unresolved]. Neither names a frame for its reading, and both draw on r91WkR10W2A maxresdefault. The prompts below use furled sails ("furled cream sails"), which suit the moored read; billowing sails are the alternative state for a separate scene, never mixed within one [BBG News invention]. In the house finish: smooth painted wood with soft grain, soft metallic brass, soft matte canvas and cloth-like rope (STYLE.md, Cloth).
- **Market props.** Striped awnings, a hand-cart, crates and barrels. The crates' stamped marks stay blank (overview §5.2 item 5).
- **The flag.** A tattered black flag with a white bear head over crossed cutlasses [Observed]. Render it as a generic white bear skull over crossed cutlasses, not a copy of the BBG app-icon bear head (overview §5.2 item 7).
- **No weather or particles** are documented [Unverified], so add none.

**Palette, in words:**
- **Autumn crowns:** glowing tangerine to burnt orange in the sun, deep rust-brown in shade.
- **Roofs:** warm brick-red to cherry-red clay, wine-red in shadow.
- **Grass:** pale sunlit yellow-olive to soft khaki-olive in shade, low saturation.
- **Plaster and white stone:** warm ivory to bright off-white.
- **Shaded tower stone and legs:** cool slate-grey.
- **Limestone:** bright cream-white in the light, cool grey-olive in shade.
- **Birch bark:** off-white with soft grey-brown bands.
- **Paving:** pale sage-grey stone.
- **Sky:** pale powder blue overhead deepening to soft mid-blue, a warm cream haze at the horizon, and a blush-pink cloud bank far below.
- **Sea:** soft teal-blue near the island, deeper steel-blue further out.
- **Galleons:** dark chocolate-brown hulls; gold-yellow trim, sampled once as bright gold-yellow (lineup §8) and once as antique ochre-gold (new-maps addendum); reading the two as lit and shade is [Inference]; cream sails.
- **Wood props:** warm mid-brown planks and caramel-tan crates.
- **Flag:** black with white.
- **Team colour:** none is documented in the scenery. Keep any team colour to the characters (overview §3).

## 5. Hazards, gags and easter eggs

**Verified:**
- **Open island edges** with a long drop to the sea [Observed]. "SUICIDE" feed entries appear in official footage, but their cause is [Unverified] (new-maps addendum, Hazards). That falling off is a kill, as on Sky View and Shortest Parsec, is [Inference] (lineup §8, Hazards). Every fall ends in an intact cartoon pratfall or pop, and the "SUICIDE" wording stays off screen (overview §5.5 item 16).
- **No moving hazards or switches** are documented [Unverified].
- **Ships.** In BBO "Pirate ships fly around the map" [Fan-wiki], and in 2015 a ship hovers above the houses [Observed]. In 2026 the ships appear moored [Observed]; lineup §8 reads them as "moored or hovering alongside" [Observed]. Whether any fly free in BBG is [Unverified].

- **Pickups.**
  - The Bearscream Sandwich sits at the tower's foot [Observed]. The wiki gives 2× damage for 20 s on one page and 3× on another [Fan-wiki, conflicting].
