## 1. What it is

- **Haunted Castle**, spelled that way in SkyVu's 2.4 notes ("Haunted Castle Returns") and as soundtrack track 11, "Haunted Castle", 1:01 [Canon]. Fan titles that spell it "Hunted Castle" are typos [Unverified]. It is not the 2018 daytime Battle Royale castle, a different map [Observed]; that map's music is probably the "Castle Royale" track [Inference] (dossier, Setting; overview §6 item 18). Its own name is [Unverified] (conventions addendum §4).
- **Setting.** A Halloween castle, always at night, on a figure-8 floating island that ends in black void [Observed]. Inside a blocky slate-blue curtain wall is a terracotta courtyard with a tall tower on legs. Outside the wall, graveyard lawns hold each team's spawn [Observed; spawns Inference].
- **Era.** It debuted in BBR 1.3.3, dated Oct 10, 2012 [Game text via wiki; date Fan-wiki]. It was absent when BBG relaunched on May 24, 2013 [Inference]. It came back in BBG 2.4 on Oct 24, 2013, "To celebrate the holiday" [Canon].
- **2026.** SkyVu's store text does not name it [Canon, by absence].
  - Seen in the Oct 15 final playtest [Observed: a night castle with red banners and a moon; identified by a fan chapter label and by matching SkyVu's PICK LEVEL tile, Inference]. The frames come from InstableGR's PTB match (HK 69:50–74:40; fan chapter label plus team HUD) and SkyVu's own stream (ZMhLVXWT6OY 46:00–50:10; map identity Inference) (lineup addendum part 1 §3 and §7; part 2 §2).
  - SkyVu's "Official Playtest Highlights" (C7uFxvSZ10c, 1:44–1:46, July builds) shows a night castle wall, jack-o'-lanterns and a blue banner [Observed] (overview §2.3; conventions addendum, Corrections item 6). It predates the Oct 15 pass, so it does not count for the 2026 look (see §7).
  - 2026 modes: PTB Oct 15 [Observed]; KC Jul 29, FFA (Sep 25) and TDM (Oct 9) [fan text, Unverified] (overview §2.3; lineup addendum part 1 §3).
  - Launch verdict: "Very likely; whether it is seasonal is unknown" [Inference] (overview §2.3).
  - It is not in the Steam trailer or the Steam screenshots [Observed]. Those are 2013 footage [Inference, strong]: caption them "classic 2013 look", and never present one as this map.

## 2. Layout for staging

**Plan** (dossier, Layout; main source the 2012 overhead [Observed]):
- A square walled compound sits in the middle of the figure-8 island. Two big graveyard lobes lie at diagonally opposite corners, with smaller lawns at the other two [Observed].
- Edge: a pinkish dirt rim, then black void [Observed].
- 180° rotational symmetry [Observed]; "It is a symmetrical map" [Fan-wiki].

**Spawns:**
- Red markers sit in the top-right lobe and blue in the bottom-left, in clusters of four [Observed]. That they are spawns is [Inference]. The in-game compass is [Unverified].
- Each lobe opens onto a square gatehouse midway along the wall [Observed]. In a 2012 capture, a red-team player stands on the lawn outside a red-banner gate [Observed]. Opposite gatehouses fly different colours: blue banners on one wall and red on the opposite gatehouse in the same view [Observed: m6YizQMJ3UA hq1]. A blue-team player also sees red pennants [Observed: Plant_The_Bomb_Mode_Bomb_Explosion.jpeg] (conventions §3). Which team owns which gate is [Inference] (dossier); the conventions dossier calls the banners' team meaning [Unverified].
- Spawn shield: translucent pale-green shields with a plus emblem, for 4.5 s [Fan-wiki; Observed] (overview §3).

**Lanes** [BBG News invention, on the documented plan]:
- **The open courtyard.** The two Bearscream Sandwiches sit "at opposite ends of the map with a huge open space in between" [Fan-wiki]. The wiki's map page also puts them "On top of the Castle walls" [Fan-wiki], so their position conflicts; putting them on the courtyard floor is a staging choice [BBG News invention].
- **The wall-walk ring.** "on top of the Castle walls" [Fan-wiki], reached by long low ramps against two inside corners [Observed; "walkway up" Inference].
- **The underground.** "Underground tunnels leading to both teams' bases" [Fan-wiki]. They are entered by the dark octagonal pits in two opposite inside corners [Observed; Inference] and by a cave-like opening in each lobe [Observed; Fan-wiki].
- **The outer ledge.** A "very narrow ledge" round the outside of the castle leads to "a jagged hole leading into the main courtyard" [Fan-wiki].

**Vertical spots.** "4 levels": underground, ground, wall tops and "the Castle's planks in the middle" [Fan-wiki].
- **The tower planks** are roughly 6–8 player heights above the courtyard [Observed, rough estimate].
- **Jump pads.** "2 jump pads" in the centre of the ground level lead up to the planks, and one pad in the middle of the underground leads to the main level [Fan-wiki]. A pad is seen on a warm-lit floor beside a translucent blue octagonal panel [Observed, 2013 fan thumbnail].
- **The gate switches.** The wiki gives two locations that conflict [Fan-wiki]: "There is one switch above the gates of each team's base, which can be accessed by climbing up the Castle walls" and "There are two levers on the map, each located inside a tower near each team's spawn point" (dossier, Gates and switches; conventions §4). Any shot that places the lever follows one of them as a fan take.

**Sightlines:**
- "sniping spots on the high castle walls which surround a large open area" [Fan-wiki].
- Across the courtyard between the two Sandwiches [Fan-wiki].
- From the lawn up to the gatehouse and the moon [Observed].

**Scale.** Wall height against a bear is not measurable [Unverified]. Use the house rule: "doors about 1.5–2× a bear's height; rails and sandbags at knee to waist height; tarps and crate stacks give full cover" [Inference] (overview §3). So a crate-and-pumpkin stack hides a whole bear. The wiki blames "the largeness of the map" for slow classes' troubles [Fan-wiki].

**Camera positions** [BBG News invention]:
1. **Corner-tower wall-walk.** High three-quarter, looking diagonally down into the courtyard. This is the dossier's establishing view and is for openers only.
2. **Courtyard floor.** Low and wide across the open earth, with the stilted tower centre frame. Use it for the Sandwich dash.
3. **Under the tower.** Low angle up the legs to the planks and the green window.
4. **Gate from the lawn.** Eye-level medium on a portcullis between two banners. Use it for spawn exits and the "red team" beat.
5. **Graveyard.** Medium at the memorial slab, with its face turned away or kept blank (see 5 and 7).
6. **Outer ledge.** Side-on, bear height, with the wall on one side and black void on the other.
7. **Lever room or tunnel.** Bear height only, because these are roofed interiors. Base them on the observed blue stone room with a cobweb or the teal flagstone room [Observed]; the lever and room layout are [BBG News invention].

## 3. Look in the house style

Follow STYLE.md (The look; Sets and light) and overview §5.3:
- Keep the layout, the landmark silhouettes, what each thing is made of, the palette family and the light sources.
- Re-render every surface in the feature-film finish.
- No ink outlines, and never write "low-poly".
- The game's textures paint dark outlines on the stone blocks [Observed]. Drop them too.

**Shapes.** The game uses blocky slate stone with square crenellations and rounded corner towers [Observed]. Re-render them as:
- plump, softly bevelled stone blocks;
- chunky rounded merlons;
- squat rounded corner towers;
- a tall, narrow square tower on four thick legs;
- rounded plank walkways on angled brackets;
- round, soft pumpkins;
- soft-edged boxy hedges;
- rounded headstones.

**Materials** [Observed] (dossier, Look, Materials and Signature props), re-described:
- **Stone.** Chunky rectangular blocks with painted cracks become soft matte stone with gently rounded edges, soft seams and a few shallow rounded cracks.
- **Wood** (walkways, brackets, crates). Weathered planks become rounded planks with soft grain. The crates keep their diagonal braces.
- **Iron** (portcullis bars, banner rods, graveyard railing). Black iron becomes clean painted dark metal with rounded spike tips (STYLE.md, Robot parts, extended to set metal).
- **Banners.** Long swallowtail banners become soft matte fabric with simple folds (STYLE.md, Cloth). The bear-head emblem becomes a small abstract dark mark (overview §5.2 item 7); prompts name it as "a small plain dark diamond", so a tool does not fill it with a crest or letter.
- **Jack-o'-lanterns, windows and pad cores.** These become clean soft emissive glow with a gentle bloom on nearby stone and earth (STYLE.md, Glows). Pumpkins are smooth and rounded, with carved faces.
- **Ground.** Lawns are soft dark grass with rust-orange leaf litter. The courtyard is smooth packed earth, and the grave plots are soft dark earth.
- **Moon.** A smooth pale disc whose darker markings form the carved face.

**Palette, in words:**
- **Walls:** cool slate blue-teal, into blue-violet.
- **Central tower:** near-black slate.
- **Courtyard:** warm terracotta earth with deep plum-brown shadows, and a sand-gold pool of light under the tower.
- **Lawns:** dark moss green to near-black green, with olive-black hedges.
- **Graveyard:** mauve-rose headstones over near-black earth plots. The slab and stone are violet on the blue side and warm beige on the red [Observed; per-team tint Inference].
- **Banners:** deep crimson, or a very saturated royal blue.
- **Iron:** near-black.
- **Pumpkins and glow:** pumpkin orange, with lantern glow from deep orange to bright orange.
- **Crates:** warm brown.
- **Accents:** soft green windows (some teal or blue-violet) and icy cyan pad cores.
- **Sky:** black to near-black violet, tiny stars, and a faint purple-magenta glow over low dark hills.
- **Moon:** pale grey with a soft white halo.
- **Team colour** appears only as accents, here the banners (overview §3).

**Lighting, classic 2013 look (the default)** [Observed] (dossier, Look, Lighting):
- **Light.** It is night. Jack-o'-lanterns throw warm orange pools on the terracotta courtyard and the dirt paths. A sand-gold pool sits under the tower, and small windows glow green, teal or blue-violet.
- **Walls.** They stay cool slate blue-teal. In the overhead, long hard shadows fall one way, from a single moonlight key [Inference].
- **No weather or particles** are seen, and no flame torches appear in any image [Observed, by absence].
- **House rig** [BBG News invention]. Overview §5.3 item 11 sets only the general rule (the map's own practical light is the key) and gives no rig for this map; STYLE.md lists both jack-o'-lanterns' kind of practical light and moonlight as allowed keys. The dossier reads the overhead's one-way shadows as a single moonlight key [Inference]. This sheet picks:
  - warm jack-o'-lantern glow as the key, in warm local pools;
  - a soft cool moonlit fill that keeps the walls slate blue-teal and lays soft moonlit shadows across the courtyard;
  - a gentle rim on characters;
  - soft global illumination and cinematic depth of field.

  A cool moonlight key with the jack-o'-lanterns as warm local pools is the alternative that follows the dossier's reading; pick one per short and keep it.

  Keep one setup per scene (STYLE.md habit 2). The magenta wash in the 2013 Halloween promo and the 2.4 splash is promo grading [Inference]; never use it.

**Lighting, Oct 2026 lighting pass (Oct 15 build)** [Observed, low-res] (lineup addendum part 1 §3; overview §3):
- **What changed.** The map reads much darker and browner. 57–81% of each sampled frame is in the dark class. The stone is charcoal, the courtyard deep umber, and the jack-o'-lantern glow orange with a yellow core.
- **What stayed.** The black sky, the dark purple horizon band, and a pale grey full moon high in the sky (seen in two frames).
- **Why.** The change fits "Lighting improvements" [Canon], but the direction of the change is a reading [Inference]. BBG 3.0 (Nov 2021) already listed "New Map Lighting" [Game text via wiki] (overview §0 item 5), and the Sep 25, 2026 fan sample is already dark (terracotta #43241D; 2026-lineup §9), so the darkening may predate the Oct 15 pass [Inference]. The measured comparison is 2013 against Oct 15 only, not before and after the pass.
- **House rig.** The same rig, with neutral charcoal stone, umber earth and a lower, cooler fill. Raise exposure for readability but keep the hue relationships [BBG News invention]. Confirm against full-resolution demo captures before calling a frame the "2026 look".

## 5. Hazards, gags and easter eggs

**Verified** (dossier, Hazards and interactive elements):
- **Lever gates.** "When hit with a melee attack, there are gates in a passageway underneath that will open or close" [Fan-wiki]. The wiki calls it "the first map in the game that features switches you can interact with" [Fan-wiki], and the 1.3.3 notes promise "interactive features" [Game text via wiki].
  - Tactics: "limit the paths they can take out of their spawn", and in PTB make the bomb carrier "take a longer way" [Fan-wiki].
  - What the lever looks like is [Unverified]. No lever shows in any 2026 frame [Unverified for 2026].
- **The void.** Past the ledge there is "only black abyss leading to death" [Fan-wiki]. Shielded players "can still die by falling off a stage" [Fan-wiki]. Black void surrounds the island [Observed]. In Plant the Bomb, a carrier who falls off the map detonates the bomb [Fan-wiki] (conventions §8; overview §3); render any blast as a cartoon effect.
- **Jump pads** launch players "either vertically or at an angle" [Fan-wiki].
- **Side routes.**
  - The outer ledge needs the Turkish SW Boots or "at the very least, Jump" [Fan-wiki].
  - "In the blue team's base, past the big break in the wall, behind the fence, there's a big hole in the wall." [Fan-wiki]
- **Nothing else.** No other environmental damage is documented [Unverified]. No lava, water, fire, fog or weather is seen [Observed].

**Easter eggs and trivia** (dossier, Easter eggs and references; Setting):
- **The memorial slab.** The wiki says it carries the names of five SkyVu members, two "deceased Battle Bears characters" (Jackson and Grimes) and two forum beta testers [Fan-wiki]. They are engraved in three columns [Observed].
  - The slab is "a credits-style easter egg, not evidence that Grimes died"; the wiki's Grimes page says "It is unknown whether he died" [Fan-wiki] (overview §6 item 24).
  - Never render any name (overview §5.2 item 6).
  - A caption may say, as wiki trivia, that the slab carries Jackson's and Grimes's names [Fan-wiki]. Never call it a memorial to two dead characters, and never caption the staff or forum names [BBG News invention, editorial].
- **The moon's face** matches the 2012–13 Halloween app icons [Observed icons; link Inference]. The banner emblem resembles the logo bear [Inference]; that is why it stays abstract.
- **The 2014 blurb.** "The Battle Bears Family. Their house is a haunted castle." [Unverified quote]. The Addams Family reading is [Inference]. Never caption either as official.
- **Halloween.** The return was "To celebrate the holiday" [Canon], and fans date play to Halloween 2015 and Oct–Nov 2021 [Unverified].
- **Music.** Track 11 [Canon]. Nobody has heard it [Observed absence], and shorts use original music only (STYLE.md, Sound).

**Gag ideas [BBG News invention]** (none may appear in a "what's in BBG 2026" post):
- **Gate slam.** A bear whacks the switch above its gate, and the gate in the tunnel below drops in front of a pursuer, who skids nose-first into the bars (overview §4 pitch; dossier, staging spot 1). This placement follows the wiki's "switch above the gates" line; the other wiki line puts the lever in a tower near each spawn [Fan-wiki], so the placement, the lever's look and the gate's speed are fan takes.
- **The long way round.** A slow, heavy bear puffs across the huge courtyard toward the far Sandwich just as a quick one pops off a pad onto the planks above. This plays the wiki's "largeness of the map" line [Fan-wiki]; SkyVu's stated reason is [Unverified].
- **Ledge tiptoe.** A bear shuffles along the outer ledge, glances down at the void, gulps and pops through the jagged hole into the courtyard (dossier, staging spot 4). The hole's shape is a fan take.
- **Moon double take.** A bear notices the carved face on the moon and does a double take. The moon never moves or emotes. Use this gag for the classic look only.
- **Pumpkin peek.** A bear hides behind the crate-and-pumpkin stack, and the jack-o'-lantern lights its face from below as it peeks out. Keep the scene's base lighting fixed.
- **A moment of respect.** A bear pauses at the slab, whose face stays turned away or blank (dossier, staging spot 2, with the names removed). It is a nod to a credits-style easter egg, not a funeral: never imply that Grimes died (overview §6 item 24).

**Teen rating.** Cartoon violence only.
- Spooky-cute, never horror: no skeletons, corpses, open graves or gore.
- A void fall is a cartoon whistle-drop and a cut away, or an intact pop at spawn. Never show an impact.
- A slammed gate is a bonk and a held squash pose.
- The game's "SUICIDE" wording stays off screen (overview §5.5 item 16).
