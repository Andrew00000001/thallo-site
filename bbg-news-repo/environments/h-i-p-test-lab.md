# H.I.P. Test Lab: house-style environment sheet

This sheet is for BBG News shorts in the feature-film 3D house style (`characters/STYLE.md`). Facts come from the verified maps research in `research_notes/BBG maps (verified)/`, and each keeps its source label. Short names used below: **Dossier** = `h-i-p-test-lab.md` (this map has no addendum); **Overview** = `00_OVERVIEW.md`; **Conventions** = `environment-conventions.md`; **Conv. addendum** = `environment-conventions.addendum.md`; **Lineup** = `2026-lineup.md` and its addendum. Hex values are approximate (about ±15%), sampled from compressed frames [Inference] (Overview §0 item 4).

---

## 1. What it is

- **Name and spelling:** "H.I.P. Test Lab", with full stops and capital T and L. The wiki's map-select tile reads "H.I.P. TEST LAB" [Game text via wiki], and the Overview gives it as the map's BBG name [Fan-wiki]. It launched in BBR 1.0 (2011) as "Huggable Factory" [Fan-wiki] and was renamed for the 2013 BBG relaunch [Inference]. The 2026 soundtrack keeps "Huggable Factory" as the music title (track 5, 0:54; track 21, Full Version, 2:52) [Canon]. The 2026 in-game display name is [Unverified] (Dossier: Official text, Era and status; Overview §5.4 item 14). The 2026 jukebox reads "NOW PLAYING: Huggable Factor…" (the music name), and the 2026 level tile shows no name [Observed] (Overview §5.4 item 14; Lineup addendum §7), so neither settles the map's display name, and no image ever carries the name. "H.I.P." stands for "Huggable Instrumentality Project" [Canon per our notes; not re-fetched].
- **Setting:** a bright white Huggable lab and factory [Observed], "generally created with two levels" [Fan-wiki]. The two tube rooms sit at opposite corners of the top-down [Observed]; which tube room is which team's base is [Inference], and the bases' second sections (the end rooms) sit at the far left and right ends [Observed]. The symmetry type is [Inference] (see section 7). It is indoors throughout, with no windows or sky [Observed]; the wiki's infobox says "Type: Indoor" [Fan-wiki]. Time of day: none, because it is an interior (STYLE.md: Sets and light; Overview §5.1 item 4).
- **2026 status:** SkyVu's 2026 text does not name the map [Canon by absence]. The tube room appears in build 3.0.7 under "GET KILLS TO SCORE POINTS" [Observed], and the map is very likely in the launch lineup [Inference, strong] (Overview §2.3). The Steam trailer clip (about 34.3–36.8 s) and Steam screenshot screen_00 are 2013 footage [Inference, strong], so caption them "classic 2013 look".

## 2. Layout for staging

- **Overall:** "a symmetrical factory-like stage, generally created with two levels" [Fan-wiki]. In the 2012 top-down render, the footprint is a stepped rectangle with an S/Z outline. The two tube rooms stick out at opposite corners (top-left and bottom-right), two octagonal towers are offset diagonally, and large pink-violet rooms sit at the far left and far right ends [Observed] (Dossier: Layout).
- **Spawns and bases:**
  - Each base is "split into two connected sections, in which players can spawn virtually anywhere" [Fan-wiki]:
    - the Huggable tube room;
    - "a big, empty room with a big downhill entrace" (sic) [Fan-wiki], probably the pink-violet end rooms [Inference].
  - Each base has "four entrances/exits": two in the tube section and two near the big spawn room [Fan-wiki].
  - A respawning bear is circled by translucent pale-green shields for 4.5 s [Fan-wiki; effect Observed] (Conventions §4).
  - In FFA a player spawns at either team's spawn point [Fan-wiki] (Conventions §4).
- **Teams:**
  - The top-left tube room is probably red's and the bottom-right blue's [Inference]. Compass sides are [Unverified].
  - Door frames glow in team colour: blue in the Steam trailer, red in BBG fan footage [Observed]. A BBG flythrough shows the red-side twin of the trailer walkway, a raised walkway to a red-framed door with red neon wall slots [Observed].
  - A red lead's home is the red-framed side. The Steam walkway view is the blue side. Team colour is an accent only (Overview §3).
- **Lanes:**
  1. **Upper level:** "a series of bridges connecting the bases to two big towers, furthermore connected by a middle bridge. This room is the biggest in the map" [Fan-wiki].
  2. **Lower level:** "a giant floor fitted with a total of 35 crates" that "stretches across most of the main room, allowing players to move underneath the bridges" [Fan-wiki].
  3. **Base ramps:** "a relatively steep incline" at each end runs from the lower floor up to the team base, with "a small two-stage hill" near each [Fan-wiki]. The trailer's raised walkway may be one of these inclines [Inference].
  4. **Jump pad:** one pad beside the blue arrow pillar [Observed: screen_00]. Its up-arrows suggest it launches to the upper level [Inference]; where it lands is [Unverified] (Dossier: Hazards).
- **Vertical spots:**
  - The **centre bridge** between the towers, "in the dead center of the stage", where the sandwich pickup sits [Fan-wiki].
  - The **tower tops**, whose floors carry radial medallions [Observed: top-down; BBG footage].
  - **Crate tops** at the centre, where the two basket pickups sit [Fan-wiki].
  - The **magenta lofts** above the walkway [Observed]. Whether players can reach them is [Unverified]; treat them as set dressing.
- **Sightlines:**
  - Down the long raised walkway to the team doorway [Observed: screen_00].
  - From the centre bridge out over the crate floor to both towers [Fan-wiki layout; the view is Inference].
  - Across a tube room to the translucent pink wall [Observed: tile].
- **Ambush spots:** the wiki's May 2013 mine spots are "Between entrance/exits", "Under bridges" and "Above jump pads" [Fan-wiki; images Observed].
- **Where the camera can sit:**
  - **Locked-off on the walkway,** looking toward the team door, as in the trailer at 34.3–36.5 s [Observed; 2013 footage, Inference, strong]. This is the best-documented view.
  - **The reverse angle,** toward the overhead claw, the struts and a display capsule (about 36.5–36.8 s) [Observed; 2013 footage, Inference, strong].
  - **Tube-room medium,** the tile's composition [Observed: tile; 2026 frame].
  - **Ground level in the crate hall,** under the bridges and hanging dolls [Observed: May 2013; 2026 frames]. The exact composition is [BBG News invention].
  - **On the centre bridge** for a standoff, or **low on the crate floor** looking up at the bridges [BBG News invention, on the Fan-wiki layout].
  - **Floor level by the jump pad,** looking up the arrow pillar [BBG News invention, on screen_00's elements].
- **Framing:** in 9:16, keep the key landmark and the staging ground out of the bottom fifth and off the right edge (the Reels interface). Use one landmark per shot (STYLE.md: Frame; Overview §5.3 item 13).
- **Scale:** doors are about 1.5–2× a bear's height, and crate stacks give full cover [Inference] (Overview §3; Conventions §11). The trailer doorway measured at least 1.4–1.5× the height of a Huggable standing in it, and about two bears wide [Observed, approximate]. That measurement is against a Huggable, which the README scale chart puts at about 0.65× a bear [BBG News invention], so this doorway is at least about one bear tall, short of the house rule. The gap is [Open]; for staging, use the house rule of 1.5–2× [Inference].

## 3. Look in the house style

**Shapes and materials.** Everything is re-rendered per STYLE.md: soft rounded forms, bold readable silhouettes, no ink outlines, and never "low-poly" (Dossier: Look, Materials; Overview §5.3 items 10–12).
- **Lab walls:** smooth white painted panels with generously rounded edges and soft panel seams, a satin finish and warm-grey shading. The wiki calls the stage "metallic" [Fan-wiki], so the panels can read as painted metal [Observed: rounded white panels].
- **Floors:** pale floors with softly grooved conduit lines [Observed: top-down]. The raised walkway shows faint lengthwise streaks, and it is static, not a conveyor [Observed: frame differencing; static is Inference] (Dossier: Conveyor floor?).
- **Crates:** chunky matte charcoal boxes with softly bevelled edges, raised X-bracing and tiny glowing red corner lights [Observed]. Some carry a round pink grinning Huggable-face emblem (white teeth, black eyes) [Observed]; render it as a clean decal.
- **Machinery:** dark gunmetal, rendered as clean painted metal (STYLE.md: robot parts) [Observed].
- **Huggable tubes:**
  - Clean teal-tinted glass with soft refraction and rising bubbles.
  - Dark octagonal pedestals with angular feet.
  - Cyan glow rings at the top and base, as soft emissive light with gentle bloom (STYLE.md: Glows).
  - Dark graphite ceiling caps with glowing teal panels, a pale ribbed accordion connector, and dark segmented hoses arcing to ceiling sockets.
  - All [Observed].
- **Pink wall:** a translucent frosted pink panel strung with vertical chains of small white diamond lights, which glow softly with bloom [Observed].
- **Dolls and floating specimens:** pink Huggable figures [Observed], given the Huggable's stitched-plush finish (STYLE.md: Surfaces).
- **Rainbow column:** glossy painted diagonal stripes [Observed].
- **Team door frames:** soft emissive strips in team colour [Observed]. The cyan-white core is seen on the blue door [Observed: Steam walkway shot]; on the red side only a red-framed door and red neon wall slots are recorded, so a red door's core is [Unverified].
- **Jump pad:** clean dark-metal octagon with a yellow-and-black hazard rim, a glowing cyan star core and faint stacked cyan octagon rings [Observed] (Conventions §7).

The pale ice-blue floor hex was sampled here, but the cyan-lit tube-room floor itself is classic too [Observed: Dossier Lighting; the 2012 top-down shows the tube rooms in a pale blue glow].

- **2012 top-down:** pale grey floor, lilac and pink-violet end rooms, periwinkle-blue trim [Observed].

**Lighting**
- **Classic 2013 look (the default):**
  - High-key, almost shadowless white interior light, with coloured accent sources [Observed]: magenta-lit lofts, team-coloured door frames and trims, and cyan tube rings that light the tube-room floor.
  - House rig [BBG News invention]: the bright, even white lab light is the key, keeping the map's high-key look. The observed practical and emissive sources are the accents: the magenta loft glow, the team door strips, the cyan tube rings and the jump-pad cyan. Keep them as the identity-anchor emissives (Overview §5.3 item 11). Keep a cool fill (the cyan and blue accents justify it) and a gentle rim. In the house finish, add soft global illumination and gentle soft shadows; never the flat, shadowless look of the game render.
  - No light fixtures are described in the research. Adding visible ceiling fixtures would be [BBG News invention], so leave them out.
  - Keep one rig per scene. Weapon flashes may tint characters within a shot (STYLE.md habit 2).
- **Secret screening room:** warmer. The amber floor pool is the key, with red light from the monitor and taupe walls [Observed: RhoBBG.jpg]. Treat it as its own scene.
- **Oct 2026 lighting pass** ("Lighting improvements" [Canon]):
  - The 3.0.7 final-playtest frames of this map still look high-key and white [Observed]. They show a hotter magenta-pink wall and a cyan-lit floor in the tube room [Observed, low-res]; the cyan floor is also in the classic look (see Palette).
  - A fan says the pass overhauled every map [Unverified fan report].
  - No before-and-after was measured for this map; Conv. addendum §5 covers No Bear's Land, Desert Airmine, Space Oddity and Frozen Pundra only.
  - 2026 rig: the classic rig with the 2026 pink. Anything beyond that is [Open].

## 5. Hazards, gags and easter eggs

**Verified**
- **No environmental hazards in normal play are documented** [Fan-wiki: none listed]. No lava, ooze, lasers or fall-outs are documented here; never invent one and present it as real (Overview §5.1 item 2).
- **Loading-bay kill floor:** the bay "will cause the player to suicide if they attempt to stand on its floor" [Fan-wiki]. The infobox's "Suicide" row read "Yes (huggables)" from Jan 2013 [Fan-wiki], so only Huggable glitchers reach it [Inference]. Keep it off screen. The game's "SUICIDE" wording never appears (Overview §5.5 item 16).
- **Jump pads:** "The Huggable Factory has the least number of Jump Pads of any map, with only two" (a BBR-era trivia line, removed Jun 2013) [Fan-wiki]. The BBG and 2026 counts are [Open].
- **Moving parts:** dolls and crates move on overhead rails [Observed, Dossier: Setting]; a hanging-crate conveyor runs over a yellow-walled bay in Sep 2012 footage [Observed]. In the trailer a large doll crosses the foreground while a distant one stays put [Observed]. The wiki's "conveyor belts abundant throughout the factory" is [Fan-wiki], but the walkway floor does not scroll [Observed]; never show a scrolling floor.
- **Pickup timing:** the Sandwich page says the sandwich "doubles the damage" for 20 s and "respawns every 90 seconds"; the Pick-Ups page says "Triples" [Fan-wiki, conflicting]. Keep the multiplier out of any fact caption, and don't build a plot on it (Overview §5.1 item 1). Shields give 10 s of immunity [Fan-wiki].
- **Joule drops:** three floating blue lightning-bolt tokens on dark hexagon-like badges where a bear falls, since BBG 2.1 (Jul 15, 2013) [Game text via wiki; Observed 2026] (Conventions §6, §16 module F). Show them in a late-2013 or 2026 KO beat, never in a pre-July 2013 classic short.
- **Bonus joules (BBR era):** "Hidden under the bridges outside the far left side of both bases" [Fan-wiki]; on the page from Nov 2012, removed Aug 27, 2013.

**Easter eggs**
- **The Huggable "training" rooms:** "around twenty Huggables are standing in rows, staring at a screen with repeating images of Rho" [Fan-wiki]. They are seen in BBR 2012 footage and in a Jul 2013 BBG screenshot [Observed].
  - The wiki names the screen figure Rho [Fan-wiki]. Whether it matches the dark, single-red-eye Rho art is [Unverified].
  - The rooms were reached by a Huggable using So Fly; the fix is listed in BBR 1.4.2 [Fan-wiki]. "The Easter Egg was removed, but still workable, but with Mini Me and Turkish SW Boots" [Fan-wiki], and the room is seen in a Jul 2013 BBG-HUD screenshot [Observed: RhoBBG.jpg]. Whether they exist in 2026 is [Unverified].
- **A hidden dropship** "somewhere deep within the walls", reachable only by glitching [Fan-wiki]. If you show it, make it a generic dropship (Overview §5.2 item 8).
- **The name:** "a parody of Neon Genesis Evangelion's Human Instrumentality Project" [Fan-wiki]. It is a third-party nod, so add no visual references to it.
- **Rainbow motifs:** the rainbow column and the face crates echo the Huggable rainbow motif [Inference].
- **Blue-side glitch:** "The glitch on getting on the top of the map only works on blue side." [Unverified fan description].

**Gag ideas** (all [BBG News invention], built on the facts above; cartoon violence only)
1. **Specimen check:** a bear taps a tube. The teddy floating inside snaps its head round to follow the bear, its bead eye glinting, and the bear backs away. Its grin never moves (huggable.md face rule). This revises the Dossier's "smiles back" idea, which would break that rule.
2. **Odd doll out:** a line of pink dolls glides along the overhead rail. One drops off its hook, a live Huggable, for a hug ambush. The KO is a floppy, intact pratfall.
3. **Sandwich standoff:** two rivals face off over the sandwich on the centre bridge, both lunge, and both drop onto the crate field under the hanging dolls.
4. **Movie night:** a bear peeks through the pink wall, and twenty plush heads in the hidden room turn at once. Caption it as a fan-wiki easter egg, never "in BBG 2026".
5. **Spawn pop:** a bear bursts out of the team door inside four circling pale-green shields, lit by the door glow (Conventions §16, staging spot 1).
6. **Up the arrows:** a bear hits the pad at the arrow pillar and rockets past the hanging dolls. The pad's real target is [Unverified], so don't present it as a route.
7. **Claw prize:** the overhead claw plucks a bear like an arcade-machine toy. The claw's motion is undocumented, so this is a pure gag, never a real hazard.
