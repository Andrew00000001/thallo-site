# Marecraft: house-style environment sheet

This sheet is for BBG News shorts in the feature-film 3D house style (`characters/STYLE.md`). Facts come from the verified maps research in `research_notes/BBG maps (verified)/`, and each keeps its source label. Short names used below: **Dossier** = `marecraft-map.md` (this map has no addendum); **Overview** = `00_OVERVIEW.md`; **Conventions** = `environment-conventions.md`; **Conv. addendum** = `environment-conventions.addendum.md`; **Lineup** = `2026-lineup.md` and its addendum; **Story** = `story-locations.md`. Hex values are approximate (about ±15%), sampled from compressed frames [Inference] (Overview §0 item 4).

**Teen-rating rule for this map, first:** Marecraft is shown as "a stylised warm-orange organic cavern with soft cartoon ribs and glowing green pools, never gore" (STYLE.md: Sets and light; Overview §5.5 item 15). No blood, no wet viscera, nothing that reads as a real organ. Every look note below is written to that rule.

---

## 1. What it is

- **Name:** "Marecraft" in captions, as on the 2026 soundtrack (track 8, 0:57) [Canon]. The 2013 teaser title is "The Battle Bears Marecraft Arena" and its overlay reads "THE MARECRAFT" [Canon]. The wiki page is "Marecraft (Map)" [Fan-wiki] (Dossier: Official text).
- **Setting:** inside the digestive system of the Marecraft Carrier, the *Battle Bears: Zombies* boss: a giant unicorn with an aircraft carrier on its back [Fan-wiki]. Dark-metal forts stand over orange-red living tissue, lime-green pools and tube tunnels [Fan-wiki; Observed]. It is an enclosed interior, so there is no sky or time of day [Observed; Inference] (Dossier: Setting; STYLE.md: Sets and light).
- **Era and 2026:** added in BBR ("Battle Bears") 1.4.3 / 1.4.3.1, Apr 15 and 23, 2013 [Fan-wiki], and in BBG from 1.0 (May 24, 2013) [Fan-wiki]. SkyVu's 2026 text does not name it [Canon], but a build 3.0.2 playtest thumbnail shows it [Observed; map ID Inference, strong]: very likely at launch [Inference, strong] (Overview §2.3). The Steam trailer shot (about 38.3–41.5 s) is 2013 footage [Inference, strong], so caption it "classic 2013 look" (Overview §0 item 1).

## 2. Layout for staging

(Dossier: Layout; Conventions §3–§4)
- **Footprint:** a long flesh floor about twice as long as it is wide along the team axis. Dark-metal flank decks widen its middle half, so the whole map is roughly square [Observed]. Each half is the other turned through 180° [Observed] (Overview §3).
- **Spawns and teams:**
  - Spawn markers sit in two staggered rows at each end, red at one end and purple at the other [Observed]. Purple is very likely the blue team [Inference]. The crosshairs are overlay graphics on the top-down render, so never draw them in the world.
  - A respawning bear is circled by translucent pale-green shields for 4.5 s [Fan-wiki; effect Observed] (Conventions §4). One shield shows a faint cross or plus emblem [Observed] (Conventions §4; Overview §3). In FFA a player spawns at either team's points [Fan-wiki].
  - A red lead's home is the red end, with the red-faced ring, the red bridge socket and the red-brown deck tint [Observed; team coding Inference]. Opponents default to blue (STYLE.md: Teams in frame).
- **Lanes:**
  1. **Central bridge** along the team axis: a dark-metal span with four square post brackets per side and torn flesh edges at both ends [Observed]. A dark A-frame gateway arch spans its end [Observed: Imagefbvhiqhb]. That there is one at each end is [Inference]. The bridge separates the two sides [Fan-wiki].
  2. **Cross-walkway** at mid-length, running side to side from flank deck to flank deck [Observed], below the bridge ("intersection below the large bridge") [Fan-wiki].
  3. **Flank decks:** dark metal along both long sides, the wiki's "metal landing pad" that links the halves "around the outside" [Fan-wiki; Observed].
  4. **Underground:** "Below each team's base is a big room" that looks "like a tube or intestine", with "a jump pad at the end that brings players up to a base" [Fan-wiki]. A dark octagonal ring sits at each far end [Observed]. The wiki says a ring marks the entrance to the underground [Fan-wiki], but places it "at the two ends of the intersection". Treating the far-end rings as those entrances is [Inference] (see §7). The teaser looks through one ring into the tunnel [Observed: teaser maxres2].
- **Four green pools** fill the quadrants between bridge and walkway [Observed]. "Below each tower is a canal of green digestive juices" [Fan-wiki].
- **Each base half** (described for the red end; the purple end is rotated 180°) [Observed unless marked]:
  - a crimson mound between the spawn and the bridge;
  - a squat round sniper tower at the outer corner of a pool, its dish facing the centre;
  - a woven orange fibre mound in the corner on the tower's side;
  - in the opposite corner, a round crater mound inside a dark castellated wall with spiky fins, function undocumented [Unverified];
  - at the far end, the dark octagonal ring portal.
- **Vertical spots (three levels)** [Inference from Fan-wiki text + Observed]:
  - (1) the underground tube rooms;
  - (2) the flesh floor and the lower walkway over the pools;
  - (3) the bridge, the battlements and the tower tops.
  - A teal-cored pad among each tower's legs [Observed] probably launches players onto the tower [Inference].
  - The bumpy gland ledges let a player who falls from the battlements get out without dropping into the canal [Fan-wiki].
- **Sightlines:** the towers overlook the bridge and pools [Observed]. This fits the archived Astoria tip about "tall towers" that "cover most of the battlefield", with enemies who "can easily surprise you" [Canon per prior notes; Unverified this session]. Another line runs down the bridge axis under the arch [Inference from Observed elements: Imagefbvhiqhb arch; overview layout]; no source shows that view. A line also runs through a ring into its tunnel [Observed: teaser maxres2].
- **Where the camera can sit:**
  - **Low on the red-orange floor, looking at a long dark ledge or bridge rail** across the middle ground, with the dish-topped tower and pale apricot ribs behind it and the grey arm overhead. This is the Steam trailer's composition (a firefight on a dark ledge, about 39.5 and 41.4 s) [Observed; 2013 footage Inference, strong] (Dossier: Look and Corrections 1; Lineup §6), and the best-documented view. The trailer shows no pool.
  - **At the tower foot by its pad,** looking up the legs [Observed: teaser maxresdefault]. File:Aaaaa.jpg shows a jump pad between two thick dark pillars under an overhang, probably the same spot [Inference] (Dossier: Layout, Tower access).
  - **Through a ring** into the red tube tunnel, with the cyan pad at the far end [Observed: teaser maxres2].
  - **Low on a nub-lined pool edge,** looking up at the bridge rail (Dossier: Staging spot 1) [BBG News invention].
  - **Down the bridge axis** under the A-frame arch, or **from a tower top** down over the bridge [BBG News invention, on Observed elements].
  - Avoid a true top-down: the only overhead is a cut-away render with the ceiling removed [Observed].

This sheet extends that rule to the landmark and the staging ground [BBG News invention]. Use one landmark per shot (STYLE.md: Sets and light; Overview §5.3 item 13).

## 3. Look in the house style

**Shapes and materials.** Everything is re-rendered per STYLE.md: soft rounded forms, bold readable silhouettes, no ink outlines, and never "low-poly" (Overview §5.3 items 10–12). The game's look is chunky, low-poly geometry with simple painted textures: soft matte-glossy flesh against flat, angular, weathered dark-metal plating [Observed] (Dossier: Look, Materials). Keep that soft-against-hard contrast.
- **Organic walls and floor:** smooth, softly undulating surfaces with a dry satin finish, like soft moulded clay, crossed by thin darker-red branching lines [Observed lines; finish BBG News invention]. Never wet, glistening, porous or skin-like (Overview §5.5 item 15). The Dossier's optional "faint wet sheen" [BBG News invention] is dropped for that reason.
- **Ribs and arch:** huge, smooth tubular arches in pale apricot that rise and lean inward to the ceiling, with a broad pale arch behind them [Observed]. These are STYLE.md's "soft cartoon ribs": rounded and calm.
- **Hanging column:** a huge cream-gold column hanging at the centre, segmented by dark maroon chevron bands [Observed]. The Dossier reads it as a throat [Inference]. Render it as a clean banded column, never as anatomy.
- **Hanging lobes:** soft, rounded orange teardrops dangling from the ceiling [Observed], like plush lanterns [BBG News invention].
- **Dark metal:** chunky gunmetal plating with softly bevelled edges and a lightly worn painted finish (STYLE.md: robot parts are clean painted metal). Under the orange light it reads warm brown-grey [Observed].
- **Pools:** smooth grass-to-lime green liquid traced with thin branching paler-green lines [Observed] (the Dossier's "paler vein lines"), with a soft emissive glow (STYLE.md: Glows). Making the liquid translucent is [BBG News invention]; the research does not say it is. The pools read as very bright green in Marecraftt.jpg [Observed]; whether they really emit light is [Unverified]. Never murky or bubbling by default.
- **Edge nubs:** rows of small rounded pale pink-white beads with red-orange tips along the pool edges [Observed]. The tips glow red-orange only in one undated fan thumbnail, iwPrlkuDEJ4 [Observed; era unknown]. Render them as smooth beads like candy buttons [BBG News invention]. In classic-2013 prompts the tips are plain red-orange; a gentle glow is optional and era-unknown.
- **Draped cords:** pale-pink branching cords lying over the bridge deck [Observed: veins]. Render them as soft rounded cords, like thick yarn.
- **Mounds:** low, smooth crimson hillocks [Observed]. The Dossier's "tongue- or liver-shaped" reading must not show: give them a simple rounded silhouette. Make the woven mounds soft straw-like orange fibre, like a giant nest [Observed].
- **Tube tunnels:** ribbed round tunnels with soft cell-like bumps and branching lines, a mauve-pink tube along the floor [Observed: teaser; Fan-wiki]. Render them bouncy and cartoony, never visceral.
- **Spiky fins and thorns:** keep the silhouettes of the battlement fins and the paired orange edge spikes [Observed], with softly rounded tips [BBG News invention, a house-style and teen-rating guard].
- **Jump pads:** a clean dark-metal octagon with a yellow-and-black hazard rim, a glowing cyan star core and faint stacked cyan octagon rings [Observed] (Conventions §7). Under the orange light the core reads soft teal [Observed].
- **No weather or particles:** none is visible in any still [Observed, absence]. Dripping, steam and bubbles are undocumented [Unverified].

**Palette (words)** (classic 2013 look; strength "saturated but natural" [house style])
- Floor: saturated red-orange to vermilion. Walls: saturated orange with dark-red lines; shaded walls in burnt orange.
- Mounds: deep crimson to raspberry red.
- Ribs and column: pale apricot to warm cream-gold, shaded in tan, with dark maroon bands on the column.
- Draped cords: dusty salmon pink. Tunnel tube: muted mauve-pink.
- Metal: warm brown-grey gunmetal, deep brown in shade, near-black brown on tower caps and pillars. The tower dish is pale grey with a warm cast.
- Team tints: the flank decks shade from dark red-brown at the red end to dark navy at the purple end [Observed; team coding Inference]. The ring frame at the purple end is slate navy. The bridge sockets are deep red and deep royal blue.
- Pools: deep grass-to-lime green with thin branching paler leaf-green lines.
- Woven mounds: burnt orange to terracotta.
- Jump-pad core: soft teal-cyan with a mustard-yellow and black rim.
- **The two 2013 sources differ in grade:** the 2013 renders are less saturated than the graded Steam trailer, which pushes the flesh to a hotter orange-red [Observed]. Both count as "classic 2013 look". Pick one per short (STYLE.md habit 6: one grade per short).

**Lighting**
- **Classic 2013 look (the default):**
  - In-game: an enclosed interior with no sky or daylight, lit warm orange and fairly evenly, with little cast shadow [Observed]. The Dossier records no lamps or fixtures.
  - Practical glows: the bright green pools [Observed in one image; emission Unverified], and the cyan jump-pad cores [Observed]. The nub tips glow red-orange only in one undated fan thumbnail [Observed; era unknown], so the classic-2013 rig leaves them unlit by default.
  - **House rig** [BBG News invention, on the observed light]:
    - Key: soft, broad, warm-orange interior light from above (the map's own light; Overview §5.3 item 11).
    - Underlight: lime-green glow rising from the pools onto the bridge's underside and onto characters near the edge.
    - Fill: cool, carried by the green pool bounce and the cyan pads, which justify STYLE.md's cool fill.
    - Rim: gentle and pale apricot.
    - Shadows: soft contact shadows only, plus soft global illumination; avoid harsh black shadow pools.
    - Add no visible lamps or fixtures. None is documented, so they would be [BBG News invention].
  - Keep one rig per scene. Weapon flashes may tint characters within a shot (STYLE.md habit 2; Sets and light).
- **Tunnel scenes:** the same warm key, dimmer and enclosed, with the far pad's cyan glow as the accent [Observed: teaser maxres2; rig BBG News invention]. Treat the tunnel as its own scene.
- **2026 builds:**
  - **Before the pass:** the Aug 2026 playtest thumbnail (build 3.0.2) shows the same geometry and warm palette, with deep shadows under the metal structures [Observed]. Whether that reflects BBG 3.0's 2021 "New Map Lighting" is [Inference]. This is pre-pass footage. It may not show the launch look [Inference] (Overview §0 item 5).
  - **Oct 2026 pass** ("Lighting improvements" [Canon]): the Lineup addendum saw Marecraft in Oct 15 final-playtest storyboard frames (build 3.0.7: InstableGR HKbwq7PolNc 64:40–69:30; JustNubz 1MP1rFi4t-Q 48:20–51:10 and 70:50–71:20) [Observed] (Lineup addendum §6), but nobody described or measured its look. ## 5. Hazards, gags and easter eggs

**Verified**
- **The pools kill:** "Any player who falls into the canal will die and will be considered as a suicide" [Fan-wiki]. A bomb carrier who falls into "a suicide pit" detonates the bomb [Fan-wiki, general rule]; applying that to these pools is [Inference]. On screen, a fall ends in an intact cartoon splash or pop, and the "SUICIDE" wording stays off screen (Overview §5.5 item 16).
- **A Shield doesn't save a pool fall:** "A Shield does not stop fall deaths" [Fan-wiki] (Overview §3; Conventions §9). Never stage spawn shields or a Shield pickup rescuing a bear from a pool.
- **Gland-ledge escape:** after a fall from the battlements, a player can travel "along the uneven and protruding gland" without dropping into the canal [Fan-wiki].
- **Jump pads:** one in each tube room brings players "up to a base" [Fan-wiki]; one among each tower's legs [Observed], whose landing spot is [Inference].
- **Pickups:** the sandwich on the bridge; the basket at the intersection below; Shields at the walkway sides; Health Packs on the towers [Fan-wiki] (Dossier: Hazards and interactive elements).
- **Bridge sockets:** probably the Plant the Bomb hardware [Inference; Unverified]. The 2012 bomb is a team-coloured round bomb on a same-colour stand [Fan-wiki; Observed 2012] (Conventions §8).
- **"Projectile vomit":** reportedly in the official blurb, but whether it is a hazard or a joke is unknown [Unverified]. Never show it: it is unverified and fails the tone guard.
- **Glitch:** a shrunken player can squeeze into a ring and end up "in the ground" [Fan-wiki]. It is a glitch, not a route. Gag 3's dive through a ring is a cartoon shortcut [BBG News invention], neither this glitch nor a documented route.

**Easter eggs and references**
- **The Assassin (Botch) leak:** "two mysterious outlines of the same bear with a chewed-off ear" at the bottom of the bridge [Fan-wiki], called "the first known instance of a class being intentionally leaked in an Easter Egg" on the Assassin (BBG) page [Fan-wiki]. No image was found [Unverified], and whether it survives later builds is [Open]. The wiki says the face "has actual features such as pink fur and yellow skin", and the Assassin page says the egg was "placed under the map's main bridge" [Fan-wiki]. Any depiction is [BBG News invention] and should keep those details; caption it as a fan-wiki easter egg.
- **"Two separate uvula, each next to a battlement"** [Fan-wiki]: the hanging orange lobes may be these [Inference]. The research gives no lobe count, so any count on screen is [BBG News invention].
- **The premise line:** "Have you ever tried to take out a team of highly skilled players INSIDE A GIANT UNICORN??!!!!" [Canon]. Use it as caption copy only, never as text in an image.
- **The "can't SEE it" blurb:** "you're just going to have to trust us that this is the Marecraft Carrier, you can't really SEE it cause you're, you know, in it." [Canon per prior notes; Unverified this session]. Caption it as an archived SkyVu blurb.
- **Name pun:** mare + aircraft carrier [Inference].
- **Crew:** Huggables crew the Carrier in the BBZ cutscene [Fan-wiki], so the forts were probably Huggable-built [Inference]. The BBH "128" skin is based on a Huggable marshalling aircraft in that cutscene [Fan-wiki].
- **Fan in-joke:** the wiki's "Having flying korses before it was cool!" poster is a fan upload [Observed; fan-made Inference]. Don't reproduce it.
