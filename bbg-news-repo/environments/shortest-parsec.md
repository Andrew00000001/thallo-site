# Shortest Parsec: house-style environment sheet

This sheet is for BBG News shorts in the feature-film 3D house style (`characters/STYLE.md`). Facts come from the verified maps research in `research_notes/BBG maps (verified)/`, and each keeps its source label. Short names used below: **Dossier** = `shortest-parsec.md` (this map has no addendum); **Overview** = `00_OVERVIEW.md`; **Conventions** = `environment-conventions.md`; **Lineup** = `2026-lineup.md` and its addendum; **Story** = `story-locations.md`. Hex values are approximate (about ±15%), sampled from compressed frames [Inference] (Overview §0 item 4).

**Orientation used throughout:** face the laser tower from the far (switch) tower. Red is then on the left and blue on the right [Observed] (Dossier: Layout, "Spawns and team sides").

---

## 1. What it is

- **Name and spelling:** "Shortest Parsec" in captions, as on the 2026 soundtrack (track 9, 1:13) [Canon]. SkyVu's 2013 iOS notes say "The Shortest Parsec" [Game text via wiki]. The wiki page dropped the "The" on 2013-04-21 [Fan-wiki: page history] (Dossier: Official text, "The name").
- **Setting:** a small space station floating in a starfield, with almost no walls [Fan-wiki; Observed]. There is no sun, planet or ground, so it has no time of day [Observed] (Dossier: Look, "Sky" and "Lighting"). SkyVu's archived blurb [Canon per notes; Unverified this session] (Dossier: Official text; Setting): "Kinda like a robotic someone I've seen before exploded a few miles away and peppered it with its body parts. Kinda has a sinister feeling, like some kind of private arms dealer uses it to transfer power to its facilities based on the surface of a mysterious planet on the fringes of the known galaxy." (The two archived pieces overlap, so they read as one passage [Inference].) So the orange beam is plausibly the power-transfer beam [Inference]. The planet is off-map and never in frame.
- **Era and 2026:** added in BBR ("Battle Bears") 1.4.2, Feb 28, 2013 [Game text via wiki]; in BBG until at least 2021 [Inference] (Dossier: Era and status). SkyVu's 2026 text does not name it [Canon]. The 2026 build evidence is its tile in the PICK LEVEL carousel of SkyVu's Oct 15 stream [Observed match] and storyboard stills of the Oct 15 final playtest [Observed]. The Overview's verdict is "very likely" [Inference, strong] (Overview §2.3); the Dossier calls it "likely, … not confirmed by SkyVu" [Inference]. Its Steam trailer shots (24.0–28.25 s, 49.5–51.75 s) and Steam screenshots screen_02 and screen_04 are 2013 footage [Inference, strong], so caption them "classic 2013 look" (Overview §0 item 1).

## 2. Layout for staging

(Dossier: Layout; Overview §3; Conventions §3, §4, §7 and §11)
- **Footprint:** a compact station along one spine, mirrored left to right [Observed] (Overview §3). From the laser end to the far end [Observed: overhead; trailer 24.5 and 25.75 s]:
  1. **Laser tower:** a block at one end, joined to the central module by two dark legs.
  2. **Team arms:** two L-shaped decks flank the laser tower and wrap down to the central module. Each has red or blue dashed edge lights, team-coloured crosshair-circle marks [Observed: overhead; possibly spawn markers, not floor paint: the Dossier reads them as floor decals, Conventions §3 reads the same overhead marks as spawn glyphs, and whether the overheads are SkyVu renders is Unverified (Conventions open question 7)], a boxy equipment pod with pipes at its outer top corner and a jump pad on the deck. Thick ribbed black pipes with dark-red couplings join each arm to the module. Whether the pipes are walkable is undocumented. Keep the crosshair-circle marks out of prompts until a frame clearly shows them painted on the deck.
  3. **Central module** (the wiki's "middle dome"; boxy, not domed [Observed]): a big white block holding a long hall.
  4. **Spine walkway:** narrow, carrying a large red emblem medallion, from the module to the far tower.
  5. **Far tower** (the switch tower, or "meteor tower"): grey decks with jump pads at the corners, a cracked red floor, a jade-green console and a large teal asteroid lodged against it.
- **Spawns:** both sit at the laser end, one on each side of the beam [Inference], from the wiki's "orange beam … parting the two teams spawn points" [Fan-wiki] and a 2014 fan thumbnail of a player at spawn facing the beam [Observed]. If the team-coloured crosshair-circle marks on the arms are spawn glyphs (Conventions §3), they also put the spawns on the arms [Inference]. A removed 2014 paragraph puts spawns "on the upper or lower level of the base" [Fan-wiki]. A respawning bear is circled by translucent pale-green shields for 4.5 s [Fan-wiki; effect Observed] (Conventions §4). A red lead's home is the left (red) arm [Inference]; opponents default to blue (STYLE.md: Teams in frame).
- **The hall** (Dossier: Layout, "The central module"):
  - Wide enough for several bears abreast, under a long open roof slot [Observed].
  - Red ceiling beams, a dark slate floor, white tiles at both ends, and a jump pad on the tiles at the laser-tower end only [Observed].
  - At the far end (the end facing the switch tower [Inference], from the door colours), a raised lit ramp with a red medallion, and a white wall carrying the crosshair glyph [Observed: New_map_2; the 2013 tile; rXOhC9jH7KI/maxres3]. It matches the emblem's crosshair-like monogram, not its bear figure [Inference].
  - A red-lit door and a purple-lit door in the side walls. They probably open toward the red and blue arms [Inference]. The wiki adds entry "from the front or from two small openings on each team side" [Fan-wiki: removed 2014 paragraph].
- **Vertical spots** [Observed unless marked]:
  - Three levels: upper decks, the hall, and a lower floor.
  - The lower level (Jan 2013 preview, New_Map_4): a dark indigo-to-violet floor lit lavender-purple, round glowing ceiling lights, four jump pads, low red rails and a white central block, open to the stars.
    - **Where:** a 2014 fan thumbnail shows a similar purple-lit, red-railed floor below the far tower's white wall [Observed: Fh8uf0FyWCs/maxres3], so the lower level probably sits under the far (switch) tower [Inference].
    - That location ties it to the wiki's spots: the bombs sit "underneath the tower across from the spawn points" and the Health Pack is "By the pipeline on the lower floor" [Fan-wiki]; the Shield and the graffiti wall are also placed there (section 4). That this floor is the bomb platform is [Inference].
  - Jump pads link the levels. An Assault with "Turkish SW Boots and Jump" can "jump from the tower … to either team's spawn points" [Fan-wiki: Metal Crush].
  - The asteroid tops and two tower tops are high perches, reached with So Fly, Metal Crush or the Jump Boots [Fan-wiki] (section 5, "High perches").
- **Sightlines:**
  - From spawn: the beam and the pickup inside it [Observed: fan thumbnail].
  - From the far-tower asteroid: the red-lit deck top left and the blue-lit deck top right [Observed: EE3].
  - Through the hall's roof slot: the laser tower's red panels and the hex-grid emitter [Observed: screen_04].
  - Down the spine: the walkway lines up the far tower, the module and the laser tower [Observed overhead; the sightline is Inference].

## 3. Look in the house style

**Shapes and materials.** Everything is re-rendered per STYLE.md: soft rounded forms, bold readable silhouettes, no ink outlines, and never "low-poly" in a prompt (Overview §5.3 items 10–12). The game builds the station from "clean, toy-like hard-surface blocks" [Observed] (Dossier: Look). Keep that clean, toy-like quality.
- **Hulls:** off-white to lavender blocks with engraved circuit-like panel lines [Observed]. Render them as clean painted panels with softly bevelled edges and crisp, soft-edged grooves, like a glazed toy [finish BBG News invention, after STYLE.md's "clean painted metal or plastic"].
- **Laser tower:** glossy red inlay panels with black seams [Observed]. Render them as glossy lacquered red with rounded seams. The two light bars glow mint-white with soft bloom [Observed; bloom per STYLE.md: Glows].
- **Pipes:** ribbed black with dark-red couplings [Observed]. Render them as soft, thick ribbed hoses.
- **Spikes and nubs:** red-tipped antenna spikes on the tower tops and pods; small white spikes and nubs under the platforms [Observed]. Give the tips a slight rounding [BBG News invention].
- **Beam:** a translucent orange-gold cone full of drifting sparkles [Observed]. Render it as a soft volumetric cone with gentle bloom, and never as fire.
- **Emitter:** a pale sky-blue sphere with a hexagon grid [Observed], as a softly glowing engraved sphere.
- **Hall:** saturated red beams and door frames, pale panelled walls, red angled benches or consoles, a dark slate floor [Observed]. Use a smooth lacquer on the red, matte pale walls and a soft satin floor. In prompts, call the beams "red-painted ceiling girders", so they don't read as light beams or a red laser; nothing documents them giving off light [BBG News invention].
- **Asteroids:** big, faceted, dark-teal rocks pierced by hexagonal holes [Observed]. Render them as chunky rock masses with broad, softly bevelled facets and clean hexagonal holes [BBG News invention, after STYLE.md's faceted-effects rule, as the Frozen Pundra and Aztec Attack sheets do].
- **Far-tower dressing:** a jade-green console (the Dossier's prose says lime; its only sample, #1F8D64 from the New_map_3 preview, reads jade, so re-check against a trailer-grade frame) with screens (bar graphs, a smiley face, a keypad) beside an orange-red down-arrow plate; red-and-white or black-and-white striped poles with red tips; a black cable [Observed]. Show the screens as simple glowing bars and shapes, never as text.
- **Jump pads:** octagonal, with a black rim, yellow-and-black hazard stripes and a glowing cyan core that throws up stacked holographic cyan rings [Observed] (Conventions §7).
- **Sky:** a painterly starfield, never a photo. There is no weather [Observed]. Out of bounds below the station, teal-green and blue-violet aurora-like streaks [Observed: 2014 fan glitch thumbnail ySZY61sRFiQ/maxres3].

**Palette (words)** (classic 2013 look; strength "saturated but natural" [house style])
- Space: near-black with a faint violet cast, dense white star flecks, a broad indigo-violet galactic band with a paler periwinkle edge. On the laser-tower side [Inference], a warm violet-magenta (plum-wine) nebula [Observed: Scary_Light; trailer 28.0 s]. Out of bounds below the station, teal-green and blue-violet aurora-like streaks [Observed: 2014 fan glitch thumbnail ySZY61sRFiQ/maxres3].
- Hulls: off-white with a cool lavender cast. The trailer grade blows them to near-white with icy highlights; the preview grade is soft lavender-periwinkle, shaded blue-violet.
- Laser tower: saturated scarlet panels with salmon highlights and deep brown-red shadow; white light-bar cores with pale mint-grey edges; a peach-orange emblem plate.
- Beam: golden-yellow body with a pale butter-yellow core. Pad rim hot orange-red when lit; pad core white. Emitter pale sky-blue.
- Hall (trailer grade): vivid scarlet beams, dusky mauve-beige walls, dark blue-slate floor; coral-red glow at one door, vivid violet at the other. Preview grade: deeper wine-red beams, lilac walls, dark navy floor, a pink-rose red-side door and a blue-violet purple-side door.
- Medallion: muted brick-red to raspberry with black line-art. Far-tower floor: raspberry red. Console: deep jade green.
- Asteroids: deep teal (trailer) or desaturated grey-teal (preview). Jump-pad core: cyan-turquoise.
- Blue arm deck: dusky slate blue (trailer 25.75 s); the red arm's deck was not sampled. Lower level (preview grade only, New_Map_4): deep indigo to violet floor, lit lavender-purple. The purple-lit graffiti wall (muted lavender-purple) is sampled from EE4, a 2021 screenshot, and its spot is [Inference; Unverified], so it is not a 2013 colour.
- **The two 2013 sources differ in grade.** The Jan 2013 preview renders look cooler and lilac; the Steam trailer and screenshots look more saturated red [Observed]. The cause is unknown. There are two candidates [Inference] (Dossier: Look, "Lighting"; Official text, 1.4.2.1): the 1.4.2.1 "graphical rollbacks" (Mar 1, 2013), or renders that simply differ from the shipped game. So a preview-grade short may not show the shipped look (see section 7 for its caption). Pick one grade per short (STYLE.md habit 6).

## 5. Hazards, gags and easter eggs

**Verified hazards** (Dossier: Hazards and interactive elements)
- **The void:** "The map has almost no walls … suicides are very abundant" [Fan-wiki]. Most outer deck edges have no railing; low red rails line some walkways and the lower level [Observed]. On screen, a fall is a quick, intact cartoon tumble into the stars that ends on a pop or pratfall beat, and the "SUICIDE" wording stays off screen (Overview §5.5 item 16). A camera looking down past the edge can show the aurora-like streaks below the station (section 3, "Sky").
- **The laser:** it "activates periodically and cannot be controlled" [Fan-wiki]. The wiki disagrees on whether it kills anyone who stays under it or "does damage quickly over time" [Fan-wiki, conflicting]. Whether a Shield or caffeine boost gets you through, and which projectiles it stops, are also contested [Fan-wiki, conflicting]. Its period, warning cue and sound are undocumented [Unverified]. Show a hit as a cartoon singe: sooty fur and smoking ears, never a burn [BBG News invention].
- **The floor switch:** a melee hit on the switch "found on the tower across from the spawn points" moves the module's floor. It "can be used to lure players to their deaths, even your own teammates" [Fan-wiki]. The 1.4.2 article says only "a removeable floor" [Fan-wiki]. A removed 2014 paragraph calls the room a "garbage chute" whose floor "can be opened and closed by a lever" [Fan-wiki]. From these the Dossier reads it as a trapdoor onto open space [Inference]. The only first-hand account cuts against that: a fan wrote that a teammate "decided to hit the switch which made my character SHOOT across the map" [Unverified: fan video description, 6YRBMycML0A, Mar 3, 2013]. So the floor may launch players rather than only drop them. Its animation, speed and open time are [Unverified].
- **Jump pads:** a stall bug can leave a player hanging "midway between the normal landing point and the Jump Pad at the edge of each team's base", "especially dangerous on Parsec" [Fan-wiki].
- **Plant the Bomb:** both bombs sit "on the bottommost platform right next to each other, underneath the tower across from the spawn points" [Fan-wiki].
- **Beta exploit:** So Fly plus a jump pad once reached "the asteroids on the other side" [Fan-wiki].

**Easter eggs and references** (Dossier: Easter eggs and references)
- **The unreadable sign:** a panel reading "YOU SHOULDN'T BE ABLE TO READ THIS!", printed twice (one copy rotated 180°), with five stars and two annoyed bear faces [Observed: EE1]. It fills an outer face of the laser tower above the arch, and the wiki says it is read from the asteroid near the blue base [Fan-wiki]. **Render the panel blank, keeping the stars and the two squinting faces as plain shapes.** The blank panel keeps the joke's irony (Overview §5.2 item 5); carry the line in the caption or voice-over.
- **High perches (the wiki's Easter-egg list)** [Fan-wiki]: three meteorites plus two tower tops:
  - the red-base meteorite ("can be reached only by using So Fly … and Metal Crush");
  - the blue-base meteorite, from which the sign is read;
  - from the blue base, So Fly plus the Jump Boots reach "the top of the tower where the huggable corpse is", and repeating it reaches "the top of the place where the laser comes from" (the laser tower);
  - the top of the switch-tower meteorite.
- **Huggable-only claim:** contested and outdated [Fan-wiki; Inference]. Never caption it as fact.
- **Scorch "face":** two drippy smudges like weeping eyes on the white block under the beam pad [Observed: EE2]. Whether it is deliberate is [Unverified].
