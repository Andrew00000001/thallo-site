# Voice casting: Episode 1 (original AI voices)

Bryce ruled on 2026-10-20 that voices may be original AI voices: Higgsfield preset voices, never cloned from or imitating a real person. Robot treatment (B-1000's digital grit) is added in the edit.

**How the shortlist was made:** I measured each of the 44 male Higgsfield preset previews for median pitch (f0), pitch variability (semitone SD) and brightness (spectral centroid), and matched them to each soul's voice spec. The final pick is by ear, from auditions of the characters' actual Episode 1 lines.

| Character | Spec (soul / Ep 1) | Shortlist (f0 Hz · pitch SD · brightness) |
|---|---|---|
| B-1000 | Mid-low, evenly clipped, deadpan (flat pitch), light digital grit, then a bright "tee-hee" | Benji (108 · 2.2 · 1013), Desmond (109 · 2.8 · 656), Caspian (82 · 1.7 · 774), Alexey (82 · 1.8 · 1256) |
| Sanchez | Warm, slightly gravelly baritone with operatic gravitas | Brooks (96 · 6.2 · 717), Jasper (86 · 5.4 · 818), Grady (108 · 5.8 · 892), Mark (117 · 3.8 · 853) |
| Oliver | Bright, mid-range young-adult male with a grin in it; deadpan crack | Dylan (145 · 5.2 · 1440), Archie (165 · 9.0 · 1177), Reid (167 · 4.7 · 1962), Andre (143 · 6.2 · 1212) |

**Audition lines** (seed_audio, 0.2 credits each, 12 lines = 2.4 credits):
- B-1000: "B one thousand. Live. Scanning location. Hazard: crashed dropship. Status: still crashed. Tee-hee."
- Sanchez: "Oliver, we'll getcha... home. This arm, I did not ask for. But look at it."
- Oliver: "Sanchez! Buddy! You look great! ...Fry? ...That's their ship."

**Status:** the audition batch was refused on 2026-10-20 by Higgsfield's grace-period daily generation limit, which counts audio as well as images and video. It runs as soon as there is free daily capacity.
