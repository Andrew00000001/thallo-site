## 1. The show

**Name:** BBG News: LOCAL HAZARDS

**Format hook: "Real map. Fan-made news."** B-1000, the half-ton robot "transmitting their data to the bad guys" (the in-game text, as the wiki quotes it), files a deadpan field report from one real BBG map each week. He reads out the map's real hazard like an error log. Then bears walk straight into it. The scenery has never lost. Each week his report to the bad guys stalls at 99% and goes out "TO: EVERYONE" instead, which is why it is on BBG News. Who keeps stealing the upload is the season's mystery. The show, the ledger, the upload storyline and the hijacker are all [BBG News invention].

**Season arcs:**
1. **The scoreboard.** SCENERY N · BEARS 0. Will the bears ever score?
2. **The 99% upload.** Who keeps rerouting it? It is a Huggable's head radio, hinted at in Eps 1, 5 and 6 and revealed in Ep 8. This builds on huggable.md's dynamic "The Huggable tries to hug his signal" and pays off as its logline 1 ("Hug Me, Over") does: the hug crashes the upload [BBG News invention].
3. **"Next location: ___".** Every sign-off names the next map, so viewers can guess which bears will meet which hazard.

**Episode shape:** 5 clips, or 6 in Eps 3, 4 and 6, where an overloaded beat is split so each clip carries one action (STYLE.md habit 4). Each clip is a 10 s Seedance 2.0 generation of which about 6-8 s is used, except the C1 cold open, which uses up to about 9 s. With the sting (~1.5 s), sign-off (~6 s) and end card (~2 s), that gives about 42-51 s per five-clip episode and 48-59 s per six-clip one. Season: 43 clips ≈ 1,935 credits, ≈ 2,900 with a 1.5× retake budget. The retake budget (≈ 970 credits) is one season-wide pool, not a per-episode share, and Ep 1 draws on it first: its C4 alone may take three retakes (135 credits; see §5). Re-run this total after any further clip split. The map plates are image credits and are not counted here. The teaser costs nothing extra, because it reuses Ep 1's clip 1.

## 2. Signature pieces (same every week)

### Cold open: "LIVE" (clip 1, up to ~9 s)
- **Shot:** the same locked-off live framing every week. B-1000 stands slightly left of centre in the upper two-thirds, with the map's one landmark behind him. Faces stay out of the bottom fifth and the right edge.
- **Sheet:** `b-1000-assault-signature`, the only B-1000 Element built (SHEETS.md: Minor, usable), all season: the rail-gun on his right forearm, the three-blade cyan claw gauntlet on his left and the white winged jump-pack on his back [Observed]. All three are 2013 launch weapons [Fan-wiki], so the kit fits every episode's era. No Satellite: it arrived in BBG 2.7 (Jan 2014) [Fan-wiki] and would need its own orb sheet (`b-1000-assault-orb`, b-1000.md). In his solo shots the sheet takes the glossy-armour wording (STYLE.md sanctioned variant).
- **Line:** always "B-1000. Live. Scanning location.", then a 3-4 line hazard report in error-log deadpan, about 17 words in all with the opener, each line landing on a hold.
- **HUD:** thin cyan corner brackets lock onto the real hazard. This is b-1000.md's HUD spec, added in the edit as original art with no game UI.
- **Lip-sync:** his jaw plate drops only a little (STYLE.md face rules). Land each line on a hold so lip-sync is never an issue.

### Sign-off: "THE LEDGER" + "99%" (~6 s, edit only, then the end card)
1. **The ledger card** slams up: SCENERY N · BEARS 0, plus a **METHOD** line that carries the punchline (e.g. "METHOD: WAS PAINT"). The METHOD cards are designed to stack into a collectible grid on the profile.
   - If anyone went down (a bear or the Huggable), three blue-bolt Joule tokens plink onto the card. Otherwise the empty card goes "thunk".
   - The tokens appear only on the card, as the ledger's point marker, never dropped in a scene. Labels (README, Teen rating): the tokens [Observed]; their identity as Joules [Fan-wiki; identification Inference]; their use as the ledger's point marker [BBG News invention, after the Inference kill-marker use].
   - The card uses an original layout and typeface. It never copies the kill feed and never uses the word "SUICIDE" (maps overview §5.5 rule 16).
2. **The stamp** lands: **REAL MAP. FAN-MADE NEWS.**
3. **B-1000 VO:** "Next location: ___. Uploading to the bad guys..."
4. **The upload bar** fills, then freezes at 99%. A 3-frame pink static glitch with a stitched-seam texture (the hijacker clue) plays with a radio "kssht", and the bar reads "REROUTING... TO: EVERYONE".
5. **B-1000:** "Tee-hee." This is his one "Tee-hee" per short, used as the button (b-1000.md). Hard cut.
6. **End card,** word for word, held about 2 s inside the safe area: "Unofficial fan film, not affiliated with SkyVu; characters © SkyVu."

### Production habits (human-made feel)
- **Shots:** follow the STYLE.md shot formula, with one scene line and one light rig per scene, taken word for word from the map's environment sheet and pasted into every shot of that scene; the camera sentence may change between scenes (STYLE.md habit 2; the environment sheets). Character, map and weapon names stay out of prompts. One action per clip. At most two Element characters per shot; a Huggable wave counts as one.
- **Map plates:** before any clip is generated, make the environment sheet's plate for each scene (establishing, medium or low-angle, as the scene's camera needs) in the house finish and use it as that scene's start frame. Build it from the map's era-matched references, used for the eye only or cropped and cleaned under habit 8. This keeps the landmarks recognisable.
- **Edit only:** HUD, tags, ledger, bar, stamp, status sprites, crosshairs and crossword lettering are all added in the edit.
- **Finish:** one grade plus grain per episode, hand-timed holds, original Foley, original music, and cast original voices only (never an imitation). Whether a tool-generated voice counts as an original performer is [Open]; the call is Bryce's.
- **Voices and mouths:** no spoken line ever goes into a prompt (STYLE.md shot formula), so every line, B-1000's sign-off VO included, is a separate voice track, recorded and dubbed in the edit. Get Bryce's ruling on tool-generated voices in the Oct 19-26 week, then cast and record (see §4). Talking bears are prompted with a generic "says a short line" beat in place of the words, and the cast line is dubbed over it; a line a mouth won't carry lands on a hold, off-face or off screen, as B-1000's do. The Huggable never lip-syncs (it talks through its radio), and Botch's flat mouth barely moves (STYLE.md, Sound) [BBG News invention].
- **Rating and teams:** KOs are intact floppy pratfalls. Falls end off screen. Signs are blank, red team leads and opponents are blue. The Huggable's grin never moves.

## 5. Produce first: Ep 1, We'll Getcha Home

1. **Two dates depend on it.** Its C1 is the Oct 28 teaser, and the episode itself is the launch-day post on SkyVu's first-named map.
2. **It builds the whole kit.** The cold-open lock-off, B-1000's signature sheet in the glossy solo wording, the HUD, title sting, THREAT LEVEL tags, ledger, 99% bar, stamp, end card and caption template all get built here, and every later episode reuses them at zero credits.
3. **It tests the riskiest shot earliest.** The jump-pad arc (C4) is the hardest motion in the season. Testing it first leaves the most room for retakes.
4. **Its sheets carry the season.** B-1000 is in all eight episodes, Oliver returns in Ep 4 and the blue Huggable in Ep 6, so their sheets are season-critical anyway.

**Fallback:** if C4 fails after 3 retakes (135 credits from the season-wide retake pool, which Ep 1 draws on first), restage the launch as an edit match-cut (launch frame, then landing frame) and keep the beat.
