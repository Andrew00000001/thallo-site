## What does official SkyVu material say?

### Takeaway
SkyVu's own words define all three characters clearly and consistently from 2011 to 2026:
- **Astoria** is a cold-seeming, "wicked smart" special-ops sniper who secretly cares about the team.
- **Graham** is a "gruff but lovable" old mechanic, armorer and inventor who keeps the ship running.
- **Tillman** is a gung-ho, foul-mouthed, "mostly panda, ALL ROBOT" explosives maniac. He started as an enemy "Delta Bear" and is now on the team.

None of the official sources I checked names a voice actor or gives a detailed backstory for any of the three.

The Nov 28, 2012 date also fits the official Classes page carrying Graham's blurb by Nov 29, 2012. — [Wiki: Battle Bears Royale](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Bears_Royale); [Wiki: Updates](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Updates)

## What does the Battle Bears Fandom wiki say, and which claims can be corroborated?

### Takeaway
The wiki adds most of the specific color: BBG voice lines, weapons, skins, BBO and BBF kits, story beats in BB0, BBF and the comic, and voice-actor names. Several items are corroborated by official sources:

- Tillman as a Delta Bear and robot panda

### Inferences
- **[INFERENCE]** The BBG voice-line sets give each character a very distinct comic register:
