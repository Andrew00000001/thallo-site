## Key Question 1: What does official SkyVu material say about the Huggable, Botch and B-1000?

### Takeaway
Official SkyVu text is short and joke-driven.
- **Playable Huggable.** A "Modified Huggable… a safer version of the standard Huggable", fast and strong in melee. In BB Heroes it is named "Huggy" and called "he". It belongs to the pink, "Hug me!"-only species that is "paradoxically feared for their deadly hugs".
- **B-1000.** "A robot made to kill" and "a mysterious enemy" who studies the Battle Bears in order to annihilate them and send their data to "the bad guys"/"villains".
- **Botch.** "A bear of very few words with a mysterious past" who has "powerful radioactive powers". I found no origin story for him in any SkyVu source I could reach.

### Inferences

## Key Question 4: What concrete details would let a writer portray these characters faithfully?

### Takeaway
This is a summary of the findings below. Each item carries the label of its source.
- **Huggable:** a scarred, stitched, radio-wired pink teddy (BBG: official item text plus wiki and my observation) whose speech is basically "Hug me!" (official, BBH site). It is lethal through "love" (official BBG item text), fast and fragile (90 health; class data via wiki), and fights with bear-trap hands (wiki) and rainbow beams (official BBG item text).
- **B-1000:** a "half-ton murderous robot" (official BBG item text), red and black in BBH art or team-coloured in BBG. He talks in cheerful computer jargon and boasts (BBG voice lines as transcribed by the wiki). His mission is to harvest the Battle Bears' combat data for "the bad guys" (official BBG description via wiki and the BBH site).
- **Botch:** "a bear of very few words" (official, BBH site) who speaks in "grainy one-or-two word quotes" and levitates (wiki, BBG). He has glowing green eyes and a notched ear (official icon), and radioactive powers (official). His BBG kill lines ("Pretty!", "Delightful!") relish destruction, and his defeat line shrugs it off ("Eh. Whatever.") (wiki transcription). [INFERENCE] The narrator's joke item text makes him read as lonely and odd.

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] Botch's voice lines mix childlike wonder ("Pretty!", "Ooh!", "Wee!", "Cozy!") with weary indifference ("Eh. Whatever.", "Finally..." on dying). Together with the "lonely… created two friends" joke, this supports writing him as an eerie, innocent-seeming loner. A writer must present any tragic origin as fan invention. The official line is only that he has a "mysterious past" and that "radiation was involved" (that phrase is in the wiki's BBH text).
- [INFERENCE] B-1000's lines are PC and error-message puns. Give him a deadpan synthetic voice with sudden cheerful glee ("tee-hee", "Boom-shaka-laka!"). "Transmitting their data to the bad guys" makes him a good recurring spy or hunter antagonist who could join an arena match. Leave his masters unnamed, or label "H.I.P." as a fan link.
- [INFERENCE] The playable Huggable works best as a near-mute physical comedian. Its "love" is the weapon, and it lives with a tension between cute and horrific. Its radio pack and team banner suggest it has been conscripted or retrofitted. Who did the retrofitting is not stated officially.
- [INFERENCE] In BBG all three are simply arena classes fighting alongside or against the Battle Bears with no story explanation. A fan film can put them on the same team as Oliver and co. without breaking anything, as long as it doesn't claim official reasons why.

### Gaps
- No official height, age or origin date for any of the three, and no story-mode appearance for Botch or B-1000.
- No official confirmation of who voices Botch or B-1000, or the Huggable in BBG.
- No transcription of the Huggable's BBG voice lines.
- I found nothing official tying Botch to radiation-sewer origins (Toxic Terror), to Pish & Posh, or to any team, or tying B-1000 to Rho or H.I.P.
