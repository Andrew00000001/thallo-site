- **[OFFICIAL]**: SkyVu or Ben Vu material, meaning battlebears.com (live or archived), Steam pages and announcements, App Store and Google Play text, the Epic Games Store FAQ, and official YouTube titles.
- **[FAN-WIKI]**: battlebears.fandom.com, which fans write. Many wiki claims are transcriptions of in-game cutscenes or descriptions, and I could not check those against the games themselves.

- **[UNVERIFIED]**: a claim I could not confirm from any source I could reach.
- **[OBSERVED]** (added 2026-10-18): what a researcher saw in an image or video frame that was actually opened (official art, trailer frames, wiki-hosted screenshots, fan captures). It is a reading, not an official spec, and the bracket names the image.

## Q1. What does official SkyVu material say (titles, story, setting, factions, tone, comics/TV, 2026 framing)?

### Takeaway
SkyVu's own texts define Battle Bears as a comedic sci-fi action series by ex-*Coraline* animator Ben Vu. Robotic or cybernetic teddy-bear soldiers (Oliver, Riggs, Wil and friends, crew of the flagship *Ursa Major*) fight the Huggables, pink bears that want to "HUG [you] TO DEATH". SkyVu refers to "the original trilogy" of story games but, in the sources I found, never lists its three members; Steam calls BB 1: Zombies "the first of the BB trilogy games", and BB Zero is called "the encore to the BATTLE BEARS story", so reading the trilogy as BB 1: Zombies, BB -1 and BB Zero is [INFERENCE]. The multiplayer games (Royale, Gold, Ultimate, Overclock) have no story campaign in the official copy I found; BB Heroes is the exception, since it includes a campaign that recreates BB -1. The 2026 BBG re-release is sold almost entirely on nostalgia ("Your childhood is back!"). Its official copy mentions no new plot ([INFERENCE] from absence), but it does add one new-to-BBG map shown under two names (Sky Pirates, called "Sky World" in the Aug 19 Steam post and in SkyVu's "Sky Pirates in BBG!" short), a demo, and sign-up skins.

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] Official canon gives the world in broad strokes: cyborg teddy soldiers, the *Ursa Major*, a pink planet of Huggables, a shadowy organization with "evil robots", unicorn-themed bio-weapons, and a zombie-raising Huggable wizard. The fine detail lives in cutscenes, and SkyVu's own texts describe that lore in a joking voice rather than as a formal bible.
- [INFERENCE] None of the 2026 BBG official copy (Steam, site, playtest posts, channel description) mentions plot, villains or setting beyond map names. The re-release positions BBG as a nostalgia-driven competitive arena, not a story chapter. Fan films that treat BBG matches as "training", "rivalry" or "exhibition" bouts would therefore not contradict anything official.
- [INFERENCE] The official map blurbs read like a sarcastic, fourth-wall-breaking narrator with fake legal disclaimers and dark jokes. This is a reusable "house voice" for fan-film title cards and narration.

## Q2. What does the Battle Bears Fandom wiki say, and which claims can be corroborated?

### Takeaway
The wiki holds the only detailed scene-by-scene plot of the story games. In story order: BB -1, the Huggable invasion of the *Ursa Major*. Then BB Zero, Wil and ABBi against the H.I.P. Then BB 1: Zombies, Oliver on the Huggable planet with "mystical treasures", Sanchez's crash, the Necromancer's "Zombocalypse", and Riggs's rescue. The wiki also covers the H.I.P. (Huggable Instrumentality Project), the Delta Bears (Tillman, Swanny, Skiles), Rho, Nyx and the bosses. Official sources back the broad strokes: BB -1 as "prequel", Zero as "encore", Ursa Major, Huggable invasion, H.I.P. complex, mystical treasures, Necromancer, Sanchez "near death", Tillman as former enemy. Fact-check correction: the "Huggable Instrumentality Project" expansion, Nyx as an H.I.P. defector, Jackson's hug "accident" in BB -1, and the "Delta Bear" team name are also official (archived battlebears.com pages from 2011 and 2024; see Q1-E). Still wiki-only: Rho's name, Captain Malcolm, Grimes, Skiles, Nyx's BB Zero dialogue, ABBi's lines and the scene-by-scene plot.

## Q3. Where do games and sources disagree, and what changed in the 2026 BBG re-release?

### Takeaway
Release years differ between SkyVu's own list and the wiki: BBG "2012" official vs 2013 relaunch, Zero "2013" vs Oct 2012, Fortress "2014" vs 2013, Ultimate "2015" vs June 2014. Class titles shift across BBG, BBH, Steam and the store copy: Wil is "Chub Scout" or "Scout"; Saberi is "Combat Tech", "Medic" or "Healer"; Tillman is "Demolition", "Demolitions" or "Demo"; the Necromancer is "Sorcerer", "Wizard" or "Zombocalypse". Character roles also shift: the Huggable is an antagonist in story games but playable in BBG; Tillman is an enemy, a bounty hunter or a gung-ho soldier depending on the source; Sanchez is killed, then "resurrected"; Tillman's team is the "Delta Bear team" in 2011 official copy, and the wiki puts Swanny on that team, but 2024 official copy calls Swanny "A member of rival group, the Alpha Bears". The 2026 BBG keeps the 11 classes and PvP modes, adds PC/Mac/controller support, one new-to-BBG map (Sky Pirates, also called "Sky World"; a Battle Bears Overclock map per the fan wiki), a demo and sign-up skins, and states no story changes.

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] A faithful fan short would probably:
  - pair cute-looking enemies with ultra-violent, deadpan punchlines;
  - let the bears be competent but petty or incompetent (Oliver abandoning Wil over an arrow "wound", Riggs's toothbrushing interrupted);
  - use pop-culture parody (Star Wars, Aliens, Halo, Evangelion, Portal and Dragon Ball are all signposted in official or wiki material; an Addams Family nod in the Haunted Castle blurb is only my reading, see Q1-F);
  - break the fourth wall in moderation ("you lost to the first boss").
- [INFERENCE] Safe relationship anchors from official text: Astoria exasperated by Oliver and Wil; Riggs protective of the team; Saberi as the team's "momma bear"; Wil and ABBi as a bickering duo; Tillman's grudging respect after losing to Wil; B-1000 obsessed with harvesting the bears' data; the Necromancer revered by Huggables. Romance (Oliver/Astoria) rests only on wiki claims: an Aztec Attack trivia line ("may show that Oliver has a crush on Astoria"), an "O+A" heart carving in Toxic Terror, and an uncited wiki assertion that "in Battle Bears Fortress, Oliver states that he has a crush on Astoria". None of these is in official text I found, so treat it as [UNVERIFIED], not canon.
