Research date: 2026-10-13. Labels used throughout:
- **[OFFICIAL]**: SkyVu-published text, such as battlebears.com, Steam or App Store copy written by SkyVu, or SkyVu-made character cards and videos.
- **[OFFICIAL-ART via wiki]**: an image of an official SkyVu character card. The image is hosted on the fan wiki, and I have not verified where it was first published.

- **[INFERENCE]**: my own reasoning or my own reading of an image. It is not a sourced fact.

## What does official SkyVu material say about Oliver, Riggs and Wil?

### Takeaway
SkyVu's official material sets a consistent core for each bear:
- **Oliver** is crafty, fun-loving and reckless. He invents weapons on the spot, causes collateral damage and needs an audience.
- **Riggs** is the brave, serious, fatherly captain and leader. SkyVu describes him as "part Captain America, part Jean Luc Picard."
- **Wil** is a naive computer savant. He is fearless only because he doesn't notice danger, he is eager to please, and Oliver often talks him into trouble. (His food obsession is not stated outright in official text. It comes from the wiki, and is only implied officially by the card art of him eating an ice pop and by the "masticator of mayhem… appetite for destruction" wordplay. That reading is [INFERENCE].)

In the 2026 Battle Bears Gold (BBG) re-release they are listed as SOLDIER: Oliver, HEAVY: Riggs and CHUB SCOUT: Wil. The official text has little story detail. Most plot points (cutscene events, quotes, relationships) come from the fan wiki.

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] SkyVu's character voice has stayed stable since about 2012. Oliver's "crafty, fun-loving… collateral damage" text recurs almost word for word on the 2012 card, in the BBH description and on today's site. Riggs's "Captain America + Picard" line also recurs. A writer can treat these as canon pillars.
- [INFERENCE] The official framing is a "workplace sitcom" squad. Riggs is the responsible dad figure. Oliver is the show-off ringleader. Wil is the gullible sidekick whom Oliver dares into danger.

### Gaps
- I found no SkyVu-published biography for any of the three: no age, rank, homeworld or origin. Battlebears.com has no lore pages beyond /pages/characters (checked via the sitemap).
- I could not reach the 2026 "Q&A with the Creator" or the 2026 teaser for transcripts, so they may hold lore statements I could not check.

## What does the Battle Bears Fandom wiki say, and which of those claims can be corroborated?

### Takeaway
The wiki supplies nearly all plot detail and quotes:
- the three BB-1 campaigns (2010)
- Oliver's Huggable Planet treasure mission in BB Zombies
- Wil's ABBi saga in BB Zero
- Fortress (BBF) cameos
- BBG voice lines and taunts
- voice actor names

Several claims are corroborated by official text or by press:
- Oliver as the treasure-hauling hero of BB Zombies (official)
- ABBi as Wil's sarcastic robot backpack (official)
- Wil having beaten Tillman (official)

## Where do games disagree, and what changed in the 2026 Battle Bears Gold re-release?

### Takeaway
The trio's personalities are stable across titles, but these details shift:
- Oliver's design and description (2012 round-eyed card vs 2013+ angular redesign)
- Riggs's title (captain/leader vs Malcolm commanding in BB-1)
- Wil's class name ("Chub Scout" in BBG and the 2026 Steam listing; "Scout" on today's battlebears.com)
- weapons and abilities per game
- several release dates

For 2026, official sources confirm only:
- the same 11 classes with Oliver, Riggs and Wil in their original roles
- PC, iOS and Android release on Nov 4, 2026
- the content counts on the Steam page
- a map new to BBG: the third Steam playtest note lists "New Map: Sky World," and a 2026-08-31 YouTube upload is titled "Sky Pirates Map Reveal." SkyVu's own short "Sky Pirates in BBG!" (2026-09-07) is described "Enjoy the highlights from our Sky World playtest!" [OFFICIAL], which pairs the two names. So Sky World and Sky Pirates are one map (a strong [INFERENCE]). The fan wiki lists Sky Pirates as one of the six launch maps of Battle Bears Overclock, and BBO 3.0's 2020 notes, as the wiki quotes them, say "check out how Skyworld has benefitted!" [WIKI]. So it is a returning BBO map, new only to BBG [INFERENCE]. It is not the 2013 BBG rooftop map Sky View.
- legacy-account and sign-up skin rewards
- an all-human-made-assets pledge

I found no official 2026 change to the trio's lore.

### Inferences
These are writer guidance from my own synthesis. They are not canon text.
- **Oliver:** a show-off improviser. Voice is cocky and jokey, mid-pitch, with pop-culture-quoting barks ("Another one bites the dust," "Mama said knock you out"). He caused the mess, then claims credit for saving the day (BBF card). He bosses Wil around but is soft on him underneath (the "Keep Wil outta my stuff" line; the wiki's crying-O.C.O. ending). Fan-film beat: Oliver turns whatever is at hand into a weapon.
- **Riggs:** military-clipped, low and gruff ("Target neutralized," "Fall in!," "Hostile detected"), with dry, macho humour ("Thank you, may I have another?," "War, it's fantastic!"). He is a protective father figure who will tactically retreat (official). He is also a brawler-engineer (grenade down the throat, chainsaw from elevator parts). He calls Oliver "Soldier" (wiki).
- **Wil:** childlike, gamer-speak ("PWNED," "noob," "This game CHEATS!," "I'm telling!"), always hungry, never swears. He is good with computers and blames his "stupid, nub hands." He is easily talked into danger by Oliver and fearless through obliviousness, not courage. ABBi rides on his back as a sarcastic foil.
- Many of the quotes are movie and music references: Queen (noted by the wiki), plus my own identifications of LL Cool J, *They Live*, *Scarface* and *Animal House*. This fits the press observation that the cutscenes are full of "nods to classic film franchises" ([Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/battle-bears-1/review/)). Fan films can keep that referential humour.
