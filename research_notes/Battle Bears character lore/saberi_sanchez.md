# Saberi (Combat Tech) and Sanchez (Arbiter): Battle Bears lore research notes

Source labels used throughout:
- **[OFFICIAL]**: SkyVu material. This covers battlebears.com (live, or archived on the Wayback Machine), SkyVu's own blog posts, Steam, App Store, Google Play, Microsoft Store and Epic listings, and official art.
- **[OFFICIAL IMAGE, my description]**: an official image I viewed myself. The description is my own reading of the picture.
- **[FAN-WIKI]**: battlebears.fandom.com, which fans write. The wiki often quotes in-game text as "~ Official Battle Bears Gold description", and I could not check those quotes against the game files. They are labelled **[FAN-WIKI, quoting in-game text]**.
- **[THIRD-PARTY]**: press, Wikipedia, and fan YouTube uploads.
- **[INFERENCE]**: my own reasoning. It is never lore.
- **Takeaway** paragraphs summarise the cited findings beneath them. They carry the same labels as those findings and add no new facts.

Game abbreviations: **BBZ** is Battle Bears: Zombies / Battle Bears 1: Zombies (2009). **BBR** is Battle Bears Royale (2011–2013). It was relaunched as BBG in May 2013, in a new app that replaced it. **BBG** is Battle Bears Gold (2013–2024 original run, re-releasing Nov 4, 2026). **BBH** is Battle Bears Heroes (soft launch 2023, global launch 2024).

## Q1. What does official SkyVu material say about Saberi and Sanchez (first appearance, backstory, personality, abilities, look), and who are the other named characters?

### Takeaway
Neither character was in the BBG launch roster. Saberi (Combat Tech) arrived in BBG update 2.4 on Oct 24, 2013, and Sanchez (Arbiter) in update 2.6 on Dec 20, 2013. Both blog posts are on SkyVu's own archived site. Sanchez is older. According to the fan wiki (no official source found), he first appeared as the Pelican't pilot in BBZ (2009) whose rescue of Oliver ends in a crash. SkyVu's later BBH-era copy says he was "resurrected and re-engineered from near death". Official text gives Saberi almost no backstory beyond "powerful healer and momma bear". The richest official-flavoured material for both is BBG item and skin text, but that reaches me only through the wiki (see Q2).

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] Saberi and Sanchez were post-launch BBG additions, two months apart in late 2013. That makes them the newest of the original 11 and the least lore-backed. Neither appears in the story campaigns (BB -1, BB Zero, Fortress; Sanchez's BBZ cutscene aside) or in the archived official character bios. Wayback captures (May–Jul 2014) of the official bio gallery show bio images only for Oliver, Riggs, Wil, Astoria, Graham and Tillman ([CDX listing of battlebears.com/wp-content/gallery/characters/ files](https://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=battlebears.com/wp-content/gallery/characters/&matchType=prefix&collapse=urlkey)). Captures may be incomplete.
- [INFERENCE] Saberi's class label drifts from a gun-and-gadget role toward a healer role.
  - BBG "Combat Tech" was an offensive gadgeteer class: rifles plus a backpack "third arm" carrying missiles or a minigun.
  - BBH relabels her "Medic"/"Healer" with a Healing Turret.
  - The 2026 Steam page reverts to "COMBAT TECH".
  - Writers should treat "tinkerer-engineer who also patches up her team" as the common ground.

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] The wiki's BBG flavour text fits SkyVu's documented joking house style seen in official blog copy, e.g. "Caution may cause severe salt", "Saberi Smash!". I therefore consider these quotes plausible in-game text, but they remain unverified transcriptions.
- [INFERENCE] Saberi's voice lines make her a cheerful, slightly surprised inventor whose gadgets sometimes work better than she expected ("Ooh, that worked?", "Now why didn't that work?").
