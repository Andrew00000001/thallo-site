# Animation and voice reference for the Battle Bears franchise (for BBG fan shorts)

Labels used throughout:
- **[OFFICIAL]**: live SkyVu/BattleCo text (battlebears.com, Steam, official YouTube titles and descriptions, the official Creator Program form).
- **[OFFICIAL-ARCHIVED]**: SkyVu text that survives only in a Wayback capture.
- **[OFFICIAL-FRAME]**: what I saw myself in an official image or video frame. This covers the Steam trailer (downloaded and frame-sampled), Steam screenshots, the official hero icons, and YouTube's thumbnails and auto-generated frames (`hqdefault`, `hq1`, `hq2`, `hq3`, `maxresdefault`, and the Shorts frames `oar1`, `oar2`) of official uploads. The description is my observation; the image itself is official.
- **[FAN-WIKI]**: battlebears.fandom.com.
- **[FAN-UPLOAD]**: a YouTube video posted by a non-SkyVu channel.
- **[PRESS]**: third-party press.
- **[INFERENCE]**: my own reasoning. **[INFERENCE, strong]** marks reasoning that the evidence comes close to proving, though no source states it.

Method notes:
- YouTube watch pages and the player API returned "Sign in to confirm you're not a bot", and the transcript API returned HTTP 400 "FAILED_PRECONDITION". I could not listen to or transcribe any video.
- Video titles, dates, durations and full descriptions came from YouTube's public `youtubei/v1/browse` and `youtubei/v1/next` responses for the three official channels:
  - @BattleBears (UCwPClz0LJVpdkRjoidDZjEg)
  - @BattleBearsGame (UCXcfJ9HMyEvFdSkC8roUDDw)
  - @SkyVu (UC2uNjmvDfcXdPAOMfxQKLvA)
- I downloaded the Steam trailer and sampled it at one frame per second. For the 2026-10-18 maps corrections it was re-checked at two frames per second with scene-cut detection and full-resolution extracts, so the map timings in "In-game 3D and logo" are good to about ±0.5 s.
- I checked this on 2026-10-13/14. The maps corrections were checked on 2026-10-18 (see the fact-check log).

## Q1. What does official SkyVu material say? (animated media, art style, music, voices, fan-content stance, art sources)

### Takeaway
SkyVu's animated output comes in three groups:
- Short 2D cartoon cutscenes for the early story games (2009–2012).
- Character-card trailers and dev videos for BBR/BBG (2011–2014).
- A 2019 animated motion comic, plus 2024–2026 trailers and livestreams.

I found no evidence that a series was ever produced. [PRESS] A TV-series development deal with W!LDBRAIN was announced in Oct 2012 ([Cartoon Brew](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/biz/wldbrain-entertainment-to-extend-battle-bears-brand-71151.html)); [FAN-WIKI] the wiki says the show "never was released" ([Wiki: Battle Bears TV](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Bears_TV)) and the movie was "scrapped in December 2011" ([Wiki: SkyVu Entertainment](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/SkyVu_Pictures)). No official cancellation statement was found. Official music and voice credits exist and can be quoted exactly. SkyVu officially invites fan art and creators, through an email and Discord submission route and a Creator Program form. Its site Terms of Use are a blanket all-rights-reserved clause. No official policy on fan *films* was found.

#### B. Art style as seen in official frames

**Early cutscenes (2009–2012)**
- [OFFICIAL-FRAME] **BB Zombies cutscenes (2009–2010).** The look is soft, painterly 2D with sketchy dark outlines and simple flat backgrounds: green hill, blue sky, white clouds.
  - Oliver is a plain, unclothed grey-brown/taupe bear with a scowl. When hugged he gets spiral "dizzy" eyes and is crushed under a pink Huggable.
  - The unicorns are white with a pink mane, and lilac with a blue mane. They have panicked faces.
  - A green launcher tube with yellow/black hazard stripes. [INFERENCE] This is probably the Bearzooka (the name comes from the 2009 OST track "The Bearzooka (Stage 2)"; the frame itself carries no label).
  - Sources: [hq1](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/9LEbWq27ThQ/hq1.jpg), [hq2](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/9LEbWq27ThQ/hq2.jpg), [z01TDHzMy6g hq1](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/z01TDHzMy6g/hq1.jpg), [hq3](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/z01TDHzMy6g/hq3.jpg). The thumbnail for z01TDHzMy6g is a grinning, hyper-muscular pink "The Bearzerker" title card in multicolour lettering ([hqdefault](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/z01TDHzMy6g/hqdefault.jpg)).
- [OFFICIAL-FRAME] **BB-1 (2010) and BB Blast (videos from 2011).** Year note: the official About page lists "Battle Bears Blast (2010, iOS/Android)", while the official Blast intro (May 13, 2011) and "Battle Bears BLAST Gameplay" ("AVAILABLE NOW", Jul 1, 2011; [opaf8JXb5TA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=opaf8JXb5TA)) are 2011 uploads ([about](https://battlebears.com/pages/about)). Crisp digital 2D cel style: clean, uniform black outlines, flat fills, minimal shading, simple dot eyes, and textured or near-photographic backgrounds such as a concrete wall or a tiled bathroom.

**In-game 3D and logo**
- [OFFICIAL-FRAME] The BBG in-game 3D look (Steam screenshots and the Steam trailer) uses chunky, simple low-poly models, and effects are bright magenta, cyan and yellow. Colour is often highly saturated, but it varies by map. Each environment is a named map (identifications follow our verified maps dossiers):
  - Glossy white and red sci-fi interiors: the white-and-magenta H.I.P. Test Lab (screen_00; trailer about 34.3–36.8 s) and the red-and-white Shortest Parsec space station (screen_02, screen_04).
  - A matte amber-orange, olive and cyan spaceship interior: Space Oddity (screen_01; trailer about 30.0–32.8 s).
  - The rocky caves are the Gold Digger map, a moonlit open-pit mine with timber-shored tunnels (screen_03; trailer about 1.5–5 s, 7–10 s and 52–54 s). Its environment is low-key and muted, not highly saturated: the rock walls sample around #170E0F and the lit dirt floor #3D2B15, and the 52 s frame averages RGB(34,22,15). Saturation is limited to glowing props (gold load #DDA612–#FFD50E, blue gem #0206E7, jump-pad cyan #31BFE1) and weapon effects ([INFERENCE]: my pixel sampling).
  - There are no lava caverns. The trailer's only lava is the open-air Aztec Attack map: a temple plateau island in a lava sea, with a central lava pool, under a starry crimson-to-indigo night sky. The temple shots at about 12.25–16.4 s show the crimson sky, not lava; the lava pool and the surrounding sea fill the aerial shot at about 43.1–45.7 s.
  - The enclosed orange-red space at about 38.3–41.5 s is not lava either. It is a flesh interior with vein-lined walls and pale rib columns. Its dish-topped round tower and dark bridge rails match SkyVu's "THE MARECRAFT Coming soon..." teaser frame ([SSwlTVbW8Wg maxresdefault](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/SSwlTVbW8Wg/maxresdefault.jpg)), so it is the Marecraft map, the inside of a giant unicorn [INFERENCE].
  - **Era: this is most likely 2013 footage, not the 2026 build.** The trailer's class cards are near pixel-identical to the auto frames of SkyVu's May 30, 2013 BBG trailer (rfAyqVJrli4 hq1, hq3, hqdefault and maxresdefault match the Steam trailer at about 11.9, 42.8 and 29.9 s, greyscale 160×90 MSE 3.6–6.2 against medians of 6,490–7,476 over all 540 trailer frames at 10 fps; this is the run the verified maps overview cites in section 0, which I re-ran), and each Steam screenshot is a frame of the Steam trailer (screen_00 ≈ 36.4 s, screen_01 ≈ 30.7 s, screen_02 ≈ 50.6 s, screen_03 ≈ 9.8 s, screen_04 ≈ 26.4 s) ([OFFICIAL-FRAME]: my pixel comparison). That the Steam trailer re-edits the 2013 launch trailer, which advertised "Choose from 8 classes", the same eight the cards show, is [INFERENCE, strong]. My own 2013 matches are all class cards, but the gameplay matches too: the verified maps research found it in the 2013 trailer's YouTube storyboard (160×90, one frame a second) at a constant offset of about 4 s, 45 of the 55 storyboard frames from 5 to 59 s, including the Graham segment on Gold Digger (2013 12–13 s = Steam 7.95–8.95 s) and the Space Oddity clip (2013 35–36 s = Steam 30.85–31.95 s) (BBG maps overview, section 0, item 1; I viewed its side-by-side sheets). ## Q3. Where do games and media disagree, and what changed in the 2026 Battle Bears Gold re-release?

## Q4. What concrete details would let a writer and animator portray these characters faithfully?

### Takeaway
Use the official cutscene descriptions for comedic beats and personality, the taunts and voice lines for body language and delivery, and the BBH portraits and BBG cards for look. Recurring gags are well documented in official text:
- being "hugged to death"
- rainbow Huggable guts and souls
- unicorns
- Wil's incompetence and nubs
- Riggs's cheerful brutality
- Oliver's cocky nonchalance
- puns on "bear"
- a mock-epic, self-aware narrator voice
