# Toxic Terror

Toxic Terror is the radioactive sewer map that came to Battle Bears Gold (BBG) in update 2.3 in September 2013 [Fan-wiki]. It is a dark, two-level sewer with glowing green ooze and a large climbable tree in the middle [Fan-wiki][Observed].

**What I could reach and what I could not:**
- **Reachable:**
  - the fan wiki through its API (the page, all 26 revisions, backlinks, related pages and file metadata)
  - every image on the page, plus the map's preview tile and a concept-art compilation found by searching the wiki's File namespace
  - YouTube thumbnails and oEmbed titles (watch pages answer "Sign in to confirm you're not a bot")
  - the Steam store API, the Steam News API and the Steam soundtrack page
  - the DeviantArt page of the artist who painted the concepts and textured the level
  - the local trailer frames and Steam screenshots
- **Not reachable:**
  - web.archive.org: every CDX and snapshot request was reset at the proxy, and WebFetch refuses the domain. SkyVu's own archived 2.3 release-notes post therefore could not be found (its URL is unknown).
  - archive.org's availability API answered, but found no snapshot for the URL guesses I tried.
- I did not watch any video. Everything I describe from video comes from thumbnails I opened.

## Setting

- **Place:** a sewer under a city street at night [Fan-wiki: "cavernous sewer system"]. The official fly-through ends looking up a green-lit shaft at a brown-framed grate, with dark blue night sky and a single star beyond it [Observed: I4 hq2/hq3].
- **Theme:**
  - radioactive waste: "radioactive waste flowing through" [Fan-wiki]
  - glowing green ooze pouring from pipes and pooling under floor grates [Observed: I2, I4, I8]
  - toppled radiation drums [Observed concept: I5 panel 5; in-game, a toppled barrel near an ooze puddle: I12/I13 tile]
- **Comic squatter-lair layer:**
  - chalk tally marks and a weapon rack in ninja-turtle colours [Observed: I6, I5]
  - chalk drawings of bears [Observed: I2]
  - the concept art adds a "poopman" and a ninja-masked bunny doll [Observed concept: I5]
  - In-game presence of the poopman and the bunny doll is [Unverified].
- **Tone:** underground "lair" comedy (Ninja Turtles sewer jokes) mixed with radioactive horror [Inference].
- **Botch link:** the wiki speculates the waste "might be a hint to Botch's origins" [Fan-wiki, speculation]. No SkyVu text links Botch to this map [Observed absence across Steam, OST and news text].

## Layout

- **Overall shape:**
  - "double-floored", meaning two storeys [Fan-wiki].
  - Two team bases at opposite ends, each with an upper storey inside the spawn [Fan-wiki: Health Pack "In the corner of the upper part of the spawn area"; Plant the Bomb bombs are "inside each team's own spawnpoints, one on each of the upper storeys"].
  - A "large climbable tree" stands "in the middle of the map" [Fan-wiki].
  - The paired Health Packs, paired Shields and paired bomb sites suggest a mirror-symmetric map along the tree axis [Inference].
- **Pickups [Fan-wiki]:**
  - Bearscream Sandwich: "One to the left side of the tree from the Blue base's point of view."
  - Imperial Basket Grande: "One to the right side of the tree from the Blue base's point of view."
  - Health Pack: "Behind a brown lump on the upper stories, at the right side of each team's spawn points" (2 total).
  - Shield: "Two, each behind a small barrier next to the tree facing the two team's spawn points."
- **Plant the Bomb:** "the bombs of each team are, very unusually, placed inside each team's own spawnpoints, one on each of the upper storeys. The respective bomb tubes, meanwhile, are next to the large tree to the left side from the perspective of the bomb carriers leaving their base." [Fan-wiki]
- **No jump pads:** "Skate or Die, Sky View, and Toxic Terror are the only maps that contain no Jump Pads." [Fan-wiki: BBG Maps]
- **Spawn quirk:** "the only map where there is a possibility of a player spawning directly onto a power-up… the Health Packs in each team's base" [Fan-wiki].
- **Blue base:** "In the Blue team's base, on the ground floor, there are the weapons of the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles just beside the tally marks." [Fan-wiki] A 2013 gameplay thumbnail shows exactly this: chalk tally marks on a dark brick wall beside a rack holding two blue-hilted swords and a purple staff, under large pipes with blue-violet rim light [Observed: I6 maxresdefault].
  - Which end is the Red base is not documented [Unverified].
- **Routes and verticality:**
  - Seen in game: raised concrete ledges and walkways with railings over green-lit channels [Observed: I8 hq1, wooden railing over an ooze fall]; glowing floor grates set into the walkway [Observed: I2, I8]; the central tree with thick branches spreading under the ceiling [Observed: I7 hq1/hq3].
  - Seen in the concepts: barred arched tunnel mouths, lowered square pits with manholes, ladder shafts rising to round manholes, and railed walkways [Observed concept: I5].
  - Whether the ladders can be climbed in game is [Unverified].
  - The wiki adds that the dark space makes players "a little bit harder to find" [Fan-wiki]. A 2013 forum post adds that "sixth sense/radar make it a lot eaiser to find people" [Fan-wiki forum, fan opinion].
- **No overhead map or loading-screen layout image exists on the wiki** [Observed: File-namespace searches for "toxic", "sewer", "O+A" and "radioactive"]. The exact floor plan is unknown [Unverified].

## Look

**Overall:** a dark, damp concrete-and-brick sewer lit mostly by its own green ooze, with warm amber caged lamps and accents of red and blue-violet light [Observed: I2, I4, I6–I8, I12/I13]. The final game is darker and greener than the concepts, which have slate-blue walls and warmer light [Observed comparison I5 vs I2/I4]. The artist says the colour schemes were iterated and that the in-game version has "really awesome lighting" [Observed on DeviantArt].

## Landmarks

1. **The central climbable tree.**
   - A thick, twisted brown trunk grows up through the sewer floor, with branches spreading overhead [Fan-wiki][Observed: I7].
   - The concept shows it bursting through broken concrete from a lower level inside wooden railings, roots cracking the slabs and vines hanging, under a warm spotlight [Observed concept: I5 panel 3].
   - Pickups, Shields and bomb tubes cluster around it [Fan-wiki].
2. **The "O+A" heart carving on the tree.** A heart outline is carved into the bark. In the wiki's screenshot an "O" and a "+" are legible, and the third mark is faint [Observed: I1, BBG aiming reticle in frame].
3. **The drain-pipe pool.** A bent pipe pours glowing ooze into a grated pool beside a brick pillar with the poster, the DANGER sign and an amber lamp. This is the official preview's hero shot [Observed: I4; concept I5 panel 2].
4. **The Blue-base tally wall and weapon rack.** Five groups of chalk tally marks sit beside a rack of two blue-hilted swords and a purple staff [Observed: I6]. The concept rack also holds red-gripped sai and orange nunchucks: the four Ninja Turtle weapons [Observed concept: I5 panel 5].
5. **Glowing floor grates and ooze channels.** Lime-green light comes up through square grates set into the walkways [Observed: I2, I8].
6. **The manhole shaft to the night sky.** A green-lit shaft rises to a grate with stars beyond [Observed: I4 hq2/hq3]. The concept shows a ladder in a red-lit shaft [Observed concept: I5 panels 2 and 5].

## Hazards and interactive elements

- **Climbable tree** [Fan-wiki].
- **No jump pads** [Fan-wiki].
- **Pickups:** Bearscream Sandwich (doubles all weapon damage for 20 s and respawns every 90 s, per the wiki's general Bearscream page), Imperial Basket Grande, 2 Health Packs, 2 Shields [Fan-wiki].
- **Spawn quirk:** you can spawn on top of a Health Pack and waste it [Fan-wiki].
- **Plant the Bomb:** bombs inside the spawns on the upper storeys, bomb tubes beside the tree [Fan-wiki].
- **Ooze:** whether the radioactive waste damages players, and whether the map has kill pits, is not documented [Unverified]. The ooze seen in images is under grates or in shallow puddles [Observed], so it may be decorative [Inference].
- **Darkness** makes enemies hard to spot [Fan-wiki].

## Easter eggs and references

- **"O+A" heart carving:** the carving is in the game [Observed: I1]. The wiki reads it as Oliver + Astoria, "for in Battle Bears Fortress, Oliver states that he has a crush on Astoria" [Fan-wiki, Unverified]. In 2013 that line read "Oliver says that he loves Astoria" [Fan-wiki revision 56455]. The romance is not canon [Inference from the absence of any SkyVu text].
- **Tally marks:** chalk tally groups on the Blue-base wall [Observed: I6]. The 2013 wiki text said "scratch marks on the walls marking 22 days" [Fan-wiki, revisions 54885–56592], later shortened to "Tally marks" [Fan-wiki]. The concept shows about 22–23 strokes: three groups of five on top, then a group of five and a short group below [Observed concept: I5 panel 5; count approximate].
- **Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles weapons** beside the tally marks [Fan-wiki][Observed: I6; concept I5]. Taken together, a sewer, turtle weapons, tally marks and a masked critter read as a TMNT sewer-lair gag [Inference].
- **"Drawing of Botch":** a wiki claim [Fan-wiki]. Not found in any image I opened. The only wall drawings I saw are three chalk bears: one with a red-orange burst at its side, one dark bear with arms raised and mouth open, and one large tan bear pointing with surprise lines. None is identifiable as Botch [Observed: I2].
- **Concept-only gags** (in-game presence [Unverified]):
  - a pile of poop with two stick arms, the "poopman" a DeviantArt commenter names [Observed concept: I5 panel 4; DA comment]
    - The wiki's Health Packs sit "behind a brown lump", which may be this poopman [Inference].
  - a grey bunny doll wearing a purple ninja mask peeking from a pit; the same commenter calls it a "Teenage Mutant Ninja Bunny doll" [Observed concept: I5 panel 4]
  - a small animal skeleton [Observed concept: I5 panel 5]
  - a banana peel [Observed concept: I5 panel 4]
  - the graffiti "THE END IS NE…" [Observed concept: I5 panel 1]
  - the stencil "08B" [Observed concept: I5 panel 5]
- **Name puns:** the beta name "X Marks the Pain" plays on "X marks the spot" [Inference].
