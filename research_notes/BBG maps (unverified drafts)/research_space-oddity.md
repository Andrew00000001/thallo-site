# Space Oddity

Space Oddity is a small, mirror-symmetric battle arena inside a spaceship [Fan-wiki; Observed]. Its walls are amber-orange tinted windows full of stars, its metalwork is olive and grey-green, and cyan lights run through it [Observed]. In the middle stands a squat octagonal pillar with a glowing white-cyan top. A cyan ring light in the ceiling sits directly above it, and the Bearscream Sandwich spawns on top [Observed; Fan-wiki]. A two-level team base sits at each end [Fan-wiki].

The map launched with Battle Bears Royale as **"Spacecraft"** [Fan-wiki]. It was renamed "Space Oddity" for Battle Bears Gold in 2013 [Fan-wiki; Inference on timing]. SkyVu names it as one of the "Iconic Maps" returning on November 4, 2026 [Canon].

- **BBR modes:** the 2012 page was categorised under Team Battle and Plant the Bomb [Fan-wiki, revision 17692].

## Setting

- **A spaceship interior in space.**
  - The original name was "Spacecraft" [Fan-wiki; Canon per our notes]. The artist renders are captioned "Spaceship Level" [Observed]. The wiki's first infobox gave "Type = Indoor" and "Time of day = Unknown" [Fan-wiki, revision 16502].
  - Every official view is enclosed, with ceilings, bulkheads and walls of windows [Observed: trailer 30.0 s; screen_01; tile].
  - Through the long window walls you see small star specks on orange-tinted, finely meshed glass [Observed: trailer 30.0 s crop `t30_leftwall.jpg`].
  - Through a base's big window you see orange hexagon-mesh panels and a blazing white star [Observed: Spacecraft2.jpg].
- **Outside the arena:**
  - "With So Fly and Mini Me you can exit from the arena and see the spaceship" [Fan-wiki].
  - A fan BBR video titled "Battle Bears Royale- Glitches on Spaceship (1.3.2 update)" (RetroMonk3y BBR, PGYIJlmoo4Q, about 2012) has a thumbnail from outside the arena [Observed: hq1.jpg; fan footage]. ## Layout

- **Overall shape:** a long rectangle with chamfered ends, like a capsule, mirrored end to end and side to side [Observed: Spaceship.png]. The wiki says: "relatively small … symmetrical in every way, even with pick-ups" [Fan-wiki]. A 2012 edit called it "the smallest map yet" [Fan-wiki, revision 18800].
- **Team bases at the two short ends.** The wiki says: "Each base has two levels, the main level where the main operating systems are and the big window is … There is a basement…" [Fan-wiki].
  - **Main level:**
    - It holds consoles with twin cyan screens and huge angled windows [Observed: Spacecraft2.jpg].
    - An octagonal opening in the floor, edged with glowing yellow strips, looks down into the basement [Observed: Spacecraft2.jpg; fan thumbnail XlFSvuNEqF0 hqdefault].
    - From above, each base end shows a glowing yellow-orange window band, a dark teal recess with yellow trim, cream-tan floor panels and three round cyan-gold medallions [Observed: Spaceship.png].
    - "There are 3 Jump Pads in each base" [Fan-wiki]. So the three medallions are probably those pads [Inference].
  - **Basement:**
    - A row of cryo pods. Each has a frosted cyan window, a green LCD, a yellow triangle with a paw print and "CRYO" lettering, and a red keypad beside it [Observed: Spacecraft2.jpg crop `r2_lockers.jpg`].
    - "There is an entrance to the basement in the fronts of the bases." [Fan-wiki]
    - The basement "can be shot through with explosives to breach into the main level" [Fan-wiki]. That probably means firing up through the floor opening [Inference].
  - **Front doors:** "two doors on each front ends of the base that inside hold jump pads to go up to the next level." [Fan-wiki]
  - **Spawns:** "spawn points are located either on the upper or lower level of the base" [Fan-wiki].
  - **The big window** "can be jumped on if you have SW Boots or So Fly" [Fan-wiki]. A 2012 edit adds that Huggables could "So Fly up and get behind the giant window" [Fan-wiki, revision 18800].
- **Centre:**
  - A squat octagonal pillar of dark olive metal [Observed: trailer 30.0 s; screen_01].
    - Its face has a round black porthole, a column of stacked mustard up-chevrons and a dark hatch [Observed].
    - Its top glows white-cyan [Observed].
    - A heavy octagonal ceiling unit above it has a bright cyan ring light and a translucent cyan cone of light shining down [Observed; also in Spacecraft1.jpg].
  - The Bearscream Sandwich sits on top [Fan-wiki]. The Bearscream Sandwich page puts it "On top of the pillar surrounded by 4 jump pads." [Fan-wiki]
  - From above: a white-cyan octagon ringed by four small cyan-gold medallions at the compass points and eight brown-orange chevron shapes, inside a larger octagonal outline [Observed: Spaceship.png]. The four medallions are probably the four jump pads [Inference].
  - In the trailer a cyan-ringed floor pad sits to the right of the pillar [Observed: trailer 30.0 s].
- **Long sides:**
  - "On the left and right sides of the map are bridges leading to each base." [Fan-wiki]
  - In the trailer, the left wall has two tiers of grey-green steel catwalks with truss-grating edges and twin-screen consoles. They run in front of a wall of amber-orange tinted windows divided by angled orange bulkheads with triple slot vents, with cyan octagonal hatch pods between the tiers [Observed: trailer 30.0 s; screen_01].
  - From above, both long sides show these orange window strips with cyan lights [Observed: Spaceship.png].
  - Eight stubby rod-like protrusions stick out from the hull, four per long side [Observed: Spaceship.png]. Their function (guns, thrusters, struts) is [Unverified].
- **Corners:** "boxes stacked up (seemingly Huggable building parts) that is a great place for Engineers to set up Turrets as it overlooks the expanse which players have to cross to reach the opposing team's base." [Fan-wiki] These are the stacks of charcoal crates with pink Huggable faces [Observed: trailer; Spacecraft3.jpg].
- **Pickups:**
  - **Bearscream Sandwich:** "On the raised platform in the middle of the Spacecraft" [Fan-wiki].
  - **Imperial Basket Grande, 2:** "Under the middle area of the long decks crossing between each spawn point" [Fan-wiki].
  - **Health Pack, 2:** "By the rightmost exit of each spawn point" [Fan-wiki].
  - **Shield:** "On the other side of the Medic Pack" [Fan-wiki].
- **Plant the Bomb:**
  - "The red bomb is located on the central platform with the blue bomb." [Fan-wiki]
  - "The Bomb Tubes are located at each team's spawn point." [Fan-wiki]
- **Red vs blue:** which end is which is not documented [Unverified].
- **Do not use the 2014 wiki paragraph** about a "laser compound" and a lever-operated "garbage chute". A 2015 edit removed it because "That entire paragraph was about the 'Shortest Parsec' map, not this one." [Fan-wiki, revisions 66673 and 69711]
  - Its surviving first sentence ("a simple yellow-themed course … bases are in two levels") does match Space Oddity's look and the wiki's other layout text [Observed; Inference].

## Landmarks

1. **The central pillar.** A dark olive octagonal pillar with a glowing white-cyan top, under a cyan ring-light ceiling emitter and its cone beam [Observed]. Four jump pads surround it and the Bearscream Sandwich sits on top [Fan-wiki].
2. **The amber star-window walls.** Long walls of orange-tinted, star-specked window panes behind two tiers of grey-green catwalks, the "bridges" to each base, with warm light shafts slanting through [Observed; Fan-wiki].
3. **Huggable crate stacks.** Charcoal cargo crates with pink grinning Huggable faces, stacked in the corners [Observed]. The wiki calls them the Engineer turret nest [Fan-wiki].
4. **The base command deck.** Consoles under a giant angled window looking out at a white star, around a yellow-edged octagonal floor opening down into the cryo-pod basement [Observed: Spacecraft2.jpg; Fan-wiki].
5. **Yellow-framed octagonal doorways and windows**, with orange glowing alcoves below them [Observed]. The BBG tile is built around one [Observed].
6. **The "Death Star" hologram.** A cyan wireframe sphere with a dish, hovering over a console [Observed: Death_Star.jpg; caption "Space Oddity 'Death Star'" Fan-wiki]. Its exact location in the map is [Unverified].
