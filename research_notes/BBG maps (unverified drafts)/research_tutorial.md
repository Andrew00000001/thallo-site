# Tutorial

The Tutorial is Battle Bears Gold's single-player training level. It is not a PvP arena. It is a short, linear "virtual" course set in a black void full of falling columns of green code glyphs. It has neon-green walkways, floating circuit-panel blocks, amber instruction signs and practice targets. The course ends at a line of jump pads and a green wireframe hologram of the Pelican't drop ship.

- It came over from Battle Bears Royale. The wiki's earliest screenshot of it is dated July 21, 2012.
- It was still playable in the first 2026 playtest build (2.54.0), according to the thumbnails of a fan video published on July 9, 2026.
- SkyVu's 2026 soundtrack has a track called "Tutorial".

## Setting

- **The wiki's framing:** a virtual training arena that you finish "by approaching the virtual ship" [Fan-wiki]. The Vehicles page adds: "There is also a Pelican't in the Tutorial" [Fan-wiki].
- **What it looks like:** an endless black void. Vertical columns of small green glyphs stream down every side [Observed: all tutorial screenshots, 2012–2026]. The ground is a near-black green floor with a faint square grid [Observed].
  - The digital rain closely resembles the code rain in The Matrix [Inference]. BBG's matchmaking screen quotes The Matrix several times [Game text via wiki], so a deliberate homage is plausible [Inference]. No source says so [Unverified].
- **The glyphs** are Latin capital letters, not katakana. At least one column reads "CONSECTETUR", a word from the "Lorem ipsum" placeholder text [Observed, Tutorial_BBG_.jpg, around x≈1500–1530, y≈300–720 and x≈150, y≈330–650 at 2048×1536; blurry, so this reading is partial]. Other columns look like more Latin placeholder words [Inference].
- **No in-universe location is documented** [Unverified]. Placing it, for example, as a simulation aboard the Pelican't would be [BBG News invention].
- **The mode cards are not part of the arena:**
  - The BBR card shows a pink baby Huggable with a blue pacifier and spiky blue collar. It holds up a dark-green four-pod toy spaceship and sits on a rainbow-ringed disc beside grey toy blocks, one with a smiley face [Observed: File:Tutorial.png, File:Bbr.jpg]. The wiki calls it "a baby huggable playing with a newly opened spaceship toy" [Fan-wiki]. A September 2013 edit briefly called it a "Pelican't toy" [Fan-wiki, revision 52484, since removed].
  - The BBG card shows Oliver from behind, aiming the A Salt Rifle at glowing yellow-amber Huggable-face outlines in green code rain [Observed: File:Tutorial_2.jpg].

## Layout

- **Overall shape:** a linear path, unlike the open PvP maps [Fan-wiki]. It is enclosed by invisible "air barriers" [Fan-wiki]. Laser walls along the way stay up until you finish a task, such as "shooting the moving targets" [Fan-wiki].
- **The start**, the same view in 2012, January 2013 and June 2013 [Observed: Tutorial-gameplay-old-controls-royale-142.png, Photo_(2).png, Tutorial-button-mode.png, Tutorial-no-button-mode.png]:
  - The player stands on the dark grid floor. Directly ahead is an amber "MOVE" panel between two bent neon-green posts. A big floating 3D "MOVING" label hangs upper-left, with a yellow leader line pointing down to the panel.
  - Further off are floating labels "SHOOTING" and "MELEE/ WEAPON SWITCH" (left), "PICKUPS" and "USE SPECIAL ITEM" (right) and "OPTIONS" (high right, by a raised walkway).
- **Walkways:**
  - Glowing neon-green walkways with chevron arrows run across the middle distance at different heights [Observed].
  - Near-right arrows point left and left-side arrows point right [Observed, Tutorial_BBG_.jpg]. That suggests a path that doubles back on itself [Inference].
  - A raised walkway ramps up across the upper right [Observed].
- **Verticality:** raised walkways, hovering blocks, and a jump-pad run at the end [Observed]. How high the walkways sit relative to the floor is [Unverified].
- **Stations seen** (their order is undocumented except start and finish):
  - **Signs:** amber panels on paired green posts [Observed].
  - **Targets:**
    - Glowing yellow-amber Huggable-face outlines and small orange figures standing along a far walkway [Observed: Tutorial_BBG_.jpg].
    - An orange lattice (wireframe-mesh) bear dummy beside the WEAPON SWITCH / MELEE ATTACK sign [Observed: 2026 sd1/hq1].
  - **"TEAM COLORS" station:** two or more red wireframe-mesh bear dummies, one with a blue cap, below a TEAM COLORS label [Observed, low-res: Mqdefault_(6).jpg, a 320×180 YouTube thumbnail of unknown source uploaded May 16, 2013].
  - **Pickups area:**
    - A row of large dark translucent panels with glowing green frames under the "PICKUPS" label [Observed: Tutorial_BBG_.jpg right edge; 2026 maxresdefault].
    - The pickup "in the centre of the map" is a Bearscream Sandwich that "does not respawn after the usual 90 seconds" [Fan-wiki]. In January 2013 the wiki called it the "Bearzerker pickup" instead [Fan-wiki, revision 20563].
    - Thin red laser lines cross the floor in front of the panels [Observed: 2026 maxresdefault; Tutorial_BBG_.jpg horizon]. That these are the laser walls is [Inference].
  - **Finish:**
    - A line of round grey jump pads with glowing cyan centres, yellow dashed rims and cyan ripple rings leads toward the hologram Pelican't, which floats inside thin concentric green rings [Observed: i.ytimg.com/vi/-RSuAlfOUow/sd3.jpg].
    - The wiki mentions "the end line of jump pads" [Fan-wiki]. You finish by "approaching the virtual ship" [Fan-wiki].
- **Teams and spawns:** there are no team bases [Inference]. The HUD shows a red-versus-blue 0–0 score [Observed], and the Oliver in every capture wears a red bandana [Observed]. So the player is probably on Red [Inference].

## Hazards and interactive elements

- **No damage:** "the character's damage intake is set to 0%" [Fan-wiki]. You cannot die [Fan-wiki].
- **Laser walls** block progress until a task is done [Fan-wiki]. Red laser lines are visible [Observed].
- **Practice targets:** the "moving targets" [Fan-wiki], Huggable-face outlines, orange figures and lattice bear dummies [Observed].
- **Bearscream Sandwich** in the centre: it doubles weapon damage for 20 seconds and does not respawn here [Fan-wiki: Bearscream Sandwich and Tutorial pages]. In the pickup's own wiki photo, uploaded April 18, 2013, it hovers over a dark green grid that looks like the Tutorial floor [Observed: File:Bearscream_Sandwich.jpg]. That it was shot in the Tutorial is [Inference].
- **Jump pads** at the end [Fan-wiki; Observed].
- **Out-of-bounds history** [Fan-wiki]:
  - In BBR, players could get past the air barriers and "fall *endlessly* with no way of returning". This was later patched.
  - After Pro-Mode Jump arrived, a double jump onto a sign and then a laser wall let the disappearing wall launch players "high above the map". This was also patched.
  - As of 2014: "it is still possible to barely vault over the end line of jump pads by double jumping just before stepping on them, where the player can then walk off the end of the path."
- **Settings:** settings changed while online revert to default when you play offline [Fan-wiki].
