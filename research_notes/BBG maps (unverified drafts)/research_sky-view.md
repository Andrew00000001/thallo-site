# Sky View

Sky View is a Battle Bears Gold map set on an unfinished skyscraper. The fight takes place on one flat, open steel-frame floor at the top of the building, at dusk, high above an office-tower city. The floor is a grid of red-oxide I-beams. Between the beams are black grating panels and open holes. Pale wooden plank walkways cross the grid. Cover comes from plywood boards on grey posts and glossy blue construction tarps. A yellow tower crane stands beside the deck with its hook hanging over it. Nothing at the edges stops you from falling. SkyVu added the map at the BBG launch in May 2013, as one of "3 New Maps". The 2026 Steam text does not name it. However, it has a track on the 2026 soundtrack, and it appears in SkyVu's own "BBG Maps Ranked part 1" short (October 11, 2026).

## Setting

- **Where it is:** "It features skyscrapers and other tall buildings. The map itself is a contruction site high on top of a building." [Fan-wiki] (the wiki's spelling). SkyVu's own blurb: "high above this nondescript city… a construction site" [Canon per our notes; Unverified this session].
- **What the images show** [Observed: `img/Sky_View_4.png`]:
  - The arena is the open top floor of a red steel skeleton.
  - The skeleton rises several storeys above the flat brown roof of a lower building, whose roof edge has a pale balustrade.
  - Office towers of similar and greater height surround it.
- **Which city:** never named [Unverified]. "Nondescript" is the official joke [Canon per notes].
- **Firsts:** "the first map to be on top of a building or in any urban area" [Fan-wiki].
- **Name:** "most likely a pun on 'SkyVu'" [Fan-wiki]. Treat the pun as [Inference]. SkyVu never said so in anything I could read.

## Layout

- **Shape and size:**
  - "This is the smallest map in Battle Bears Gold so far, and its single-level design makes direct close-range combat almost unavoidable." [Fan-wiki, written October 2013]
  - The trailer overhead shows a roughly rectangular deck on one main level, with a few tilted steel-plate ramps and wooden decks raised slightly above it [Observed: trailer about 47.8–49.2 s; `frames/tr/sv_008.jpg`, `frames/tr/sv_012.jpg`].
- **Floor:**
  - Red I-beams with rows of bolt holes divide the deck into square bays.
  - Most bays are filled with black ribbed grating.
  - Some bays are open holes that look down onto lit lower floors [Observed: SV1, SV3, trailer overhead].
  - The wiki speaks of "numerous gaps" and says "there are no walls around this map" [Fan-wiki].
- **Routes:**
  - Pale wooden plank walkways, two to four boards wide, run across the beams. Some bays hold full wooden decks [Observed].
  - Grey perforated steel plates are laid as short ramps [Observed: SV1, SV2, trailer].
  - Orange square-tiled panels that look like pallets sit at some corners [Observed: `frames/tr/sv_012.jpg`]. What they are is unclear.
- **Cover:**
  - "Construction tarps, metal poles or planks are placed all around the map, just enough for a wise player to walk around safely." [Fan-wiki]
  - In the images, free-standing plywood boards are fixed to grey square posts, some as V-shaped pairs. Glossy blue tarps are stretched between posts. Together they form a staggered maze of waist-to-chest-high screens [Observed].
- **Centre:** a "large, light blue tarp in the middle of the map" divides the two halves [Fan-wiki]. The overhead shows a line of tarps running through the middle of the deck [Observed].
- **Team sides and spawns:**
  - Red and Blue spawn on opposite sides of the central tarp, very close to each other [Fan-wiki, Plant the Bomb section].
  - "some spawn points directly face the edge of the map in such a way that the smallest misstep at the beginning of the match could result in a Suicide." [Fan-wiki]
  - Which compass side or which side in the images is Red or Blue is not documented [Unverified].
- **Plant the Bomb:**
  - "the bombs are placed on opposite sides of the large, light blue tarp in the middle of the map very close to the two teams' spawnpoints… each placed on the opposing team's side of the tarp."
  - The carrier then has "to carry the bomb a short distance to the right corner of their own side." [Fan-wiki]
- **Jump pads:** none. "Skate or Die, Sky View, and Toxic Terror are the only maps that contain no Jump Pads." [Fan-wiki, BBG Maps page]. The Sky View page still says "one of only two", which was written before Toxic Terror existed.
- **Off-deck set pieces:**
  - The yellow tower crane stands beside the deck. Its jib reaches over it and a hook block hangs above the play area [Observed: SV2, SV3, SV4, `frames/oar2_upright.png`].
  - A pale, faceted round tower stands next to the crane [Observed: SV2, `frames/oar2_upleft.png`].

## Look

**Lighting and time of day** [Observed]:
- Dusk turning to night.
- The upper sky is deep navy with small scattered white stars.
- In one direction a sunset glow rises from magenta-rose to pink-peach at the horizon. Distant skyline silhouettes stand against it in a hazy gold-olive (SV1, SV4, B-1000).
- Light is low and warm. Plank tops and tower faces pick up amber and salmon highlights. Tower windows glow pale.
- Shadows are soft. Nothing has a hard sun-shadow.
- No weather or particles [Observed: none in any frame].

**Sky and backdrop:**
- "This map uses the same skybox as Gold Digger." [Fan-wiki]. An earlier edit said "the same red urban backdrop as Gold Digger's" [Fan-wiki, rev 57221, 2013-11-23].
- Gold Digger's sky in the trailer has a full moon [Observed: `weapons/visuals/trailer/t_0009.jpg`]. No moon appears in any Sky View image I opened, so a moon over Sky View is [Inference] only.
- Gold Digger's sky also samples darker than Sky View's (#00162A against #092A4D) [Observed]. This may be grading or a different part of the sky.
- A ring of tall, thin dark silhouettes lines the horizon. They read as poplar-like trees or jagged distant spires, speckled and edged with orange-red light [Observed: `frames/zoom_sv2_horizon.png`, `frames/zoom_b1000_right.png`]. Which they are is unclear.

**Materials** [Observed]:
- Riveted red-oxide I-beams with bolt-hole rows.
- Black ribbed grating.
- Pale blond plywood planks with visible grain.
- Grey perforated or riveted steel plate.
- Glossy blue plastic tarps with streaky cyan-white highlights (`frames/zoom_sv3_tarp.png`).
- Square grey metal posts.
- A yellow lattice crane with cross-bracing, black cables, a grey hook block and a counterweight (`frames/zoom_sv2_crane.png`).
- Office towers: flat dark-brown roofs with lighter rims, and warm grey-white facades in a horizontal grid of panels and glass. The glass reflects teal and sky tones.

1. **The yellow tower crane** against the navy dusk sky, its jib over the deck and a hook block dangling [Observed: SV2, SV3, SV4, icon, oar1 tile, oar2]. It is the hero element of the map icon.
2. **Glossy blue construction tarps on posts**, including the large one across the middle [Observed; Fan-wiki].
3. **The red I-beam grid with black grating and open holes**, plus pale plank walkways laid over it [Observed].
4. **A deck with no edges, high above a block of office towers.** From outside, the arena is a thin platform on red columns rising from a rooftop (SV4) [Observed].
5. **The magenta-to-peach sunset over a starry navy sky**, with a hazy distant skyline [Observed].
6. **The pale faceted round tower** beside the crane [Observed]. It is secondary.

## Hazards and interactive elements

- **Falling:**
  - "It is very easy to suicide on this map due to the numerous gaps and the fact that there are no walls around this map. If you fall into the city, you will suicide." [Fan-wiki]
  - Some spawns face the edge [Fan-wiki].
- **Exposure:** "surviving on this map without deaths is very difficult due to the teams' close proximity to each other, the susceptibility to Suicide, the bare amount of cover as well as the difficulty in securing health power-ups without exposing yourself to fire." [Fan-wiki]
  - Safe movement "is made more difficult when facing players with explosive weapons" [Fan-wiki].
- **Jump pads:** none [Fan-wiki].
- **Crane:** no source says it moves or can be used [Unverified]. Treat it as scenery.
- **Pickups** (era: 2013–14 wiki text; the wiki's pages disagree):
  - **Bearscream Sandwich.** The Sky View page says "Directly behind the blue tarp in the middle of the map, on the Blue team's side." The Bearscream page says "On a wooden plank near the middle of the map, slightly closer to the Blue team's base." [Fan-wiki]
  - **Imperial Basket Grande.** The Sky View page says "On the small ramp on the Red Team's side". The IBG page says "At the top of one of the ramps near the center", with a count of 2. [Fan-wiki; the two conflict]
  - **Health Pack.** "In front of a small vertical plank on the right side of each team's spawn point, each adjacent to a Shield power-up." The Health Pack page says "At the central edge of the map near the shield pickup", count 2. [Fan-wiki]
  - **Shield.** "Behind a small vertical plank on the right side of each team's spawn point" [Fan-wiki].
  - No 2026 pickup layout is published [Unverified].
- **Old bug:** "There used to be a glitch where would get stuck in the ramp" [Fan-wiki, added 2014-08-06].

## Prompt pack

**Establishing shot, 9:16, environment only.**
> Vertical high-angle establishing shot over the open top floor of an unfinished skyscraper at dusk. The floor is a grid of riveted red-oxide steel I-beams with rows of bolt holes. Most of the square bays between the beams are filled with black ribbed metal grating. A few bays are open holes that drop to faintly lit floors below. Pale blond plywood plank walkways, three or four boards wide, cross the grid. Short grey perforated-steel plates lean as low ramps. Waist-to-chest-high cover is scattered in a staggered maze: plywood boards fixed to square grey metal posts, some set in V shapes, and glossy blue plastic construction tarps stretched taut between posts, with streaky cyan-white highlights. A line of blue tarps runs across the middle of the deck. The deck has no railing or wall at its edges; it simply ends in a sheer drop into the city. Beside the deck a bright yellow lattice tower crane rises, its long cross-braced jib reaching over the floor with a grey hook block hanging on a cable. Next to the crane stands a pale, faceted round tower. All around are flat-roofed office towers with dark brown roofs and warm grey-white facades in a horizontal grid of panels and reflective glass, windows softly glowing. Sky: deep navy overhead with small scattered stars, fading toward one horizon into a magenta-rose band and a hazy peach-pink glow. A distant skyline in gold-olive silhouette stands against the glow, and tall thin dark silhouettes edged with orange light line the horizon. Low, warm, soft light: amber on the planks, salmon on the tower faces, no hard shadows, clear air. No characters, no text, no logos.
