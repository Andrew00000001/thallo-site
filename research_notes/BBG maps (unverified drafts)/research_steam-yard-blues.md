# Steam Yard Blues (2026 spelling: "Steamyard Blues")

Steam Yard Blues is the last original map added to Battle Bears Gold. It arrived in update 2.8 together with the King of the Windmill mode. The wiki dates 2.8 to March 4, 2014 [Fan-wiki]. SkyVu posted a 35-second fly-through preview on February 21, 2014 [Canon].

In the images I opened, the map is a steampunk airship yard at dusk. It has:
- purple airship envelopes held in brass rib cages;
- slate-blue fighter planes with shark-mouth nose art, tied down with ropes;
- dark wooden plank decks;
- jump pads set in brass housings shaped like gears;
- green tunnels;
- a pale yellow sunset glow behind mauve mountain silhouettes.

(Each element is [Observed] below; "steampunk airship yard" is my summary of them [Inference].)

In 2026 SkyVu still treats the map with affection, but mostly as a joke. The Steam page names it only as "that yearly match … most likely just for the soundtrack" [Canon]. SkyVu's own ranking short places it in the bottom "13-16" tier [Observed].

## Setting

- **The name.** "Steam Yard" plus the theme suggest a steam-age airship and aircraft yard: a place where aircraft are moored and serviced [Inference from the name and the observed props]. "Blues" reads as a music joke, and SkyVu leans into the music angle in 2026 ("most likely just for the soundtrack") [Canon quote; Inference on intent].
- **What the images show:** a yard built high in the air or on a high platform, open at the edges [Observed].
  - A distant ridge of mauve-brown mountains stands against a pale yellow horizon glow.
  - Below the horizon is a near-black maroon expanse: a plain, sea or cloud deck far below [Observed; what it is is Unverified].
- **Wiki edge notes:** "There is a ledge beneath the edge on an entire side of the map" [Fan-wiki]. This fits a map with open drop-offs [Inference].
- **Where it is in the BB world:** never stated [Unverified]. No lore text ties the map to a story location.

## Layout

Everything in this section is from the wiki unless marked otherwise. No top-down map exists in any source I reached.

- **Overall:** "There are a total of three levels in the map" [Fan-wiki]. The wiki numbers the levels by importance, not by height. Its "First level" is the deck, which it also calls "the highest part of the map" [Fan-wiki].
  - **"First level", the deck:** "A deck accessed through either one of the four jump pads on the ground" (the wiki's spelling is "accesed") [Fan-wiki].
    - The Bearscream Sandwich sits on "A lead off on the deck at the highest part of the map" [Fan-wiki]. A "lead off" is presumably a projecting ledge or plank [Inference].
    - "On the deck, you can jump on the gear-like wall and go behind it" [Fan-wiki].
  - **"Second level", the main ground:** "The largest area of the map". It holds the Imperial Basket Grande, "two jump pads leading to the first level, and one of the two Health Packs" [Fan-wiki].
    - "There is one plane on each side of the map with a jump pad into the third level each, the tunnel entrances can also be entered with the Turkish SW Boots" [Fan-wiki].
    - "There are two slopes on the edge of the map, the two Shields and the last two jump pads for going on the first level are here as well. Just behind the slopes is an entrance into the third level." [Fan-wiki]
  - **"Third level", the tunnels:** "A tunnel like structure with multiple paths, the other Health Pack is located here" [Fan-wiki]. Earlier the page says the jump pads lead "up into a tunnel like structure", so the tunnels sit above the main ground [Fan-wiki]. Read with the deck being "the highest part of the map", the order bottom to top is probably main ground, tunnels, deck [Inference].
- **Pickups** [Fan-wiki]:
  - Bearscream Sandwich: "A lead off on the deck at the highest part of the map".
  - Imperial Basket Grande: "Underneath the plane on the left side of the map".
  - Health Pack: "At the intersection in the tunnels or underneath the plane on the right side of the map".
  - Shield: "Beside the two jump pads leading to the deck".
  - The planes are therefore raised high enough to have space underneath [Inference]. The wiki's Health Pack and Imperial Basket Grande location tables do not list this map [Fan-wiki, by absence].
- **Jump pads:** at least six. That is four to the deck (two on the main ground and two by the slopes) plus one on each plane into the tunnels [count is Inference from Fan-wiki text]. The wiki's BBG Maps page lists only Skate or Die, Sky View and Toxic Terror as maps without jump pads, so it implies this map has them [Fan-wiki].
- **Symmetry:** "one plane on each side" suggests two mirrored halves [Inference]. The pickups are not mirrored: the Imperial Basket Grande is under the left plane and a Health Pack is under the right one [Fan-wiki].
- **Team bases and spawns:** undocumented. Which side is Red and which is Blue is unknown [Unverified].
- **What the official preview frames add** [Observed]:
  - The maxres/sd frame looks down on a wooden plank floor. Two slate-blue fighter planes face each other across it, one on each side, noses toward the middle. Each wing carries a jump pad (cyan centre, black-and-yellow hazard ring) in a gear-shaped housing. This matches the wiki's "one plane on each side … with a jump pad into the third level" [Observed; the match is Inference].
  - Between the planes stand two orange woven "wicker" drums with white sandbag-like tufts. Each is capped with a gold gear plate holding a cyan jump-pad disc. They may be the "two jump pads leading to the first level" [Observed; the function is Inference].
  - In the foreground, a bulky yellow-gold gear machine holds an orange cog and two more cyan pads [Observed].
  - Frame `hq2` looks out from inside a **green tunnel with an octagonal mouth** [Observed].
  - A 2014 fan gameplay thumbnail ([3vBGASwuXEE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3vBGASwuXEE) hq2) shows the same green tunnel mouth looking down onto a plane wing with its jump pad. That fits the plane-pad-to-tunnel route [Observed; fit is Inference].

## Look

**Lighting and time of day**
- Dusk or sunset [Inference from Observed].
- A pale butter-yellow horizon glow sits behind dark mauve mountain silhouettes. Everything below the horizon is near-black maroon. The upper sky in the preview frames is a murky plum haze. Grey clouds sit over a pale yellow sky in a 2014 fan capture [Observed].
- I saw no sun disc, moon or stars in any image [Observed].
- Props catch warm gold highlights, and shadows go deep brown and plum. It is a warm and dark scene, not a bright daylight map [Observed].

**Weather and particles**
- No steam, smoke, rain or snow is visible in any image I opened [Observed]. The steam in the name does not show in the art I saw.
- Small effects are visible but unidentified [Observed]:
  - thin red-white vertical glints near both plane noses (maxres frame);
  - a violet-blue vertical light beam near a plane (`hq1` and the wiki screenshot).
- What they are is unknown. They might be pickup spawn markers or exhaust [Unverified].

**Materials**
- Riveted brass and gold (gears, frames, ribs).
- Copper (columns and tubes).
- Purple fabric (airship envelopes).
- Riveted slate-blue painted metal with small white speckles (planes).
- Dark wooden planks.
- Tan rope.
- Woven wicker.
- Cream and pink canvas panels.
- Green tunnel walls.

All [Observed]. The textures are painted and toon-like, as elsewhere in BBG [Observed].

**Signature props and set dressing** [Observed]

1. **Airships.**
   - A cigar-shaped **purple blimp inside a brass rib-and-lattice cage**, ending in a long spike (`hq3`). Its lower band repeats a dark-purple stencil of a **round-eared bear head**. The head has a white ring around one eye, like a monocle, and a two-blade propeller shape above it (crops `mr_rightblimp.jpg`, `hq3_blimp.jpg`, `c_bearpanel.jpg`).
   - A second, **upright balloon** with a purple dome and gold diamond panels sits on a copper column (`hq1`).
   - A big gold-framed structure with purple window panels looms overhead in the wiki screenshot.
2. **Two shark-mouth fighter planes.**
   - Slate-blue, riveted, with a brass-framed cockpit canopy.
   - Nose art of white teeth on a dark red mouth.
   - A blue-white-red **roundel** with a yellow outer ring, and the yellow-green lettering **"BBG"** on the fuselage (wiki screenshot; maxres).
   - Tan **ropes** run from brass hex-plate anchors on the wings, tethering the planes.
   - Reading the roundel and shark mouth as WWII fighter references (RAF roundel, P-40-style nose art) is [Inference].
3. **Gear-themed jump pads.** Standard BBG octagonal pads (cyan star centre, black-and-yellow hazard rim; compare wiki `Jump_Pad.jpg`) are set into gold **gear-shaped housings**, on plane wings, on wicker drums and on a gear machine [Observed].
4. **Copper columns**, octagonal, with flared crown-like tops (wiki screenshot) [Observed]. Possibly smokestacks or masts [Inference].
5. **An orange slab with round holes** and a rope post (wiki screenshot) [Observed]. Possibly a mooring platform [Inference].
6. **Flat cream and pink canvas panels**, edged in red, printed with mauve diamonds and yellow lozenges, scattered and layered on the deck. A red-and-white striped edge piece sits by a plane nose [Observed]. They might be spare wings, kites or tarps [Unverified].
7. A gold **gear-framed porthole** with a jewelled ring [Observed, `hq1`].
8. **Green tunnels** with octagonal mouths [Observed].

## Landmarks

1. **The caged purple blimp.** It sits in brass ribs and is stencilled with propeller-and-monocle bear heads [Observed: `hq3`, maxres, 2026 short tile]. It is the clearest identifier: it is the part of the map visible in the 2026 ranking-grid tile [Observed].
2. **The twin shark-mouth fighters.** Slate-blue planes with "BBG" lettering and roundels, roped down, one on each side, with a jump pad on each wing [Observed; "one on each side" is Fan-wiki].
3. **Gear jump pads**: cyan pads in brass gear housings, including the gear-topped wicker drums in mid-deck [Observed].
4. **The green tunnel network** with octagonal mouths, the "third level" [Observed `hq2`; Fan-wiki].
5. **The high deck and its "lead off"** (the Bearscream Sandwich perch) **and the gear-like wall** you can hide behind [Fan-wiki; not clearly identified in any image I saw].
6. **The dusk backdrop**: pale yellow glow behind mauve mountains, with a dark maroon void below [Observed].

## Hazards and interactive elements

- "This map has no interactive features" [Fan-wiki].
- **Jump pads** throw players to the deck and the tunnels, and the Turkish SW Boots can reach the tunnel entrances [Fan-wiki].
- **Edges and falls:** "There is a ledge beneath the edge on an entire side of the map" [Fan-wiki]. Whether falling off kills you is not documented [Unverified].
- **King of the Windmill (2014–2018 only):**
  - The windmill landed in the middle of the map or on the deck's lead-off [Fan-wiki].
  - "Players who are in the receiving end at the landing of the Windmill, the player will be heavily damaged or even killed" [Fan-wiki, King of the Windmill page].
  - "When the windmill is about to land, chicken leg icons will fly out of the ground and into the air" [Fan-wiki].
  - A 2014 fan thumbnail from a video titled "Battle Bears Gold: 2.8 Steam Yard Blues and the new King Of The Windmill gamemode" shows a grey steel **lattice tower** on grey block feet, with a tall tan pole, standing inside a translucent **green ring** [Observed: [3vBGASwuXEE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3vBGASwuXEE) maxresdefault]. That is presumably the landed windmill and its scoring circle [Inference]. SkyVu's mode art shows the windmill as a purple-and-white pinwheel on a lattice mast, on a green hill [Observed: wiki `BBG_windmill_game_mode-300x225.png`]. That art is not set on this map.
- **Pickups:** Bearscream Sandwich, Imperial Basket Grande, 2 Health Packs and 2 Shields [Fan-wiki].
