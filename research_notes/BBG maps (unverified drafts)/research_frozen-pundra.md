# Frozen Pundra

Frozen Pundra is a Battle Bears Gold (BBG) map set in an enclosed, snow-floored ice cave. It was added in BBG update 2.7. Three things identify it: a squat dark rock "dome" wearing a thick snow cap at the centre, a raised snow-topped ring track that circles it, and a ceiling fringed with icicles. You can shoot some of the icicles down. The fan wiki page is a stub. No SkyVu prose description of the map was retrievable, so most of this dossier comes from the wiki plus images I opened myself.

## Setting
- Wiki: "a cave covered almost entirely in snow with large icicles hanging off the ceiling" [Fan-wiki].
- It is fully underground and enclosed [Observed]. Every view I opened has an ice or rock ceiling with no sky, sun or horizon (Pundra_map.png, the Ev5WvMIp1_4 thumbnails, the 2026 tile and the fan gameplay thumbnails). The wiki also calls it "quite enclosed" [Fan-wiki].
- No story or location context is documented: no planet, base or faction [Unverified]. "Pundra" reads as a pun on "tundra" [Inference].
- **Do not confuse it** with the 2.6 Christmas loading screen ("NEW CHARACTER SANCHEZ / NEW CHRISTMAS SKINS", wiki File:Sanchezhohoho_loading.jpg, uploaded 2013-12-21) [Observed]. That art is an *outdoor* snowfield under a starry purple night sky, with icy mountains, candy canes and a horde of pink Huggables. It is not Frozen Pundra.

## Layout
- Wiki description [Fan-wiki]: "A circular highway-like rock track raised above the ground by rocks circles the map, while a large rock dome occupies the centre of the map. There are also many tall mounds of snow situated in between. Overall, the map is quite enclosed, with [[Turkish SW Boots]] users often finding themselves hitting obstacles."
- **Ring track** [Observed]:
  - It is a broad, smooth, flat-topped slab of snow-covered rock with rounded edges. From a spawn view it sits about head height above the floor (1HxwjhH-Qao hq1, -_OustCemeQ hq2).
  - Chunky stone supports hold it up (Pundra_map.png, right side), and from floor level dark rock arches pass over the snow (D7yAXVPGi5E hq2).
  - In SkyVu's preview thumbnail it rises as a winding ramp between a rock wall and ice formations (Ev5WvMIp1_4 sddefault).
  - Icicles hang from its underside or edge (iy_MND-ch1s hq2).
- **Central dome** [Observed]: a squat, blocky mass of dark faceted rock under a thick, overhanging snow cap, rather like a frosted cake. At ground level it has a dark archway, and round jump pads sit under and beside the arch (-_OustCemeQ hq2). The wiki adds that the Bearscream Sandwich is "Located inside the near the roof of the rock dome directly in the centre of the map, accessible by a Jump Pad below" [Fan-wiki, quoted verbatim, typo included].
- **Pickups** [Fan-wiki]:
  - Imperial Basket Grande: "Each located on opposite sides of the map, below the track". The Imperial Basket Grande page instead says "At the top and bottom corners of the map", two of them.
  - Health Pack and Shield: "Each located on large snow-covered rocks on opposite sides of the map".
  - Paired pickups on opposite sides suggest a mirror-symmetric two-sided layout [Inference].
- **Other features** [Observed]:
  - pale floating ice ledges in front of dark rock (D7yAXVPGi5E hq3);
  - a round, shallow frozen bowl in the floor seen from high above (iy_MND-ch1s hq1);
  - snow slopes and mounds;
  - small ice stalagmite cones (kp3JRiORqx8 hq2);
  - "hourglass" columns where an icicle nearly meets a floor stalagmite (Pundra_map.png).
- **Verticality:** there are three levels: the cave floor, the track, and the dome interior near its roof, reached by jump pad [Fan-wiki + Observed]. The wiki's BBG Maps page does not list Frozen Pundra among the maps with no jump pads [Fan-wiki].
- **Spawns and team sides:** not documented [Unverified].
  - A fan frame with "YOU'RE ON TEAM RED" looks across the track at the snow-capped dome, so the red spawn faces the centre [Observed, 1HxwjhH-Qao hq1].
  - In the re-upload of SkyVu's preview, a high-angle frame shows blue-glowing figures at the left and red-armoured figures around a pale cyan disc at the right [Observed, iy_MND-ch1s hq1]. These are probably the opposing spawn groups [Inference].
  - Which compass side is red or blue is unknown.

## Look
- **Overall:** a bright, cool, candy-blue ice cave, not a dark or scary one [Observed]. The rock is low-poly and faceted with painted cracks. The snow is smooth and rounded, with soft blue streaks. The ice is translucent with sharp facets and a few glassy masses that look like frozen waterfalls (Ev5WvMIp1_4 hq3).
- **Lighting:** flat, even ambient light with no visible source and no hard cast shadows [Observed]. Floors read bright cyan. Some areas carry a lavender or pink tint on snow and rock, seen in hq1 and hq3, in -_OustCemeQ, in 1HxwjhH-Qao and across the whole 2026 tile [Observed]. That suggests a warm-pink fill colour in parts of the cave [Inference]. Time of day does not apply because the map is underground [Inference].
- **Weather and particles:** no falling snow appears in any frame I viewed [Observed]. None is documented [Unverified]. One twinkle "star" glint sits on a background rock (Ev5WvMIp1_4 hq2) [Observed].

## Landmarks
1. **Snow-capped central rock dome**: a dark faceted block under a thick, overhanging snow cap, with a ground-level archway and jump pads [Fan-wiki + Observed].
2. **Raised ring track**: a broad snow-topped slab on dark rock arches that circles the dome and rises as a ramp in places [Fan-wiki + Observed].
3. **Icicle-fringed ice ceiling**, including the big pale shootable icicles [Fan-wiki + Observed].
4. **Hourglass ice columns and glassy ice masses** at the edges [Observed].
5. **The small snowman** outside the track [Fan-wiki trivia]. A small white figure in front of the track in the 2026 tile is probably it [Observed, identity Inference].
6. **Tall snow mounds** between the dome and the track [Fan-wiki + Observed].

## Hazards and interactive elements
- **Falling icicles.** The wiki: "It is the game's third interactive map, as some large lightly-coloured icicles can drop from the ceiling and deal damage to players below if they are dealt enough damage. However, due to the fact that they can take several seconds to drop, do not deal significant damage and each have a small area of effect, using these icicles in direct combat is not a recommended strategy." [Fan-wiki]. Trivia: "Firing at the icicles causes them to fall off and disappear on contact with the floor." [Fan-wiki]. How many icicles there are and whether they respawn is [Unverified].
- **Jump pads**: at least one under the dome leading to the Bearscream Sandwich [Fan-wiki]. Hazard-striped pads are visible under and beside the dome arch [Observed].
- **Enclosure**: "Turkish SW Boots users often finding themselves hitting obstacles" [Fan-wiki].
- No slippery-ice, cold-damage or other environmental mechanic is documented [Unverified].
