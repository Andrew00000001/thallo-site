# Aztec Attack (originally "Facing Temples")

Aztec Attack is a two-temple arena. Two big stepped sandstone pyramids face each other across a cracked, burnt-orange plateau. The plateau sits like an island in a sea of lava, under a starry crimson-to-indigo night sky. A lava pool in the middle splits the field. It holds a large obsidian rock joined to the banks by stepping stones, with a ruined pavilion on each side. The map began in Battle Bears Royale (BBR) v1.3.1 (June 28, 2012) as "Facing Temples" and was renamed "Aztec Attack" when the game became Battle Bears Gold (BBG) in 2013. It appears in the official 2026 Steam trailer, and "Aztec Attack" is track 13 on the official 2026 soundtrack.

## Setting

- **Theme:** Mesoamerican-style temple ruins on a plateau ringed by a lava sea, at night [Observed]. The ruins have stepped pyramids, carved mask faces, scroll reliefs, stepped-fret border bands and gem mosaics. The wiki says this map and Unreal Tournament's Facing Worlds "have vague Mayan motifs" [Fan-wiki]. The name says "Aztec" and the art mixes generic Aztec and Maya cues [Inference].
- **Island:** the wiki calls it an "island-like field surrounded by lava" [Fan-wiki]. The trailer aerial and a 2013–14 fan thumbnail (_b0dKkg0OPQ) show the sand plateau's edges falling straight into an open lava sea that fills the background [Observed]. The lava sea is therefore original, not a 2026 addition.
- **Story location:** none documented (no planet, island name or backstory) [Unverified]. I could not read the 2013 official blurb.

## Layout

- **Shape:** a peanut or hourglass-shaped island. The long axis runs temple to temple, and the island narrows at the waist where the lava pool sits [Observed: File:Facingtemples.png top-down render; trailer aerial]. The layout is mirror-symmetric [Observed].
  - The 2012 top-down has a transparent background outside the island, so the lava sea is not drawn there [Observed].
  - It carries a "BATTLE BEARS" logo watermark. That it is an official render is [Inference]; its provenance is [Unverified].
- **Temples (one per lobe).** From the top-down render [Observed]:
  - a narrow front walkway or staircase facing the centre;
  - two side stair wings, which give a cross-shaped footprint;
  - concentric terraces;
  - a column of four cyan pad markers climbing the back terraces;
  - a square summit platform carrying a glowing square panel. This panel glows orange on the red-marked temple and pale lavender-white on the blue one.
- **Temples in-game** [Observed: IMG_0525, Maxresdefault(100).jpg, trailer t_0025]:
  - a wide, steep central staircase up to a dark doorway;
  - gem-mosaic panels flanking the door;
  - a smaller summit shrine with a pyramidal roof cap;
  - a colonnade of short square pillars with block capitals around the base.
  - The wiki's glitch notes mention "pillars at the back of the temple" [Fan-wiki].
- **Bases and spawns:**
  - "Each team's base is on the inside of each temple" [Fan-wiki].
  - The 2012 top-down marks red dots around the top temple and blue dots around the bottom one (stair ends and front walkway) [Observed]. Reading them as spawn markers is [Inference].
  - In the 2026 aerial, one temple has glowing red panels (≈#CD3823) and the other has teal gem panels [Observed]. That the red temple is the red base is [Inference].
- **Centre:**
  - An oval lava pool fills the waist [Observed].
  - In it sits a large, faceted, near-black obsidian rock carrying two cyan jump pads [Observed].
  - A line of smaller dark stepping stones, about two on each side, runs across the pool through the big rock and joins the two ruins [Observed: Maxresdefault(100).jpg; trailer t_0087].
  - Wiki: "floating platforms of rock, with a Bearscream Sandwich and two jump pads at the largest floating platform. The jump pads bring players across the lava pool to the pyramid." [Fan-wiki]
- **Ruins:** two small sandstone pavilions flank the pool to left and right. Each has [Observed: Maxresdefault(100).jpg; trailer t_0087; official Tillman Overhaul thumbnail]:
  - open colonnades of square pillars under a carved frieze;
  - a dark grey carved roof slab;
  - a cobblestone plinth with ramps;
  - a crumbled hole in one wall.
  - The wiki puts Shields "under the two structures next to the lava pool" and says the roofs are "usually only accessible by using So Fly or Metal Crush" [Fan-wiki].
- **Routes** [Observed top-down + Inference]:
  1. The left flank around the pool, past the left ruin.
  2. The right flank, past the right ruin.
  3. Four jump pads at the pool corners. In the top-down their white trajectory lines run lengthwise across the lava along each flank.
  4. The central rock, reached by stepping stones from either ruin; its pads launch players toward a pyramid [Fan-wiki].
  5. The vertical route: a jump-pad chain up the back of each temple to the summit. The summit is a sniper perch overlooking the whole island; the official Astoria Overhaul thumbnail shows exactly this view [Observed].
- **Jump-pad count:** the wiki says the map "has 12 jump pads, containing the most jump pads out of any map" [Fan-wiki: BBG Maps]. I count 14 cyan markers on the 2012 top-down [Observed]: 4 up the back of each temple, 4 at the pool corners and 2 on the central rock. The discrepancy is unresolved.
- **Pickups** (all [Fan-wiki]):
  - **Bearscream Sandwich:** "Located on the largest floating platform at the center of the lava pool."
  - **Imperial Basket Grande:** "Located on the sides of the pyramids next to a rectangular block", and elsewhere "In front of a block to the right of each temple".
  - **Health Pack:** "Located at the entrance/exit of temples facing lava pool". An orange glowing box with a red cross is visible on a temple in IMG_0525 [Observed]; that it is the Health Pack is [Inference].
  - **Shield:** "Located under the two structures next to the lava pool."
- **Scatter:** about 16 dark stone cubes with carved grimacing faces on the sand, rows of short pillar stubs, and thin dark cracks across the ground [Observed: top-down; trailer aerial].

## Hazards and interactive elements

- **Lava:** "If a player falls into the lava, it will be counted as a suicide." [Fan-wiki] Both the central pool and the outer sea are lava [Observed]. That the outer sea is also a kill zone is [Inference, likely].
- **Jump pads** launch players, and "If the user moves while in the air after being launched by the Jump Pads, it affects the landing point" [Fan-wiki: BBG Maps].
  - The pad model (generic File:Jump_Pad.jpg): an octagon with a yellow-and-black hazard rim, a cyan crystalline centre and stacked cyan octagonal hologram rings [Observed].
  - On this map the pads read as glowing cyan discs from a distance [Observed].
- **No switches, traps or moving parts** are documented for this map [Unverified].
- **Glitches** [Fan-wiki]:
  - Squeezing under the pyramids through the back pillars with Mini Me (old).
  - Entering a pyramid's sides through "the gap between the roofs at the third level, third jump pad counting from ground level".
  - "an invisible barrier on the right steps of the temples … This has not been fixed."
  - A mid-air stall near "the Jump Pad at the edge of each team's base" that lets players reach the ruin roofs.
- **Caution:** a 2013 fan thumbnail shows flames on a temple summit. The video's title is "Killdoba fire lasting forever, facing temples", so the fire is a weapon glitch, not set dressing [Inference]. Do not add braziers on the strength of that image.
