# H.I.P. Test Lab (originally "Huggable Factory")

H.I.P. Test Lab is one of the four launch maps of Battle Bears Royale (BBR). It is a bright white, two-level Huggable "factory" and lab. Each team base holds glass tubes with pink Huggables floating inside. The main hall has twin octagonal towers joined by bridges over a field of crates. Overhead conveyors carry pink Huggable dolls, and hidden high rooms hold rows of Huggables watching a monitor. SkyVu's own 2026 soundtrack still calls the map's music "Huggable Factory", and the official 2026 Steam trailer and a Steam screenshot show the map.

## Era and status

- **BBR 1.0, 2011.** Launched as "Huggable Factory", one of the four launch maps with Battlefield Airpad (later No Bear's Land), Spacecraft (later Space Oddity) and Desert Airmine [Fan-wiki] ([BBR Version 1.0](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/BBR_Version_1.0)).
  - The wiki dates BBR to "October 8th 2011" [Fan-wiki].
  - SkyVu's trailer said "AVAILABLE NOW on the App Store" on Nov 23, 2011 [Canon].
  - SkyVu's About page lists "Battle Bears Royale (2011, iOS/Android)" [Canon] ([about](https://battlebears.com/pages/about)).
- **Music in 2012.** On Mar 6, 2012, composer R Con uploaded "Battle Bears Royale: Huggable Factory Music (FULL VERSION)" [composer's YouTube upload; title via oEmbed and wiki-stored metadata]. This confirms the map and its theme existed by March 2012 [Inference].
- **Plant the Bomb, 2012.** The mode arrived in BBR 1.3: "Available May 3rd … NEW Game Mode: "Plant the bomb"" [Canon] ([wNpM1woYv7k](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wNpM1woYv7k), May 2, 2012). One thumbnail of that official trailer shows this map: white walls, black crates with pink-lit emblems, a red-glowing capsule on a white counter, and a red round object that is probably the PTB bomb [Observed: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/wNpM1woYv7k/hq1.jpg; bomb ID is Inference].
- **BBR 1.4.2, Feb 28, 2013.** The wiki's changelog lists a fix for the "So Fly exploit in Huggable Factory" [Fan-wiki] ([BBR Version 1.4.2](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/BBR_Version_1.4.2)). A July 2013 revision of the map page instead says it was fixed "after the 1.4.3 update" [Fan-wiki, rev 44784]. The two conflict.

## Setting

- **Type.** An indoor Huggable production lab and factory run by H.I.P. [Inference from name and props]. The wiki infobox (2012–13 revisions) said "Type: Indoor" and "Time of day: Unknown" [Fan-wiki].
- **Theme.** The wiki says decorations give "the impression of a true place for manufacturing Huggables, including shipping crates and conveyor belts" [Fan-wiki]. The wiki's H.I.P. page says the map suggests H.I.P. "could be involved in the creation of the Huggables" [Fan-wiki]. The franchise has never officially explained it [Inference].
- **Where it is.** Not documented [Unverified].
  - The wiki says each base has a loading bay "against the backdrop of a large ship in space" [Fan-wiki], which would put the lab in space or in orbit [Inference].
  - In Battle Bears Fortress, ABBi says "The H.I.P. is spread all over that planet!" and "I think they're manipulating the Huggables as well…" [Observed game text in fan screenshots HIP_2/HIP_3/HIP_4.jpg; Game text via wiki]. That is the lore context, not a statement about this map.
- **The H.I.P. logo** (a Vitruvian-style Huggable in a circle) appears on Space Oddity [Observed: Steam screen_02]. It was **not** seen in any H.I.P. Test Lab image I opened [Observed].

## Layout

- **Overall.** A "symmetrical factory-like stage, generally created with two levels"; "all rooms are mirrored … with the exception of the team colors and each team base" [Fan-wiki].
  - The 2012 top-down render (File:Factory.png) shows **point (180°) symmetry**, not a left-right mirror [Observed; symmetry type is Inference]. The two Huggable-tube rooms stick out at opposite corners (top-left and bottom-right), and the two octagonal towers are offset diagonally.
  - Overall the footprint is a stepped rectangle with an S/Z silhouette [Observed].
- **Team bases.** Each base is "split into two connected sections" [Fan-wiki]:
  - A tube room, where "a group of tubes, containing Huggables in an unknown fluid, is placed and connected to both floor and roof" [Fan-wiki]. The top-down shows five tube objects per room, with cables radiating and a pale blue glow [Observed].
  - "a big, empty room with a big downhill entrance" [Fan-wiki]. These are probably the large pink-violet-lit rooms at the far left and right ends of the top-down [Inference].
  - Players "spawn virtually anywhere" in the base [Fan-wiki].
  - Each base has four entrances: "two of which are positioned in the Huggable tube section and two near the big spawn room" [Fan-wiki].
- **Upper level.** "a series of bridges connecting the bases to two big towers, furthermore connected by a middle bridge. This room is the biggest in the map" [Fan-wiki]. The top-down shows two octagonal towers with radial floor medallions linked by diagonal bridges. One tower is red-trimmed (left half) and one blue-trimmed (right half) [Observed].
- **Lower level.** "a giant floor fitted with a total of 35 crates" that "stretches across most of the main room, allowing players to move underneath the bridges" [Fan-wiki]. An older revision gave each crate as "around 80x80x80 units" [Fan-wiki, removed Oct 2013]. The top-down shows dark braced crates in loose rows [Observed].
- **Connections.**
  - "In each end of the stage are placed a relatively steep incline, leading up from the main room lower floor, to the team base" [Fan-wiki].
  - "a small two-stage hill is also placed near these at each base" [Fan-wiki].
  - The wiki also mentions "the stages escalator bonds" [Fan-wiki; meaning unclear].
- **Verticality.** The official 2026 shot shows a long raised white walkway running to a blue-framed doorway, with a magenta storage loft above and a lower floor beside it. That fits the base inclines and ramps [Observed: screen_00.jpg / trailer 34.2–36.1 s; mapping to the wiki's "incline" is Inference].
- **Team sides.**
  - In the top-down, one half has red accents (red-trimmed tower, red wall triangles) and the other half blue [Observed].
  - Door frames glow in team colour: blue in the 2026 trailer and in fan footage, red in fan footage [Observed: trailer t_0070; https://i.ytimg.com/vi/1MQ9bS-Ewrs/hq1.jpg; https://i.ytimg.com/vi/1NuJT2vl1sQ/hq3.jpg].
  - Which team spawns on which compass side is [Unverified]. Presumably red spawns on the red-trimmed side [Inference].
- **Plant the Bomb.** "Both bombs are located under the main bridge next to each other. The bomb tubes are located at each base behind the huggable containers" [Fan-wiki].

## Landmarks

1. **The Huggable tube room** in each base: a row of glowing glass tubes with floating pink Huggables against a pink wall of diamond lights [Observed: map tile; Fan-wiki]. Shields spawn here [Fan-wiki], and the HUD shows "SHIELDS" in fan footage of that room [Observed: 1NuJT2vl1sQ hqdefault/hq2].
2. **Twin octagonal towers and the center bridge** over the 35-crate floor. "the Bearscream Sandwich can be found on the upper level bridge" in "the dead center of the stage" [Fan-wiki]. Towers observed in the top-down [Observed].
3. **The production line**: an overhead rail of hanging pink dolls, the robotic claw, the rainbow column and the magenta crate lofts [Observed: 2026 trailer and screen_00].
4. **Team portal doors and arrow pillars** with a jump pad at the base [Observed: screen_00].
5. **The hidden "training" room**: "around twenty Huggables are standing in rows, staring at a screen with repeating images of Rho" [Fan-wiki]; also seen in 2012 footage [Observed: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/sdX5LvCKXt0/hq1.jpg].
6. **The loading bay with a Pelican't**, inaccessible [Fan-wiki]. Not confirmed visually.

## Hazards and interactive elements

- **Jump pads.**
  - The 2012–13 wiki infobox said "Jump Pads: Yes" [Fan-wiki].
  - A 2013 trivia line, since removed, said it "has the least number of Jump Pads of any map, with only two" [Fan-wiki].
  - One jump pad, beside the blue-arrow pillar, is visible in 2026: an octagonal pad with a yellow-and-black hazard rim and a cyan star-pattern centre [Observed: screen_00; matches wiki File:Jump_Pad.jpg].
  - Where it launches to is [Unverified].
- **Pickups** [Fan-wiki]:
  - Bearscream Sandwich on the centre bridge.
  - Two Imperial Basket Grande, "Atop the crates at the center of the map" under the sandwich.
  - Health Packs: the map page says "far corners … near the inclines", while the Health Pack page says "Beside the stack of crates outside on the right side of each spawn point". Same spots described differently, or a conflict [Unverified].
  - Shields in the tube rooms.
  - BBR-era "Bonus Joules … under the bridges" (dropped from the page in Aug 2013).
- **Kill floor.** The loading bay "will cause the player to suicide if they attempt to stand on its floor" [Fan-wiki]. The old infobox said "Suicide: Yes (huggables)", meaning only reachable by Huggable glitching [Fan-wiki; reading is Inference].
- **No environmental hazards** in normal play are documented [Fan-wiki: none listed].

## Easter eggs and references

- **The Huggable "training" rooms.** "In each side of the stage are a high placed room … around twenty Huggables are standing in rows, staring at a screen with repeating images of Rho" [Fan-wiki].
  - Called the first map with Easter eggs [Fan-wiki].
  - The Rho page adds: "on a large monitor behind a purple-pink force field which can be accessed by the Huggable through otherwise inaccessible holes" [Fan-wiki].
  - In a 2013 BBG fan screenshot, the monitor shows a red-visored robotic figure with a ring over its head and blade-like arms, on a static-filled dark screen, in a taupe room with an amber floor glow [Observed: wiki File:RhoBBG.jpg].
  - 2012 BBR footage shows pink Huggables in rows, arms out, facing that screen [Observed: sdX5LvCKXt0 hq1].
  - The wiki calls the figure Rho [Fan-wiki]. It does not obviously match the dark, single-red-eye Rho art on the wiki (Hip_sim.jpg) [Observed/Inference].
- **Access.** Originally reached by Huggable with So Fly. That was patched in BBR 1.4.2 per the wiki changelog, or "after 1.4.3" per a 2013 revision. The rooms were "still workable" with Mini Me plus Turkish SW Boots [Fan-wiki]. Whether the rooms exist in 2026 is [Unverified].
- **Hidden Pelican't.** "glitches deep into the H.I.P. Test Lab stage, there is a Pelican't hidden somewhere deep within the walls" [Fan-wiki: Vehicles]. A blurry, unused wiki upload (Pelican.jpg, Jan 2013) shows a grey ship under a yellow-and-black hazard-striped frame beside white walls with pink markers. It may be this bay [Observed; location Unverified].
- **Name.** "Instrumentality Project" echoes Evangelion's "Human Instrumentality Project" [Inference; no source states it]. The wiki reads "Huggable Instrumentality" as "using Huggables as a means to an end" [Fan-wiki].
- **Rainbow motifs.** The rainbow column and Huggable-face crates echo the Huggable rainbow motif [Inference].
