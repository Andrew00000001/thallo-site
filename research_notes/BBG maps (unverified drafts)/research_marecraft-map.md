## Era and status
- **Origin.** The Marecraft Carrier is the Stage 3 boss of *Battle Bears: Zombies*: a "colossal mare (female horse, or unicorn, in this case) that holds Marecrafts… kawaii-but-deadly unicorns on magic carpets that dive-bomb towards Oliver" [Fan-wiki] (Marecraft Carrier; Machine Gun page for "Stage 3").
- **Teased** on Apr 5, 2013 [Canon] (SSwlTVbW8Wg).
- **Added** in update 1.4.3 of the app then marketed as "Battle Bears" (the fan wiki files it under Battle Bears Royale, BBR). The official description says "update 1.4.3… in April, 2013" [Canon]. The wiki dates 1.4.3 to April 15, 2013 and the re-release 1.4.3.1 to April 23, 2013 [Fan-wiki] (Updates; the BBR Version 1.4.3 page says "This update has been released, but as 1.4.3.1"). The wiki's map page says "added in Update 1.4.3 of Battle Bears Gold" because BBR was later renamed BBG [Fan-wiki].
- **Weapons in the same patch:** Dead Eye, Fairy's Dust, Static Shuffle, Señor Potato Cannon, Sole's Bane and Gernobyl [Fan-wiki transcription].
- **BBG.** BBG 1.0 is dated May 24, 2013 [Fan-wiki] (Updates). Marecraft is in the wiki's BBG stage gallery ("14 different stages") [Fan-wiki]. A BBG-style "MARECRAFT" map-select icon was uploaded Jun 15, 2013 [Observed: File:Marecraft.png, upload metadata and image]. A May 28, 2013 iPad screenshot with the BBG HUD shows the map [Observed: File:Aaaaa.jpg].
- **Modes.**
  - Team Battle (TDM): Plant the Bomb "takes place in the same stages as Team Battle" [Fan-wiki].
  - Plant the Bomb: same stages [Fan-wiki].
  - Free-For-All: added in v2.5 (Nov 27, 2013) and uses the team spawn points [Fan-wiki].
  - King of the Windmill: v2.8–2.29; the windmill "will land in a random place on the map" [Fan-wiki].
  - No source lists modes for Marecraft specifically [Inference: it would have run all of them].

## Setting
- Players "fight inside the Carrier's body" [Fan-wiki], specifically "inside the Marecraft carrier's digestive system" [Fan-wiki] (Marecraft (Map)). The BBR 1.4.3 wiki page calls the map "a resuscitation of the Marecraft Carrier from Battle Bears: Zombies" [Fan-wiki].
- **Exterior reference (BBZ boss art):** a huge cream-yellow unicorn (coat ≈ #F9D485) with a blue mane and tail (≈ #80AAF8), a spiral horn and a visible udder. A grey aircraft-carrier deck (≈ #9C9A9A) with superstructure is strapped to its back. Small unicorns on flying rugs circle it under a stormy grey sky (≈ #848484) above dark green hills [Observed: File:BBbossMarecraftcareor.png].
- The Marecrafts themselves are pale pink, mint or lilac cartoon unicorns with pastel manes, standing on ornate Persian-style flying carpets [Observed: File:Marecraft.jpg].
- **Theme:** biological horror played as a cartoon. Living flesh, veins, glands and digestive juice share space with blocky, militarised metal scaffolding [Observed]. The wiki guesses the structures "were possibly built to support the huge Carrier on top of the giant mare" [Fan-wiki, speculation]. Huggables run the Carrier in the BBZ cutscene ("Unicorn A-45 ready for landing. Get that crap off the deck Sailor!") [Fan-wiki], so Huggable-built fortifications are a reasonable reading [Inference].

## Layout
Based on the wiki's top-down overview File:Marecraft_Arena.jpg, which carries a BATTLE BEARS logo watermark; its official origin is [Unverified].
- **Overall shape:** a near-square arena, longer along the team axis, with 180° rotational symmetry. Each half is the other half turned around [Observed: overview]. A red 5×2 grid of crosshair spawn markers sits at one end and a purple one at the other [Observed]. Purple is most likely the blue team [Inference].
- **Team-coded ring entrances.** Each base has a dark octagonal ring-like structure: the one at the red end has a red rim, the one at the purple end a blue rim [Observed: overview]. The wiki describes "a ring-like object that is dark blue or black in colour" where "the entrance into the underground of the team's bases" is [Fan-wiki]. The teaser shows such a ring opening onto pink-red flesh with a pink tube running in [Observed: SSwlTVbW8Wg hq2 and maxresdefault].
- **Centre.**
  - A long dark-metal bridge with squat square posts runs between the two halves, along the team axis [Observed: overview, teaser, BBG icon]. The Bearscream Sandwich sits on the bridge [Fan-wiki].
  - A lower cross-walkway runs side to side under the bridge (the wiki's "intersection"). It holds two Shields at its ends and an Imperial Basket Grande in the middle [Fan-wiki]; the overview shows three white pickup squares on it [Observed].
  - Four rectangular pools of green "digestive juices" fill the corners of the resulting cross [Observed: overview] [Fan-wiki].
  - Two square sockets, one red and one blue, each with a dark round hole, sit on the bridge deck [Observed: overview crop; a blue square is also visible on the bridge in the fan-posted "UPDATE 1.4.3/1.4.3.1 Release Notes" image]. They may be Plant the Bomb bomb tubes or platforms [Inference; Unverified].
- **Flanks.** Dark metal decks run down both outer sides: the "metal landing pad" that connects the sides "around the outside" [Fan-wiki] [Observed: overview]. Long curved yellow-tan rib-like arcs with spiky tips line one outer wall, and orange thorn-like spikes line the other [Observed: overview].
- **Each base half** has:
  - The spawn grid [Observed].
  - A crimson, tongue- or liver-like organ mound beside the spawn [Observed].
  - A round, squat sniper tower ("battlement") with a satellite dish. "There is a sniper tower on each side" [Fan-wiki], with Health Packs "On the towers by each team's spawn point" (2 in total) [Fan-wiki]; the overview shows a white pickup square on each tower [Observed].
  - A mound of woven, wicker- or straw-like orange fibre in a corner [Observed]. It could be the "unicorn stables" the wiki mentions [Fan-wiki for the stables; Inference for the identification].
  - An orange flesh dome inside a dark castellated wall with spiky fins, in the opposite corner [Observed]. Its function is undocumented [Unverified].
- **Verticality: three levels** [Fan-wiki] [Observed].
  - (1) The underground: "Below each team's base is a big room, with surface and texture that looks like a tube or intestine, with cell like structures and veins and a jump pad at the end that brings players up to a base" [Fan-wiki]. The jump pads are hexagonal, with a cyan-glowing core and a yellow-and-black hazard rim, and they emit rising cyan rings when active [Observed: File:Aaaaa.jpg; fan thumbnail CAttz4sjbl0 maxresdefault].
  - (2) The flesh floor and the lower cross-walkway over the canals [Fan-wiki] [Observed].
  - (3) The main bridge, the battlements and the sniper towers [Fan-wiki] [Observed].
  - "If the players fall off the battlements onto the edge where the gland-like organs are, it is still possible to get out without falling into the canal, by travelling along the uneven and protruding gland" [Fan-wiki].
- **Sightlines:** the towers overlook the bridge and canals, which fits the official Astoria tip about "tall towers" [Canon per prior notes; Unverified this session] [Inference].

## Hazards and interactive elements
- **Digestive canals:** "Any player who falls into the canal will die and will be considered as a suicide" [Fan-wiki]. In Plant the Bomb, if the carrier "falls into a suicide pit, the bomb will instanly explode" [sic] [Fan-wiki, general mode rule; applying it to the canals is my Inference].
- **Gland ledges** offer a way out after a fall from the battlements [Fan-wiki].
- **Jump pads** in each underground room launch players up into their base [Fan-wiki] [Observed: pad design in File:Aaaaa.jpg].
- **Pickups** [Fan-wiki]:
  - Bearscream Sandwich: "On the main bridge at the center of the map".
  - Imperial Basket Grande: "Under the bridge connecting the two team's spawn points" (1).
  - Shields: "on opposite sides of the intersections, at the sides of the arena".
  - Health Packs: on the towers or small battlements (2).
- **Red and blue bridge sockets:** possibly Plant the Bomb objectives [Inference; Unverified].
- **"Projectile vomit"** appears in the official blurb per our prior notes. Whether it is a real hazard or a joke is unknown [Unverified].
