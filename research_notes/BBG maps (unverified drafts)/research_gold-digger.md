# Gold Digger

Gold Digger is a Battle Bears Gold map. It is a gold mine at night: a round pit open to the sky, ringed by walls of stacked rock. In the middle are two deep black shafts with a narrow stone-edged path (the "bridge") between them. Timber-framed rail tunnels lead off the pit, and wooden mine carts full of glowing gold sit around the edge. SkyVu added it at the launch of BBG in May 2013 as one of three new maps. SkyVu names it among the "Iconic Maps" coming back on November 4, 2026.

## Layout

- **Overall shape:** a round to oval bowl ringed by tall walls of stacked rock (wiki `gd2`, `gd3`; trailer at 52.1 s) [Observed].
- **Centre:**
  - Two deep shafts sit side by side, each a rounded teardrop or kidney shape. From above they form a figure-8 [Observed, `gd3` and trailer 52.0–53.5 s].
  - Flat grey slab stones edge their rims. The shaft walls are sheer, streaked olive-brown, and drop into pure black [Observed].
  - A narrow, stone-edged dirt causeway runs between the shafts [Observed]. The wiki calls it "a bridge in the middle of the map" [Fan-wiki].
  - The causeway looks roughly two bears wide [Inference, judged against character size at 52.1 s].
- **The dirt floor:** a sandy ring of floor runs between the shafts and the walls. Big rounded grey boulders sit at the shaft lips and act as cover [Observed].
- **The edge of the pit:**
  - Stone-curbed raised ledges or terraces run along the walls. Gold carts sit on stone-paved ledges [Observed, `gd2`, `gd3`, 52.1 s].
  - A pickup floats on a rock ledge up the wall, top left of the overhead shot. I could not identify which pickup it is [Observed].
- **Tunnels:**
  - Timber-framed tunnel mouths open at the foot of the walls. One wide mouth is closed off by a wooden post-and-rail fence with mine rails running behind it (52.1 s) [Observed].
  - One long straight rail tunnel has timber posts and beams and hanging dome lamps on a cable, and ends at a gold cart (`gd4`; map icon) [Observed].
- **Team bases and spawns:**
  - The wiki says the map has "team-coloured jewels to indicate which spawn point the player is in" [Fan-wiki].
  - In the images, a faceted gem glows red in the rock beside one timber tunnel mouth (`gd1`) [Observed]. A faceted blue gem sits beside another (Steam `screen_03`; trailer 7.0–10.0 s; blue glow at the fenced tunnel at 52.1 s) [Observed].
  - Health Packs are "Inside the spawn area near the central cave" [Fan-wiki, Health Pack page]. So the spawns are side chambers or tunnels off the main pit [Inference].
  - Which side is red and which is blue is not documented [Unverified]. They are probably on opposite sides [Inference].
- **Height and movement:**
  - The map is mostly one level, with deadly shafts below. Two jump pads are set in the dirt ring, one left and one right (trailer 52.5–53.0 s) [Observed].
  - The wiki says jump pads appear on every BBG map except Skate or Die, Sky View and Toxic Terror [Fan-wiki]. Where Gold Digger's pads launch you is unknown.
- **Pickups, according to the wiki:**
  - Bearscream Sandwich: "Located at the middle of the bridge."
  - Shield: "Located outside both teams' spawn, next to Imperial Basket Grande."
  - Health Pack: "Located inside both teams' spawn."
  - Imperial Basket Grande: "Located outside Red teams' and Blue teams' spawn, next to Shields."
  - The Imperial Basket Grande page adds "On a raised area near the shield pickup", 2 of them. [Fan-wiki]

## Easter eggs and references

- **Sky View in the background.** If you glitch out of the map, Sky View's city shows behind it. The wiki's Sky View page also says the two maps share a skybox. [Fan-wiki]
- **Pickup imbalance trivia.** The wiki claims "This is the only map where a team has a direct advantage over another…" because only Red had an Imperial Basket Grande [Fan-wiki]. A 2026 wiki edit says both sides now have one [Fan-wiki].
- **The name.** "Gold digger" is an idiom for someone chasing money. The blurb's "equally avaricious opponents" plays on that [Inference].
- **Nothing else documented.** No hidden graffiti, character cameos or secret rooms are recorded for this map [Unverified].
