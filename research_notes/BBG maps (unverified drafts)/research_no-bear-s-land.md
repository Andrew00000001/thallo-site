# No Bear's Land

No Bear's Land is the big, sunny, open grass map. Two olive concrete base keeps face each other across a square field: one carries red stripes and the other blue. A zig-zag network of plank-floored trenches runs between them. Two crashed Pelican't dropships lie on the other two sides, each with a long dirt furrow ploughed behind it. A small pillar at the dead centre holds the Bearscream Sandwich. The map launched in Battle Bears Royale 1.0 (October 2011) as **"Battlefield Airpad"**. It was renamed at the move to Battle Bears Gold in mid-2013 and got winter, summer and spring reskins between 2017 and 2018. SkyVu names it first among the "classics" returning in the November 4, 2026 BBG release, and it is track 2 on the 2026 soundtrack. SkyVu's own 2026 playtest-highlights thumbnail shows it, and so do fan playtest videos.

## Setting
- **Theme:** a pun on the WWI term "No Man's Land": "the large open gap between the trenches of fighting armies… Since the map itself is mostly a vast open space with trenches in between, it very much resembles No Man's Land" [Fan-wiki]. The original name "Battlefield Airpad" and the two crashed dropships suggest a forward landing field gone wrong [Inference].
- **Mixed-era dressing:** WWI-style trenches and sandbags, black X-shaped steel tank traps of the Czech-hedgehog kind (a WWII-style obstacle), and sci-fi Pelican't dropships [Observed; identifying the period of each prop is Inference].
- **In-world location:** undocumented [Unknown]. If our notes' quote of the official blurb is right, it jokes that the place was "probably really nice before you came along… quaint old folk's home" [Canon per notes; Unverified].
- **Asset name:** the 2012 renders are captioned "Fields Level model & textures" under a "BATTLE BEARS ROYALE" badge and a www.kevinsalki.com watermark [Observed]. So "Fields Level" was probably the working name, and those renders are an artist's portfolio images [Inference].
- **Infobox:** "Type: Open-air", "Time of day: Day, Sunlight", "Jump Pads: Yes", "Suicide: Yes" [Fan-wiki].

## Layout
- **Overall shape:** a square, flat-topped grass plateau rimmed by a thin yellow curb, with an angular dark pylon at each corner [Observed: File:Battlefield.png top-down; fan glitch thumbnails Pxl7w2mprMQ and fMBTcV9EVtU]. The trench network is **rotationally symmetric**: the Blue half is the Red half turned 180°, not mirrored left to right [Observed, top-down]. The wiki just says "symmetrical" [Fan-wiki].
- **Team ends** [Observed, top-down]:
  - **Red** holds the top (north) end in the wiki's top-down. Red spawn markers spread along that band, the corner pylons there show red lights, and the keep carries red stripes.
  - **Blue** holds the bottom (south) end, with blue markers, blue pylon lights and a blue-striped keep.
  - The wiki file name "Oliver-battlefield-airpad-red-base-front" matches the red-striped keep [Fan-wiki].
- **Bases** [Observed, top-down; Fan-wiki]:
  - The wiki: "two towers connected to a wall… Each team's spawnpoint is located near or behind this wall… To get on top of the towers, the players must use a Jump Pad located behind the wall" [Fan-wiki].
  - From above, each base is an **octagonal keep** with a cobblestone roof and a round grey central pad, a chunky olive parapet, and a **rectangular battlement wing** attached to one side with a mounted gun turret on its cobbled deck [Observed].
  - The jump pad sits in a trapezoid alcove on the back of the keep, facing the map edge [Observed].
  - In profile the keep's face has a recessed double door framed by two cracked pilasters, star-shaped bullet cracks, and twin team-colour bands along the top [Observed: renders; 2012 and 2026 in-game frames].
- **Centre:** "a grassy flatland with symmetric trenches scattered around the field. In the very center of the map is a small pillar, and the Bearscream Sandwich pickup is located on top of this. To reach this, players must use the Jump Pads to jump up the pillar or double-jump" [Fan-wiki]. The top-down shows the pillar at a trench junction with a jump pad on its left and right [Observed]. Note that the wiki's Plant the Bomb section calls it "the pillar with the Bearzerker pickup", which clashes with its other sections; this is probably an old or wrong name [Fan-wiki; Inference].
- **Trenches** [Observed: top-down, renders, BBR trailer thumbnail]:
  - Sunken channels with golden plank floors and yellow-edged lips, running in an S/zig-zag from each base toward the centre.
  - Some stretches are roofed with grey slatted covers; small green bushes grow along them.
  - The cut walls show layered soil: a grass cap, then an orange band, then brown, then near-black.
- **Side wrecks:** "two small Pelican'ts are located — one on each side of the field — which the players can climb on top of… On these Pelican'ts special Jump Pad is located which will launch you high over the field, making the player land right next to the opposite spacecraft" [Fan-wiki]. From above [Observed]:
  - The **west wreck has red bands** and its nose points south (toward Blue). The **east wreck has blue bands** and its nose points north (toward Red).
  - Each has a long orange-edged dirt furrow trailing behind it toward its own end, a broken-off engine pod lying beside the nose, and a jump pad on the nose.
- **Verticality** [Inference from Fan-wiki + Observed], three levels:
  - Trench floors below grade.
  - Rolling hills on the field: the keeps sit on rises, and the crash furrows climb mounds.
  - Keep roofs and Pelican't tops, reached by jump pad or wing.
  - The Pelican't super-pads give the highest arcs on the map.
  - The wiki adds that the Assault's Metal Crush plus Turkish SW Boots can "jump from one battlement on one side of No Bear's Land to another" [Fan-wiki].
- **Play pattern:** "works very well with long range classes like the Sniper and Demo. It is recommended that short range classes stay in the corners of the map or in the trenches" [Fan-wiki]. The wiki compares Skate or Die's "very wide and open area, much like No Bear's Land" [Fan-wiki].

## Look
**Lighting and sky** [Observed]
- Bright high midday sun with crisp short shadows. The wiki infobox says "Day, Sunlight" [Fan-wiki].
- In-game skies are saturated mid-blue, fading paler toward the horizon, with a few soft white cumulus clouds (BRVP0IZEw-k 2013, C7uFxvSZ10c 2026).
- The 2012 "Fields Level" renders are hazier: sky-blue fading to near-white at the horizon, with a large sun bloom in the upper right.
- The horizon shows faint misty hills in the 2012 BBR screenshot. They are clearer in the 2026 frames, where blue-grey tree-covered hills sit beyond the far keep [Observed, low-res].
- Overhead glitch views (2013) show nothing but pale void beyond the yellow curb [Observed].

**Materials and props** [Observed]
- Ground: yellow-green turf flecked with tiny white daisies.
- Keeps: olive-drab weathered concrete with team-colour stripes.
- Trenches: golden plank floors with layered soil walls; tan sandbags laid in curved arcs and C-shapes.
- Tank traps: black steel X-shaped hedgehogs in clusters of two or three.
- Wrecks: dark olive-grey/gunmetal Pelican'ts, each with four fat cylindrical engine pods on stub wings and team-colour bands.
- Smaller dressing: blue barrels along the crash furrows; small grass-topped wooden crates.
- Corner pylons: angular dark pylons with small red or blue lights.
- **Food stands:** a red-and-yellow canopy over a small grey wheeled cart with a dark round vent. Its round emblem shows a black-and-white cartoon figure holding a chicken drumstick (BRVP0IZEw-k thumbnail, Airpad3 render, 2026 frames). The wiki calls it "the food stand" that the Imperial Basket Grande sits behind [Fan-wiki].
- **Jump pads:** octagonal, with yellow-black hazard rims, a glowing cyan snowflake-like centre and cyan holographic rings [Observed: File:Jump_Pad.jpg, a generic pad].
- **Weather and particles:** none documented for the base map [Unknown].

## Landmarks
1. **The two base keeps:** octagonal olive concrete keeps joined to a gun battlement. One has red stripes, the other blue. Each has a cracked double-door face, a cobbled roof with a round pad, and a jump pad at the back [Observed; Fan-wiki].
2. **The trench zig-zag:** golden plank floors, yellow-edged lips and striped soil walls cut into bright turf, with sandbag arcs at the rim [Observed; Fan-wiki].
3. **Two crashed Pelican'ts:** four-engine dropships, one banded red and one banded blue, lying in opposite directions. Each has a long ploughed dirt furrow behind it and a snapped-off engine pod, plus a health pack and a super jump pad on top [Observed; Fan-wiki].
4. **The centre pillar:** a small block at the trench junction, flanked by two jump pads, holding the Bearscream Sandwich (an angry brown bear-face ice-cream sandwich) [Observed: top-down, File:Bearscream_Sandwich.jpg; Fan-wiki].
5. **The food stands:** a red-and-yellow canopy over a grey cart with a drumstick-mascot emblem, beside each base [Observed; Fan-wiki "food stand"].
6. **Battlefield clutter:** black X-shaped tank traps, daisies in the grass, dark corner pylons with team lights [Observed].

## Hazards and interactive elements
- **Jump pads**, three kinds [Fan-wiki; Observed positions in the top-down]:
  1. At the back of each keep, up to the roof.
  2. Two beside the centre pillar.
  3. On each Pelican't, launching the player "at an extremely high altitude" to land beside the opposite wreck.
- **Pickups** [Fan-wiki]:
  - **Bearscream Sandwich ×1** on the centre pillar ("respawns every 90 seconds" per its own page).
  - **Imperial Basket Grande ×2** "on the left side of each base tower behind a shielding parasol" / "Behind the food stand at the left corner of each spawn point". In the top-down the parasols sit at the north-east and south-west, which is each team's left as it faces the centre [Observed; Inference].
  - **Health Pack ×2** on top of each Pelican't ("use the port wing… to climb"). It takes 45 seconds to respawn.
  - **Shield ×2** on top of each keep.
- **Plant the Bomb:** "two bomb tubes and two bomb stands. The bombs will both be placed in the middle of the trenches near the pillar… while the bomb tubes will be positioned on top of the each of the base towers" [Fan-wiki].
- **Infobox "Suicide: Yes":** the wiki does not explain it [Fan-wiki]. It might mean falling or launching out of bounds over the curb [Inference, Unverified].
- **Mounted guns** on the battlement wings, and the corner pylons: no record says they are usable. Treat them as set dressing [Inference].
- **Open sightlines:** long lines of fire favour Snipers and Demos; the trenches and corners are the cover [Fan-wiki].
- **Glitches:** the BBR 1.4.2 Mini Me glitch fix [Fan-wiki]. Fan video titles say "No bears land has too many glitches" (2013) and, in Turkish, ask how to get inside the tower ("Kuleye Nasıl Girilir?") [Unverified: titles only].

## Easter eggs and references
- **The name** puns on WWI's "No Man's Land" [Fan-wiki].
- **The Pelican't** is described as "a pun from the ship Pelican in the Halo series", with a design "based of the UD-4 'Cheyenne' from the Aliens 1986 movie" [Fan-wiki: Vehicles]. Two of them lie wrecked here [Fan-wiki; Observed].
- **"War. What is it good for?"** If our notes' quote of the official blurb is accurate, this echoes Edwin Starr's song "War" [Canon per notes, Unverified; the song link is Inference].
- **Keep stencil** "XEL-PMOC 20-11", which reads "11-02 COMP-LEX" backwards [Observed]. Its meaning is unknown [Unknown].
- **A cartoon fossil bone** is embedded in the trench-wall soil in the 2012 render Battlefield_Airpad2 [Observed]. Whether it is in-game is unconfirmed [Unverified].
- **The food-stand mascot** holds a chicken drumstick. It sits next to the Imperial Basket Grande pickup, which is a red basket of fried food [Observed]. The pairing looks deliberate [Inference].
- **Christmas tree on the centre pillar** in the winter reskin [Fan-wiki; Observed].
