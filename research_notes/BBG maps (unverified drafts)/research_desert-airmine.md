# Desert Airmine

Desert Airmine is a desert map from the Battle Bears Royale era. Its islands float above a red-rock canyon. A central oil drill with Graham's face on it rises and drops, a windmill tower overlooks the whole map, and the Blue team spawns at a helipad. The fan wiki says the map shipped with BBR 1.0 (Oct 8, 2011), was cut when the game became Battle Bears Gold (v2.0, about May 2013), and returned in BBG v2.7 (Jan 23, 2014). SkyVu's 2026 Steam soundtrack lists a "Desert Airmine" track. A fan's 2026 playtest video lists a "Desert Airmine FFA" chapter. No SkyVu-written description of the map was reachable this session.

## Era and status
- **Added:** the wiki says the map was "released in the initial 1.0 release together with the No Bear's Land, Space Oddity and H.I.P. Test Lab" [Fan-wiki]. The [BBR Version 1.0](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/BBR_Version_1.0) page lists it under 1.0 maps as "Desert Airmine (since removed)" [Fan-wiki]. Its release date, "October 8th 2011", comes from the official update descriptions the wiki quotes [Game text via wiki]. The earliest wiki image of the map was uploaded 2012-07-21 [Fan-wiki metadata]. The earliest fan gameplay upload I saw is dated "14y ago", about 2012 [Unverified].
- **Drill change, BBR 1.4.3 (Apr 15, 2013):** "the movement of the drill was made much more erratic than the simple up-and-down movement before" [Fan-wiki]. The official 1.4.3 notes do not mention it [Game text via wiki].
- **Removed in "update 2.0":** the reason given is "not only because they were the least popular maps, but to also allow more server space" [Fan-wiki]. The Haunted Castle page adds that the maps lost "a poll on the Battle Bears website" [Fan-wiki, Unverified]. BBR's v2.0 became the separate app Battle Bears Gold, whose 1.0 shipped May 24, 2013 [Fan-wiki; Game text via wiki]. So the removal dates to about May 2013 [Inference]. The page was changed to "was a stage… since this map is no more" on 2013-12-12 [Fan-wiki history].
- **Returned in BBG v2.7 (Jan 23, 2014):** "It was re-released in v2.7" was added to the wiki on 2014-01-24 [Fan-wiki]. The BBG-style map icon was uploaded 2014-01-29 [Fan-wiki metadata]. Fan video titles agree: "Battle Bears Gold 2.7 Desert Airmine returns!" and "Battle Bears Gold Update 2.7 - Desert Airmine Map Returns!" [Unverified: fan titles, not watched].
- **Later years:** a fan video titled "Desert airmine ffa (battle bears)", about 2021, shows the map still in play [Unverified]. BBG 3.0 (Nov 15, 2021) lists "New Map Lighting" across the game [Game text via wiki]. Whether that changed this map's look is unknown [Inference].
- **Modes:**
  - Team Battle/TDM [Fan-wiki: general].
  - Plant the Bomb: the map page has a section for it [Fan-wiki].
  - Free-for-All: fan titles from about 2021 and 2026 [Unverified].
  - King of the Windmill (BBG 2.8–2.29): not documented for this map [Unverified].
- **2026 status:** probably in the game [Inference].
  - **Evidence for:** the 2026 OST has a Desert Airmine track [Canon]. The YouTube search snippet for JustNubz's "Battle Bears Gold Playtest #3 Part 2" (about Sep 2026) lists chapters "1:08 Marecraft TDM … 19:59 Desert Airmine FFA" [Unverified: fan description text, video not watched].
  - **Evidence against or missing:** the map is not shown in the 54 s Steam trailer or the 5 Steam screenshots, and it is not named in the store copy [Observed/Canon].
  - **"16 wacky maps":** the OST has 16 map-named tracks including Desert Airmine. But it also has "Castle Royale" and no Sky World track, so that list cannot be equated with the 16 maps [Inference].
- **BB Heroes (2024):** no evidence the map appears [Unverified].

## Setting
- **Wiki descriptions:**
  - "Taking place in desert-like surroundings"
  - "a massive-looking mine, featuring a large oil drill in the very center of the map"
  - "several levels of floating desert islands" above "the big gaping canyon"
  - Old infobox: "Type: Open-air", "Time of day: Day, Sunlight"

  [Fan-wiki] ([page](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Desert_Airmine); infobox from revision 16497, 2012).
- **What the images show:** a deep, stepped red-rock canyon with flat-topped buttes and mesas, close to the American Southwest [Observed + Inference]. The islands are sand-topped mesas floating in mid-air, held up by or hung with steel lattice towers, cables and girders [Observed].
- **Story:** nothing in the lore says who runs the mine. The wiki thinks Graham built it "due to the presence of a small toilet and three tool sheds" [Fan-wiki, speculation]. The name probably puns on "air" (floating) + "mine" [Inference].

## Layout
- **Overall shape:** asymmetrical, in three main height tiers of floating islands. "The very first asymmetrical stage in the game. It is built in three general levels, all split up on various smaller and bigger floating islands" [Fan-wiki]. The same page later calls it "the second asymmetrical stage", so the wiki contradicts itself. The Abusement Park page calls that map "asymmetrical, like Desert Airmine" [Fan-wiki].
- **Overhead plan** (wiki File:Desertmine.png, a 2012 top-down shot on a flat green backing, probably an editing backdrop [Inference]): about seven sand islands packed into a rough square [Observed].
  - Grey metal truss bridges with X-bracing link them [Observed].
  - The drill tower is the central spine, with a red-rimmed radial head [Observed].
  - Eight red spawn crosshairs sit on the two upper islands. One holds a corrugated-roof shed with a blue launch pad, the other cacti and a bladed rotor over a box structure [Observed]. The rotor is plausibly the windmill seen from above [Inference].
  - Blue crosshairs sit on the lower-left island around a large round helipad marked with a red "H", and on the lower-middle island [Observed].
  - Round teal discs are jump pads, with thin white trajectory lines [Observed; reading Inference].
- **Level 1, the lowest (Red base):** "three large islands" [Fan-wiki].
  - The first has "the spawn points for all red teams players as well as a small hut which also holds a small Jump Pad".
  - The second has "a cactus" and a jump pad up to level 2.
  - The third sits "beneath the second level" with another jump pad up.

  [Fan-wiki]
- **Level 2, the largest (Blue base):** "one big, though weirdly shaped island as well as a smaller island, accessible by a bridge". The Blue spawn is "most recognizable by the big helicopter landing pad". From here players can drop to any level or jump-pad up [Fan-wiki]. The bases are "very close to each other, only separated by a Jump Pad and a high level-difference" [Fan-wiki].
- **Level 3, the top:** "2 smaller islands only connected by a very long jump using a Jump Pad which involves crossing the oil drill". The second island gives access to "a small windmill with a small platform", a sniper perch with "overview of almost the entire stage". From it, "the Blue team even has the ability to watch the entire Red team's base" [Fan-wiki].
- **Hidden fourth island:** "When the oil drill is up, players can access a small hole in the floor" in the Blue base. It drops to a small island with the Bearscream Sandwich. "The jump pad there will launch you up and to the Red Team's base". An Engineer mine on that pad can trap you [Fan-wiki].
- **Routes and shortcuts:** jump pads are the main way to move between islands. The shortcuts include "a plank across the big gaping canyon". There are "several small gaps in between the islands that can be jumped over" with SW Boots or Jump [Fan-wiki].
- **Pickups:**
  - Bearscream Sandwich on the hidden island.
  - Imperial Basket Grande on the windmill platform ("Atop the windmill tower").
  - Health Pack "on a small plank crossing the wide-open gap". The Health Pack page instead says "On the edge of the bunker at the red team's spawn point" and "In the middle of the narrow ledge around the center of the map".
  - 2 Shields: one on the third bottom-level island, one on level 2 "accessible by a tiny Jump Pad on a small container".

  [Fan-wiki]
- **Plant the Bomb:** "The red bomb is located on the second level beneath the windmill, while the blue bomb is positioned on the third level, close to the windmill Jump Pad." "The Bomb Inserters are located at each enemy team spawn point" [Fan-wiki].

**Lighting and time of day**
- **Renders:** a low sun on frame-left with strong bloom, a hazy aqua-white sky, warm rim light on the rock, and very dark island undersides. They read as late afternoon [Observed; time-of-day reading Inference].
- **In-game:** flat, bright daylight, a powder-blue sky with soft haze, and much more saturated yellows and greens [Observed]. The wiki infobox says "Day, Sunlight" [Fan-wiki]. The 2026 build's lighting is unobserved; the Oct 14, 2026 Steam post lists "Lighting improvements" [Canon].

**Materials**
- Smooth sand tops over striated red-rock cliff faces that taper underneath like upside-down mesas [Observed].
- Riveted steel lattice towers and girders, corrugated metal roofs, chain-link fencing, wooden planks and beams [Observed].
- A rope-and-plank bridge with rope rails in an about-2021 fan thumbnail; grey truss bridges in the 2012 overhead [Observed]. Whether both kinds exist or one replaced the other is open.

**Signature props and set dressing**
- Saguaro cacti, two in the renders and in-game wearing sombreros (yellow with a red band in-game) [Observed].
- Grey or white barrels and black-and-white crates printed with a black bear-face icon [Observed].
- A corrugated work shed with a chain-link front, loose tyres and a green-screen console [Observed].
- A blue portable toilet booth next to a cactus in a BBR-era fan thumbnail [Observed]. It matches the wiki's "small toilet" [Fan-wiki].
- A satellite dish and antenna mast on a lattice tower [Observed].
- Power cables slung between towers [Observed].
- Small rock discs on poles, and loose rock chunks floating in the distant sky [Observed].
- Round metal-grate launch pads; in-game the pads glow cyan [Observed].

**Weather and particles:** none documented. The renders show only sun bloom and atmospheric haze [Observed].

## Landmarks
1. **The Graham drill tower (centre):**
   - A tall steel machine with an octagonal head [Observed]. Its paddle-like arms are slatted panels with blue-lit slats in the renders, and the head looks tan or cream in game [Observed].
   - A long drill rod plunges into the canyon [Observed].
   - A round yellow emblem of a bear in a yellow hard hat, teal goggles and a white handlebar moustache sits on the head [Observed]. This is Graham's look in SkyVu's 2011 "BATTLE BEARS ROYALE Sneak Peek" key art [Observed]. The wiki says "Graham's face is drawn onto the oil drill's sides" [Fan-wiki].
   - A BBR fan thumbnail shows a large funnel-shaped drill head with a studded underside hanging over a square shaft in the sand [Observed]. This is probably the "up" position that opens the hole [Inference].
2. **The windmill tower ("the tower"):** a steel lattice tower topped by a farm-style multi-bladed windmill rotor, with a cable running off it [Observed]. A small platform is the sniper perch, with the Imperial Basket Grande [Fan-wiki].
3. **The Blue helipad:** a large round pad with concentric rings and a red "H" [Observed, overhead]; it is the "big helicopter landing pad" [Fan-wiki].
4. **Floating mesa islands over the canyon:** sand-topped, red-cliffed slabs hanging over a drop, with steep canyon walls and buttes all round [Observed].
5. **Sombrero cacti:** saguaros wearing straw sombreros [Observed]. No source documents them.
6. **The plank and the Red-base shed:** the wiki's "plank across the big gaping canyon" [Fan-wiki]. Long wooden beams and planks span the gaps in fan thumbnails [Observed]. The Red-base hut carries a jump pad [Fan-wiki].

## Hazards and interactive elements
- **Falling off:** this is a suicide. "This stage was dreaded due to the many places to commit suicide. Falling off the stage or hitting the drill is very likely to happen" [Fan-wiki].
- **The drill:** "If the player hits the oil drill in mid-air, it will count as a suicide. To safely cross this, the player must use the Jump Pads while the drill is down" [Fan-wiki]. After BBR 1.4.3 its movement was "much more erratic" [Fan-wiki]. The Graham page calls it "the spinning drill" [Fan-wiki]; the map page describes an up-and-down movement. The exact motion is unresolved.
- **The windmill rotor:** "Hitting the windmill itself will kill any player, counting it as a suicide" [Fan-wiki].
- **Jump pads:** they are the main way around, and good spots for Engineer Proximity Mines, especially the only exit from the bottom island and the hidden island's pad [Fan-wiki].
- **Hole under the Blue base:** a safe drop to the hidden power-up island, open "when the oil drill is up" [Fan-wiki].
- **Fighting tips from the wiki:** fire Demo's Happy Hour up at island undersides from below, and use Sixth Sense Goggles [Fan-wiki].

## Easter eggs and references
- **Graham emblem on the drill:**
  - The wiki calls it "the first clue of the addition of Graham" and "his first appearance in Battle Bears" [Fan-wiki].
  - That claim is too strong. SkyVu showed Graham's ortho on its blog in Sept 2011 [Canon per our prior notes, Unverified this session]. He is also in the key art on the thumbnail of SkyVu's 2011 "BATTLE BEARS ROYALE Sneak Peek" (IucMFqzR_Qw) [Observed]. At most the drill is his first in-game appearance [Inference].
  - The Engineer class itself arrived in BBR 1.4 (Nov 28, 2012) [Game text via wiki].
- **"A small toilet and three tool sheds":** the wiki reads these as Graham's workplace [Fan-wiki]. I saw one blue portable toilet in a fan thumbnail [Observed]. I did not count the sheds.
- **Bear-face barrels and crates, and the sombrero cacti:** visible [Observed], meaning undocumented.
- **Fan culture:** several 2013 fan Minecraft recreations, for example "BBR DESERT AIRMINE MINECRAFT PE TRAILER". They show fan attachment to the map, but they are not reference for its look [Unverified].

## Sound and music
- **2026 OST:** track 12, "Desert Airmine", 1:13 [Canon]. The album credits "Rob Ruiz, AnDrue Humphreys, and Benjamin Vavak" for the original score collectively; no per-track credit is given [Canon].
