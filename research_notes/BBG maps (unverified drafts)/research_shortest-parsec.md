# Shortest Parsec

Shortest Parsec is a small space station floating in a starfield, with almost no walls. A tall tower with red panels stands between the red and blue spawns. On a timer, it fires an orange cone of light onto a pad below. A white-and-red central module holds a floor that a switch on the far tower can move. Teal asteroids with hexagonal holes sit against the station and float near it. The map came out in Battle Bears update 1.4.2 on Feb 28, 2013, the update that renamed Battle Bears Royale (BBR) to plain "Battle Bears". It carried over into Battle Bears Gold (BBG). The official 2026 Steam trailer shows it, and so do two of the five 2026 Steam screenshots. The 2026 soundtrack lists "Shortest Parsec" as track 9.

## Setting

- **A space station.** SkyVu's teaser calls it a "Space Station map" [Game text via wiki]. The wiki says "The stage is set on a space station with lots of details" [Fan-wiki].
- **Its purpose, per the official blurb:** a "private arms dealer" uses it "to transfer power to its facilities based on the surface of a mysterious planet on the fringes of the known galaxy". The blurb also says a "robotic someone I've seen before exploded a few miles away and peppered it with its body parts" [Canon per notes; Unverified this session].
  - That makes the big orange beam plausibly the power-transfer beam [Inference].
  - No planet is visible in any image I opened. The backdrop is only a starfield, a violet band and a magenta nebula [Observed].
- **H.I.P. connection.**
  - The wiki's H.I.P. page says "the Shortest Parsec map has their symbol on a large tower at one end of the stage" [Fan-wiki].
  - The Assault page says B-1000's and Rho's chest symbol "also appears on the Shortest Parsec stage" [Fan-wiki].
  - I saw the H.I.P. emblem in several places on the map [Observed]: a bear figure whose arms form looping orbits, above a circle holding a crosshair and a "P"-like glyph. It matches File:Hip_sim.jpg.
  - Official copy never names H.I.P. for this map [Inference].
- **Jokes in the name:**
  - The title riffs on the Star Wars "Kessel Run in less than twelve parsecs" line. A parsec is a distance, not a time [Inference]. The wiki only explains the unit [Fan-wiki].
  - The tagline inverts the *Alien* poster line "In space no one can hear you scream" [Inference].

## Layout

**Overall shape** [Observed: overhead File:Shortest_Parsec.jpg; trailer 24.5 s and 25.5 s].
- The station is compact and runs along one spine.
- **Laser tower:** a block at one end with two white lights on its edge, joined to the central module by two dark legs.
- **Team arms:** two L-shaped decks flank the laser tower. With the laser end at the top, the left arm has red dashed edge lights and red crosshair-circle floor decals, and the right arm has the same in blue. Each arm has a boxy equipment pod at its outer top corner and a jump pad near its inner bottom corner. Each joins the central module through thick pipes.
- **Central module:** a big white block. Inside is a long hall with red walls and white tiled ends, each end carrying a jump pad.
- **Stem walkway:** a narrow walkway carrying a large red H.I.P. floor medallion runs from the central module to the far tower.
- **Far tower:** the switch tower or "meteor tower". It has grey decks with jump pads at the corners, a cracked red floor with a lime-green console, a black cable and a red-and-white striped pole. A large teal asteroid is lodged against it.
- A second teal asteroid floats off the red arm, and a small one floats below the laser end.

**Spawns and team sides.**
- The orange beam is "parting the two teams spawn points" [Fan-wiki: 1.4.2 article]. The switch is "on the tower at the far end of the stage" [Fan-wiki].
- The pickups list puts the Imperial Basket Grande "On the tower across from the blue and red team bases" [Fan-wiki].
- So both spawns sit at the laser end, one on each side of the beam, and the switch tower is the far end of the map [Inference].
- The red side is on the left and the blue side on the right when you face the laser tower from the far end [Observed: overhead lighting; trailer 26.25 s and screen_04, where the hall has a red-lit door on the left and a purple-blue-lit door on the right]. That these lit arms are the team bases fits the wiki's "meteorite at the Red team's base", since the asteroid sits by the red arm [Fan-wiki; Inference].

**Verticality.**
- The map has several levels: the upper decks, the hall inside the central module, and a lower floor.
- The wiki places the Health Pack "By the pipeline on the lower floor". It puts the bombs on "the bottommost platform right next to each other, underneath the tower across from the spawn points" [Fan-wiki].
- File:New_Map_4.jpg shows that kind of underside level: a dark navy floor under purple-lit circular ceiling lights, several jump pads, red rails and a white pillar, open to the stars [Observed]. That it is the bomb level is [Inference].
- Jump pads link the levels [Observed]. The wiki says an Assault using Metal Crush with Turkish SW Boots and a jump can "jump from the tower on Shortest Parsec to either team's spawn points" [Fan-wiki].

**The "middle dome."**
- The wiki's "central dome" or "middle dome" is the central module [Inference].
- In the images it looks boxy, not domed [Observed]. Its hall is about four bears wide and has a long open slot in the roof. Through the slot you can see the laser tower from inside [Observed: screen_04; trailer 26.25 s].
- At the far end, a lit ramp leads out to the medallion walkway [Observed: File:New_map_2.jpg]. That the ramp faces the switch end is [Inference].

**Pickups** [Fan-wiki]:
- Bearscream Sandwich: "Below the huge laser or inside the central dome".
  - The Bearscream page says one is "Guarded by a laser in between the two team's spawn points, and another in the room near the spawn point".
  - The Skate or Die page says this is one of two maps with two Sandwiches.
- Imperial Basket Grande: "On the tower across from the blue and red team bases", or "At the far corner of the meteor tower". There is one.
- Health Pack: "Underneath tower across from bases, on Red team's side", or "By the pipeline on the lower floor". There is one.
- Shield: "Underneath tower across from bases, on the Blue Team's side and across from the Bearscream Sandwich inside the central dome".

## Look

**Overall.** The station is built from clean, toy-like hard-surface blocks. The hulls are off-white to lavender, with engraved circuit-like panel lines. Glossy red inlay panels with black seams cover the laser tower, and the central hall's walls are red. Ribbed black pipes with dark-red couplings join the blocks. Antenna spikes with red tips sit on the tower tops, and small spikes and nubs hang under the platforms [Observed: New_map_1, New_map_3, EE1, screen_02].

**Sky.** There is no sun, planet or ground [Observed].
- Mostly near-black space with dense white star flecks and a broad indigo-violet galactic band [Observed: screen_02; trailer 24.0–25.75 s and 49.75–51.25 s; EE1–EE3].
- Behind the laser tower's windows the backdrop turns a warm violet-magenta nebula [Observed: Scary_Light.png; trailer 28.0 s].
- So the skybox probably has a nebula on one side [Inference].

**Lighting.**
- Cool lavender-white light on the hulls, with soft bloom on light bars.
- Team-coloured lights on each arm: red dashed strips on one, blue on the other.
- Mint-white light bars on the laser tower.
- A warm orange spill from the beam.
- Inside the hall, saturated red with a red-lit door on one side and a purple-lit door on the other.
- The lower level is purple-lit [Observed: screen_04, New_Map_4, overhead].
- The Jan 2013 previews look cooler and lilac-tinted. The 2026 trailer looks more saturated red [Observed; reason unknown].
- There is no time of day, since the map is in space [Observed].

**Particles and weather.** The beam is full of drifting sparkles [Observed: Scary_Light]. There is no weather [Observed].

**Signature props** [Observed]:
- Octagonal jump pads with a black rim, yellow-and-black hazard stripes and a glowing cyan core that throws up stacked holographic cyan rings (New_map_2, New_Map_4).
- A lime-green console with screens showing bar graphs, a smiley face and a keypad, next to a red down-arrow plate (New_map_3, EE3).
- Red-and-white striped poles and a black cable on the far tower (overhead, EE3).
- Red angled consoles or benches in the hall (New_map_2).

## Landmarks

1. **The laser tower and its beam** [Observed: Scary_Light, EE1, EE2, screen_02; trailer 49.75–51.25 s].
   - A tall lavender-white tower faced with bold red geometric panels and two mint-white light bars, topped with red-tipped antennas.
   - Under its arch, a pale-blue sphere with a hexagon grid fires a translucent orange-gold cone onto a circular pad with a red-orange rim and a glowing white core.
   - Seen from below, the tower is framed by lavender steel window arches with the magenta nebula behind [Observed: Scary_Light; trailer 28.0 s].
   - It is "the orange beam parting the two teams spawn points" [Fan-wiki].
   - A small red-and-white object sits on the pad inside the beam [Observed: EE2 crop]. It is probably the Bearscream Sandwich "Below the huge laser" [Inference; Fan-wiki].
2. **The central module or "middle dome"** [Observed: overhead; trailer 25.5–26.25 s; screen_04; New_map_2].
   - A chunky white block with circular hatch details.
   - Inside, a red hall under an open roof slot with a dark slate floor, a red-lit door on one side and a purple-lit door on the other.
   - Its floor is the part the switch moves [Fan-wiki].
3. **The H.I.P. medallion walkway** [Observed: screen_02; trailer 25.5 s; overhead; EE3]. A narrow spine walkway whose floor carries a large red circular H.I.P. emblem in black line-art. The wiki says there are "a few logos in the map reading 'H.I.P.'" [Fan-wiki]. I saw the emblem but no lettering [Observed].
4. **The switch tower or "meteor tower"** [Observed: New_map_3; EE3; screen_02 crop; overhead].
   - A cracked red floor with a curved inlay, the lime-green console with its red down-arrow, a black cable and red-and-white striped poles.
   - A tall white wall carries a large **outline** engraving of the H.I.P. emblem.
   - A small pink Huggable hangs skewered on the black rod against that wall, above a pink splash. It does not move between trailer 50.25 s and screen_02 (about 50.6 s) [Observed]. It is probably the wiki's "Huggable corpse" [Inference]. The wiki instead places it on "the large tower adjacent to each team's spawn points" [Fan-wiki], so the location is an open question.
5. **The teal asteroids** [Observed: New_map_1; overhead; trailer 24.5 s; EE2; EE3].
   - Big, low-poly, faceted, dark-teal rocks pierced by hexagonal holes.
   - One is lodged against the switch tower, one floats by the red arm, and a small one floats below the laser end.
   - The wiki calls them "meteorites" and puts Easter eggs on top of them [Fan-wiki].
6. **The team arms** [Observed: overhead; trailer 24.5–25.5 s]. L-shaped decks edged with red and blue dashed running lights and team-coloured crosshair-circle floor decals.

## Hazards and interactive elements

**The void.**
- "The map has almost no walls, and that way it is very easy to suicide. It is considered a very dangerous map as suicides are very abundant" [Fan-wiki].
- Most deck edges I saw have no railing [Observed: overhead, screen_02]. Some red rails appear on the lower level and a chrome rail on a base deck [Observed: New_Map_4; EE2].
- Hazard deaths "cause a suicide and deduct a kill" [Fan-wiki: Health].
- The 2021 screenshots show the red team score at -1 and then -2 while the player hunts Easter eggs [Observed]. That those are suicides is [Inference].

**The laser.**
- It "activates periodically and cannot be controlled" [Fan-wiki].
- The Scary Light caption says it "kills any player who stays under its ray, resulting in suicide" [Fan-wiki]. The Health page says it "does damage quickly over time" rather than killing instantly [Fan-wiki].
- The wiki says "You actually can run through the laser if you use caffeine boost or shield" [Fan-wiki]. The Pick-Ups page instead says players with Shield "can still suicide from natural hazards" [Fan-wiki]. The two claims conflict.
- Projectiles:
  - The map page says some projectiles collide with the beam, "such as the TNT Launcher, whilst others do not, such as the Proximity Mine" [Fan-wiki].
  - The Bouncing Betty page claims Betty is "the only explosive weapon or projectile that could penetrate the Shortest Parsec's laser beam" [Fan-wiki]. This conflicts with the map page.
- Its timing, warning cue and sound are not documented [Unverified].

**The floor switch.**
- The map is "the second map with an interactive feature in the form of the switch found on the tower across from the spawn points". The switch "controls the position the floor of the middle dome and is activated by your melee weapon. It can be used to lure players to their deaths, even your own teammates" [Fan-wiki].
- The 1.4.2 article calls it "a removeable floor" [Fan-wiki].
- The lime-green console with the red down-arrow plate is very likely the switch [Inference].
- How the floor moves (drops, retracts or slides) is [Unverified].

**Jump pads.**
- There are several on each level [Observed].
- The wiki describes a bug where a player "suddenly stalls midway between the normal landing point and the Jump Pad at the edge of each team's base", which is "especially dangerous on Parsec" [Fan-wiki].

**Plant the Bomb.** The bombs sit "on the bottommost platform right next to each other, underneath the tower across from the spawn points" [Fan-wiki].

## Easter eggs and references

- **"YOU SHOULDN'T BE ABLE TO READ THIS!"** This text is on top of the laser tower [Fan-wiki; Observed EE1].
  - It is printed twice in black capitals, one copy mirrored, with five stars between them and two dark bear-head faces with frowning or half-closed eyes [Observed].
  - The wiki says it is "written twice and there are two confused bear faces" [Fan-wiki]. The wiki's gallery caption misspells the text as "Your shouldn't…".
  - Reaching it needs So Fly, the Turkish Jump Boots and a meteorite [Fan-wiki].
- **Meteorite tops.** Several asteroids can be climbed:
  - The red-base one "can be reached only by using So Fly[Huggable] and Metal Crush[B-1000]", and some players claim a double jump with the Turkish Jump Boots works.
  - One floats near the blue base, and one sits by the switch [Fan-wiki].
  - The Huggable (BBG) page instead says "the Huggable is the only class that can reach the map's Easter Egg" [Fan-wiki]. The two pages conflict.
  - EE2 and EE3 are taken standing on top of the teal rocks [Observed].
- **Face on the laser housing.** The laser housing seen from a meteorite shows two dark, drippy scorch smudges placed like sleepy eyes on its white panel [Observed: EE2]. Whether this is a deliberate face is [Unverified].
- **"Killerbear500."** These letters are scrawled in dark, smeared paint across a backlit purple panel wall, near a glowing Shield pickup [Observed: EE4]. The wiki says "KillerBear500 was the name of a fan who went to the SkyVu offices" [Fan-wiki]. Its exact spot on the map is [Unverified].
- **Huggable corpse.** The wiki says "There is a Huggable corpse on this map, on the side of the large tower adjacent to each team's spawn points" [Fan-wiki]. A skewered pink Huggable is visible at the switch tower [Observed]. See Landmarks.
- **H.I.P. emblems.** The emblem appears as a red floor medallion, as an outline engraving on the switch-tower wall and as a back-wall glyph in the hall [Observed]. It links the map to the H.I.P., B-1000 and Rho [Fan-wiki].
- **Orange emblem on the laser tower.** A different orange emblem sits on the tower's roof and face: a bear figure inside an octagonal cross frame [Observed: overhead; trailer 24.5 s; New_map_1]. What it means is [Unverified].
- **Name and tagline.** They riff on Star Wars and *Alien* [Inference].
