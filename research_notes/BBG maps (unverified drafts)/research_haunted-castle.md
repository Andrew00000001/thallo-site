# Haunted Castle

Haunted Castle is the Battle Bears Halloween map. It is a slate-blue walled castle at night, with a tall central tower on legs, a torch-lit terracotta courtyard full of crates and jack-o'-lanterns, and graveyard lawns outside the walls where the teams spawn [Observed]. It debuted in **Battle Bears Royale v1.3.3** (Oct 2012), the app that was later renamed BBG [Game text via wiki; Fan-wiki]. It was dropped around the 2013 move to BBG [Fan-wiki]. It came back in **BBG 2.4 (24 Oct 2013)** "To celebrate the holiday" [Canon].

## Era and status
- **Debut:** BBR v1.3.3, the Halloween update [Game text via wiki].
  - The wiki's update table dates it October 10, 2012 [Fan-wiki].
  - The wiki page was created Oct 15, 2012 [Fan-wiki, page history].
  - A fan upload dated Oct 13, 2012, "Battle Bears Royale: Halloween skins and new map", has the description "I play with the Demo in the new Haunted Castle..." [Unverified, fan video metadata].
  - The wiki page says "added to Battle Bears Gold in v1.3.3". That is anachronistic: BBG's own 1.0 was May 24, 2013, and BBG is "previously known as Battle Bears Royale" [Fan-wiki]. In Oct 2012 the game was BBR [Inference].
- **Late 2012:** our notes list it on the official BBR maps page of Nov 2012 ("Haunted Castle, Abusement Park, Facing Temples, Battlefield Airpad, Desert Airmine, Huggable Factory, Spacecraft.") [Unverified this session]. A fan video dated Dec 3, 2012 is titled as normal play in the map [Unverified].

## Layout
From the overhead map image (File:Haunted_Castle.jpeg, 796×816, with a "BATTLE BEARS" logo watermark) [Observed]. The watermark suggests an official SkyVu overhead image [Inference].

- **Overall shape:** a square castle compound sits in the middle of an irregular, figure-8 plot [Observed]. Two lobes of open grounds sit diagonally opposite each other: top-right and bottom-left in the image [Observed]. The layout has 180° rotational symmetry: pits, crates, ramps and spawns mirror through the centre [Observed]. The wiki says "It is a symmetrical map" [Fan-wiki].
- **Team bases:** red spawn markers sit in the top-right lobe and blue in the bottom-left lobe of the overhead [Observed]. The in-game compass is unknown [Unverified].
  - Red swallowtail banners flank a portcullis gate in HC_1 and IMG_0178 [Observed]. Blue banners flank a gate in the preview tile, the 1.3.3 banner and the 2.4 loading screen [Observed].
  - So each team's gatehouse probably flies its own colour [Inference].
- **Curtain wall:** slate stone walls with crenellations [Observed]. There are round towers at the four corners [Observed]. Square gatehouse towers sit midway along the two walls that face the team lobes [Observed]. The wall-walk is playable: "on top of the Castle walls" [Fan-wiki].
- **Courtyard:**
  - Open terracotta ground [Observed].
  - In the centre, a tall square tower stands on four legs, with wooden plank walkways crossing it in an X when seen from above [Observed].
  - Two dark octagonal patches sit in opposite inside corners [Observed]. They are probably the "large holes leading to the underground" [Inference from Fan-wiki].
  - Long low blocks stand against the other two inside corners [Observed]. They are probably the "walkway up to the top of the Castle walls" [Inference from Fan-wiki].
  - Crate stacks with pumpkins sit near the pits [Observed].
  - Small cyan-glowing fixtures stand at the foot of the left and right walls; their function is unknown [Observed].
- **Team yards:**
  - Dark lawns with rust-coloured leaf litter, rows of paired brown graves or coffins along the outer edge, and tall L-shaped hedges [Observed].
  - In each yard, a dome-shaped rock mound with a dark arched mouth [Observed]. The wiki says "Each team's base has another cave-like entrance to the underground tunnels" [Fan-wiki].
  - White lamp glows and a small lit structure near each spawn [Observed].
- **Edge:** a pinkish dirt rim, then black void [Observed].
- **Verticality:** "There are 4 levels of this map: Underground, ground level, on top of the Castle walls, and on top of the Castle's planks in the middle of the map." [Fan-wiki]
- **Underground and jump pads:**
  - Tunnels run to both teams' bases [Fan-wiki].
  - "In the middle of the underground is a Jump Pad that leads to the main level." [Fan-wiki]
  - "In the center of the ground level, there are 2 jump pads that lead up to the high planks of the middle castle." [Fan-wiki]
  - A 2013 revision mentions "two jump pads below the tower in the middle of the arena linking the tunnels to ground level" [Fan-wiki].
  - I saw a glowing round pad on the floor under the central tower's arch (preview tile) [Observed]. A fan thumbnail (ZnXHrdLGclw hq3) shows a hazard-striped cyan jump pad next to an octagonal glowing opening [Observed].
- **Gates and switches:**
  - "There is one switch above the gates of each team's base, which can be accessed by climbing up the Castle walls." [Fan-wiki]
  - "There are two levers on the map, each located inside a tower near each team's spawn point." [Fan-wiki]
- **Pickups** (the wiki contradicts itself):
  - Imperial Basket Grande ×2: "Inside brief structures atop the battlements left and right of the main area" [Fan-wiki].
  - Health Pack ×2: "On each side of the undersection of the main area" [Fan-wiki]. The map page instead says "On top of the Castle walls, are Imperial Basket Grandes, Medic Packs, Bearscream Sandwich power-ups, and switches." [Fan-wiki]
  - Bearscream Sandwich ×2: "placed at opposite ends of the map with a huge open space in between" [Fan-wiki].
  - Shields: "located across the entrances to the underground tunnels" [Fan-wiki].
- **Side routes:**
  - A narrow ledge runs around the outside of the castle to "a jagged hole leading into the main courtyard". Getting through it needs the Turkish SW Boots or the Pro-Mode Jump [Fan-wiki].
  - "In the blue team's base, past the big break in the wall, behind the fence, there's a big hole in the wall." [Fan-wiki]
- **Sightlines:** "there are sniping spots on the high castle walls which surround a large open area" [Fan-wiki, 2013 revision].
- **Scale:** the walls stand roughly 3–4 bears high next to a player [Observed, IMG_0178, approximate]. The wiki blames "the largeness of the map" for the problems slow classes have [Fan-wiki].

## Hazards and interactive elements
- **Lever gates:** "When hit with a melee attack, there are gates in a passageway underneath that will open or close." [Fan-wiki] The wiki also calls it "the first map in the game that features switches you can interact with" [Fan-wiki]. The 1.3.3 notes say "interactive features" [Game text via wiki].
  - Uses: to "limit the paths they can take out of their spawn", and in Plant the Bomb "so your opponent carrying the bomb have to take a longer way" [Fan-wiki, 2013 revision].
  - A fan clip, "BBR BONUS- Haunted castle Map Lever", has a thumbnail captioned "Haunted Castle Map Interaction". It shows a blue-grey flagstone tunnel with a Huggable [Observed]. I never saw what the lever looks like [Unverified].

- **Pickups:** two Bearscream Sandwiches, making it "the first to have two" [Fan-wiki]. Also 2 Grandes, 2 Health Packs and Shields [Fan-wiki].
- **Environmental damage:** none is documented [Unverified]. Black void lies beyond the map rim [Observed]. Whether falling off kills is [Unverified].
