# Riggs (Heavy): BBG weapons dossier

Riggs is the Heavy in Battle Bears Gold. Steam's 2026 store text lists "-HEAVY: Riggs" [Canon] (https://store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=4408320, fetched today). The wiki quotes the in-game class blurb as: "Riggs the heavy is built like a tank, and moves like a stone. Keep clear of wide open spaces as his primary weapons have poor accuracy! Your melee attack is devastating!" [Game text via wiki] (Template:Class:Heavy (BBG)).

The BBG arsenal has 20 items: 8 primaries, 6 secondaries and 6 melees [Fan-wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_(BBG)). The original BBR kit, per the class page's first revision (2012-02-03), was Beebeegun, Electric Honey and Zombee Gun; Hivey League, Buzz Kill and Sucker Punch; Barbed Gloves, Huggable Gloves and Zombee Gloves [Fan-wiki]. Later additions:

**Shared visual language**
- Heavy guns are oversized boxy launchers or miniguns, held low at the hip, each topped with a themed "topper" [Observed]:
  - a hive on Beebeegun, Electric Honey, Hivey League and Buzz Kill
  - a cheese lump on Provolowned
  - a Huggable or Huggable head on Field Medic, Zombee Gun, Bounce Lazer and Sucker Punch
  - a plasma globe on Plasma Orb
  - a vine-bound log as the whole weapon on Mother of Nature
- Shared meshes [Observed]:
  - Beebeegun and Electric Honey are one mesh, recoloured.
  - Field Medic and Zombee Gun are one stubby mesh, recoloured. This matches the wiki's "retexture" note.
  - Hivey League and Buzz Kill share a body and hive but have different muzzles.
  - Barbed, Huggable and Zombee Gloves are one creature-shaped mitt mesh, recoloured.
- **Default 3D Riggs in the 2013 BBG gear-ups** [Observed, sampled; File:BuzzKill.jpg and others]:

  - red forearm and shin bands, red round backpack

## Beebeegun (primary; default since BBR 2012; rebalanced in BBG 2.6, Dec 20 2013; 2026 unverified)
- **Official text:**
  - BBG item text: "Artificially accelerated and naturally angry bees. Also the bees are fired backwards. And their stingers are infused with adamantium." [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Beebeegun)
  - BB Heroes era: "Wielding his "Beebee gun," he excels in close combat, delivering a burst of shells that unleash severe damage when focused on a single target, proving lethal at short distances." [Canon] (https://battlebears.com/pages/characters, fetched today)
  - The BBH line describes the 2023–24 BB Heroes attack, which the wiki lists as "5 x 306" damage [Fan-wiki]. It is a shotgun-like burst, not the BBG minigun [Inference]. Do not mix the two.
- **History:**
  - The first page revision (2012-03-26, anonymous) reads "default heavy machine gun. moderate damage. has long continous fire" [Fan-wiki, early].
  - The BBG 2.6 page lists: clip 160→150, reload 3→4.5, dispersion 0.05→0.07, and "Damage increased from 7 to 6" (sic) [Fan-wiki].
  - These conflict with the current infobox (100 ammo, 4 s, 0.08).
  - 2026: not seen in the trailer or the Steam screenshots [Unverified].
- **Look:** [Observed: File:Photo_19-04-13_12_50_44_PM.png (2013 BBG gear-up) and File:Beebee.png (2012 BBR gear screen, Riggs in a brown skin with a red suit). Zoom: /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-thallo-site/91c60972-6d3f-51de-9a92-c1d563bcf5a4/scratchpad/weapons/dl/riggs-weapons/sheets/z_beebee.jpg]
  - Body: a long, faceted, charcoal-black boxy minigun (#232423–#2A2A2A) with a long octagonal barrel shroud.
  - Sides: glowing amber slatted panels (#E0AA44, shade #AC843C).
  - Topper: a faceted, horizontally ridged gold hive (#C7A93A, highlight #E8D790, shade #8D5E1F) on top near the rear. A bent yellow-and-black striped tube, like a bee's abdomen, runs from the hive to the back of the gun.
  - Bees: tiny white-winged yellow cartoon bees hover beside the hive and sit on top of the body. These are the wiki's "small bees circling its hive" [Observed, sampled; Fan-wiki].
  - Held low at the right hip, barrel angled down and forward.
  - Length ≈0.7× Riggs's height ≈0.85× Oliver.
  - Possible official look: the 2012 SkyVu BBR trailer frame https://i.ytimg.com/vi/0gYEZCMQfmA/hq3.jpg (crop /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-thallo-site/91c60972-6d3f-51de-9a92-c1d563bcf5a4/scratchpad/weapons/dl/riggs-weapons/crops/bbr_tr_hq3_gun.png) shows a white-and-blue Riggs holding a lighter tan-grey boxy gun with glowing orange slats and a gold striped hive dome [Observed]. That it is the Beebeegun is [Inference].
- **Firing:**
  - Automatic minigun with "Wind Up" and "Wind Down" barrel spin (wind-up value 375, wind-down 450, max rotation 3000).
  - 0.1 s per shot, 5.5 damage, 100 ammo, 4 s reload, dispersion 0.08, +10% Health.
  - All of the above [Fan-wiki]. Hitscan: "Automatics, shotguns, and others use hitscans" [Fan-wiki, Weapon Statistics].
  - Muzzle flash and sound are undocumented [Unverified].
- **Projectile:**
  - The in-game text says angry bees fired backwards [Game text via wiki].
  - I found no image of Beebeegun fire [Unverified].
  - The closest observed reference is the same-mesh Electric Honey in the 2026 trailer, which draws a straight dark tracer streak to the target. Transferring that to the Beebeegun is [Inference].
- **Impact / end result:** no splash or status effect [Fan-wiki]. The hit visual is unknown [Unverified].
- **Images viewed:**
  - File:Photo_19-04-13_12_50_44_PM.png, File:Beebee.png
  - https://i.ytimg.com/vi/0gYEZCMQfmA/hq3.jpg
  - File:Beebeegun_original_concept.jpg (grey sketch labelled "Riggs: Plasma Globe Glob Launcher", a three-barrel gun with a plasma globe and red recoil arrows)
  - File:Riggs-heavy.png (BBR 2D lineup art: a black triple-barrel gun with yellow striped accents and a pink plasma globe). The wiki calls this art "a hybrid of two successful concepts, which are today know as the Beebeegun and Plasma Orb" [Fan-wiki].
- **Depiction for shorts:** the barrels spin up with a whine, then a straight amber tracer stream fires, peppered with tiny tail-first cartoon bees. Small yellow "sting" sparks pop on the target, with no explosion [BBG News invention, modelled on the Observed Electric Honey tracer].
- **Confidence:** partial.
