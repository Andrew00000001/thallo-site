# Tillman (Demo / Demolition): BBG weapons bible

Tillman is the Demo in Battle Bears Gold. On Steam the class is "DEMOLITION: Tillman" [Canon]. The class blurb reads: "Tillman does not have a weapon on hand that doesn't have splash damage! Throwing explosives into a group of enemies is a great way to turn the tides of a battle but be very careful to avoid hitting yourself!" [Game text via wiki] (Demo page revision 69512, 2015). The BBG arsenal has 18 items: 8 primaries, 7 secondaries and 3 melees, per the wiki class page "Demo (BBG)" (revid 77289) [Fan-wiki]. One of the 18, Bouncing Betty, is marked "Unavailable" [Fan-wiki]. The previous researcher's inventory is complete, and I found no missing Demo weapon.

## Big Shot (primary, default; BBR launch loadout 2011 → BBG; held in the 2026 Steam trailer)
- **Official text:** "Launches a giant missile out at a straight trajectory." [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Big_Shot). The 2011 blog line "Primary: Big Shot (Chest Cannon)…" is [Canon, not re-verified today]; it is quoted in /home/user/thallo-site/research_notes/Battle Bears character lore/astoria_graham_tillman.md line 82. The 2.6 log reads: "Big Shot / Maximum Damage increased from 35 to 40 / Fire Rate decreased from 1.5 to 1 / Magazine size increased from 5 to 6 / Projectile Speed increased from 1000 to 1200 / Minimum Damage Splash range increased from 90 to 100 / Max Damage Splash Range increased from 45 to 50 / Explosive +2 increased from 18 to 20" [Canon].
- **Look:**
  - It is a squat drum or turbine launcher held two-handed, horizontally across the belly and chest, muzzle forward [Observed: A/png/Big_Shot.png].
  - The barrel is olive-grey metal (#77796D–#818475) inside a dark-purple bracket cage (#441F45) with grey edges. The muzzle is a round notched yellow disc (#E0BB0D/#BF9E0F) [Observed].
  - **It is a hand-held weapon, not a hatch in his chest** [Observed]. Read the 2011 "(Chest Cannon)" as "a cannon braced at the chest" [Inference].
  - The same model appears in the BBG 2.1 loadout with the Cyan Tillman skin [Observed: A/png/Demolition_holding_Big_Shot.png]. The 2012 BBR list icon is a purple-and-grey drum with a yellow disc [Observed: A/png2/Dark_Matter.png].
  - In the trailer at about 21.7–22.2 s, a white-and-blue-trim Tillman holds this drum at chest height. The side view shows a fat cylinder about as long as his torso is deep [Observed: M/fr_bs/b_024–b_029; V/trailer/t_0045].
  - **Size:** the muzzle disc is about 0.25× Tillman's height across and the whole launcher about 0.45× wide, roughly 0.4× Oliver [Inference].
- **Firing:** The wiki says it fires "damaging missiles in an arc" at a 0.5 s rate of fire, with 7 ammo in the infobox and 6 in the text [Fan-wiki]. The official text says "straight trajectory" [Game text via wiki], and the wiki trivia flags the conflict [Fan-wiki]. As of Dec 2013 the magazine held 6 and fired every 1 s [Canon]. No muzzle effect or sound is documented [Unverified].
- **Projectile:** A dark navy/black rounded bullet with white eye-like markings, trailing a pale white-grey smoke streak [Observed: A/png/8_battle_bears_royale.png, wiki caption "Big Shot projectile (seen to the right)"; zoom M/small/bs_proj_mine.png]. The wiki compares it to Bullet Bill [Fan-wiki]. It travels at 1,100 u/s per the infobox, or 1,200 per the 2.6 log [Fan-wiki/Canon].
- **Impact / end result:** It "will explode, damaging any opposing players within its AoE" (AoE 100/200) [Fan-wiki]. No captioned impact image exists [Unverified]. In the trailer at 19.4–23 s, an orange glowing faceted ball with grey smoke puffs flies across the "[RED]" arena while Wil is blown back in an orange fireball, and Tillman stands in the background with the Big Shot [Observed: M/small/bs_mid.jpg, M/small/bs_proj_zoom.jpg]. The shot's origin is not shown and it could be Wil's own weapon, so **this is not a documented Big Shot impact** [Inference].
- **Images viewed:**
  - A/png/Big_Shot.png
  - A/png/Demolition_holding_Big_Shot.png (via M/small/Demolition_holding_Big_Shot_s.jpg)
  - A/png/8_battle_bears_royale.png and M/small/bs_proj_mine.png
  - A/png2/Dark_Matter.png
  - M/small/bs_sheet_a.jpg, M/small/bs_mid.jpg, M/small/bs_proj_zoom.jpg, M/small/bs_holder.jpg, M/small/bs_seq.jpg (from V/trailer.mp4)
  - M/small/trailer_B_s.jpg
- **Depiction for shorts:** Tillman braces the purple-and-grey drum at his belly. A black "bullet with eyes" leaves the yellow muzzle on a white smoke streak in a shallow lob (the arc is per the wiki) [Fan-wiki]. It ends in a small orange pop with grey smoke and spark confetti [BBG News invention].
- **Confidence:** partial (look and projectile observed; impact unknown).

## Gold Member (primary; BBR by March 2012 → BBG; 25,000 Joules, level 3)
- **Official text:** "For gold medalist shooters only. Smaller explosion than the Big Shot but with more damage on direct impact." [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Member). The 2.6 log reads: "Gold Member / Minimum Damage decreased from 30 to 25 / Maximum Damage increased from 45 to 50 / Fire Rate decreased from 2 to 1.5 / Magazine Size decreased from 8 to 6 / Reload increased from 3 to 3.5 / Minimum Damage Range decreased from 100 to 90 / Maximum Damage Range increased from 45 to 50" [Canon]. The BBR 1.4.2 changes (Feb 28, 2013) are [Fan-wiki].
- **Status:** In the BBR loadout list it reads "GOLD MEMBER BUY NOW! 25,000" [Observed: A/png2/Dark_Matter.png, uploaded 2012-03-29].
- **Look:** It is the Big Shot drum in polished gold (#ECC23E with dark recesses) with an orange glowing muzzle disc (#FF6E13–#FB934C), held the same way [Observed: A/png/GoldMember.png, A/png/Gold_Tillman.png, A/png2/DEMO_GOLD_MEMBER.png, and A/png/GoldMember.png in M/small/primaries3.jpg]. It is the same size as the Big Shot [Inference]. The wiki calls it a Big Shot reskin [Fan-wiki].
- **Firing / projectile:** The wiki describes a "gold bullet-like missile that moves with a straight trajectory", with no arc, 1.5 s rate of fire and 1,200 u/s [Fan-wiki]. No image exists [Unverified].
- **Impact / end result:** It explodes on contact [Fan-wiki]. The official text says the blast is smaller than the Big Shot's [Game text via wiki], and the wiki's own AoE numbers agree (75/150 against 100/200), although its prose says "larger AoE" [Fan-wiki conflict]. No image exists [Unverified].
- **Images viewed:** A/png/GoldMember.png, A/png/Gold_Tillman.png (via M/small/Gold_Tillman_s.jpg), A/png2/DEMO_GOLD_MEMBER.png and A/png2/Dark_Matter.png (via M/small/r_dm1.jpg), M/small/primaries3.jpg.
- **Depiction for shorts:** Fire a gold bullet-missile dead straight from the orange muzzle, ending in a tight gold-orange pop that is smaller than the Big Shot's [BBG News invention].
- **Confidence:** partial.

## Dark Matter (primary; BBR by March 2012 → BBG; 60,000 Joules, level 9)
- **Official text:** "Amazing blast range, and utterly disorientating when consumed by one. Be very careful with friendly fire and consider using ammo equipment to up its 1 shot reserve!" [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Matter_(BBG)). The 2.6 log reads: "Dark Matter / Maximum Damage decreased from 70 to 60 / Clip size increased from 1 to 3 / Explosive +1 decreased from 12 to 10 / Explosive +2 increased from 18 to 20" [Canon]. So the "1 shot reserve" wording describes the clip before Dec 2013 [Inference]. The infobox now shows 4 ammo [Fan-wiki].
- **Status / history:** The price went 50,000 → 20,000 → 60,000 Joules [Fan-wiki]. A glowing tip was added in BBR 1.4.3 (Apr 15, 2013): "Purple pulsing light emitting from the spinning barrel" [Fan-wiki].
- **Look:**
  - It is a long, chunky violet blaster (#73298A/#9140AF, near-black #200928), held two-handed across the body. It has a yellow light slot on top (#F4D864) and a crown of black-and-purple claw prongs around a glowing magenta-violet tip (#EA6CFB/#C857EF glow) [Observed: A/png/DarkMatter.png].
  - The 2012 BBR model (A/png2/Dark_Matter.png) is the same gun with no tip glow, which fits the 1.4.3 note [Observed].
  - In 2D art the casing is purple with starry speckles and gold panels, and black prongs hold a dark core in a purple glow. The gun is carried angled up past his shoulder [Observed: A/png/Demo-darkmatter.png, A/png/TILLMAN_Blog.png, M/img2/Demolition.png].
  - **Size:** about 0.8–1.0× Tillman's height long, roughly 0.75–0.9× Oliver [Inference].
- **Firing:** A "single, slow moving purple orb" travels in a straight line at 600 u/s, firing every 1 s [Fan-wiki].
- **Projectile:** White-hot cores (#FFFFFF) in a hot-pink/magenta glow (#FD78B2/#EE81B4), wrapped in curved pink blade-like swirl segments (#E85D94), with thin pale-blue electric arcs between orbs [Observed: A/png/Dearly_matter_close_up.png, wiki caption "Dark Matter projectiles"]. **They read hot pink rather than the deep purple of the wiki text** [Observed vs Fan-wiki].
- **Impact / end result:** The wiki calls the explosion "notably large, dark, and electrified". It says "a sphere of energy blocks the target's vision… unable to see for a second or two, regardless of whether they are a teammate or on the enemy team" [Fan-wiki]. No captioned impact image exists [Unverified].
  - **Correction to the earlier lead:** the giant translucent magenta sphere in the trailer at 14–16 s (V/trailer/t_0029–t_0033) surrounds **Astoria** in her segment. It is more likely an Astoria effect, so it is **not** evidence of the Dark Matter look [Observed placement; attribution Inference].
  - A/png2/Infinite_Light_Matter_hacker.png shows a purple Dark-Matter-style gun in first person amid blue orbs and cyan lightning, with the HUD at 0/2. Its title and contents disagree, so do not use it [Observed; meaning Unverified].
  - M/img2/Light_matter.png ("Light matter.jpeg", 2013) shows a magenta-glowing gun previewed at the Dark Matter price of 60,000 [Observed].
- **Images viewed:** A/png/DarkMatter.png, A/png2/Dark_Matter.png, A/png/Demo-darkmatter.png, A/png/TILLMAN_Blog.png, M/img2/Demolition.png, A/png/Dearly_matter_close_up.png, A/png2/Infinite_Light_Matter_hacker.png, M/img2/Light_matter.png, M/small/tr_dm.jpg (V/trailer t_0025–t_0033), M/small/orb_seq.jpg.
- **Depiction for shorts:** A slow hot-pink orb with a white core and crackling pale-blue arcs [Observed]. It bursts into a dark-violet energy sphere [Fan-wiki "dark"] that briefly blanks the victim's view; show this as a dark vignette with dizzy stars [BBG News invention].
- **Confidence:** partial.

## Light Matter (primary; added BBR 1.3.1 on June 28, 2012 → BBG; 70 Gas, level 1)
- **Official text:** "It's not to be taken lightly WAKKA WAKKA WAKKA!" [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Light_Matter). The 2.6 log reads: "Light Matter / Minimum Damage increased from 20 to 45 / Maximum Damage increased from 80 to 90 / Clip size increased rom 1 to 2 / Reload speed decreased form 4 to 3.5 / Projectile speed increased from 450 to 500 …" [Canon, typos sic].
- **Status:** The weapon page says 70 Gas and the class page says 60 [Fan-wiki conflict]. The in-game shop showed "LIGHT MATTER BUY NOW! 70" with a gas-can icon in April 2013 [Observed: A/png2/Dark_matter_s_opposite.png]. BBR 1.4.2 raised projectile speed 350→450, cut reload 5→4 and changed the price from 100,000 Joules to 70 Gas [Fan-wiki]. A "Blue pulsing light" tip was added in 1.4.3 [Fan-wiki].
- **Look:**
  - It has the Dark Matter body in white/silver (#F5F3EE/#E4DFD7) with black stripes (#282622) and white-and-black claw prongs around a glowing cyan tip (#86FDFC/#69D8FB) [Observed: A/png/LightMatter.png, A/png2/Light_Matter.png].
  - The 2012 version (pumpkin-head skin) is the same white gun with no glow [Observed: A/png2/Light_matter.png].
  - In the official 2014 "Tillman Overhaul" thumbnail hq2, the first-person view shows the white clawed gun with a cyan-white muzzle glow; the HUD reads 0/8 and "RELOAD" [Observed: A/yt/q1Y8Siqfvn4_hq2.jpg].
  - It is the same size as the Dark Matter [Inference].
- **Firing:** A "single, slow moving blue orb" travels at 750 u/s per the infobox, or 500 per the 2.6 log, and fast classes can outrun it [Fan-wiki/Canon].
- **Projectile:** An electric-blue orb with a white core, cyan (#2BFDFE) and blue (#0CAFFA/#022FCB), inside a ring of curved blue segments and trailing lightning streaks [Observed: A/png/Light_Matter_Orb.png, caption "Light Matter projectile"]. A second image shows a blue orb throwing two long lightning bolts upward [Observed: A/png2/Light_matter_orb.png].
- **Impact / end result:**
  - It makes a huge, opaque, faceted **white dome** (#F7F7F0) wrapped in jagged **cyan lightning**. The HUD shows 0/1 ammo, which fits the 1-round clip before 2.6 [Observed: A/png/Photo.png, caption "Light Matter explosion", 2012-06-30].
  - The official thumbnail hq1 shows the same white dome with cyan lightning while the HUD weapon icon is the white Light Matter [Observed: A/yt/q1Y8Siqfvn4_hq1.jpg; zoom M/yt_hud_zoom.png].
  - The same dome flings orange body pieces in Graham's trailer segment at 8.5–10 s and in Steam screen_03 [Observed: V/trailer/t_0018–t_0021, V/steam_screens/screen_03.jpg]. The shooter is not shown there [Inference].
  - The wiki says "notably large, blinding, and electrified" [Fan-wiki].
- **Images viewed:** A/png/LightMatter.png, A/png2/Light_Matter.png, A/png2/Light_matter.png, A/png2/Dark_matter_s_opposite.png, A/png/Light_Matter_Orb.png, A/png2/Light_matter_orb.png, A/png/Photo.png, M/yt_overhaul_sheet.png, M/yt_hud_zoom.png, M/small/tr_graham.jpg, M/small/screen_03_s.jpg.
- **Depiction for shorts:** A blue ring-orb drifts slowly, trailing lightning, then the screen whites out into a faceted dome with cyan bolts. Victims tumble out as sparks and plush confetti [Observed look; aftermath is BBG News invention].

## S'no Matter (primary; added BBG 2.15 on Dec 20, 2014; 60 Gas, level 15)
- **Official text:** "It S'no Matter how fast you can move, I can just freeze you! " [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/S%27no_Matter).
- **Look:**
  - In-game, the held weapon is swamped by a bright icy white-cyan glow (#EBFEFE/#C3E7E8) at belly height, so its shape is hard to read [Observed: A/png/Snomatter.png, 2015-02].
  - The concept sheet is hand-labelled "Tillmans S'no Matter". It shows a red tartan gun body (#751815/#572B26) with a white snowflake ornament on top, cyan hexagonal gems (#18C6C1/#B7EBEB) and a dark curved hook nose. A second variant has a grey studded body with ice shards [Observed: A/png/Snomatterconcpetar.png].
  - Holiday art, which the wiki calls "Additional Concept Art for S'no Matter", shows a snowman-faced panda (carrot nose) holding a chunky grey blaster with red-and-white candy-cane stripes, claw prongs and cyan glowing rings, under a "BATTLE BEARS ROYALE" logo. That makes it BBR-era holiday key art [Observed: A/png/AcreamboPistolsandS'noMatterconceptart.png].
  - **Size:** the in-game shape is unreadable [Unverified]. The concept is roughly Dark Matter size [Inference].

- **Impact / end result:** It explodes and "freezes anyone in the blast radius for 2 seconds, leaving them unable to move or rotate the camera". A reload in progress restarts, and teammates freeze too [Fan-wiki]. The concept shows the target encased in a jagged cyan ice block (#7DD4D4/#5DA4AE) [Observed concept]. ## Bouncing Betty (primary; BBG 2.2 on Aug 21, 2013; REMOVED)
- **Official text:** "The master of explosions has a lovely friend named Betty. Tillman and her go way back, bouncing around exploding everywhere. Oh, to be young and explosively in love!" [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Bouncing_Betty).
- **Status (corrected):**
  - A 2014–2016 revision says "After the nerf in 2.10, the cool down was increased from 1 to 1.5 second, and the ammo was decreased to 2" [Fan-wiki, rev 68735].
  - An edit on 2018-08-03 says "After the change in 2.31, the cool down was decreased from 1.5 to 1 second, and the ammo was increased to 3" [Fan-wiki, rev 75803]. The wiki's Updates table dates 2.31 to Mar 8, 2018 [Fan-wiki]. **So the weapon was still live in 2018**, later than the earlier inventory implied.
  - The page switched to past tense on 2020-05-22 (rev 76611), and the class page marked it "Unavailable" in the November 2020 edits [Fan-wiki revision history]. Removal therefore fell between about August 2018 and May 2020 [Inference]. Whether it returns in 2026 is [Unverified].
  - Stats were updated on 2014-06-07 "as of June 6, 2014 (Demo Overhaul)" [Fan-wiki edit summary].
- **Look:**
  - It is a stubby launcher with a very wide bell mouth, held at face height. The shell is pale grey-white (#D2C9D3), with a red crosshair emblem and a yellow tab (#D2B311) [Observed: A/png/Bouncing_Betty.png].
  - In first person it is white/silver with an orange band bearing a black bomb decal with red lips and white "PU[SH]" lettering [Observed: M/img/Tillman_playing_with_Betty_conv.png, zoom M/small/betty_zoom.jpg].
  - Two official concept sheets carry a SkyVu watermark and BBG logo. They show 6 colour variants (olive, white, black and grey) with a "PUSH" band, a bomb-face icon, an arrow and a round button [Observed: A/png/Bouncing_Betty_Concept.png, A/png/Bouncingbettyconceptart.png].
  - **Size:** the mouth is about 0.35× Tillman's height across [Inference].
- **Firing:** It "lobbed a large, black bomb with a lit fuse"; the bomb bounces 3 times, with angle of reflection equal to the angle of incidence [Fan-wiki].
- **Projectile:** A round black cartoon bomb with white winking eyes, red lips and a sparking fuse, trailing pink puffs [Observed: M/small/betty_zoom.jpg]. A/png/CRAZY_DEMO_LAND.png (caption "Bouncing Betty projectiles") shows several small black lit-fuse bombs with sparkle trails [Observed].
- **Impact / end result:** It explodes on each bounce and detonates on the third; it "could not directly hit players" [Fan-wiki]. No explosion image exists [Unverified].
- **Images viewed:** A/png/Bouncing_Betty.png, A/png/Bouncing_Betty_Concept.png, A/png/Bouncingbettyconceptart.png (via M/small/r_betty1.jpg), M/img/Tillman_playing_with_Betty_conv.png and A/png/CRAZY_DEMO_LAND.png (via M/small/r_betty2.jpg), M/small/betty_zoom.jpg.
- **Depiction for shorts:** Use it only for retro or flashback gags. A winking red-lipped bomb boings three times, popping a small blast at each bounce [Fan-wiki mechanics; blast look is BBG News invention].
- **Confidence:** partial.

## Blast-O-Mole (primary; BBG 2.5 on Nov 27, 2013; Tillman's BBH basic attack)
- **Official text:**
  - "Tillman's pet mole. Playing fetch has never been so explosive!" [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Blast-O-Mole).
  - BBH, re-fetched today from https://battlebears.com/pages/characters: "Tillman's "Blast-o-Mole" attack involves launching a robotic mole that burrows and explodes, damaging enemies within its blast zone until it vanishes." [Canon]
  - The 2.6 log reads: "Blast O Mole / Fire Rate increased from 1 to 2 / Ammo +1 and Ammo +2 removed" [Canon]. Oddly, it sits under the "Graham Changes:" heading [Canon; probably a layout slip, Inference].
- **Status:** The weapon page says 80 Gas and the class page says 60 [Fan-wiki conflict]; level 1.
- **Look:**
  - A chunky robot mole held two-handed at the hip. The body is purple/violet (#6C4DB5/#967AC8, dark #332637), with a silver spiral drill nose (#D6CCE2/#9689A0), a glowing magenta eye (#F156F0) and silver spikes underneath [Observed: A/png/Blast-O-Mole.png].
  - **Size:** about 0.4× Tillman's height long, roughly 0.35× Oliver [Inference].
- **Firing:** It is thrown "in the direction of the player's aim with a slight arc", 1.25 s, ammo 2 [Fan-wiki].
- **Impact / end result:**
  - BBG: "huge damage-dealing jets of flame-like explosions burst forward from the site of impact… in a straight line until approximately six explosions" [Fan-wiki]. The class page calls it "a column of fire" [Fan-wiki].
  - BBH: it burrows and explodes in a blast zone [Canon].
