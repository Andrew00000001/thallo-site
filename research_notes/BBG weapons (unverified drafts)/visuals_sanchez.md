# Sanchez (Arbiter): weapons dossier

Sanchez is the Arbiter class of Battle Bears Gold. He was added in BBG 2.6 on Dec 20, 2013 [Fan-wiki: Updates page, Arbiter (BBG) changelog]. SkyVu's 2.6 notes listing his kit are quoted in our prior notes, but I could not re-open them today [Canon, quoted in prior notes; Unverified today]. The 2026 Steam page lists "ARBITER: Sanchez" among the 11 classes and gives no kit text [Canon, Steam appdetails 4408320, re-fetched 2026-10-16]. The fan wiki's class page gives him 14 BBG weapons: 6 primaries, 5 secondaries and 3 melees [Fan-wiki]. One primary is marked removed (Greased Lightning), and the default primary was renamed and reworked from Bind Gun to Adrenaline Gun in mid-2021 [Fan-wiki].

**Scale:** Sanchez is 1.0x Oliver on the BBG News scale chart [BBG News invention, README]. Weapon sizes below are fractions of Sanchez's height, estimated from the in-game loadout renders [Inference].

**What this pass adds to or corrects in the previous inventory** (details are in each section):
1. **Bind Gun beam:** now observed. It is a wavy hot pink-magenta-to-violet beam with a white core. Hits throw a white-pink starburst with violet lightning tendrils, and the target gets a yellow "Z" between two white curved arrows. Seen in fan gameplay thumbnails from Dec 2013 and Jan 2014 [Observed].
2. **Kelowna poison cloud:** now observed in thumbnails from SkyVu's official Mar 9, 2018 stream. It is a swarm of translucent purple puffs, some shaped like cartoon skulls [Observed]. The Sanchez clone is still unobserved.
3. **Drain Field in play:** a giant translucent **red** creature projection ringed with white fangs [Observed, Apr 2016 fan thumbnail]. The wiki calls the field "dark" with bats; neither was seen.
4. **Carpet Bomb:** the rolled carpet has a face. One end is a black cap with two white cartoon eyes and a grey screw-like spike [Observed]. Also seen in flight and as a yellow-white fireball.
5. **Glue Bomb:** now seen in flight (a dark sphere with a pink cross). At detonation it shows as a pale cyan bubble with a ring, or as blue ripple rings [Observed].
6. **Greased Lightning beam:** a thin pink-magenta laser with a white core [Observed, Apr 2016]. The HUD showed a 22-round magazine in 2016, which matches neither wiki value (40 or 30) [Observed vs Fan-wiki].
7. **Spin Baby Spin burst:** green and lime star sparkles with curly swirls (2017), and a lime-green "?" over a victim (2022) [Observed].
8. **Kelowna and Stealth Knife renders:** the "unidentified black-and-gold skin" is **Judge Sanchez** [Observed, matched against File:Judge_Sanchez.png].
9. **Blade Augment card diagram:** the slash arc is **grey**, not red, as the inventory said. The red jagged slashes belong to the Harvester panel [Observed].
10. **2026:** a Sep 20, 2026 playtest thumbnail shows a magenta beam and the yellow-Z Slowed icon. Slowing still exists in the 2026 build, but the frame does not show who fired the beam [Observed; attribution Unverified].

## Shared visual language (sub-topic: status icons, HUD, skins)

- **Slowed icon.** A chunky yellow "Z" (about #F9F430) sits between two white curved arrows that form a circle, floating over the victim's head [Observed]. It appears in GoF3Hy4QQis hq3 (Jan 30, 2014), tTqrtzqG-dU hq3 (Mar 8, 2014), xzRwifNTXWU hq1 and hq3 (Dec 23, 2013), Wfy_1E0CZBE hq2 (Dec 27, 2014), t-Dupfa6rsE hq1 (Jan 15, 2017), RRI-ejnlcww hq3 (May 30, 2017) and JVEQQqyafzE hq1 and hq2 (Sep 20, 2026).
  - The wiki describes it only as "a yellow Z" [Fan-wiki, Status Effects].
  - The wiki lists these Slowed sources: Slow Motion Field, Phantom Hug, Slow Field, Bind Gun and Glue Bomb [Fan-wiki]. A Z on screen therefore does not prove which weapon caused it [Inference].
  - Several Sanchez videos show an hourglass Special button in the HUD [Observed]. That is plausibly the Slow Motion Field [Inference].
- **Controls Reversed:** "a green question mark" [Fan-wiki]. Observed as a lime-green "?" beside a Huggable in a Mar 2022 fan frame [Observed, eEw00KhDdTw hqdefault].
- **Poisoned:** "a purple/green skull" [Fan-wiki]. The Kelowna cloud puffs are themselves skull-shaped [Observed].
- **Damage Resistance** (Invulnerable Field): "No overhead icon shown" [Fan-wiki].
- **Forearm lamp.** In every loadout render, the red-and-black prosthetic forearm carries a glowing red lamp panel (about #750404) [Observed]. The wiki's "command gauntlet" and "button on his arm" for the fields are text only [Fan-wiki].
- **Skins seen holding his weapons** [Observed]:
  - Default: maroon helmet, red visor, camo.
  - Judge Sanchez: black suit, gold stars on the helmet band, gold-rimmed visor. Seen in the Kelowna and Stealth Knife renders.
  - Gold Sanchez.
  - Tron-Tastic!: black with glowing lines. The lines are red-orange in 2014, 2015, 2018 and 2026 frames and cyan in a Jan 2016 frame. The colour difference is probably team colour [Inference].
- **Unexplained pink crackle.** A pink-magenta electric crackle (about #BE2658, #DA7EC7, #FFF0FC) sits around Sanchez's lower body or arm in three fan frames [Observed]: tTqrtzqG-dU hqdefault (ammo 0/5), O60Fq06wfec hq2 (ammo 0/5) and 0njNOuanL3E hq1. Its cause is undocumented [Unverified]. It may be a reload or slowed effect [Inference]. Do not animate it as a weapon effect until confirmed.

## Bind Gun → Adrenaline Gun (primary, default; Bind Gun Dec 2013–Jun 2021, then Adrenaline Gun; 2026 identity unverified)

- **Official text**
  - Bind Gun: "One weapon to slow them all!" [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/index.php?oldid=58371, Dec 21, 2013; also in the current page's trivia).
  - Adrenaline Gun: "The only bad thing about being faster than light is that you always live in darkness" [Game text via wiki]. The wiki itself says this was "submitted by community member Wilpower784" (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Adrenaline_Gun).
  - The official Dec 2013 weapon card labels the gun "BIND GUN" with an up-arrow [Observed, wiki copy File:Sanchez_weapons.jpg].
  - Class blurb: "Use his unique weaponry to slow down opponents and finish them off with his devastating melee attacks." [Game text via wiki, Updates page 2.6 entry].
  - BB Heroes (a different game, 2024): "Sanchez's "Adrenaline Gun" rapidly fires four staggered projectiles with a fast reload time." [Canon, https://battlebears.com/pages/characters, re-fetched 2026-10-16]. Do not use this for BBG.
- **Rework history.** The wiki says the gun "used to be called Bind Gun with a speedMultiplier of 0.8, but it was changed into the adrenaline gun on the 19th of June 2021" [Fan-wiki]. The page history agrees: SSP Rism edited on 2021-06-18, and Thecheeselover moved the page on 2021-07-17 with the comment "New name for the weapon" [Fan-wiki revision history]. Both dates are before BBG 3.0 (Nov 15, 2021) [Fan-wiki Updates].
- **Look**
  - The 3D model is a chunky, glossy, faceted sci-fi blaster in cyan/teal-blue [Observed]:
    - midtones about #468FAB and #3C7089
    - highlights about #6CBCD0
    - dark panel lines about #1A465F and #0F1C24
    - a round muzzle with a glowing white core (about #E7F0F7) haloed in pale blue (about #8BBCE9)
  - Sanchez holds it two-handed at the hip or chest. The red-black prosthetic right hand grips the rear and the gloved left hand is under the front [Observed].
  - It is about 0.35–0.4x his height in the render [Inference].
  - The card draws it longer (about 0.6x) and brighter cyan (#43B2D1, #1586BA, #0A638F), with grooved side panels [Observed].
  - The 2D promo art and the 2.6 banner draw it darker steel/royal blue (about #326581, #5798B0, deep #061A39), with a large round barrel, a blue-lens muzzle and a glowing blue glyph panel [Observed].
  - In third-person play it shows as the same cyan block at the right hip [Observed, GoF3Hy4QQis hq1 and hq3].
  - **2026:** the "Arbiter (Sanchez)" chapter thumbnail of JustNubz's Sep 20, 2026 showcase (chapter at 47:23, per the video description) shows Tron-Tastic! Sanchez on the loadout screen holding the same cyan gun with a glowing white muzzle [Observed; the video itself was not watched].
  - Mid-2022: Gold Sanchez with the same blue gun, HUD "55 / 55" [Observed, EXf5g_uzhc0 hq1, May 18, 2022].
- **Firing**
  - Automatic [Fan-wiki]. Rate of fire 0.15 s from 2020 [Fan-wiki].
  - The beam leaves the muzzle in a white-pink flare [Observed, GoF3Hy4QQis hq1].
  - HUD ammo seen [Observed]:
    - "32 / 70" (Dec 21, 2013 PTB video). 70 = 50 plus +20 from Ammo +2 under the Dec 2013 equipment table [Inference from rev 58371].
    - "34 / 50" and "25 / 50" (Jan 30, 2014).
    - A blue gun at "0 / 40" (Jul 22, 2015). That this is the Bind Gun is [Inference].
    - "50 / 50" (Jan 15, 2017).
    - "55 / 55" (May 2022).
  - Wiki ammo history: 50 (2013), 45 (Apr 2020), 55 (Nov 2020 and the Adrenaline Gun) [Fan-wiki].
  - No sound is documented [Unverified].
- **Projectile**
  - The wiki says it "seems to shoot a purple beam of energy... the beam tends to move left and right slightly" (Dec 2013), and later "tends to move in an oval-like wave" [Fan-wiki].
  - **Observed:** a continuous hot pink-magenta-to-violet beam with a white-hot core (about #DEA2E0, #D67090, #E9D9F6 and white #FFEEF9), drawn as a jagged, wavering line rather than a straight laser [Observed, GoF3Hy4QQis hq1 and hq3, Y2IbsH718QA hq3].
  - No image shows the 2021+ Adrenaline beam [Unverified]. The wiki still calls it "a purple beam" [Fan-wiki].
- **Impact / end result**
  - At the hit point there is a white-pink starburst flash with thin violet lightning tendrils crackling outward (about #CB90B8 and #925866) [Observed, GoF3Hy4QQis hq3].
  - The victim gets the yellow-Z Slowed icon [Observed, GoF3Hy4QQis hq3, target "HARRY"].
  - 2013–2021 Bind effect: each hit slows the target briefly, and the slow stacks, "possibly to the point of becoming immobile" [Fan-wiki rev 61247, Mar 2014]. Apr 2020: 10% per hit for 1 s. Nov 2020: 20% for 1.25 s [Fan-wiki].
  - 2021+ Adrenaline effect: "Speed Effect 50% per hit" for 0.5 s. Targets are "drastically sped up", "an easy way to cause them to fall off the map, run into a Sanchez melee or barely miss their intended destination" [Fan-wiki]. No overhead icon for the speed-up is documented [Unverified].
  - Shielded targets still get the effect but take no damage [Fan-wiki]. Firing while backing up can hit yourself [Fan-wiki].
  - **2026:** a Sep 20, 2026 playtest thumbnail shows a magenta beam with a white core and the yellow-Z icon over a slowed bear (orange hard hat, red box backpack) [Observed, JVEQQqyafzE hq1 and hq2]. The shooter is off screen, so this shows that slowing exists in 2026 but not that the Bind Gun survived [Inference].

## Glue Bomb (primary, unlock; BBG 2.6, Dec 20, 2013, to present)

- **Official text:** "Glue plus bomb equals very slow battle bears." [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Glue_Bomb). The card reads "GLUE BOMB / Throw at Enemy" [Observed].
- **Status:** launched at 90,000 Joules; now 60,000 [Fan-wiki].
- **Look**
  - A sphere of rounded black/gunmetal armour plates (about #1C1B23, #423C4F, #5A566B), split by glowing seams that form a cross or X [Observed, Glue_Bomb.png].
  - The seams are pale lilac-magenta in the render (about #CBA1D7, deep #5D1B66) [Observed].
  - It is palm-sized, about 0.2x his height [Inference], and is carried low in the prosthetic right hand [Observed].
  - Card icon: dark navy plates (about #151826) with a violet glowing cross (about #463571) and a white glint [Observed].
  - HUD icon: the same sphere with a hot-pink cross [Observed].
  - Stuck on a wall, it is a fist-sized dark ball with a glowing pink cross [Observed, Glue_bomb_on_a_wall.png, a fan screenshot from Dec 22, 2013].
- **Firing**
  - Thrown in an arc. It "stick[s] to the first solid object it comes into contact with" [Fan-wiki].
  - The card diagram shows a stick figure lobbing a purple ball along pale-blue arc lines [Observed].
  - Rate of fire 2.25 s, ammo 4, reload 4 s [Fan-wiki].
  - HUD ammo seen [Observed]:
    - "2 / 3" (Dec 22, 2013)
    - "1 / 3" (Dec 23, 2013 and Jan 21, 2018)
    - "2 / 5" and "0 / 5" (Jan 30 and Mar 8, 2014). These counts are probably with Ammo+ equipment [Inference].
  - The wiki gave ammo 3 in Dec 2013 [Fan-wiki rev 58550].
- **Projectile**
  - In flight it is a small dark sphere with a glowing pink cross [Observed, tTqrtzqG-dU hq3, Mar 8, 2014].
  - Detonation delay: the wiki contradicts itself. The text says "after a one second delay", the infobox gives "Explosion Delay 0 seconds", and the Usage paragraph also says "explodes on impact" [Fan-wiki].
- **Impact / end result**
  - Wiki: damages and slows everyone in the area of effect (slow 80% for 2 s, explosives 15–40). It "affects a large radius, potentially bring whole teams to a standstill" [Fan-wiki].
  - Observed looks:
    - (a) Two large, thin, concentric blue rings (about #274678 and #263864 on a dark scene) around a small pale inner ring, with no fireball [Observed, Sonic_wave_glue_bomb.png, wiki caption "A Glue Bomb releasing a slowing field" (Fan-wiki)].
    - (b) A translucent pale-cyan bubble sphere with a white-blue core and a flat ring around it (about #BBF6FE, #9ED7ED, #818EAB) [Observed, jS2I01x4ES4 hq1, "FIVE GLUE BOMBS!!!!????", CheeseclanBBG, Jan 30, 2014].
    - (c) Faint pale-blue ripple rings on the snow beside a victim who shows the yellow-Z icon [Observed, tTqrtzqG-dU hq3].
    - (d) A big yellow-white flash over a victim showing the Z [Observed, xzRwifNTXWU hq3, Dec 23, 2013]. The player's HUD shows the Glue Bomb, so the flash is probably the bomb [Inference].
  - Card diagram: the victim stands in blue ripple rings (about #4F6AAD, #7D9ED4, #AAC8E9), wrapped in a crackling purple outline [Observed].
