# Graham (Engineer): BBG weapons dossier

Graham is BBG's Engineer. The wiki calls him "a defensive class, due to dependence on static weapons for support on the field" with "relatively slow movement speed" [Fan-wiki: Engineer (BBG)]. The wiki's class template carries this description: "Don't be fooled by his bushy gray mustache, this class relies more on brains than brawn or speed. This wise whiskered bear specializes in defending areas of the map and controlling enemy traffic." [Fan-wiki template text, probably the in-game class blurb; Unverified as game text]. His BBG arsenal is 17 weapons: 6 primaries, 7 secondaries and 4 melees [Fan-wiki: Engineer (BBG) weapon list].

## Shared visual systems

### Radiation status (Gamma Ray, Gernobyl, MuskIt, Radiation Rod)
- **How the wiki describes it:**
  - "a yellow circle will appear over the player's head and a noise from a Geiger counter will begin sounding ... As the intake increases, three portions of the circle will dissolve into the common radioactivity symbol, where further exposure will result in death." [Fan-wiki: Status Effects]
  - All four Engineer radiation weapons are listed under Irradiated [Fan-wiki: Status Effects].
- **Icon stages:**
  - The wiki's four icons go from a full 6-wedge disc (Hazard-3, 0–24%) to 5 wedges (Hazard-2) to 4 (Hazard-1) to the 3-blade trefoil (Hazard-0, 75–99%) [Observed: File:Hazard-0.png to File:Hazard-3.png; ranges Fan-wiki]. /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-thallo-site/91c60972-6d3f-51de-9a92-c1d563bcf5a4/scratchpad/weapons/dl/graham-dossier/crops/hazard_sheet.png
  - The wiki draws the wedges black on a transparent background [Observed].
  - In game the wedges are yellow on a black disc with a black hub and rim [Observed]:
    - A Jan 2014 fan clip shows a large yellow disc split into wedges, with one wedge gone [Observed: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/0GuhKiAnNyc/hq2.jpg; crops/gr_fan_zoom.png].
    - From 8.3 to 8.7 s the trailer shows three yellow blades (#FDC917) on black (#130F0B) above the target [Observed: frames f_016–f_020; crops/icon_seq.png].
    - That the trailer icon is the final 75–99% stage is [Inference from the Fan-wiki table].
  - The 2013 in-match HUD shows the same yellow-and-black disc [Observed: File:Radioactive_puddle.jpeg; crops/puddle_icon.png].
- **Sound:** Geiger-counter ticking that "will also become faster and faster - this sound is audible to all players nearby" [Fan-wiki: Gamma Ray].
- **Decay:** radiation wears off at about 3–5 RP/s once exposure stops, and the icon steps back [Fan-wiki].
- **Detonation:**
  - At full radiation the target "will die instantly in a relatively large explosion". The blast radius is 150–200 units and damages nearby players, so suicide is possible [Fan-wiki: Gamma Ray].
  - The explosion animation "is the same as the Piece Maker and Phat Man", and its size scales with the victim [Fan-wiki].
  - The wiki contradicts itself on the threshold: Status Effects says maximum radiation is 100 RP, while the Gamma Ray and Radiation Rod pages say it equals the target's maximum health [Fan-wiki].
- **What the trailer shows (9.0–9.6 s):**
  - The victim pops into separate toy-like orange body pieces (head and limbs) inside a white-hot burst with yellow edges (#FEF34A).
  - A white hexagonal shockwave ring spreads across the floor [Observed: f_023–f_029; crops/c_f023.png].
  - That this is the radiation kill is [Inference]: the trefoil appeared 0.3 s earlier and Graham was firing the Gamma Ray.
- **2012 beta:** after a kill, with the Gamma Ray equipped, a pale yellow-white puffy cloud and grey smoke hang over the field [Observed: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/U3XDJXo1Ej4/maxresdefault.jpg; crops/u3_maxres_crop.png]. That this is a radiation detonation is [Inference].
- **Turret immunity:** the Turret is immune to status effects [Fan-wiki: Turret].

### Slowed status (Bind Field)
- "a yellow Z will appear over the player's head, indicating a reduction in speed" [Fan-wiki: Status Effects].
- Slow Field (the Bind Field's original name) is on that list. Black Hole is not [Fan-wiki].

## Power Glove (primary, default; BBR v1.4 Nov 28, 2012 through BBG; 2026 status unverified)
- **Official text:** "The precursor to the O.C.O. Glove, this weapon can rapidly fire small orbs of energy. When charged up, it delivers a larger and more destructive orb that explodes on impact. This weapon can HANDle any situation." [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Power_Glove)
- **History:**
  - Default primary since v1.4 [Fan-wiki].
  - The 2012 primary list shows only "GAMMA RAY" and "POWER GLOVE / EQUIPPED" [Observed: File:Power_glove.png, uploaded 2012-11-29].
  - BBR 1.4.2 cut the charge time from 1.8 to 1.5 s. The wiki dates the release Feb 28, 2013 and compares builds from Feb 28 and Mar 1 [Fan-wiki: BBR Version 1.4.2].
  - The page now says the charge takes 2.25 s [Fan-wiki]. The stats have drifted.
  - 2026 status [Unverified].
- **Look:**
  - An oversized robotic glove over his right hand (viewer's left in the loadout render). It has three chunky fingers and a thumb [Observed].
  - Pale grey shell, about #7F8A87 in shade and #959F9E lit, with dark knuckle joints [Observed].
  - A glowing cyan hexagonal core on the back of the hand (#33F0F2), cyan circuit traces, and a cyan dot on each fingertip [Observed].
  - Fingertips to cuff it is about 0.45 H, and the palm is about as wide as his head [Observed, approximate; held toward the camera].
  - His other hand keeps its red glove [Observed: File:Power_Glove.jpg, File:Power_glove.png].
  - A 2013 in-match screenshot shows a red-team Graham raising the same white glove with cyan fingertip dots [Observed: File:4_mines_one_is_hidden.jpg].
  - Concept "Graham's Self-Enhanced Hand": a chrome-grey glove with cyan lights [Observed: File:Graham_glove.png]. The class page captions it "Power Glove Concept" [Fan-wiki]. SkyVu provenance is [Unverified].
- **Firing:**
  - Semi-automatic. Taps fire small orbs; holding builds a charge [Game text via wiki; Fan-wiki].
  - Full charge is "indicated by the appearance of a small energy ring formed around the Glove" [Fan-wiki].
  - A charged shot uses the whole magazine [Fan-wiki].
  - Cooldown is 0.1 s uncharged and 1.0 s charged; 100 ammo; 3.5 s reload [Fan-wiki].
  - Sound is undocumented [Unverified].
- **Projectile:** "small orbs of energy", or when charged "a larger and more destructive orb" [Game text via wiki]. They travel at 1,400 u/s [Fan-wiki]. No image of the orb was found, so its colour and trail are [Unverified]. Cyan is only a lead, taken from the glove's lights [Inference].
- **Impact / end result:**
  - An uncharged hit does 20 damage with no splash [Fan-wiki].
  - A charged orb "explodes on impact" [Game text via wiki]. It does 50 on a direct hit, plus 60 within 150 units and 30 at 150–300 units [Fan-wiki].
  - What the explosion looks like is [Unverified].
- **Images viewed:** File:Power_Glove.jpg, File:Power_glove.png, File:Graham_glove.png, File:4_mines_one_is_hidden.jpg.

Holding grows a ring of light around the glove [Fan-wiki], then a big orb pops into a ring of sparks. Orb and burst colour (cyan-white) and the spark burst are [BBG News invention].

- **Official text:** "Turn your enemies in to walking explosives with this radioactive ray gun. It does very little damage on initial impact, but once you charge them up all the way...BOOM! The radioactive symbol over their head indicates how charged they are. The bigger they are, the longer it takes to blow them up, however yielding a much larger and more aggressive explosion." [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Gamma_Ray)
- **History:**
  - Shipped in v1.4 [Fan-wiki].
  - It is the primary in SkyVu's own video "BATTLE BEARS ROYALE Update 1.4 Beta - Engineer Class (Graham)" (U3XDJXo1Ej4, channel "BATTLE BEARS", published Oct 31, 2012, 170 s). The video description reads: "Battle Bears Royale (BBR) Update 1.4 is in beta testing now! Get a sneak peak at the all new class being added, the Engineer (aka Graham)." [Canon: oEmbed and wiki file metadata]. The beta loadout is Gamma Ray / Turret / T Square [Observed: File:Graham-gamma-ray.png].
  - BBR 1.4.2: reload 4.0 to 3.6 s [Fan-wiki].
  - BBG 2.6 (Dec 20, 2013): damage 1 to 2 and clip 120 to 80 [Fan-wiki: Versions/2.6]. The infobox now says damage 1 and ammo 100 [Fan-wiki], which conflicts.
  - Clip counters I saw on HUDs [Observed]:
    - 164/250 in the 2012 beta (U3XDJXo1Ej4 maxres).
    - /120 in a Jan 3, 2014 fan clip (0GuhKiAnNyc).
    - 100/100 and 0/100 in 2026 closed-playtest #3 chapter thumbnails, where the weapon identification is [Inference] (crops/qt_combo.png).
  - Ammo equipment changes the maximum, so these counters don't settle the base value [Inference].
  - **2026:** the Engineer chapter thumbnail (34:52) of JustNubz's "BBG All Classes Showcase ... (from Closed Playtest #4)" shows Graham in the Old School skin holding the Gamma Ray on the loadout screen [Observed: crops/jn_ch_engineer_3x.png, from https://i.ytimg.com/vi/-lAtv-j9Sms/hqdefault_2092166.jpg].
- **Look:**

  - Vent slots, three green LED dots and a glowing green core strip or lens: #66FF08 in 2012, #37B328 on the card strip [Observed].
  - A black octagonal muzzle ring gripped by orange clamp-like prongs [Observed].
  - The 2012 beta frame shows a small radiation-trefoil sticker on top [Observed].
  - Held two-handed at the hip. It is about as big as his head, roughly 0.25–0.3 H long [Observed, approximate].

  - "The end of the projectile emits a type of smoke-like vapour." [Fan-wiki]
  - What I saw: pale-gold concentric arcs (#FDF4B2 to #FEF446) ripple out from the muzzle like sound waves [Observed: trailer 8.1–8.6 s, crops/beam_seq.png; fan thumbnails https://i.ytimg.com/vi/0GuhKiAnNyc/maxresdefault.jpg and hq1.jpg].

- **Projectile:** the arcing ripples themselves; there are no bullets [Observed + Fan-wiki].
- **Impact / end result:**

  - The target takes radiation, and the yellow disc over its head loses wedges. At full radiation: "BOOM!" [Game text via wiki]. See the shared radiation section.
  - In the trailer the target glows yellow and white sparkles burst off it from 7.9 to 8.6 s [Observed]. That the yellow tint is an irradiation glow is [Inference].

## Rivet Gun (primary; BBG 2.1, Jul 15, 2013)
- **Official text:** "Not just for fixing. This weapon shoots electrified rivets that stick into walls and create a ball of death!" [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Rivet_Gun)
- **History:**
  - It is pictured in the "BB 1.4.3 COMING SOON" promo [Observed: File:1.4.3Weapons_B.png, uploaded 2013-04-09; SkyVu provenance Inference].
  - Its release was "delayed to make improvements to its function". The original design had rivets "connect to each other using beams to form a kind of damage-dealing barrier" [Fan-wiki].
  - The wiki's Updates row for 2.1 does not list it [Fan-wiki]. But on 2013-07-17, two days after 2.1 shipped, the page changed from "It will come in in v2.1" to "released in Update 2.1" [Fan-wiki: revision history, revids 45659 and 45678]. So 2.1 is likely [Inference].
  - That July 2013 revision said the fields "last for about 20 seconds", that rivets "can only stick to the ground", and that the "base ammo clip" was 3 [Fan-wiki, early].
  - BBG 2.6: reload 6 to 4 s and "Time to Live" 10 to 7 s [Fan-wiki].
- **Look:**
  - A long boxy gun with a dark olive-brown body (about #72685B) and orange-brown bands (#805432).
  - A coil spring along the top and a grey canister under the barrel, banded in orange with an orange emblem.
  - A silver tube muzzle in a hex-nut-shaped housing, a black-tipped upright lever or handle, and a black hose looping from the rear to the canister [Observed: File:Rosie.png turnaround, crops/rosie3x.png; File:1.4.3Weapons_B.png].
  - The emblem is too low-resolution to call a hazard symbol [Observed].
  - Two loose canisters with silver nozzle caps sit beside the turnaround. They are probably spare rivet canisters [Inference].
  - Graham aims it forward at chest height. The muzzle housing is about 0.11 H across, and the gun is roughly 0.4 H long [Observed, approximate: File:Rivet_Gun.jpg].
- **Firing:** 4 per clip, 1 s rate, 4 s reload, 1,600 u/s [Fan-wiki]. The muzzle effect is [Unverified].
- **Projectile:** "electrified rivets" [Game text via wiki]. What they look like is [Unverified].
- **Impact / end result:**
  - Rivets "land on the ground or any wall and immediately form light purple energy fields around their landing area, which last for 7 seconds. The rivets themselves cannot directly damage players." [Fan-wiki]
  - Damage is higher toward the centre, 30 DPS [Fan-wiki].
  - The fields "are in fact square when viewed from above" despite the "ball of death" text [Fan-wiki].
  - No image of a field was found [Unverified].
- **Images viewed:** File:Rivet_Gun.jpg, File:Rosie.png, File:1.4.3Weapons_B.png.
- **Depiction for shorts:** a rivet thunks into the floor or a wall, crackles, and blooms into a light-purple electric box-shaped field [Fan-wiki colour and shape]. Anyone inside gets zapped with harmless sparks until it fizzles out after about 7 s. The spark style is [BBG News invention].
- **Confidence:** partial.

## Turret (secondary, default; BBR v1.4 through BBG; BBH "Turret Summon" is a different game)

- **History:**
  - One of the v1.4 originals [Fan-wiki]. It appears in the 2012 secondary list [Observed: File:Turret.png] and the beta loadout [Observed: File:Graham-gamma-ray.png].
  - The wiki's first revision (Nov 2, 2012, pre-release, IP edit) said: "Graham lays the turret on the ground as the turret rises up. It shoots the same bullets as the Electric Honey."
