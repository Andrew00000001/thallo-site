# Graham (Engineer): BBG weapons dossier

Graham is BBG's Engineer. The wiki calls the class "a defensive class, due to dependence on static weapons for support on the field" with "relatively slow movement speed" [Fan-wiki: Engineer (BBG)]. Its class template carries this blurb: "Don't be fooled by his bushy gray mustache, this class relies more on brains than brawn or speed. This wise whiskered bear specializes in defending areas of the map and controlling enemy traffic." [Fan-wiki template; probably the in-game class text, Unverified].

His BBG arsenal is **17 weapons**: 6 primaries, 7 secondaries and 4 melees [Fan-wiki: Engineer (BBG) weapon list]. `weapons_by_class.json` has only 15. MuskIt and Bind Field are missing because their pages lack Category:BBG Weapons [Fan-wiki].

## Shared visual systems

### The in-game Graham who holds these weapons (2012–2015 BBG model)
- **Model** [Observed: File:Power_glove.png, File:Gamma_ray.png, File:Hand_Cannon.png, File:Slowfield.png]:
  - an orange hard hat with dark grooves
  - blue-framed goggles with glowing cyan lenses
  - a white handlebar moustache
  - dark-grey fur with a light-grey belly
  - a boxy radio-style backpack with an antenna and a side speaker grille
  - red gloves, a red sash, and a red-and-yellow tool belt with a silver buckle
  - white stripe bands on the arms
- **Team colour:** the backpack and gloves take the team colour. Red is the default. They are teal-blue in SkyVu's beta-video thumbnail (https://i.ytimg.com/vi/U3XDJXo1Ej4/hq2.jpg) and on the in-game "GI GRAHAM" bundle banner (File:GIgramhambundle.png) [Observed].
- **Weapon hand:** most one-handed weapons are held in his right hand (viewer's left in front-facing renders) [Observed].
- **Outlines:** the 2026 closed-playtest thumbnails and the trailer footage both draw black toon outlines on characters [Observed: ch_qt43iTdANbM_491866.jpg; g_004.png]. The 2012–2015 loadout renders have none [Observed].

### Radiation status and detonation (Gamma Ray, Gernobyl, MuskIt, Radiation Rod)
- **Wiki description:** "a yellow circle will appear over the player's head and a noise from a Geiger counter will begin sounding ... As the intake increases, three portions of the circle will dissolve into the common radioactivity symbol, where further exposure will result in death." [Fan-wiki: Status Effects]. All four Engineer radiation weapons are on that page's "Irradiated" list [Fan-wiki].
- **Icon stages:**
  - The wiki's four icons step from a full 6-wedge disc (Hazard-3, 0–24%) to 5 wedges (Hazard-2) to 4 (Hazard-1) to the 3-blade trefoil (Hazard-0, 75–99%) [Observed: File:Hazard-3.png to File:Hazard-0.png; percentage bands Fan-wiki].
  - The wiki's icons are black silhouettes on a transparent background, not game colours [Observed: pixel check].
- **In-game icon colours** [Observed]:
  - Early stage: a bright yellow disc (#FCFE13) split into wedges by thin dark lines, with a dark hub. Jan 2014 fan clip, one wedge already missing [https://i.ytimg.com/vi/0GuhKiAnNyc/hq2.jpg].
  - Late stage: three yellow-orange blades (#FFC30B) on a black disc (#190C01), i.e. the classic trefoil [g_008.png at 8.25 s; g_010.png at 8.75 s].
  - The 2013 HUD also shows the yellow-and-black trefoil at the screen edge while standing in goo [File:Radioactive_puddle.jpeg].
- **Sound:** a Geiger-counter tick that gets "faster and faster - this sound is audible to all players nearby" [Fan-wiki: Gamma Ray].
- **Decay:** about 3–5 RP/s once exposure stops, and the icon steps back [Fan-wiki].
- **Detonation:**
  - At full radiation the target "will die instantly in a relatively large explosion" with a 150–200 unit radius that damages nearby players, so suicide is possible [Fan-wiki].
  - The explosion "is the same as the Piece Maker and Phat Man", and its size scales with the victim [Fan-wiki].
  - The wiki conflicts on the threshold: Status Effects says 100 RP, while the Gamma Ray and Radiation Rod pages say it equals the target's maximum health [Fan-wiki].
- **What a detonation looks like in the Steam trailer** [Observed: g_011–g_015, 9.0–10.0 s]:
  - The target pops into toy-like orange body pieces (head, limbs).
  - They fly out of a blocky white-hot cloud with yellow (#FFE83C / #FFF5AA) and orange (#FF9222) edges.
  - A white hexagonal shockwave ring spreads across the floor.
  - That this is the radiation kill is [Inference]: the trefoil was showing and Graham was firing the Gamma Ray.
- **Other detonation sightings:**
  - 2012 beta: with the Gamma Ray equipped and a fresh kill in the feed, a puffy pale-yellow/white cloud with grey smoke hangs over the field [Observed: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/U3XDJXo1Ej4/maxresdefault.jpg]; that this is a detonation is [Inference].
  - Jan 2014 fan clip: the kill frame is washed yellow-white [Observed: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/0GuhKiAnNyc/hq3.jpg].
- **Turret immunity:** the Turret is immune to status effects [Fan-wiki: Turret].

### Slowed status (Bind Field)
- "a yellow Z will appear over the player's head, indicating a reduction in speed" [Fan-wiki: Status Effects].
- "Slow Field" (the Bind Field's original name) is on that list. Black Hole is not [Fan-wiki].

## Power Glove (primary; default; BBR v1.4, Nov 28, 2012, through BBG; 2026 unconfirmed)
- **Official text:** "The precursor to the O.C.O. Glove, this weapon can rapidly fire small orbs of energy. When charged up, it delivers a larger and more destructive orb that explodes on impact. This weapon can HANDle any situation." [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Power_Glove)
- **Era / status:**
  - Default primary [Fan-wiki infobox].
  - One of the v1.4 launch six [Fan-wiki: Updates]. The 2012 primary list shows "GAMMA RAY" and "POWER GLOVE / EQUIPPED" [Observed: File:Power_glove.png, uploaded 2012-11-29].
  - BBR 1.4.2 (released Feb 28, 2013) changed "Charge time ... from 1.8 to 1.5 seconds" [Fan-wiki: BBR Version 1.4.2]. The page now says 2.25 s [Fan-wiki], so the stats have drifted.
  - It is in the "Groundhog Day" bundle [Fan-wiki: Bundle Deals].
  - 2026: not seen [Unverified].
- **Look:**
  - An oversized robotic glove worn over his right hand. Three chunky fingers and a thumb, a pale-grey shell (#77827F median in the render's lighting) and dark knuckle joints [Observed: File:Power_Glove.jpg, File:Power_glove.png].
  - A glowing cyan hexagonal core on the back of the hand (#27E5E9, with a darker teal rim #127377), cyan circuit traces, and a cyan dot on each fingertip [Observed].
  - Size: about 0.45 H from fingertip to cuff, and the palm is as wide as his head. It is held up toward the camera, so it looks bigger than it is [Observed, approximate].
  - His other hand keeps its red glove [Observed].
  - A 2013 in-match screenshot shows a Graham (brown radio backpack) raising the same white glove with cyan fingertip lights [Observed: File:4_mines_one_is_hidden.jpg].
  - Concept "Graham's Self-Enhanced Hand": a chrome-grey mechanical hand with cyan lights [Observed: File:Graham_glove.png]. The class page gallery captions it "Power Glove Concept" [Fan-wiki]. SkyVu provenance is [Unverified].
- **Firing:**
  - Semi-automatic. Taps fire small orbs, and holding builds a charge [Game text via wiki; Fan-wiki].
  - Full charge is "indicated by the appearance of a small energy ring formed around the Glove" [Fan-wiki].
  - A charged shot "will be considered using a full magazine of ammo" [Fan-wiki].
  - Cooldown 0.1 s uncharged and 1.0 s charged; 100 ammo; 3.5 s reload [Fan-wiki].
  - The 2012 stat panel shows DAMAGE / EXPLOSIVES / RELOAD / AOE bars [Observed: File:Power_glove.png].
  - Sound [Unverified].
- **Projectile:**
  - "small orbs of energy", or when charged "a larger and more destructive orb" [Game text via wiki], travelling at 1,400 u/s [Fan-wiki].
  - No image of an orb exists, so its colour and trail are [Unverified]. Cyan is only a lead, taken from the glove's lights [Inference].
- **Impact / end result:**
  - An uncharged hit does 20 damage with no splash [Fan-wiki].
  - A charged orb "explodes on impact" [Game text via wiki]: 50 on a direct hit, plus 60 within 150 units and 30 at 150–300 units [Fan-wiki].
  - What the explosion looks like is [Unverified].

## Rivet Gun (primary; BBG 2.1, Jul 15, 2013, likely)
