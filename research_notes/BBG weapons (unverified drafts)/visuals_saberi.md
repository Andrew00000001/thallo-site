# Saberi (Combat Tech) weapons dossier

Saberi is Battle Bears Gold's Combat Tech. She was added in update 2.4 on Oct 24, 2013 [Canon: 2.4 notes, "New Combat Tech Class: Saberi"].
- **How her kit works:** she holds one primary in her paws. A jointed "third arm" on her backpack deploys her secondary for a timed window [Game text via wiki: Missiles; Fan-wiki: Combat Tech (BBG)]. Her melee weapons are hammers or a club.
- **Kit size:** the BBG kit is 5 primaries, 4 secondaries and 3 melees [Fan-wiki class page]. Battle Bears Heroes (BBH, 2024) recasts her as a Medic with two new weapons [Canon].

## Class-wide visual rules (apply to every weapon below)
- **The third arm:**
  - In game it is a thin khaki-grey rod arm with rounded tan/brass joint caps (≈#61563B shade, #756443 lit). It reaches from behind her back to hold the secondary beside her head [Observed: P/SideMissiles.png, P/SideMinigun.png, P/Landmine.png].
  - SkyVu's 2014 art shows the same khaki jointed arm (joint ≈#61553A, lit #998861) [Observed: W/dl/saberi/official/prior/saberi29.png].
  - The wiki says the secondaries "emerge from the right side of the Combat Tech's backpack" [Fan-wiki: Missiles].
- **Signature glow:**
  - Every glowing Combat Tech part is magenta/hot pink, not violet. Samples: PMG core ≈#E301E9-#F301F7, Minigun barrel ≈#A8009A-#C000B0, Plasma Shotgun crystal ≈#DB31E3, Gamma Burst rim ≈#C138CD [Observed, sampled from P/ images].
  - The 2014 art reads ≈#DB3AE7 to #FB47FE [Observed: saberi29.png].
  - Exceptions: the Landmine's cyan circuitry (≈#31A3A7) and the Toxic Rifle's pink/lilac liquids [Observed].
- **Secondary system:**
  - Tapping the secondary deploys it for a set time. The button "will turn blue and not respond to tapping", then the weapon recharges [Fan-wiki: Missiles; Minigun (Combat Tech); Gamma Burst].
  - She is "the only class that cannot switch weapons at all and the only class which adds a button to the HUD" [Fan-wiki: Combat Tech (BBG)].
  - In fan footage the secondary button shows a cyan ring when ready or active, and a red-and-green progress ring while recharging [Observed: e.g. T/jnkMl7m2qmk sd frames, T/wAQLCQDHUZk_maxres1.jpg; the meaning of the ring is Inference].
- **Status icons used by her weapons:**
  - Irradiated: a yellow disc (≈#EAC404 in 2014, ≈#FBFA3C in 2026) split into wedges around a black centre floats over the target [Observed: T/Rn_X7MA23ho_maxres1.jpg; T/Q46ZMPrChm4_maxres1.jpg; T/s9W6eLXj9qc_maxres3.jpg; official SkyVu footage T/wUWf9O-YX4A_maxres2.jpg].
  - The wiki's account of Irradiated: the icon "dissolve[s] into the common radioactivity symbol" in 4 stages; a Geiger-counter ticking speeds up; at 100 RP the target explodes "regardless of any health remaining" (blast radius 150-200 units); radiation decays at about 3-5 RP/s [Fan-wiki: Status Effects].
  - Poisoned: "a purple/green skull will appear over the player's head" [Fan-wiki]. Observed as a dark purple skull (≈#3A0130 to #4B124A) [Observed: T/JO5-kfnENtM_maxresdefault.jpg; official footage T/xp5B76ExuAI_maxres2.jpg, where the skull belongs to another class's poison].
- **Melee contact:** yellow 5-point cartoon stars scatter around a target struck at close range [Observed: T/n84q9Ptu13c_maxres3.jpg, 2015; that this was a Saberi melee hit is Inference]. This matches the shared Battle Bears Gold (BBG) melee-star convention in the combat-VFX dossier.
- **2026:**
  - A 2026 playtest thumbnail shows the default polka-dot Saberi with the Plasma Shotgun and Gamma Burst [Observed: T/s9W6eLXj9qc_maxres3.jpg. That is M1kaa717's "BATTLE BEARS GOLD 2026 FINAL BETA HIGHLIGHTS", Oct 16, 2026. The corner build stamp starts "3.0.7"; it is low-res].
  - No other 2026 source shows her weapons.
  - The official Steam trailer and screenshots re-use 2013 launch footage that predates her. She is absent from them [Observed: D/sheets/trailer_01.png, trailer_02.png, steam_01.png].
  - The JustNubz showcase chapter "44:12 Combat Tech (Saberi)" [Canon-free fan metadata, from W/dl/2026-changes/yt/next_-lAtv-j9Sms.json] is unwatched.
