# Botch, the Assassin: weapons dossier (BBG 2013–2021, 2026 playtests, BBH 2024)

Botch is the Assassin class of Battle Bears Gold. He was added in BBG 2.1 (July 15, 2013 [Fan-wiki: Updates]). SkyVu's store text read "New in 2.1: - New Character Class! ASSASSIN: Botch" [Canon, archived Google Play 2013-08-15, quoted in /home/user/thallo-site/research_notes/Battle Bears character lore/huggable_botch_b1000.md; not re-fetched today]. The 2026 Steam page still lists "-ASSASSIN: Botch" and "80+ PRIMARY WEAPONS / 60+ SECONDARY WEAPONS / 50+ MELEES" [Canon, Steam appdetails API for app 4408320, fetched 2026-10-16]. He has 15 BBG weapons: 7 primaries, 5 secondaries and 3 melees. He also has one BB Heroes-only attack, the Doom Star.

## Class-wide visual rules
- **Construct style.** Most of his weapons are floating, translucent, glowing lime energy constructs. Each hovers just off one paw and is never gripped. Examples: the hand, fist, spiked ball, cloud, orbs, disc and flame. Sampled construct lime is #A8FE20–#B4FE22 [Observed: ChaosTossCR, CrassKnucklesCR, DoomCloudCR].
  - Some are deeper green: Ball N Pain #4ADB0F, Fear Orbit #A7F533, Gravity Well disc #B9FD0A [Observed; sampled].
- **Blasts are greener and cooler than the constructs:**
  - Cosmic Bomb blast: #5EF14A–#6BFD6A.
  - Gravity Well dome: #72E464, with a cyan #55FDEA inner shell.
  - [Observed; sampled.]
- **Yellow accents:**
  - Orbs in play: #F6FC37.
  - Cosmic Bomb falling comet: #FBFD75 with a pale cyan tail, #98F4FB.
  - Tips of the Doom Cloud needles.
  - [Observed.]
- **Exceptions to the lime rule:**
  - Pain Field is pink to magenta [Observed; the Fan-wiki agrees].
  - Hazard Toy is dark bottle-green, #1D501F, and its poison puffs are purple, #53246D [Observed].
  - Tonguechuks is a physical black, red, silver and green object [Observed].
  - The one Energy Bombs blast I saw is an orange-yellow fireball [Observed; attribution Inference].
- **Status icons.** The wiki says poisoned players get "a purple/green skull" overhead [Fan-wiki: Status Effects]. I saw both colours:
  - Yellow-green, sack-shaped skull blobs (#E3F47C) over enemies in a Fear Orbit context.
  - Purple skull-faced puffs and bubbles in a Hazard Toy context.

It appears in NLUd2D8csII hq1 (Nova), wR_l6LC13LU hq1 (Ball N Pain), 8WG4_WXJy1U hq3 (Chaos Toss) and vG31XcG7yT8 hq1 (Doom Cloud, 2026) [Observed]. Its source is [Unverified]. ## Doom Cloud (primary; default since BBG 2.1, July 2013; seen in 2026 playtests; BBH Super)
- **Official text:**
  - "This cute little thing is hiding a deadly secret. Once it's out a myriad of lasers create a cloud of doom around itself, but it sure is adorable, huh?" [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Doom_Cloud).
  - BBH: "Botch's "Doom Star" acts as a boomerang, potentially dealing double damage, while his "Doom Cloud" Super unleashes a bullet-raining cloud for widespread damage." [Canon] (https://battlebears.com/pages/characters, re-fetched live 2026-10-16).
- **Status:**
  - Level 1, "Price: Default"; 45 DPS, duration 4 s, ammo 1, reload 5.5 s; Damage+1/+2 add 2.5/5 [Fan-wiki infobox].
  - A 2.1-labelled loadout screen shows "PRIMARY / DOOM CLOUD" with its starfish icon [Observed: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/3LlUZgQWmWQ/hq3.jpg, "BATTLE BEARS GOLD "BOTCH" FIRST LOOKS AND REVIEW", rockstartanwille].
  - No rework is recorded.
  - **2026:**
    - The HUD shows the Doom Cloud icon at 0/1, with health 85/85 [Observed: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/vG31XcG7yT8/hq1.jpg and hq3.jpg, JustNubz "Battle Bears Gold Closed Playtest #2 Match 1", about Aug 2026].
    - Default Botch stands in the menu with the Doom Cloud beside him [Observed: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/Rt0mWn6rk0c/hqdefault.jpg, "1 hour of unedited BATTLE BEARS GOLD playtest gameplay video"].
  - **BBH 1.0.12 (Oct 3, 2024):** "Botch – Attack Damage Increased (+15%); Ultimate Charge Rate from Ultimate use Reduced (-50%)" [Fan-wiki: Updates].
- **Look:**
  - The idle cloud is a translucent, jelly-like lime blob with about five drooping, outlined starfish points. Lime is #B0F524–#B4FE22 [Observed: DoomCloudCR.png; c8HwXqm9_Rc maxres; sampled].
  - It is about 0.6–0.8 Botch-heads tall [Observed estimate]. It floats at shoulder height beside him, or low in front of him in third-person play.
  - Wiki: "follows the player around as a floating mass of green substance" [Fan-wiki].
  - **HUD/loadout icon:** a bright lime starfish splat topped by a small white dome (#63FF10–#BDFF10) [Observed: Doom-cloud-navi-icon.png, 70×54; 2.1 loadout; 2026 HUD].
- **Firing:**
  - "the Assassin launches a green starfish-like cloud into the air. It spins around and releases a rain of neon green bullets" [Fan-wiki].
  - "it rotates then raises itself into the air under the Assassin's direction" [Fan-wiki]. The cloud's height sets how wide the rain spreads [Fan-wiki].
  - It follows him for its duration. He cannot melee or switch weapons while it is active [Fan-wiki].
  - In a 2026 thumbnail, a flattened lime splat hangs at the very top of the frame while streaks fall from it [Observed: vG31XcG7yT8 hq1; that this is the raised cloud is Inference].
- **Projectile:**
  - Long, tapering lime needles slant down to the floor and yellow toward their tips. They leave small yellow V-shaped splashes where they hit [Observed: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/c8HwXqm9_Rc/hq3.jpg (Jan 2014); vG31XcG7yT8 hq1/hq3 (2026)]. That these are Doom Cloud bullets is [Inference] from the Doom Cloud HUD icon at 0/1 in the same frames.
  - The bullets "disappear on contact with solid surfaces", yet the cloud still hurts players under roofs [Fan-wiki].
- **Impact / end result:**
  - Damage only: no status effect and no explosion [Fan-wiki]. Killed enemies fly apart [Observed: c8HwXqm9_Rc hq3].
  - At the end, "the cloud lowers itself to the ground and peters out in a burst of green light" [Fan-wiki].
  - It "immediately self-destructs" when Botch dies. Touching a solid surface or passing through a platform destroys it at once [Fan-wiki].
  - Glitch: it keeps firing into the reload animation, "making it look that Botch has two Doom Clouds" [Fan-wiki].

## Cosmic Fire (primary; 60 Gas, Level 1; added 2.3, Sept 27, 2013; changed in 2.6)
- **Official text:** "Sometimes "killing it with fire" is the only way to deal with something, amirite?" [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Cosmic_Fire)
- **Status:**
  - 65.0 DPS, with "testing reveals inconsistent results"; ammo 1; reload 5 s; Damage+1/+2 add 7.5/15 [Fan-wiki].
  - Added in 2.3 [Fan-wiki: Updates].
  - **2.6:** "Duration increased from 5 to 10", "Reload speed increased from 3 to 5" [Fan-wiki: Versions/2.6]. The weapon page says it ceases "after 5 seconds" with "a 5 second reload" [Fan-wiki]. This conflict is unresolved.
  - It has a positive Health Modifier [Fan-wiki: Weapon Statistics]. Not seen in 2026 [Unverified].
- **Look:**
  - A flame cupped in his upturned palm: a white-hot core, lime outer tongues (#89C420 to #B6FA23), and small teal-cyan sparks rising [Observed: CosmicFire.png; sampled]. It is about 0.5 Botch-heads tall.
  - The identical flame appears in DefaultBotch2.jpg and in seven skin shots uploaded the same day (2013-10-27) by the same user: Brown, Dry Bones, Emo, Green, Jester, Purple and White Botch [Observed]. **It is this weapon, not part of his base look.**
  - **HUD icon:** a lime flame [Observed: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/aPELJZs3Wc8/hq1.jpg, LXSXL "Fun With Cosmic Fire"; https://i.ytimg.com/vi/6i--TMJwBOw/hq2.jpg].
