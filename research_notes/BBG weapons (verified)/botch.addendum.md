# Botch: Doom Star addendum (BB Heroes only)

This addendum covers one gap: what Botch's **Doom Star** looks like. The Doom Star is his basic attack ("Attack") in **Battle Bears Heroes (BBH)**: soft launch May 23, 2023, global launch Oct 3, 2024 [Fan-wiki]. It does not exist in BBR (2012) or BBG (2013–2021), and nothing I saw puts it in the BBG 2026 re-release [Unverified]. The main dossier still holds for everything else.

## Doom Star (BBH basic attack; BB Heroes only, 2024–present)

**Official text**
- "Botch, a bear of very few words with a mysterious past, boasts powerful radioactive powers. Known for his fearsome ranged abilities alongside swift movement speed, he's a formidable foe. Yet, his significant weakness lies in his low health. Botch's "Doom Star" acts as a boomerang, potentially dealing double damage, while his "Doom Cloud" Super unleashes a bullet-raining cloud for widespread damage." [Canon] (https://battlebears.com/pages/characters, re-fetched 2026-10-18)
- Store text in both stores lists "Botch the Toxic Assassin" [Canon]. The wiki says he "was formerly dubbed 'Botch the Toxic Assassin'" and is now "Botch the Assassin" [Fan-wiki].

**Status and era**
- Wiki BBH table columns are "Attack | Ultimate". Botch's row reads Attack "Doom Star", Ultimate "Doom Cloud", price "1200 Shards", release "Spring 2024" [Fan-wiki].
- Wiki Doom Star stats: Damage "2 x 730", Range "Short", Reload Speed "Very slow" [Fan-wiki].
- Wiki Doom Cloud (Ultimate) stats: "Damage per second" 48, Range "Long" [Fan-wiki].
- Official teaser "New BATTLE BEARS game launching soon!" (eQBsn_45x5w, Jul 3, 2024): its auto-frame 1 shows default Botch under a "NEW HERO" card [Observed]. This sits a little later than the wiki's "Spring 2024" [Inference].
- Balance change: "Botch – Attack Damage Increased (+15%)" and "Ultimate Charge Rate from Ultimate use Reduced (-50%)" [Fan-wiki: Updates].
  - The wiki dates this balance row **October 14, 2024**, under version 1.0.12, whose launch row is Oct 3, 2024.
  - "Attack" here is the Doom Star [Inference].
- No rework is recorded [Fan-wiki].

**Look**
- **What is observed.** In every official BBH render and menu shot of Botch, one weapon-like prop floats beside him [Observed]: a glowing lime "star". The sightings:
  - The official BBH lineup render. It appears as File:BBH_Splash.png and in the auto-frames of o0qNL2DHufw (default and 1), b6K9Z9mVskU (1), ISutMIXTUc8 (3) and ihkXJS4Oatk (2 and 3).
  - The BBH key art (File:BBH_KeyArt.png; also Google Play screenshot 8), where Botch flies in the sky beside the star.
  - The store "HUNDREDS OF SKINS" screenshot (Google Play ss_12; iPad `BBHsep24GRsshotiPad_150Skins.png`).
  - The Jul 3, 2024 "NEW HERO" card.
  - The official short "Top 5 Botch #skins" (6a55EdwBmZM).
- **That this prop is the Doom Star is [Inference], fairly strong:**
  - It is the only weapon-like prop BBH ever shows with Botch.
  - In the same lineup the other heroes hold their basic-attack weapons (Riggs a minigun, Wil a pistol).
  - It is literally star-shaped.
  - Against this: no official caption names it, and it is a remodel of his BBG Doom Cloud blob (see below).
- **Shape** [Observed]:
  - A domed centre with four visible arms, probably five with one hidden [Inference]. The arms splay down and out like a starfish draped over a ball and end in blunt, cut-off tips.
  - Pale outline seams run along the arms, with jagged zig-zag "crack" seams on the dome.
  - Seen low and from the side (store screenshot), it reads as a wide, flat, five-pointed star.
  - Seen side-on (lineup), it reads as a dome with three or four claw-like points.
- **Same design family as the 2013 BBG Doom Cloud** [Observed comparison]. In the wiki's DoomCloudCR.jpg and Botch_Skin.jpg, the old Doom Cloud is the same domed, drooping-armed lime blob floating at his shoulder. The BBH version is crisper, yellower and glows more.
- **Colours** (sampled; approximate):
  - In-game skin screen (glowing): median #F8FE4C, brightest #FDFE83, most saturated #EBFD41, with a soft green halo. The Jul 2024 "NEW HERO" card samples median #F2FF4F [Observed].
  - Official 3D render (lineup splash, no bloom): body median #B4D960, pale seam highlight #CBE181, shadow #6BA13B [Observed].
  - Lime sparkle dots trail from it and fall below it [Observed: ss_12, iPad screenshot, 6a55EdwBmZM default frame, eQBsn_45x5w frame 1].
- **Size** [Observed estimates]:
  - About 0.5–0.75 of Botch's head width. Store screenshot: star body ≈153×71 px against a face ≈205 px wide. iPad: ≈195 px against ≈360 px from ear to ear. Splash: ≈104 px against a head of more than 190 px.
  - On the BBG News chart (Oliver = 1.0x), that is roughly 0.2–0.25x Oliver's height across [Inference].
- **How it is carried** [Observed]:
  - Nobody holds it. It hovers free at head or shoulder height, about one head-width from Botch.
  - In the lineup render it sits just past his raised fist, as if just tossed or about to be caught.
  - In menus it drifts around him: beside him (store screenshot), low and in front (NEW HERO card), and peeking up behind his head (fan Diakev clip aTq1yrlI-kM, frame 3).
- **Skin variant (fan image)** [Observed in fan thumbnail]:
  - S8 KBW_Overwatch's "Botch The Plague Doctor Gameplay" (_vc1P7jwWEc, Feb 16, 2025) shows Plague Doctor Botch on the skin platform. In place of the star, a dark-green marbled sphere trails green smoke (median #2EB33F, dark #0BA020).
  - That this is the Plague Doctor version of the Doom Star is [Inference]; Astoria's BBH rifle also changes with her skin (Astoria addendum).

**Firing**
- "acts as a boomerang" [Canon]. Range "Short" and reload "Very slow" [Fan-wiki].
- BBH controls: "Slide RIGHT fire button to aim, then let go to fire. To Auto-Fire just tap on the RIGHT fire button." [Game text via wiki]
- A fan match frame shows a three-segment ammo bar under Botch's health [Observed: _vc1P7jwWEc auto-frame 3].
- The throw itself (arm motion, launch flash, sound) is **not observed** [Unverified].

**Projectile**
- What actually flies, how fast it spins, its path and its trail are **not observed** [Unverified].
- That the hovering star itself is thrown is [Inference].

**Impact / end result**
- "potentially dealing double damage" [Canon]. The wiki's "2 x 730" fits two hits, one on the way out and one on the way back [Inference].
- The hit visual is **not observed** [Unverified].
- **Two Botch-related BBH effects were seen, but neither can be pinned to the Doom Star:**
  1. **A lime whirlwind centred on Botch** [Observed]. A spiral of thin curved lime streaks (bright ≈#ADE145) about 4–5 Botch-heights across, with a yellow-white glow inside.
     - Where seen: official store screenshots "FAST 3v3 MODES" (Google Play ss_06) and "4 PvP MODES" (ss_14), where the bear at the centre has Botch's notched ear; and the official short 6a55EdwBmZM auto-frame 1, where player "DrippyCheeseYT" is at the centre.
     - Ammo clue: in the short, his ammo bar shows all three segments full [Observed]. So this is probably **not** a Doom Star throw; it may be the Doom Cloud Super being cast [Inference, weak].
  2. **A translucent lime-rimmed ground disc** [Observed: _vc1P7jwWEc auto-frame 3, fan, 2025].
     - It holds lime puddle splats and a bright green tentacle-burst on an enemy (1014 HP), above which six stacked "-70" damage numbers float.
     - Damage clue: many small ticks fit the Doom Cloud's "bullet-raining cloud" and DPS stat better than two hits of about 730 [Inference].
     - Ammo clue: Botch's ammo bar is nearly empty in the same frame [Observed], so recent Doom Star throws cannot be ruled out.
- The green cracked splash in the 2023 beta clip H1l15KqPZtg (uploaded by W3Gamer, not SkyVu) predates Botch's 2024 release, so it is not attributed to him [Inference].

**The SKILL tab the gap asked about**
- In the fan frames I opened, the hero screen's SKILL tab lists only Speed, Offensive, Defensive and "Ultimate <name>" [Observed]:
  - Jackson: "Ultimate Wheely Hot" (KLcjIqjf5sc frame 3, Sep 26, 2026).
  - Pish & Posh: "Ultimate Posh-Catching" (3hA9WatT6vo default frame).
- So Botch's SKILL tab would name the Doom Cloud, not the Doom Star [Inference]. Botch's own SKILL tab appears in none of the frames I opened.
