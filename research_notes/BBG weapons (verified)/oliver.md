# Oliver (Soldier): BBG weapons dossier (verified edition)

Oliver is the Soldier class in Battle Bears Gold. SkyVu's Steam copy lists him as "-SOLDIER: Oliver" [Canon, Steam appdetails 4408320, re-fetched today]. The wiki's Soldier (BBG)/Weapons table lists 21 BBG weapons for him: 9 primaries, 7 secondaries and 5 melees [Fan-wiki]. This dossier covers all 21. It also covers two extra topics:
- **Turkish SW Boots.** They are a Special that every class can use, but SkyVu names them alongside his defaults: "Grab your A Salt Rifle, Bearzooka and those Turkish SW Boots and fight it out with friends across beloved Battle Bears locations." [Canon, Steam].
- **The unidentified rifle Oliver carries in the Steam trailer** (2013 footage [Inference, strong]; see the era notes).

The 2026 Steam copy also promises "80+ PRIMARY WEAPONS", "60+ SECONDARY WEAPONS", "50+ MELEES" and "10 SPECIAL EQUIPMENT" [Canon]. None of SkyVu's 7 Steam news posts (Feb 24 to Oct 14, 2026) gives a Soldier weapon list, stat change or price [Canon, absence re-checked today].

## A Salt Rifle (ASR) (primary; default since BBR v1.0, 2011; named in SkyVu's 2026 Steam copy)
- **Official text:** "Standard pun rifle issued to all soldiers. Help it out by equipping extra damage equipment!" [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/A_Salt_Rifle). Steam: "Grab your A Salt Rifle, Bearzooka and those Turkish SW Boots..." [Canon].
- **Look:**
  - A sage/mint-green rifle (about #98B7A8, shade #527A71) with salmon-pink panels (about #9D6F6D), a ribbed green forend, a grey ribbed pistol grip, a thin silver barrel and a squared stock.
  - **On top is a white salt shaker (about #E3F8F1), fed through a curved, ribbed sleeve.**
  - Sources: [F]/png/Oliver3d.png (File:Oliver3d.png, Dec 2012) and [F]/png/A_salt_rifle.png (BBR store, Mar 25, 2012) [Observed].
  - Concept sheet ASRconcept.png: AR3 is a glass shaker fed through a ribbed hose on top, which is what shipped. AR1 already pairs a shaker with a big red ring, an early form of the HASR ring [Observed; the links are Inference].
  - Held two-handed at chest or hip; about 0.6x long [Inference].
  - Wiki icon "asr" (Template:WeaponLI code, sprite offset from MediaWiki:Common.css): a green/red rifle with a white shaker [Observed, [F]/crops/sprite_soldier.png].
  - **2026 (fan footage).** In the closed-playtest loadout menu ("SKIN NIGHT BEAR" on screen), Oliver holds a grey gun with a white block on top, upright beside his head [Observed, [K]/-lAtv-j9Sms_284966_w336.jpg, 4:44.966]. That matches File:Night.jpg (Oct 2014), where Night Bear holds the ASR with its shaker [Observed, [F]/png/Night.png]. That the 2026 gun is the ASR is an [Inference].
- **Firing:**
  - Stats: "Primary - Automatic", long range, "perfect accuracy"; damage 6, rate of fire 0.15 s, ammo 25, reload 3.0 s [Fan-wiki].
  - The Apr 2013 HUD reads 0/25, 17/25, 24/25 and 0/25 [Observed, [F]/yt/guopL_SYkYA_sddefault, sd1, sd2, sd3].
  - The Tutorial version has 70 ammo [Fan-wiki]. The Tutorial capture shows 70/70 with the ASR icon [Observed, [F]/png/Tutorial_BBG_.png].
  - The muzzle flash is a small yellow-orange star [Observed, sddefault]. Sound is undocumented.
- **Projectile:**
  - A thin pale line runs to the target in sd1 (17/25) [Observed]; that it is a tracer is an [Inference].
  - The name puns on salt, and the Sci-Fi ASR text contrasts lasers with "analog salt-dust" [Game text via wiki]. That the ASR "fires salt" is an [Inference].
- **Impact / end result:** A yellow-orange star pops on the struck enemy [Observed, sddefault; also the wiki thumbnail [F]/png3/Oliver_Primary_-_A_Salt_Rifle.png, 0/25]. No status effect [Fan-wiki].
- **Images viewed:** [F]/png/Oliver3d.png; [F]/png/ASRconcept.png; [F]/png/A_salt_rifle.png; [F]/png/Night.png; [F]/png/Tutorial_BBG_.png; [F]/png/Tutorial_2.png; [K]/-lAtv-j9Sms_284966_w336.jpg; https://i.ytimg.com/vi/guopL_SYkYA/{sddefault,sd1,sd2,sd3}.jpg; [F]/png3/Oliver_Primary_-_A_Salt_Rifle.png; [F]/crops/sprite_soldier.png.

## Heavy A Salt Rifle (HASR) (primary; one of the original three, BBR v1.0, 2011; 110,000 Joules)
- **Official text:** "With higher capacity and lower damage the Heavy A Salt Rifle gives its owner more room for error before reloading but at the cost of stopping power." [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_A_Salt_Rifle).
- **Status:** 110,000 J, level 1; price history 8,000 → 10,000 → 100,000 → 110,000 J [Fan-wiki]. Its damage of 8 is higher than the ASR's 6, which contradicts "lower damage"; the stats probably changed after the text was written [Inference].
- **Look:**
  - An AK-style rifle ("based off of the AK-47" [Fan-wiki]) in olive green (about #40553E in shade) with salmon/red panels.
  - **A large grey-and-red ring (about #6D433B in shade) loops from the stock round the grip.** The ring is about 0.3x across; the gun is about 0.55x long [scale is Inference].
  - Sources [Observed]: [F]/png/Photo_19-04-13_12_02_04_PM.png; [F]/png/HASR_weapon_concept.png ("AR5"); [F]/png/Soldier_Weapon.png ("AR5"); [F]/png/Heavy_A_salt_Rifle.png (BBR store, Mar 2012); [F]/png/Oliver_overhaul.png (top right, 2014 key art); sprite "hasr".
  - Held at the hip, angled down [Observed, photo and [F]/yt/HlYoml_9Wx0_sd3.jpg].
- **Firing:**
  - Stats: automatic, mid range, "partial inaccuracy"; damage 8, rate of fire 0.125 s, ammo 60, reload 5 s, dispersion 0.08, -15% speed [Fan-wiki].
  - The Apr 2013 HUD reads 21/60, 60/60 and 0/60 [Observed, HlYoml_9Wx0 sddefault, sd1, sd2, sd3].
  - **In SkyVu's Apr 2014 Oliver Overhaul video, the HUD shows the HASR icon at x/30:** 5/30 at 68.966 s [Observed, [K]/9qYIIH8NV-4_68966_w336.jpg] and 8/30 in sd1 [Observed, [F]/yt/9qYIIH8NV-4_sd1.jpg]. - **Impact / end result:**

  - In the 2014 Overhaul frames with the HASR equipped, a white bear target throws small red and lime-green flecks [Observed]. Crediting these to the HASR is an [Inference].
- **Images viewed:** [F]/png/Photo_19-04-13_12_02_04_PM.png; [F]/png/HASR_weapon_concept.png; [F]/png/Soldier_Weapon.png; [F]/png/Heavy_A_salt_Rifle.png; [F]/png/Oliver_overhaul.png; https://i.ytimg.com/vi/HlYoml_9Wx0/{sddefault,sd1,sd2,sd3}.jpg; [K]/9qYIIH8NV-4_68966_w336.jpg; https://i.ytimg.com/vi/9qYIIH8NV-4/{maxresdefault,sd1}.jpg; [F]/png3/Oliver_Secondary_-_Heavy_A_Salt_Rifle.png.

## Soaker (primary; one of the original three, BBR v1.0, 2011; 60,000 Joules, level 10; stats reduced later)
- **Official text:** "The range is limited compared to other rifles, but it's way more accurate!" [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Soaker).
- **Status** [Fan-wiki]:
  - Quoted exactly: "It used to have one of the fastest rate of fire out of all the [[Soldier]]'s primary weapons,tying in with [[Urine Trouble]] and [[Minigun (Soldier)]] before its stats were reduced."
  - Quoted exactly: "It was the highest damaging primary(dealing 100 damage per second without the use of damage equipment or [[Propbearganda]])after [[Urine Trouble]] before it's stats were reduced." So it was second only to Urine Trouble. No date is given.
  - BBG v2.6 (Dec 20, 2013): "Soaker and Urine Trouble issues have been fixed" [Fan-wiki, Versions].
- **Look:**
  - "This weapon is based on the Super Soaker" [Fan-wiki].
  - A green-grey water-gun body with salmon panels. **On top sits a big bright-yellow tank (about #F9E124) with red-orange diagonal stripes (about #D56A19)**, plus a small round yellow tank and a pump grip under the barrel.
  - Sources [Observed]: [F]/png/Soaker_Concept.png ("AR4"); [F]/png/ASRconcept.png; [F]/png/Photo_19-04-13_12_02_08_PM.png; [F]/png/Soaker.png (store, Mar 2012); sprite "soa".
  - About 0.55x [Inference].
- **Firing:**
  - Stats: automatic, close range (950 units); damage 5, rate of fire 0.1 s, ammo 60, reload 4 s, +10% speed [Fan-wiki].
  - The Apr 2013 HUD reads 35/60, 34/60 and 0/60 [Observed, Y8uyyLgcsrQ hqdefault, hq1, hq3]. In hq2 he has switched to the Bearzooka (1/1).
  - A May 13, 2014 Everyplay clip titled "Another Oliver Gameplay #BattleBearsGold #Oliver #Soaker" (dZWA5i0krBY) reads 0/70 with RELOAD in all four frames [Observed]. The weapon icon is hidden behind RELOAD, so only the title identifies the gun.

- **Projectile:** No frame shows it [Unverified]. The Super Soaker basis, and its retexture Urine Trouble firing a liquid jet, suggest a stream [Inference].
- **Impact / end result:**
  - A pink target shows a white, cyan and pink flash ringed with small yellow stars (hqdefault) [Observed].
  - A faceted pale-yellow polygon with grey smoke puffs, plus a yellow-orange starburst at the target (hq1) [Observed].
  - Crediting either to the Soaker is [Unverified].
