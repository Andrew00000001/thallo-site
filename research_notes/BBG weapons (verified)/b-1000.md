# B-1000 (Assault) weapons dossier

B-1000 is BBG's Assault class, a "half-ton murderous robot" [Game text via wiki, Shock N Claw]. Steam's 2026 store text lists "ASSAULT: B-1000" and "80+ PRIMARY WEAPONS 60+ SECONDARY WEAPONS 50+ MELEES", with no list per class, and gives the release date as Nov 4, 2026 [Canon, Steam appdetails 4408320, re-fetched 2026-10-17].

He did not exist in BBR (2012). The BBR 1.4.3 notes (April 15, 2013) only tease "NEW Character Class- the B-1000! A robot made to kill." [Fan-wiki: wiki transcription of SkyVu App Store notes]. He launched with BBG 1.0 on May 24, 2013 [Fan-wiki, class template].

The wiki lists 14 weapons [Fan-wiki]:
- **Primaries:** Grenader, Bearail Gun, Energy Overflow, Super Rapid Chain Gun (SRCG), Lightning Bolt, Bass Cannon.
- **Secondaries (back modules):** Last Resort (removed), Metal Crush, Energy Barrier, Smoke Screen, Satellite.
- **Melees:** Axecutioner, Shock N Claw, Primus Sword.

The class page, Category:Assault and our class mapping agree on these 14. None of the five unassigned pages (Adrenaline Gun, Catastrophe, ClubCobra, Hazard Toy, The Incredible Riggs) is an Assault weapon; SkyVu's 2.20 post credits ClubCobra to Saberi. One unshipped concept, "focus Rods", is covered at the end.

## House look (applies to every weapon)
- **Mounting (fixed).** The primary is built onto B-1000's RIGHT forearm, the melee onto his LEFT forearm, and the secondary is a BACK module. He never holds anything in a hand.
  - Evidence [Observed]: wiki renders `B-1000Skin`, `Gold1000`, `Orange1000`, `Mu123`, `Grenader`, `EnergyOverflow`; back views `Last_Resort`, `Metal_Crush`, `Satellite_`; the official grey T-shirt art (https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0370/2469/4408/files/BB_shirt_GreyB1000_model.png); Steam `screen_04`.
  - The wiki says he is "the only class that displays all of its weapons all the time" [Fan-wiki].
  - The 2D class portraits (`B-1000.png`, `Assaultimage.png`) draw the gun and claws MIRRORED [Observed]. Do not copy their arm layout.
- **Weapon colours and skins.**
  - In 2013–2015 renders, skins recolour only the armour. Weapon hardware stays orange, white and black on every skin [Observed: default, Silver (`SRCG.jpg` store), Gold, Orange and Mega Ursatron renders].
  - Cyan appears only on some weapons [Observed]: Energy Overflow fins, the SRCG side window, the Lightning Bolt lens and strip, the Satellite band and lights, and the Shock N Claw blades and hose rings.
  - There is NO cyan on the Grenader, Bearail Gun (its muzzle tip glows yellow-orange), Last Resort (dark navy-grey discs), Metal Crush, Energy Barrier or Axecutioner [Observed].
  - Two weapons have their own schemes: the Primus Sword (silver, hot pink, pale cyan) and the Bass Cannon (olive and black with blue).
  - **Possible 2026 exception.** The custom thumbnail of fan upload jbdhitBvSL8 (maxres) shows a red, black and gold "gothic" B-1000. Its claw gauntlet is black and gold, its hose red, and its gun trim red and gold [Observed]. It is a stylised fan thumbnail with title text over a painted backdrop, so whether 2026 skins recolour weapons in-game is [Unverified].
- **Approximate hex values** [Inference: sampled from official in-game art, not official codes]:
  - Hardware orange #E58D2B lit, #A75C10 shaded.
  - Cream-white #F3ECD2 / #E0D7CF; near-black #1C1A18.
  - Eye cyan #38D6D7; claw cyan #2BDCF1.
- **Scale.** B-1000 is about 1.5x Oliver's height on the BBG News scale chart [BBG News invention, from the character file]. Weapon sizes below are estimated as fractions of B-1000's height [Inference] and converted to Oliver units.
- **How a kill looks.** Every kill we observed breaks the body into chunky separate pieces, with no gore:
  - Steam trailer 4.95–5.3 s: a green-furred bear carrying the single coral Bearzooka comes apart into head (blue bandana), belted torso and launcher arm, which fly upward [Observed]. The Bearzooka is the Soldier's weapon, so it is a Soldier, probably the Green Oliver skin [Inference]. (Corrected Oct 18 from "a green Demo", per shared.addendum.md, round 3.)
  - Steam `screen_04`: red armour chunks fly through the air [Observed].
  - mX-wRChpK1w hq2 (Oct 16, 2013): red-and-white chunks lie on the floor after a SUICIDE [Observed].
  - That every BBG kill looks like this is [Inference].
  - The horizontal orange-yellow streaks around the trailer's claw kill (4.7–5.1 s) reappear on the B-1000 title card (5.8–7.0 s). They are probably editorial overlay rather than an in-game hit effect [Inference].
- **Splash sizes (AoE).** The wiki gives two numbers per weapon, read here as max-damage and min-damage radii in game units [Fan-wiki numbers; Inference on meaning]. The 2020-11-09 edit changed four rows:

| Metal Crush | 150/300 | 125/400 |
| Grenader | 85/215 | 125/250 |
| Satellite | 100/250 | 125/250 |

  Lightning Bolt and Energy Overflow have no AoE [Fan-wiki].
- **Signature promo loadout: Bearail Gun + Shock N Claw + Metal Crush.**
  - It appears in the Steam trailer from 1.5 to 7.0 s, including the B-1000 title card at 5.8–7.0 s, on the T-shirt and in most store renders [Observed].
  - It was probably the pre-launch default [Inference]:
    - On 2013-05-20 the wiki's Bearail page (rev 37475) read "This is the default weapon for the B-1000 class." and carried [Category:Default] until rev 40735 on 2013-06-05 [Fan-wiki old revision].
    - File:B-1000_default.jpeg, uploaded 2013-05-20 before launch, shows Bearail + Shock N Claw [Observed by the researcher].
  - The launch default kit is Grenader + Axecutioner + Last Resort [Fan-wiki]. Since the 2020 edit the wiki lists Smoke Screen as the default secondary.
- **Equipment caveat.** The BBG 3.0 notes (Nov 15, 2021) say "All +1 Equipment has been removed except Speed +1" and "Equipment Slot 2 has been removed" [Fan-wiki: wiki transcription of SkyVu App Store notes]. The wiki's +1 equipment rows therefore no longer apply.

## Grenader (primary, launch default; 2013 launch; 2026 unconfirmed)
- **Official text:** "Got a weak arm? Fear not! This honey of a weapon does the throwing for you. Now you can sit back and relax while pelting your enemies with explosives from afar." [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Grenader)
- **Status and history:**
  - Default primary, level 1 [Fan-wiki].
  - Trivia: "In the beta version of Battle Bears Gold, it was tenatively called the 'Grrnade Launcher'" [Fan-wiki]. The page was created under that name on 2013-05-20 and moved on 2013-05-31 [Fan-wiki page history].
  - The 2020-11-09 edit (rev 75686 to 77023, unsourced) changed [Fan-wiki]:
    - explosives 55–75 to 45–70; AoE 85/215 to 125/250;
    - rate of fire 1 s to 1.5 s; ammo 3 to 4; reload 5.25 s to 5 s;
    - projectile speed 700 to 750 u/s.
  - The usage prose still says "an ammo clip of three and a 1 second rate of fire" [Fan-wiki].
  - HUD ammo maximum in fan clips [Observed]:
    - "/4": Jun 4, 2013 (NJKRl54kc54) and Dec 20, 2013 (6-bciTQmJn4).
    - "/6", possibly "/8" (low-res): Jul 21, 2013 (uDul96UTAJY hq2).
    - "/6": Mar 14 and Aug 3, 2014 (PLZG_TQe9YE, UBZwQSTEx1k).
    - "/3": 2015 (SJx-s0--z7I, ZEwCAXkkplc, sxYKPH3GAx0) and Mar 28, 2018 (6zv-C5K11i4).
  - There is no clean year-by-year pattern. Ammo+ equipment adds rounds [Fan-wiki], so most counters cannot show the base clip [Inference].
  - The 2.23 clip sxYKPH3GAx0 (Dec 16, 2015) lists its set as "Grenader, Smoke Screen, Primus Sword, Turkish SW Boots, Armour+1, Melee Damage+2", with no Ammo+. So "/3" was probably the base clip then [fan description; Inference].
  - **2026:** no playtest sighting.
    - A B-1000 carries it in Steam screenshot `screen_04` and trailer 26.0–26.8 s, whose capture date is unknown [Observed].
    - The JustNubz showcase gun, earlier read as a probable Grenader, is the Bearail Gun (see below).
    - Presence in the 2026 build is [Unverified].
- **Look** [Observed: `Grenader.png`, `Axecutioner.png` front views; `screen_04`]:
  - Overall: a chunky octagonal cylinder launcher fitted over the RIGHT forearm.
  - Body: riveted white panels framed in orange.
  - Muzzle: a black octagonal tube.
  - Hose: a black ribbed hose arcs over the top like a carry handle.
  - Sides: a black ribbed grille and small orange fin spikes.
  - In `screen_04` it hangs muzzle-down with two black ribbed hoses looping down its sides. The orange-and-blue B-1000 carrying it also wears Metal Crush plates and a cyan Shock N Claw blade [Observed].
  - Length: about 0.4x B-1000's height [Inference], so about 0.6x Oliver.
  - Colours: orange #E58D2B lit / #A75C10 shaded; white #E0D7CF; black #1E1F1C; gunmetal #686561 [Inference: sampled]. No cyan.
- **Firing:**
  - Semi-auto; it "shoots grenades in an arc" [Fan-wiki].
  - Reload look per the wiki: "the barrel will look like it's charging like the Power Glove, but with strong golden light, eventually forming a golden orb" [Fan-wiki].
  - Seen in-game [Observed: ZEwCAXkkplc hq1/hq2, sxYKPH3GAx0 hq2, 6-bciTQmJn4 hq2, SJx-s0--z7I hq3, 6zv-C5K11i4]: a soft glowing ball at least as wide as the muzzle sits on the black muzzle cap. Its core is white-yellow #FBFDDE, then lemon #F5FC93, with an orange rim.
- **Projectile:**
  - The wiki says: "The projectile looks like it's set on fire with something else rotating or orbiting it, also emitting fire" [Fan-wiki].
  - Seen [Observed, low-res: uDul96UTAJY hq2 (Jul 2013), sxYKPH3GAx0 hq2 (Dec 2015)]: a bright white-yellow ball (#FBFDDE core, gold halo #B69431) followed by a comet trail of yellow-orange blobs (#ECB235).
  - The "orbiting" element cannot be made out at thumbnail resolution [Unverified].
  - Speed 750 u/s (700 before 2020) [Fan-wiki].
- **Impact / end result:**
  - Per the wiki, it explodes on contact with any player or surface "into a damaging, golden orb", "a rather huge and bright blast that deals between 45-70 damage" (55–75 before 2020), with AoE 125/250 (85/215 before). "The grenades launched have a significant knockback effect when they hit airborne players". Its main weakness is "unintentional self-harm" [Fan-wiki].
  - [Observed, ambiguous: PLZG_TQe9YE hq3]: a soft golden dome of light with white sparks on the floor near a Grenader-armed B-1000 at 1/6. It is probably a Grenader blast [Inference].
  - Do NOT use the trailer's 26.4–27.4 s blast as a reference. It is centred on a yellow radiation-symbol bomb under a light column, also visible in `screen_04`, not on a grenade [Observed].

- **Depiction for shorts:**

  - On firing, the launcher bucks and lobs a glowing white-gold ball with a short tail of yellow-orange fire blobs in a high arc.

## Bearail Gun (primary; 2013 launch; seen in 2026 playtests)
- **Official text:** "This devastating laser creates a colossal explosion upon impact. Anything this powerful takes time so keep the enemies in your sight while it builds up. Trust us, vaporizing your friend has never been more fun!" [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Bearail_Gun)
  - The beta description "Gun go boom" is only in old revisions (rev 37475, 2013-05-20: "Gun go boom - beta description") [Fan-wiki old revision].
- **Status and history:**
  - Costs 110,000 Joules at level 1 [Fan-wiki]. The v2.7 store shows it "EQUIPPED" [Observed: `SRCG.jpg`].
  - It was briefly the wiki's default (rev 37475 to 40735, 2013-05-20 to 06-05); see House look.
  - "Upon its initial release, the Bearail Gun originally had no golden laser from the shooter to the target" [Fan-wiki trivia]. The wiki says SkyVu added the beam in a later update; the version is unknown.
  - The 2020-11-09 edit (rev 75774 to 77024) changed explosives 55–75 to 75–90 and reload 3.5 s to 3 s. The usage prose says "a minimum of 70 damage and a maximum of 90" [Fan-wiki].
  - **2026** [Observed]:
    - Official video "Battle Bears Gold launches Nov 4, 2026 - Official Playtest Highlights" (channel BATTLE BEARS, uploaded Aug 16, 2026; title [Canon]). Its description says "Highlights from our first two Discord playtests" [Canon]. In hq1 a maroon B-1000 arm holds the Bearail Gun with the HUD at 0/1 and a pale yellow streak running into the distance.
    - Fan upload "Battle Bears Gold Closed Testing EXTENDED Footage" (M1kaa717, Sept 22, 2026), hq2/hq3: Bearail Gun at 0/1 on B-1000s wearing the Energy Barrier, health "/180".
    - JustNubz, "BBG All Classes Showcase [Skins, Taunts, Weapons, and Melee] (from Closed Playtest #4)" (Sept 20, 2026), hq1: a brown, holly-wreathed B-1000 in the store has a tall slab gun hanging muzzle-down. It has the orange hexagonal front plate, black window and two white light bars of `B-1000Skin.png` and `Orange1000.png`, not the Grenader's cylinder [Observed; identity by shape match].
    - So it is in the 2026 playtest builds. Whether it ships on Nov 4 is [Unverified].
  - **BBH (2024):** the wiki lists B1000's attack as "Shock N Claw + Bearail Gun" [Fan-wiki]. The official site says "B1000s twin functionality attack 'Slice n Bite' offers both range and melee" [Canon, battlebears.com/pages/characters, re-fetched 2026-10-17].
- **Look** [Observed: `B-1000Skin.png`, `Orange1000.png`, `Mu123.png`, `Bearail_G.png`, the v2.7 store back view `Satellite_.jpg`, trailer 4.5 s, 2026 thumbnails]: a long, slab-sided rectangular arm-gun on the RIGHT forearm.
  - Front/outer face: a large orange hexagonal plate with a black window holding two white light bars, over white and dark-grey panels.
  - Back/inner face: a black core with orange frame rails running its length and white end panels. This is the face shown in `Bearail_G.png`.
  - Side: a round orange port with a dark centre (`B1000battlepackbundle.png`, `Mu123.png`, C7uFxvSZ10c hq1).
  - Muzzle: a short black barrel ending in a glowing yellow-orange emitter tip, visible even at idle (pale core #FEE995, orange #F3BC67 / #DB8B3C, sampled from trailer 4.5 s).
  - Idle pose: every store render hangs it vertically, muzzle down. `Bearail_G.png` and gameplay (jbdhitBvSL8 hq3) show it raised.
  - Length: about 0.44x B-1000's height [Inference], so about 0.65x Oliver.
  - Colours: orange #E58D2B, cream #F3ECD2, dark #362C26 [Inference: sampled from File:B1000battlepackbundle.png]. No cyan.
- **Firing:**
  - "When activated, the Bearail Gun will charge for a second before firing an extremely fast, golden laser" [Fan-wiki].
  - Trivia: "If the player is reloading the Bearail Gun while cloaked, the light from charging the said gun will show." [Fan-wiki]. The wiki gives no colour for the charge light.
  - The 2013 concept sheet "B-1000 PRIMARY ATTACKS" shows the sequence red: "Charge-up" (a round emitter with a red glowing core), then a big red orb, "then a quick particle blast" [Observed: `B-1000_concept_1.png`]. In-game it is gold.
  - In-game, when it fires (HUD 0/1, RELOAD), a golden flare with spiky forward rays bursts at the muzzle, and long yellow-white streaks run toward the target [Observed: q77eEbDzOiY hq2 ("Battle Bears Gold 2.5", Dec 8, 2013), gNxIQSmQcJY hq2, YUZdF7gPZeA hq2, sViZH-3shTM hq2, and 4OtByuL2bI4 hq3, which is the wiki's own "Bearail Gun Demonstration" Everyplay clip from Jan 2014].
- **Projectile:**
  - A hitscan beam: "no travel time, infinite range", "zero dispersion angle" [Fan-wiki].
  - The banner File:B1000battlepackbundle.png (wiki upload 2015-05-08) shows a straight, pencil-thin lemon-yellow beam, #EDDC31–#F7E95C (#F7ED6F–#FFF278 in a second sample), with a pale core #F9F2A3 [Observed]. The banner is promo illustration, not a gameplay capture, so its beam width is a guide only.
  - In-game, C7uFxvSZ10c hq1 (2026) shows a pale yellow diagonal streak [Observed].
  - The thin yellow line at trailer 1.5 s is NOT a Bearail shot. It starts at the gun of a small, slim blue-white bear at far right [Observed: frame and crop below].
- **Impact / end result:**
  - Per the wiki, it creates "a colossal explosion upon impact with any surface or player", "exploding with a large AoE, emitting particles and light. It also produces a dim light wave". Explosives 75–90 (55–75 before 2020); AoE 90/220 [Fan-wiki].
  - The banner ends the beam in a starburst of pale-yellow sparks (#FDF4B3) [Observed, promo art].
  - In 2lmiVEBA8_8 hq3 (Aug 11, 2013) a white-gold flash engulfs a target at close range [Observed, low-res].
  - At 4.5 s in the trailer, B-1000 levels the gun point-blank at a Demo before the claw kill. No clean beam hit is shown [Observed].
