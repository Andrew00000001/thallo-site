# Tillman: impact-gaps addendum (Twin Bearzookas, Fireworks, Rocket Propelled Lance, Gold Member, Reverse Fountain)

Tillman is the Demo class in Battle Bears Gold. Steam lists it as "DEMOLITION" [Canon]. This addendum covers only the five weapons whose impact had "no image" in the verified Tillman bible (/home/user/thallo-site/research_notes/BBG weapons (verified)/tillman.md). For each one it adds what the shot and the hit look like. It also corrects five things in the bible and two in the character file.

## Twin Bearzookas (primary; added BBG 2.17, Mar 8, 2015; seen in fan footage to Jul 2016; 2026 status unknown)
- **Official text:**
  - No in-game description was found [Unverified].
  - The wiki's transcription of the 2.17 notes reads "Twin Bearzookas for Demo!" [Fan-wiki transcription].
- **Look:**
  - Two pale olive-lime tubes (#93994A–#9A9F4F) with silver octagonal muzzles, one on each shoulder [Observed in the bible: File:Ohmagawdyas.jpeg]. They are not red-and-orange; that wiki line fits Oliver's Bearzooka [bible].
  - The Mar 9, 2015 custom thumbnail shows the in-game model holding both tubes at shoulder height, muzzles toward the viewer [Observed: TG/thumbs/vS8uDIRxgu8_maxresdefault.jpg].
  - **First person:** olive tube(s) jut from the lower left and/or right of the screen, with a grey octagonal rim [Observed: TG/thumbs/vS8uDIRxgu8_maxres2.jpg, _maxres3.jpg; eSQAtmxfypw_maxres1–3; GORd_pL67eg_maxres1–3].
  - **HUD icon:** one olive segmented tube with dark grey end caps and a pistol grip. In that loadout the small secondary icon is the RPL [Observed: TG/crops/eS_icon.jpg, from eSQAtmxfypw_maxres1.jpg, Jul 26, 2015].
- **Firing:**
  - Infobox: 1 s rate of fire, 3 ammo, 5 s reload, 750 u/s, -10% speed. The "incoming" (pre-release) stats were clip 4 and 900 u/s [Fan-wiki].
  - Muzzle: a big yellow-orange glow just ahead of the tube as the HUD drops to 2/3 [Observed: TG/crops/574_m3.jpg from 574_vzigCqI_maxres3.jpg, Mar 11, 2015]. That this is the muzzle flash is [Inference].
  - Fan HUD maximums vary [Observed: vS8uDIRxgu8_maxres1–3, 574_vzigCqI_maxres1–3, eSQAtmxfypw_maxres1 and _maxres3, GORd_pL67eg_maxres1–3, NuhUIKiiGtM_maxres1]:
    - 0/3 on Mar 9, 2015;
    - 0/3 and 2/3 on Mar 11, 2015;
    - 4/4 on Mar 13, 2015 (the bible's VPgGpX_8xQg);
    - 5/5 and 0/5 RELOAD in Jul 2015;
    - 4/4 and 0/4 in 2016.
  - The wiki lists "Ammo +2 | + 1 bullets" [Fan-wiki]. So these counts cannot date a clip change [Inference].
- **Projectile:**
  - Two stubby rockets fly **side by side**. Each has a silver-grey body (#868084), a square gold-orange collar band and bright yellow star-shaped tail fins (#EFC63F), and trails round grey low-poly smoke puffs (#656167) [Observed: TG/crops/Nu_def_proj.jpg from NuhUIKiiGtM_maxresdefault.jpg]. That thumbnail (Jun 28, 2016) carries "TWIN BEARZOOKAS" text, but the game frame under the text looks unedited [Inference].
  - A second edited thumbnail shows the same pair from above, flying parallel: grey rockets with yellow bands [Observed: TG/crops/GO_def.jpg from GORd_pL67eg_maxresdefault.jpg, Jul 12, 2016].
  - At range the shot reads as a thin white streak with a faint yellow edge. The olive tube is in view and the HUD reads 0/3 [Observed: TG/crops/vS_m1.jpg from vS8uDIRxgu8_maxres1.jpg; attribution Inference].
  - That both tubes fire at once is [Inference] from the two rockets flying abreast.
  - Size: each rocket fits a muzzle about 0.2× Tillman's height across, so it is under 0.2× Tillman (about 0.18× Oliver) in diameter [Inference].
- **Impact / end result:**
  - **Blast:** a huge white jagged starburst (#FEFDFF) with long yellow spike rays (#FCFE60) in a cyan haze (#52F8FB). It fills the view at close range, with the HUD at 0/4 and the Twin Bearzookas icon showing [Observed: TG/crops/Nu_m1_flash.jpg from NuhUIKiiGtM_maxres1.jpg]. That it is the player's own rocket is [Inference, moderate]. It matches the white starburst with yellow rays that the bible saw in VPgGpX_8xQg_hq2 [bible, Observed].
  - **On a target:** an orange-pink fireball with peach smoke (#CBA2B5 / #D89069) engulfs an enemy, with thin violet electric squiggles [Observed: TG/crops/nR_def.jpg from nRMpTrQFW60_maxresdefault.jpg, edited thumbnail, Mar 5, 2016].
  - **Afterwards:** grey faceted low-poly smoke puffs hang around the hit area [Observed: TG/crops/574_def.jpg from 574_vzigCqI_maxresdefault.jpg].
  - **The violet squiggles** also appear on the player's own arm in 574_vzigCqI_maxres3. Other thumbnails from the same videos have "POWER UP" in the kill feed. So they are more likely a power-up aura than a weapon effect [Inference, weak].
  - AoE "Max Dmg 125.0, Min Dmg 250.0" [Fan-wiki]. No kill frame was found [Unverified].
  - NuhUIKiiGtM_maxres2 and _maxres3 show another class: 137 max health and a grey-and-orange launcher icon. They are not Tillman [Observed; Inference].

## Fireworks (secondary, default; BBR launch loadout 2011 → BBG; in fan footage 2015–2016; 2026 status unknown)
- **Official text:**
  - "Quick to lock and fire these vertical missiles are great for harassing distant and slow moving opponents! Wait for targeting reticule to gain a full lock before firing." [Game text via wiki] (Fireworks, rev 76874)
  - 2.6 log, Dec 2013: "Fireworks / Maximum Damage decreased from 25 to 20 / Minimum Damage increased from 8 to 10 / Reload speed decreased from 9 to 4.5 / Clip size decreased from 30 to 10 / Descend speed decreased from 2500 to 2000 / Lock radius increased from 300 to 350 / Lock time increased from 0.8 to 1" [Canon] (bbg26_text.txt, lines 304–311)
  - Successor in BBH (2024): "His Ultimate, "Firework Flurry," sends a series of fireworks over a targeted area, causing ongoing explosions and spreading wider with distance." [Canon] (battlebears.com/pages/characters, re-fetched 2026-10-18)
- **Look (new detail):**
  - Each red rocket carries a grinning pink-violet Huggable face: round ears, two big dark eyes with white highlights and a wide white toothy grin [Observed: TG/crops/JW_face_zoom.jpg and JW_m2_rocket.jpg, from JWxY0Ung2Rs_maxres2.jpg, Feb 7, 2016, HUD 19/35].
  - Colours: face #C0598E–#CF4770 with violet shading #85457C; red body #D02C34; orange-red cone #DA4122 [Observed, sampled from the same crops].
  - This confirms the wiki trivia "The Fireworks have a Huggable's face on its surface" [Fan-wiki].
  - The rack look (four rockets upright on a black-and-grey back bar) is in the bible [Observed there].
  - **HUD icon:** four red rockets with grey tips [Observed: TG/crops/4C_icons.jpg (4Cf8HVYxaAA, Dec 16, 2015), TG/crops/DI_tr.jpg (DIZKnVQ-p2Y, Feb 17, 2016), TG/crops/q1_m1_icon.jpg (SkyVu, 2014)].
- **Firing:**
  - **Launch:** rockets rise straight up past the camera, two side by side. Each rides a yellow-white flame plume longer than the rocket (core #FEFEFE, edge #FEFC1D) [Observed: TG/crops/JW_m2_rocket.jpg].
  - **Rate and clip:**
    - Infobox: "Rate of Fire 0.5 seconds" and "Ammo 15" [Fan-wiki].
    - Fan HUDs read x/30 in Dec 2015 and Feb 2016 (4Cf8HVYxaAA, DIZKnVQ-p2Y) and x/35 in Jan–Apr 2016 (V8XLmy-1wgY, JWxY0Ung2Rs, HpfErDNAcB0) [Observed]. In DIZKnVQ-p2Y the loadout's other weapon, the Gold Member, has a 6-round counter, so the 30-round counter is the Fireworks [Inference, strong].
    - SkyVu cut the clip from 30 to 10 in Dec 2013 [Canon]. So a large clip came back later, probably in the June 2014 overhaul [Inference].
  - **Path:** "landing a second or two later in a spiralling cascade of firecrackers" and they "land wherever the targeting reticule was at the time of launch" [Fan-wiki].
  - **Descending:** one rocket reads as an orange hexagonal nose with a long white-and-yellow streak behind it [Observed: TG/crops/Hp_sd3.jpg from HpfErDNAcB0_sd3.jpg, Apr 2, 2016, HUD 10/35; identity Inference].
  - **Lock:** the 2016 Fireworks frames show no lock bracket. The red triangle over an enemy in V8XLmy-1wgY_maxres1 looks like the ordinary enemy marker [Observed; Inference]. The wiki says "It cannot lock on to targets." [Fan-wiki]
- **Impact / end result:**
  - Wiki: "Wherever they happen to land, they expode into a flurry of damaging, orange sparks." [Fan-wiki, sic]; AoE "Max Dmg 125.0, Min Dmg 250.0".
  - Three frames show it. In all three the Fireworks is the active weapon, going by its counter or its icon [Observed HUD]. That these bursts are its landings is [Inference, moderate].
    - A scatter of several chunky low-poly orange fire puffs (#E7912A–#EA962A) along a corridor. They have yellow hot cores, with small red, pink and white spark flecks around an enemy; HUD 20/35 [Observed: TG/crops/V8_m1_right.jpg from V8XLmy-1wgY_maxres1.jpg, Jan 15, 2016].
    - A single small yellow flame burst (#F3E73A) with red sparks beside an enemy; HUD 18/35 [Observed: TG/crops/V8_m3_mid.jpg].
    - A yellow-white faceted fire cloud (#FEFDF0) above an enemy; HUD 12/30 [Observed: TG/crops/DI_m3.jpg from DIZKnVQ-p2Y_maxres3.jpg].
  - An edited thumbnail shows spiky yellow-orange starbursts (#E9B238) in the night sky, orange starbursts on the deck beside an enemy, and a yellow ring on the floor [Observed: TG/crops/JW_def_mid.jpg from JWxY0Ung2Rs_maxresdefault.jpg]. They may be added by the editor or be the map's sky [Unverified].
  - **Official Steam trailer, Tillman segment.** A ragged row of six small orange-yellow flame jets (#FDB959–#FED73B, darker tips #BC774E) burns with grey smoke on the floor beside a jump pad [Observed: TG/frames/fw_03–fw_27, TG/sheets/fw_right.jpg, TG/crops/fw05_full.jpg, fw05_flames.jpg; V/trailer/t_0061–t_0063].
    - They flicker in place from the start of the shot (about 30.0 s) to about 31.7 s and fade to smoke by about 32.1 s.
    - A red missile (the bible's Happy Hour) dives at top left in the same shot.
    - That the flames are landed Fireworks is [Inference, weak]. No shooter is shown.
  - No kill frame was found [Unverified].
