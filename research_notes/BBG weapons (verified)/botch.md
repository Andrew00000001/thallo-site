# Botch, the Assassin: weapons dossier (verified edit; BBG 2013–2021, 2026 playtests, BB Heroes 2024)

Botch is the Assassin class of Battle Bears Gold (BBG). The wiki's update table dates his arrival to BBG 2.1, July 15, 2013: "New character class - Assassin: Botch" [Fan-wiki: Updates]. A fan video showing the 2.1 loadout screen was uploaded that same day [Observed: oEmbed and publish date of https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3LlUZgQWmWQ]. Prior notes quote SkyVu's Google Play text: "New in 2.1: - New Character Class! ASSASSIN: Botch" [Canon per /home/user/thallo-site/research_notes/Battle Bears character lore/huggable_botch_b1000.md. I could not re-verify it today: Wayback returned no connection on all 12 attempts]. The 2026 Steam store text still lists "-ASSASSIN: Botch" and "80+ PRIMARY WEAPONS", "60+ SECONDARY WEAPONS" and "50+ MELEES" [Canon: Steam appdetails API for app 4408320, fetched 2026-10-17; release date "Nov 4, 2026"].

He has 15 BBG weapons [Fan-wiki: Category:Assassin lists exactly these 15 weapon pages]:
- 7 primaries: Doom Cloud, Chaos Toss, Toxic Plague, Cosmic Fire, Orbs, Energy Bombs, Hazard Toy.
- 5 secondaries: Pain Field, Fear Orbit, Cosmic Bomb, Nova, Gravity Well.
- 3 melees: Crass Knuckles, Ball N Pain, Tonguechuks.

He also has one attack that exists only in BB Heroes (BBH), the Doom Star. Hazard Toy is missing from the "Assassin (BBG)" class page and from the "_unassigned" bucket of weapons_by_class.json. Its infobox says Class: Assassin and it is in Category:Assassin [Fan-wiki]. "Assassin (BBG)/Weapons" is a one-line stub: "Detailed list of Weapons for the Assassin" [Fan-wiki].

**What this verification changed in the draft**
1. **Doom Cloud HUD icon.** It is a plain lime five-armed splat. The "small white dome" belongs to a separate icon: the small icon at the top-right of the big one is the *other* weapon slot (see "HUD layout" below).
2. **Energy Bombs blast.** The orange-yellow "fireball" pours out of the enemy's own gun barrel. It is not an Energy Bombs blast, so that blast is still unseen.
3. **Hazard Toy poison colour.** The colour is not settled. In 2018 the clouds are purple, skull-faced puffs. A 2021 Hazard Toy loadout shows green haze and a green skull icon over a poisoned Wil.
4. **Fear Orbit price.** The 2.1 store shows Fear Orbit at "BUY NOW 100" Gas, at high resolution (File:FearOrbit.jpg). The wiki says 60.
5. **Chaos Toss "3-round" clip.** It is not new to 2026. A c. 2013–2014 HUD with a Cosmic Bomb loadout also reads 0/3, so equipment may explain it.
6. **Hazard Toy ammo.** A June 2017 HUD reads 1/2. That adds a third observed value besides 4 (2018) and 3 (2021 and 2024).
7. **Health.** Two 2021 HUDs read 90/90 or 50/90, not 85.
8. **Fear Orbit skull blobs (2024).** That loadout also carried the Hazard Toy, so either weapon could have caused them.
9. **Concept art provenance.** The Cosmic Fire and Cosmic Bomb concept art also appears in a BBG-logo graphic titled "Update 2.3 Botch Weapon Concept Art". It was uploaded on 2013-09-24, three days before 2.3 shipped.
10. **2.1 launch kit.** Ball N Pain, Fear Orbit and Crass Knuckles are now each seen on a "2.1"-labelled screen.
11. **Dates.** I corrected the publish dates of several 2026 playtest thumbnails and added one more 2026 sighting of the Doom Cloud (JustNubz "FINAL PLAYTEST").

## Class-wide visual rules
- **Construct style.**
  - Most of his weapons are floating, translucent, glowing lime energy constructs that hover off one paw and are never gripped: the skeletal hand, spiked fist, spiked ball, starfish cloud, orbs, frisbee disc and palm flame [Observed: infobox renders].
  - Construct lime samples are DoomCloudCR #B0FF23, ChaosTossCR #A5FD23 and CrassKnucklesCR #ADFE24 [Observed; sampled].
  - Ball N Pain is a deeper green, #4ED611 [Observed; sampled].
- **Explosions are greener and cooler than the constructs.**
  - Cosmic Bomb blast: about #19EE50–#7DFB6C [Observed: Explosion.png; sampled].
  - Gravity Well domes: lime with a cyan inner shell [Observed].
- **Yellow accents.**
  - Orbs read bright yellow in play [Observed].
  - The descending Cosmic Bomb comet is about #FEFE84, with a pale cyan tail up to #9BFFFF [Observed: Bolt.png; sampled].
  - Doom Cloud needles turn yellow at their tips [Observed].
- **Exceptions to the lime rule.**
  - Pain Field is pink to magenta: idle #E25572, deployed about #B557D5 [Observed; sampled; the Fan-wiki agrees on the colour].
  - The Hazard Toy is dark bottle-green, #1F4C21 [Observed; sampled].
  - The Tonguechuks are a physical object: black, red (#AC192A), silver and green [Observed; sampled].
- **HUD layout (needed to read the fan screenshots).**
  - The big icon at top right shows the currently selected weapon, with its clip below it ("x / y") [Observed].
  - A small icon at that icon's top-right corner shows the other weapon slot [Inference]. In Imagef.png, Pain Field is the big icon and the Doom Cloud starfish is the small one. In Doom Cloud loadouts the order is reversed.
  - The round button with a cooldown ring, bottom right, is the Special. In many shots it is a Healing Tent [Observed; Inference].

## Doom Cloud (primary; default since BBG 2.1, July 2013; in 2026 playtests; BBH Super)
- **Official text:**
  - "This cute little thing is hiding a deadly secret. Once it's out a myriad of lasers create a cloud of doom around itself, but it sure is adorable, huh? " [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Doom_Cloud).
  - "Botch's "Doom Star" acts as a boomerang, potentially dealing double damage, while his "Doom Cloud" Super unleashes a bullet-raining cloud for widespread damage." [Canon] (https://battlebears.com/pages/characters, fetched 2026-10-17). That this text describes BBH is [Inference]: it uses "Super", and the page's Botch image is "Botch_BBH_icon".
- **Status:**
  - Level 1; "Price: Default"; 45 DPS; duration 4 s; ammo 1; reload 5.5 s; Damage +1/+2 add 2.5/5 [Fan-wiki infobox].
  - 2.1 default, confirmed twice [Observed]:
    - File:BotchSkin.jpg (wiki, uploaded 2013-07-23, "2.1" label) reads SKIN Botch, PRIMARY Doom Cloud, SECONDARY Pain Field, with the cloud beside him.
    - https://i.ytimg.com/vi/3LlUZgQWmWQ/hq3.jpg (rockstartanwille, published 2013-07-15) adds MELEE Crass Knuckles.
  - No rework is recorded [Fan-wiki].
  - **2026:** four sightings [Observed]:
    - HUD Doom Cloud at 0/1 and health 85/85 (https://i.ytimg.com/vi/vG31XcG7yT8/hq1.jpg and hq3.jpg; JustNubz "Battle Bears Gold Closed Playtest #2 Match 1", published 2026-07-30).

    - HUD Doom Cloud at 1/1, health 85/85, with the lime cloud beside him (https://i.ytimg.com/vi/1MP1rFi4t-Q/hq1.jpg; JustNubz "Battle Bears Gold FINAL PLAYTEST", Oct 2026).

- **Look:**
  - A translucent, jelly-like lime blob with about five drooping, outlined starfish or octopus points. Lime is about #B0FF23 [Observed: DoomCloudCR.png; Botch_Skin.png; Orange_Botch.png; Orca_treat.png; sampled].
  - From the side (File:DoomCloud.jpg, 2.1 matchmaking) it reads as a bell or teardrop shape [Observed].
  - About 0.6–0.8 Botch-heads tall [Observed, estimate].
  - It floats at shoulder height beside him in menus, or low in front of him in third-person play [Observed: Cosmic_Bomb_Demonstration-3.png].

- **Firing:**
  - Wiki: "the Assassin launches a green starfish-like cloud into the air. It spins around and releases a rain of neon green bullets" [Fan-wiki].
  - Wiki: "it rotates then raises itself into the air under the Assassin's direction" [Fan-wiki]. Its height sets how wide the rain spreads [Fan-wiki].
  - It "slowly adjusts its own movements to avoid solid obstacles" [Fan-wiki].
  - He cannot melee or switch weapons while it is active [Fan-wiki].
  - In 2026, a flattened lime splat hangs at the very top of the frame while streaks fall [Observed: vG31XcG7yT8 hq1]. That the splat is the raised cloud is [Inference].

  - Long, tapering lime needles slant down to the floor, yellowing toward their tips. They leave small yellow V-shaped splashes where they land [Observed: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/c8HwXqm9_Rc/hq3.jpg (Jan 2014); vG31XcG7yT8 hq1/hq3 (2026)].
  - That these are Doom Cloud bullets is [Inference]: the Doom Cloud icon shows 0/1 in the same frames.
  - The bullets "disappear on contact with solid surfaces", yet the cloud still hurts players under roofs [Fan-wiki].
- **Impact / end result:**
  - Damage only; there is no status effect or explosion [Fan-wiki]. Killed enemies fly apart [Observed: c8HwXqm9_Rc hq3].
  - At the end, "the cloud lowers itself to the ground and peters out in a burst of green light" [Fan-wiki].
  - It "immediately self-destructs" when Botch dies [Fan-wiki]. Contact with a solid surface, or passing through a platform, destroys it at once [Fan-wiki].
  - Glitch: it keeps firing into the reload animation, "making it look that Botch has two Doom Clouds" [Fan-wiki].
