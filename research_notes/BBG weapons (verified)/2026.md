# BBG 2026 re-release: weapon changes

This dossier compares the weapons of the 2026 re-release of *Battle Bears Gold* (Steam PC, iOS and Google Play, Nov 4, 2026) with the 2013–2021 game. It covers what SkyVu has said, what official 2026 images show, what fan playtest uploads show (titles, descriptions and thumbnails only), and how the fan wiki changed in 2026.

**Sources reached:**
- The Steam store API for app 4408320: store text, 5 screenshots, the header and a 1.4 s embedded store video.
- The Steam news API: all 7 BBG posts from Feb 24 to Oct 14, 2026, plus their images.
- The official "Battle Bears Gold Premium - Trailer" (54 s), as local frames.
- battlebears.com and play.battlebears.com (the official sign-up page).
- YouTube metadata through the innertube `next` endpoint (exact titles, descriptions, dates and chapters) for 10 official and about 17 fan videos, and their thumbnails.
- The fan-wiki API: class and item pages, image files, and the full 2026 recent-changes log.

**Not reachable:**
- YouTube video, storyboards and captions (LOGIN_REQUIRED; the transcript API returned FAILED_PRECONDITION). **No video was watched.** That includes the JustNubz showcase, the Oct 12 Q&A and the livestreams.
- Reddit (blocked) and Discord (not accessible). No Discord-sourced weapon claim turned up on the open web.
- Web search, which returned nothing about the 2026 re-release. Archive.org was not needed.

**Verification and edit pass (Oct 17, 2026).** A source check and an image check were applied. Where they disagreed with the draft, I re-opened their comparison sheets, re-read the wiki pages, and extracted new trailer frames (1.8–3.8 s, 2.70–3.20 s, 10.8–11.4 s and 28.4–29.4 s) into /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-thallo-site/91c60972-6d3f-51de-9a92-c1d563bcf5a4/scratchpad/weapons/dl/2026-edit/f/. The main changes:
- Several weapons in the trailer were identified wrongly:
  - The glove is the Blood Spurt, not the Barbed Gloves.
  - The B-1000's gun-arm is the Bearail Gun, not the Grenader.
  - The 7–10 s Graham shot is Gernobyl goo followed by a radiation kill, not a Gamma Ray beam. A Light Matter blast also appears in it.
  - Riggs's "unidentified" gun is the Electric Honey.
- The "magenta Dark O.C.O." evidence was a different weapon.
- The chainsaw on the Jul 6 banner is the Huggable's Seesaw, and the gatling on the same banner is the Beebeegun.
