# 2026 addendum: uncited playtest uploads, Hazard Toy / Energy Overflow / Rake, A Salt Rifle and Bearzooka

This addendum covers only the three "2026" gaps. Topic "2026" means what SkyVu's Battle Bears Gold re-release (Steam, iOS and Google Play, Nov 4, 2026) shows of each weapon, as seen in the five 2026 playtests.

**What I could reach (Oct 17, 2026)**
- **YouTube metadata.** The innertube `next` endpoint returned exact titles, dates, descriptions and chapters for all 28 listed videos, plus 4 older reference videos. The search endpoint returned run times.
- **YouTube thumbnails.** For every video, i.ytimg.com serves the custom thumbnail (`maxresdefault`/`sddefault`) and three automatic frames. **New this round:** the automatic frames also exist at 1280×720 as `maxres1.jpg`, `maxres2.jpg` and `maxres3.jpg`, not just the 480×360 `hq1`–`hq3`. Most of the evidence below comes from these.
  - The automatic frames are **not** reliably at 25/50/75% of the run time. For example, aujTcdFuxdE `maxres1` shows a Kill Confirmed match on Sky Pirates, but 25% of 52:11 falls in Match 4 on Steamyard Blues. So a frame cannot be tied to a chapter by its timing [Observed; Inference].
  - I watched no video; playback is blocked from this sandbox.
- **Fan wiki (MediaWiki API).** One request hit a Cloudflare page, and a retry with a user-agent worked. I read page wikitext, revision history, diffs, one editor's contributions and image info.
- **Steam.** The News API (7 posts) and appdetails both worked.

- **Status:**
  - Infobox: 60 Gas, level 1, damage "Uncharged: 45 / Charged: 140", ammo 2, reload 4 s, "+10% Speed Modifier", and "Charge Time 3" [Fan-wiki].
  - **"Charge Time 3" was added on 2026-09-20 at 05:17 UTC** by Alphabbr ("information edit") [Fan-wiki diff 77026→77724]. That is about 15 hours *before* PT4 opened (1 pm PDT = 20:00 UTC) [Canon for the playtest time]. So it does not come from PT4 [Inference]; its source is [Unverified].

- **Look:**
  - **The wiki image** (File:EnergyOverflow.jpg, uploaded 2013-09-28 by SirAdven) shows it CLOSED on B-1000's right forearm [Observed]:
    - A short, wide cylinder of white panels (≈ #DED6CF; lit #EDE7E2) framed in orange (≈ #A85E1A; light #CC9A5B).
    - A dark hexagonal muzzle (≈ #201D19) with a grey rim (≈ #999691).
    - A ring of small cyan lights (≈ #30BED3, brightest #0FDEFA).
