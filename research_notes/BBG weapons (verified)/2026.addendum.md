# 2026 addendum: uncited playtest uploads, Hazard Toy / Energy Overflow / Rake, A Salt Rifle and Bearzooka

This addendum covers only the three "2026" gaps. Topic "2026" means what SkyVu's Battle Bears Gold re-release (Steam, iOS and Google Play, Nov 4, 2026) shows of each weapon, as seen in the five 2026 playtests.

**What I could reach (Oct 17, 2026)**
- **YouTube metadata.** The innertube `next` endpoint returned exact titles, dates, descriptions and chapters for all 28 listed videos, plus 4 older reference videos. The search endpoint returned run times.
- **YouTube thumbnails.** For every video, i.ytimg.com serves the custom thumbnail (`maxresdefault`/`sddefault`) and three automatic frames. **New this round:** the automatic frames also exist at 1280×720 as `maxres1.jpg`, `maxres2.jpg` and `maxres3.jpg`, not just the 480×360 `hq1`–`hq3`. Most of the evidence below comes from these.
  - The automatic frames are **not** reliably at 25/50/75% of the run time. For example, aujTcdFuxdE `maxres1` shows a Kill Confirmed match on Sky Pirates, but 25% of 52:11 falls in Match 4 on Steamyard Blues. So a frame cannot be tied to a chapter by its timing [Observed; Inference].
  - I watched no video; playback is blocked from this sandbox.
- **Fan wiki (MediaWiki API).** One request hit a Cloudflare page, and a retry with a user-agent worked. I read page wikitext, revision history, diffs, one editor's contributions and image info.
- **Steam.** The News API (7 posts) and appdetails both worked.

## Hazard Toy (Assassin primary; BBG 2.27, May 29, 2017; 2026 presence unverified)
- **Official text:** "Banned from elementary schools world wide, this weapon is as dangerous as it looks!" [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Hazard_Toy, revid 77721).
- **Status:**
  - Infobox: "Primary - Explosives", 60 Gas, level 1, explosives 25–50, damage 4, duration 5 s, AoE "Max Dmg 75.0, Min Dmg 150.0", rate of fire 1.5 s, ammo 3, reload 3.5 s, 1,100 u/s [Fan-wiki].
  - **The 2026 edit:** Alphabbr changed "35-50" to "25-50" and "5" to "4" on **2026-09-18 18:54 UTC**, with no summary [Fan-wiki revision diff 77309→77721]. That is two days before PT4 (Sep 20, 1 pm PDT) [Canon for the playtest time].

  - It is not in any 2026 thumbnail (see the catalogue) [Observed absence].
- **Look:**

## Energy Overflow (Assault primary; BBG 2.3, image uploaded Sep 28, 2013; 2026 presence unverified)
- **Official text:** "Sometimes you have hold it in and wait for the right moment, then release all over your enemy. What? I'm talking about this energy shotgun. What'd you think I was talking about?" [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Energy_Overflow, revid 77724). It is innuendo; do not quote it in a 13+ post.
- **Status:**
  - Infobox: 60 Gas, level 1, damage "Uncharged: 45 / Charged: 140", ammo 2, reload 4 s, "+10% Speed Modifier", and "Charge Time 3" [Fan-wiki].
  - **"Charge Time 3" was added on 2026-09-20 at 05:17 UTC** by Alphabbr ("information edit") [Fan-wiki diff 77026→77724]. That is about 15 hours *before* PT4 opened (1 pm PDT = 20:00 UTC) [Canon for the playtest time]. So it does not come from PT4 [Inference]; its source is [Unverified].
  - Not in any 2026 thumbnail.
    - The only B-1000 HUD seen in 2026 (aujTcdFuxdE maxres1) reads 0/1. Energy Overflow has 2 ammo, so that is a different gun, probably the 1-ammo Bearail Gun [Observed; Inference].
- **Look:**
  - **The wiki image** (File:EnergyOverflow.jpg, uploaded 2013-09-28 by SirAdven) shows it CLOSED on B-1000's right forearm [Observed]:
    - A short, wide cylinder of white panels (≈ #DED6CF; lit #EDE7E2) framed in orange (≈ #A85E1A; light #CC9A5B).
    - A dark hexagonal muzzle (≈ #201D19) with a grey rim (≈ #999691).
    - A ring of small cyan lights (≈ #30BED3, brightest #0FDEFA).
    - Length about 0.33× B-1000's height, so about 0.5× Oliver; diameter about 0.33× Oliver [Inference: B-1000 1.5×].
  - **In play it is OPEN** (2013 footage): white fins with glowing cyan panels stand out from the orange-and-white barrel [Observed: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/1ykwGM9cCs4/hq1.jpg and hq3.jpg; "B1000 New Weapon: Energy Overflow", GoogleMyFist, Oct 7, 2013].
- **Firing:**
  - Two uncharged shots, or one charged blast. While charging, "particles converge on the barrel of the gun"; at full charge "orbs of light will appear at the tip of the barrel" [Fan-wiki].
  - "its very short range" [Fan-wiki].
  - In the Jan 6, 2014 demo (HUD 2/2), short yellow-gold streaks fly ahead and a flat yellow oval glow sits on the floor in front of B-1000 [Observed: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/gL12RHDQ_mI/maxresdefault.jpg, "Energy Overflow Demonstration", CheeseclanBBG].
  - Sound is undocumented.
- **Projectile:** "a single golden burst of damaging energy" when charged [Fan-wiki]. It is probably a short hitscan spread [Inference].
