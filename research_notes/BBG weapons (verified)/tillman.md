# Tillman (Demo / Demolition): BBG weapons bible (verified edition, 2026-10-17)

Tillman is the Demo class in Battle Bears Gold. Steam lists the class as "DEMOLITION: Tillman" [Canon] (Steam appdetails 4408320, re-fetched 2026-10-17). The class blurb reads: "Tillman does not have a weapon on hand that doesn't have splash damage! Throwing explosives into a group of enemies is a great way to turn the tides of a battle but be very careful to avoid hitting yourself!" [Game text via wiki] (Demo page revision 69512, 2015-03-10, which I re-fetched). Earlier notes also cite this blurb from SkyVu's archived 2012 BBR classes page as [Canon], but I could not re-open that page.

**Arsenal.** The BBG arsenal has 18 items: 8 primaries, 7 secondaries and 3 melees [Fan-wiki]. The source is the class page "Demo (BBG)", revid 77289, re-fetched today.
- Category:Demo intersected with Category:BBG Weapons gives the same 18 pages. None of the 5 "_unassigned" pages is a Demo weapon. The first researcher's inventory is complete.
- Bouncing Betty is marked "Unavailable". That marker was added on 2020-11-13 (rev 77258) [Fan-wiki revision history].

**Background facts used throughout:**
- Gibbing: "he has pink and red flesh as revealed when he is gibbed in-game" [Fan-wiki] (Demo (BBG)). Shorts show sparks and scrap only [BBG News invention].
- The backpack: "In Battle Bears Gold, this backpack is not used for flying at all and is only used to carry the Demo's Secondary weapons" [Fan-wiki] (Tillman page).
- BBG 2.13 notes (Oct 31, 2014): "Demo's rockets now have homing capabilities" [Fan-wiki transcription of official App Store notes] (Updates page).

## Big Shot (primary, default; BBR launch loadout 2011 → BBG; in the Steam trailer, likely 2013 footage)
- **Official text:**
  - Game text: "Launches a giant missile out at a straight trajectory." [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Big_Shot, rev 76866)
  - 2011 blog: "Primary: Big Shot (Chest Cannon)…" [Canon, not re-verified]. It is quoted in /home/user/thallo-site/research_notes/Battle Bears character lore/astoria_graham_tillman.md, and the archive was unreachable on 2026-10-16 and 2026-10-17.
  - Official 2.6 log (Dec 2013): "Big Shot / Maximum Damage increased from 35 to 40 / Fire Rate decreased from 1.5 to 1 / Magazine size increased from 5 to 6 / Projectile Speed increased from 1000 to 1200 / Minimum Damage Splash range increased from 90 to 100 / Max Damage Splash Range increased from 45 to 50 / Explosive +2 increased from 18 to 20" [Canon].
- **Look:**
  - A squat drum launcher held two-handed across the belly, muzzle forward [Observed: VF/img/png_Big_Shot.png].
  - Ribbed olive-grey barrel (#73776A–#818473) inside a dark-purple bracket frame (#391641–#502151) with grey edges, and a notched yellow muzzle disc (#D9B50F) [Observed, sampled].
  - It is a hand-held weapon, not a hatch in the chest [Observed]. The 2011 "(Chest Cannon)" most likely means "a cannon braced at the chest" [Inference].
  - The same model appears in the BBG 2.1 loadout with the Cyan Tillman skin [Observed: VF/img/png_Demolition_holding_Big_Shot.png, version "2.1" on screen].
  - The 2012 BBR list icon is a purple-and-grey drum with a yellow disc [Observed: VF/img/png_Dark_Matter.png, uploaded 2012-03-29].
  - In the trailer at 21.7–22.2 s, a Tillman with white-and-blue trim holds this drum at chest height [Observed: VF/frames/bsh_01–06; V/trailer/t_0045].
  - Size: muzzle disc about 0.25× Tillman's height across; the whole launcher about 0.45× his height wide, roughly 0.4× Oliver [Inference].
- **Firing:**
  - The wiki says it "fires damaging missiles in an arc". Its infobox gives a 0.5 s rate of fire and 7 ammo; its text says 6 [Fan-wiki].
  - The official text says "straight trajectory" [Game text via wiki]. The wiki trivia flags the conflict, and the class page calls it a launcher with "a curved… trajectory" [Fan-wiki].
  - As of Dec 2013 it held 6 rounds with a 1 s cooldown [Canon].
  - No muzzle effect or sound is documented [Unverified].
- **Projectile:**
  - A dark navy/black rounded bullet with white eye-like markings, trailing a pale white-grey smoke streak [Observed: VF/crops/bbr8_zoom.png from File:8 battle bears royale.jpg]. The wiki caption reads "Big Shot projectile (seen to the right)."
  - The wiki compares it to Bullet Bill [Fan-wiki].
  - Speed: 1,100 u/s in the infobox [Fan-wiki]; 1,200 in the 2.6 log [Canon].
- **Impact / end result:**
  - The wiki says the missile explodes on contact with any surface or player, "damaging any opposing players within its AoE" (AoE "Max Dmg 100, Min Dmg 200") [Fan-wiki].
  - No captioned impact image exists [Unverified].
  - Trailer, Wil's segment, on Abusement Park's red base (the "[RED]" stencil wall; map ID per research_notes/BBG maps (verified)/abusement-park.md): at 19.0–19.8 s the white-and-blue Tillman faces Wil. At about 19.9–20.4 s an orange-yellow fiery glow engulfs Wil and he lurches toward the camera [Observed: VF/frames/bs_01–10, bsf_01–08].
  - The launch is not shown, but Tillman is the only other bear visible. So this may be a Big Shot hit: a hot orange-yellow fireball [Inference; contested by the next bullet].
  - Competing reading (frames re-extracted at 0.1 s steps for 17.5–23.4 s on 2026-10-18): Wil holds a Batterito at 17.9 s, eats it at about 18.1–18.4 s, then shows blue tears and a gaping mouth [Observed]. The glow at 19.5–20.4 s floods in from the right, the side his open mouth faces, while Tillman stands at left with no launch shown, and Wil then turns his back to the camera and heads for Tillman, arms out and feet often off the floor [Observed]. Whether he runs or is hurled is unclear. So the glow may be the start of Wil's own Batterito fire, as the Wil dossier reads it [Inference]. The maps research also sees Wil holding the Batterito here, but reads the glow as Tillman's shot (abusement-park.md; 2026-lineup.md). Which reading is right is open.
  - The orange glowing faceted ball with grey smoke puffs that then flies away above Wil's head (about 20.5–21.5 s) is a separate projectile whose shooter is not shown [Observed: M/small/bs_proj_zoom.jpg, M/small/bs_mid.jpg]. An earlier reading made it Wil's return fire (a flaming spud). But once the Batterito is eaten, "the player cannot switch to their primary weapon … until the attack ends" [Fan-wiki: Batterito (BBG)], so a spud from Wil is unlikely [Inference], and its source is [Unverified].
  - The fire that rolls in behind Tillman at about 22.0–22.2 s and lights him white is most likely Wil's Batterito fire-breath, not the Big Shot [Inference]. Wil is out of frame then. But flame plumes billow beside his head as he heads for Tillman (20.5–21.5 s), and the "Wil / CHUB SCOUT" card that follows (22.5–23.5 s) shows a flame jet from his open mouth reaching the same white-lit Tillman [Observed: VF/frames/bsh_04–06; VF/crops/bsh_all.png; the re-extracted frames].

It ends in an orange-yellow fireball pop that tosses the target back with sparks and grey smoke [trailer fireball, attribution Inference and contested; sparks and confetti are BBG News invention].

## Gold Member (primary; BBR by March 2012 → BBG; 25,000 Joules, level 3)
- **Official text:**
  - "For gold medalist shooters only. Smaller explosion than the Big Shot but with more damage on direct impact." [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Member, rev 76705)
  - 2.6 log: "Gold Member / Minimum Damage decreased from 30 to 25 / Maximum Damage increased from 45 to 50 / Fire Rate decreased from 2 to 1.5 / Magazine Size decreased from 8 to 6 / Reload increased from 3 to 3.5 / Minimum Damage Range decreased from 100 to 90 / Maximum Damage Range increased from 45 to 50" [Canon].
  - The BBR 1.4.2 changes (Feb 28, 2013) are [Fan-wiki].
- **Status:** the 2012 BBR loadout list reads "GOLD MEMBER BUY NOW! 25,000" [Observed: VF/img/png_Dark_Matter.png, uploaded 2012-03-29].
- **Look:**
  - The Big Shot drum in polished gold (first-editor sample #ECC23E) with an orange glowing muzzle disc (#FF6E13–#FB934C), held the same way [Observed: VF/img/png_GoldMember.png, png_DEMO_GOLD_MEMBER.png, png_Gold_Tillman.png (on the Gold Tillman skin), in VF/crops/set_gm.png].
  - The wiki calls it a Big Shot reskin [Fan-wiki]. It is the same size [Inference].
- **Firing / projectile:** a "gold bullet-like missile that moves with a straight trajectory", "no arc", 1.5 s rate of fire, 1,200 u/s; ammo is 8 in the infobox and 6 in the text [Fan-wiki]. No image of the shot exists [Unverified].
- **Impact / end result:**
  - Explodes on contact [Fan-wiki].
  - The official text says the blast is smaller than the Big Shot's [Game text via wiki]. The wiki's infobox numbers agree (AoE 75/150 against 100/200), but its prose and trivia say "larger AoE" [Fan-wiki conflict].
  - The Dec 2013 official values also make it smaller: minimum-damage range 90 against the Big Shot's 100 [Canon; comparison Inference].
  - No image exists [Unverified].
- **Images viewed:** VF/img/png_GoldMember.png, png_DEMO_GOLD_MEMBER.png, png_Gold_Tillman.png, png_Dark_Matter.png (VF/crops/set_gm.png, set_dm.png).

- **Confidence:** partial.

## Dark Matter (primary; BBR by March 2012 → BBG; 60,000 Joules, level 9)
- **Official text:**
  - "Amazing blast range, and utterly disorientating when consumed by one. Be very careful with friendly fire and consider using ammo equipment to up its 1 shot reserve! " [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Matter_(BBG), rev 77345)
  - 2.6 log: "Dark Matter / Maximum Damage decreased from 70 to 60 / Clip size increased from 1 to 3 / Explosive +1 decreased from 12 to 10 / Explosive +2 increased from 18 to 20" [Canon]. So the "1 shot reserve" wording describes the clip before Dec 2013 [Inference]. The infobox now shows 4 ammo [Fan-wiki].
- **Status / history:**
  - Price went 50,000 → 20,000 → 60,000 Joules [Fan-wiki].
  - "Up until v1.4.3, the tip of the Dark Matter had never glowed" [Fan-wiki].
  - The wiki's 1.4.3 preview lists: "Purple pulsing light emitting from the spinning barrel run." [Fan-wiki, sic]. The Updates table dates 1.4.3 to April 15, 2013 and 1.4.3.1 to April 23, 2013; the 1.4.3 page says the update "has been released, but as 1.4.3.1" [Fan-wiki].
  - The "spinning barrel" wording implies the prong crown spins [Inference].
- **Look:**
  - A long, chunky violet blaster (#A545C4 lit; first-editor #73298A/#9140AF; near-black #1B161A), held two-handed at the hip.
  - Yellow light slot on top (#F7D360) and a crown of black-and-purple claw prongs around a glowing magenta-violet tip (#FD6BFD/#A153DF) [Observed: VF/img/png_DarkMatter.png, sampled].
  - The 2012 BBR model is the same gun with no tip glow, which fits the 1.4.3 trivia [Observed: VF/img/png_Dark_Matter.png].
  - In April 2013 the in-game shop preview shows the gun at "BUY NOW 60,000" with a strong magenta glow (Pandini skin) [Observed: VF/img/png_Light_matter_jpeg.png, File:Light matter.jpeg, which is misnamed].
  - In 2D art the casing is purple with starry speckles and a gold panel, and black prongs hold a dark core in a purple glow. He carries it angled up past the shoulder [Observed: VF/crops/art2d.png].
  - Size: about 0.8–1.0× Tillman's height long, roughly 0.75–0.9× Oliver [Inference].
- **Firing:** a "single, slow moving purple orb" that travels in a straight line at 600 u/s, firing every 1 s [Fan-wiki].
- **Projectile:**
  - White-hot cores in a hot-pink/magenta glow, wrapped in curved pink blade-like swirl segments, with thin pale-blue electric arcs between orbs [Observed: VF/img/png_Dearly_matter_close_up.png; wiki caption "Dark Matter projectiles"; first-editor samples #FD78B2/#E85D94].
  - On screen they read hot pink, not the "purple" of the wiki text [Observed vs Fan-wiki].
- **Impact / end result:**
  - The wiki: "This explosion is notably large, dark, and electrified". "When it detonates, a sphere of energy blocks the target's vision and deals damage, hence making them unable to see for a second or two, regardless of whether they are a teammate or on the enemy team" [Fan-wiki].
  - No captioned impact image exists [Unverified].
  - A giant translucent magenta sphere fills the trailer at about 14–16 s (V/trailer t_0029–t_0033) [Observed]. This sits in the segment just before the Astoria card (t_0034); each card follows its character's segment, so the sphere is most likely Astoria's Fairy's Dust, not Dark Matter [Inference].
  - File:Infinite Light Matter hacker.png shows the screenshot-taker holding a purple Dark Matter (HUD 0/2 RELOAD) amid blue orbs and cyan lightning [Observed: VF/crops/lm_sheet.png]. The blue effects match Light Matter shots, probably from the hacker named in the title [Inference]. The image comes from a cheating context, so do not use it as a Dark Matter reference.
- **Images viewed:** VF/img/png_DarkMatter.png, png_Dark_Matter.png, png_Dearly_matter_close_up.png (VF/crops/set_dm.png, dm_lm_pair.png); VF/crops/art2d.png; VF/crops/lm_sheet.png (Infinite Light Matter hacker, Light matter.jpeg); VF/crops/tr_sheet3.png.
- **Depiction for shorts:** a slow hot-pink orb with a white core, pink swirl blades and crackling pale-blue arcs [Observed]. It bursts into a large, dark, crackling energy sphere [Fan-wiki "dark", "electrified"] that briefly blanks the victim's view; show it as a dark vignette with dizzy stars [BBG News invention].
- **Confidence:** partial.
