## Super Size Me! (secondary ability; added in BBG 2.20, 29 May 2015; 2026 status unknown)

**Official text**
- "Super Size your huggable! Damage foes with the stomps of your feet while they scream in terror of your size! " [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Super_Size_Me!)
- SkyVu's 2.20 Facebook post, as shown in SW's screen recording "BATTLE BEARS GOLD | UPDATE 2.20 | COMING TODAY! 5-29-15" [Canon; Observed only in fan thumbnails https://i.ytimg.com/vi/cDW-mWikVAk/sddefault.jpg and hq1.jpg, low resolution]:
  - The header reads "Photos from BATTLE BEARS's post".
  - Text: "The update has been approved and will appear on your device with-in a few hours! Here are two sweet new bundles coming out for the update! Check out the new items: -B1000 Bass Cannon -Huggable Super-Size Me -Saberi ClubCobra -Wil Sleepover -Sandchez … Loud & Proud Bundle -Bass Cannon -Super Size Me -Acreambo Pistols".

**Status and era**
- Added in 2.20, which the versions table dates "May 29th, 2015" [Fan-wiki: Battle Bears Gold/Versions]. SW's video title "COMING TODAY! 5-29-15" agrees [Observed: oEmbed title].
- Level 10, 150,000 Joules [Fan-wiki].
- Sold in two bundles [Fan-wiki: Bundle Deals]:
  - "Loud & Proud" (with Bass Cannon and Acreambo Pistols), which the SkyVu post confirms [Canon];
  - a "World Cup 2015 bundle" (with Headlights and Charged Run). Its art (Image3.png, uploaded 2015-06-27) shows only a headless Huggable with a rainbow column and a running Huggable with a rainbow trail. No giant appears [Observed].
- "It used to be the only Huggable secondary that heals while being used, although it no longer heals" (undated) [Fan-wiki].
- It appears in no 2026 frame I opened. Whether it exists in 2026 is [Unverified].

The robot is B-1000 with the Bass Cannon [Inference from the bundle contents "-B1000 Bass Cannon", Canon].

  - Because B-1000 stands in the foreground, the true in-game multiplier cannot be read from the art [Inference].
- **Face:** a wide, open, happy mouth; round dark-plum eyes (#794F65) with white catchlights [Observed].
- **Head band:** a tan band (#D9CCB2 to #E5D8C0) crosses the top of the head. It is probably the strap of the radio the BBG Huggable wears on its head [Inference from the wiki's "radio attached to its head" and the strap in the World Cup art].

## Double Rainbow! (primary ability, default; BBR Gear Up by Oct 2012, then BBG; seen in the 2026 official trailer)

**Official text**
- "Attack in all directions for a brief period of time. Use when diving into large groups of hapless victims!" [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Double_Rainbow!)
- The matchmaking screen has a line "DOUBLE RAINBOW! ACROSS THE SKY", which the wiki notes is "A quote from the hilarious double rainbow youtube video" [Game text via wiki: Matchmaking Screen Comments].

**Status and era**
- The default primary [Fan-wiki].
- Infobox [Fan-wiki]: Explosives (per second) 45–90; AoE "Max Dmg 125.0, Min Dmg 250.0"; Duration 4.0 s; Recharge 7 s.
- History (from the main dossier): BBG 2.6 changed the duration from 3 to 5, which conflicts with today's 4.0. Since v2.2 it no longer keeps spinning after the Huggable dies [Fan-wiki].
- **2026:** the official Steam trailer shows a rainbow hoop around an orange Huggable at 7.2–8.5 s [Observed]. I identify it as Double Rainbow! [Inference, strong]: it matches the Gear Up card pose (arms out, ring at the waist) and the wiki's "rotate around the user".

### Look (new in-game evidence)
**2026 official trailer** (D/trailer/dr_007–dr_020, D/trailer/dr_7p40.png; crops trz_sheet.jpg, dr_7p40_zoom.png, dr_7p40_wide.png):
- An orange Huggable carrying a blue team flag stands with its arms out in a T. A thin, flat rainbow hoop circles its belly and hips, seen almost edge-on [Observed].
- The hoop's outer diameter is about 331 px against the Huggable's ears-to-feet height of about 137 px, so roughly 2.4× the Huggable's height [Observed, measured at 7.4 s]. On the BBG News scale chart (Huggable = 0.65× Oliver), that is about 1.5× Oliver's height across [Inference].
- Stripes at the hoop's left tip run from red-orange outside (#DE4A36, #FF6A3D, #FFA83B) through yellow-green (#BBC340) to blue-violet inside. The samples blend because of motion blur [Observed, sampled].
- It reads as one continuous hoop. Whether that is two arcs blurred by speed is [Unverified].

### Firing
- One tap, centred on the Huggable. It "creates two spinning rainbows that rotate around the user and damage anyone within its range, lasting for several seconds" [Fan-wiki].
- Using a melee weapon ends it early [Fan-wiki].
- "As it is nearing the end of its duration, the range of the two rainbows will slowly decrease until they completely disappear" [Fan-wiki].
- Sound: undocumented. A fan audio rip, "Battle Bears Gold: Huggable All Voice Lines & Sounds" (PLqiq1cIhwI, Slinkster), exists but I could not play it [Unverified].

None. The ribbons orbit the user [Fan-wiki; Observed].

## Key finding: the "CANDY!" box is a skin, not a Phantom Hug launcher

- **What the box is.** It is the head gear of the **Gummable** skin. The wiki says it has "a box of gum in the place of a walkie-talkie on its head", and that on the red team the body is "a bright, sleek red surface, meant to resemble a type of gummy bear" (blue on the blue team) [Fan-wiki: Gummable].
  - Skin text: "The ultimate sugar rush! Big pharmaceutical will never be able to produce enough ritalin to slow you down!" [Game text via wiki]. The skin costs 120 Gas [Fan-wiki]. Keep the Ritalin joke out of 13+ shorts [BBG News invention].
- **Texture and renders.** The skin texture (Gummable_expanded_model.png, uploaded 2012-11-27) has a tile reading "CANDY!" in white on pink rings and lime-yellow ground, with two teal wrapped candies [Observed].
  - Sampled: pink #EB4BCC, lime-yellow #E3F84D, teal #03BCC6 to #2494E4.
  - The renders show a glossy red (#FC0025) or blue (#00AFEC) gummy body with a green-ball antenna tip [Observed: Gummable.png, Gummable_skin.png].
- **In the thumbnails.** NhymMnwYDGk hq2 shows the CANDY! box strapped on top of the red Huggable's head, where the radio normally sits, with the paw raised empty [Observed]. The "crank and green-ball lever" in the earlier note is the antenna with its green ball tip [Observed].
- **72lHonSZBAI hqdefault.** It shows a pink Huggable's head from behind with the normal green radio box and bandage, not a CANDY! box [Observed: crops/72l_box.png]. The video was uploaded 2014-03-30, not 2013 [Invidious metadata].

## Phantom Hug (secondary, saboteur; BBG 2.2, 21 Aug 2013; not seen in 2026)

- **Official text:**
  - "The Huggable's ability to love will make time seem like it comes to a halt. When actually it's just your feet and you're about to die." [Game text via wiki] (https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Phantom_Hug)
  - The 2.2 in-game Bundle Deals panel reads "PHANTOM HUG / EQUIPPED", with stat bars DURATION, RELOAD and COOLDOWN and "SPEED +5%". It is stamped "2.2" [Observed: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/NhymMnwYDGk/hq3.jpg; crops/nhym_hq3_menu.png; fan upload 2013-08-21]. This confirms the wiki's "5% speed boost" in game footage.

**Look**
- **No launcher exists in footage.**
  - In PrinceofBB's "Phantom Hug Function" the red Gummable fires with empty paws. In every storyboard frame from 42 to 104 s, and in hq2, one arm is raised and nothing is held [Observed: sb/NhymMnwYDGk/cs_01.jpg, cs_02.jpg].
  - GoogleMyFist's pink default Huggable is also empty-handed [Observed: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/HhqYgxFKCdw/hqdefault.jpg].
  - The raised-arm pose appears in clips with other loadouts too (DxcoExF0w5Q, 1NuJT2vl1sQ). So it is the general in-game Huggable pose, not a Phantom Hug stance [Observed across clips; Inference].
- **Icons.** The menu and HUD icon is a white Huggable face followed by three grey copies [Observed: NhymMnwYDGk hq3, labelled]. This upgrades the earlier [Inference].
  - In the same list: Tough Love is a pink face with red hearts, Mini Me is a pink face with a dark silhouette, and Peek A Boo is a pink face in a white cloud [Observed].
- **Menu display.** It is the promo art: a pink ghost Huggable hugging the face of a screaming white bear in a red belt [Observed: hq3].

**Firing**
- Single shot. Ammo 1, reload 5 s, hold duration 2.5 s [Fan-wiki infobox].
- PrinceofBB's description: "It freezes the enemy for a second. It's best to partner with Static Shuffle." [fan text, NhymMnwYDGk description via Invidious].
- No muzzle flash or launch pose distinct from the idle pose is visible [Observed absence, low resolution].

**Projectile**
- **In flight.** A small pale pink-white Huggable-shaped figure followed by two or three hollow pink rings in a line [Observed: m9abgJLwXus storyboard t=10 s, crops/m9ab_proj.png]. Sampled #F87B99 to #F99AF6.
- **From the victim's camera.** It reads as a white-hot glowing Huggable silhouette with pink outlines and a small yellow-orange glow at its base [Observed: x1d0ggW_W64 t=5 s, crops/x1d_5s_ghost.png]. Sampled core #F7FFFF to #FFCFFD, mean #E3AEDB.
- **The "clone".** A solid-looking, bright pink-magenta, featureless Huggable figure with arms out, about the same height as the shooter (≈0.65x Oliver) [Observed: m9abgJLwXus t=26 s, crops/m9ab_clone.png]. Sampled mean #F19ACE, #FA95F5, highlights #FDC4ED.
  - Reading this as the wiki's "briefly creates a 'clone' of the character" is [Inference] [Fan-wiki for the quote].
- The wiki calls it "a cluster of pink energy in the shape of a Huggable" [Fan-wiki].
- Speed is not measurable from the storyboards [Unverified].

**Impact / end result**
- **Burst.** A soft pale pink-white cloud flares over the target, not a hard-edged sphere [Observed]:
  - NhymMnwYDGk t=60 s: #F29DD4, #F5D0D8, #FFD3FF.
  - NhymMnwYDGk t=90 s: pink-white rings and blobs between shooter and dummy, #C9A7C8 to #FFD8FF.

  - x1d0ggW_W64 t=6 s: the victim's screen is fogged white-pink.

  - A bold yellow block "Z" (#FDFC04) floats over the victim's head, with two white-grey crescent arrows (#E9DEE2) curving around it like an orbit [Observed: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/HhqYgxFKCdw/hq2.jpg; HhqYgx storyboard 205, 320 and 370 s; Ac4XjUzc6tY 14 to 17 s; x1d0ggW_W64 6 to 7 s; NhymMnwYDGk 98 s].

  - Pale pink "ghost arms" flare on both sides of a held bear [Observed: Ac4XjUzc6tY t=14 s, crops/ac4_14s.png]. Sampled #E67DDA and #F6BDF2.
  - This matches the wiki's yellow Z for "Slowed", with Phantom Hug listed as "(immobile)" [Fan-wiki: Status Effects].
- **Held targets still take hits.** Yellow melee hit-stars burst on a held bear while the Z stays up [Observed: Ac4XjUzc6tY 16 to 17 s; HhqYgx hq2]. The Z was visible in four consecutive 1 s frames (≥3 s) in Ac4, against the wiki's 2.5 s [Observed vs Fan-wiki; the cause is Unverified].
