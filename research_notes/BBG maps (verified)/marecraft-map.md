# Marecraft (Map): "Marecraft Arena" / "The Marecraft"

Marecraft is the BBG arena set inside the Marecraft Carrier, the giant unicorn that carries an aircraft carrier and is a boss in *Battle Bears: Zombies* [Fan-wiki]. Teams fight on dark-metal fortifications built over orange-red living tissue, lime-green digestive pools and intestine-like tunnels [Fan-wiki] [Observed]. The map has several names:
- Wiki page title: "Marecraft (Map)" [Fan-wiki].
- Wiki prose and the official teaser title: "Marecraft Arena" [Fan-wiki] [Canon].
- Teaser overlay: "THE MARECRAFT" [Canon].
- 2026 soundtrack track title: "Marecraft" [Canon].

## Era and status
- **Origin.** The Marecraft Carrier is the Stage 3 boss of *Battle Bears: Zombies* [Fan-wiki] ("obtained in Battle Bears: Zombies directly before the Stage 3 boss battle, the Marecraft Carrier", Machine Gun page). The wiki describes it as "a colossal mare (female horse, or unicorn, in this case) that holds Marecrafts, which appear as kawaii-but-deadly unicorns on magic carpets that dive-bomb towards Oliver in a kamikaze-style attack" [Fan-wiki] (Marecraft Carrier).
- **Teased** Apr 5, 2013 [Canon] (SSwlTVbW8Wg).
- **Added** in update 1.4.3 of the app then marketed as "Battle Bears" [Canon: "New level coming to Battle Bears in update 1.4.3… in April, 2013"]. The wiki files this app as Battle Bears Royale (BBR); 1.4.3 was BBR's last update before the BBG relaunch. Dates:
  - 1.4.3: April 15, 2013 [Fan-wiki: Updates].
  - "This update has been released, but as 1.4.3.1" [Fan-wiki: BBR Version 1.4.3]. 1.4.3.1: Apr 23, 2013 [Fan-wiki: Battle Bears Royale infobox and Updates].
  - The wiki map page was created Apr 23, 2013 and last edited Aug 7, 2014 [Observed: API revision list, 48 revisions]. It therefore describes the 2013–14 game.
  - The map page says "added in Update 1.4.3 of Battle Bears Gold", because the wiki files BBG as the same game, "previously known as Battle Bears Royale" [Fan-wiki]. BBG was not a plain rename: the wiki's own History section says the game "was re-released on the App Store as Battle Bears Gold, shutting down the original Battle Bears Royale app" on May 24, 2013, and its BBR page calls BBG "a completely separate app" [Fan-wiki].
- **New weapons in the same update:** Dead Eye, Fairy's Dust (called "Fairy's Sparkle" in the 1.4.3.1 notes), Static Shuffle, Señor Potato Cannon, Sole's Bane and Gernobyl [Fan-wiki transcription].
- **BBR and BBG lists:**

  - File:Aaaaa.jpg is a 2048 × 1536 (iPad-size) in-match screenshot of the map, uploaded May 28, 2013, four days after BBG 1.0 [Observed]. That it shows the BBG build is [Inference].

## Setting
- "Players fight inside the Carrier's body" [Fan-wiki]. "The Marecraft is fought inside the Marecraft carrier's digestive system" [Fan-wiki]. The BBR 1.4.3 wiki page calls the map "a resuscitation of the Marecraft Carrier from Battle Bears: Zombies - the original Battle Bears game" [Fan-wiki].
- **Exterior reference (BBZ boss art)** [Observed: File:BBbossMarecraftcareor.png, uploaded 2012; official origin Inference]:
  - A huge pale-yellow unicorn (lit coat ≈ #FFEA91) with a grumpy face, a blue mane and tail (≈ #76A2F5 and #8BBBFF), a lavender spiral horn and a pink udder (≈ #F7AD86).
  - A grey aircraft-carrier deck with superstructure (≈ #9E9E9E) sits on its back.
  - Small unicorns on flying rugs fly around it under a stormy grey sky (≈ #828282) above dark olive hills (≈ #4D4D44).
- **The Marecrafts** [Observed: File:Marecraft.jpg]:
  - Off-white cartoon unicorn foals with pink, mint or lilac manes and grey-striped spiral horns.
  - They stand on ornate red Persian-style carpets (the background foals ride blue and purple carpets) under a pale-blue sky.
  - The wiki's Huggable Planet page shows a different, comic-style golden unicorn on a red carpet captioned "Marecraft" [Observed: File:Unicorn_on_carpet.jpg; source not given]. It says Marecrafts "only ever leave the carpet to eat grass" and "reside on the Marecraft Carrier and appear to be tamable" [Fan-wiki].
- **Theme:** body horror played as a cartoon. Living flesh, veins, glands and digestive fluid share space with blocky, militarised dark-metal scaffolding [Observed].
  - The wiki guesses the structures "were possibly built to support the huge Carrier on top of the giant mare, but this is just a guess" [Fan-wiki, speculation].
  - Huggables crew the Carrier in the BBZ cutscene: "Unicorn A-45 ready for landing. Get that crap off the deck Sailor!" / "Aye Aye, captain!" [Fan-wiki]. So reading the forts as Huggable-built is reasonable [Inference].

## Layout
The basis is the wiki's top-down overview File:Marecraft_Arena.jpg (700 × 703 px, uploaded May 2, 2013). It is a cut-away render overlaid with spawn crosshairs, white pickup squares and a BATTLE BEARS watermark [Observed]. Its official origin is [Unverified]; the same image is the thumbnail of fan upload H7AmDfTYK8k [Observed]. Below, "red end" is the top of the overview and "purple end" the bottom.
- **Overall shape.** The flesh floor is a long rectangle, about twice as long as it is wide along the team axis. Dark metal flank decks widen its middle half, so the whole footprint is roughly square [Observed]. Each half is the other turned through 180° [Observed].
- **Spawns.** Two staggered rows of crosshair markers at each end, nine visible per end: red at the top, purple at the bottom [Observed]. Purple is very likely the blue team, because a blue socket and a navy-faced ring sit at that end [Inference].
- **Centre.**
  - A dark-metal bridge runs along the team axis between the two halves. Each side carries four square post brackets, and torn flesh edges overlap both ends [Observed: overview].
    - A dark A-frame gateway arch spans its end [Observed: File:Imagefbvhiqhb.jpg]. The black bars at the bridge ends in the overview are probably these arches seen from above [Inference].
    - The wiki: "The sides are separated by a bridge and connected around the outside by a metal landing pad. On the bridge is a Bearscream Sandwich and under it is two shields and an Imperial Basket Grande" [Fan-wiki].
  - A cross-walkway runs side to side at mid-length, from flank deck to flank deck [Observed]. The wiki's "intersection below the large bridge" puts it under the bridge [Fan-wiki].
  - Three white pickup squares lie in a line across the map: two on the walkway near the sides, and one at the centre where bridge and walkway cross [Observed]. That fits the Shields "at the sides of the arena" and the stacked Sandwich and Imperial Basket Grande at the centre [Fan-wiki for the pickups; Inference for the match].
  - Four rectangular green pools fill the quadrants between bridge and walkway [Observed]. The wiki: "Below each tower is a canal of green digestive juices" [Fan-wiki].
  - Two square sockets with dark round holes sit on the bridge deck: a red one toward the red end and a blue one toward the purple end [Observed: overview]. A blue square is also visible on the bridge in File:Marecraftt.jpg and the ZTRPKofM0cw thumbnail, and a red one in the 16-map grid's Marecraft tile [Observed]. The wiki says Plant the Bomb stages "feature a bomb in each team's color and a bomb tube in each team's color", with each bomb starting "on a bomb platform in the same color" [Fan-wiki]. So these are probably bomb tubes or platforms [Inference].
- **Flanks.** Dark-metal decks run along both long sides [Observed]: the wiki's "metal landing pad" connecting the halves "around the outside" [Fan-wiki].
  - Each deck shades from a dark red-brown tint at the red end (≈ #432A27 to #512E2A) to a dark navy tint at the purple end (≈ #302D3A to #393238) [Observed]. That this is team-coded lighting is [Inference].
  - Pairs of orange thorn-like spikes cross both outer edges. The east edge also has a long, thin yellow-tan curved arc, like a rib; the west edge is a thick curved orange-red wall of flesh [Observed].
- **Each base half** (positions given for the red end; the purple end is rotated 180°):
  - A crimson, tongue- or liver-shaped organ mound between the spawn and the bridge [Observed].
  - A squat round sniper tower at the outer corner of a green pool. Its satellite dish faces the centre and a white pickup square sits on top [Observed]. Wiki: "There is a sniper tower on each side"; Health Packs are "On the towers by each team's spawn point" (2) [Fan-wiki: Health Pack] or "on the small battlements in each team's base" [Fan-wiki: map page].
  - A mound of woven, straw- or wicker-like orange fibre in the corner on the tower's side [Observed]. It may be bedding for the wiki's "unicorn stables" [Fan-wiki for the stables; Inference for the identification].
  - In the opposite corner, a round flesh mound or crater with a pale bumpy rim and a deep-red centre, inside a dark castellated wall with spiky fins [Observed]. Its function is undocumented [Unverified].
  - At the far end of the arena, a dark octagonal ring with flesh visible inside [Observed].
    - At the red end its inner face reads red. At the purple end it reads navy (≈ #394A64 to #363D56 in teaser maxres2) [Observed]. That the colours are team-coded is [Inference].
    - Wiki: "At the place where the entrance into the underground of the team's bases are located, at the two ends of the intersection, there is a ring-like object that is dark blue or black in colour" [Fan-wiki]. The overview places the rings at the team ends, not at the walkway's side ends [Observed], so the wiki's "intersection" may mean the whole central axis [Inference].
- **Small orange hexagonal nubs** dot the flesh floor: six are visible in the overview, two of them on the organ mounds. The icon shows two round orange bulbs (≈ #CE642E) flanking the bridge end [Observed]. Their function is undocumented [Unverified].
- **Verticality: three levels** [Inference from Fan-wiki text + Observed]:
  - (1) **Underground:** "Below each team's base is a big room, with surface and texture that looks like a tube or intestine, with cell like structures and veins and a jump pad at the end that brings players up to a base" [Fan-wiki]. The teaser shows the view through the navy ring into a red flesh tunnel, with a pink tube running along its floor and a teal-cored pad at the far end [Observed: SSwlTVbW8Wg maxres2/hq2].
  - (2) **Flesh floor and the lower walkway** over the pools [Fan-wiki] [Observed].
  - (3) **Bridge, battlements and tower tops** [Fan-wiki] [Observed]. "If the players fall off the battlements onto the edge where the gland-like organs are, it is still possible to get out without falling into the canal, by travelling along the uneven and protruding gland" [Fan-wiki].
- **Tower access.** A teal-cored octagonal pad lies on the flesh floor among each tower's legs [Observed: teaser maxresdefault; 2026 thumbnail _R4rvvGlRsM/maxres1]. File:Aaaaa.jpg shows a jump pad between two thick dark pillars under an overhang, probably the same spot [Inference]. That the pad launches players onto the tower is [Inference].
- **Sightlines:** the towers overlook the bridge and pools [Observed], which fits the archived Astoria "tall towers" tip [Canon per prior notes; Unverified this session].
