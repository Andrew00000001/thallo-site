# Haunted Castle

Haunted Castle is the Battle Bears Halloween map. A blocky slate-blue walled castle stands at night on a figure-8 island. Inside the walls is a terracotta courtyard with a tall central tower on legs, lit by jack-o'-lanterns. Outside the walls, graveyard lawns hold each team's spawn [Observed]. The sky has a grey full moon carved with a jack-o'-lantern face [Observed, 2013 gameplay screenshot]. The map debuted in **Battle Bears Royale (BBR) v1.3.3**, dated October 10, 2012 by the wiki [Game text via wiki; Fan-wiki]. Battle Bears Gold (BBG) was a separate app that replaced BBR on May 24, 2013 [Fan-wiki]. The map was dropped at the 2013 BBR-to-BBG switch [Fan-wiki; Inference] and came back in **BBG 2.4 (24 Oct 2013)** "To celebrate the holiday" [Canon]. SkyVu has not named it for the 2026 re-release. However, fan video metadata places it in the July and September 2026 playtest builds [Unverified].

## Era and status
- **Debut:** BBR v1.3.3, the Halloween update [Game text via wiki].
  - Dated October 10, 2012 in the wiki's Updates table [Fan-wiki]. The wiki page itself was created 15 Oct 2012 [Fan-wiki, page history].
  - Plant the Bomb already existed; BBR 1.3 added it on May 3, 2012 [Game text via wiki].
  - A fan upload dated Oct 13, 2012, "Battle Bears Royale: Halloween skins and new map" (5bmlVjX-oK8), has the description "I play with the Demo in the new Haunted Castle..." [Unverified, fan metadata].
- **The page's own wording is anachronistic.** It says "added to Battle Bears Gold in v1.3.3"; a 4 Jun 2013 edit summarised "Changed BBR to BBG" made that change [Fan-wiki].
  - The wiki's BBG intro says BBG was "previously known as Battle Bears Royale", but its History section says that on May 24, 2013 the game "was re-released on the App Store as Battle Bears Gold, shutting down the original Battle Bears Royale app", and its BBR page calls BBG "a completely separate app" [Fan-wiki]. So BBG was a new app that replaced BBR, not a rename [Inference]. In Oct 2012 the game was BBR [Inference].
- **Late 2012 to spring 2013 (BBR):**
  - Our notes list the map on the official BBR maps page of Nov 2012 ("Haunted Castle, Abusement Park, Facing Temples, Battlefield Airpad, Desert Airmine, Huggable Factory, Spacecraft.") [Unverified this session].
  - Fan upload "Battle Bears Royale: Gnifle & RJC in the Haunted Castle" is dated Dec 3, 2012 [Unverified].
  - Wiki-hosted in-game screenshots were uploaded on May 10 and May 14, 2013 [Fan-wiki file metadata]. That the map was still in BBR then is a weak [Inference], since an upload date is not a capture date.
- **Removal.** Current page: "It was later removed alongside the Desert Airmine map in the 2.0 update, not only because they were the least popular maps in a poll on the Battle Bears website, but to also allow more server space." [Fan-wiki]
  - **What "2.0" means.** A 4 Jun 2013 revision, written before BBG 2.1 existed (15 Jul 2013), already said "As of version 2.0, the Haunted Castle was removed from Battle Bears Gold and will likely be playable around the Halloween period as a special event map." [Fan-wiki, old revision]

  - The Updates table has no "2.0" row; it runs 1.0 (May 24, 2013), 1.0.1, 1.0.2, then 2.1 (July 15, 2013) [Fan-wiki]. A 18 Jul 2013 edit changed the wording to "As of version 2.1"; it was changed back to "2.0" on 22 Jul [Fan-wiki, history].
  - Best reading: the wiki's "2.0" is the BBG relaunch of May 24, 2013, so the map was absent from BBG at launch [Inference].
  - **Stated reasons, in old revisions** [Fan-wiki, old revisions; SkyVu's statement Unverified]:

## Setting
- A haunted castle dressed for Halloween, always shown at night [Observed]. The wiki says it "has several Halloween decorations, like pumpkins, tombstones and graves" [Fan-wiki].
- The castle sits on a floating island that ends in black void [Observed: overhead]. No in-universe location is given [Unverified].
- **2014 blurb.** If the official blurb is accurate, the castle is home to "The Battle Bears Family" [Unverified quote]. Reading that as an Addams Family joke is [Inference].
- **Theme kit:** stone castle, graveyard, jack-o'-lanterns, pumpkins, crates, cobwebs, iron railing, team banners and a jack-o'-lantern moon [Observed]. A coffin and a pink stitched bear dummy appear only in a developer prop capture [Observed; in-map presence Unverified].

  - That castle is daytime: beige stone, teal- and red-capped towers, a plank-walkway tower, hedges, and a headstone graveyard behind a black railing, on a floating island under a blue sky [Observed].
  - That this is the map of the soundtrack track "Castle Royale" is [Inference]. Do not use it for Haunted Castle scenes.
