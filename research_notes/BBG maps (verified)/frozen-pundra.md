# Frozen Pundra

Frozen Pundra is a Battle Bears Gold (BBG) map: an enclosed, snow-floored ice cave added in update 2.7 (January 2014) [Fan-wiki + Canon]. Four things identify it:
- a squat dark rock "dome" under a thick, overhanging snow cap, with an archway at its base [Fan-wiki + Observed];
- a broad, raised, snow-topped ring track that circles the dome [Fan-wiki + Observed];
- a ceiling fringed with icicles, some of which can be shot down [Fan-wiki + Observed];
- a small snowman outside the track [Fan-wiki].

No SkyVu prose description of the map has been found [Unverified]. The layout and mechanics therefore come from the fan wiki, and the look comes from images I opened myself.

## Setting
- Wiki: "a cave covered almost entirely in snow with large icicles hanging off the ceiling" [Fan-wiki].
- It is fully underground and enclosed [Observed]. Every view I opened has an ice or rock ceiling and no sky, sun or horizon: the wiki tile, the official preview frames, the grid tile, the 2014 fan frames and the probable 2026 frame. The wiki calls it "quite enclosed" [Fan-wiki].
- No story or location context is documented: no planet, base or faction [Unverified]. "Pundra" reads as a pun on "tundra" [Inference].
- **Do not confuse it with the 2.6 Christmas loading screen** (wiki File:Sanchezhohoho_loading.jpg, uploaded 2013-12-21) [Observed]. That art:
  - reads "NEW CHARACTER SANCHEZ / NEW CHRISTMAS SKINS / LOADING...";
  - shows an *outdoor* snowfield under a starry purple night sky, with icy blue mountains, candy canes and a horde of pink Huggables.
  - It is not Frozen Pundra.

## Layout
- Wiki description [Fan-wiki]: "A circular highway-like rock track raised above the ground by rocks circles the map, while a large rock dome occupies the centre of the map. There are also many tall mounds of snow situated in between. Overall, the map is quite enclosed, with [[Turkish SW Boots]] users often finding themselves hitting obstacles."
- **Ring track** [Observed]:
  - It is a broad, smooth, flat-topped slab with rounded edges. Its top is snow, cyan in some views and lilac-grey in others.
  - From the cave floor it passes above head height: it crosses the frame in front of the dome in -_OustCemeQ (hq720) and crosses diagonally overhead in kp3JRiORqx8 hq2.
  - In 1HxwjhH-Qao hq1 a dark rock block sits beneath it. In D7yAXVPGi5E hq2 a dark faceted rock arch spans the snow floor. In the wiki tile a stubby pale support stands under the track at right.
  - In SkyVu's preview (Ev5WvMIp1_4 sddefault and hqdefault) a broad snow path winds upward between a dark indigo rock wall and an ice spire. It is probably a stretch of the ring track [Inference].
  - The draft's "icicles hang from its underside" is **not supported**. iy_MND-ch1s hq2 shows icicles hanging *over* the track from above [Observed].
- **Central dome** [Observed]:
  - A squat, blocky mass of dark faceted rock with painted cracks, under a thick, overhanging snow cap that gives it a frosted-cake silhouette (wiki tile; -_OustCemeQ hq720).
  - A dark archway runs through its base. Round launch pads with cyan glow rings sit under the arch and just beside it (-_OustCemeQ hq720).
  - The wiki adds that the Bearscream Sandwich is "Located inside the near the roof of the rock dome directly in the centre of the map, accessible by a Jump Pad below" [Fan-wiki, verbatim, typo included]. The pad under the arch is probably that launch [Inference].
- **Pickups** [Fan-wiki]:
  - Imperial Basket Grande: "Each located on opposite sides of the map, below the track". The Imperial Basket Grande page instead says "At the top and bottom corners of the map", 2 of them. The two pages disagree.
  - Health Pack: "Each located on large snow-covered rocks on opposite sides of the map".
  - Shield: "Each located on large snow-covered rocks on opposite sides of the map."
  - The Bearscream Sandwich page's own location list omits Frozen Pundra, but says "There is always at least one Bearscream Sandwich in every map".
  - Paired pickups on opposite sides suggest a mirror-symmetric two-sided layout [Inference].
- **Other features** [Observed]:
  - pale snow-topped ledges jutting in front of dark indigo rock (D7yAXVPGi5E hq3);
  - snow slopes and mounds;
  - small pale ice stalagmite cones (kp3JRiORqx8 hq2 and hqdefault);
  - an "hourglass" column on the right of the wiki tile, where a hanging icicle nearly meets a floor stalagmite;
  - glassy translucent ice masses (Ev5WvMIp1_4 hq3);
  - a large round cyan form seen from high above in the preview's title-card frame (iy_MND-ch1s hq1), either a shallow floor bowl or the top of the dome's snow cap. Its identity is [Unverified].
- **Verticality:** three levels: the cave floor, the raised track, and the dome interior near its roof, reached by jump pad [Fan-wiki + Observed]. The BBG Maps page names Skate or Die, Sky View and Toxic Terror as "the only maps that contain no Jump Pads", so Frozen Pundra has them [Fan-wiki]. Pads are visible here [Observed].
- **Spawns and team sides:** not documented [Unverified].
  - The "YOU'RE ON TEAM RED" frame (1HxwjhH-Qao hq1, score 0 : 0) looks across the track at the snow-capped dome [Observed]. The red spawn probably faces the centre [Inference].
  - In the preview's title-card frame (iy_MND-ch1s hq1, a re-upload credited to SkyVu), blue-glowing figures stand at the left and red-armoured figures around a pale cyan disc at the right [Observed]. These are probably the opposing spawn groups [Inference].
  - Which compass side is red or blue is unknown.

## Look
- **Overall:** a bright, cool, candy-blue ice cave, not a dark or scary one [Observed].
  - The rock is low-poly and faceted with painted cracks.
  - The snow is smooth and rounded, with soft blue streaks.
  - The ice is translucent with sharp facets.
  - The ceiling is a rippled periwinkle-to-cyan sheet hung with dense icicle fringes.

## Hazards and interactive elements
- **Falling icicles** [Fan-wiki]:
  - "It is the game's third interactive map, as some large lightly-coloured icicles can drop from the ceiling and deal damage to players below if they are dealt enough damage. However, due to the fact that they can take several seconds to drop, do not deal significant damage and each have a small area of effect, using these icicles in direct combat is not a recommended strategy."
  - Trivia: "Firing at the icicles causes them to fall off and disappear on contact with the floor."
  - How many there are and whether they respawn is [Unverified].
  - An earlier interactive map was Haunted Castle: a 2012 update line reads "NEW Haunted Castle map with interactive features!" [Fan-wiki, quoting the update text].
- **Jump pads** [Fan-wiki + Observed]: at least one under the dome leads to the Bearscream Sandwich [Fan-wiki]. Pads are visible under and beside the dome arch and on the cave floor [Observed].
- **Pickup timings** [Fan-wiki]:
  - Bearscream Sandwich "doubles the damage of all the player's weapons for 20 seconds" and "respawns every 90 seconds".
  - The Imperial Basket Grande heals 50% of maximum health, can overheal to 200%, and takes "60 seconds to respawn".
- **Enclosure:** "Turkish SW Boots users often finding themselves hitting obstacles" [Fan-wiki].
- No slippery ice, cold damage or other environmental mechanic is documented [Unverified].
