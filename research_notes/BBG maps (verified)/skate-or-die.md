# Skate or Die

Skate or Die is a Battle Bears Gold (BBG) map: a sunny, walled concrete skatepark under two elevated highway decks [Fan-wiki; Observed]. Battle Bears graffiti covers its walls, piers and floor [Fan-wiki; Observed]. Its signature pieces [Observed]:
- dry concrete bowls painted to look like water, one with a leaping painted goldfish;
- honey-coloured plywood kickers and fun-boxes with rust-orange rails, and long black steel ledges;
- a giant round camera eye set into a wall: a grey bezel, a glowing blue lens and a trailing black cable, next to an "ABBi" tag (the wiki captions a screenshot of it "ABBi in Battle Bears Gold" [Fan-wiki]);
- a pink, bone-limbed Huggable graffito next to a pier tagged "Oops, Killed ya!";
- a spray-painted copy of SkyVu's 2012 *Battle Bears* TV-series key art (Oliver, Wil and Tillman under "BATTLE BEARS"), which the wiki captions "Battle Bears TV show in the map" [Fan-wiki].

**History and 2026 status:**
- SkyVu listed it among "3 New Maps" for BBG in its trailer description, published May 30, 2013 [Canon].
- The 2026 Steam page does not name it [Canon], but it is track 7 on the official 2026 soundtrack [Canon].
- SkyVu's YouTube channel showed it in the short "Dangerous Skate Parks" (May 7, 2025) [Canon date; Observed frames] and in a 16-map grid in "BBG Maps Ranked part 1" [Observed]. That short ends on a "COMING NOVEMBER 4" card, so it belongs to the 2026 campaign [Inference].
- Fan uploads show it in the July 29 and August 21, 2026 PC playtests [Observed metadata and thumbnails].

## Setting

- **The wiki's descriptions:**
  - "It is a skating park below two highways and there is plenty of graffiti and other things from all of the Battle Bears series." [Fan-wiki]
  - "Skate or Die is a map that appears to be based on a skating park (also because of the name), and has a very wide and open area, much like No Bear's Land." [Fan-wiki]
  - "Skate or Die also has many bridges, tunnels and hills, most notably a large hill near the center of the map containing a health pack on top." [Fan-wiki]
- **What the images show:**
  - An open-air plaza enclosed by tall board-formed concrete walls with rows of tie-holes and panel seams [Observed: wiki Skate_or_Die_1–4; `abbi_bbg`].
  - Two elevated concrete highway decks cross overhead at different heights. They curve or step and rest on massive T-headed (hammerhead) piers that stand inside the play area [Observed: Skate_or_Die_2, _3].
  - Above the walls there is only sky, a "BATTLE BEARS." billboard on a pole, and a second billboard on posts [Observed: Skate_or_Die_3, _1].
- **Place in the Battle Bears world:** not documented [Unverified]. It reads as an Earth-like urban underpass skatepark [Inference]. The name plays on the skater slogan "skate or die" [Inference].
- **How SkyVu framed it in 2025:** as one of the "TOP 3 DEADLIEST SKATE PARKS", next to the real Ramp 1 in the United Kingdom [Observed]. The description's "Number 1 shouldn't be a surprise!" [Canon], and Skate or Die appearing last in the thumbnail sequence, suggest it was ranked #1 [Inference; Unverified].

## Layout

- **Overall shape:** a large, roughly rectangular walled plaza, mostly on one level, with sunken bowls and with raised platforms, banks and ramps [Observed: trailer 46.0–47.2 s; wiki screenshots].
  - The wiki calls it large ("Due to its large size Snipers can be very effective, although cover is very abundant and useful for breaking locks") and "very wide and open" [Fan-wiki].
- **Trailer overhead** (≈46.0–47.2 s; my frames `f_022` 46.1 s, `f_027` 46.6 s, `f_032` 47.1 s; provided t_0094–t_0095) [Observed]:
  - **The long painted pool:** a long, irregular, river-shaped bowl runs diagonally down the left side. Its floor is painted as water (white foam, splashes) with a painted orange goldfish leaping out of a white splash. It has steel coping, and a wooden-faced bank runs along its far edge.
  - **The small painted pool:** a smaller, angular bowl, also painted as water, sits right of the central pier in front of the rainbow block.
  - **The central pier:** an overpass pier stands mid-frame. Against it sits a black box with steel edges and a ramp sloping off one side. A small white box pickup with orange marks sits on top, an orange glow hovering above it.
  - **Street-course props:**
    - a honey-coloured plywood fun-box/kicker with a rust-red rail along its top;
    - a long, black, open-topped steel ledge (a trough);
    - short orange flat rails;
    - three short black ledges in a row by the back wall.
  - **Along the far wall, left to right:**
    - the green meadow mural with tiny pink Huggables, behind the wooden-faced bank;
    - a black-and-white graffito;
    - two wooden A-frame wedge ramps;
    - the giant ABBi camera eye set into the wall, with the "ABBi" tag and its black cable;
    - a raised concrete block painted with a rainbow ending in a skull;
    - the pier tagged "Oops, Killed ya!";
    - on the right-hand wall, the large pink bone-limbed Huggable.
  - The official 2026 grid tile and the 2013 flythrough confirm this arrangement from ground level: meadow mural left of the central pier, ABBi lens right of it [Observed: `jk_sodtile_v`, vLNAENqXIf8 hq1]. (The draft placed the meadow in "the opposite corner"; that was wrong.)
- **From the ground** (wiki gallery and fan thumbnails) [Observed]:
  - **A tall wooden quarter-pipe or half-pipe** with orange coping in a corner, near the orange kite graffito and the TV mural [Skate_or_Die_3].
  - **A wide raised wooden platform** on chunky wooden box legs, which players can walk under [flythrough vLNAENqXIf8 hq3].
  - **Concrete stairs with steel handrails** rising to a raised level, in front of the wall of pasted posters [vLNAENqXIf8 hq3; Skate_or_Die_2].
  - **Concrete banks, hips and humps**, one with a drain grate [trailer `f_022`].
  - **An octagonal, bolted steel pipe mouth** with a rusty orange interior, set into a concrete hump at the edge of the painted pool [Skate_or_Die_2]. The wiki mentions "many bridges, tunnels and hills" [Fan-wiki]; this opening is presumably one of the tunnels [Inference].
- **Central high point and pickups:**
  - The map page describes "a large hill near the center of the map containing a health pack on top" [Fan-wiki] and puts a Health Pack "on the large spire directly in the middle of the map, facing the Red team's side" [Fan-wiki]. The Health Pack page says "On top of the central ramp" [Fan-wiki].
  - The white box on the central black box in the trailer matches the wiki's Health Pack icon: a white box with an orange cross and a grey window [Observed comparison: `tr_pickups_v` vs File:HealthPack.png]. It is that Health Pack [Inference, strong].
- **Verticality:**
  - Three working levels: sunken bowls, the main floor, and raised platforms, banks and the half-pipe [Observed].
  - The highway decks are scenery above the play space; no source shows players on them [Unverified].
  - **Jump pads:** none. "Skate or Die, Sky View, and Toxic Terror are the only maps that contain no Jump Pads" [Fan-wiki: BBG Maps]. The map page contradicts itself: it says "one of the only two maps without Jump Pads, the other being Sky View", written in Aug–Nov 2013 before Toxic Terror existed, and also "one of the 3 maps that does not have jumping pads" [Fan-wiki].

  - "The two team's spawn points are very large on this map, extending almost the width of the map." [Fan-wiki]
  - In FFA, players "spawn in the same points as they would in a team-based Team Battle or Plant the Bomb game" [Fan-wiki: Free-For-All].
  - **Red side** [Fan-wiki]:

## Landmarks

1. **The overpass.** Two crossing elevated highway decks on hammerhead piers frame the park and throw big shadows [Observed: Skate_or_Die_2, _3]. The wiki describes it as "below two highways" [Fan-wiki].

   - Dry concrete bowls are painted to look like water: pale aqua with white foam in the wiki screenshots and 2026 thumbnails, deep blue in the trailer [Observed].
   - The long pool has a painted orange goldfish leaping from a white splash [Observed: trailer; _R4rvvGlRsM hq3].
