# Tutorial

The Tutorial is Battle Bears' single-player training level. It is a game mode, not a PvP arena. It is a short, linear "virtual" course in an endless black void. Columns of green code glyphs fill the void, and the course is built from neon-green walkways, floating circuit-etched blocks, amber instruction signs, practice dummies and red laser gates. It ends at a row of standard BBG jump pads in front of a green line-lit hologram of the Pelican't drop ship.

- **Origin:** it dates from Battle Bears Royale (BBR). A "TUTORIAL" mode card already appears on the menu in SkyVu's official pre-launch "BATTLE BEARS ROYALE Sneak Peek" (Aug 31, 2011). The arena itself is first seen in a fan video from Apr 18, 2012.
- **Status:** it carried over into Battle Bears Gold (BBG) in 2013. It was still playable in 2026 playtest-era builds, in fan uploads from Jul 9 and Sep 20, 2026.
- **2026 soundtrack:** SkyVu's 2026 soundtrack has a 0:32 track called "Tutorial".

## Setting

- **The wiki's framing:** a training arena completed "by approaching the virtual ship" [Fan-wiki]. The Vehicles page adds "There is also a Pelican't in the Tutorial" [Fan-wiki].
- **What it looks like:**
  - An endless black void with long vertical columns of small green glyphs on every side [Observed: every capture, 2012–2026].
  - The floor is near-black green with faint green line-work in uneven rectangles, like circuit traces [Observed].
  - The digital rain closely resembles the code rain in The Matrix [Inference]. BBG's own text quotes The Matrix several times:
    - The matchmaking lines above.
    - The Acreambo Pistols description: "Run around and shoot like your in the matrix!" [Game text via wiki].
    - So a deliberate homage is plausible [Inference]. No source states it [Unverified].
- **The glyphs** are Latin capital letters, not katakana [Observed].
  - In Tutorial_BBG_.jpg (2048×1536), one column near x≈1505, y≈300–720 reads "CONSECTETUR", followed by letters that look like "ADIPISC…". Another column near x≈150, y≈330–650 also reads "CONSECTETUR".
  - These are the opening words of the "Lorem ipsum" filler text ("consectetur adipiscing") [Observed, low-res; partial legibility].
  - That the whole rain is Lorem-ipsum text is [Inference].
- **No in-universe location is documented** [Unverified]. Framing it as, for example, a simulation aboard the Pelican't would be [BBG News invention].

## Layout

- **Overall shape:** a linear course, unlike the open PvP maps, enclosed by invisible "air barriers" [Fan-wiki].
  - Seen from outside the barriers (Jul 17, 2012 out-of-bounds capture), the course reads as a chain of zig-zag, neon-edged walkways and platforms floating in the void, with the hologram ship nearby [Observed, low-res: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/2FWgvahqHgE/hq3.jpg].
- **The start** is the same view in 2012, Jan 2013, Jun 2013, 2025 and Sep 2026 [Observed: Tutorial-gameplay-old-controls-royale-142.png; Photo_(2).png; Tutorial-button-mode.png; Tutorial-no-button-mode.png; 6mredTN7z-k/sd1.jpg; lgd9Gqw6ahA/maxresdefault.jpg]:
  - The player stands on the open dark-grid floor facing an amber "MOVE" panel between two bracket-shaped neon-green posts. A large floating 3D "MOVING" label hangs upper left, with a yellow leader line.
  - Beyond, the hologram ship hangs ahead inside concentric rings.
  - The floating labels "SHOOTING" and "MELEE/ WEAPON SWITCH" are to the left, "PICKUPS" and "USE SPECIAL ITEM" to the right, and "OPTIONS" high right beside a raised walkway that ramps up across the upper right.

  - In the 2014 start view, the near-left walkway's arrows point right and the right-hand walkway's arrows point left, both toward the centre [Observed: Tutorial_BBG_.jpg]. A path that doubles back is [Inference].

- **No damage:** "the character's damage intake is set to 0%". "At no point through the Tutorial can the player die" [Fan-wiki].
