# Sky View

Sky View is a Battle Bears Gold (BBG) map set on a construction site on the open top floor of an unfinished skyscraper, high above a city of office towers [Fan-wiki; Observed].
- **The deck:** a flat grid of red-oxide steel I-beams with black grating, open bays and pale plank walkways [Observed].
- **Cover:** plywood boards on grey posts and glossy blue construction tarps [Observed].
- **The crane:** a yellow tower crane rises at the deck's edge, and its hook block hangs high above [Observed].
- **Time of day:** dusk turning to night [Observed].
- **Edges:** nothing stops you falling off [Fan-wiki].
- **Origin:** it launched with BBG in 2013 as one of SkyVu's "3 New Maps" [Canon].
- **In 2026:** the 2026 Steam text does not name it [Canon]. But it is in the Steam trailer SkyVu put on the 2026 Steam page [Observed] (2013 footage [Inference, strong]), has its own track on the official 2026 soundtrack [Canon], and appears in SkyVu's short "BBG Maps Ranked part 1" (Oct 11, 2026) [Observed in thumbnails]. A fan uploader's playtest chapter lists show it was played in the Aug 21 and Oct 15, 2026 playtests [Unverified: fan chapter titles].

## Setting

- **Where it is:** "It features skyscrapers and other tall buildings. The map itself is a contruction site high on top of a building." [Fan-wiki] (the wiki's spelling).
  - SkyVu's own blurb: "high above this nondescript city… a construction site" [Canon per our notes; Unverified this session].
- **What the images show** [Observed: `img/Sky_View_4.png`, `frames/z_sv4_deck.png`]:
  - The arena is a single deck on top of a tall, open red steel skeleton.
  - The skeleton has no intermediate floors: columns and beams rise several storeys from the flat brown roof of a lower building, whose roof edge has a pale balustrade.
  - Office towers of similar and greater height stand all around.
- **Which city:** never named [Unverified]. "Nondescript" is the official joke [Canon per notes; Unverified this session].
- **Firsts:** "This is the first map to be on top of a building or in any urban area." [Fan-wiki]
- **Name:** "It is most likely a pun on 'SkyVu', the creators of the Battle Bears franchise." [Fan-wiki]. No SkyVu text I could read confirms the pun [Unverified].

## Layout

- **Shape and size:**
  - "This is the smallest map in Battle Bears Gold so far, and its single-level design makes direct close-range combat almost unavoidable." [Fan-wiki, first written 2013-10-17]
  - From outside, the deck is a roughly square platform on red columns [Observed: SV4].
  - The trailer overhead shows one main level, with a few tilted steel plates and plank decks raised slightly above it [Observed: `frames/tr/v_008.jpg`, 48.4 s].
- **Floor:**
  - Red I-beams with rows of bolt holes divide the deck into square bays [Observed].
  - Most bays are filled with black ribbed grating [Observed].
  - Some bays are open. Through them you see the red steel frame dropping away to a neighbouring building's pale facade and a brown roof far below [Observed: `frames/z_sv3_holes.png`, `frames/z_sv1_holes.png`; SV4].
  - The wiki speaks of "numerous gaps" and says "there are no walls around this map" [Fan-wiki].
- **Routes and surfaces:**
  - Pale wooden plank walkways and plank decks, about three to five boards wide, run across the beams [Observed: SV1-SV3, trailer].
  - Grey steel plates covered in rows of small round studs lie flat or as short low ramps [Observed: `frames/z_sv3_holes.png`, SV1, SV2, trailer]. (The draft called them "perforated"; they are studded and not see-through.)
  - Orange-brown panels with a square grid pattern sit in some bays [Observed: SV1, SV2 `frames/z_sv2_horizon_left.png`, trailer v_008]. Whether they are pallets, tiles or something else is unclear.
- **Cover:**
  - "However, to prevent an all-out shootout, cover in the form of construction tarps, metal poles or planks are placed all around the map, just enough for a wise player to walk around safely." [Fan-wiki]
  - In the images, free-standing plywood boards are fixed to grey square posts, some in V-shaped pairs [Observed: SV1, trailer v_008].
  - Glossy blue tarps are stretched between posts [Observed].
  - Together they make a staggered maze of waist-to-chest-high screens [Observed].
- **Centre:**
  - The wiki names "the large, light blue tarp in the middle of the map" [Fan-wiki].
  - That it divides the two team halves is [Inference] from the PtB text below.
  - In the trailer overhead, several tarps sit across the middle of the deck [Observed: v_008].
- **Team sides and spawns:**
  - The PtB text puts the bombs "on opposite sides of the large, light blue tarp in the middle of the map very close to the two teams' spawnpoints", "each placed on the opposing team's side of the tarp" [Fan-wiki]. So the spawns sit close together on either side of the central tarp [Inference].
  - "Interestingly, some spawn points directly face the edge of the map in such a way that the smallest misstep at the beginning of the match could result in a Suicide." [Fan-wiki]
  - Which side in any image is Red or Blue is not documented [Unverified].
- **Plant the Bomb:**
  - "Fortunately, the player will then only have to carry the bomb a short distance to the right corner of their own side." [Fan-wiki]
  - This clashes with the general rule on the PtB page: "carry the team's bomb to the opposing team's bomb tube" [Fan-wiki]. Which reading is right on Sky View is [Unverified].
- **Jump pads:** none. "[[Skate or Die]], [[Sky View]], and [[Toxic Terror]] are the only maps that contain no Jump Pads." [Fan-wiki, BBG Maps]
  - The Sky View page still says "one of only two maps". That was written on 2013-06-28, before Toxic Terror arrived in v2.3 on Sep 27, 2013 [Fan-wiki].
- **Set pieces beyond the deck:**
  - **The crane:** a yellow lattice tower crane rises at the deck's edge [Observed].
    - From on the deck, its lower mast shows behind the cover boards at deck level [Observed: SV2 `frames/z_sv2_crane_mast.png`; Android Preview `frames/z_0jr_crane.png`].
    - From outside, it stands apart from the red skeleton [Observed: SV4].
    - Its jib swings over the deck, and the hook block hangs high above the deck's edge [Observed: SV2, SV4, oar2].
  - **The tall pale tower:** clad in narrow vertical panels with horizontal bands, it stands behind the crane. Its faces meet at shallow angles, so it reads as faceted [Observed: SV2, icon, oar2, `frames/z_towers_cmp.png`]. Whether it is round is unclear.

## Look

**Lighting and time of day** [Observed: SV1-SV4, B-1000, Android Preview, 2014 developer update, trailer, oar2]:
- Dusk turning to night.
- The upper sky is deep navy, with small scattered white stars.
- In one direction a sunset glow rises from purple through magenta-rose to a rose-peach horizon. A hazy, pale-gold distant skyline stands against it, with a bright white glow behind it (SV1, SV4, B-1000).
- The light is low and warm. Plank tops and tower faces pick up amber and salmon highlights, and tower windows glow pale.
- Shading looks soft and baked. I saw no crisp sun shadows in the 2013 images.
- There is no weather or environmental particles in any frame. The white puffs in the trailer are gameplay smoke.

**Sky and backdrop:**
- "This map uses the same skybox as Gold Digger." [Fan-wiki]
  - An earlier edit (rev 57221, 2013-11-23) said "This map has the same red urban backdrop as Gold Digger's." [Fan-wiki]
- Gold Digger's sky in the trailer is navy and starry with a full moon [Observed: `weapons/visuals/trailer/t_0009.jpg`]. The Gold Digger wiki gallery shows no sky at all [Observed].
- No Sky View image I opened shows a moon. A moon over Sky View is [Inference] only.
- Gold Digger's trailer sky samples darker (#00162B-#001A2D) than Sky View's 2013 sky (#092A4D) [Observed]. That may be a different build or grade.
- A ring of tall, columnar, dark red-brown silhouettes, speckled with orange-red light, lines the horizon behind the towers [Observed: `frames/z_sv2_horizon_left.png`, `frames/z_9q_horizon.png`]. Whether they are stylised trees or distant spires is unclear.

**Materials** [Observed]:
- Riveted red-oxide I-beams with rows of bolt holes.
- Black ribbed grating.
- Pale blond plywood and planks with visible grain.
- Grey steel plate with raised round studs.
- Glossy blue plastic tarps with streaky cyan-white highlights and a wave-like sheen.
- Square grey posts.
- The crane:
  - a yellow cross-braced lattice mast and jib, topped by an A-frame apex;
  - black pendant cables;
  - a grey counterweight block on the counter-jib;

- Office towers with flat dark-brown roofs and lighter rims, and warm grey-white facades in a horizontal grid of panels and glass that reflects teal and sky tones.

## Hazards and interactive elements
