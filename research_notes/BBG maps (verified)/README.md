# BBG maps and environments research (verified)

Start with `00_OVERVIEW.md`. It covers sources, the confirmed 2026 lineup and modes, conventions every map shares, which shorts suit each map, rules for showing maps in the house style, corrections and the confidence summary.

- `<map>.md`: one verified dossier per BBG map, including the Tutorial. Each gives official text, era and 2026 status, setting, layout, look and palette, landmarks, hazards, easter eggs and a style-neutral prompt pack.
- Topic dossiers:
  - `2026-lineup.md`: includes the new Sky Pirates / Sky World map and identifies the map in every Steam trailer shot.
  - `environment-conventions.md`
  - `story-locations.md`: the Ursa Major, the Pink Planet, the H.I.P. complex and others.
- `<key>.addendum.md`: gap passes run after the completeness critic.
- `quickref_all.json`: one row per map or topic item. `corrections_all.json`: verified corrections to our notes.

**How it was checked:**
1. Draft.
2. One combined source and image check per dossier: every quote re-fetched, every cited image re-opened.
3. Completeness critic and gap passes.
4. Overview.

**Not reachable:** web.archive.org (resets and 429s) and YouTube playback. YouTube evidence comes from thumbnails and storyboard frames only.
