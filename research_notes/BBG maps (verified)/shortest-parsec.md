# Shortest Parsec

Shortest Parsec is a small space station floating in a starfield, with almost no walls [Fan-wiki; Observed]. Its main parts:

- **Laser tower.** A tall tower with glossy red panels stands at the spawn end. On a timer it fires an orange-gold cone of light onto a round pad between the red and blue spawns [Fan-wiki; Observed].
- **Central module.** A white-and-red block whose floor a melee-hit switch on the far tower can open [Fan-wiki].
- **Asteroids.** Teal asteroids with hexagonal holes press against the station and float near it [Observed].

**History.**
- **Added:** iOS update 1.4.2 (Feb 28, 2013). That update renamed Battle Bears Royale (BBR) to plain "Battle Bears" [Game text via wiki; Fan-wiki].
- **BBG:** carried into Battle Bears Gold (BBG) and still there in 2021 [Fan-wiki; Observed].

That footage is most likely re-edited 2013 footage, so it shows the 2013 look, not the 2026 build [Inference, strong]. The 2026 soundtrack lists "Shortest Parsec" as track 9 [Canon].

Storyboard stills of a fan upload of the Oct 15 final playtest show it in the 2026 build [Observed], and its tile appears in the PICK LEVEL carousel of SkyVu's Oct 15 stream [Observed match] (see Era and status, "2026").

## Setting

- **A space station.** SkyVu's teaser calls it a "Space Station map" [Game text via wiki]. The wiki says "The stage is set on a space station with lots of details" [Fan-wiki: 1.4.2 article].
- **Its purpose, per the official blurb:**
  - A "private arms dealer" uses it "to transfer power to its facilities based on the surface of a mysterious planet on the fringes of the known galaxy". It sits where "a robotic someone I've seen before exploded a few miles away and peppered it with its body parts" [Canon per notes; Unverified this session].
  - That makes the orange beam plausibly the power-transfer beam [Inference].
- **What the backdrop shows.** No planet is visible in any image I opened [Observed]. The backdrop is a starfield, an indigo-violet galactic band and a magenta nebula [Observed]. An out-of-bounds fan view below the station shows teal-green and blue-violet aurora-like streaks [Observed: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/ySZY61sRFiQ/maxres3.jpg, a 2014 glitch video].

  - The H.I.P. page says the logo "is that of a Huggable with its arms stretched out, as a reference to Leonardo da Vinci's famous drawing, the Vitruvian Man". It adds that "the Shortest Parsec map has their symbol on a large tower at one end of the stage" [Fan-wiki].
  - The Assault (BBG) page says the B-1000 and Rho chest symbol "also appears on the Shortest Parsec stage" [Fan-wiki].
  - Three forms of the emblem appear on the map: a red floor medallion, an outline engraving on the far tower and a back-wall glyph seen from the hall [Observed]. The full emblem is a bear figure whose arms form looping orbits, inside a circle, above a smaller circle holding a crosshair-like monogram with dots and a "P" form. It matches File:Hip_sim.jpg [Observed].
  - That the monogram spells H.I.P. is [Inference]. Official copy never names H.I.P. for this map, going by the quoted fragments [Inference].
- **Jokes in the name:**
  - The title riffs on the Star Wars "Kessel Run in less than twelve parsecs" line. A parsec is a distance, not a time [Inference]. The wiki only explains the unit [Fan-wiki].
  - The tagline inverts the *Alien* poster line "In space no one can hear you scream" [Inference].

## Layout

**Overall shape** [Observed: overhead File:Shortest_Parsec.jpg; trailer 24.5 s and 25.75 s; New_map_1 and New_map_3]:
- **The spine.** The station is compact and runs along one spine.
- **Laser tower:** a block at one end. From above it shows an orange emblem and two white lights on one edge. Two dark legs join it to the central module.
- **Team arms:** two L-shaped decks flank the laser tower and wrap down to the central module.
  - With the laser end at the top, the left arm has red dashed edge lights and red crosshair-circle floor decals; the right arm has the same in blue.
  - Each arm has a boxy equipment pod with pipes at its outer top corner, and a jump pad on the deck.
  - Thick black pipes with dark-red couplings join each arm to the central module.
- **Central module** (the wiki's "middle dome" or "central dome"):
  - A big white block. Inside is a long hall with red ceiling beams, a dark slate floor and white-tiled floor at both ends.
  - A jump pad sits on the white tiles at the **laser-tower end** [Observed: overhead; trailer 25.75 s; New_map_2]. I saw none at the far end.
- **Stem walkway:** a narrow walkway carrying a large red H.I.P. floor medallion runs from the central module to the far tower.
- **Far tower** (the switch tower or "meteor tower"):
  - Grey decks with jump pads at the corners.
  - A cracked red floor with a curved inlay, a lime-green console, a black cable and red-and-white striped poles.
  - A large teal asteroid is lodged against it.
- **Other asteroids:** a second teal asteroid floats off the red arm. A small one floats below the laser end [Observed: trailer 24.5 s].

## Look

**Overall:**
- **Construction.** The station is built from clean, toy-like hard-surface blocks [Observed: New_map_1, New_map_3, EE1, screen_02].
- **Hulls.** Off-white to lavender, with engraved circuit-like panel lines.
- **Laser tower.** Covered in glossy red inlay panels with black seams.
- **Pipes.** Ribbed black, with dark-red couplings joining the blocks.
- **Spikes.** Antenna spikes with red tips sit on the tower tops and pods. Small white spikes and nubs hang under the platforms.
- **Hall interior** [Observed: screen_04, New_map_2]:
  - Saturated red ceiling beams and red door frames.
  - Pale panelled walls: mauve-beige in Steam screen_04 (2013 footage [Inference, strong]), lilac-white in the Jan 2013 preview. The Oct 15, 2026 storyboard stills also show warm pale walls under red beams [Observed, 320 × 180].
  - Red angled benches or consoles, and a dark slate floor.

**Sky.** There is no sun, planet or ground [Observed].
- **Main backdrop:** near-black space with dense white star flecks and a broad indigo-violet galactic band [Observed: screen_02; trailer 24.0–25.75 s and 50.0–51.25 s; EE1–EE3].

- **Hulls:** cool lavender-white light, with soft bloom on light bars.

**Particles and weather.** The beam is full of drifting sparkles [Observed: Scary_Light]. There is no weather [Observed].

## Landmarks

1. **The laser tower and its beam** [Observed: Scary_Light, EE1, EE2; trailer 28.0 s, beam lit at 49.75–50.25 s; fan thumbnails https://i.ytimg.com/vi/_jnFIHnbd2w/hq3.jpg (2013), n4fRAhVtjKc/maxres1.jpg (2014), 37ZrrTlQWdU/maxres3.jpg (2021)]:
   - **Tower:** tall and lavender-white, faced with bold red geometric panels and two mint-white light bars, topped with red-tipped antennas.
   - **Beam:** under its open arch, a pale-blue sphere with a hexagon grid fires a translucent orange-gold cone onto a circular pad with a red-orange rim and a glowing white core.
   - **Beam off:** with the beam off, the pad shows a red rim and a pale blue-white core [Observed: trailer from 50.5 s; screen_02]. Whether the beam switched off there or the explosion's bloom washed it out is [Unverified].
   - **Pickup inside:** the pickup inside the beam is the Bearscream Sandwich [Observed shape match; Fan-wiki].
   - **Wiki:** it is "the orange beam parting the two teams spawn points" [Fan-wiki]. The wiki also calls it "the red beam" [Fan-wiki], but it is orange-gold in every image [Observed].
2. **The central module, or "middle dome"** [Observed: overhead; trailer 25.5–26.25 s; screen_04; New_map_2]:
   - A chunky white block with circular hatch details.
   - Inside, a red-beamed hall under an open roof slot, with a dark slate floor, a red-lit door on one side and a purple-lit door on the other.
   - Its floor is the part the switch opens [Fan-wiki].
3. **The H.I.P. medallion walkway** [Observed: screen_02; trailer 25.5–25.75 s; overhead; EE3; https://i.ytimg.com/vi/rXOhC9jH7KI/maxres2.jpg]:
   - A narrow spine walkway whose floor carries a large red circular emblem in black line-art: the looping-armed bear above a small circle holding the crosshair-and-"P" monogram.
   - The wiki says there are "a few logos in the map reading 'H.I.P.'" [Fan-wiki]. The monogram is the likely "reading" [Inference]. There is no plain lettering [Observed].
4. **The switch tower, or "meteor tower"** [Observed: New_map_3; EE3; screen_02; overhead; https://i.ytimg.com/vi/Fh8uf0FyWCs/maxres3.jpg]:
   - A cracked red floor with a curved inlay, the lime-green console with its down-arrow plate, a black cable and striped poles.
   - A tall white wall carries a large **outline** engraving of the H.I.P. emblem.
   - A small pink Huggable hangs skewered on a black rod against that wall, with a pink smear running down below it. It does not move between trailer 50.25 s and screen_02 (50.67 s) [Observed].
   - It is very likely the wiki's "Huggable corpse" [Inference]. The EE3 caption, "On top of the Meteorite near the Huggable corpse", fits this spot, since EE3 is taken from the far-tower meteorite [Fan-wiki; Observed]. The wiki's trivia instead places the corpse "on the side of the large tower adjacent to each team's spawn points" [Fan-wiki], so its location stays an open question.
5. **The teal asteroids** [Observed: New_map_1; overhead; trailer 24.5 s; EE2; EE3]:
   - Big, low-poly, faceted, dark-teal rocks pierced by hexagonal holes.
   - One is lodged against the switch tower, one floats by the red arm, and a small one floats below the laser end.
   - The wiki calls them "meteorites" and puts Easter eggs on top of them [Fan-wiki].
6. **The team arms** [Observed: overhead; trailer 24.5–25.75 s]. L-shaped decks edged with red or blue dashed running lights, with team-coloured crosshair-circle floor decals.

## Hazards and interactive elements

**The void:**
- **Wiki:** "The map has almost no walls, and that way it is very easy to suicide. It is considered a very dangerous map as suicides are very abundant" [Fan-wiki].
- **Railings:** most outer deck edges I saw have no railing [Observed: overhead, screen_02]. Low red rails line some walkways and the lower level [Observed: New_Map_4; EE2; EE4; https://i.ytimg.com/vi/W_wQleI7B7g/hq1.jpg].
- **Scoring:** hazard deaths "will cause a suicide and deduct a kill" [Fan-wiki: Health]. In the 2021 screenshots the red team's score reads -1 in EE1 and -2 in EE2–EE4 [Observed]. That these are suicides while hunting Easter eggs is [Inference].

**The laser:**
- **Timing:** it "activates periodically and cannot be controlled" [Fan-wiki].
- **Damage, per the wiki:**
  - The Scary Light caption says it "kills any player who stays under its ray, resulting in suicide" [Fan-wiki].
  - The Health page says it "does damage quickly over time" rather than killing instantly [Fan-wiki].
  - The misplaced 2014 paragraph says "a deadly laser is activated periodically and any player caught in it will quickly lose HP and suicide" [Fan-wiki: Space Oddity revision 66673].
- **Shield and caffeine boost:**
  - The map page says "You actually can run through the laser if you use caffeine boost or shield" [Fan-wiki].
  - The Pick-Ups page says players with Shield "can still suicide from natural hazards". The BBR page says the Shield "Will not prevent you from suiciding because of natural hazards though" [Fan-wiki].
  - These claims conflict.
- **Projectiles:**
  - The map page says some projectiles contact the beam, "such as the TNT Launcher, whilst others do not, such as the Proximity Mine" [Fan-wiki].
  - The Bouncing Betty page says Betty is "the only explosive weapon or projectile that could penetrate the Shortest Parsec's laser beam" [Fan-wiki].
  - The two pages conflict.
- **Not documented:** the beam's period, warning cue and sound [Unverified].

**The floor switch:**
- **What it does.** The map is "the second map with an interactive feature in the form of the switch found on the tower across from the spawn points". The switch "controls the position the floor of the middle dome and is activated by your melee weapon. It can be used to lure players to their deaths, even your own teammates" [Fan-wiki].
- **How the floor moves:**
  - The 1.4.2 article calls it "a removeable floor" [Fan-wiki].
  - The misplaced 2014 paragraph calls the central room a controlled "garbage chute" and says "The floor of the garbage chute can be opened and closed by a lever found of the far tower. Players hit said lever with their melee to activate the change" [Fan-wiki: Space Oddity revision 66673].
  - So the floor opens like a trapdoor onto open space [Inference].
- **A fan's account.** One player wrote that while he was "about to grab the shield" in the middle of the map, a teammate "decided to hit the switch which made my character SHOOT across the map" [Unverified: fan video description, 6YRBMycML0A, Mar 3, 2013].
- **The console:** the lime-green console with the down-arrow plate is very likely the switch [Inference].
- **Not documented:** the floor's animation, speed and how long it stays open [Unverified].

**Jump pads:**
- There are several on each level [Observed].
- The wiki describes a bug where a player "suddenly stalls midway between the normal landing point and the Jump Pad at the edge of each team's base", which is "especially dangerous on Parsec" [Fan-wiki].

**Plant the Bomb.** The bombs sit "on the bottommost platform right next to each other, underneath the tower across from the spawn points" [Fan-wiki].

## Easter eggs and references

- **"YOU SHOULDN'T BE ABLE TO READ THIS!"**
  - **Where:** the wiki says it is readable from "the meteorite that can be seen floating in the space close to the blue team's base, if a player reaches it with the So Fly or the Turkish Jump Boots", and that it is "behind the tower where the laser appears, it is written twice and there are two confused bear faces" [Fan-wiki].
  - **What it looks like:** the panel fills an outer face of the laser tower above the open arch where the emitter hangs, between the two flanking pods, seen from below [Observed: EE1].
  - **Layout:** black capitals printed twice, the second copy rotated 180° so it reads from the other side. Five stars run between them, with two dark bear-head faces with squinting, annoyed eyes [Observed: EE1 crop].
  - **Caption typo:** the wiki's gallery caption misspells the text as "Your shouldn't…" [Fan-wiki].
- **The wiki's Easter-egg list** [Fan-wiki]:
  1. The meteorite at the red base: "can be reached only by using So Fly[Huggable] and Metal Crush[B-1000]". Some players claim "Double Jump[Jump Pro Mode feature + Turkish Jump Boots]".
  2. The floating meteorite near the blue base, from which the sign above is readable.
  3. From the blue base, So Fly plus the Jump Boots reach "the top of the tower where the huggable corpse is". Repeating it reaches "the top of the place where the laser comes from".
  4. The top of the meteorite by the switch.
  - EE2 and EE3 are taken standing on teal meteorites [Observed].
- **"Only the Huggable can reach it."**
  - The Huggable (BBG) page says "the Huggable is the only class that can reach the map's Easter Egg" [Fan-wiki].
  - It echoes the map page's own 2013–2014 wording, "There is an Easter Egg on this map that can only be reached by Huggables" (spelled "reach" in revisions 32031–32590, Apr 20–21, 2013). That wording was replaced on 2014-05-27 (rev 64712) by the So Fly plus Metal Crush text [Fan-wiki: page history].
  - So it is a contested, outdated claim [Inference].
- **A "face" on the pad block.** The white support block under the beam pad has two dark, drippy scorch smudges placed like closed, weeping eyes [Observed: EE2, captioned "Meteorite Easter Egg"]. Whether this is a deliberate face is [Unverified].
- **"Killerbear500":**
  - The name is scrawled in dark, smeared paint across a backlit purple panel wall, beside a glowing green shield pickup and a low red rail [Observed: EE4].
  - The wiki says "KillerBear500 was the name of a fan who went to the SkyVu offices" [Fan-wiki].
  - **Probable spot:** the wiki lists a Shield "Underneath tower across from bases, on the Blue Team's side", and the lower level is purple-lit. So the graffiti is probably on the lower level under the far tower, blue side [Inference; Unverified].
- **Huggable corpse.** A skewered pink Huggable is visible on the far tower's wall [Observed]. See Landmarks 4.
- **H.I.P. emblems.** The emblem appears as the red floor medallion, as the outline engraving on the far tower and as the crosshair glyph on a wall seen from the hall [Observed]. It ties the map to H.I.P., B-1000 and Rho [Fan-wiki].
- **Orange emblem on the laser tower:**
  - A red-orange stepped-octagon frame holds a circle with a bear figure, arms out, with loop lines. It sits on the tower's top and upper face [Observed: overhead; trailer 24.5 s; New_map_1].
  - It resembles the bear half of the H.I.P. logo without the monogram circle [Inference]. It may be the "symbol on a large tower at one end of the stage" that the H.I.P. page means [Fan-wiki; Inference].
- **Name and tagline.** They riff on Star Wars and *Alien* [Inference].
