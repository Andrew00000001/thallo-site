# No Bear's Land

No Bear's Land is BBG's big, sunny, open grass map. It is a square plateau with cut corners [Observed]. A Red base keep and a Blue base keep stand at opposite ends, each an olive concrete octagon joined to a gun battlement [Observed; Fan-wiki]. A zig-zag of plank-floored trenches runs between them to a small centre pillar that holds the Bearscream Sandwich [Fan-wiki; Observed]. Two crashed Pelican't dropships lie on the other two sides, each with a long ploughed dirt furrow behind it [Fan-wiki; Observed].

The map launched in Battle Bears Royale 1.0 (Oct 8, 2011) as "Battlefield Airpad" [Fan-wiki]. It was renamed when the game relaunched as Battle Bears Gold (wiki: "version 2.0"; BBG released May 24, 2013) [Fan-wiki]. It got winter, summer and spring reskins in 2017–2018 [Fan-wiki]. SkyVu names it first among the "classics" returning on Nov 4, 2026 [Canon], and it is track 2 on the 2026 soundtrack [Canon]. It appears in three official SkyVu YouTube uploads from 2026 and in fan playtest footage [Observed].

## Setting
- **Theme.** The wiki: "The term 'No Bear's Land' is a pun off of the World War One term 'No Man's Land'… Since the map itself is mostly a vast open space with trenches in between, it very much resembles No Man's Land" [Fan-wiki]. The original name "Battlefield Airpad" and the two crashed dropships suggest a forward landing field gone wrong [Inference].
- **Mixed-era dressing** [Observed; dating each prop is Inference]:
  - WWI-style trenches shored with wooden posts, and sandbags.
  - Black X-shaped steel tank traps of the Czech-hedgehog kind, a WWII-style obstacle.
  - Sci-fi Pelican't dropships.
  - A fast-food stand.
- **In-world location:** undocumented [Unverified]. If our notes' quote of the official blurb is right, it jokes that the place was "probably really nice before you came along… quaint old folk's home" [Canon per notes; Unverified].
- **Asset name.** The three 2012 renders are captioned "Fields Level / model & textures" under a "BATTLE BEARS ROYALE" badge, with a "www.kevinsalki.com" watermark [Observed]. So "Fields Level" was probably the working name, and the renders are an artist's portfolio images [Inference].
- **Infobox:** "Type: Open-air"; "Time of day: Day, Sunlight"; "Jump Pads: Yes"; "Suicide: Yes" [Fan-wiki].

## Layout
- **Overall shape** [Observed: File:Battlefield.png top-down; fan overhead thumbnails Pxl7w2mprMQ and fMBTcV9EVtU]:
  - A square grass plateau with cut (chamfered) corners.
  - Rimmed by a pale olive ledge with a bright yellow stripe (about #FBF617).
  - A dark olive, angular, fin-shaped pylon sits on each cut corner. It carries three red lights on the north corners and blue on the south.
  - Small red or blue light domes stud the rim, red on the north half and blue on the south.
  - The wiki adds "small hills and some jagged terrain" [Fan-wiki].
- **Symmetry.** The wiki says "symmetrical" [Fan-wiki]. The top-down shows it is **rotationally** symmetric: the Blue half is the Red half turned 180°, not mirrored [Observed].
  - The Red keep's battlement wing is on its west side; the Blue keep's is on its east.
  - The food stands sit north-east and south-west.
  - The red-banded wreck is west and the blue-banded wreck is east.
- **Team ends** [Observed: top-down]:
  - **Red** holds the north end in the wiki's top-down: red spawn markers, red pylon lights and the red-striped keep.
  - **Blue** holds the south end.
  - The 2026 frame lFr8-yHbbzQ 38:34 ("YOU'RE ON TEAM BLUE") looks north past the red-banded wreck on the left toward the Red keep. That fits this orientation [Observed].
- **Bases.** The wiki: "two towers connected to a wall… Each team's spawnpoint is located near or behind this wall to create some protection from enemies. To get on top of the towers, the players must use a Jump Pad located behind the wall" [Fan-wiki]. What the images show [Observed: top-down, renders, 2012/2013/2026 frames]:
  - From above, each keep is an octagon with a cobblestone roof, a pale round central pad and a chunky olive parapet.
  - A rectangular battlement wing is attached to one side, with a mounted gun turret on its cobbled deck. In-game the turret is a squat, boxy, dark-grey housing with a slotted visor.
  - The jump pad sits in a trapezoid alcove on the back of the keep, facing the map edge.
  - A small olive block marked with a yellow triangle stands beside each keep. Its purpose is undocumented [Unverified].
  - In profile, the keep face has a tall dark recessed doorway holding two upright slab panels, chipped pilasters, star-shaped bullet cracks, and a double band of team colour along the top.
- **Centre.** The wiki: "a grassy flatland with symmetric trenches scattered around the field. In the very center of the map is a small pillar, and the Bearscream Sandwich pickup is located on top of this. To reach this, players must use the Jump Pads to jump up the pillar or double-jump" [Fan-wiki]. The Bearscream Sandwich page calls it "the small podium in the center of the map" [Fan-wiki].
  - The top-down shows a grey square block at the central plank junction, with a cyan jump pad on its left and right [Observed].
  - The wiki's Plant the Bomb section calls it "the pillar with the Bearzerker pickup", which clashes with the rest of the page [Fan-wiki]. It is probably an old or wrong name [Inference].
- **Trenches** [Observed: top-down, renders, 2011/2012 BBR frames, 2026 frames]:
  - Sunken channels with golden plank floors run in an S/zig-zag from each base to the centre.
  - The cut walls show cartoon soil strata: a grass cap with a dripping edge, an orange band, brown, then near-black. Wooden shoring posts with splintered tops line the walls.
  - Some sections are bridged or roofed by **grey slatted decks and ramps with yellow edge stripes**. The yellow stripes belong to these decks, not to the trench lips. One concrete box-bunker sits in the trench (Airpad1 render).
  - Small green bushes and grass-topped wooden crates with a dark barrel on top sit in and beside the trenches.
- **Side wrecks.** The wiki: "two small Pelican'ts are located - one on each side of the field - which the players can climb on top of to gain some protection or take better aim at opponents. On these Pelican'ts special Jump Pad is located which will launch you high over the field, making the player land right next to the opposite spacecraft" [Fan-wiki]. From above [Observed]:
  - The **west wreck has red bands** and its ploughed furrow trails north, toward Red.
  - The **east wreck has blue bands** and its furrow trails south, toward Blue.
  - So each wreck slid toward the opposite team's end [Inference].
  - Each keeps three of its four engine pods. The fourth lies broken off in the grass near the wreck's leading end, where the jump pad sits.
  - The furrows are long orange-edged dirt troughs with slate-blue barrels and a grass-topped crate in them. In 2026 frames the furrow runs downhill from a wreck perched on a hilltop [Observed: lFr8-yHbbzQ 8:23 and 17:42].
- **Verticality** [Fan-wiki + Observed; the summary is Inference]:
  - Trench floors below grade.
  - Rolling mounds on the field: the keeps sit on rises and the wrecks on hills.
  - Keep roofs and Pelican't tops, reached by jump pad or (for the wrecks) "the port wing" [Fan-wiki].
  - The Pelican't pads give the highest arcs on the map.
  - The Metal Crush trivia: an Assault with Turkish SW Boots can "jump from one battlement on one side of No Bear's Land to another" [Fan-wiki].
- **Play pattern:** "This stage works very well with long range classes like the Sniper and Demo. It is recommended that short range classes stay in the corners of the map or in the trenches if there is a Demo or Sniper on the opposing team" [Fan-wiki]. The Skate or Die page compares its "very wide and open area, much like No Bear's Land" [Fan-wiki].
- **Pickup markers on the top-down:** nine pure-white 6-pixel squares sit exactly where the wiki puts the pickups [Observed: pixel clusters at both keep roofs, beside both food stands, on both wrecks, and two at the centre pillar]. They are probably pickup markers added to this map image [Inference].

## Look
**Lighting and sky** [Observed]
- Bright high sun with crisp short shadows. The infobox says "Day, Sunlight" [Fan-wiki].
- In-game skies are saturated mid-blue, paler toward the horizon, with large soft white cumulus clouds.
  - This holds in the 2012 BBR screenshot, the Oct 2, 2013 fan tour (BRVP0IZEw-k), and the 2026 official and fan frames (C7uFxvSZ10c, 6ghQ4IUBqEY maxres1, lFr8-yHbbzQ chapters).
- The 2012 "Fields Level" renders are hazier: sky-blue fading to near-white at the horizon.
  - The sun bloom is at the upper right in Airpad1 and the upper centre in Airpad2. No sun is in frame in Airpad3.
- The horizon shows misty blue-grey hills, with tree-covered hills clearer in the 2026 frames.
- Overhead glitch views from about 2013–14 show pale void beyond the rim.
- **Ambient sky detail:** a small dark winged craft with two green-glowing pods and a hanging spike hovers above the field.
  - Seen in BRVP0IZEw-k maxres2 (2013), EQwloJnrOko maxres2, lFr8-yHbbzQ 38:34 and 6ghQ4IUBqEY maxres1 (all 2026) [Observed].
  - What it is (a flying Pelican't, a drone or set dressing) is undocumented [Unverified].

**Materials and props** [Observed]
- **Ground:** yellow-green turf flecked with small white daisies with yellow centres.
- **Keeps:** olive-drab weathered concrete with team-colour double bands.
- **Trenches:** golden plank floors; layered soil walls; wooden posts; grey slatted decks with yellow edge stripes.
- **Sandbags:** tan, laid in curved arcs and C-shapes on the mounds.
- **Tank traps:** black steel X-shaped hedgehogs in clusters of two or three.
- **Wrecks:** dark grey Pelican'ts with an olive-green cast (charcoal blue-grey in the Airpad3 render). Each has four fat cylindrical engine pods on stub wings and team-colour bands.
  - The Airpad3 render shows a cockpit window and twin round headlamps on a wreck's nose.
  - The wiki's general description is "a dark green ship, with four engines on the sides of the vehicle" [Fan-wiki: Vehicles].
- **Food stands:**
  - A red-and-yellow pyramid/parasol canopy on a single dark pole over a squat grey cart.
  - In the 2012 render the cart is a pale grey box with a dark open hatch and a fat ribbed dark drum on a wooden leg.
  - The canopy carries red-ringed roundels showing a black-and-white rubber-hose-style cartoon mascot holding a roast drumstick [Observed: clearest in EQwloJnrOko maxres2 (2026) and BRVP0IZEw-k (2013); also the Airpad3 render].
  - The wiki calls it "the food stand" that the Imperial Basket Grande sits behind [Fan-wiki].
- **Jump pads:** octagonal, with a yellow-black hazard rim, a glowing cyan snowflake-like centre and cyan holographic rings [Observed: File:Jump_Pad.jpg, a generic pad not tied to this map].
- **Weather and particles:** none documented for the base map [Unverified].

## Landmarks
1. **The two base keeps:** octagonal olive concrete keeps with team double bands (red north, blue south), each joined to a battlement wing with a boxy grey gun turret. Each has a recessed slab doorway with bullet-cracked pilasters, the "XEL-PMOC 20-11" stencil strip, a cobbled roof with a round pad, and a jump-pad alcove at the back [Observed; Fan-wiki].
2. **The trench zig-zag:** golden plank floors, striped cartoon soil walls with wooden shoring posts, grey slatted decks with yellow edge stripes, and sandbag arcs on the lips [Observed; Fan-wiki].
3. **Two crashed Pelican'ts:** dark grey four-engine dropships, one red-banded (west) and one blue-banded (east), on hills [Observed; Fan-wiki].
   - Each trails a long ploughed orange-edged dirt furrow with barrels in it, and has a snapped-off engine pod lying nearby.
   - Each carries a health pack and a super jump pad on top.
4. **The centre pillar:** a small grey block at the plank junction, flanked by two jump pads, holding the Bearscream Sandwich (an angry brown bear-face ice-cream sandwich) [Observed: top-down, File:Bearscream_Sandwich.jpg; Fan-wiki].
5. **The food stands:** a red-and-yellow parasol canopy with a drumstick-mascot roundel over a grey cart beside each keep, with the glowing Imperial Basket Grande (a red basket of golden fried food) on the grass beside it [Observed: BRVP0IZEw-k maxres2 (2013), EQwloJnrOko maxres2 (2026); Fan-wiki].
6. **Battlefield clutter and edge:** black X-shaped tank traps, daisies, and the cut-corner rim with its yellow stripe and fin-shaped corner pylons with team lights [Observed].

## Hazards and interactive elements
- **Jump pads**, three kinds [Fan-wiki; positions Observed on the top-down]:
  1. At the back of each keep, up to the roof.
  2. Two beside the centre pillar ("situated on each site of pillar" [sic]).
  3. On each Pelican't, "allowing the players to fly across the field at a extremely high altitude" [sic] to land "right next to the opposite spacecraft".
- **Pickups** [Fan-wiki]:
  - **Bearscream Sandwich ×1** on the centre pillar. It "doubles the damage of all the player's weapons for 20 seconds" and "respawns every 90 seconds".
  - **Imperial Basket Grande ×2**, "situated on the left side of each base tower behind a shielding parasol" / "Behind the food stand at the left corner of each spawn point". It heals 50% of max health, can stack to 200%, and takes 60 s to respawn.
    - On the top-down the stands are north-east and south-west, which is each team's left as it faces the centre [Observed; Inference].
    - A glowing basket next to the cart is seen in 2013 and 2026 frames [Observed].
  - **Health Pack ×2**, "situated on top of each of the crashed spacecrafts. To reach these, use the port wing on both planes to climb the spacecraft". The wiki also calls them "Medic Packs". They take 45 s to respawn.
  - **Shield ×2**, one "on the top of each base tower". It gives 10 s of immunity, but players "can still die by falling off a stage".
- **Plant the Bomb:** "two bomb tubes and two bomb stands. The bombs will both be placed in the middle of the trenches near the pillar… while the bomb tubes will be positioned on top of the each of the base towers" [Fan-wiki].
- **"Suicide: Yes"** (infobox) [Fan-wiki]. The wiki uses "suicide" for environmental deaths: lava (Aztec Attack), "fall off the bridge" (Gold Digger), canals (Marecraft), "falling off of the map" (Plant the Bomb page) [Fan-wiki]. On this map it most likely means falling off the plateau edge [Inference].
- **Mounted guns and corner pylons:** no record says either is usable. Treat them as set dressing [Inference].
- **Open sightlines:** long lines of fire favour Snipers and Demos; the trenches and corners are the cover [Fan-wiki].

## Easter eggs and references
- **The name** puns on WWI's "No Man's Land" [Fan-wiki].
- **The Pelican't** "seems to be a pun from the ship Pelican in the Halo series and its design is based of the UD-4 'Cheyenne' from the Aliens 1986 movie" [Fan-wiki: Vehicles]. "In No Bear's Land, there are two downed Pelican'ts at the sides of the stage" [Fan-wiki].
- **"War. What is it good for?"** If our notes' quote of the official blurb is accurate, this echoes Edwin Starr's song "War" [Canon per notes, Unverified; the song link is Inference].
- **Keep stencil "XEL-PMOC 20-11"** reads "11-02 COMP-LEX" backwards [Observed; reading is Inference]. Its meaning is undocumented [Unverified].
- **A big white cartoon bone** is embedded in the dark lower soil of the trench walls in the 2012 renders [Observed: Airpad2, clearly; Airpad1, partly]. Whether it is in the shipped game is unconfirmed [Unverified].
- **The food-stand mascot** holds a roast drumstick right next to the Imperial Basket Grande, a basket of fried food that Battle Bears -1 advertised as "3 pounds of deep fried pain" [Observed; Fan-wiki quoting the BB-1 gag ad]. The pairing looks deliberate [Inference].
