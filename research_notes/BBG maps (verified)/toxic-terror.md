# Toxic Terror

Toxic Terror is Battle Bears Gold's radioactive sewer map. It was added in update 2.3 (Sep 27, 2013, iOS date) [Fan-wiki]. It is a dark, two-storey sewer with glowing green ooze under floor grates and a large climbable tree in the middle [Fan-wiki][Observed]. **New in this verified edition:** SkyVu's own thumbnail for the October 2026 final-playtest livestream shows the map, with the 2026 build label "3.0.7_2026.11.19_b7" in the corner [Observed: I17]. So the map is in the 2026 build, even though no SkyVu text names it [Inference].

## Setting

- **Place:** a "cavernous sewer system with radioactive waste flowing through" [Fan-wiki].
  - In the official fly-through, an octagonal green-lit shaft rises to a square, brown-framed grate with dark navy night sky (#011027) and a single star beyond it [Observed: I4 hq2/hq3]. Two green-lit bars hang down from the grate, perhaps ladder rails [Observed; identity Inference].
  - That it lies under a city street is [Inference]: no street is visible.
- **Theme: radioactive waste.**
  - Glowing green ooze pours from pipes, pools under floor grates and runs in channels [Observed: I2, I4, I8].
  - In the concept, a radiation-trefoil drum lies toppled in a green puddle [Observed concept: I5 panel 5].
  - In game, a toppled drum lies by a green puddle [Observed: I12/I13 tile].

  - chalk tally marks and a weapon rack in Ninja Turtle colours [Observed: I6; I5]

## Landmarks

1. **The central climbable tree.**
   - A thick, twisted brown trunk with branches spreading overhead [Fan-wiki][Observed: I7].
   - The concept shows it bursting from a lower pit through cracked concrete, inside moss-draped wooden railings, under a warm spotlight [Observed concept: I5 panel 3].
   - Pickups, Shields and the bomb tubes cluster around it [Fan-wiki].
2. **The heart carving on the trunk.** A heart outline is carved into the bark. Inside are a ring-shaped mark and a cross-shaped mark; the third mark is faint [Observed: I1, with a BBG-style green bracket reticle in frame].
3. **The drain-pipe pool.** A bent pipe pours ooze into a round grated pool beside the poster pillar, a DANGER sign and an amber lamp. This is the official preview's default frame [Observed: I4].
4. **The big-grate corner.** A large glowing floor grate with the poster pillar, a red capsule lamp, amber lamps and a STOP sign. It appears in 2013-14 fan footage and in the 2026 livestream thumbnail [Observed: I14, I17]. Whether it is the same spot as landmark 3 is [Unverified].
5. **The Blue-base tally wall and weapon rack.** Chalk tallies beside two blue-hilted swords and a purple staff [Observed: I6]. The concept rack also holds red-gripped sai and orange nunchucks: all four Turtle weapons [Observed concept: I5 panel 5].
6. **The shaft to the night sky.** An octagonal green-lit shaft up to a square, brown-framed grate and stars [Observed: I4 hq2/hq3].

## Hazards and interactive elements

- **Climbable tree** [Fan-wiki]. Standing on top of the trunk is a fan claim [Unverified].
- **No jump pads** [Fan-wiki].
- **Pickups:**
  - Bearscream Sandwich: "doubles the damage of all the player's weapons for 20 seconds" and "respawns every 90 seconds" [Fan-wiki].
  - Imperial Basket Grande.
  - 2 Health Packs.
  - 2 Shields, which make you "immune to damage for 10 seconds" [Fan-wiki].
- **Spawn quirk:** you can spawn on top of a Health Pack and waste it [Fan-wiki].
- **Plant the Bomb:** bombs inside the spawns on the upper storeys; tubes beside the tree [Fan-wiki].
- **Ooze:** whether it damages players, and whether the map has suicide pits, is undocumented [Unverified]. The ooze seen in images is under grates, in channels or in shallow puddles [Observed], so it may be decorative [Inference].
- **Darkness** makes enemies hard to spot [Fan-wiki]; radar and Sixth Sense offset it [Fan-wiki forum].
