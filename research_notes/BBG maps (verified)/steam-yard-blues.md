# Steam Yard Blues (2026 spelling: "Steamyard Blues")

Steam Yard Blues is a Battle Bears Gold arena that arrived in update 2.8 together with the King of the Windmill mode [Fan-wiki]. The wiki dates 2.8 to March 4, 2014 [Fan-wiki]. SkyVu posted a 35-second fly-through preview on February 21, 2014 [Canon]. It is the last map in the wiki's BBG Maps list and the last "New Map" entry in the wiki's Updates table, so it was probably the last new standard arena of BBG's original run [Inference].

The images show a steampunk airship yard at dusk [Observed: SkyVu preview frames; "steampunk airship yard" is my summary, Inference]:
- purple airship envelopes in brass rib cages, one with a long golden nose spike;
- two slate-blue fighter planes with shark-mouth nose art and nose propellers, tied down with ropes;
- a dark wooden plank deck;
- cyan jump pads set in gold, gear-shaped housings;
- a central gold gear platform;
- a panelled wall studded with big gold gears;
- green tunnels;
- a dusty peach sky over a butter-yellow glow, hazy taupe-to-mauve mountains, and a near-black maroon void below.

**2026 status.** On the Steam page SkyVu treats the map as a joke: "that yearly match on Steamyard Blues, most likely just for the soundtrack" [Canon]. In one frame of SkyVu's own "BBG Maps Ranked part 1" short, it sits in the bottom "13-16" tier [Observed]. Unlike the first draft, this check found the map in **2026 playtest builds**:
- fan thumbnails carrying build stamps 3.0.2 and 3.0.7 show it [Observed];
- a fan uploader's chapter lists log Free-for-All, Team Battle and Kill Confirmed matches on "Steamyard Blues" [Unverified: fan video descriptions].

## Setting

- **Name.** "Steam Yard" plus the props suggest a steam-age yard where airships and aircraft are moored and serviced [Inference]. "Blues" reads as a music joke, and SkyVu leans into the music angle in 2026 [Canon quote; Inference on intent].
- **What the images show.** A yard high in the air, open at the edges:
  - a distant ridge of hazy mountains on the horizon;
  - a near-black maroon expanse below the horizon, which could be a plain, a sea or cloud far below [Observed: preview maxres1/maxres3, 2026 HKbwq7PolNc maxres2; what it is is Unverified].
- **Edge trivia from the wiki:** "There is a ledge beneath the edge on an entire side of the map" [Fan-wiki]. It was added under the edit summary "Added some glitches in trivia", so it was probably an exploit spot, not a designed area [Inference].
- **Place in the BB world:** never stated [Unverified].

## Layout

The layout comes from the wiki (2014) unless marked otherwise. No top-down map exists in any source I reached.

- **Overall:** "There are a total of three levels in the map" [Fan-wiki]. The wiki numbers the levels by importance, not height. Its "First level" is the deck, also called "the highest part of the map" [Fan-wiki].
- **"First level", the deck:** "A deck accesed through either one of the four jump pads on the ground" (sic) [Fan-wiki].
  - The Bearscream Sandwich sits on "A lead off on the deck at the highest part of the map" [Fan-wiki]. A "lead off" is presumably a projecting ledge [Inference].
  - "On the deck, you can jump on the gear-like wall and go behind it" [Fan-wiki]. This was added as "glitches" trivia [Fan-wiki edit summary].
  - A likely candidate for the raised deck and its "gear-like wall" is the large panelled structure:
    - cream and pink panels with mauve diamonds and yellow square plates, gold rails, and big gold gears on its face (one gear holds a green-glass porthole) [Observed: preview maxres1 and maxres3; 2026 ORYnnvqn19w maxres2];
    - in the 2026 frame, characters stand on the gold rail along its top [Observed];
    - its identification as the wiki's deck and gear wall is [Inference].
- **"Second level", the main ground:** "The largest area of the map", with the Imperial Basket Grande, "two jump pads leading to the first level, and one of the two Health Packs" [Fan-wiki].
  - "There is one plane on each side of the map with a jump pad into the third level each, the tunnel entrances can also be entered with the Turkish SW Boots" [Fan-wiki].
  - "There are two slopes on the edge of the map, the two Shields and the last two jump pads for going on the first level are here as well. Just behind the slopes is an entrance into the third level." [Fan-wiki]
- **"Third level", the tunnels:** "A tunnel like structure with multiple paths, the other Health Pack is located here" [Fan-wiki]. The page also says the jump pads lead "up into a tunnel like structure", so the tunnels sit above the main ground. The likely bottom-to-top order is main ground, tunnels, deck [Inference].
- **Centre** [Observed: preview maxresdefault; 2026 ORYnnvqn19w maxres1, HKbwq7PolNc maxres3]:
  - a two-tier gold gear platform on the plank deck: a raised gold gear disc over a gear-shaped base whose arms hold two jump pads, with an orange cog set into it;
  - just beyond it, two orange checker-woven drums, each with white sacks hanging from the rim, a gold gear-plate lid and a jump pad on top.
  - These may be the main-ground pads up to the deck [Inference]. The centre is also the wiki's "middle of the map" windmill landing spot [Inference].
- **Pickups** [Fan-wiki]:
  - Bearscream Sandwich: "A lead off on the deck at the highest part of the map".
  - Imperial Basket Grande: "Underneath the plane on the left side of the map".
  - Health Pack: "At the intersection in the tunnels or underneath the plane on the right side of the map".
  - Shield: "Beside the two jump pads leading to the deck".
  - So the planes are raised high enough to have space underneath [Inference].
  - The Health Pack and Imperial Basket Grande location tables on their own wiki pages do not list this map [Fan-wiki, by absence].
- **Jump pads:** at least six. That is four to the deck plus one on each plane into the tunnels [count is Inference from Fan-wiki text]. Images show pads on the plane wings, on the drum lids and on the gear platform [Observed]. The wiki's BBG Maps page names only Skate or Die, Sky View and Toxic Terror as having no jump pads [Fan-wiki].
- **Symmetry:** "one plane on each side" suggests mirrored halves [Inference]. In the preview, the two planes face each other across the deck with noses toward the middle [Observed: maxresdefault]. The pickups are not mirrored: the Imperial Basket Grande is under the left plane, a Health Pack under the right [Fan-wiki].
- **Tunnels in images:** a green tunnel with a polygonal, roughly octagonal mouth looks out over an airship hull and a plane [Observed: maxres2]. A 2014 fan frame looks from a tunnel mouth down onto a plane wing and its pad, which fits the plane-pad-to-tunnel route [Observed: 3vBGASwuXEE hq2; fit is Inference].
- **Team bases and spawns:** undocumented. Which side is Red and which is Blue is unknown [Unverified].

## Look

**Lighting, time of day and sky**
- Dusk [Inference from Observed].
- The sky runs top to bottom as follows [Observed: preview maxres1, maxres2, maxres3]:
  - dusty peach-pink upper sky;
  - a butter-yellow glow along the mountain line;
  - hazy taupe-grey to mauve mountain silhouettes, darker toward their base;
  - a plum haze band;
  - a near-black maroon void below.
- In the downward-looking maxresdefault frame only the plum haze and void show. The first draft called the "upper sky" plum; that holds only for that frame [Observed].
- In the 2.8 promo art and a 2014 fan capture, grey-mauve clouds also float over the pale yellow glow [Observed: fb28; 3vBGASwuXEE maxresdefault].
- No sun disc, moon or stars appear in any image [Observed].
- Props catch warm gold highlights, and the shadows go deep brown and plum [Observed].
- The 2026 final-playtest frames (build 3.0.7) keep the same peach-and-yellow sky and maroon void [Observed: HKbwq7PolNc maxres2; ORYnnvqn19w maxres2].
- In the August 3.0.2 frame the planes read a brighter, more saturated blue [Observed: 3Zw0Q3aR2q4 maxres3]. SkyVu lists "Lighting improvements" for the final playtest [Canon]. A fan says the final build restored the old lighting on all maps [Unverified: InstableGR description].

**Signature props and set dressing** [Observed unless tagged]
1. **Cigar airship.**
   - A purple envelope in a brass rib-and-lattice cage, with an upper fin and a long golden spike with filigree at the nose (maxres3; 2026 HKbwq7PolNc maxres2).
   - Its tan-brass lower band repeats a dark-purple stencil of a round-eared bear head (crop `m3_blimp_stencil.jpg`):
     - one eye ringed by a pale monocle or goggle ring with a slit pupil;
     - the other eye a sleepy line;
     - a small cat-like "ω" mouth;
     - a dark two-blade propeller shape above the head, like a propeller beanie.
   - In the 2.8 promo art the ringed eye has a yellow iris with a black slit pupil [Observed: fb28_left].
   - The same stencil covers the orange-brass hull seen from the tunnel (maxres2).
2. **Upright balloon.**
   - A purple envelope latticed in gold, with a large glowing amber diamond panel with sunburst rays at the front.
   - It sits on a ring of pointed copper "crown" petals atop an octagonal riveted copper column.
   - Thin ropes run from it down to the plane-wing anchors (maxres1).
3. **Two shark-mouth fighters.**
   - Slate-blue and riveted, with a yellow-gold canopy frame.
   - White shark teeth on a dark red mouth, plus a painted white eye (maxres1, maxres3, mr crop).
   - A roundel: blue ring, white ring, red centre, thin yellow outline (maxresdefault).
   - Yellow-green "BBG" lettering on the fuselage (wiki screenshot; 2026 3Zw0Q3aR2q4 maxres3).
   - **A two-blade nose propeller with white blades edged in red on a gold gear hub** (maxres1). The draft's "red-and-white striped edge piece" is this propeller.
   - Tan ropes rise from brass hex-plate anchors on the wings toward the airships above (wiki screenshot, maxres1). So planes and airships are moored together [Inference].
   - Reading the roundel and shark mouth as WWII fighter references (RAF-style roundel, P-40-style nose art) is [Inference].
4. **Gear jump pads.** Standard BBG pads (cyan star centre, black-and-yellow hazard rim) sit in gold gear-shaped housings: on the plane wings, the drum lids and the central gear platform. Active pads throw up rising cyan light rings (maxres2, maxres3).
5. **Central gold gear platform with an orange cog** (see Layout).
6. **Two orange basket-weave drums** with white hanging sacks and gear lids. Wicker baskets hung with sandbags suggest balloon gondolas [Inference].
7. **The panelled gear wall.** Cream panels with pink-mauve diamonds and yellow square plates, gold rails, and big gold gears, one holding a green-glass porthole ringed with rivet studs (maxres1, maxres3; 2026 ORYnnvqn19w maxres2). The draft's "jewelled ring" is this ring of rivets.
8. **Orange slab with round holes** and a rope post (wiki screenshot, maxres1). Possibly a mooring block [Inference].
9. **Loose cream, tan and pink panels with red edges** scattered over the deck (maxresdefault). Spare wing or hull plating is a guess [Unverified].
10. **Green tunnels** with polygonal mouths (maxres2; 3vBGASwuXEE hq2; 5vPIJnVd1i4 hq1).

## Hazards and interactive elements

- "This map has no interactive features" [Fan-wiki].
- **Jump pads** throw players to the deck and the tunnels. The Turkish SW Boots can also reach the tunnel entrances [Fan-wiki].
- **Edges:** the map is open at the edges above a void [Observed]. "There is a ledge beneath the edge on an entire side of the map" [Fan-wiki]. Whether falling off kills you is not documented [Unverified].
- **King of the Windmill (2014–2018 only):**
  - The windmill landed in the middle of the map or on the deck's lead-off [Fan-wiki].
  - "The player must stand inside the circle until the windmill blasts off" [Fan-wiki].
  - "Players who are in the receiving end at the landing of the Windmill, the player will be heavily damaged or even killed" [Fan-wiki].
  - "When the windmill is about to land, chicken leg icons will fly out of the ground and into the air" [Fan-wiki].
  - **In the 2.8 promo art** the windmill is a purple-and-white pinwheel on a grey steel lattice tower. It lifts off on lilac-white rocket boosters in white smoke from a gold cog-shaped platform on the plank deck [Observed: fb28].
  - **In a 2014 fan thumbnail** the in-game windmill is a grey lattice tower on grey block feet with a tall tan pole, inside a translucent green band [Observed: 3vBGASwuXEE maxresdefault]. That is presumably the landed windmill and its scoring circle [Inference].
  - SkyVu's separate mode illustration shows the pinwheel on a lattice mast atop a green hill under a blue sky, not this map [Observed: wiki BBG_windmill_game_mode-300x225.png].
- **Pickups:** Bearscream Sandwich, Imperial Basket Grande, 2 Health Packs, 2 Shields [Fan-wiki].
