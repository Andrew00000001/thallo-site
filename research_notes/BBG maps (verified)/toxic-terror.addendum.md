# Toxic Terror: addendum (2026 status, floor plan, storey connections, props)

This addendum covers only the open gap items for Toxic Terror (BBG 2.3, Sep 2013). Earlier findings are in `/home/user/thallo-site/research_notes/BBG maps (unverified drafts)/research_toxic-terror.md`.

**Headline:** Toxic Terror is playable in the 2026 pre-release builds. I found it in fan footage of two sessions:
- the Sep 20, 2026 Steam/Discord playtest, in one team match;
- the Oct 15, 2026 final playtest, in one team match and at least two Free-for-All matches.

SkyVu has still not named the map in any text, so launch-day inclusion remains an inference, though a strong one.

**What I could reach:**
- **Storyboard sprites (`i.ytimg.com/sb/...`):** I sliced them into frames and opened them, at 120×90 to 429×180 px per frame. The signed sprite URLs came from Invidious API metadata (`invidious.f5.si/api/v1/videos/<id>`). Videos covered: about 40, including:
  - all four final-playtest uploads I could find;
  - SkyVu's own Sep 20 and Oct 15 livestreams;
  - the 2013 tour and the 2013 official preview.
- **Steam:** the store API for apps 4408320 and 5225670, and the Steam News API.
- **Official pages and video metadata:** battlebears.com and battlebears.com/signupyt, plus YouTube `next`/`search` metadata.
- **Fan wiki:** the saved Toxic Terror page and its forum replies.

**What I could not reach:**
- **Video streams.** The Invidious stream proxy sits behind an Anubis bot-check page. I did not try to bypass it. The YouTube player API returns "UNPLAYABLE" and the Piped instances are blocked. **I did not watch or listen to any video.** Every [Observed] item below is a storyboard frame I opened.
- **Timing:** timestamps are storyboard slot times, ±1 interval (2 s for short videos, 10 s for long ones).

## 2. Layout: floor plan and storey connections

**Sources:** the wiki text [Fan-wiki]; the 2013 fan walk-through WfTBPWZAQCs "Battle Bears Toxic Terror Tour and Soundtrack" (132 frames at 2 s; it is a near-empty match that ends "RED TEAM WINS", and the recorder is "ON TEAM RED"); the official 2.3 preview HquG87_5Xa0 (21 frames at 1 s); and the 2026 frames above. All frames are low-resolution.

### Overall shape
- **Wiki:** "a double-floored cavernous sewer system" [Fan-wiki].
  - Each team's spawn has an upper storey: Health Packs are "In the corner of the upper part of the spawn area"; Plant the Bomb bombs are "inside each team's own spawnpoints, one on each of the upper storeys" [Fan-wiki].
  - "a large climbable tree … springs up in the middle of the map" [Fan-wiki].
  - Bomb tubes are "next to the large tree to the left side from the perspective of the bomb carriers leaving their base" [Fan-wiki].
- **Size:** compact. A 2013 forum post (Some guy13, 2013-09-30) says "I think its way too small" [Fan-wiki forum, fan opinion].
- **Plan, as I read it:** spawn/base (two storeys), then the ooze floor, the central tree, the ooze floor, and the other spawn/base (two storeys). It is roughly mirror-symmetric [Inference from the paired pickups and bomb sites, plus the tour route below].

This is one player's path, so it shows connections, not a full survey [Observed, 120×90].

   - 2013: tour 1:32 and 3:56 [Observed, low-res].
   - 2026: a tall frame with horizontal slats beside the upper railing (KkDhuNbHn3I ~10:30; 1MP1rFi4t-Q ~38:00) [Observed].
   - Whether these are stairs, a ladder or a louvred door is unresolved [Unverified].
2. **Wooden ramps:** tour 3:08 and 3:18 [Observed, low-res].
3. **The climbable tree:**
   - [Fan-wiki].
   - A 2013 player writes "if botch uses death cloud on top of the tree trunk and jumps around, then u r soooo dead" [Fan-wiki forum]. So players can stand on the trunk [Inference].
4. **A ladder top in a floor hatch:** official preview frame 0:00 shows two rails with rungs coming up through a floor opening, in front of the arch-and-pipes view [Observed]. Whether it can be climbed is [Unverified].
5. **Open drops:** ledge views straight down to the ooze floor (tour 0:20–0:24) [Observed view]. That players can drop down there is [Inference].

- **Upper level over the spawn:** "If Cosmic Bomb explodes on the top level of a spawn, it can kill of the ground floor. … its layout is unique from existing stages" (Kodokor12, 2013-09-29) [Fan-wiki forum]. This implies the spawn's top level sits directly over its ground floor [Inference].

  - Red-tinted: tour 0:04–0:16 and 2:32–2:46; KkDhuNbHn3I ~13:40–13:50; MS2wiArCiCA ~53:20 [Observed].

- **Which end is which:**
  - The red-team recorder walks through the red zone straight after spawning (tour 0:04–0:16). He meets the blue zone only after crossing the tree (1:20).
  - The wiki puts the ninja-turtle weapon rack in the Blue base, and the earlier draft observed it under blue-violet rim light.
  - Together these suggest each base is tinted in its team's colour, with the tree between [Inference, weak; one route, low-res]. Not documented [Unverified].

## Prompt pack

**Establishing shot** (vertical 9:16, environment only, style-neutral; append the house-style suffix):

> Vertical 9:16 establishing shot inside a compact two-storey sewer hall at night, seen from an upper balcony railing looking down and across. In the centre a thick, twisting brown tree grows up through the floor, with yellow-brown ridged bark, and its branches spread under a low ceiling of big diagonal olive pipes. The ground floor is covered in square steel grates glowing neon lime from below. A bent drain pipe pours glowing ooze into a grated pool. Beside it stand pale-yellow drums marked with black radiation trefoils, and a round pit of ooze sits under a grid. Wooden railings and a narrow flight of wooden steps link the glowing floor to balconies on both sides. The dark brick walls hold paired amber barred windows and red capsule lamps. Between two diagonal pipes, a pointed arch glows lime-white. The far side is washed in deep red light. The near side is lit cool blue through barred four-pane windows, with a tall plain blue banner hanging from the balcony. A red octagonal sign and a yellow warning sign are mounted on pillars, both blank. Dark, damp, glowing: green bounce light on everything, soft haze. No characters, no readable text or logos.
