# Desert Airmine

Desert Airmine is a desert map from Battle Bears Royale (BBR). Its sand-topped islands float in tiers inside a deep red-rock canyon. A drill tower with Graham's face on it stands in the centre and moves up and down. A windmill on a lattice tower overlooks the map, and the Blue team spawns around a helipad.

- **History:** the fan wiki says the map shipped with BBR 1.0 (Oct 8, 2011). It was removed when the game relaunched as Battle Bears Gold (BBG; "update 2.0", about May 2013) and came back in BBG v2.7 (Jan 23, 2014).
- **2026:** SkyVu's 2026 Steam soundtrack has a "Desert Airmine" track. A fan frame from the Oct 15, 2026 final playtest (build 3.0.7) shows the map in the new build. Fan chapter titles list Team Battle and Free-for-All matches on it. SkyVu has not named it in its launch copy.

## Setting
- **Wiki descriptions** [Fan-wiki] ([page](https://battlebears.fandom.com/wiki/Desert_Airmine)):
  - "Taking place in desert-like surroundings"
  - "a massive-looking mine, featuring a large oil drill in the very center of the map, which constantly alternates in going up and down"
  - "several levels of floating desert islands" with "a plank across the big gaping canyon"
  - Old infobox (2012 to Aug 2014): "Type: Open-air", "Time of day: Day, Sunlight", "Jump Pads: Yes", "Suicide: Yes"
- **What the images show:** a deep, stepped red-rock canyon with flat-topped buttes, close to the American Southwest [Observed + Inference]. The islands are sand-topped mesas floating in mid-air, held up by or hung with steel lattice towers, cables and girders that run out to the canyon walls [Observed: renders; overhead].
- **Story:** no source says who runs the mine. The wiki suggests "Graham himself built this course, due to the presence of a small toilet and three tool sheds" [Fan-wiki, speculation]. The name probably puns on "air" (floating) and "mine" [Inference].

## Layout
- **Overall shape:** asymmetrical, with three height tiers of floating islands.
  - "The Desert Airmine was the very first asymmetrical stage in the game. It is built in three general levels, all split up on various smaller and bigger floating islands" [Fan-wiki].
  - A section pasted in on 2014-08-05 calls it "the second asymmetrical stage", so the page contradicts itself [Fan-wiki].
  - The Abusement Park page says "The stage is asymmetrical, like Desert Airmine" [Fan-wiki].
- **Overhead plan** (File:Desertmine.png, 795×763, uploaded 2012-07-21) [Observed]:
  - **Format:** a top-down render on a transparent background. Islands from different heights overlap in plan.
  - **Watermark:** a translucent "BATTLE BEARS" logo with a bear face runs across the centre. The wiki's other BBR overheads (Battlefield.png, Factory.png, Spaceship.png, Facingtemples.png) carry the same watermark, so this is probably a SkyVu-made set [Inference].
  - **Islands:** about seven sand islands packed into a rough square, plus one bare island off to the right. Grey X-braced truss bridges and pale-green mesh grate platforms link them. Long grey girders run off the edges [Observed].
  - **Red markers:** nine red crosshair markers sit on the two islands in the top half of the image.
    - Top-left (4 markers): a corrugated-roof shed whose corner holds a blue-framed jump pad, plus barrel clusters.
    - Top-right (5 markers): two small cacti, a yellow disc (possibly a sombrero seen from above [Inference]), and a box structure under a bladed rotor (probably the windmill [Inference]). A post-and-rope railing runs along its right edge.
  - **Blue markers:** nine blue crosshairs. Seven sit on the large island at the bottom, around a big round helipad with concentric rings and a red "H"; two are on a small island at the lower right [Observed]. These are probably spawn markers [Inference], which fits the wiki's Red base (first island with hut) and Blue base (helipad).
  - **Centre:** the drill tower seen from above: a red-rimmed octagonal head with cream radial blades, a long shaft running north–south, a box body and slatted panels with teal slats.
  - **Paths:** a long tan wooden beam (the "plank") runs from a grey machine on the left towards the centre.
  - **Jump pads:** small round teal pads, some with thin white lines that are probably launch trajectories [Observed; reading Inference].
- **The first revision of the file (2012-11-28) captioned it "Battlefield Airpad top-down view"**, a copy-paste error that was later fixed [Fan-wiki].
- **Level 1, the lowest (Red base):** "three large islands" [Fan-wiki]:
  - The first has "the spawn points for all red teams players as well as a small hut which also holds a small Jump Pad".
  - The second has "a cactus", which was "a sombrero cactus" in the text from 2012-11-28 to 2013-08-17, and "a jump pad which allows for access to the second level".
  - The third is "positioned beneath the second level" with another pad up.
- **Level 2, the largest (Blue base):** "one big, though weirdly shaped island as well as a smaller island, accessible by a bridge" [Fan-wiki].
  - The spawn is "most recognizable by the big helicopter landing pad".
  - From here players reach every level "either through a free fall or by using Jump Pads".
  - The bases are "very close to each other, only separated by a Jump Pad and a high level-difference".
- **Level 3, the top:** "2 smaller islands only connected by a very long jump using a Jump Pad which involves crossing the oil drill" [Fan-wiki].
  - The second island gives access to "a small windmill with a small platform", a sniper perch with "overview of almost the entire stage".
  - "The Blue team even has the ability to watch the entire Red team's base from the windmill platform."
  - From Jan 2013 to Jan 2014 the page added that on both top islands players could use SW Boots "to jump above the grey ammo stocks" [Fan-wiki, historical text].
- **Hidden fourth island:**
  - Current text: "When the oil drill is up, players can access a small hole in the floor, giving them access to a small island". The Bearscream Sandwich is there, and "A small Jump Pad allows for access back to the stage itself". The second section says "The jump pad there will launch you up and to the Red Team's base" [Fan-wiki].
  - The original Nov 2012 text said "When the oil drill is down", and placed "the Bearzerker pickup … along with the Bonus Joules" there. It was changed to "up" on 2012-12-07 and to Bearscream Sandwich on 2012-12-30, and Joules were dropped from the page on 2014-01-25 [wiki history].
  - Joule pickups were "removed in 2.1" [Fan-wiki, Joules page].
- **Routes:** jump pads are the main way around. There are "many small shortcuts", and "several small gaps in between the islands that can be jumped over given that the player has enough speed" with SW Boots or Jump [Fan-wiki].
- **Pickups** (current text) [Fan-wiki]:
  - Bearscream Sandwich: on the hidden island, "This is behind the jump pad".
  - Imperial Basket Grande: on the windmill platform ("Atop the windmill tower" on the item's page).
  - Health Pack: "on a small plank crossing the wide-open gap", called "Medic Pack" in 2012. The Health Pack page instead says "On the edge of the bunker at the red team's spawn point" and "In the middle of the narrow ledge around the center of the map".
  - 2 Shields: one on the third bottom-level island, and one on level 2 "accessible by a tiny Jump Pad on a small container".
- **Plant the Bomb:** "The red bomb is located on the second level beneath the windmill, while the blue bomb is positioned on the third level, close to the windmill Jump Pad." "The Bomb Inserters are located at each enemy team spawn point." The 2012 text called these "Bomb Tubes" [Fan-wiki].

   - On the head is a round yellow emblem of a bear in a yellow hard hat, teal goggles and a white bushy moustache [Observed: Desert_Airmine2 crop]. This matches Graham in SkyVu's 2011 "BATTLE BEARS ROYALE Sneak Peek" key art [Observed].

   - Beneath it, a heavy bit hangs over a square shaft cut into the sand [Observed]:

## Hazards and interactive elements
- **Falling off:** "This stage was dreaded due to the many places to commit suicide. Falling off the stage or hitting the drill is very likely to happen on this particular map and will count as a suicide." [Fan-wiki] Related BBG patch rows: 2.14 "Fixed Suicide giving players -1 kills" and 2.19 "Suicides are penalized in team battle" [Game text via wiki].
- **The drill:** "If the player hits the oil drill in mid-air, it will count as a suicide. To safely cross this, the player must use the Jump Pads while the drill is down." [Fan-wiki]
  - After BBR 1.4.3 its movement was "much more erratic" [Fan-wiki].
  - The Graham page calls it "The spinning drill" [Fan-wiki], while the map page describes up-and-down motion. The exact motion is unresolved.
- **The windmill rotor:** "Hitting the windmill itself will kill any player, counting it as a suicide." [Fan-wiki]
- **The hole in the Blue base:** a drop to the hidden pickup island. The current text says it opens when the drill is "up"; the Nov 2012 text said "down" [Fan-wiki].
  - "If you fall in that particular hole, do not worry."
  - If an Engineer mines its only jump pad "and you have no explosive weapons, it could trap you here."
- **Jump pads:** these are prime spots for Engineer Proximity Mines. "Placing a mine on the Jump Pad at the bottom island below the oil drill is also very effective as it is the only exit from that island." [Fan-wiki]
- **Shield caveat:** the BBR page says Shields grant immunity "from weapons" but "Will not prevent you from suiciding because of natural hazards though" [Fan-wiki].
