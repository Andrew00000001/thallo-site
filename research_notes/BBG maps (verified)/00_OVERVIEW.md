# BBG News maps and environments bible: overview

This is the top page of the BBG News maps and environments bible. It covers:
- which sources were reached;
- what is confirmed about the 2026 map lineup and modes;
- the environment conventions every map shares;
- which kinds of shorts suit each map;
- the rules for showing real BBG maps in the house style;
- the important corrections to our notes;
- how confident we are, and how to close the remaining gaps.

The detail lives in the dossiers published with this page:
- **Per-map dossiers:** one for each of the 15 legacy BBG arenas, plus the Tutorial. 2026 addenda are folded in for Toxic Terror, Space Oddity, Aztec Attack, Frozen Pundra, Steam Yard Blues, Desert Airmine and Haunted Castle.
- **3 topic dossiers:**
  - **BBG 2026 map lineup.** This one also covers the new map, Sky Pirates / Sky World, and identifies the map in every Steam trailer shot.
  - **Shared environment conventions.**
  - **Franchise story locations.** The Ursa Major, the "Pink Planet", the H.I.P. complex and others.
- **Quick-reference rows** cover these mode and prop topics: Kill Confirmed tags, Joule drops, the King of the Windmill windmill, the 2018 Battle Royale map ("Castle Royale"), the Oct 2026 lighting pass, and the main menu / Gear Up room.

This page does not repeat per-map detail. Where the inputs disagreed, I re-opened the deciding evidence for this page and say so. I re-checked:

## 0. Five facts that change how the evidence reads

1. **The Steam trailer and all five Steam screenshots do not show the 2026 look. They are most likely re-used 2013 footage.**
   - The trailer is "Battle Bears Gold Premium - Trailer" [Canon]. Its class cards are pixel-identical to SkyVu's May 30, 2013 trailer rfAyqVJrli4 [Observed: greyscale MSE 3.6–6.2 against medians of 6,490–7,476].
   - Its gameplay matches that trailer's YouTube storyboard (160×90, one frame a second) at a constant offset of about 4 s: 45 of the 55 storyboard frames from 5 to 59 s match (greyscale MSE 15–830 against medians of about 4,700–13,000), including the Graham segment and the Space Oddity clip [Observed: match table scratchpad edit2026_tr/sb_match.txt; storyboard comparison recorded in research_notes/BBG weapons (verified)/2026.md, "The Steam trailer is 2013 footage"; sheets and frames re-opened for this page, side by side in scratchpad review_ov/so_sb_check.jpg].
   - It shows exactly the 8 classes of the 2013 description, "Choose from 8 classes" [Canon; Observed].
   - It shows only maps that existed in May 2013 [Observed].
   - Each screenshot is a frame of the trailer [Observed].
   - So the gameplay is 2013 footage that SkyVu re-used on the 2026 Steam page [Inference, strong]. The cards are matched at full size, the gameplay only at storyboard size [Observed]. Call these images "the Steam trailer" or "Steam screenshot screen_0N", caption them "classic 2013 look" where the era matters, and never "2026 gameplay".
   - **The Space Oddity clip** (about 30.0–32.8 s; screen_01 is a frame of it) is 2013 footage too [Inference, strong]. The same moment, bloom included, is in the 2013 storyboard (2013 35–36 s = Steam 30.85–31.95 s) [Observed], and the Space Oddity dossier now dates the clip to 2013 as well (Era and status, "Age of the official footage"). Its pillar top blooms to near-white, which 2013–2021 fan captures lack [Observed]; that once suggested a newer or relit capture [Inference], but the 2013 storyboard already shows the bloom. Why SkyVu's capture blooms more than fan captures is [Unverified]. It does not show the Oct 2026 lighting either [Observed: so_steam_vs_hk.jpg, Space Oddity addendum].
   - **The Graham segment** (about 7.25–10.5 s, Gold Digger) is 2013 footage too [Inference, strong]: it is in the 2013 storyboard (2013 12–13 s = Steam 7.95–8.95 s) [Observed]. graham.md once dated it "probably a 2018-or-later capture" from its toon outlines alone, but some 2013 fan captures already have outlines (Gold Graham, Jul 2013; circuit-skin Graham, Sep–Nov 2013) [Observed: graham.addendum.md, Incidental findings 1]. Dec 2013 fan captures of its Instagraham skin have none [Observed], so why the trailer's Graham is outlined stays open [Unverified].
2. **SkyVu's 2026 text names only five maps** [Canon]:
   - four on Steam: "No Bears Land, Gold Digger and Space Oddity" and "Steamyard Blues";
   - the new "Sky World" / "Sky Pirates".

   Every other 2026 placement rests on frames and fan text.
3. **The 16-tile "BBG Maps Ranked part 1" grid is level-select art, not a roster, and not proof of a 2026 look.**
   - It shows 15 legacy maps with Space Oddity twice (r1c3 and r3c3), and no Sky Pirates [Observed: jk_grid_big.png, wiki_vs_ranked.jpg].
   - Eight tiles match tiles in the 2026 custom-match PICK LEVEL carousel [Observed: pixel correlation r 0.82–0.98].
   - Several tiles are the 2013 map-select renders with the lettering removed (Haunted Castle r2c3, Space Oddity r3c3) [Observed].
4. **Nobody watched a video or heard any audio.**
   - YouTube evidence is thumbnails, storyboard sprites (roughly 160×90 to 430×180 px, one frame about every 10 s), titles, descriptions and chapter lists [Observed / Unverified as labelled].
   - Hex values from these are approximate, about ±15% [Inference].
5. **The final playtest (Oct 15, 2026) added "-Lighting improvements"** [Canon], and its banner says "LIGHTING, EFFECT & TEXTURE IMPROVEMENTS" [Observed].
   - 2026 footage from before Oct 15, including the Sky Pirates reveal (build 3.0.3), may not show the launch look [Inference].
   - BBG 3.0 (Nov 15, 2021) had already applied "New Map Lighting" game-wide [Game text via wiki].

## 3. Shared environment conventions (summary)

Full evidence is in the conventions dossier.

**Team bases and colour**
- Two teams of up to 4, Red and Blue [Fan-wiki].
- The HUD puts red on the left and blue on the right, with a cog between them, in every era from 2012 to 2026 [Observed].
- Scenery shows team colour **only as accents**, never as a repaint [Inference from all maps]. Examples [Observed]:
  - No Bear's Land keep bands and Pelican't stripes;
  - H.I.P. door strips;
  - Shortest Parsec's base arms and doors;
  - Abusement Park's "[RED]" stencil (and "RED" painted in blue on the Blue side);
  - Gold Digger's gems;
  - Marecraft's ring rims;
  - Aztec's red-gem/gold-disc and silver-disc spawn rooms. The red team takes the red-gem temple [Inference, supported by 2026 frames].
- In FFA every opponent shows as Blue [Fan-wiki; Observed 2026].
- **Symmetry is the default** [Fan-wiki; Observed overheads]:
  - 180° point symmetry: No Bear's Land, Marecraft, Aztec Attack;
  - diagonal: Haunted Castle;
  - mirror: Shortest Parsec, H.I.P. Test Lab.

  Desert Airmine and Abusement Park are asymmetric [Fan-wiki].

**Spawns**
- The spawn shield gives "a few seconds of invincibility" (BBR 1.2.1, Jan 27, 2012) [Game text via wiki], 4.5 s per the wiki [Fan-wiki].
- It shows as pale-green shields with a plus emblem, in 2013 [Observed] and in 2026 (1MP1rFi4t-Q storyboard 39:50–40:40) [Observed].

**Pickups**
- Bearscream Sandwich: an angry brown bear-face ice-cream sandwich [Observed], usually central [Fan-wiki]. The wiki says 2× damage on one page and 3× on another [Fan-wiki, conflicting].
- Imperial Basket Grande, Health Pack and Shield [Fan-wiki].
- Joule drops: three dark hexagonal tokens with a blue bolt, at every death site, since 2.1 (Jul 15, 2013) [Game text via wiki; Observed 2026]. They also appear in non-KC modes, so they are not KC tags [Observed; Inference].
- **Gas is a currency, not a pickup, and there is no ammo pickup** [Fan-wiki].
- The Oct 2026 No Bear's Land banner shows the Sandwich face and a green shield icon on the keep roof [Observed; identification Inference].

**Jump pads**
- One design everywhere: a dark octagon with a yellow-and-black hazard rim, a glowing cyan star core and stacked cyan rings. It is the same in the Steam trailer (2013 footage [Inference, strong]) and in 3.0.7 [Observed].
- None on Skate or Die, Sky View or Toxic Terror [Fan-wiki].
- The wiki says Aztec has 12; the top-down render shows 14 markers [Fan-wiki; Observed].

**Plant the Bomb hardware**
- A team-coloured round bomb on a same-colour stand, carried to the enemy tube [Fan-wiki; Observed 2012].
- 2-minute fuse that pulses in the last 30 s; the carrier cannot attack; falling with the bomb detonates it; a deposit pays 225 Joules [Fan-wiki]. The 2026 feed reads "DEPOSIT +225" [Observed: Frozen Pundra].
- 2026 hardware differs by map and is only partly seen [Observed, low-res]:
  - Space Oddity: bomb stands on the pillar top and a maroon funnel tube;
  - Frozen Pundra: a clamshell bomb hatch with warning signs;
  - Aztec Attack: a device on a red plinth;
  - a red countdown box ("0:24:74", Toxic Terror).

  The tube model is otherwise unknown [Unverified].

**Kill zones**
- Environmental deaths count as "SUICIDE" [Fan-wiki; Observed feed]. A Shield does not stop fall deaths [Fan-wiki].
- Fall-outs: Sky View, Shortest Parsec, Desert Airmine, Gold Digger's pits, Haunted Castle's void [Fan-wiki].
- Lava on Aztec Attack; Marecraft's canal; H.I.P.'s loading bay; Shortest Parsec's laser; Desert Airmine's drill and windmill [Fan-wiki].
- Interactive maps: Haunted Castle levers, Shortest Parsec's floor switch, Frozen Pundra's icicles [Fan-wiki]. Steam Yard Blues "has no interactive features" [Fan-wiki].

**Art direction of the game**
- Chunky low-poly geometry with hand-painted textures [Observed].
- One dominant hue plus an accent per map, mood carried by coloured practical light (lava, moon, neon, gems, the laser) [Observed].
- No ink outline on environments [Observed]. Characters have a thick outline in the Steam trailer (2013 footage [Inference, strong]) and in 2026 captures [Observed]. It is absent from most 2012–14 phone captures (some 2013 fan captures of Gold and circuit-skin Graham have it; graham.addendum.md, Incidental findings 1) [Observed].
- "All assets in this game are created by human artists." [Canon]

**Scale**
- Doors about 1.5–2× a bear's height; rails and sandbags at knee to waist height; tarps and crate stacks give full cover [Inference from single frames].

**Music**
- Every legacy arena has its own looping theme, 0:38–1:29 [Canon: OST durations; loop is Inference].
- Credits are collective: "Rob Ruiz, AnDrue Humphreys, and Benjamin Vavak" [Canon].
- 2026 adds a menu Jukebox [Canon]. That players can assign songs to maps is a fan claim [Unverified].

**The Oct 2026 lighting pass, as measured** [Observed, Python-sampled from low-res storyboards]:
- **No Bear's Land:** deeper shadow faces and a paler sky. This is the only same-channel before/after.
- **Desert Airmine:** paler, yellower sand; canyon unchanged.
- **Space Oddity:** warmer amber-ochre with bright yellow trim, close to BBR 2012.
- **Haunted Castle:** darker than 2013.
- **Toxic Terror:** deeper, yellower greens.
- **Frozen Pundra:** saturated cyan and cobalt, with purple under 3% of pixels.

A fan says the pass restores "what they looked like back in the day" [Unverified]. The measurements support that only in part [Inference].

## 5. Rules for showing maps accurately

These rules are production policy. Their basis is the house files, `bbg-news-repo/characters/STYLE.md` and `README.md`, or this research.

### 5.1 Documented versus invented
1. **Tier every element before it goes in a script or prompt:**
   - **[Canon] / [Observed]:** show freely.
   - **[Fan-wiki]:** show, but never caption as official.
   - **[Inference]:** show cautiously; never make it the punchline of a "fact" post.
   - **[Unverified] / conflicting:** don't build a plot on it. Examples: Aztec's OCO/Astoria temple symbols, the "Botch drawing", the Space Oddity hologram subject, Mos Delish as a BLAST character, damage from Toxic Terror's ooze.
   - **[BBG News invention]:** allowed for camera, staging, timing and gags. It must not contradict a documented fact, and must never appear in a "what's in BBG 2026" post.
2. **Never invent a hazard and present it as real.**
   - Skate or Die's "water" is paint [Fan-wiki; Observed].
   - Sky Pirates' moving ships are documented for BBO only [Fan-wiki].
   - Gold Digger's carts are static props [Observed].

   If a gag needs one of these, its description says it is a fan take.
3. **Pick one era per short and say which.**
   - **"Classic 2013 look":** the Steam trailer and screenshots (2013 footage [Inference, strong]; section 0) and 2013–14 captures.
   - **"2026 look":** only from 3.0.7 frames, the CT5 banner, or demo footage.
   - Level-select tile art and pre-Oct-15 2026 footage are not proof of the 2026 look (section 0).
4. **Keep the documented time of day and one lighting setup per scene**, per STYLE.md [house style]:
   - No Bears Land: noon.
   - Gold Digger, Aztec Attack, Haunted Castle, Abusement Park, Toxic Terror: night.
   - Sky View, Steamyard Blues: dusk.
   - Desert Airmine, Skate or Die, Sky Pirates: day.
   - Interiors (Space Oddity, H.I.P., Marecraft, Frozen Pundra): no sky.

   [Observed for each per the per-map dossiers.]

### 5.2 No readable text, logos or third-party designs
5. **Keep every sign's shape, colour and placement, but render it blank or with abstract marks.** This covers:
   - stencils ("[RED]", "PMOC 20-11", "AIRLOCK");
   - graffiti ("Oops, Killed ya!", "ABBi", "BBR#1");
   - posters, marquees, "BOMB HOLE WARNING" signs, and the "BATTLE BEARS" billboard;
   - the Shortest Parsec "YOU SHOULDN'T BE ABLE TO READ THIS!" panel. A blank panel keeps that joke's irony.

   Basis: the shared negative list includes "official logos, text, watermarks".
6. **Never render Haunted Castle's gravestone names.** They are real SkyVu staff and forum members [Fan-wiki; Observed].
7. **Keep official emblems abstract.** The H.I.P. roundel becomes a loose round-eared figure over a ring. The Sky Pirates Jolly Roger becomes a generic white bear skull over crossed cutlasses, not a copy of the BBG app-icon bear head. This refines the lineup dossier's "keep it".
8. **Make third-party nods generic.** These game references must not be reproduced recognisably:
   - Space Oddity's "Death Star" hologram [Fan-wiki];
   - Toxic Terror's Ninja-Turtle weapon rack [Observed; Inference];
   - Tutorial's Matrix-like code rain [Observed];
   - the Halo/Aliens Pelican't lineage [Fan-wiki].

   Use a wireframe sphere, a generic weapon rack, and glyph-free green rain.
9. **Strip all HUD from reference frames:** score bars, kill feeds, reticles, radar, timers, build stamps and chat overlays.

### 5.3 Matching the house style (feature-film 3D, chosen Oct 17, 2026)
10. **The game's look is a source, not a target.**
    - Keep the layout, landmark silhouettes, material identity, palette family and light sources.
    - Re-render everything in polished, rounded, feature-film forms with soft global illumination, a warm key, a cool fill and a gentle rim [house style].
    - **Do not write "low-poly" in a prompt.** It is on the shared negative list, and several draft prompt packs use it (section 6, item 15).
11. **Use the map's own practical light as the key**, and keep emissive props as identity anchors: jump-pad cyan, gem glows, ooze green, neon, lava. Examples:
    - Gold Digger: warm lamp and gold glow, cool moon fill.
    - Aztec Attack: lava underglow.
    - Toxic Terror: green bounce with amber lamps.
12. **Treat sampled hexes as palette families** (±15%), at "saturated but natural" strength [house style]. Do not add ink outlines; the house style has none.
13. **Keep backgrounds uncluttered and staging simple** [house style]. One landmark per shot beats a full map tour.

### 5.4 Names in captions
14. Spell the map names this way:
    - "No Bears Land" and "Steamyard Blues", as on Steam and in the OST [Canon].
    - "Sky Pirates (Sky World)" on first mention [Canon: both names].
    - "H.I.P. Test Lab" for the map, its BBG name [Fan-wiki]. The 2026 in-game name is unverified, and "Huggable Factory" titles the music [Canon]; the 2026 jukebox truncates it as "Huggable Factor…" [Observed].

    Never confuse Sky View with Sky World.

### 5.5 Teen rating (13+)
15. **Marecraft** is a stylised warm-orange organic cavern: soft cartoon ribs and glowing green pools. No blood, wet viscera or recognisable organs [house rule: "No blood, gore or organs"].
16. **Lava, ooze, pits and void falls end in intact cartoon pratfalls or pops** [house rule]. The game's "SUICIDE" wording stays off screen.

### 5.6 Every short
17. End card, word for word: "Unofficial fan film, not affiliated with SkyVu; characters © SkyVu." [house rule]
18. Red team by default. Scenery team colour stays an accent [house rule; Inference from section 3].
