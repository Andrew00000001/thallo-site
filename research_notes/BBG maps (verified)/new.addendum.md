# Sky Pirates (2026 name in Steam news: "Sky World")

Sky Pirates is a daylight autumn village on a floating island. It has white-plaster, dark-timbered houses with red roofs, a white clock tower raised on stone stilts, birch trees with orange crowns and a pale limestone mesa. Pirate galleons flying bear-skull flags are moored at the edges [Observed]. It is the only brand-new arena SkyVu has shown for the 2026 relaunch. It is not new to the franchise: the map comes from Battle Bears Ultimate/Overclock [Fan-wiki; Game text via wiki].

# Castle Royale (the 2018 Battle Royale map)

A sprawling grey-stone fortress complex on floating grass islands over a sea of clouds, in bright daylight. One fortress has teal-capped crenellations and a very tall round central keep. A sister fortress has red-capped crenellations. Around them are hedges, an iron-fenced graveyard, pumpkins and sandy paths [Observed]. It was the arena of BBG's 2018 Battle Royale mode, "Battle Royale with Cheese". The only official source for the name "Castle Royale" is the 2026 soundtrack.
