# Franchise story locations: the Ursa Major, the "Pink Planet" (Huggable Planet), the H.I.P. complex and others

This dossier covers the places where the Battle Bears story games and official media are set, so BBG News shorts can use them as settings. The main locations are:
- SkyVu's flagship *Ursa Major*.
- The planet the Huggables come from. SkyVu calls it the "pink planet" or "Pink Planet", in video copy and in its own comic; the fan wiki calls it the "Huggable Planet".
- The H.I.P. (Huggable Instrumentality Project) complex from *Battle Bears Zero*.

Smaller entries cover the Pelican't dropship, the BLAST holodeck and kitchen, the Zombocalypse pyramid, the Huggable Fortress, Wil's video-game world, the *Fortress* worlds and the 2019 board-game crash planet. A table shows which of these locations also exist as, or appear inside, BBG multiplayer maps.

This is the verified and edited version of the draft. Every quote was re-fetched and every [Observed] image was re-downloaded and opened again this session. Changes from the draft are folded in silently; the substantive ones are listed under "Corrections to our notes" and "Open questions".

### Era and status
- **BB-1 (2010).** The ship is the setting of all three campaigns.
  - Oliver's campaign shipped in v1.0 (June 21, 2010).
  - v1.2 (Aug 11, 2010) already added a "New level for both campaigns", so Riggs's campaign existed by then.
  - Wil's campaign came in v1.5 (Dec 18, 2010) [Fan-wiki update table].
  - Survival mode moves "through an endless number of rooms on the Ursa Major", reusing rooms from Oliver's Stages 1 and 3 but not the Dojo [Fan-wiki].
- **BLAST (2011).** The kitchen and the holodeck [Canon].
- **Comic (2011).** The invasion seen from Oliver's and Riggs's side; Chapter 2 "takes place after Battle Bears -1 the game & before Battle Bears Zero." [Canon comic text; Observed: File:Page1.jpg].
- **BB0 (2012/13).** The ship is seized, landed on the planet and fought over [Fan-wiki plot]. "reclaim your mothership" [Canon per our notes; Unverified this session].
- **BBF.** In level 1-6 Wil appears as a hologram and "jettisons towards the planet without setting coordinates" [Fan-wiki].
- **2020 Steam re-release of BB-1.** "This special PC edition includes all 3 campaigns, full HD cutscenes" [Canon].
- **BBH (2024).** The re-imagined BB-1 campaign. SkyVu's App Store screenshot shows a top-down 3D deck: grey steel plates with yellow trim, a hazard-framed "AIRLOCK" floor hatch, and the lilac Mecha-Bearzerker with "42" on its chest [Observed: official/bbh_campaign_ipad.jpg].
- **BBG (2013–21) and BBG 2026.** There is no Ursa Major map [Unverified; not in the map lists or the Steam copy]. The only tie is the Radiation Rod text [Game text via wiki].
  - The 2026 OST adds "I'm a Battle Bear (feat. Vivyd, Brian Bell & Leck One.)" and "The Right to Bear Arms (feat. Vivyd, Brian Bell & Leck One.)" as "select bonus vocal tracks from across the series" [Canon].
  - Remixes of both songs are on the 2010 BB-1 OST, as its "DJ Guest" mixes, tracks 1–4 [Canon: Apple Music album 383419699, via iTunes lookup], and "I'M A BATTLE BEAR" first came with BBZ v1.5 (Dec 2009) [Fan-wiki update table].

### Setting
- A crewed capital ship, the Battle Bears' "flagship" [Canon].
- The crawl implies they owe money on it ("pay off their ship") [Game text via wiki]. The wiki says the Pot O' Gold was meant "to pay off the debt of renting the Ursa Major" [Fan-wiki]. Riggs calls the job a "GALACATASURE job" and himself a "MERC" [Canon comic text; Observed: File:Page1.jpg].
- It orbits a planet ringed by Huggables: "I'M STILL LOOKING INTO THAT. BUT I ESTIMATE THERE ARE MILLIONS OF THESE HUGGABLES ORBITING THE PLANET." [Canon comic text; Observed: File:Comics_10.jpg].
- **Crew.**
  - The wiki names Oliver, Captain Malcolm, Riggs, Wil, Jackson and Grimes [Fan-wiki].
  - In SkyVu's comic, Oliver is the ship's "MAINTENANCE BEAR", and Malcolm is named [Canon comic text; Observed].
  - "Captain" and "leader" are the wiki's [Fan-wiki].
  - Astoria is mentioned ("BUT WHAT ABOUT ASTORIA AND THE-") [Canon comic text; Observed: File:Comics_13.jpg]. That she and Tillman were "on another ship" is the wiki's reading [Fan-wiki].
- The ship's AI is ABBi. Its single camera eye "appears on computers and doorways all over the Ursa Major during Wil's campaign" [Fan-wiki]. A round lens with cyan-lit slats in a dark frame edged with cyan light strips is shown [Observed: xc9p5NkhuII hq2].
- The wiki says the ship is "similar to the Tantive IV" and that the BB-1 opening echoes *A New Hope* [Fan-wiki].

### Layout: rooms and routes documented in official media and the wiki
No deck plan exists [Unverified]. Steam BB-1's controls include "To Access MAP: Tab" [Canon], and its screenshots show a "MAP" button [Observed: ss_1482340_3], so the game has an in-game map that I could not view [Unverified]. The rooms below run in roughly the order the BB-1 campaigns pass through them.

- **Bridge / cockpit.**
  - Malcolm (a brown bear in a brown long coat with gold epaulettes and medals) pilots from a chair with twin control yokes.
  - Around him are dark consoles with orange ship schematics and cyan readouts, a white-panelled wall, and a second small bear crewman at a console [Observed: File:Malcum_from_battle_bears.png, a wiki-hosted BB-1 cutscene frame].
  - The wiki says Malcolm "returned to the ship bridge, controlled the ship and tried to sweep Huggables off the window via windshield wiper", and that Riggs yells at him to fly the ship straight [Fan-wiki]. It also says a picture of a cake is shown on his computer [Fan-wiki].
- **Corridors ("staggering corridors" [Canon, channel unconfirmed]).** Three looks are documented.
  - *Cutscenes:* grey concrete-look wall panels, white square floor tiles, yellow-and-black hazard bands at the base of the walls, and heavy grey doors with rounded-rectangle windows [Observed: P6z5l7toC3w maxres/hq1, 7IBdqMm5nKg hq1, E6lLj4HfAsc maxres]. A white bolted door framed in vertical yellow-black hazard stripes appears in the official BB-1 Steam trailer [Observed: video/bb1/full/b_33.jpg, 0:33].
  - *3D gameplay:* white tiled floors and dark grey panel walls, with yellow-black hazard stripes along the floor edges, hexagonal-grid ceilings, heavy doors with a red lamp above, and dark doorways under a yellow lamp [Observed: Steam BB-1 trailer 0:16–0:29 and 0:54–0:57; File:Screen_Shot_2013-05-07_at_11.11.18_PM.png].
