# Space Oddity

Space Oddity is a small battle arena inside a spaceship, mirrored end to end [Fan-wiki; Observed]. Its long walls are banks of amber-orange tinted windows with stars behind them. The metalwork is olive and grey-green, and cyan lights run through it [Observed]. In the middle stands a squat, dark olive octagonal pillar with a striped white-cyan top. A heavy ceiling unit with a cyan ring light hangs directly above it, and the Bearscream Sandwich sits on top [Observed; Fan-wiki]. A two-level team base sits at each end, with a basement [Fan-wiki].

The map launched in Battle Bears Royale 1.0 (wiki date October 8, 2011) as **"Spacecraft"** [Fan-wiki]. It became "Space Oddity" with the "version 2.0" relaunch as Battle Bears Gold (iOS, May 24, 2013) [Fan-wiki]. SkyVu's 2026 Steam page names it as one of the "Iconic Maps" that will return; the game launches Nov 4, 2026 [Canon].

## Layout

- **Shape:** a long rectangle with chamfered ends, mirrored end to end and side to side [Observed: Spaceship.png].
  - Wiki: "relatively small … It is symmetrical in every way, even with pick-ups." [Fan-wiki]
  - Wiki, 2012 edit: "the smallest map yet" [Fan-wiki, revision 18800].
  - Wiki: "short range classes are very effective on this stage, although a skilled mobile Sniper can still be very effective." [Fan-wiki]
- **Team bases at the two short ends:** "Each base has two levels, the main level where the main operating systems are and the big window is, which can be jumped on if you have SW Boots or So Fly. There is a basement which can be shot through with explosives to breach into the main level. There are 3 Jump Pads in each base." [Fan-wiki]
  - **Main level:**
    - Consoles with twin cyan screens sit under huge angled windows [Observed: Spacecraft2.jpg].
    - A long, angular floor opening edged with glowing yellow strips looks down into the basement [Observed: Spacecraft2.jpg; ranked grid r1c3].
    - From above, each base end shows a glowing yellow-orange end band, a dark teal recess with yellow trim, cream-tan panels and three round cyan-and-yellow medallions [Observed: Spaceship.png]. The medallions are probably the three base jump pads [Inference].
  - **Looking out:** a fan thumbnail looks from a base through a large yellow-glow-edged opening into the hall, toward the amber window wall, the cyan ceiling disc and the pillar [Observed: rJufLQwYCek hq1].
  - **Basement:**
    - A row of cryo pods. Each has a frosted cyan window, a green LCD, red indicator lights, and a yellow triangle with a paw print and "CRYO" lettering. Red keypads sit beside them [Observed: Spacecraft2.jpg].
    - "There is an entrance to the basement in the fronts of the bases." [Fan-wiki]
  - **Front doors:** "There are also two doors on each front ends of the base that inside hold jump pads to go up to the next level." [Fan-wiki]
  - **Spawns:** "spawn points are located either on the upper or lower level of the base." [Fan-wiki]
  - **The big window:** a 2012 edit says Huggables could "So Fly up and get behind the giant window" [Fan-wiki, revision 18800].

  - Wiki: "In the middle of the map is the Bearscream Sandwich power up on top of a podium, which can be jumped onto with So Fly, Turkish SW Boots or one of the four jump pads on every side." [Fan-wiki]

  - **From above:** a striped octagon ringed by four cyan-and-yellow medallions at the compass points and eight brown-orange chevron shapes, inside a larger octagonal outline [Observed: Spaceship.png]. The medallions are the four jump pads [Inference, matching the wiki's four pads].

  - **BBR-era view:** in Spacecraft1.jpg the cyan cone falls in front of an orange-lit octagonal window, over a round grated floor disc [Observed]. How that render relates to the BBG pillar is [Unverified].
