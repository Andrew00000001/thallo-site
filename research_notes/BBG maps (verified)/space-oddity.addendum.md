# Space Oddity: the 2026 final-build look and 2026 modes (addendum)

This addendum covers only two questions: what Space Oddity looks like in the final 2026 playtest build, and which modes it runs in 2026. It adds to `research_notes/BBG maps (unverified drafts)/research_space-oddity.md` and does not replace it.

**Sources I could reach:**
- **Steam store API (app 4408320) and Steam news API**, re-fetched 2026-10-18. The store text is byte-identical to the 2026-10-16 copy, with the same five screenshots and the same one trailer.
- **SkyVu's own livestream on the BATTLE BEARS channel.** "BBG Livestream Final Playtest before Steam NextFest Oct 19" (ZMhLVXWT6OY), with its description from Invidious and YouTube storyboard frames.
- **Fan playtest uploads, storyboard frames only:**
  - InstableGR, final playtest (HKbwq7PolNc) and playtest #3 (aujTcdFuxdE). I fetched the playtest #3 storyboards myself this session.
  - Left4Dead (MS2wiArCiCA), Vada Zora Reva Nova on iPad (dz4iqZE8nQE), droid BBG (Dwc66f0q9Rk, CmYil-JJdC0) and Slinkster (tNcHwpk5UuY).
  - Older fan thumbnails for BBR 2012, BBG 2.5 and about 2021.
- **The wiki Updates page** (saved copy) for the v3.0 notes.

**What these frames are.** YouTube storyboard frames are preview stills sampled every 10 s (429×180 or 320×180) or every 2 s (320×180) for short videos. **I watched no video and heard no audio.** Every visual claim below comes from stills I opened with Read. Chapter titles and descriptions written by fans are [Unverified] fan metadata.

**What "final build" means here.** It is the build used in the final Discord playtest on Oct 15, 2026. The build stamp in the lower-right corner reads "3.0.7" at low resolution [Observed: MS2wiArCiCA f_1060000; HKbwq7PolNc frames], matching our weapons notes. The Nov 4 release build has not been seen by anyone outside SkyVu [Unverified].

## Look: final 2026 build (3.0.7) compared with earlier builds

### What changed

**1. A warmer, yellow-olive grade with less grey.**
- **Method:** I sampled the side bands of every frame (x 4–32% and 68–88%, y 14–56%), which avoids the player model and the HUD, and dropped HUD-green pixels. The figures are % of sampled pixels, with "warm" = amber + olive hues and "achromatic" = saturation < 0.18. Script: `maps/dl/space-oddity-2026/scripts/side_stats.py` [Observed measurement].
- **The four 2026 builds before 3.0.7:**
  - 3.0.2 (Aug 21): warm 14%, orange 30%, achromatic 18%.
  - 3.0.5 Slinkster (Sep 20): warm 20%, orange 20%, achromatic 23%.
  - 3.0.5 droid (Sep 21): warm 16%, orange 18%, achromatic 14%.
- **The five 3.0.7 captures:**
  - InstableGR: warm 45%, achromatic 6%.
  - droid, Oct 16: warm 38%, achromatic 7%.
  - Left4Dead: warm 37%, achromatic 6%.

### Lighting and other look notes

- **Overall:** a brighter, warmer, golden key with a yellow-olive (slightly green) overall cast; cyan practical lights unchanged [Observed]. Mean brightness of the side bands is 0.47–0.48 in two 3.0.7 PC captures, against 0.39–0.43 in 3.0.2 and 3.0.5 [Observed measurement].
- **Light shafts:** diagonal shafts through the windows are not clearly resolvable at this resolution [Unverified].
- **Stars:** whether stars still show through the glass is [Unverified] at this resolution.
- **Steam store media do not show the 3.0.7 lighting.** screen_01 and the trailer shot (30–32.6 s) are more saturated, with orange-red glass and teal shadows [Observed: `so_steam_vs_hk.jpg`]. SkyVu asked testers for UI-free footage "for our Steam and store pages" [Canon], so the store media may be replaced [Inference].
- **Trailer age:** the trailer's hue profile sits closer to the BBG 2.5 thumbnails (distance 34) than to 3.0.2 (52) or to BBR (58). That weakly supports the draft's view that it is 2013-era footage [Inference, weak].

## Prompt pack: Space Oddity, 2026 final-build lighting

**Establishing shot (9:16, environment only; append the house-style suffix).**

Vertical 9:16 interior establishing shot of a compact, mirror-symmetric spaceship battle hall, lit in a warm golden-olive grade.
- **Camera:** chest height on a raised cream-olive deck at one end, looking down the hall.
- **Centre:** a squat octagonal pillar of near-black olive metal (#2A2A1A) with a round black porthole and a column of stacked mustard up-chevrons (#837334). Its flat top glows white with a cyan tint (#E6FAEA to #FEFFFA).
- **Above the pillar:** a heavy octagonal ceiling unit with a bright cyan ring light (#ADE2BC to #F7FFFC) casts a soft pale cone onto the pillar top.
- **Floor:** olive-tan (#656A3A), with an ochre ring (#4D4622) around the pillar. Four round launch pads sit at the compass points, each a glowing cyan disc (#8CE7D2) inside a yellow-and-black chevron hazard ring.
- **Side walls:** long walls of golden-amber glass panels (#D3AF50 to #F3BA46), fronted by two tiers of cream-olive catwalks. Every ledge, floor-opening edge and octagonal doorframe is outlined in a bright glowing yellow strip (#F6AB34 to #FFF446).
- **Back wall:** cyan light pipes in ringed segments (#3FB494, peaking near #91DCB2) bend diagonally up either side of the pillar. Tall cryo tubes glow cyan (#85FAF5) beside low consoles with twin cyan screens (#5ABAC0).
- **Details:**
  - A dark wall screen shows cyan line-drawn schematics.
  - A yellow-green warning triangle sits under a console.
  - Stacks of near-black green cargo crates (#172017) stand in the corners.
  - Orange glowing wall alcoves (#9B7436) carry a pale three-pronged emblem.
- **Mood:** warm golden key light against cool cyan practical lights, soft bloom, faint green cast in the shadows (#304332).
- **No:** characters, readable text or logos.
- **[BBG News invention]:** the exact camera height and lens, the far base being fully visible, the strength of the bloom and cone, and the schematic screen's content beyond "cyan line drawings". Stars in the glass are also [BBG News invention], because they were not resolvable in 2026 frames.

**Staging spots:**
1. **Bomb on the pillar.** A blue bomb on a dark-blue square stand and a red stand at the far edge of the glowing pillar top, under the cyan ring light [Observed in the 3.0.7 PTB frames]. The grab or steal choreography is [BBG News invention].
2. **The red bomb tube by the consoles.** A maroon funnel tube beside a bank of cyan-screen consoles under the golden windows, as the "deliver it" beat [Observed]. A fuse-hiss countdown gag is [BBG News invention].
3. **The schematic screen by the cryo tubes.** A dark screen of cyan line drawings (a dish, a satellite, a bear-shaped mech) as a sight gag or lore tease, next to the glowing cryo tubes and the paw-print warning triangle [Observed, low-res]. Any animation, or naming the mech, is [BBG News invention].
