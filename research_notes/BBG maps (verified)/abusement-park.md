# Abusement Park

Abusement Park is a night-time carnival arena with a horror streak, walled in by cracked stone [Observed]. A roller coaster ridden by grinning pink Huggables loops the whole perimeter. The middle is packed with striped tents, booths, joke signs and teaser posters [Observed].

- **Added:** Battle Bears Royale (BBR) update 1.3.2, notes dated Aug 24, 2012 [Game text via wiki].
- **BBG:** stayed in BBG from the 2013 relaunch through the BBG 3.0 "New Map Lighting" update of Nov 15, 2021 [Game text via wiki] [Observed].
- **2026, official media:** the map appears in the official Steam trailer (red base), in the "SAME" panel of the official teaser, and on the map tile in the thumbnail of the official Short "BBG Maps Ranked part 1" [Observed]. The 2026 soundtrack opens with "Abusement Park" [Canon].
- **2026, playtests:** fan uploads from playtest #3 (Aug 2026) list matches on it, and one 2026 build frame shows its EXIT stairs [Unverified fan text] [Observed].
- **Not named:** no official text, including the Steam store copy, names the map.

## Setting

- The wiki calls it "a horror-themed carnival" with "many attractions like the House of Mirrors, and a giant crate which reads 'CONDEMNED', and many advertisement posters", and says "a large carnival-like place with many circus tents in the middle" [Fan-wiki].
- The name is "a pun on 'Amusement Park', with the pun between 'Amuse' and 'Abuse'" [Fan-wiki].
- What the images show [Observed]:
  - A run-down carnival at dusk turning to night, sealed inside tall cracked grey stone-block walls. Outside the walls there is only void: a fan "Out of the map" upload shows a player falling into black (XqiPkFluvII hq3).
  - Signs read "ONLY ONE CAN LEAVE", "[NO ENTRY]" and "CONDEMNED". The only "EXIT" costs "Only 400,000 Tix".
- This fits the blurb's joke that the park is a trap you cannot afford to leave [Canon per prior notes, Unverified] [Inference].
- No source gives a real-world or in-universe location [Unverified]. No source documents the map in BB Heroes (2024), BBU or BBO [Unverified].

## Layout

**Overall shape**
- A roughly square arena enclosed by stone walls [Observed] (top-down File:Abusement_Park.jpg; overview File:Abusement_Park.png).
- An irregular closed coaster loop runs around the perimeter at roughly roof height, on red-and-white striped pylons [Observed].

**Asymmetry**
- "The stage is asymmetrical, like Desert Airmine" [Fan-wiki].
- Rev. 9056 (2012-08-24): "Like the desert stage, the abusment park has no even spawns." [Fan-wiki].
- Dec 2012: "one of the only 2 maps that has a non-symmetrical shape (the other being the Desert Airmine)" [Fan-wiki].

**Team bases**
- **Spawn markers (top-down image) [Observed]:**
  - About 11 red spawn crosshairs sit on wooden decks and nearby asphalt in one corner (NW in that image). About 11 blue crosshairs sit on a wooden deck in the diagonally opposite corner (SE). The compass orientation is arbitrary.
  - The red corner is lit warm salmon-orange and the blue corner cool blue-grey.
- **Coloured pads:**
  - Each base has a pad in its own colour with a nub in the other team's colour (a red pad with a blue nub, a blue pad with a red nub) [Observed].
  - Plant the Bomb stages "feature a bomb in each team's color and a bomb tube in each team's color" [Fan-wiki: Plant the Bomb]. These pads are probably the bomb tubes [Inference].
- **Red base:**
  - A raised plank deck with chunky yellow U-rails, under cracked stone walls stencilled "[RED]" with dripping red stars [Observed] (trailer t_0037/t_0044; 0n34M2VxJDE hq3; Fbm3TY_D5HE hq1).
  - From the red deck the yellow EXIT sign is visible (HaZhpTFlWAY hqdefault). The twin blue posters stand both beside the [RED] wall (trailer; overview) and beside the EXIT stairs (c6qGpQ4zxVA hq1; 3Trxex7dVDw) [Observed]. So the EXIT stairs sit near the red base [Inference].
  - Wiki: Imperial Basket Grande "Beside the ramp leading out of red's spawn point" and "At the 'exit' above a flight of steps" [Fan-wiki: Imperial Basket Grande].
  - 2012 text: Blue can "go through the tunnel under the Red Team's base, (where you will find a sign that says 'Not Wil's Candy Stash')" [Fan-wiki, revisions 9298–53046].
- **Blue base [Observed]:**
  - A paler, cool-lit plank deck with blue spray graffiti on the stone walls (a blue ring, "RED" in blue, a blue bear-head silhouette, "BBR#1"), next to wooden stairs (Screenshot (509); 0n34M2VxJDE hq1; KsddBonDY88 hq3).
  - A blue-and-white striped structure with white sawtooth trim and a wide grey elevated walkway on square pillars stand beside the blue deck (overview).
  - The backlit lightning-bolt sign box, a yellow-black hazard pillar and the glowing yellow doorway with a toaster cutout are near the blue-graffiti wall (Screenshot (509); NewLevelLights; 6PU2KP9ayN8 hqdefault).
  - Wiki: "the bridge" near Blue's base (Shield) and, in BBR, Bonus Joules "under the bridge" [Fan-wiki]. The grey walkway is probably that bridge [Inference].
  - Red can "go around and go through the back side of the Blue Team's base or go straight and walk through/jump through the platform" [Fan-wiki].

**Centre [Observed]**
- The largest roof is a big square maroon-and-white striped tent near the red side, with a concentric pyramid-style roof.
- Three pale-green peaked tents stand beside it.
- Orange and yellow striped booths include the Mirrored House and the Pump-A-Chump shooting gallery.
- An elongated octagonal yellow-and-maroon striped tent stands near the blue deck. This is probably the "gazebo-like structure" holding the Bearscream Sandwich "right next to" Blue's spawn [Fan-wiki] [Inference].

**Plant the Bomb**
- "once a player moves out of their base, they will automatically see the bombs, both red and blue in front of the House of Mirrors" [Fan-wiki].
- The top-down image shows a small blue icon and a small red icon side by side on the top edge of a central yellow booth [Observed]. This is probably the bomb platforms [Inference].

**Routes**
- "the narrow corridor between the Red and Blue Teams' spawn points" (map page) and "the corridor connecting the two sides of the park" (Health Pack page) [Fan-wiki].
- The top-down image shows a dark corridor through the south-centre with a rainbow-striped floor section [Observed].
- "The narrow alley next to [the Bearscream gazebo] is a very good place to send explosive projectiles" [Fan-wiki].
- Also mentioned [Fan-wiki]: "the raised walkway opposite the Red team's spawn points" and "the hole at the corner of the arena" (map page), and "the 'exit' above a flight of steps" (Imperial Basket Grande page).
- Blue can "jump through the jump pads and go straight forward" [Fan-wiki].

**Verticality**
- Raised decks and walkways, a tunnel under the red base, stairs and jump pads [Fan-wiki] [Observed].
- The coaster runs overhead. Players appear on the track only in glitch content: HuggableCart.jpg shows a player standing on the track, and 6PU2KP9ayN8 (titled "Abusement Park's best glitch") shows a player beside a car [Observed]. It is probably not normal play space [Inference].

## Landmarks

1. **Perimeter roller coaster** [Observed] (HuggableCart.jpg; File:Abusement_Park.jpg; File:Abusement_Park.png).
   - A dark oxblood rail with cream edges and a red stripe, carried on red-and-white candy-striped pylons around the whole arena.
   - Boxy lime-green cars with a red-orange starburst decal and two headlights. Each carries a grinning pink Huggable with raised arms.
   - Wiki caption: "This is what the Huggable in a cart looks like in the roller-coaster in Abusement Park, for those who didn't know the look of it." [Fan-wiki].
2. **The red base** [Observed] (trailer about 17.6–22 s; 2026 teaser "SAME" panel).

   - A maroon-and-white striped tent wall stands alongside.

   - A white sign reads "LIKE A HOU[SE] / BUT BACKWA[RDS]" (etzDXojqUKc). On the tile, part of the sign also looks reversed.
