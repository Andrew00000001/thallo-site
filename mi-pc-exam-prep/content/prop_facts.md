## Michigan Property and Casualty Guaranty Association (MCL 500.7901–.7949)

### Membership and structure
- Chapter 79 is the "property and casualty guaranty association act." [MCL 500.7901]
- Every insurer authorized to write insurance other than life or disability in Michigan must belong, as a condition of keeping its authority to do business. The Michigan Basic Property Insurance Association (the FAIR plan pool) is exempt. [MCL 500.7911(1)]
- A surplus lines insurer (coverage procured "solely by virtue of chapter 19") is not an authorized insurer for this chapter, so it is not a member and its insureds are not protected. [MCL 500.7911(2)]
- Life and disability insurers may not be members. [MCL 500.7911a]
- "Insolvent insurer" means a member insurer against which a final order of liquidation, with a finding of insolvency, has been entered by a court in its state of domicile. [MCL 500.7921(a)]
- The board of governors has 5 member insurers (3 domestic and 2 foreign; at least 2 stock and 2 nonstock) plus 2 public members. The commissioner appoints all of them to 3-year terms. [MCL 500.7912(1)]
- The plan of operation requires the commissioner's prior written approval. [MCL 500.7914(1)]

### Covered claims
- A covered claim is an unpaid obligation of an insolvent insurer that meets all of these conditions: [MCL 500.7925(1)]
  - it arises from a policy issued to, or payable to, a Michigan resident;
  - it is presented by the claims-filing deadline;
  - it was incurred before, at, or **within 30 days after** the receiver was appointed;
  - it arises from any kind of insurance **except life and disability**.
- **Unearned premium:** covered claims exclude unearned-premium refunds above the **first $500** per person per insolvent insurer. That base is adjusted annually for cost of living. **No refund under $50** is made. [MCL 500.7925(2)(a)]
- For July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027 the maximum unearned premium refund is **$2,063**. It was $1,998 for 7/1/2025–6/30/2026. [DIFS Bulletin 2026-13-INS]
- Covered claims exclude these obligations: [MCL 500.7925(2)(b)]
  - those arising after the policy expires;
  - those arising after the insured replaces the policy;
  - those arising after the association cancels the policy.
- **Net worth exclusion:** no first- or third-party claim by or against an insured whose net worth exceeds **$25,000,000** on December 31 of the year before the insolvency. Net worth includes affiliates on a consolidated basis. The figure is CPI-adjusted every January 1. [MCL 500.7925(4)]
- The 2026 net worth limit (1/1/2026–12/31/2026) is **$39,920,000**. [DIFS Bulletin 2026-04-INS]
- Covered claims never include the part of a claim above the policy limit. [MCL 500.7925(5)]
- **Claim cap:** the portion of a claim over **$5,000,000** is not a covered claim. Workers' compensation claims and no-fault PIP claims (MCL 500.3107) are exempt from the cap. The cap is CPI-adjusted every January 1, and the cap in effect when the claim is paid applies. [MCL 500.7925(6)]
- The 2026 covered claim cap (1/1/2026–12/31/2026) is **$7,980,000**. [DIFS Bulletin 2026-04-INS]
- Amounts owed to reinsurers, insurers, pools, HMOs or health care corporations as subrogation or contribution are not covered claims. [MCL 500.7925(3)]
- Covered claims exclude the insolvent insurer's own pre-receivership adjustment fees, attorney fees, court costs, interest and bond premiums. [MCL 500.7925(7)]
- **Deductible:** the Michigan statute sets no per-claim deductible for covered claims. The only small-amount rule is the $50 minimum unearned-premium refund. [MCL 500.7925 (full text reviewed)]

### Duties and powers
- The association may pay claims directly, through a servicing facility, or by reinsurance or transfer of liabilities to a member insurer. [MCL 500.7931(1)]
- It stands in the insolvent insurer's shoes: it may appear, defend, investigate, settle, pay or deny claims. [MCL 500.7931(2)]
- **Other insurance first:** amounts recoverable from other policies, such as a claimant's own coverage or a self-insured program, are credited against the covered claim. The claimant must exhaust that coverage first. A claimant's own life or disability policy is not counted. [MCL 500.7931(3)]
- **Coverage continuation:** in-force policies continue until the earliest of these: [MCL 500.7931(4)]
  - the policy expires;
  - the insured replaces it;
  - the association cancels it;
  - **30 days after the receiver is appointed** (the outside limit).
- The association may cancel the insolvent insurer's policies on **10 days' written notice**. [MCL 500.7931(5)]
- **Assessments:** members are assessed in proportion to their net direct written premium, separately for 5 categories. [MCL 500.7941(1)–(2)]
  - The categories are: workers' compensation; automobile; title; fire/allied/farmowners/homeowners/inland marine/earthquake/credit; and all other lines except life and disability.
  - Members get at least 30 days' notice before an assessment is due.
- No member may be assessed more than **1% of its prior-year net direct written premium** in a calendar year. Assessments are recognized in ratemaking. [MCL 500.7941(3)–(4)]
- **Stay of court proceedings:** court proceedings involving the insolvent insurer are stayed for **6 months** after the receiver is appointed. [MCL 500.7945(1)]
- The association is exempt from state and local taxes and fees except property taxes. [MCL 500.7947]
- Members, the association, its agents and employees, and the commissioner have no liability for actions taken under the chapter. [MCL 500.7948]
- The association is subject to the commissioner's regulation. A member may appeal an association action to the commissioner within **30 days**. [MCL 500.7949]

## Mandatory Fire Policy Provisions (MCL 500.2833)
- The prescribed "standard fire policy" form (former 500.2832) was repealed effective Jan 1, 1992. [MCL 500.2832]
- Every fire policy must still contain the provisions in 500.2833(1) and at least the coverage of the former standard fire policy. [MCL 500.2833(2)]
- Required provisions under MCL 500.2833(1): [MCL 500.2833(1)(a)–(s)]
  - (a) minimum coverage is the **actual cash value** of the property at the time of loss;
  - (b) minimum coverage is direct loss by **fire and lightning**, plus **pro rata coverage for 5 days** for property moved to another location to protect it from a covered peril;
  - (c) the policy may be void for misrepresentation, fraud or concealment;
  - (d) a statement of the property not covered;
  - (e) a statement of the perils not covered;
  - (f) the conditions that suspend or restrict the insurance;
  - (g) how a provision may be waived or changed;
  - (h) the **insured may cancel at any time**; the minimum earned premium is the **greater of pro rata or $25**;
  - (i) the **insurer may cancel** by mailing at least **10 days'** written notice to each named insured; the minimum earned premium is the greater of pro rata or $25, and any untendered excess is refunded on demand;
  - (j) mortgagee provisions (see below);
  - (k) the insurer pays no more than its **pro rata share** with other insurance;
  - (l) notice requirements after a loss;
  - (m) appraisal (see below);
  - (n) the insurer may repair, replace, rebuild or take the property;
  - (o) **no abandonment** of property to the insurer;
  - (p) loss is payable **within 30 days after the insurer receives proof of the amount of loss**, except as provided in 500.2845;
  - (q) suit is allowed only after complying with the policy; suit must begin **within 1 year after the loss or within the period stated in the policy, whichever is longer**; the time is **tolled** from when the insured notifies the insurer of the loss until the insurer formally denies liability;
  - (r) the insurer is subrogated to the insured's rights of recovery;
  - (s) the policy takes effect at **12:01 a.m. standard time** at the property's location.
- **Mortgagee (j):** [MCL 500.2833(1)(j)]
  - The insurer may cancel the mortgagee's interest on **10 days'** written notice.
  - If the insured fails to file proof of loss, the mortgagee must file it **within 60 days** after notice.
  - If the insurer claims no liability to the owner, it is subrogated to the mortgagee's rights to the extent it pays the mortgagee. Alternatively, it may pay off the mortgage and take an assignment.
- **Payment exception (500.2845):** on a fire or explosion loss to real property, when the final settlement exceeds 49% of the insurance, the insurer withholds **25%** of the ACV or final settlement, whichever is less. [MCL 500.2845(1),(15)]
  - For residential property the withholding is capped at a $12,000 base (from 2015), CPI-adjusted each January 1. The cap is **$16,383** for 1/1/2026-12/31/2026. [MCL 500.2845(1); DIFS Bulletin 2025-27-INS] Section 2845 covers only municipalities with fewer than 50,000 people in counties with fewer than 425,000 people. Municipalities in counties of 425,000 or more, or with 50,000 or more people, use the parallel MCL 500.2227 instead. That statute also covers vandalism, malicious mischief, wind, hail, riot and civil commotion losses. Since July 1, 2024 (2024 PA 82) its residential cap has a $24,000 base, CPI-adjusted each July 1. That cap is **$25,090** for 7/1/2026-6/30/2027. [MCL 500.2845(12); MCL 500.2227(1),(12); DIFS Bulletin 2026-16-INS]
  - This applies only in participating cities, villages and townships, which can escrow the money to make sure the site is repaired or cleared.

## Appraisal (MCL 500.2833(1)(m))
- If the insured and insurer disagree on ACV or the amount of loss, either party may make a **written demand** for appraisal. [MCL 500.2833(1)(m)]
- Each party picks a competent, **independent** appraiser and identifies that appraiser to the other side **within 20 days** after receiving the demand. [MCL 500.2833(1)(m)]
- The two appraisers pick an impartial **umpire**. If they cannot agree **within 15 days**, either party may ask a **circuit court judge** in the county where the loss occurred or the property is located to choose one. [MCL 500.2833(1)(m)]
- How the amount is set: [MCL 500.2833(1)(m)]
  - The appraisers set ACV and the amount of loss item by item.
  - A written agreement between the two appraisers sets the loss.
  - If they cannot agree, they submit their differences to the umpire, and **any 2 of the 3** can set the loss in writing.
- **Each party pays its own appraiser.** The umpire and the other appraisal expenses are split **equally**. [MCL 500.2833(1)(m)]
- The ISO HO and DP forms use the same 20-day and 15-day steps. Their umpire is chosen by "a judge of a court of record in the state where the residence premises is located." [ISO HO 00 03 05 11, Sec. I Cond. F; ISO DP 00 03 12 02, Cond. G]

## Cancellation and Nonrenewal (MCL 500.2123, 500.3020)
- **Home and auto insurance (Essential Insurance Act):** a "termination" is a refusal to continue to insure for any reason other than nonpayment. [MCL 500.2104(5), 500.2123(1)]
  - Termination requires **at least 30 days'** written notice, delivered or mailed to the named insured.
  - The notice must state the effective date and **each specific reason**.
- A termination notice mailed or delivered **within the first 55 days** after a policy is first issued can take effect **not less than 20 days** after it is mailed. [MCL 500.2123(2)]
- A termination for **nonpayment of premium** takes effect as provided in the policy. [MCL 500.2123(3)]
- A termination is effective only if its reasons conform to the insurer's underwriting rules. [MCL 500.2123(4)]
- Section 2123 does not authorize terminating auto insurance in violation of chapter 32. [MCL 500.2123(5)]
- Where other parts of the Code conflict with chapter 21, chapter 21 governs auto and home insurance. [MCL 500.2106(4)]
- **Casualty policies (all classes except workers' comp and mortgage guaranty), including motor vehicle coverage:** [MCL 500.3020(1)(a)–(c)]
  - The insured may cancel at any time and receives a pro rata refund.
  - The insurer may cancel with **not less than 10 days'** written notice. For a **private passenger auto** policy, 10 days' notice is enough only for cancellation for **nonpayment of premium**: chapter 32 does not apply to nonpayment, and under chapter 21 a nonpayment cancellation takes effect as provided in the policy [MCL 500.3212, 500.2123(3)]. Any other cancellation of a private passenger auto policy must also meet chapter 32 (at least **20 days'** notice by certified mail [MCL 500.3224]) and chapter 21 (**30 days'** notice for a termination, or 20 days within the first 55 days [MCL 500.2123]).
  - Minimum earned premium is the greater of pro rata or **$25**, except for auto.
- An insurer may refuse to renew a **malpractice** policy only with **not less than 60 days'** written notice. [MCL 500.3020(1)(d)]
- Cancellation does not affect claims that arose before it. Mailing the notice is prima facie proof of notice, and delivery counts the same as mailing. [MCL 500.3020(5)]
- The ISO HO and DP forms' own cancellation terms (Michigan law overrides them where it differs): [ISO HO 00 03 05 11, Secs. I & II Cond. C–D]
  - 10 days for nonpayment;
  - 10 days for any reason during the first 60 days of a new policy;
  - after 60 days, only for material misrepresentation or substantial change in risk, with 30 days' notice;
  - nonrenewal on 30 days' notice.

## Terrorism Risk Insurance Program (15 U.S.C. 6701 note; P.L. 107-297, 109-144, 110-160, 114-1, 116-94)
- The program was created by TRIA, P.L. 107-297, signed Nov 26, 2002. [home.treasury.gov TRIP page]
- It has been extended four times: [home.treasury.gov TRIP page]
  - P.L. 109-144 (TRIEA, 2005), through 12/31/2007;
  - P.L. 110-160 (TRIPRA, 2007), through 12/31/2014;
  - P.L. 114-1 (2015), through 12/31/2020;
  - P.L. 116-94 (2019), through **December 31, 2027**.
- **The program terminates December 31, 2027** under current law. [TRIA §108(a), 15 U.S.C. 6701 note]
- **Certification:** the **Secretary of the Treasury** certifies an act of terrorism, **in consultation with the Secretary of Homeland Security and the Attorney General**. [TRIA §102(1)(A)]
  - The act must be violent or dangerous to human life, property or infrastructure.
  - It must cause damage in the U.S. (or to a U.S. air carrier, U.S. vessel or U.S. mission abroad).
  - It must be committed to coerce the U.S. civilian population or influence U.S. government policy.
- No act may be certified if it is part of a **war declared by Congress** (workers' comp excepted), or if aggregate P&C losses **do not exceed $5,000,000**. Certification decisions are final and not subject to judicial review. [TRIA §102(1)(B)–(C)]
- **Insurer deductible** = **20%** of the insurer's direct earned premium for the **preceding calendar year**. [TRIA §102(7)]
- **Federal share** = **80%** of insured losses above the insurer deductible. It was 85% before 2016 and dropped 1 point a year until it reached 80% in 2020. [TRIA §103(e)(1)(A)]
- **Program trigger:** no federal payment unless aggregate industry insured losses from certified acts exceed **$200,000,000** (calendar year 2020 and later). [TRIA §103(e)(1)(B)]
- **Cap:** neither the Treasury nor an insurer that has met its deductible pays any part of annual aggregate insured losses above **$100,000,000,000**. [TRIA §103(e)(2)]
- **Mandatory availability:** each insurer must make terrorism coverage available in all of its P&C policies, on terms that do not differ materially from its other coverage. [TRIA §103(c)]
- **Required disclosure:** the insurer must clearly and conspicuously disclose the terrorism premium (as a separate line item) and the federal share. Since 2007 it must also disclose the $100 billion cap at offer, purchase and renewal. [TRIA §103(b)(2)–(3)]
- **Lines covered:** commercial P&C, including **workers' compensation**, excess, and **D&O**. [TRIA §102(11)]
- **Lines excluded:** [TRIA §102(11)]
  - commercial auto;
  - burglary and theft;
  - surety;
  - professional liability;
  - farmowners multiple peril;
  - crop;
  - mortgage and title;
  - financial guaranty;
  - medical malpractice;
  - health and life;
  - NFIP flood;
  - reinsurance.
- Participation is mandatory for every entity that meets the definition of "insurer." [TRIA §103(a)(3)]

## Termination of Authority to Represent Insurer (MCL 500.1209)
- **Servicing after termination:** when a producer's authority ends, a producer with property rights in the renewals stays responsible until existing policies are canceled, replaced or expire. [MCL 500.1209(1)]
  - The written agency agreement governs what the producer may do after notice of termination.
  - If the agreement is silent, the producer may **service existing policies** but may **not bind new risks, renew policies, or increase the insurer's obligation** without the insurer's approval.
- An insurer **may not cancel or nonrenew a policy because the producer's contract was terminated**. [MCL 500.1209(1)]
- Subsection (1) does not apply in these cases: [MCL 500.1209(1)]
  - life insurers and their producers;
  - employee producers;
  - captive (single-insurer or single-group) producers, if the insurer owns the renewals and the change does not cancel or nonrenew any policy.
- **Auto and home producers may be terminated only for these reasons** (this does not apply to employee producers or captive producers when the insurer owns the renewals and the termination does not cause any home or auto policy to be canceled or nonrenewed [MCL 500.1209(4)]): [MCL 500.1209(2)]
  - (a) malfeasance;
  - (b) breach of fiduciary duty or trust;
  - (c) violation of the Insurance Code;
  - (d) failure to perform under the contract;
  - (e) **fewer than 25 home and auto applications in the preceding 12 months**.
- A termination may not be based **primarily** on any of these: [MCL 500.1209(3)]
  - the **geographic location** of the producer's business;
  - loss experience tied to that location;
  - the producer's performance of Essential Insurance Act (chapter 21) duties.
- The insurer must notify the Director within 30 days after a termination takes effect. It must mail a copy to the producer within 15 days of notifying the Director (for a 1239 for-cause termination, by certified mail, return receipt requested, or by nationally recognized overnight delivery). The producer may file comments within 30 days. [MCL 500.1208b(1),(2),(4)]

## Essential Insurance Act (MCL 500.2101–.2131)

### Scope and purpose
- Chapter 21 was added by 1979 PA 145. Sections 2101–2105, 2107 and 2131 took effect Jan 1, 1980; the rest of the chapter took effect Jan 1, 1981. [MCL 500.2131]
- An auto or home policy may not be offered, bound, issued, delivered or renewed in Michigan unless it conforms to chapter 21. [MCL 500.2105(1)]
- How the chapter works: insurers may refuse, nonrenew or limit coverage for an **eligible person** only under written underwriting rules limited to the criteria the statute allows. This is what makes home and auto coverage available to eligible persons. [MCL 500.2117(1), 500.2118(1), 500.2119]
- Statute does not have a separate "purpose" section. The availability purpose is inferred from 2117 and 2118. [MCL 500.2101–.2131 (all sections reviewed)]

### Definitions
- **"Home insurance"** means fire insurance on the insured's eligible dwelling, plus other nonbusiness insurance included in or endorsed to it. It does not include commercial, industrial, professional or business property or liability. [MCL 500.2103(3)]
- **"Eligible person," home insurance:** an owner-occupant or tenant of a house, condominium unit, cooperative unit, room or apartment, or the **owner-occupant of a multiple-unit dwelling of not more than 4 units**. [MCL 500.2103(2)]
- A person is **not** an eligible person for home insurance if any of the following applies: [MCL 500.2103(2)(a)–(k)]
  - convicted of arson, conspiracy to commit arson, or certain related crimes within the preceding **5 years**;
  - had a home insurance claim **successfully denied within 5 years** for arson or fraud;
  - the dwelling is used for an **illegal or demonstrably hazardous** purpose;
  - refuses to buy at least **80% of replacement cost** under a replacement cost policy;
  - refuses to buy **100% of market value** under a repair cost policy;
  - refuses to buy **100% of ACV** under a tenant policy;
  - had a policy **canceled for nonpayment within 2 years**, unless the premium is paid in full;
  - the insured value is **below $15,000** (repair cost policy) or **below $35,000** (replacement cost policy; the director may change the latter by rule);
  - the dwelling has physical conditions that clearly present an **extreme likelihood of a significant loss**;
  - **property taxes are delinquent 2 or more years**;
  - fails to keep a uniformly required club or group membership.
- **"Eligible person," auto insurance:** an owner or registrant of a Michigan-registered vehicle, or a Michigan licensed driver. [MCL 500.2103(1)]
  - Excluded, for example: suspended or revoked license; more than **6 insurance eligibility points**.
- **"Declination"** means any of these: [MCL 500.2102(4)]
  - an agent refuses to submit an application;
  - an insurer refuses to issue a policy;
  - the applicant is offered insurance at higher rates through a different insurer than the one requested;
  - the applicant is offered less favorable terms than requested.
- **"Repair cost policy":** home coverage based substantially on **market value**, paying to repair with like kind and quality or with conventional materials and methods. [MCL 500.2104(2)]
- **"Replacement cost policy":** coverage based substantially on replacement cost. [MCL 500.2104(3)]
- **"Termination"** includes moving an insured to an affiliate at higher rates or with less coverage unless the insured asked for it. It also includes offering less favorable terms than before. [MCL 500.2104(5)]
- **"Underwriting rules"** are the insurer's written standards for issuing, refusing, renewing or limiting coverage. They must be phrased so a person of ordinary intelligence can understand them. [MCL 500.2104(6)]

### Home insurance underwriting (MCL 500.2117)
- As a condition of keeping its certificate of authority, an insurer may not refuse, nonrenew, or limit home insurance for an eligible person except under underwriting rules allowed by 2117 and 2119. [MCL 500.2117(1)]
- Home underwriting rules may be based **only** on the following: [MCL 500.2117(2)]
  - (a) criteria identical to the 2103(2) eligibility standards;
  - (b) **physical condition** of the property, using objective rules tied to the insured perils and based on specific codes or standards, **without regard to the age of the structure**; a certificate of compliance from a government agency creates a rebuttable presumption that the dwelling meets the insurer's physical-condition rules;
  - (c) **for renewal**, the claim history in the preceding **3 years**, but only claims arising from the insured's negligence, or the insured's failure to correct a physical condition after written notice;
  - (d) the relationship between market value and replacement cost, but only if the insurer offers a repair cost policy;
  - (e) **for nonrenewal**, paid-claim history excluding liability claims: **3 paid claims in 3 years** totaling at least **$3,000** excluding weather claims, or at least **$4,000** including weather claims;
    - The dollar figures are statutory base amounts, CPI-adjusted on Jan 1, 2006 and every 6th year after. Since **Jan. 1, 2024** the adjusted amounts are **$5,000** (excluding weather claims) and **$6,600** (including weather claims). The next adjustment is Jan. 1, 2030. [MCL 500.2117(5); DIFS Bulletin 2024-02-INS]
    - An insurer may also use a 3-claim rule for insureds who have been with it continuously for a period it sets between 5 and 10 years.
  - (f) a number of residences that is inconsistent with the insurer's approved forms;
  - (g) a dwelling **unoccupied more than 60 days** with evidence of intent to vacate;
  - (h) an **adjacent physical hazard** that causes a significant risk of loss; neighboring residential property and traffic patterns do not count;
  - (i) failure to buy more than 80% of replacement cost, if that is a condition of sale and the insurer also offers an 80% replacement cost form;
  - (j) threats, harassment or physical assault on insurer or agency staff, if reported to law enforcement.
- **Repair cost policy rules:** [MCL 500.2117(3)–(4)]
  - It must be substantially equivalent to the insurer's replacement cost policy, and at least equivalent to the HO-2 replacement cost policy of the principal rating organization as of Oct 1, 1979.
  - Its premium may not exceed **105%** of the premium for 80% of replacement cost under the equivalent replacement cost policy.

### Other underwriting and notice rules
- All underwriting rules must be **in writing** and applied **uniformly** throughout the state. [MCL 500.2119(1)–(2)]
  - They are filed with the commissioner for information before use and are open to public inspection.
  - Rules for new applicants may differ from renewal rules only for persons who are not eligible persons.
- Every rate filing must state that the insurer will not refuse, nonrenew, or limit coverage because of **the location of the risk**. That practice is **redlining**, and it is prohibited. [MCL 500.2108(8)]
- **Inspections:** the criteria for choosing which dwellings to inspect may not be based on these factors: [MCL 500.2121(1)]
  - race, color, creed or national origin;
  - marital status or sex;
  - residence or age;
  - disability or lawful occupation.
- **Home insurance rating classifications** (other than inland marine floaters or endorsements) may be based only on the factors below. Separately, an insurer may use additional factors if the plan is consistent with the act's purposes and reflects reasonably anticipated increases or reductions in losses or expenses [MCL 500.2111(9)]. [MCL 500.2111(7)–(8)]
  - amount and type of coverage;
  - security and safety devices;
  - repairable structural defects;
  - fire protection class;
  - construction;
  - loss experience the insured controls;
  - smoking materials;
  - distance from a hydrant;
  - availability of law enforcement or crime prevention services.
  - Home risks may also be grouped by territory.
- Insurers may offer a home insurance discount to seniors **55 or older**, applied uniformly. [MCL 500.2111d]
- **Declination:** the insurer or agent must give the applicant **each specific reason**. [MCL 500.2122(1)–(2)]
  - The explanation must be in writing if the application was in writing.
  - If the application was oral, the reasons may be given orally, with a written explanation offered if the applicant requests it **within 90 days**.
  - Refusing to provide an application form counts as a declination.
- **Dispute process:** a person who believes coverage was improperly denied or the premium is wrong is entitled to a private, informal, **managerial-level conference** with the insurer. [MCL 500.2113]
  - If the insurer does not provide the conference and a proposed resolution **within 30 days**, or the person disagrees with the result, the person may get a determination from the commissioner.
  - Either side may then request a contested-case hearing.
- **Agent duties (a condition of licensure):** for each eligible person, the agent must: [MCL 500.2116(1)]
  - give a premium quotation for the coverages the agent's insurers offer;
  - disclose how many insurers the agent represents;
  - not channel the person away from a coverage or insurer;
  - submit an application on request.
- Insurers may not pay an agent less because of the agent's loss experience or geographic location. [MCL 500.2116(2)]
- **Annual notice:** at least once a year, with a renewal notice or bill, the insurer must tell the policyholder that rating classification information and appeal rights are available on request. [MCL 500.2112(1)]
- People who give reasons for a cancellation, nonrenewal or declination are immune from civil liability unless they act with gross negligence or bad faith with malice. [MCL 500.2124]
- An insurer may suspend acceptance of **all** new home or auto applications by filing a notice with the commissioner. The commissioner may disapprove it after a hearing. [MCL 500.2126]

## Michigan Basic Property Insurance Association (FAIR plan) (MCL 500.2901–.2954)
- Every insurer writing property lines in Michigan must be a member of the pool, as a condition of its authority. [MCL 500.2920(1)]
- **Governance:** the board has 13 governors serving 1-year terms. [MCL 500.2921(1)]
  - The commissioner appoints 4: 2 agent representatives and 2 public representatives.
  - Participating members elect the other 9.
- "Basic property insurance" means: [MCL 500.2901(a)]
  - fire with extended coverage and vandalism and malicious mischief;
  - certain burglary and robbery coverages.
- The pool also writes **home insurance**. [MCL 500.2901(e)]
- **Qualified property** (basic property insurance): [MCL 500.2901(c)]
  - not used for farm purposes;
  - meets building codes;
  - not used for an illegal purpose;
  - other conditions in the statute.
- **Qualified applicant** (home insurance): excludes anyone who within 5 years was: [MCL 500.2901(g)]
  - convicted of arson or related crimes; or
  - successfully denied a pool claim of more than $2,000 for fraud.
- **Agents:** an agent licensed to write these lines for any participating member may write for the pool **without an additional license**. The pool sets the commissions. [MCL 500.2923(1)–(2)]
- Pool losses on an agent's policies may not be counted in that agent's loss ratio with an insurer. [MCL 500.2923(3)]
- **Home insurance underwriting program:** [MCL 500.2924(2)]
  - a replacement cost policy must cover at least **80% of replacement cost**, determined by inspection;
  - a repair cost policy must cover at least **100% of market value**, and may cover up to 120%.
- **Basic property applications:** [MCL 500.2925]
  - The property must have been inspected within the preceding **6 months**.
  - The applicant must have made a good-faith effort to buy coverage in the regular market.
  - There must be no unpaid prior premium.
  - Policies have a **1-year term**.
  - Binders may run up to **60 days**.

## Flags
- **Amounts that change over time:**
  - MPCGA claim cap: $7,980,000 and net worth limit: $39,920,000, both for 1/1/2026–12/31/2026 (Bulletin 2026-04-INS).
  - MPCGA maximum unearned premium refund: $2,063 for 7/1/2026–6/30/2027 (Bulletin 2026-13-INS).
  - Statutory base amounts are $5,000,000, $25,000,000 and $500. Exam items may use either the base amounts or a current figure.
- **TRIA:**
  - Under current law the program ends 12/31/2027.
  - H.R. 7128 (passed the House June 2026) and S. 4395 (passed the Senate by unanimous consent Sept 28, 2026, per trade press) would extend it to 2034. The House bill would also raise the $5M certification threshold to $10M starting in 2029.
  - As of 10/12/2026 neither bill had been confirmed as enacted. Recheck before the exam.
  - The PSI outline cites only P.L. 107-297, 109-144 and 110-160, so it is outdated.
- **500.2117(2)(e) nonrenewal claim thresholds:** $3,000 and $4,000 are statutory base figures, CPI-adjusted every 6th year (Jan. 1, 2006, 2012, 2018, 2024). Since Jan. 1, 2024 the amounts are **$5,000** excluding weather claims and **$6,600** including weather claims (DIFS Bulletin 2024-02-INS). The next adjustment is Jan. 1, 2030.
- **Fire-loss withholding residential caps:** MCL 500.2845 has a $12,000 base, CPI-adjusted each January 1. The cap is **$16,383** for 1/1/2026-12/31/2026 (DIFS Bulletin 2025-27-INS). MCL 500.2227 (larger municipalities) has had a $24,000 base since July 1, 2024 (2024 PA 82), CPI-adjusted each July 1. That cap is **$25,090** for 7/1/2026-6/30/2027 (DIFS Bulletin 2026-16-INS).
- **Essential Insurance Act purpose:** there is no express purpose section. The "availability for eligible persons" purpose is inferred from 2117 and 2118.
- **Fire cancellation notice conflict:** 500.2833(1)(i) allows a 10-day notice for fire policies generally. For home insurance, 500.2123 requires 30 days (20 days during the first 55 days), and chapter 21 controls under 500.2106(4).

## Characteristics and Purpose
- The dwelling forms cover a dwelling used principally for dwelling purposes, at the "Described Location." [ISO DP 00 03 12 02, Coverage A; DP 00 01 07 88, Coverage A] Under the ISO Dwelling Program eligibility rules, the forms may be used for a **1- to 4-family** residential building. [ISO Dwelling Policy Program Manual eligibility, per FC&S "ISO Dwelling Program"]
- Coverage applies only to coverages with a limit and perils with a premium shown in the Declarations. [DP 00 03 12 02, Coverages]
- The forms contain **no liability or medical payments coverage**. Those are added with the **Personal Liability Supplement (DL 24 01)**. [DL 24 01 12 02]
- The DP forms define only "you" (named insured and resident spouse) and have no HO-style "insured" definition. Coverage C still covers personal property of family members residing with you, on the Described Location. [DP 00 03 12 02, Definitions]
- **Perils by form:** [ISO DP 00 01 07 88; DP 00 02 07 88; DP 00 03 12 02]
  - **DP-1 Basic** covers **fire or lightning** and **internal explosion**. [DP 00 01 07 88, Perils 1A–1B]
  - Paying the **Extended Coverage** premium adds windstorm or hail, explosion (replacing internal explosion), riot or civil commotion, aircraft, vehicles, smoke, and volcanic eruption. [DP 00 01 07 88, Perils 2–8]
  - **Vandalism and malicious mischief** is a separate optional premium. [DP 00 01 07 88, Peril 9]
- **DP-2 Broad** covers 16 named perils for Coverages A, B and C. [DP 00 02 07 88, Perils 1–16]
  - The 16 perils: fire or lightning; windstorm or hail; explosion; riot or civil commotion; aircraft; vehicles; smoke; vandalism or malicious mischief; damage by burglars; falling objects; weight of ice, snow or sleet; accidental discharge of water or steam; sudden and accidental tearing apart, cracking, burning or bulging; freezing; artificially generated electrical current; volcanic eruption.
- **DP-3 Special** covers Coverages A and B against **risk of direct physical loss** (open perils) unless excluded. Coverage C is covered for the **named broad perils**. [DP 00 03 12 02, Perils Insured Against A–B]
- **Theft:** dwelling Coverage C perils include "**Damage by Burglars**" but **not theft**. V&MM excludes pilferage, theft, burglary and larceny. [DP 00 03 12 02, Coverage C Perils 8–9]
- Theft coverage is added by endorsement. [ISO Dwelling Manual Rule 510]

## Conditions
- **Loss settlement, DP-1:** **ACV**, but not more than the cost to repair or replace. [DP 00 01 07 88, Cond. 5]
- **Loss settlement, DP-2 and DP-3:** [DP 00 03 12 02, Cond. E]
  - Personal property, awnings, carpeting, appliances and non-building structures are paid at ACV.
  - **Buildings** are paid at **replacement cost** if insured to at least **80%** of replacement cost. Below 80%, the insurer pays the greater of ACV or the proportional amount.
  - Only ACV is paid until repairs are complete, unless the loss is under 5% of the insurance and under $2,500.
  - The insured may take ACV first and give notice within **180 days** to claim replacement cost.
- **Duties after loss:** proof of loss **within 60 days** after the insurer's request. [DP 00 03 12 02, Cond. D.6]
- **Appraisal:** 20 days to name appraisers, 15 days for the umpire. [DP 00 03 12 02, Cond. G]
- **Other insurance:** pro rata with other fire insurance. Coverage is excess over a service agreement. [DP 00 03 12 02, Cond. H]
- **Suit against us:** within **2 years** after the loss (12 02); within **1 year** (07 88). Michigan law requires at least 1 year with tolling. [DP 00 03 12 02, Cond. J; DP 00 01 07 88, Cond. 11; MCL 500.2833(1)(q)]
- **Loss payment:** 60 days after proof of loss and agreement, judgment or appraisal award under the unendorsed ISO form. Michigan statute requires payment within **30 days** after proof of the amount of loss. The **insurer's option** to repair or replace requires written notice within **30 days** after proof of loss. [DP 00 03 12 02, Cond. K–L; MCL 500.2833(1)(p)]
- **Mortgage clause:** the mortgagee gets **10 days'** notice of cancellation or nonrenewal and must file a sworn statement of loss within 60 days after notice. [DP 00 03 12 02, Cond. N]
- **Cancellation and nonrenewal:** under the unendorsed ISO form, the same as the HO forms: 10 days (nonpayment, or the first 60 days), 30 days otherwise, nonrenewal 30 days. In Michigan, for home insurance (for example, an owner-occupied dwelling), MCL 500.2123 controls. A termination (cancellation or nonrenewal for any reason other than nonpayment) needs at least **30 days'** written notice, or not less than **20 days** if the notice is mailed or delivered within the first **55 days** after the policy is first issued. Nonpayment cancellation takes effect as the policy provides. [DP 00 03 12 02, Cond. P–Q; MCL 500.2123, 500.2104(5)]
- **Liberalization:** a broadening applies if it is implemented within 60 days before or during the policy period. [DP 00 03 12 02, Cond. R]
- **No benefit to bailee.** [DP 00 03 12 02, Cond. O]
- **Volcanic eruptions within 72 hours** count as one eruption. [DP 00 03 12 02, Cond. X]

## Selected Endorsements

  - Covers theft and V&MM resulting from theft, **on and off premises**.

- **Personal Liability Supplement (DL 24 01):** [DL 24 01 12 02, Coverages L–M, Additional Coverages]
  - **Coverage L – Personal Liability** includes a defense even if the suit is groundless; the duty to defend ends when the limit is exhausted.

  - payment within 30 days after proof of the amount of loss, except as Michigan's fire-loss withholding law provides; [MCL 500.2833(1)(p); MCL 500.2845]
  - suit within 1 year or the policy's longer period, tolled from notice of loss until formal denial; [MCL 500.2833(1)(q)]
  - competent, independent appraisers; if they cannot agree on an umpire within 15 days, either party may ask a circuit judge to select one; [MCL 500.2833(1)(m)]

  - "Home insurance" includes fire insurance on an insured's dwelling of a type listed in the eligible-person definition: a house, condominium unit, cooperative unit, room or apartment of an owner-occupant or tenant, or an **owner-occupied** multiple-unit dwelling of not more than 4 residential units. [MCL 500.2103(2)–(3)]
  - Except as the next two points provide, a termination needs at least **30 days'** written notice stating the effective date and each specific reason. [MCL 500.2123(1)]
  - A notice mailed or delivered within the first **55 days** after the policy is first issued may take effect after not less than **20 days**. [MCL 500.2123(2)]
  - Cancellation for nonpayment takes effect as the policy provides. [MCL 500.2123(3)]
  - By contrast, the unendorsed DP-3 lets the insurer cancel a policy that has been in effect less than 60 days, and is not a renewal, for any reason on 10 days' notice. That is less notice than Michigan requires for home insurance. [ISO DP 00 03 07 14, Conditions P.2.b; MCL 500.2123(1)–(2)]
- **Innocent co-insured (fire loss):** the unendorsed DP-3 denies coverage to every insured for an intentional loss, even those who took no part in it. Under its Concealment or Fraud condition, one insured's fraud voids coverage for all insureds. [ISO DP 00 03 07 14, General Exclusions A.8; Conditions C]
  - Michigan courts hold that the fire policy law (MCL 500.2833(2), which keeps the coverage of the former standard fire policy) requires coverage for innocent co-insureds, despite any policy exclusion for intentional acts or fraud by an insured. [Wilson v. State Farm Fire & Cas. Co., No. 2:17-cv-11243 (E.D. Mich. Nov. 21, 2019), quoting Hicks v. Auto Club Grp. Ins. Co. (Mich. Ct. App. Jan. 24, 2012) and following Williams v. Auto Club Grp. Ins. Co., 224 Mich App 313 (1997)]
  - We could not verify how DP 01 21 itself words this point. [Flag: DP 01 21 text not seen]

  - **$1,500**: securities, accounts, deeds, evidences of debt, letters of credit, manuscripts, personal records, passports, tickets, stamps.
  - **$1,500**: watercraft, including trailers, furnishings, equipment and outboard motors.
  - **$1,500**: trailers not used with watercraft.
  - **$1,500**: **theft** of jewelry, watches, furs, and precious and semiprecious stones.
  - **$2,500**: **theft** of firearms and related equipment.
  - **$2,500**: **theft** of silverware, goldware, platinumware and pewterware.
  - **$2,500**: business property **on** the residence premises.
  - **$1,500**: business property **away from** the residence premises.
  - **$1,500**: portable electronic equipment in or on a motor vehicle.
  - **$250**: antennas, tapes, wires, records, disks and other media in or on a motor vehicle.
- **Property not covered:** [Coverage C.4]
  - articles **separately described and specifically insured**;
  - **animals, birds or fish**;
  - motor vehicles, except non-registered vehicles used to service a residence or to assist the handicapped;
  - aircraft (model or hobby aircraft are covered) and hovercraft;
  - property of roomers and boarders who are not related to an insured;
  - property in an apartment rented to others (except Landlord's Furnishings) or rented to others off premises;
  - business data;
  - credit cards (except under the additional coverage);
  - water or steam.
- **Coverage D – Loss of Use:** one total limit for three parts. [Coverage D]
  - **Additional living expense:** for the shortest time to repair, or to settle elsewhere.
  - **Fair rental value** of the rented part.
  - **Civil authority:** up to **2 weeks**.
  - The time periods are **not limited by policy expiration**. Lease cancellation is not covered.

### Section I Additional Coverages (HO 00 03 05 11, E.1–12)
- **Debris removal** is included in the limit for the damaged property. If damage plus debris removal exceeds the limit, an **additional 5%** of that limit is available. [E.1.a]
- **Tree removal:** up to **$1,000** per loss, **$500 per tree**, for trees felled by windstorm or hail or by weight of ice, snow or sleet. A neighbor's tree felled by a Coverage C peril also qualifies. [E.1.b]
  - The tree must damage a covered structure, or block a driveway or a handicap ramp.
- **Reasonable repairs** to protect covered property from further damage. [E.2]
- **Trees, shrubs and plants:** up to **5% of Coverage A**, **$500 per tree**. [E.3]
  - Covered perils: fire or lightning, explosion, riot, aircraft, non-resident vehicles, V&MM, **theft**.
  - **Not windstorm.**
- **Fire department service charge:** **$500**, no deductible, unless the property is within the limits of the jurisdiction providing the fire department. [E.4]
- **Property removed:** covered against any direct loss for up to **30 days**. [E.5]
- **Credit card, EFT card, forgery and counterfeit money:** **$500**, **no deductible**. [E.6]
  - Not covered: use by a household resident, by a person entrusted with the card, or in a business.
- **Loss assessment:** **$1,000**. [E.7]
- **Collapse:** abrupt collapse only, caused by: [E.8]
  - Coverage C perils;
  - hidden decay;
  - hidden insect damage;
  - weight of contents or people;
  - weight of rain on a roof;
  - defective materials or methods during construction.
- **Glass or safety glazing material:** no coverage if the dwelling was **vacant more than 60 consecutive days**, except breakage caused by earth movement. [E.9]
- **Landlord's furnishings:** **$2,500** per rented apartment, Coverage C perils **other than theft**. [E.10]
- **Ordinance or law:** up to **10% of Coverage A**, additional insurance. [E.11]
- **Grave markers:** **$5,000**. [E.12]

## Section II – Liability Coverages (HO 00 03 05 11)
- **Coverage E – Personal Liability:** [Sec. II A]
  - pays damages, including prejudgment interest;
  - provides a defense **even if the suit is groundless, false or fraudulent**;
  - the duty to settle or defend ends when the limit is exhausted.
- **Coverage F – Medical Payments to Others:** pays reasonable medical expenses incurred or medically ascertained within **3 years** of the accident. [Sec. II B]
  - It **does not apply to you or regular residents of your household, except residence employees**.
  - It covers people on the insured location with permission.
  - Off premises, it covers injury from a condition on the premises, an insured's activities, a residence employee, or an insured's animal.
- **Basic and minimum limits (manual):** Coverage E **$100,000**; Coverage F **$1,000** per person. [ISO HO Policy Program Manual Table 301 as reproduced in RIJRA HO Manual eff. 11/1/2021]
- Coverage E is one limit per occurrence regardless of the number of insureds, claims or persons injured. Coverage F is a limit per person per accident. [Sec. II Cond. A]
- **Severability of insurance:** the coverage applies separately to each insured, but this does not increase the limit. [Sec. II Cond. B]
- **Section II additional coverages** (paid in addition to the limits): [Sec. II Add'l Cov. A–D]
  - **Claim expenses**: defense costs, bond premiums, the insured's **loss of earnings up to $250/day**, post-judgment interest.
  - **First aid** to others.
  - **Damage to property of others**: up to **$1,000** per occurrence at **replacement cost**. Not covered: damage caused intentionally by an insured **13 or older**, or property owned by an insured.
  - **Loss assessment**: **$1,000**.
- **Coverage E and F exclusions:** [Sec. II Excl. A–E]
  - motor vehicle liability (with exceptions such as dead storage, service vehicles and some golf carts);
  - watercraft liability (see below);
  - **aircraft** and **hovercraft**;
  - expected or intended injury (reasonable force to protect persons or property is excepted);
  - business, with exceptions: occasional rental, part rental, an office, school or studio, and a part-time business of an insured **under 21** with no employees;
  - professional services;
  - premises an insured owns or rents that are not an insured location;
  - war;
  - **communicable disease**;
  - sexual molestation and abuse;
  - controlled substances.
- **Watercraft:** Coverages E and F apply to: [Sec. II Excl. B.2]
  - stored watercraft;
  - sailing vessels **under 26 feet**, or 26 feet and longer if not owned by or rented to an insured;
  - inboard or inboard-outdrive boats of **50 hp or less not owned** by an insured, or more than 50 hp not owned by or rented to an insured;
  - **outboard motors totaling 25 hp or less**;
  - outboards over 25 hp if not owned by an insured, or acquired during the policy period, or owned before the period but declared at inception or reported **within 45 days**.
- **Coverage E exclusions** (Excl. F; Coverage F separately excludes workers' compensation-eligible injuries and nuclear under Excl. G): [Sec. II Excl. F]
  - contractual liability, except written contracts relating to the insured location or assumed before the occurrence;
  - **property damage to property owned by an insured**;
  - PD to property rented to or in an insured's care, **except fire, smoke or explosion**;
  - injury to persons eligible for **workers' compensation** benefits;
  - nuclear;
  - bodily injury to you or another insured.

## Perils Insured Against and Exclusions (HO 00 03 05 11)
- **Coverages A and B are not covered for:** [Sec. I Perils A.2]
  - collapse (except under the additional coverage);
  - freezing, unless the insured used reasonable care to **maintain heat or shut off the water and drain** the system;
  - freezing, thawing or ice damage to fences, pools, foundations and similar items;
  - theft in or to a dwelling **under construction**;
  - **V&MM if the dwelling was vacant more than 60 consecutive days** (a dwelling under construction is not vacant);
  - mold, fungus or wet rot, unless hidden and caused by accidental discharge;
  - **wear and tear**, mechanical breakdown, latent defect, inherent vice;
  - rust, smog, dry rot;
  - pollutants;
  - settling;
  - birds, rodents and insects;
  - animals owned by an insured.
  - Accidental discharge from plumbing or appliances is excepted (covered), including tear-out.
- **Peril notes:** [Sec. I Perils B.2, B.9, B.10]
  - **Windstorm or hail:** interior rain damage is covered only if wind or hail first opens the roof or a wall. Watercraft are covered only inside a fully enclosed building.
  - **Theft:** not covered when committed by an insured, or from a dwelling under construction.
  - **Theft off premises:** trailers, campers and watercraft are not covered off premises. Property at another residence of an insured is not covered unless the insured is temporarily living there; a student's property is covered if the student was there within the past **90 days**.
  - **Falling objects:** property inside is covered only if the roof or an outside wall is damaged first.
- **Section I exclusions** apply regardless of any concurrent or sequential cause (anti-concurrent causation): [Sec. I Excl. A.1–9]
  - **ordinance or law** (except the additional coverage);
  - **earth movement** (ensuing fire, explosion or theft is covered);
  - **water**: flood, surface water, storm surge, **sewer or drain backup**, sump overflow, subsurface water (ensuing fire, explosion or theft is covered);
  - **power failure off premises**;
  - **neglect**;
  - **war** (a nuclear weapon discharge counts as war even if accidental);
  - **nuclear hazard**;
  - **intentional loss**, which bars coverage for all insureds, even innocent ones;
  - **governmental action** (destruction ordered to stop the spread of a fire is excepted).
- Coverages A and B also exclude the following, but any ensuing loss is covered: weather conditions (excluded only when they combine with a cause excluded under A); acts or decisions of any person, group or governmental body; faulty planning, design, workmanship, materials or maintenance. [Sec. I Excl. B]

## Conditions (HO 00 03 05 11)
- **Deductible:** if two or more deductibles apply, only the highest applies. [Sec. I Cond. B]
- **Duties after loss:** [Sec. I Cond. C]
  - prompt notice;
  - notify the police of theft;
  - notify the card company of a credit card loss;
  - protect the property;
  - prepare an inventory;
  - submit to an **examination under oath** (outside the presence of other insureds);
  - send a sworn proof of loss **within 60 days after the insurer's request**.
- **Loss settlement:** [Sec. I Cond. D.1–2]
  - Personal property, awnings, carpeting, appliances, outdoor antennas and equipment, non-building structures and grave markers are paid at **ACV**.
  - **Buildings under A and B** are paid at **replacement cost** with no depreciation, if insured to **at least 80% of full replacement cost**. The insurer pays the least of: the limit; the cost to replace with like kind and quality; or the amount actually spent.
- **Under 80%:** the insurer pays the **greater** of ACV, or (insurance carried ÷ 80% of replacement cost) × replacement cost of the loss, up to the limit. [Sec. I Cond. D.2.b]
- In computing the 80% figure, these are left out: excavations, foundations below the lowest basement floor, and underground flues, pipes, wiring and drains. [Sec. I Cond. D.2.c]
- Only **ACV** is paid until repair is complete, unless the loss is **under 5% of the insurance AND under $2,500**. [Sec. I Cond. D.2.d]
- The insured may take ACV first and then claim replacement cost by giving notice of intent to repair **within 180 days** after the loss. [Sec. I Cond. D.2.e]
- **Pair or set:** the insurer may repair or replace part of the set, or pay the difference in ACV before and after the loss. [Sec. I Cond. E]
- **Appraisal:** appraisers named **within 20 days**; umpire chosen within **15 days** or by a judge; each side pays its own appraiser and splits the umpire's cost. [Sec. I Cond. F]
- **Other insurance:** pro rata by limits. Coverage is **excess** over a service agreement or home warranty. [Sec. I Cond. G]
- **Suit against us (Section I):** within **2 years** after the loss under the unendorsed ISO form. Michigan law requires at least 1 year, tolled from notice until denial. [Sec. I Cond. H; MCL 500.2833(1)(q)]
- **Our option:** the insurer may repair or replace by giving written notice **within 30 days** after the sworn proof of loss. [Sec. I Cond. I]
- **Loss payment:** **60 days** after proof of loss plus agreement, judgment or appraisal award. Michigan statute requires payment within 30 days after proof of the amount of loss. [Sec. I Cond. J; MCL 500.2833(1)(p)]
- **Abandonment:** the insurer need not accept abandoned property. [Sec. I Cond. K]
- **Mortgage clause:** [Sec. I Cond. L]
  - Losses are paid to the mortgagee and you as interests appear.
  - The mortgagee's claim survives a denial of yours if the mortgagee reports changes, pays premium on demand, and files a sworn statement **within 60 days** after notice.
  - The mortgagee gets **10 days'** notice of cancellation or nonrenewal.
- **No benefit to bailee** (no coverage for anyone holding, storing or moving property for a fee). [Sec. I Cond. M]
- Other Section I conditions: [Sec. I Cond. O–Q, S]
  - **Recovered property:** at the insured's option it is returned, with the loss payment adjusted.
  - **Volcanic eruptions within 72 hours** count as one.
  - **Policy period:** loss must occur during the policy period.
  - **Loss payable clause** for scheduled personal property.
- **Concealment or fraud** voids coverage for **all** insureds under Section I, but only for the insured involved under Section II. [Sec. I Cond. R; Sec. II Cond. J]
- **Section II:** [Sec. II Cond. F–H]
  - no one may join the insurer in a suit against an insured;
  - bankruptcy of an insured does not relieve the insurer;
  - Section II coverage is **excess** over other valid and collectible insurance.
- **Sections I and II conditions:** [Secs. I & II Cond. A–G]
  - **Liberalization:** a no-cost broadening applies automatically if implemented **within 60 days before or during** the policy period. It does not apply to general program revisions.
  - **Waiver** must be in writing.
  - **Cancellation:**
    - 10 days for nonpayment;
    - 10 days for any reason if in force under 60 days and not a renewal (unendorsed ISO form; in Michigan, MCL 500.2123 requires at least 30 days' notice for a home insurance termination other than for nonpayment, or not less than 20 days after mailing or delivery if the notice is mailed or delivered within the first 55 days after the policy is first issued);
    - 30 days for misrepresentation or substantial change in risk after 60 days;
    - 30 days at the anniversary of a multi-year policy;
    - refunds are **pro rata**.
  - **Nonrenewal:** 30 days.
  - **Assignment** requires the insurer's written consent.
  - **Subrogation:** an insured may waive recovery rights in writing **before** a loss. Subrogation does not apply to Coverage F or damage to property of others.
  - **Death:** the legal representative is covered.

### Selected Endorsements > Special Provisions - Michigan
- Michigan's amendatory endorsement is **HO 01 21 Special Provisions – Michigan**. [Wolters Kluwer, Homeowners Policy Forms and Endorsements index (Oct. 2026)]
  - The current edition is **05 23**. [Wolters Kluwer, Homeowners Policy Forms and Endorsements index (Oct. 2026)]
  - The edition for the 2011 program was **HO 01 21 05 11**. [FC&S, "Special Provisions—Variations by State" (Sept. 1, 2011)]
  - The base forms listed for Michigan are the **03 22** editions (2022 program) of HO-2, HO-3, HO-4, HO-5 and HO-6. The index does not list Michigan for the 03 22 HO-8. [Wolters Kluwer, Homeowners Policy Forms and Endorsements index (Oct. 2026)]
- **History:** ISO revised HO 01 21 for policies written on or after **April 1, 1999**, in response to *Williams v. Auto Club Group Ins. Co.* (Mich. App. 1997). The revision amended the Intentional Loss exclusion and the Concealment or Fraud condition. They now cover innocent insureds who did not take part in, or direct, the intentional act that caused a covered **fire** loss. [Rough Notes, "ISO Form and Endorsement Changes" (March 1999)]
- **Innocent co-insured law:** Michigan courts hold that the fire policy law (MCL 500.2833(2)) requires coverage for innocent co-insureds, despite any policy exclusion for intentional acts or fraud by an insured. [Wilson v. State Farm Fire & Cas. Co., No. 2:17-cv-11243 (E.D. Mich. Nov. 21, 2019), quoting Hicks v. Auto Club Grp. Ins. Co. (Mich. Ct. App. 2012) and following Williams, 224 Mich App 313 (1997)]
  - Without the endorsement, the ISO HO-3 denies coverage to every insured for an intentional loss, including insureds who took no part in it. [ISO HO 00 03 05 11, Sec. I Excl. A.8]
  - In Michigan, that rule does not hold for an innocent insured's **fire** loss. [FC&S (Sept. 1, 2011), Michigan HO 01 21 05 11; Wilson (E.D. Mich. 2019)]
  - A federal court quoted one policy's Special Provisions – Michigan: the intentional loss exclusion does not apply, for a fire loss, to an insured who did not commit or conspire to commit the act, and that insured is covered "only to the extent of that insured's legal interest, but not exceeding the applicable limit of liability." The opinion does not give the form number or edition. [Brunk v. Amica Mut. Ins. Co., No. 06-15160 (E.D. Mich. Nov. 2, 2007)]
- **What HO 01 21 05 11 changes:** the bullets below come from FC&S's summary. We have not seen the endorsement's text, and the current 05 23 edition may differ. [FC&S, "Special Provisions—Variations by State" (Sept. 1, 2011), Michigan]
  - **Innocent insured, fire loss:** the Intentional Loss exclusion and the Concealment or Fraud condition do not apply to an insured who is innocent of a fire loss. That insured is covered to the extent of his or her **insurable interest**. [FC&S (Sept. 1, 2011)]
  - Unlike some states' versions, Michigan's does **not** require the innocent insured to be a victim of domestic abuse. [FC&S (Sept. 1, 2011)]
  - The insured who set the fire, or who concealed facts or committed fraud, is not covered, and the insurer may subrogate against the insured who caused the fire. [FC&S (Sept. 1, 2011)]
  - For a loss by any **other peril**, fraud or material misrepresentation by any insured still bars coverage for all insureds. [FC&S (Sept. 1, 2011)]
  - **Suit against us:** suit may be brought within **2 years** after the date of loss. The time is **tolled** from the insured's notice of loss until the insurer formally denies liability. [FC&S (Sept. 1, 2011)]
    - The 2-year period is also in the unendorsed 2011 forms' Suit Against Us condition. Michigan law requires at least 1 year, or the policy's period if longer, with the same tolling. [ISO HO 00 03 05 11, Sec. I Cond. H; MCL 500.2833(1)(q)]
  - **Appraisal:** each party must choose a "competent and independent appraiser." [FC&S (Sept. 1, 2011); MCL 500.2833(1)(m)]
  - **Spouse who moves out** during the policy period: the spouse remains "you" until the end of the policy period or the effective date of another policy naming the spouse as a named insured, whichever comes first. [FC&S (Sept. 1, 2011)]
  - **Inception:** if other coverage ends at noon standard time on the inception date, this policy's coverage does not begin until that coverage ends. [FC&S (Sept. 1, 2011)]
  - **Loss payment** is due within 30 days after a settlement agreement. Where a municipality applies Michigan's loss-withholding law, part of the payment is withheld; the mortgage clause is amended to match. [FC&S (Sept. 1, 2011); see MCL 500.2845 and 500.2227 in the mi section]
  - **Section II damages:** prejudgment interest is no longer part of damages. It is paid instead as a claim expense under the Section II additional coverages. [FC&S (Sept. 1, 2011)]
  - **Controlled substances:** the exclusion does not apply to the legitimate use of prescription drugs as a licensed health care professional ordered. It also does not apply to an insured who did not know the substance was involved; the insurer must prove that knowledge by competent evidence. [FC&S (Sept. 1, 2011)]
  - **Section II duties:** someone acting on the insured's behalf may perform them. For damage to property of others, the 60-day statement of loss is not required if it is not reasonably possible. [FC&S (Sept. 1, 2011)]
  - **Coverage E suits:** no action may be brought until the insured's obligation is fixed by a final judgment or by an agreement the insurer signs. [FC&S (Sept. 1, 2011)]
  - **Section II other insurance:** the insurer pays its pro rata share with other liability insurance. Coverage is excess for a vehicle or watercraft covered under the policy. [FC&S (Sept. 1, 2011)]
  - **Cancellation during the first 55 days:** at least **30 days'** notice, except for nonpayment. The statute allows as little as 20 days. [FC&S (Sept. 1, 2011); MCL 500.2123(2)]
  - **Cancellation after 55 days:** only for the reasons the endorsement lists, on 30 days' notice, except one listed reason (FC&S's "reason 1") that needs only 10 days. FC&S does not name the reasons. [FC&S (Sept. 1, 2011)]
  - **Premium and nonrenewal:** the minimum earned premium is the pro rata premium or $25, whichever is greater. A nonrenewal notice must be sent at least **30 days** before the policy ends. [FC&S (Sept. 1, 2011); MCL 500.2833(1)(h)]

## Flags
- **HO-2, HO-5 and HO-8:** verified only from the **10 00** edition texts.
  - The 05 11 editions may differ in some sub-limits. For example, HO-8 10 00 shows business property away from the premises at $500, while HO-3 05 11 shows $1,500.
  - Core features (perils, the HO-8 $1,000 on-premises theft limit, the 180-day repair rule) are consistent with PF&M (2014) for the 2011 program.
- **Coverage D percentages:** the manuals conflict.
  - The ISO 2000 program example (RIJRA) shows HO-3 Coverage D at **30% of A**.
  - The Universal P&C (MA/HI) and NC Rate Bureau manuals show **20% of A** for HO-3, **20% of C** for HO-4, and **40% of C** for HO-6.
  - Study guides commonly say HO-4 is 30% of C and HO-6 is 50% of C (2000+ program), but we could not verify that from an ISO manual.
  - Test-writers may use either. We did not write any question that depends on it.
- **Earthquake HO 04 54:** the $250 minimum deductible is from the 10 00 edition. Later editions may use a different minimum.
- **HO 04 90:** a 03 22 edition reportedly raised the $500 ACV-until-repaired threshold to $1,000 (secondary source). The 05 11 edition says $500.
- **ISO's current homeowners forms are the 03 22 editions (2022 program).** This guide uses the 05 11 (2011 program) amounts, so every HO dollar figure should be labeled "05 11 edition".
- **Special Provisions – Michigan (HO 01 21 05 11):** form text NOT FOUND or verified. A study deck suggests it covers suit limitation with tolling, duties after loss and spousal definition changes. Only the Michigan statutory requirements are stated as fact here.
