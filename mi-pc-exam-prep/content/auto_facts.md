## Laws

### Michigan Motor Vehicle Financial Responsibility / required limits
- Residual liability policy limits for policies issued after July 1, 2020: at least $250,000 BI per person / $500,000 BI per accident; $10,000 property damage. Before July 2, 2020 it was $20,000 / $40,000 / $10,000. [MCL 500.3009(1)]
- Applicant or named insured may choose lower BI limits, but not below $50,000 per person / $100,000 per accident, only by completing the DIFS form ("Michigan Choice of Bodily Injury Liability Coverage Limits"). If no effective choice is made, the $250,000/$500,000 limits apply. [MCL 500.3009(5),(8); DIFS FIS-PUB 6310]
- On every new policy and renewal, the insurer must give the liability options, a price for each option, and the form. The form must state the risks of lower limits, and the person must acknowledge it and sign it. [MCL 500.3009(6),(7)]
- The $10,000 PD liability matters mainly for damage your vehicle does to property in another state, because in Michigan, PPI pays for property damage and PD tort liability is largely abolished. [DIFS auto FAQ "info-purchasing-auto-insurance"; MCL 500.3135(3)]
- Named excluded driver: if the insured authorizes it, liability coverage can be excluded while a named person operates the vehicle. The face/declarations page and the certificate must carry this warning: "when a named excluded person operates a vehicle all liability coverage is void—no one is insured." [MCL 500.3009(2)]
- A named excluded operator who is driving is not entitled to PIP. [MCL 500.3113(d)]
- Financial-responsibility (SR-type certified) "motor vehicle liability policy" under the Vehicle Code still recites $20,000 / $40,000 / $10,000, with US and Canada territory. [MCL 257.520(b)(2)]
- Transportation network company (TNC, e.g., ride-share) drivers and companies: [MCL 257.518b]
  - While logged on and available but not on a prearranged ride: liability of at least $50,000 / $100,000 BI and $25,000 PD, plus PIP and PPI. [MCL 257.518b]
  - While engaged in a prearranged ride: at least $1,000,000 combined single limit, plus PIP and PPI. [MCL 257.518b]
- Personal auto insurers may exclude all coverage while a TNC driver is logged on or providing a ride [MCL 500.3017]. They may also exclude all coverage during a peer-to-peer car-sharing period [MCL 500.3018, added 2024 PA 224, eff. Oct. 17, 2025].

### Proof of insurance (MCL 257.328)
- The owner or operator must produce evidence of insurance when a police officer asks. Failing to produce it, or having no insurance, is a civil infraction. [MCL 257.328(1)]
- An electronic copy on a phone is acceptable. The officer may view only the certificate, may not manipulate the device, and showing it is not consent to a search. [MCL 257.328(1)]
- A certificate of insurance (paper or electronic) is prima facie evidence that insurance is in force until the expiration date shown. It must name any excluded operator. [MCL 257.328(2)]
- If proof of valid insurance is given to the court before the appearance date, there is no fine or costs and no abstract goes to the Secretary of State. The court may charge a fee of up to $25. [MCL 257.328(3)]
- If found responsible, the court may require the license to be surrendered. The suspension lasts 30 days or until proof of insurance is submitted, whichever is later, with a $25 SOS fee. No points are assessed. [MCL 257.328(4),(7)]
- Knowingly producing false evidence of insurance is a misdemeanor: up to 1 year in jail and/or a fine of up to $1,000. [MCL 257.328(6)]
- Insurers give 1 certificate of insurance per insured vehicle and report policy/VIN data to the Secretary of State. [MCL 500.3101a(1)]

### Who must carry no-fault security
- The owner or registrant of a motor vehicle required to be registered in Michigan must maintain PIP, PPI and residual liability. Security is required only while the vehicle is driven or moved on a highway. [MCL 500.3101(1)]
- Every auto policy sold in Michigan must include PIP, PPI and residual liability (except the 3107d PIP-medical opt-out). An insurer may delete coverages and keep comprehensive on a vehicle that is not driven on a highway (storage). [MCL 500.3101(2)]
- "Owner" includes a person renting or using a motor vehicle for more than 30 days, the title holder (but not a lessor under a lease longer than 30 days), and an installment buyer. [MCL 500.3101(3)(l)]
- "Motor vehicle" means a vehicle operated or designed for operation on a public highway by power other than muscular power, with more than 2 wheels. It does NOT include motorcycles, mopeds, ORVs, golf carts, farm tractors, power-driven mobility devices, commercial quadricycles or e-bikes. [MCL 500.3101(3)(i)]
- Nonresident owners may not operate in Michigan for more than an aggregate of 30 days in any calendar year without no-fault security. [MCL 500.3102(1)]
- Operating, or knowingly permitting operation, without required security is a misdemeanor: fine of $200–$500, up to 1 year in jail, or both. [MCL 500.3102(2)]
- Motorcycle owners must carry residual liability that meets 3009(1). Insurers must also offer first-party medical benefits in $5,000 increments. [MCL 500.3103]

### Michigan Automobile Insurance Placement Facility (MAIPF)
- Every insurer authorized to write auto insurance in Michigan must participate. Its purposes are to guarantee coverage for anyone unable to get it through ordinary methods and to preserve price competition by encouraging maximum use of the normal private market. [MCL 500.3301]
- Board of governors: 11 governors. 7 are elected; 4 are appointed by the commissioner (2 represent agents, 2 the general public). [MCL 500.3310(1)]
- A qualified applicant is an owner or registrant of a Michigan-registered auto or a licensed driver. The term excludes most persons with a suspended or revoked license (listed Vehicle Code suspensions excepted). It also excludes anyone cancelled for nonpayment in the prior 2 years, unless the installment is paid in full. [MCL 500.3303(b)]
- Duties of the facility: [MCL 500.3320(1)]
  - Equitably distributes applicants to designated participating members (servicing carriers). [MCL 500.3320(1)]
  - May issue policies to some drivers with suspended licenses; those policies may be cancelled after not less than 30 days if proof of reinstatement is not produced. [MCL 500.3320(1)]
  - Administers the assigned claims plan. [MCL 500.3320(1)]
- Deferred premium payment plan: advance payment of at least 25% of the total premium or $100, whichever is greater. [MCL 500.3350(a)]
- An insurer may not count facility-placed premiums and losses in an agent's loss ratio, or use that experience to alter the agent relationship. [MCL 500.3360]
- Notice of eligibility: at least once each year, each designated participating member must notify facility insureds, on a commissioner-approved form, of the statutory eligibility standards for buying insurance in the voluntary market. [MCL 500.3365(3)]
- Applicants may request assignment, reject an assignment, or request reassignment to a designated member. Reassignment must be offered on the policy and at renewal. [MCL 500.3365(2)]

### No-Fault: Personal Injury Protection (PIP) benefits
- PIP pays, without regard to fault, for accidental bodily injury arising out of the ownership, operation, maintenance or use of a motor vehicle as a motor vehicle. Bodily injury includes death and damage to prosthetic devices in connection with the injury. [MCL 500.3105]
- Allowable expenses are reasonable charges for reasonably necessary products, services and accommodations for care, recovery or rehabilitation. [MCL 500.3107(1)(a)]
  - Hospital room charges above semiprivate are not covered unless special or intensive care is required. [MCL 500.3107(1)(a)]
  - Funeral and burial is limited to the amount set in the policy, which must be at least $1,750 and no more than $5,000. [MCL 500.3107(1)(a)]
- Work loss: [MCL 500.3107(1)(b)]
  - Pays loss of income from work the injured person would have performed during the first 3 years after the accident. Nothing is payable after death. [MCL 500.3107(1)(b)]
  - Benefits are reduced 15% for tax advantage (the "85% rule") unless the claimant proves a lower tax advantage. [MCL 500.3107(1)(b)]
  - The monthly maximum (per 30-day period) is adjusted each October 1 and applies by accident date. [MCL 500.3107(1)(b)]
- Work loss and survivor's loss maximum per 30-day period: [DIFS Bulletin 2026-20-INS; MCL 500.3107(1)(b), 500.3108]
  - $7,455 for accidents Oct. 1, 2026 – Sept. 30, 2027. [DIFS Bulletin 2026-20-INS; MCL 500.3107(1)(b), 500.3108]
  - $7,201 for accidents Oct. 1, 2025 – Sept. 30, 2026. [DIFS Bulletin 2026-20-INS; MCL 500.3107(1)(b), 500.3108]
  - $7,014 for accidents Oct. 1, 2024 – Sept. 30, 2025. [DIFS Bulletin 2026-20-INS; MCL 500.3107(1)(b), 500.3108]
- A temporarily unemployed person's work loss is based on earned income for the last month employed full time before the accident. [MCL 500.3107a]
- Replacement services: up to $20 per day for ordinary and necessary services (housework, yard work, etc.) the injured person would have done, for the first 3 years after the accident. [MCL 500.3107(1)(c)]
- A person aged 60 or older who would not be eligible for work loss benefits may waive work-loss coverage in writing at a reduced premium. The waiver applies only to those who sign it. [MCL 500.3107(2)(a)]
- PIP need not cover medical marihuana. [MCL 500.3107(2)(b)]
- Survivor's loss: paid to dependents after death [MCL 500.3108]
  - Covers lost contributions of tangible things of economic value (not services), plus up to $20/day in replacement-services expenses. [MCL 500.3108]
  - Subject to the same adjusted 30-day maximum, which is the aggregate for all survivors. [MCL 500.3108]
  - Not payable beyond the first 3 years after the accident. [MCL 500.3108]
- Conclusively presumed dependents: a spouse living with the deceased, and a child under 18 (or incapacitated) living with or regularly supported by the parent. A surviving spouse's dependency ends on death or remarriage. [MCL 500.3110(1),(3)]
- PIP benefits accrue as the expense or loss is incurred, not when the injury occurs. [MCL 500.3110(4)]
- Government-mandated benefits (federal or state law) are subtracted from PIP. Insurers may offer deductibles and coordination with other health and accident coverage at reduced premiums. These apply only to the named insured, spouse and resident relatives. [MCL 500.3109(1),(3); 500.3109a(1)]
- Not entitled to PIP: [MCL 500.3113]
  - A willing user of an unlawfully taken vehicle who knew or should have known it was taken. [MCL 500.3113]
  - An owner or registrant of an uninsured involved vehicle. [MCL 500.3113]
  - A nonresident, unless they own a Michigan-registered and insured vehicle. [MCL 500.3113]
  - A named excluded operator. [MCL 500.3113]
  - A person excluded under the TNC or car-sharing exclusions. [MCL 500.3113]
- Out-of-state accidents: PIP is payable for accidents in the US, its territories and possessions, or Canada if the injured person is a named insured, spouse or resident relative (or a qualifying occupant). [MCL 500.3111]
- PIP is payable as loss accrues and is overdue if not paid within 30 days after reasonable proof is received. Overdue benefits bear 12% simple interest per year. [MCL 500.3142]
- Statute of limitations: 1 year after the accident unless written notice is given or benefits were paid within 1 year. After that, suit must be brought within 1 year after the most recent loss is incurred, and recovery reaches back only 1 year. [MCL 500.3145(1),(2)]
- PPI actions must be brought within 1 year after the accident. [MCL 500.3145(5)]
- Parked vehicles: injury involves a parked vehicle only if: [MCL 500.3106]
  - it was parked so as to cause unreasonable risk; [MCL 500.3106]
  - the injury came from contact with permanently mounted equipment in use, or from loading/unloading; or [MCL 500.3106]
  - the person was occupying, entering or alighting from the vehicle. [MCL 500.3106]

### PIP coverage-level choices (policies issued or renewed after July 1, 2020)
- The named insured selects one PIP allowable-expense (medical) level on a DIFS-approved form, per individual per loss occurrence: [MCL 500.3107c(1)]
  - $50,000: only if the named insured is enrolled in Medicaid AND the spouse and resident relatives have qualified health coverage (QHC), Medicaid, or PIP from another auto policy. [MCL 500.3107c(1)]
  - $250,000 [MCL 500.3107c(1)]
  - $500,000 [MCL 500.3107c(1)]
  - No limit (unlimited) [MCL 500.3107c(1)]
- $250,000 with exclusions: an insurer must offer anyone choosing the $250,000 level an exclusion for persons with QHC (non-Medicare). [MCL 500.3109a(2)]
  - If the named insured, spouse and all resident relatives have QHC that covers auto injuries, the PIP medical premium is reduced 100%. [MCL 500.3109a(2)]
  - Excluded persons are not eligible for PIP medical under the policy. [MCL 500.3109a(2)]
- Opt-out: the named insured may elect no PIP medical coverage only if: [MCL 500.3107d(1),(7)]
  - the named insured is a "qualified person" (enrolled in Medicare Parts A and B); and [MCL 500.3107d(1),(7)]
  - the spouse and resident relatives have QHC or PIP from another policy. [MCL 500.3107d(1),(7)]
  - A document from the QHC provider listing covered persons is required. [MCL 500.3107d(2)]
- Qualified health coverage (QHC) is either: [MCL 500.3107d(7)(b)]
  - health or accident coverage that does not exclude or limit auto-accident injuries, with an annual deductible at or under the adjusted cap ($6,000 originally); or [MCL 500.3107d(7)(b)]
  - Medicare Parts A and B. [MCL 500.3107d(7)(b)]
- QHC deductible cap: $6,579 for July 1, 2026 – June 30, 2027 (unchanged because the $500 threshold was not met). [DIFS Bulletin 2026-08-INS]
- If QHC ends during the policy term: [MCL 500.3107d(6); 500.3109a(2)(d),(e)]
  - The named insured has 30 days to obtain PIP medical coverage. [MCL 500.3107d(6); 500.3109a(2)(d),(e)]
  - Someone injured during the 30 days may claim through the assigned claims plan. [MCL 500.3107d(6); 500.3109a(2)(d),(e)]
  - After 30 days without coverage, the person gets no PIP medical (unless another policy applies). [MCL 500.3107d(6); 500.3109a(2)(d),(e)]
  - Insurers may not penalize an eligible person for failing to obtain coverage in time. [MCL 500.3107d(6); 500.3109a(2)(d),(e)]
- Default rules: [MCL 500.3107c(3),(4); 500.3107d(4)]
  - With no effective selection but a premium paid, the premium paid is a rebuttable presumption of the level chosen. [MCL 500.3107c(3),(4); 500.3107d(4)]
  - With no selection and no presumption, unlimited PIP applies. [MCL 500.3107c(3),(4); 500.3107d(4)]
  - A failed opt-out also defaults to unlimited. [MCL 500.3107c(3),(4); 500.3107d(4)]
- The selected level applies to the named insured, spouse, resident relatives, and anyone else claiming PIP under the policy. [MCL 500.3107c(5)]
- If two or more policies pay PIP medical, the aggregate is capped at the highest single-policy limit (no stacking). [MCL 500.3107c(6)]
- TNC companies may choose only $250,000, $500,000 or unlimited. [MCL 500.3107c(7)]
- Insurers must offer, for $50,000/$250,000/$500,000 policies, a rider covering attendant care in excess of the limit. [MCL 500.3107c(8)]
- Forms under 3009, 3107c and 3107d (delivery and how a selection is made): [MCL 500.3107e]
  - Delivery: personal delivery, first-class mail, or electronic means. [MCL 500.3107e]
  - A selection may be made by marking and signing a paper form, by recorded verbal instructions (in person or by phone), or electronically. [MCL 500.3107e]
  - If a verbal selection is disputed, it is presumed NOT effective, and the insurer must rebut that with the recording. [MCL 500.3107e]
- Tort recovery: a person may sue in tort for allowable expenses above the selected PIP limit, or without limit if they opted out or were excluded. [MCL 500.3135(3)(c)]

### Chapter 31A: Managed Care (the "subject to chapter 31A" in MCL 500.3107)
- Chapter 31A (MCL 500.3181–.3189), added by 2019 PA 21, lets an auto insurer offer an optional "managed care option" for PIP allowable expenses. Examples include monitoring and adjudication of care, a preferred-provider or network program, or a similar option. [MCL 500.3181, 500.3183]
- Conditions: [MCL 500.3183, .3184, .3186]
  - It must be uniformly offered where available. [MCL 500.3183, .3184, .3186]
  - It must provide a discount reflecting anticipated savings. [MCL 500.3183, .3184, .3186]
  - It may not apply to emergency care. [MCL 500.3183, .3184, .3186]
  - An insurer offering it must also offer PIP not subject to managed care. [MCL 500.3183, .3184, .3186]
  - Deductibles and co-pays are allowed. [MCL 500.3183, .3184, .3186]
- Under a managed care option, PIP is primary and not coordinated with health coverage, and must be exhausted before other health coverage. [MCL 500.3187]
- The insurer must obtain a signed acknowledgment that the insured received a disclosure statement. The statement includes the discount range and the consequences of violating the plan. [MCL 500.3188, .3189]
- Exam impact: the managed care option is optional, so core PIP benefit facts are unchanged; recognize it only as an optional discounted PIP-medical delivery arrangement. [MCL 500.3181–.3189]

### PIP order of priority (MCL 500.3114, 500.3115)
- General rule: a person claims first under their own policy, or under the policy of a spouse or a resident relative (domiciled in the same household). If the person has their own policy, their own insurer pays everything up to its 3107c level, with no recoupment from a relative's insurer. [MCL 500.3114(1)]
- Operators and passengers of a vehicle operated in the business of transporting passengers claim from that vehicle's insurer. [MCL 500.3114(2)]
  - This does not apply to passengers in school buses, common-carrier buses, government-program buses, nonprofit buses, taxicabs, livery buses, TNC vehicles, or vehicles on an opt-out or exclusion policy, unless the passenger has no other PIP. [MCL 500.3114(2)]
- An employee (or the employee's spouse or resident relative) injured while occupying an employer-owned or employer-registered vehicle claims from the insurer of the furnished vehicle. [MCL 500.3114(3)]
- An occupant with no own or household PIP policy claims through the Michigan Assigned Claims Plan (MACP). [MCL 500.3114(4), as amended 2019 PA 21]
- A non-occupant (e.g., pedestrian or bicyclist) with no own or household PIP policy claims through the MACP. [MCL 500.3115, as amended 2019 PA 21]
- Motorcycle operator or passenger injured in an accident involving a motor vehicle, in order: [MCL 500.3114(5)]
  1. The insurer of the owner or registrant of the motor vehicle. [MCL 500.3114(5)]
  2. The insurer of the operator of the motor vehicle. [MCL 500.3114(5)]
  3. The motor vehicle insurer of the motorcycle operator. [MCL 500.3114(5)]
  4. The motor vehicle insurer of the motorcycle owner or registrant. [MCL 500.3114(5)]
- Opt-out or excluded policies are skipped in the motorcycle priority. If two or more policies are at the same level, PIP is capped at the highest single limit and insurers share by partial recoupment. [MCL 500.3114(6)–(8)]
- A motorcycle is not a "motor vehicle" under the no-fault act. A motorcycle accident that does not involve a motor vehicle is not covered by 3114(5). [MCL 500.3101(3)(g),(h),(i)]

### Property Protection Insurance (PPI) (MCL 500.3121–.3127)
- PPI pays, without regard to fault, for accidental damage to tangible property arising out of the ownership, operation, maintenance or use of a motor vehicle as a motor vehicle. Damage includes physical injury, destruction, and loss of use. [MCL 500.3121(1)–(3)]
- Benefits are the lesser of reasonable repair cost or replacement cost less depreciation, plus loss of use. The maximum is $1,000,000 per policy for all property damage from one accident. [MCL 500.3121(5)]
- Excluded: [MCL 500.3121(1); 500.3123]
  - Damage to property other than the insured vehicle occurring in the course of a business of repairing or servicing motor vehicles. [MCL 500.3121(1); 500.3123]
  - Vehicles and their contents, unless the vehicle was parked so as not to cause unreasonable risk (properly parked cars ARE covered). [MCL 500.3121(1); 500.3123]
  - Property of the named insured, spouse or resident relative, if any of them was the owner, registrant or operator of a vehicle involved. [MCL 500.3121(1); 500.3123]
  - Accidents outside Michigan. [MCL 500.3121(1); 500.3123]
  - Certain utility-line damage. [MCL 500.3121(1); 500.3123]
- Priority: insurers of owners or registrants of the vehicles involved, then insurers of their operators. [MCL 500.3125]
- DIFS summary: PPI pays up to $1 million for damage your vehicle does in Michigan to other people's property, such as buildings, fences and properly parked vehicles. It does not pay for your own car. [DIFS auto FAQ]

### Residual liability and the tort threshold (MCL 500.3131, 500.3135)
- Residual liability covers BI and PD occurring in the US, its territories and possessions, or Canada. It provides coverage equivalent to the financial responsibility law where the injury or damage occurs, and in Michigan the liability retained by 3135. [MCL 500.3131(1)]
- Noneconomic loss (pain and suffering): tort liability remains only if the injured person suffered death, serious impairment of body function, or permanent serious disfigurement. [MCL 500.3135(1)]
- Serious impairment of body function: objectively manifested; an impairment of an important body function; and it affects the person's general ability to lead his or her normal life. There is no minimum duration. [MCL 500.3135(5)]
- Comparative fault applies; a party more than 50% at fault recovers no noneconomic damages. A person who was operating his or her own vehicle without the required security cannot recover noneconomic (pain and suffering) damages, and an uninsured damaged vehicle cannot recover under the mini-tort. [MCL 500.3135(2)(b),(c)]
- Tort liability is otherwise abolished for an insured vehicle, except for: [MCL 500.3135(3)]
  - intentional harm; [MCL 500.3135(3)]
  - threshold noneconomic loss; [MCL 500.3135(3)]
  - economic loss above PIP limits (including the daily, monthly and 3-year limits); [MCL 500.3135(3)]
  - nonresidents' economic loss (threshold injury required); [MCL 500.3135(3)]
  - the "mini-tort." [MCL 500.3135(3)]
- Mini-tort: up to $3,000 for accidents after July 1, 2020 (previously $1,000) for vehicle damage not covered by insurance [MCL 500.3135(3)(e),(4)]
  - A party more than 50% at fault cannot recover. [MCL 500.3135(3)(e),(4)]
  - The suit is brought in small claims when possible. [MCL 500.3135(3)(e),(4)]
  - It is not part of required residual liability. [MCL 500.3135(3)(e),(4)]
  - No recovery if the damaged vehicle was uninsured. [MCL 500.3135(3)(e),(4)]

### Michigan Catastrophic Claims Association (MCCA) (MCL 500.3104)
- The MCCA is an unincorporated nonprofit association created in 1978 (no liability for losses before July 1, 1978). Every insurer writing Michigan no-fault security must be a member. [MCL 500.3104(1),(27)]
- It reimburses member insurers for 100% of PIP ultimate loss above the retention per loss occurrence. [MCL 500.3104(2),(27)]
  - Only for policies issued or renewed before July 2, 2020, and later policies with UNLIMITED PIP. [MCL 500.3104(2),(27)]
  - Not for the $50k/$250k/$500k levels. [MCL 500.3104(2),(27)]
- Retention: $580,000 for July 1, 2019 – June 30, 2021. It then adjusts every 2 years (July 1 of odd years) by the lesser of 6% or CPI, rounded to the nearest $5,000. [MCL 500.3104(2)(o)]
- Retention for policies issued or renewed July 1, 2025 – June 30, 2027: $675,000. [michigancatastrophic.com/what-is-catastrophic-injury]
- Per-vehicle assessment for July 1, 2026 – June 30, 2027: [MCCA Historical Assessment Chart; MCCA press release Oct. 8, 2025]
  - Unlimited PIP: $84 ($65 pure premium + $19 deficit recoupment). [MCCA Historical Assessment Chart; MCCA press release Oct. 8, 2025]
  - All other PIP levels, including opt-out: $19 (deficit recoupment only). [MCCA Historical Assessment Chart; MCCA press release Oct. 8, 2025]
  - The 2025/26 figures were $82 and $23. [MCCA Historical Assessment Chart; MCCA press release Oct. 8, 2025]
- No premium is charged for limited-PIP or opt-out cars except the deficit-adjustment portion. A historic vehicle pays 20% of the car premium. The MCCA charges each member insurer per written car-year. A member passing the charge on to an insured must pass on exactly the amount attributable to that car. [MCL 500.3104(7)(d),(20)]

### Michigan Assigned Claims Plan (MACP) (MCL 500.3171–.3175)
- The MAIPF adopts and maintains the assigned claims plan. Insurers and self-insurers participate and share costs. [MCL 500.3171(2)]
- A person may claim PIP through the MACP when: [MCL 500.3172(1)]
  - no PIP applies; [MCL 500.3172(1)]
  - no PIP can be identified; [MCL 500.3172(1)]
  - insurers dispute priority; or [MCL 500.3172(1)]
  - the insurer is financially unable to pay. [MCL 500.3172(1)]
- The MACP pays allowable expenses only up to $250,000. The cap is $2,000,000 for persons injured in the 30-day gap after losing QHC under an opt-out or exclusion. [MCL 500.3172(7)]
- Benefits are reduced by other benefit sources (except Medicaid and Medicare) for "no PIP," "can't identify" and "insolvent insurer" claims. [MCL 500.3172(5)]
- The claimant must notify the MAIPF within 1 year after the accident. The claim is then assigned to a servicing insurer, and suit timing follows 3145. [MCL 500.3174]
- The claimant must cooperate (examination under oath with at least 21 days' notice). Fraud makes a claim ineligible. [MCL 500.3173a]

### Fee schedule / utilization (MCL 500.3157): basics only
- For treatment after July 1, 2023, providers are generally limited to 190% of the Medicare amount. [MCL 500.3157(2)(c)]
  - 220% for providers with 20–30% indigent volume and designated freestanding rehab facilities. [MCL 500.3157(3)(c)]
  - 250% for 30% or more indigent volume. [MCL 500.3157(5)]
  - 230% for Level I/II trauma centers for emergency care before stabilization. [MCL 500.3157(6)(c)]
- If there is no Medicare rate, a percentage of the provider's Jan. 1, 2019 charge master applies, adjusted for medical CPI. [MCL 500.3157(7),(9)]
- In-home attendant care by family, household members or prior acquaintances is payable only up to 56 hours per week (the workers' comp limit), unless the insurer contracts for more. [MCL 500.3157(10),(11); MCL 418.315(1)]
- Ambulance emergency services are exempt from the fee schedule. [MCL 500.3157(13)]

### Cancellation and nonrenewal (Chapter 32, MCL 500.3204–.3240)
- Insurers may cancel an auto liability policy only as Chapter 32 allows. [MCL 500.3204(1)]
- Once a policy has been in effect 55 days (or immediately if it is a renewal), cancellation is allowed only if the named insured or any household/customary operator has had a driver's license suspended or revoked during the policy period, and it has become final. [MCL 500.3220]
- Within the first 55 days the insurer may cancel because the risk is unacceptable. That cancellation is not subject to appeal. [MCL 500.3220(a); 500.3224(1)]
- Cancellation notice: [MCL 500.3224(2),(3)]
  - Written notice sent by certified mail, return receipt requested (delivery is equivalent), to the last known address. [MCL 500.3224(2),(3)]
  - Mailed at least 20 days before the effective date. [MCL 500.3224(2),(3)]
  - Must state the reasons and say in bold type that the insured may appeal to the department within 7 days from mailing. [MCL 500.3224(2),(3)]
- Insured's appeal and hearing: [MCL 500.3230, .3234]
  - The insured files a written hearing request with the commissioner within 7 days after the postmark date. [MCL 500.3230, .3234]
  - A hearing is held within 10 days after the appeal is received, with notice at least 4 days before the hearing. [MCL 500.3230, .3234]
- Nonrenewal is not a cancellation if the insurer mails notice by first-class mail at least 20 days before the termination date. [MCL 500.3204(2)]
- Chapter 32 does not apply to cancellations for nonpayment of premium or to termination at the end of a policy period. [MCL 500.3208, 500.3212]
- Related Essential Insurance Act rule (Chapter 21): [MCL 500.2123]
  - A "termination" (refusal to continue for reasons other than nonpayment) requires 30 days' written notice stating each specific reason. [MCL 500.2123]
  - Within the first 55 days the notice may be 20 days. [MCL 500.2123]
  - Termination must conform to the insurer's underwriting rules. [MCL 500.2123]
  - It does not authorize terminating an auto policy in violation of Chapter 32. [MCL 500.2123]
  - See Flags. [MCL 500.2123]
- The ISO PAP's own termination clause is noted under Personal Auto Policy (Part F); state law controls. [ISO PP 00 01 09 18, Part F]

### Rental vehicle coverage
- No-fault security is required of an "owner," which includes a person renting or using a vehicle for more than 30 days. A renter for 30 days or less is not an owner. [MCL 500.3101(3)(l)]
- PIP is payable without regard to fault [MCL 500.3105(2)]. A person injured in a rental car claims PIP first from their own or household policy [MCL 500.3114(1)]. DIFS Bulletin 96-02 notes that a person renting more than 30 days is an "owner" and an "eligible person" who must be offered a full no-fault policy (PIP and PPI). [DIFS Bulletin 96-02]
- Liability policies must meet the 3009(1) limits for vehicles registered or principally garaged in Michigan. [MCL 500.3009(1)]
- A rental company leasing for 30 days or less is liable as owner only when an authorized driver or a listed immediate-family member is driving. Unless the lessor was negligent, its liability is capped at $20,000 per person / $40,000 per accident BI, and the lessee must be notified of this cap. [MCL 257.401(3),(4)]
- A lessor under a lease of more than 30 days has no common-law owner liability. [MCL 257.401(2)]
- PAP: Part A liability and Part D physical damage follow the insured to a non-owned auto (including a rental). Under the PAP (09 18), Part D covers loss of use of a non-owned auto (up to $30/day) when the policy has collision or OTC on any covered auto. [ISO PP 00 01 09 18, Part A; Part D]
- Rental-car sellers of incidental auto insurance (rentals of 90 days or less) are exempt from producer licensing. [MCL 500.1202(2)(h)]

### Aftermarket Crash Parts Act (1991 PA 158, MCL 257.1361–.1364)
- Effective Feb. 1, 1992. [MCL 257.1364]
- An "aftermarket crash part" is a replacement for a nonmechanical sheet-metal or plastic exterior part (e.g., an inner or outer panel). A "non-OEM aftermarket crash part" is one not made by or for the vehicle manufacturer. [MCL 257.1362(a),(e)]
- If an insurer requests non-OEM crash parts, the repair facility may use them only if the insured receives a written estimate that clearly identifies each non-OEM part. The estimate must carry, in at least 10-point bold type, a notice that the parts come from a source other than the manufacturer and that warranties are provided by the parts' manufacturer, distributor or insurer. [MCL 257.1363]
- "Motor vehicle" in this act excludes motorcycles, mopeds, trailers, buses, tractors and similar. [MCL 257.1362(d)]

### Michigan collision coverage options
- At the initial application for a private passenger nonfleet auto, the insurer must offer: [MCL 500.3037(1),(2),(8)(b)]
  - Limited collision: no deductible, but pays only if the operator was NOT substantially at fault. [MCL 500.3037(1),(2),(8)(b)]
  - Broad form collision: pays regardless of fault, and the deductible is waived if the operator was not substantially at fault. [MCL 500.3037(1),(2),(8)(b)]
  - Standard collision, and limited collision with a deductible, may also be offered (deductibles as approved by the director). [MCL 500.3037(1),(2),(8)(b)]
  - "Substantially at fault" means more than 50% of the cause. [MCL 500.3037(1),(2),(8)(b)]
- Rejection of both offered collision coverages must be in writing. The collision status is explained at least annually at renewal. [MCL 500.3037(4),(7)]

### Uninsured / underinsured motorists (Michigan)
- No Michigan statute requires UM/UIM to be sold, offered or rejected in writing. DIFS lists UM and UIM among "optional" coverages, paying if an uninsured or underinsured motorist seriously injures you or a household member. [DIFS auto FAQ; Rory v Continental Ins Co, 473 Mich 457 (2005)]
- The Michigan Supreme Court treats UM coverage as optional and contractual, not required by the no-fault act. [Rory v Continental Ins Co, 473 Mich 457 (2005)]
- TNC and car-sharing exclusions may expressly exclude UM/UIM coverage. [MCL 500.3017(1)(c); 500.3018(1)(c)]

## Personal Auto Policy (ISO PP 00 01 09 18)

### Definitions
- "You/your" means the named insured and a spouse who is a resident of the same household. A spouse who moves out remains "you" until the earliest of: 90 days after the change of residency, the effective date of another policy naming the spouse, or the end of the policy period. [PP 00 01 09 18, Definitions A]
- A private passenger auto, pickup or van leased under a written agreement for a continuous period of at least 6 months is deemed owned. [PP 00 01 09 18, Definitions C]
- "Family member" means a person related to you by blood, marriage or adoption who is a resident of your household, including a ward or foster child. [PP 00 01 09 18, Definitions F]
- "Occupying" means in, upon, or getting in, on, out or off. [PP 00 01 09 18, Definitions G]
- "Your covered auto" includes: [PP 00 01 09 18, Definitions J]
  - vehicles shown in the Declarations; [PP 00 01 09 18, Definitions J]
  - newly acquired autos; [PP 00 01 09 18, Definitions J]
  - any trailer you own; [PP 00 01 09 18, Definitions J]
  - a non-owned auto or trailer used as a temporary substitute while a covered vehicle is out of normal use because of breakdown, repair, servicing, loss or destruction (J.4 does not apply to Part D). [PP 00 01 09 18, Definitions J]
- "Newly acquired auto" = a private passenger auto, or a pickup or van for which no other policy provides coverage that has a GVWR of 10,000 lbs or less and is not used for delivery (except incidental to an installation/maintenance/repair business, or farming/ranching), that you become the owner of during the policy period. [PP 00 01 09 18, Definitions K.1]
- Newly acquired auto timing: [PP 00 01 09 18, Definitions K.2]
  - Liability, med pay and UM: broadest coverage on any declared vehicle if you ask the insurer to insure it within 14 days after you become the owner. [PP 00 01 09 18, Definitions K.2]
  - Collision or OTC begins on the date you become the owner. You must ask within 14 days if the Declarations show that coverage on at least one auto. [PP 00 01 09 18, Definitions K.2]
  - If no declared auto has that coverage, you must ask within 4 days, and a $500 deductible applies to a loss occurring before you asked. [PP 00 01 09 18, Definitions K.2]
  - If you ask late, coverage begins when you ask. [PP 00 01 09 18, Definitions K.2]
- "Transportation network platform" is an online app or network connecting passengers with drivers for compensation. [PP 00 01 09 18, Definitions L]

### Part A: Liability
- Pays BI and PD damages (including prejudgment interest) for which an insured is legally responsible because of an auto accident, plus all defense costs in addition to the limit. The duty to defend ends when the limit is exhausted. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Insuring Agreement A]
- Insureds: [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, B]
  - you or any family member for any auto or trailer; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, B]
  - any person using your covered auto; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, B]
  - organizations vicariously liable. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, B]
- Supplementary payments (in addition to the limit): [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Supplementary Payments]
  - up to $250 for bail bonds; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Supplementary Payments]
  - premiums on appeal bonds and bonds to release attachments; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Supplementary Payments]
  - post-judgment interest; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Supplementary Payments]
  - up to $250 a day for loss of earnings (not other income) for attending hearings or trials at the insurer's request; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Supplementary Payments]
  - other reasonable expenses at the insurer's request. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Supplementary Payments]
- Exclusions include: [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Exclusions A]
  - intentional injury; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Exclusions A]
  - damage to property owned or transported by the insured; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Exclusions A]
  - damage to property rented to, used by or in the care of the insured (except a residence or private garage); [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Exclusions A]
  - employee injury (except domestic employees not covered by workers' comp); [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Exclusions A]
  - public or livery conveyance, including time logged into a TNC platform as a driver (share-the-expense car pools and volunteer/charitable use are excepted); [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Exclusions A]
  - the auto business (selling, repairing, servicing, storing, parking); [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Exclusions A]
  - other business use of vehicles other than private passenger autos, pickups or vans (or trailers used with them); [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Exclusions A]
  - using a vehicle without a reasonable belief of entitlement; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Exclusions A]
  - nuclear; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Exclusions A]
  - personal vehicle sharing programs. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Exclusions A]
- Vehicle exclusions: [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Exclusions B]
  - vehicles with fewer than 4 wheels or designed mainly for off-road use, except in a medical emergency, trailers, and non-owned golf carts; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Exclusions B]
  - owned vehicles that are not "your covered auto" and vehicles furnished or available for your regular use; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Exclusions B]
  - family members' owned or regular-use vehicles (this exclusion does not apply to "you"); [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Exclusions B]
  - racing facilities; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Exclusions B]
  - vehicles designed for flight. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Exclusions B]
- Out of state: limits are increased to the other state's financial responsibility limits, and its compulsory minimums are provided. Coverage for a non-owned vehicle is excess. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part A, Out of State Coverage; Other Insurance]

### Part B: Medical Payments
- Reasonable medical and funeral expenses for services rendered within 3 years from the date of the accident. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part B, Insuring Agreement A]
- Insureds: you or family members while occupying a motor vehicle or as a pedestrian struck by one, and any other person occupying your covered auto. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part B, B]
- Excludes: [PP 00 01 09 18, Part B, Exclusions]
  - motorized vehicles with fewer than 4 wheels; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part B, Exclusions]
  - public or livery use (including TNC); [PP 00 01 09 18, Part B, Exclusions]
  - injuries where workers' comp is required or available; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part B, Exclusions]
  - owned or regular-use non-covered vehicles. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part B, Exclusions]

### Part C: Uninsured Motorists
- Pays compensatory damages an insured is legally entitled to recover from the owner or operator of an uninsured motor vehicle because of BI. A judgment obtained without the insurer's written consent is not binding. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part C, A]
- An "uninsured motor vehicle" is one: [PP 00 01 09 18, Part C, C]
  - with no BI bond or policy; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part C, C]
  - with BI limits below the financial responsibility minimum of the state where your covered auto is principally garaged; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part C, C]
  - an unidentified hit-and-run vehicle that hits you, a family member, a vehicle you occupy, or your covered auto; or [PP 00 01 09 18, Part C, C]
  - whose insurer denies coverage or is insolvent. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part C, C]
- Not uninsured vehicles: vehicles owned by or available to you or a family member, government-owned vehicles, self-insured vehicles (unless the self-insurer is insolvent), vehicles on rails or crawler treads, off-road vehicles while off public roads, and vehicles used as residences. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part C, C]
- No punitive damages. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part C, Exclusion D]
- Arbitration: [PP 00 01 09 18, Part C, Arbitration]
  - Both parties must agree, each selects an arbitrator, and the two select a third. If they cannot agree on the third within 30 days, a judge selects. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part C, Arbitration]
  - Coverage disputes may not be arbitrated. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part C, Arbitration]
  - If the award exceeds the FR minimum, either party may demand trial within 60 days. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part C, Arbitration]

### Part D: Coverage for Damage to Your Auto
- Collision is the upset of the auto or its impact with another vehicle or object. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, B]
- Other Than Collision (OTC) perils: missiles or falling objects, fire, theft, explosion or earthquake, windstorm, hail, water or flood, vandalism, riot, contact with bird or animal, and glass breakage. Glass broken in a collision may be treated as collision at the insured's option. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, B]
- A "non-owned auto" is a private passenger auto, pickup, van or trailer not owned by or available for the regular use of you or a family member while in your custody, or a temporary substitute. A non-owned auto gets the broadest coverage on any your covered auto. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, A, C]
- Transportation expenses (no deductible): [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Transportation Expenses]
  - Up to $30 per day, maximum $900, for temporary transportation after a covered loss to your covered auto. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Transportation Expenses]
  - Loss-of-use expenses for a non-owned auto are also covered, up to $30 per day. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Transportation Expenses]
  - Theft: payable beginning 48 hours after the theft. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Transportation Expenses]
  - Other losses: payable once the auto is withdrawn from use for more than 24 hours. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Transportation Expenses]
- Limits: [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Exclusion 10; Limit of Liability A]
  - Lesser of ACV or the cost to repair or replace with like kind and quality. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Exclusion 10; Limit of Liability A]
  - Non-owned trailer: $1,500. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Exclusion 10; Limit of Liability A]
  - Permanently installed electronics in a non-factory location: $1,000. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Exclusion 10; Limit of Liability A]
  - Custom equipment: first $1,500 only. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Exclusion 10; Limit of Liability A]
- Exclusions include: [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Exclusions]
  - public or livery or TNC use; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Exclusions]
  - wear and tear, freezing, mechanical breakdown, road damage to tires (unless from total theft); [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Exclusions]
  - war and nuclear; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Exclusions]
  - non-permanently-installed electronics; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Exclusions]
  - media (tapes, discs); [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Exclusions]
  - government destruction or confiscation (loss payees protected); [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Exclusions]
  - unscheduled trailers, camper bodies or motor homes; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Exclusions]
  - radar or laser detectors; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Exclusions]
  - non-owned autos in the auto business; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Exclusions]
  - racing; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Exclusions]
  - rental vehicles where the rental company is precluded by agreement or state law from recovering from you; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Exclusions]
  - vehicle sharing; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Exclusions]
  - aircraft. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, Exclusions]
- If more than one covered auto is damaged in the same collision, only the highest deductible applies. Appraisal is available: each party picks an appraiser, the two pick an umpire, and a decision by any two is binding. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part D, A; Appraisal]

### Part E: Duties After an Accident or Loss
- The insurer has no duty to provide coverage if failure to comply with these duties is prejudicial to it. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part E]
- Duties: [PP 00 01 09 18, Part E, A–D]
  - prompt notice of how, when and where the accident happened, with injured persons and witnesses; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part E, A–D]
  - cooperate; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part E, A–D]
  - send legal papers; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part E, A–D]
  - submit to physical exams by physicians the insurer selects (the insurer pays), examinations under oath, and recorded statements; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part E, A–D]
  - authorize medical records; [PP 00 01 09 18, Part E, A–D]
  - file proof of loss. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part E, A–D]
- UM claimants must promptly notify police of a hit-and-run. Part D claimants must protect the auto from further loss (reasonable expenses paid), notify police of theft, and permit inspection. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part E, C, D]

### Part F: General Provisions
- Policy territory: US, its territories or possessions, Puerto Rico, and Canada, including transport between their ports. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part F, Policy Period and Territory]
- Cancellation by the insurer: [PP 00 01 09 18, Part F, Termination A]
  - 10 days' notice for nonpayment, or during the first 60 days of a new policy; 20 days otherwise. [PP 00 01 09 18, Part F, Termination A]

### Motor Carrier Act of 1980: federal minimum financial responsibility [49 CFR 387.9; 387.15]
- For-hire carrier, interstate or foreign commerce, GVWR 10,001 lbs or more, nonhazardous property: $750,000. [49 CFR 387.9; 387.15]
- Oil, hazardous waste, or other hazmat not in the higher category (for-hire and private; GVWR 10,001+): $1,000,000. [49 CFR 387.9; 387.15]

## Flags
- Values that change over time, with effective dates:
  - **Work loss and survivor's loss maximum** (per 30-day period, by accident date): $7,455 for Oct. 1, 2026 – Sept. 30, 2027. Prior years: $7,201 (2025–26), $7,014 (2024–25), $6,811 (2023–24). [DIFS Bulletin 2026-20-INS]
  - **MCCA retention:** $675,000 for policies issued or renewed July 1, 2025 – June 30, 2027. The next change is due July 1, 2027. [michigancatastrophic.com] I could not find the retention in a statute or DIFS document. The figure is consistent with $635,000 × 1.06 rounded to the nearest $5,000.
  - **MCCA assessment for July 1, 2026 – June 30, 2027:** $84 per vehicle for unlimited PIP ($65 + $19), $19 for all other levels. [MCCA chart and press release] It changes every July 1.
  - **QHC deductible cap:** $6,579 for July 1, 2026 – June 30, 2027. [DIFS Bulletin 2026-08-INS]
  - **Fee schedule:** the percentages in 3157 are those for services after July 1, 2023. A 3157 CPI adjustment bulletin exists (2026-09-INS) but was not read in detail.
- **Old vs new liability limits (conflict).**
  - The Vehicle Code FR policy definition (MCL 257.520) and an outdated DIFS FAQ page still show 20/40/10.
  - MCL 500.3009 requires 250/500/10 by default after July 1, 2020, with an elected minimum of 50/100.
  - Older exam banks may still use 20/40/10. These materials use the current 3009 values.
- **The outline cites "RL 257.518b" for required limits.** The current 257.518b covers only TNC driver and TNC company limits (50/100/25 while logged on; $1M CSL during a ride). The general limits are in MCL 500.3009.
- **Cancellation notice conflict.**
  - Chapter 32: 20 days, certified mail RRR (3224); nonrenewal 20 days by first-class mail (3204).
  - Essential Insurance Act 500.2123: 30 days with specific reasons for a "termination" (20 days within the first 55 days).
  - ISO PAP: 10/20 days and a 60-day window.
  - Questions here test the Chapter 32 rules the outline cites (3204, 3220, 3224) and name the statute in the stem.
- **UM/UIM.** No statute mandating an offer or rejection was found in the Insurance Code sections reviewed. "Optional" is based on DIFS consumer material and Rory v Continental (2005). The PSI outline lists "UM/UIM Rejection / Required Limits," which is likely generic national content.
- **Effective date of the priority changes.** The changes to 3114(4) and 3115 (MACP instead of the owner's or operator's insurer) came in 2019 PA 21, which took immediate effect June 11, 2019. The act's enacting sections give no separate applicability date for them. I did not verify which accident dates they apply to.
- **Outline mismatch.** The outline pairs "Rental Vehicle Coverage" with 500.3009 and .3105(2). Neither section mentions rentals expressly. The rental facts above are assembled from 3101(3)(l), 3105, 3114(1), MCL 257.401(3) and PAP Part D.
- **Form editions.**
  - The PAP numbers come from PP 00 01 09 18: bail $250; loss of earnings $250/day; transportation $30/day, max $900. A secondary summary of the older PP 00 01 01 05 gives transportation as $20/day, max $600. I did not verify the other older-edition amounts, so questions name the 09 18 edition.
  - The BAP numbers come from CA 00 01 11 20 (transportation and loss of use $30/$900). The 10 13 edition was $20/$600.
  - The Garage form symbols come from CA 00 05 07 97.
- **Not verified from primary form text.**
  - Truckers (CA 00 12) and Motor Carrier (CA 00 20) form details and symbol numbers. The commonly cited ranges, 41–50 and 61–71, were NOT verified and are not used in questions.
  - Individual Named Insured (CA 99 17), Lessor Additional Insured (CA 20 01) and Mobile Equipment endorsement wording. These are kept conceptual.
  - Miscellaneous Type Vehicle details come from FC&S and riskeducation.org summaries, not the form itself.
- **49 CFR 387.33** (passenger carrier limits) shows an effective-date note that it was "suspended" Jan. 14, 2017 (a temporary 387.33T is in use). The amounts were not used in questions.
