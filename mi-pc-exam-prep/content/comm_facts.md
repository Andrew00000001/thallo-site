# cpp: Commercial Package Policy

Editions relied on (form text read directly unless noted): IL 00 17 11 98; CG 00 01 04 13; CG 00 09 04 13; CG 00 33 04 13; CP 00 10 10 12; CP 00 17 10 12; CP 00 18 10 12; CP 00 20 06 95; CP 00 30 10 12; CP 00 40 10 12; CP 00 50 10 12; CP 00 90 07 88; CP 10 10 10 12; CP 10 20 10 12; CP 10 30 09 17; CP 04 05 09 17; CP 04 40 06 07; CP 12 30 06 95; IL 04 15 04 98; CR 00 21 11 15; CM 00 01 09 04; FP 00 10 09 94. CG 00 02 04 13, CR 00 20, EB 00 20 01 13 and FL 00 20 are from reputable form analyses (FC&S/PropertyCasualty360, Rough Notes PF&M, Austin & Stanovich) — see Flags.

## Components of a Commercial Policy
- A commercial package policy (CPP) provides both property and liability coverage for a business in one policy (also called commercial multiple peril). [IRMI glossary "commercial package policy"]
- Building blocks: Common Policy Declarations, Common Policy Conditions (IL 00 17), interline endorsements, and coverage parts; one coverage part = monoline policy, two or more = package. [IL 00 17 11 98 preamble; IRMI glossary "commercial package policy"]
- IL 00 17 applies to all coverage parts: "All Coverage Parts included in this policy are subject to the following conditions." [ISO IL 00 17 11 98]
- Interline ("IL") endorsements modify more than one coverage part — e.g., IL 04 15 Protective Safeguards modifies both the Commercial Property and Farm coverage parts. [ISO IL 04 15 04 98]
- Some coverage parts use a separate conditions form (CP 00 90 for commercial property, CM 00 01 for inland marine); others, like CGL (CG 00 01 Sec. IV), put their conditions in the coverage form. [ISO CP 00 90 07 88; CM 00 01 09 04]

### Common Policy Conditions (IL 00 17 11 98) — six conditions
- A. Cancellation: first Named Insured may cancel by advance written notice; insurer must give written notice at least 10 days before cancellation for nonpayment, 30 days for any other reason, mailed/delivered to the first Named Insured. [ISO IL 00 17 11 98, A.]
- Insurer cancels → refund is pro rata; first Named Insured cancels → refund may be less than pro rata; cancellation is effective even if refund not yet made; proof of mailing is sufficient proof of notice. [ISO IL 00 17 11 98, A.5-6]
- B. Changes: first Named Insured may make changes with insurer consent; policy amended or waived only by endorsement. [ISO IL 00 17 11 98, B.]
- C. Examination of books and records: during the policy period and up to 3 years afterward. [ISO IL 00 17 11 98, C.]
- D. Inspections and surveys: insurer has the right but no obligation; inspections relate only to insurability and premium; insurer does not make safety inspections or warrant conditions are safe (boiler/pressure vessel/elevator certification inspections are an exception to the disclaimer). [ISO IL 00 17 11 98, D.]
- E. Premiums: the first Named Insured is responsible for payment of all premiums and is payee for return premiums. [ISO IL 00 17 11 98, E.]
- F. Transfer of rights and duties: not without insurer's written consent, except on death of an individual named insured (passes to legal representative; until appointed, to person with proper temporary custody of property). [ISO IL 00 17 11 98, F.]

## Commercial General Liability (CG 00 01 04 13)
### Coverage A — Bodily Injury and Property Damage
- Pays damages for BI/PD the insured is legally obligated to pay; insurer has right and duty to defend; duty to defend ends when limit is used up by judgments/settlements (A or B) or medical expenses (C). [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. I Cov. A 1.a]
- Trigger (occurrence form): BI/PD caused by an "occurrence" in the coverage territory and occurring during the policy period (and not known before the policy period). [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. I Cov. A 1.b]
- "Occurrence" = an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. V def. 13]
- Key exclusions: expected/intended (except reasonable force), contractual (except "insured contract"), liquor liability (only if insured is in the business of manufacturing, distributing, selling, serving or furnishing alcohol), workers comp, employer's liability, pollution, aircraft/auto/watercraft, mobile equipment transport/racing, war, damage to property, your product, your work, impaired property, recall, P&AI, electronic data, TCPA/CAN-SPAM/FCRA. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. I Cov. A 2.a-q]
- Watercraft exclusion does not apply to a non-owned watercraft less than 26 feet long not carrying persons or property for a charge, or watercraft ashore on premises you own or rent. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Excl. g.(1)-(2)]
- Damage to Property exclusion paragraphs (1),(3),(4) do not apply to damage (other than fire) to premises rented to you for 7 or fewer consecutive days. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Excl. j]
- Exclusions c. through n. do not apply to fire damage to premises rented to you or temporarily occupied with owner's permission — separate Damage To Premises Rented To You limit. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. I Cov. A 2 (final para.)]
- Damage to Your Work exclusion does not apply if the damaged work, or the work out of which damage arises, was performed by a subcontractor. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Excl. l]
### Coverage B — Personal and Advertising Injury
- Offense must arise out of your business and be committed in the coverage territory during the policy period. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Cov. B 1.b]
- P&AI offenses: false arrest/detention/imprisonment; malicious prosecution; wrongful eviction/entry/invasion of private occupancy; slander/libel/disparagement; violation of right of privacy; use of another's advertising idea in your advertisement; infringing copyright, trade dress or slogan in your advertisement. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. V def. 14]
- Exclusions include knowing violation, material published with knowledge of falsity, material first published before the policy period, criminal acts, breach of contract, failure of goods to conform to advertised quality, wrong description of prices, IP infringement (except copyright/trade dress/slogan in your advertisement), insureds in media/internet businesses. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Cov. B 2.a-p]
### Coverage C — Medical Payments
- Pays medical expenses for BI caused by an accident on premises you own/rent, on ways next to them, or because of your operations — regardless of fault; expenses must be incurred and reported within one year of the accident. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Cov. C 1.a-b]
- Covers first aid, necessary medical/surgical/X-ray/dental incl. prosthetics, ambulance, hospital, nursing and funeral services. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Cov. C 1.b]
- Excluded: any insured (except volunteer workers), hired person, person injured on part of premises they normally occupy, persons eligible for workers comp, athletics participants, products-completed operations hazard, anything excluded under Coverage A. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Cov. C 2.a-g]
### Supplementary Payments — Coverages A and B (in addition to limits)
- All expenses insurer incurs; up to $250 for bail bonds (accidents/traffic violations from use of a vehicle to which BI coverage applies; insurer need not furnish bonds); cost of bonds to release attachments (within limit); insured's reasonable expenses incl. actual loss of earnings up to $250 a day; court costs taxed against insured (not attorney fees); prejudgment interest on the part of judgment insurer pays; post-judgment interest until insurer pays/offers/deposits its share. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Supp. Payments 1.a-g]
- "These payments will not reduce the limits of insurance." Defense of an indemnitee can be paid as a supplementary payment if conditions are met (insured contract, no conflict, etc.). [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Supp. Payments 1-2]
### Who Is an Insured
- Individual: you and spouse, only for the business you solely own. Partnership/JV: partners/members and spouses re conduct of the business. LLC: members re conduct of business; managers re duties as managers. Corporation/other: executive officers and directors re duties; stockholders re liability as stockholders. Trust: trustees re duties. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. II 1.a-e]
- Also insureds: employees and volunteer workers (within scope; not for injury to co-employees), real estate manager, person with temporary custody of property if you die, your legal representative. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. II 2.a-d]
- Newly acquired or formed organization (not partnership/JV/LLC) is a Named Insured until the 90th day or end of policy period, whichever is earlier. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. II 3.a]
### Limits of Insurance
- General Aggregate = most for Coverage C + Coverage A (except products-completed operations) + Coverage B. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. III 2]
- Products-Completed Operations Aggregate = most under Coverage A for BI/PD in the products-completed operations hazard. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. III 3]
- Personal & Advertising Injury Limit = per person or organization (subject to general aggregate). Each Occurrence Limit = Coverage A + Coverage C per occurrence. Damage To Premises Rented To You Limit and Medical Expense Limit (per person) are sublimits of each occurrence. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. III 4-7]
- Limits apply to each consecutive annual period (an extension of less than 12 months is added to the last period). [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. III]
### Conditions (Section IV)
- Bankruptcy; Duties in event of occurrence/claim/suit (notify "as soon as practicable"; no voluntary payments except first aid); Legal action against us; Other insurance; Premium audit; Representations; Separation of insureds; Transfer of rights of recovery; When we do not renew. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. IV 1-9]
- Nonrenewal: written notice to first Named Insured at least 30 days before expiration. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. IV 9]
- Other insurance: primary unless an excess situation applies; sharing is by equal shares if all other insurance permits it, otherwise by limits (pro rata). [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. IV 4.c]
- Premium shown as advance premium is a deposit; earned premium computed at audit; first Named Insured keeps records. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. IV 5]
### Key Definitions
- Coverage territory: USA (incl. territories/possessions), Puerto Rico, Canada; international waters/airspace in travel between them; worldwide for products made/sold in the territory, persons away for a short time on your business, and internet P&AI — if suit on the merits is in the US/PR/Canada or settled with insurer agreement. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. V def. 4]
- "Employee" includes a "leased worker" but not a "temporary worker". [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. V def. 5]
- "Mobile equipment" includes bulldozers, farm machinery, forklifts, vehicles used solely on/next to your premises, crawler-tread vehicles, power cranes/shovels/loaders/diggers/drills, road construction equipment; but land vehicles subject to compulsory/financial responsibility laws are "autos". [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. V def. 12]
- "Pollutants" = any solid, liquid, gaseous or thermal irritant or contaminant (smoke, vapor, soot, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals, waste). [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. V def. 15]
- "Suit" includes arbitration and other ADR proceedings to which the insured submits with insurer consent. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. V def. 18]

## Premises & Operations / Products & Completed Operations
- Premises and operations exposure is covered under Coverage A within the general aggregate; BI/PD in the "products-completed operations hazard" falls under the separate Products-Completed Operations Aggregate. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. III 2-3]
- Products-completed operations hazard: BI/PD occurring away from premises you own or rent arising out of "your product" or "your work", except products still in your physical possession or work not yet completed or abandoned. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. V def. 16.a]
- Work is deemed completed at the earliest of: all work under the contract completed; all work at the job site completed (multi-site contract); or that part of the work put to its intended use by anyone other than another contractor/subcontractor on the same project. Work needing only service/repair is treated as completed. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. V def. 16.a(2)]

## Insured Contract
- "Insured contract" = lease of premises (except the part indemnifying for fire damage to rented premises); sidetrack agreement; easement/license agreement (except construction/demolition within 50 feet of a railroad); obligation required by ordinance to indemnify a municipality (except work for the municipality); elevator maintenance agreement; and that part of any other business contract in which you assume the tort liability of another for BI/PD to a third party. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. V def. 9]
- Tort liability = liability imposed by law in the absence of any contract. Not insured contracts: indemnifying a railroad for work within 50 feet of railroad property; indemnifying architects/engineers/surveyors for professional services. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. V def. 9.f]
- Contractual liability exclusion does not apply to liability assumed in an insured contract if the BI/PD occurs after the contract is executed. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Excl. b.(2)]

## Occurrence vs Claims-Made (CG 00 02 04 13)
- Occurrence form (CG 00 01) is triggered by BI/PD occurring during the policy period; claims-made form (CG 00 02) is triggered by a claim first made during the policy period (or an extended reporting period) for BI/PD that occurred after the retroactive date and before the end of the policy period. [FC&S "Claims-Made CGL Form" (propertycasualty360.com, 2014, CG 00 02 04 13); RNC PF&M 270.3-1]
- A claim is deemed made when notice of the claim is received and recorded by any insured or by the insurer, whichever comes first; notice of an "occurrence" is not notice of a claim. [FC&S "Claims-Made CGL Form" (2014)]
- Retroactive date: BI/PD before the retro date is not covered even if the claim is made during the policy period; ISO rules allow advancing the retro date only with written consent of the first named insured (and in specified circumstances). [FC&S "Claims-Made CGL Form" (2014)]
- Basic Extended Reporting Period (automatic, no charge, when coverage is canceled/nonrenewed, retro date advanced, or replaced by occurrence coverage): 5 years for claims arising from occurrences reported to the insurer not later than 60 days after the policy ends; 60 days for claims from occurrences not previously reported. Basic ERP does not reinstate or increase limits and doesn't apply if later insurance covers the claim. [FC&S "Claims-Made CGL Form" (2014); Austin & Stanovich, "The Claims-Made CGL Policy" (2012)]
- Supplemental ERP: optional, by endorsement for additional premium; written request within 60 days after the end of the policy period; additional premium will not exceed 200% of the annual premium; unlimited duration; starts when the basic ERP ends; provides reinstated (supplemental) aggregate limits; excess over other valid insurance. [FC&S "Claims-Made CGL Form" (2014); Austin & Stanovich (2012)]
- Claim information condition ("Your Right to Claim and 'Occurrence' Information"): insurer gives the first Named Insured occurrence/claim and payment/reserve information for claims-made coverage issued during the previous 3 years — no later than 30 days before termination if insurer cancels or nonrenews; otherwise on written request made within 60 days after the end of the policy period, provided within 45 days of the request. [FC&S "Claims-Made CGL Form" (2014)]

## Owners and Contractors Protective (OCP) — CG 00 09 04 13
- Covers the named insured (owner/GC) for BI/PD arising out of operations performed for it by the designated "contractor" at the specified location, or the named insured's acts or omissions in general supervision of those operations. [ISO CG 00 09 04 13, Sec. I 1.b(1)]
- Excludes BI/PD occurring after all work on the project is completed or after the part of the contractor's work causing injury is put to its intended use. [ISO CG 00 09 04 13, Excl. 2.c]
- Single Aggregate Limit plus Each Occurrence Limit; aggregate applies separately to each designated project. Insurer's cancellation notice goes to the first Named Insured and the "contractor"; premium refunds are sent to the "contractor." [ISO CG 00 09 04 13, Sec. III; Sec. IV 2]
- Same $250 bail bond / supplementary payments structure as CGL. [ISO CG 00 09 04 13, Supp. Payments 1.b]

## Pollution
- CGL pollution exclusion f. bars BI/PD from pollutants at owned/occupied premises, waste sites, and job sites where pollutants are brought on; exceptions include BI inside a building from heating/cooling/water-heating equipment smoke/fumes, heat/smoke/fumes from a "hostile fire," and escape of mobile-equipment operating fluids. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Excl. f.(1)]
- f.(2) excludes cleanup/testing costs from governmental orders or suits (but not PD damages the insured would owe anyway). [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Excl. f.(2)]
- "Hostile fire" = one that becomes uncontrollable or breaks out from where it was intended to be. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Sec. V def. 7]

## Commercial Property
### Commercial Property Conditions (CP 00 90 07 88)
- Concealment, misrepresentation or fraud voids the coverage part; Control of property (acts of others beyond your control don't affect coverage; breach at one location doesn't affect others); Insurance under two or more coverages (no more than actual loss); No benefit to bailee; Other insurance (pro rata for same-plan insurance, otherwise excess); Policy period/coverage territory (US incl. territories/possessions, Puerto Rico, Canada); Transfer of rights of recovery. [ISO CP 00 90 07 88, A-I]
- Legal action: full compliance and within 2 years after the date of direct physical loss. [ISO CP 00 90 07 88, D.]
- Liberalization: broadening revisions adopted without additional premium within 45 days prior to or during the policy period apply automatically. [ISO CP 00 90 07 88, E.]
- Waiver of subrogation allowed in writing before a loss; after a loss only for someone insured, a business firm owned/controlled by or controlling you, or your tenant. [ISO CP 00 90 07 88, I.]

### Building and Personal Property (CP 00 10 10 12)
- Covered property: Building (incl. completed additions, fixtures, permanently installed machinery/equipment, building service property), Your Business Personal Property (in the building or within 100 feet), and Personal Property of Others in your care, custody or control. [ISO CP 00 10 10 12, A.1]
- BPP includes your use interest as tenant in improvements and betterments (fixtures/alterations you made at your expense in a building you don't own and can't legally remove). [ISO CP 00 10 10 12, A.1.b(6)]
- Property not covered includes money/securities, animals (with exceptions), autos held for sale, paved surfaces, foundations below lowest basement floor, land/water/growing crops, underground pipes, electronic data (except Additional Coverage), vehicles licensed for road use, outdoor fences/antennas/trees/shrubs (except extensions). [ISO CP 00 10 10 12, A.2]
- Additional Coverages: Debris Removal (25% of paid loss + deductible, plus up to an additional $25,000 per location; reported within 180 days); Preservation of Property (loss within 30 days after property first moved); Fire Department Service Charge (up to $1,000 per premises; no deductible); Pollutant Clean-up and Removal ($10,000 per premises per 12-month period; reported within 180 days); Increased Cost of Construction (lesser of $10,000 or 5% of building limit; only with Replacement Cost; repair within 2 years); Electronic Data ($2,500 per policy year). [ISO CP 00 10 10 12, A.4.a-f]
- Coverage Extensions (only if 80%+ coinsurance or a value reporting symbol is shown; coinsurance doesn't apply to them; generally additional insurance): Newly acquired/constructed property — $250,000 per building and $100,000 BPP per building, ending at policy expiration, 30 days after acquisition/start of construction, or when values are reported; Personal effects & property of others $2,500 per premises (personal effects not covered for theft); Valuable papers & records $2,500; Property off-premises $10,000; Outdoor property $1,000 ($250 per tree, shrub or plant) only for fire, lightning, explosion, riot or civil commotion, aircraft; Non-owned detached trailers $5,000; BPP in portable storage units $10,000 (ends 90 days after placement). [ISO CP 00 10 10 12, A.5.a-g]
- Outdoor signs limited to $2,500 per sign per occurrence. [ISO CP 00 10 10 12, C.]
- Loss conditions: sworn proof of loss within 60 days after insurer's request; insurer gives notice of intentions within 30 days of proof; pays within 30 days after proof if amount agreed or appraisal award. [ISO CP 00 10 10 12, E.3-4]
- Vacancy: if the building has been vacant more than 60 consecutive days before the loss — no coverage for vandalism, sprinkler leakage (unless protected against freezing), building glass breakage, water damage, theft or attempted theft; all other covered losses reduced by 15%. [ISO CP 00 10 10 12, E.6.b]
- Vacant (owner/general lessee policy) unless at least 31% of total square footage is rented and used by tenants and/or used by the owner for customary operations; for a tenant, vacant when the unit lacks enough BPP to conduct customary operations; buildings under construction/renovation are not vacant. [ISO CP 00 10 10 12, E.6.a]
- Valuation: ACV, except building repairs of $2,500 or less paid at replacement cost if coinsurance is met; stock sold but not delivered at selling price; glass with safety glazing if required by law; tenants' improvements at ACV if repaired promptly, a proportion of original cost if not, nothing if others pay. [ISO CP 00 10 10 12, E.7]
- Coinsurance formula: (Limit carried ÷ (value at time of loss × coinsurance %)) × loss, minus deductible, not to exceed the limit. [ISO CP 00 10 10 12, F.1]
- Mortgageholders: paid as interests appear even if foreclosure started; get loss payment even if insured's claim is denied if they pay premium, submit proof of loss within 60 days, and notify of change in risk; insurer gives mortgageholder 10 days' notice of cancellation for nonpayment, 30 days otherwise, and 10 days' notice of nonrenewal. [ISO CP 00 10 10 12, F.2]
- Optional coverages: Agreed Value (coinsurance suspended until expiration of the agreed value date); Inflation Guard (automatic annual % increase prorated daily); Replacement Cost (not for property of others, residence contents, works of art/antiques, or stock unless "including stock"); Extension of RC to Personal Property of Others. [ISO CP 00 10 10 12, G.1-4]
- Replacement Cost: may claim ACV first and then RC if notice is given within 180 days after the loss; RC not paid until actually repaired/replaced. [ISO CP 00 10 10 12, G.3.c-d]

### Condominium Association (CP 00 17 10 12) and Commercial Unit-Owners (CP 00 18 10 12)
- CP 00 17 covers the association's building (incl. fixtures outside units and, if the condo agreement requires, fixtures/improvements/appliances inside units); unit-owners' personal property is not "Building." [ISO CP 00 17 10 12, A.1.a]
- Association policy is intended to be primary and not contribute with a unit-owner's insurance; insurer waives subrogation against unit-owners. [ISO CP 00 17 10 12, E.6, E.9]
- CP 00 18 covers the commercial unit-owner's BPP, incl. fixtures, improvements and alterations owned by the unit-owner; it is intended to be excess over, and not contribute with, the association's insurance. [ISO CP 00 18 10 12, A.1.a; E.3]

### Builders Risk (CP 00 20 06 95)
- Covers building under construction incl. foundations, materials, supplies and fixtures intended to be permanently part of the building (on or within 100 feet), and temporary structures if not otherwise insured. [ISO CP 00 20 06 95, A.1]
- Need for Adequate Insurance: insurer pays no greater share than limit ÷ value on the date of completion. [ISO CP 00 20 06 95, Additional Condition 2]
- Coverage ceases at the first of: policy expires/canceled; property accepted by purchaser; your interest ceases; construction abandoned; unless otherwise specified, 90 days after construction is complete or 60 days after the building is occupied in whole or part or put to its intended use. [ISO CP 00 20 06 95, Additional Condition 4]

### Business Income (and Extra Expense) (CP 00 30 10 12)
- Business Income = net income (profit or loss before taxes) that would have been earned plus continuing normal operating expenses, including payroll. Pays actual loss sustained during the "period of restoration" due to necessary suspension caused by direct physical loss at described premises. [ISO CP 00 30 10 12, A.1]
- Extra Expense = necessary expenses during the period of restoration you would not have incurred, to avoid or minimize suspension (incl. relocation). [ISO CP 00 30 10 12, A.2]
- Period of restoration begins 72 hours after the direct physical loss for Business Income, immediately for Extra Expense; ends when property should be repaired with reasonable speed or business resumes at a new permanent location; policy expiration does not cut it short. [ISO CP 00 30 10 12, F.3]
- Civil Authority: premises within 1 mile of damaged property; BI begins 72 hours after the civil authority action and lasts up to 4 consecutive weeks; EE begins immediately. [ISO CP 00 30 10 12, A.5.a]
- Extended Business Income: continues after operations resume until restored to normal level or 60 consecutive days, whichever is earlier (Extended Period of Indemnity option replaces 60 with a larger number). [ISO CP 00 30 10 12, A.5.c; E.4]
- Interruption of Computer Operations: $2,500 per policy year. Newly Acquired Locations extension: $100,000 per location, 30 days (needs 50%+ coinsurance). [ISO CP 00 30 10 12, A.5.d; A.6]
- BI coinsurance is based on 12 months of net income + operating expenses following inception/anniversary; it does not apply to Extra Expense. [ISO CP 00 30 10 12, D.]
- Optional coverages (each suspends coinsurance): Maximum Period of Indemnity (pays loss during the first 120 days of the period of restoration, up to limit); Monthly Limit of Indemnity (limit × fraction shown, e.g., 1/4, per 30 consecutive days); Business Income Agreed Value (requires work sheet; coinsurance suspended up to 12 months). [ISO CP 00 30 10 12, E.1-3]

### Legal Liability (CP 00 40 10 12)
- Pays sums the insured becomes legally obligated to pay for direct physical loss (incl. loss of use) to tangible property of others in the insured's care, custody or control, caused by accident and arising out of a Covered Cause of Loss; includes defense and supplementary payments (loss of earnings up to $250/day). [ISO CP 00 40 10 12, A.; A.3]

### Extra Expense (CP 00 50 10 12)
- For businesses that must keep operating (no BI coverage); payment limited to the percentages shown in the Declarations × limit according to length of the period of restoration: ≤30 days first %, 31–60 days second %, >60 days third % (form example: 40%–80%–100%). [ISO CP 00 50 10 12, C.3]

### Causes of Loss Forms
- Basic (CP 10 10): fire, lightning, explosion, windstorm or hail, smoke, aircraft or vehicles, riot or civil commotion, vandalism, sprinkler leakage, sinkhole collapse, volcanic action. [ISO CP 10 10 10 12, A.1-11]
- Vandalism peril excludes theft (except building damage by burglars breaking in/out); vehicle peril excludes vehicles you own or operate in your business; sinkhole collapse excludes man-made cavities. [ISO CP 10 10 10 12, A.6, A.8, A.10]
- Broad (CP 10 20) adds falling objects, weight of snow/ice/sleet, water damage (accidental discharge from a system/appliance) and an Additional Coverage for collapse. [ISO CP 10 20 10 12, A.12-14; C.]
- Special (CP 10 30): "direct physical loss unless the loss is excluded or limited" (open perils). [ISO CP 10 30 09 17, A.]
- Special form theft limits: $2,500 furs; $2,500 jewelry/watches/precious metals (not items worth $100 or less); $2,500 patterns/dies/molds/forms; $250 stamps, tickets (incl. lottery tickets) and letters of credit. [ISO CP 10 30 09 17, C.3]
- Special form excludes continuous seepage/leakage of water over 14 days or more; limited fungus coverage is $15,000 per 12-month period; Property in Transit extension $5,000. [ISO CP 10 30 09 17, B.2.f; E.3; F.1]
- "Specified causes of loss" = fire; lightning; explosion; windstorm or hail; smoke; aircraft or vehicles; riot or civil commotion; vandalism; leakage from fire-extinguishing equipment; sinkhole collapse; volcanic action; falling objects; weight of snow, ice or sleet; water damage. [ISO CP 10 30 09 17, G.2]

### Selected Endorsements
- Ordinance or Law (CP 04 05): Coverage A = loss to the undamaged portion of the building (within building limit); Coverage B = demolition cost; Coverage C = increased cost of construction; coinsurance does not apply to Coverage B. [ISO CP 04 05 09 17, C.1-3]
- Spoilage (CP 04 40): covers "perishable stock" for selected causes: Breakdown or Contamination (change in temperature/humidity from mechanical breakdown of refrigeration at the premises; contamination by refrigerant) and Power Outage (from interruption of power on or off premises due to conditions beyond your control); optional selling-price valuation. [ISO CP 04 40 06 07, A., C.3, D.]
- Peak Season Limit (CP 12 30): increases the limit on covered personal property by a scheduled amount, at described locations, only during the scheduled period(s). [ISO CP 12 30 06 95]
- Value reporting: for fluctuating values; limit set for peak exposure, premium based on values actually reported periodically (provisional premium adjusted); late/inaccurate reports penalized. [IRMI glossary "reporting form coverage"]

## Commercial Crime (CR 00 21 11 15 loss sustained; CR 00 20 discovery)
- Definitions: "Robbery" = unlawful taking of property from the care and custody of a person by one who caused or threatened bodily harm or committed an obviously unlawful act witnessed by that person. "Safe burglary" = unlawful taking from a locked safe/vault by unlawful entry evidenced by marks of forcible entry on its exterior, or taking the safe/vault itself from inside the premises. "Theft" = unlawful taking of property to the deprivation of the insured. [ISO CR 00 21 11 15, F. defs. 20, 21, 23]
- "Burglary" (general term) = theft of property from within premises by a person who unlawfully enters or exits the premises. [IRMI glossary "burglary"]
- Insuring agreements (11 15 edition): 1 Employee Theft; 2 Forgery or Alteration; 3 Inside the Premises – Theft of Money and Securities; 4 Inside the Premises – Robbery or Safe Burglary of Other Property; 5 Outside the Premises; 6 Computer and Funds Transfer Fraud; 7 Money Orders and Counterfeit Money. [ISO CR 00 21 11 15, A.1-7]
- Employee Theft includes forgery by employees; "employee" includes a person for the first 30 days after termination (unless terminated for theft/dishonesty), temporary and leased workers. [ISO CR 00 21 11 15, A.1; F. "Employee"]
- Inside the Premises – Money and Securities covers theft, disappearance or destruction; Inside – Other Property covers only robbery of a custodian or safe burglary; Outside the Premises covers money/securities and other property in the care of a "messenger" or armored motor vehicle company. [ISO CR 00 21 11 15, A.3-5]
- Money Orders and Counterfeit Money: money orders not paid on presentation and counterfeit money accepted in good faith in exchange for merchandise, money or services. [ISO CR 00 21 11 15, A.7]
- Loss sustained form: occurrence during the policy period, discovered during the policy period or within the extended period to discover — no later than 1 year after cancellation (ends immediately if replacement insurance is obtained). [ISO CR 00 21 11 15, A.; E.1.g]
- Loss sustained form covers losses from prior insurance (by same insurer, or another insurer whose discovery period expired) if coverage was continuous; for another insurer's prior policy, payment is the lesser of the limits. [ISO CR 00 21 11 15, E.1.k-l]
- Discovery form: occurrence may take place at any time; loss must be discovered during the policy period or within 60 days after cancellation. [RNC PF&M 251.4-2 (06 22 edition analysis)]
- "Discover" = when you first become aware of facts that would cause a reasonable person to assume a covered loss has been or will be incurred. [ISO CR 00 21 11 15, F. def. 5]
- Duties: notify insurer as soon as possible (and police if a law was violated, except for employee theft/forgery); detailed sworn proof of loss within 120 days. Legal action not until 90 days after proof of loss and within 2 years of discovery. [ISO CR 00 21 11 15, E.1.e, E.1.i]
- Employee Theft terminates as to an employee as soon as you (or a non-colluding partner/officer etc.) learn of theft or dishonesty by that employee (before or after hiring), or on 30 days' notice from insurer. [ISO CR 00 21 11 15, E.2.a]
- Excludes theft or dishonest acts by you, your partners or members; employee theft excludes inventory shortages proven only by inventory/profit-and-loss computation. [ISO CR 00 21 11 15, D.1.a; D.2.a]

## Commercial Inland Marine
- Nationwide Marine Definition (NAIC; 1933, revised 1953, latest 1976): classes are Imports; Exports; Domestic Shipments; Bridges, Tunnels and Other Instrumentalities of Transportation and Communication (piers, wharves, pipelines, power/phone lines, radio/TV towers); Personal Property Floater Risks; Commercial Property Floater Risks. [IMUA "Nationwide Marine Definition"]
- Michigan defines marine insurance by statute in MCL 500.614 (1956 PA 218). It covers transit risks, personal property floater risks, jewelers block, and bridges/tunnels/instrumentalities of transportation and communication (excluding buildings and their contents). It is not an adoption of the 1976 NMD. [MCL 500.614; IMUA digest]
- CM 00 01 Inland Marine Conditions: no abandonment of property to insurer; appraisal; sworn proof of loss within 60 days after request; payment within 30 days after proof; pair/sets; reinstatement of limit after loss (limit not reduced except total loss of a scheduled item); legal action within 2 years after you first have knowledge of the loss; valuation = least of ACV, cost of reasonably restoring, or cost of replacing with substantially identical property. [ISO CM 00 01 09 04]
- Filed ISO classes: accounts receivable, camera & musical instrument dealers, commercial articles, equipment dealers, film, floor plan, jewelers block, mail, physicians & surgeons equipment, signs, theatrical property, valuable papers & records. [Verisk, "Commercial Inland Marine" program sheet (2022)]
- Nonfiled classes include bailees customers, builders risk, computer systems (EDP), contractors equipment, installation, motor truck cargo (carriers, owners, contingent), annual transit, trip transit, fine arts, salespersons samples, warehouse legal liability. [Verisk, "Commercial Inland Marine" program sheet (2022)]
- Accounts receivable coverage pays uncollectible amounts due from customers, interest on loans to offset them, extra collection expenses, and cost to reestablish records — after direct loss to the records. [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. I A.6.f (same coverage structure)]
- Bailee coverage: inland marine coverage on property entrusted to the insured for storage, repair or servicing (dry cleaners, jewelers, repairers, furriers). [IRMI glossary "bailee coverage"]
- Installation floater: inland marine coverage on property (usually equipment) being installed by a contractor — a specialized type of builders risk. [IRMI glossary "installation floater"]
- General descriptions (secondary sources, not form text): commercial articles = cameras and musical instruments used commercially; equipment dealers = dealers' stock of mobile equipment; signs = neon/electric/mechanical signs; jewelers block = a jeweler's stock and customers' property; valuable papers = cost to research, replace or restore records; contractors equipment = contractors' mobile equipment and tools; EDP/computer systems = hardware, software and media. [Verisk program sheet (2022) for class names; descriptions from secondary sources — see Flags]
- Motor truck cargo covers loss of property in transit, either while carried by a common carrier or on the insured's own vehicles, depending on the form. [IRMI glossary "motor truck cargo"]
- Common carrier liability: carrier is liable for cargo loss except when caused by act of God, public enemy, act of the shipper, public authority, or inherent vice/nature of the goods — and must prove freedom from negligence. [Missouri Pacific R.R. v. Elmore & Stahl, 377 U.S. 134 (1964)]

## Equipment Breakdown (EB 00 20 01 13)

- Coverages: property damage, expediting expenses, business income/extra expense, spoilage, utility interruption (with a waiting period), newly acquired premises, ordinance or law, errors and omissions, brands and labels, contingent BI, limited fungus, green upgrades; several sublimits default to $25,000 (ammonia contamination, hazardous substance, data and media, water damage, consequential loss). - Suspension: an insurer representative may immediately suspend coverage on equipment found in a dangerous condition by written notice. [FC&S "Equipment Breakdown Coverage Form" (2016)]

# bop: Businessowners Policy

Edition relied on: BP 00 03 07 13 (form text read; © ISO 2012). Endorsements: IL 04 15 04 98 (protective safeguard symbols), BP 04 57 01 06 (utility services time element text), FC&S summary of BP 04 30 07 13 / BP 04 56 / BP 04 57 07 13 / BP 04 04 01 10.

## Characteristics and Purpose / Eligibility
- BOP packages property and liability for small-to-medium businesses; liability uses a single "Liability and Medical Expenses" limit; products-completed operations aggregate and general aggregate are each twice that limit. [IRMI white paper "Businessowners Policy Basics"; ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. II D.4]
- ISO eligibility rules (current per IRMI): no more than $6 million annual gross sales and no location over 35,000 sq ft; eligible classes include apartments, offices, mercantile, processing/service, wholesale, motels, restaurants, contractors with restrictions. [IRMI white paper "Businessowners Policy Basics"]
- Ineligible regardless of size: manufacturers; auto repair/service stations, auto/motor home/mobile home/motorcycle dealers, parking lots/garages; bars and pubs; places of amusement; banks and financial institutions. [IRMI white paper "Businessowners Policy Basics"]

## Section I — Property
- Covered causes: direct physical loss unless excluded or limited (open perils). [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. I A.3]
- Covers Buildings and Business Personal Property (in building or within 100 feet). [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. I A.1]
- Theft limits: $2,500 for furs, jewelry/watches/precious metals (not items $100 or less), patterns/dies/molds. [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. I A.4.c]
- Valuation: replacement cost (no depreciation) if limit is 80% or more of full replacement cost; if less, the greater of ACV or (limit ÷ 80% of RC) × loss. No separate coinsurance clause. [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. I E.5.d(1)]
- May settle at ACV and claim RC by notifying within 180 days; RC is paid only after repair/replacement, but if the cost to repair or replace damaged BUILDING property is $2,500 or less, the loss is settled on the RC basis whether or not repaired. [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. I E.5.d(1)(c)-(d)]
- Building limit automatically increases 8% annually (prorated) unless a different % is shown. [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. I C.4]
- BPP seasonal increase: 25% (unless another % is shown) if the BPP limit is at least 100% of average monthly values for the prior 12 months (or time in business). [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. I C.5]
- Outdoor signs attached to buildings limited to $1,000 per sign per occurrence (unless Outdoor Signs optional coverage). [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. I C.2]
### Additional Coverages
- Debris removal: 25% of loss + deductible, plus up to an additional $25,000 per location; reported within 180 days. [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. I A.5.a]
- Preservation of property: 30 days. Fire department service charge: up to $2,500 per premises; no deductible. [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. I A.5.b-c; D.3]
- Business Income: actual loss sustained during the period of restoration occurring within 12 consecutive months after the loss; not subject to the Section I limits (no dollar limit); ordinary payroll covered only for 60 days unless more shown; period of restoration begins 72 hours after loss. [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. I A.5.f; H. "period of restoration"]
- Extended Business Income: up to 60 consecutive days after operations resume (unless more shown). [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. I A.5.f(2)]
- Extra Expense: within 12 consecutive months; not subject to limits. Civil Authority: within 1 mile; BI begins 72 hours after the action, up to 4 consecutive weeks. [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. I A.5.g, A.5.i]
- Pollutant clean-up $10,000 per location per 12 months; Money Orders and Counterfeit Money $1,000; Forgery or Alteration $2,500; Increased Cost of Construction $10,000 per building; Business Income from Dependent Properties $5,000; Fire Extinguisher Systems Recharge $5,000; Electronic Data $10,000 per policy year; Interruption of Computer Operations $10,000 per policy year; limited fungi coverage $15,000. [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. I A.5.h-q; A.5 fungi]
- No deductible: Fire Department Service Charge, Business Income, Extra Expense, Civil Authority, Fire Extinguisher Systems Recharge Expense. [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. I D.3]
### Coverage Extensions (in addition to limits)
- Newly acquired/constructed: $250,000 per building, $100,000 BPP per building, 30 days. Personal property off-premises $10,000. Outdoor property $2,500 ($1,000 per tree/shrub/plant; fire, lightning, explosion, riot, aircraft only). Personal effects $2,500 (not theft). Valuable papers $10,000 on premises / $5,000 off. Accounts receivable $10,000 on premises / $5,000 off. BPP in portable storage units $10,000 (90 days). [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. I A.6.a-g]
### Conditions
- Vacancy: >60 consecutive days — no coverage for vandalism, sprinkler leakage (unless protected from freezing), building glass, water damage, theft, attempted theft; other losses reduced 15%; owner's building vacant unless at least 31% rented/used. [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. I E.8]
- Legal action within 2 years; sworn proof of loss within 60 days of request. [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. I E.3-4]
### Optional Coverages
- Outdoor Signs; Money and Securities (theft, disappearance, destruction at premises, bank, living quarters, or in transit); Employee Dishonesty (loss sustained during policy period and discovered no later than 1 year after policy end; cancelled as to an employee upon discovery of a dishonest act); Equipment Breakdown. [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. I G.1-4]

## Section II — Liability
- Business Liability covers BI, PD and personal & advertising injury (occurrence-based, same structure as CGL); Medical Expenses for accidents, incurred and reported within one year. [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. II A.1-2]
- Excludes professional services. [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. II B.1.j]
- Who is an insured mirrors CGL (individual + spouse, partners, LLC members/managers, officers/directors, employees, volunteers, real estate manager, etc.). [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. II C.]
- Limits: Liability and Medical Expenses limit per occurrence (and per person/organization for P&AI); separate Medical Expenses per-person limit and Damage To Premises Rented To You limit (amounts shown in Declarations); products-completed ops aggregate = 2× Liability & Medical Expenses limit; general aggregate = 2× that limit. [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. II D.1-4]
- Supplementary payments: bail bonds up to $250; loss of earnings up to $250 a day. [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. II A.1.f]

## Section III — Common Policy Conditions
- Insurer may cancel on 5 days' notice if the building has been vacant/unoccupied 60+ consecutive days (not seasonal or under construction; 65%+ of rental units/floor area vacant = unoccupied), permanent repairs not started/contracted within 30 days of initial loss payment, an order to vacate/demolition order/unsafe declaration exists, fixed items are being removed, or utilities not furnished 30+ days / property taxes unpaid over 1 year. [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. III A.2.a]
- Otherwise 10 days for nonpayment, 30 days other reasons; books and records up to 3 years after; Section III also contains Inspections and Surveys, Liberalization, Other Insurance, Premiums, Premium Audit and Transfer of Your Rights and Duties conditions. [ISO BP 00 03 07 13, Sec. III A.2.b-c, D, E, G, H, I, J, L]

# other: Other Coverages & Options

## Umbrella / Excess Liability (Personal and Commercial)
- Umbrella functions: (1) excess limits when underlying limits are exhausted; (2) drops down when underlying aggregate limits are exhausted; (3) covers some claims not covered by underlying policies, subject to a self-insured retention. [IRMI glossary "umbrella liability policy"]
- Excess ("follow form") policies generally follow the underlying policy's terms and don't broaden coverage. [IRMI glossary "follow form"]
- Personal umbrella sits over homeowners/auto/watercraft liability; commercial umbrella over CGL, auto, employers liability — required underlying limits are set by the insurer (no standard). [IRMI article "Plugging Liability Insurance Gaps with the Personal Umbrella Policy"; see Flags]

## Specialty Liability
- E&O: protects against liability for an error or omission in performance of professional duties; designed for financial loss rather than BI/PD. [IRMI glossary "errors and omissions insurance"]
- D&O: Side A covers directors/officers directly when the organization can't or won't indemnify; Side B (corporate reimbursement) reimburses the organization for indemnifying D&Os; Side C (entity coverage) covers the organization itself, typically for securities claims. [IRMI glossary "Side A coverage", "corporate reimbursement coverage", "entity securities coverage"]
- Fiduciary liability: liability of trustees, employers, fiduciaries and administrators for errors in administering employee benefit plans under ERISA; usually excluded from CGL; separate from the ERISA fidelity bond. [IRMI glossary "fiduciary liability"; IRMI "Fiduciary Liability Insurance Basics"]
- ERISA fidelity bond: every fiduciary/person handling plan funds must be bonded for at least 10% of funds handled, minimum $1,000, maximum $500,000 ($1,000,000 for plans holding employer securities); protects the plan against fraud or dishonesty. [29 U.S.C. 1112(a)]
- EPLI: liability coverage for wrongful acts arising from the employment process (e.g., discrimination, harassment, wrongful termination). [IRMI glossary "employment practices liability insurance"]
- Liquor liability (CG 00 33): covers injury for which the insured is liable by reason of selling, serving or furnishing alcoholic beverages; CGL liquor exclusion applies only to insureds in the business of manufacturing, distributing, selling, serving or furnishing alcohol (host liquor stays covered). [ISO CG 00 33 04 13, Sec. I 1.a; CG 00 01 04 13, Excl. c]
- Michigan: retail liquor licensees must file proof of financial responsibility (dram shop) of not less than $50,000 — liquor liability insurance, bond, cash/securities, or group self-insurance pool; licensee may cancel only on 30 days' notice to the commission with replacement proof. [MCL 436.1803(1), (4)]

## Surety Bonds
- Bond = written instrument by a principal (bidder/contractor) and a surety to assure fulfillment of the principal's obligations to a third party, the obligee; if obligations aren't met, the bond pays the obligee's loss to the extent stipulated. [FAR 48 CFR 28.001 "Bond"]
- Bid guarantee/bond: assures the bidder will not withdraw its bid within the acceptance period and will sign the contract and furnish required bonds. Performance bond: secures performance of the contract. Payment bond: assures payment to persons supplying labor or material. [FAR 48 CFR 28.001]
- Surety vs insurance: three-party contract; surety is secondarily liable and underwrites expecting no losses; the principal must reimburse (indemnify) the surety. [surety.org "What is a Surety Bond?"; SFAA/AGC Surety Claims Guide; IRMI]
- Commercial surety examples: license and permit bonds, court (judicial) bonds, fiduciary bonds — protect the public/obligees, often required for licensing or by courts. [surety.org "What is a Surety Bond?"]

## Surplus Lines (Michigan Surplus Lines Insurance Act, MCL 500.1901–.1955)
- "Eligible unauthorized insurer" = insurer not authorized in Michigan but eligible to write under chapter 19; "surplus lines insurance" = insurance procured from or continued/renewed with an unauthorized insurer (solicited, issued, negotiated, or premiums remitted in Michigan). [MCL 500.1903(1)(a),(d)]
- No one may solicit, bind or act as agent/broker for surplus lines without a license under chapter 19 and 500.1206a; license requires application, exam (waivable if licensed within prior 12 months), and compliance with 500.1204–.1206. [MCL 500.1905(1),(3)]
- Licensee must file sworn statements by February 15 and August 15 (for 6-month periods ending Dec 31 and June 30) and pay 2% premium tax plus 0.5% regulatory fee. [MCL 500.1905(3)(d)]
- Before placement, licensee must tell the insured coverage is with an insurer not licensed in Michigan and payment of loss may not be guaranteed if it becomes insolvent. [MCL 500.1905(5)]
- Insurance may not be placed with an unauthorized insurer if available from an authorized insurer; rebuttable presumption of availability for no-fault auto, private passenger auto physical damage, owner-occupied homeowners within MBPIA limits, workers comp (not partially self-insured), and any coverage readily available from 3 or more authorized insurers. [MCL 500.1910(1)-(2)]
- Presumed unavailable: risks where only part is acceptable to admitted insurers, and coverage the licensee can't procure after diligent search among authorized insurers; commissioner keeps a list of lines generally unavailable (export list), published at least quarterly, acting on add/remove requests within 30 days. [MCL 500.1910(3)-(4)]
- Written evidence (policy, binder, certificate) must be delivered within 30 days after binding when acting on prior written authority or a communication of coverage. [MCL 500.1912]
- Fees: surplus lines fee only if reasonable and not included in premium; for personal lines, fee not to exceed the greater of $100 or 10% of premium; fees disclosed in writing before sale and itemized; fees not taxed as premium. [MCL 500.1915]
- Premium paid to licensee (or authorized resident agent) is deemed paid to the insurer. [MCL 500.1916-.1917]
- Eligibility of insurer: stable, unimpaired financial condition; Add: 'Since the NRRA (15 U.S.C. 8204), states may not apply other eligibility criteria to U.S. nonadmitted insurers than the NAIC model's (capital and surplus of the greater of the home-state minimum or $15,000,000); alien insurers on the NAIC Quarterly Listing are eligible.'; must appoint the commissioner as agent for service of process. [MCL 500.1920(4); 500.1921(1)-(3)]
- Required notice on the face of each policy/cover note, in red ink, at least 10-point type: "This insurance has been placed with an insurer that is not licensed by the state of Michigan. In case of insolvency, payment of claims may not be guaranteed." [MCL 500.1922]
- Rates of unauthorized insurers are not regulated except they may not be unfairly discriminatory; forms may not misrepresent the policy. [MCL 500.1904]
- Placement with a non-eligible unauthorized insurer is allowed only after attempts with authorized then eligible insurers (unless the insured insists), with a special notice to the insured. [MCL 500.1950]
- Insureds who self-procure from an unauthorized insurer must report to the commissioner within 30 days and pay 2% tax + 0.5% regulatory fee. [MCL 500.1951]
- Knowing and willful violation: misdemeanor, up to 1 year and/or $1,000 fine. [MCL 500.1952]
- A person licensed for surplus lines in his or her home state receives a Michigan nonresident surplus lines license. [MCL 500.1206a(4)]

## National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP)
- SFIP is available from NFIP Direct (FEMA's servicing arm) or Write Your Own (WYO) companies — private P&C insurers that write and service flood policies in their own names while the federal government pays all losses; coverage is the same either way. [FEMA brochure "Federal Disaster Assistance: Meeting the Flood Insurance Requirement" (07-2024); agents.floodsmart.gov "Flood Insurance 101"]
- "Flood" = general and temporary condition of partial or complete inundation of two or more acres of normally dry land area or of two or more properties (one of which is the insured's), from overflow, runoff, mudflow, etc. [44 CFR Pt. 61 App. A(1), II.B.1]
- SFIP coverages: A Building Property; B Personal Property; C Other Coverages (e.g., debris removal; property removed to safety up to $1,000 of expenses and 45 days at the safe location); D Increased Cost of Compliance. [44 CFR Pt. 61 App. A(1), III]
- Building: single-family and 2-4 family $250,000; other residential (5+ units) $500,000; residential condo building $250,000 x units; non-residential $500,000. Contents: residential $100,000; non-residential $500,000.; building and contents bought separately, each with its own deductible. [FEMA brochures (07-2024; Commercial Summary of Coverage 01-2024)]
- ICC: up to $30,000 to elevate, demolish, relocate or floodproof after substantial/repetitive damage — paid in addition to the building claim but building + ICC cannot exceed the building maximum. [44 CFR Pt. 61 App. A(1) III.D; FEMA Commercial Summary of Coverage (01-2024)]
- Residential minimum deductibles (Risk Rating 2.0): $1,000 if building coverage $100,000 or less; $1,250 if over $100,000; pre-FIRM statutorily discounted buildings $1,500 / $2,000; contents $1,000. [FEMA "Simple Guide for Residential Manufactured/Mobile Homes" (RR2.0), citing FIM Sec. 3]
- Waiting period: typically 30 days; exceptions — purchase in connection with making, increasing, extending or renewing a loan; additional coverage on a renewal bill; building newly mapped into an SFHA (purchase within 13 months after map revision); burned federal land flooding (1-day if bought within 60 days of fire containment). [agents.floodsmart.gov "Flood Insurance 101"]
- Basements: building and contents coverage below the lowest elevated floor / in basements is limited to listed items. [44 CFR Pt. 61 App. A(1), III.A.8, III.B.5]

## Other Policies
- Boatowners policy: most common watercraft policy for individuals; combines property, liability, medical payments and uninsured boaters coverage; fills gaps in homeowners watercraft coverage. [IRMI glossary "boatowners policy"]
- Miscellaneous (recreational) type vehicles: motorcycles, motor homes, golf carts, snowmobiles, ATVs are added to a personal auto policy by the Miscellaneous Type Vehicle endorsement (PP 03 23) — see auto area for details. [ISO PP 03 23 (auto area research); see Flags]

## Alternative Funding Mechanisms
- Self-insurance: formal decision to retain risk and fund losses from a set-aside fund instead of paying premium. [IRMI glossary "self-insurance"]
- Risk retention group (Liability Risk Retention Act of 1986): its members are its owners; members engaged in similar or related businesses; chartered/licensed as a liability insurer in one state; may write only liability insurance (no personal-risk liability, no employer's liability except FELA) for members; name must include "Risk Retention Group." [15 U.S.C. 3901(a)(2),(4)]
- RRGs are exempt from most non-domiciliary state laws (chartering state regulates); a state may not require or permit an RRG to participate in its insurance guaranty association; policies must carry a 10-point notice that "State insurance insolvency guaranty funds are not available for your risk retention group." [15 U.S.C. 3902(a)]
- Purchasing group: group of members with similar liability exposures that buys liability insurance on a group basis for its members only. [15 U.S.C. 3901(a)(5)]

## Cyber
- First-party cyber covers the insured's own costs: legal counsel on notification duties, customer notification and call center, forensic investigation, business interruption income loss, cyber extortion and fraud. [NAIC "Cyber Insurance" consumer guide]
- Third-party cyber covers liability to others: claims by affected consumers, regulatory inquiry response. There are no standardized cyber policy forms. [NAIC "Cyber Insurance" consumer guide; NAIC RITF memo (2022)]
- CGL CG 00 01 excludes electronic data damages (except BI) and P&AI from TCPA/CAN-SPAM/FCRA violations. [ISO CG 00 01 04 13, Excl. p, q]
