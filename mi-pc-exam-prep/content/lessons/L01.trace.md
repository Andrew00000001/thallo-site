## Notes for the verifier

- Only fields every learner sees count as teaching a fact: prompt and reveal beats, the right option's outcome and win text
  (the win panel also shows after "Skip"), quiz and swipe texts, explore labels and spots. Wrong-option outcomes and fail texts
  are extra; see `L01.coverage.md`.
- Story outcomes and why they are clean cases:
  - Scene 4: the insurer "signed her agency contract this morning, but hasn't appointed her yet", so binding for it is barred
    (RF:8). The wrong options are a signed contract (option 1) and the reg-002 distractor "a 15-day pending window" (option 3).
    The other reg-002 distractor, "on the authority of her license alone", is the trap named in scene 3's exam field and in
    the scene 4 reveal and win text.
  - Scene 7: the insurer is an auto and home insurer, the count (18) is stated, and Dana owns her renewal rights (stated in the
    same set-up), so the 1209(4) exception for employee or captive producers whose insurer owns the renewals (RF:35) does not
    apply; 1209(2),(3) govern and the Mail 15 copy rule in scene 5 applies to her. The lesson does not teach that exception (as
    in the old lesson; see old trace Notes). The wrong options are the reg-005 trap (location) and the reg-003 trap (no notice
    needed for low production).
  - Scene 8: every condition of RF:24 is stated aloud before the swipe: the termination (prompt beat 0, "Her contract has now
    ended", after scene 7's low-count termination), the silent contract ("it is silent about what happens after that") and
    her renewal rights (scene 7's set-up and again in scene 8's prompt). Every card says "with that company" (or binds
    "with that company"), so each card is about the terminating insurer.
  - Scene 11: the deadline passed 20 days ago with hours unfinished, so she is inside the 90-day grace period. The win outcome
    says only that she turns Tony down and keeps servicing and collecting; the lesson makes no claim about what happens once
    she finishes her hours. Option 4 (she refuses all service and her commissions) is the reg-028 distractor D. The set-up hint "neither normal business
    nor a shutdown" (rows 49, 51) makes the choice answerable from what came before without naming the right option.

  fail text says 15 days is the filing deadline (RF:9) and that she can't bind until appointed (RF:8).
