## Added in the new lesson (all sourced; see L16.trace.md)

- Collision vs. other than collision as a term card, with a plain-English meaning of both and "OTC is commonly called
  comprehensive" (trace rows 4-5, 58; cp_personal-13).
- Swipe drills: rollover on an icy curve, hail, falling limb, backing into a parked car (trace rows 9-13, cp_personal-13, auto-055).
- The three Michigan collision types drawn as a chart, and a choice that turns on them, with 70% at fault and a red-light case
  (trace rows 14-21, cp_personal-14 drills).
- Transportation expenses need the matching physical damage coverage (here, other than collision for a theft) (trace row 23,
  mn_auto-22 unpack), and "$900 is a dollar cap, not a 30-day limit" (trace row 25, mn_auto-22 watch-out).
- Toledo, Ohio rear-end lawsuit as the Part A example (trace row 29, cp_personal-12 drill).
- The ride-share FAIL told as "logged in, no rider yet" (trace row 32, F:302, auto-053).
- A Michigan UM claim told as a clean case: an unknown driver, a serious injury (trace row 40, F:261, F:326, F:330).
- UIM spelled out as underinsured motorists (trace row 61, F:261).
- Endorsement defined as a form that changes or adds to the policy (trace row 60, core_facts:304), and residual liability glossed
  as liability for harm you cause others (trace row 53, F:166).
- The 14-day and 4-day windows taught before Sam's choice (trace row 59, F:280-281, auto-051).
- The medical-emergency exception to the off-road exclusion (trace row 51, F:309).
- The hook "A fortnight for most, four days for first-timers" (trace row 47, mn_auto-21).
