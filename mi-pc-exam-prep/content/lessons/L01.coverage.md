# L01 coverage map: old lesson (final/L01.json) -> new lesson (v2/L01.json)

Every fact the old lesson taught, and where it lives now. "Old row" = the Nth data row of the table in
`lessons/drafts/L01.trace.md` (counted from 1), which lists the old lesson's facts scene by scene. Scene numbers are 0-based
indexes into the new `scenes` list:
0 title, 1 explore (who's who), 2 term (counselor), 3 term (appointment), 4 choice (bind Sam's house today?), 5 hook (Hire 15,
Fire 30, Mail 15, Talk back 30), 6 quiz (May 1 / May 10), 7 choice (how to drop Dana), 8 swipe (after the contract ends),
9 sketch (tell the director), 10 hook (CE numbers), 11 choice (Tony wants a policy today), 12 hook (12-90-180), 13 recap.
Choice options are numbered 1, 2, 3 (, 4) as the buttons show them.

**Rule for this map: a fact counts as covered only where every learner meets it.** That means prompt and reveal beats,
other narration, term and hook fields, quiz texts, swipe cards and their whys, explore labels and spots, and, in a choice,
the right option's outcome and its win text (the win panel also shows after "Skip and show the answer"). In a choice the
player plays only the option the learner picks and locks the scene once the right one is picked, so wrong-option outcomes
and fail texts are listed below as "also", never as the only home of a fact.

## Added in the new lesson (all sourced; see L01.trace.md)

- An appointment defined as a term, with the trap that a license alone can't commit an insurer, the bank's reg-002
  distractor "bind for 15 days while pending", and a signed contract that is not yet an appointment (trace rows 16-23).
- What binding is: giving a binder, temporary proof of coverage until the policy is issued (core facts; trace row 19).
- A producer may not act as an insurer's agent until appointed (RF:7; trace row 16).
- An LLC is a business entity; the entity license applies when it acts as a producer (trace rows 4-5).
- The solicitor's 3 L's (hook mn_reg-04) and the director approving licenses, with "director" = "commissioner" (trace rows 7-8).
- "Before the first nail" for assumed names (hook mn_reg-06; trace row 39).
- After termination, the producer stays responsible for existing policies until they are canceled, replaced or expire
  (RF:24; swipe card 4; trace row 35).
- Owning the renewal rights keeps the auto and home limits in force (stated in the scene 7 set-up; trace row 30).
- The 12-month no-exam reinstatement does not cover a license canceled for missing CE (hook mn_reg-11 watch-out; trace row 56).
- Two new wrong options from bank traps: skipping the notice for a low-count termination (reg-003) and refusing commissions in
  the grace period (reg-028).

## Dropped

Nothing the old lesson taught was dropped, and no fact now lives only in a wrong option. Changes in form only:
- The old scenario's insurer "Lakeshore Mutual" and producer "Maria" are replaced by "the company, an auto and home insurer"
  and Dana (the brief rules out company names; the facts of the case are the same, including the 18 applications, the renewal
  rights and the silent contract).
- The 2-2-0 counselor hook is spoken in a term scene instead of being shown as a hook card (same words).
- The Zamboni hook is spoken slightly shortened (old row 40).
- The conduct reasons: all four are on screen in the scene 7 win text, which every learner sees; the narration now speaks the
  hook's own gloss ("what she did or how few applications she sent") instead of two of the reasons (old row 24).
- The scene 7 wrong option "cancel her customers' policies" was replaced by "skip the paperwork"; its fact (old row 29) moved
  to the scene 8 reveal, where every learner hears it.
