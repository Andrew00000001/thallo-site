Story set-up (not insurance claims; listed only where an outcome depends on it): Tony's Pizza is in Detroit; Dana is the agent
(producer) who writes Tony's coverage, and the insurance company insures it; Tony's first van is his only auto when he buys the
second one in March; for physical damage, Tony chose only Specified Causes of Loss; his drivers deliver in their own cars;
Gus runs a repair shop and did nothing to cause the hail; the flour arrives by semi. Michigan note: no story outcome says who pays
for injury to people or damage to other cars in a Michigan crash (Michigan no-fault changes those answers); outcomes speak only to
whether a vehicle is a covered auto, to physical damage on Tony's own van, to garagekeepers on a customer's car, and to what the
form excludes.
