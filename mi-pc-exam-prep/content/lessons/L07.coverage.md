How locations are written:
- **All indexes are 0-based**: scenes, beats, options and cards. "Scene 3 opt 2" is the third option of scene 3.
- "N0" is the first narration beat of a scene, and "R0" the first reveal beat.
- "opt k beat j" is beat j of option k's outcome. "opt k fail" and "opt k win" are that option's fail or win text.
- Other names are JSON fields (spot, card, plain, example, exam, label, note, item).
- Spot text is shown only to learners who tap the spot (since 2026-10-30 its clip also reads it aloud then). Where a row rests
  on a spot, it also names a narrated or always-shown location when there is one.
- Activity text (spots, swipe cards, choice options, quiz, why, FAIL and win texts) is read aloud by the clips from
  `lessons/clips.py`, so a fact that lives only in an activity field is still heard. Teaching-scene facts are spoken in the
  narration beats named below.

## Added in v2 (all from the allowed sources; traced in `L07.trace.md`)
- Only an endorsement issued by the insurer changes Tony's commercial policy, and the first named insured may change it only
  with the insurer's consent (1998 Common Policy Conditions, B): scene 3 (opt 0 fail, R0). Question core-bas-22.
- A landlord is not an insured just for being the landlord; it must be added as an additional insured: scene 3 opt 1.
- Waiting months to report a loss is not prompt notice, and failing a duty after loss can put the claim at risk: scene 4 opt 2.
- The 2018 PAP also covers a covered auto while it is transported between ports of the territory: scene 6 N3 and card 4.
- The commercial property loss must commence during the policy period: scene 6 N2 and card 3.
- The loss date, not the report date, decides whether the loss is inside the policy period (for the auto, homeowners and
  commercial property forms that N2 names, the editions on the cards): scene 6 N2 and card 2.
- A $60,000 loss pays only $59,750, and the policy pays its full limit once the loss reaches the limit plus the deductible
  ($60,250): scene 5 slider goal, done text and R0.
- An aggregate is a cap on the year's total: scene 9 N1. A premises accident (a slip in the shop) falls in the general
  aggregate; takeout food that makes someone sick at home is injury away from Tony's premises from his product, in the
  products-completed operations hazard and its separate aggregate: scene 9 N1 (the slip, spoken since the listen-only pass),
  N2 and drawing.
- The split-limit example is a textbook 2018 PAP example set outside Michigan, where the rules differ: scene 10 N0 (which
  since the listen-only pass also says "This example uses the 2018 personal auto policy.") and label.
- Liability limits pay what the insured is legally liable for: scene 10 N2 and example field; scene 11 q ("liable").
- Memory hooks mn-mn_core-18 (splitting the check at a Coney Island: one friend pays first, pay by your appetite, chip in until
  a wallet runs dry) and mn-mn_core-20 (Person, People, Property; one big bucket) are used, adapted, in scenes 7 and 10.
