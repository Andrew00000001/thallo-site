- Restaurants are an eligible class (Tony's Pizza has a BOP; spoken in scene 2 beat 1): CF:478.
- The lesson is a what-if (scene 0 beat 0, summary): other lessons put Tony's shop on a commercial package, and a
  restaurant could instead be on a BOP: CF:478; CF:6-7 (trace row 6).
- Car dealerships remain ineligible (scene 2 beat 1, card 4): comm-051 explain.
- Medical Expenses pays regardless of fault: comm-bop-12 explain.

- The BOP's Utility Services exclusion is why built-in Business Income does not pay for an off-premises outage (scene 11
  beat 0 and option 1): CF:535; cp_commercial-08.

  BP 04 57 C.3 and Schedule; cp_commercial-08 drill 2.

  CF:514; comm-054 explain; card 30.
- Section III holds the cancellation rules (scene 12 beat 0): CF:513-515.
- Memory hooks used in adapted form: mn_comm-17 (generous cousin), mn_comm-18 (12 months, payroll and Extended BI 60 days, the
