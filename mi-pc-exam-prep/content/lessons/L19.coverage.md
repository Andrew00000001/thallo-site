## Added in the new lesson (all sourced; see L19.trace.md)

- Story thread: Tony's new Detroit warehouse through one year (November purchase, weeks of vacancy, the Day 50 kitchen, February snow and thieves, a spring kitchen fire, a city code order, July storms).
- Business Income has to be added to a commercial package with its own form, so Dana added it (8 beat 1; vs_commercial-05).
- Time element means lost income or extra costs, not damaged things (8 beat 2; cp_commercial-08 tell, core_facts consequential loss).
- Basic and Broad pay a burglar's damage to the building (vandalism), but not the theft itself (6 option 1; CF:143, cp_commercial-06 drill).
- A building under renovation isn't vacant (5 reveal; CF:108).
- A calm day rules out windstorm, so the falling limb is a falling-objects loss (3 why and reveal).
- All three causes of loss forms exclude ordinance or law, so the endorsement buys it back (10 beat 0; mn-mn_comm-10 watch_out).
- The Ordinance or Law letter hook "A is All that's still standing, B is Bulldozer, C is Code upgrade" (10 beat 2 and buttons; mn-mn_comm-10, shortened).
- Value is measured at the time of loss, not when the policy was bought (7 reveal 1; mn-mn_comm-07 watch_out).
- The condo association's insurer can't recover even from a unit-owner who carelessly starts a fire (12 spot; comm-cpa-05).
- Spoilage covers only the causes chosen; Breakdown or Contamination does not fit a storm outage, Power Outage does (11; CF:152, comm-cpa-06).

## Dropped

Nothing the old lesson taught was dropped. Changes of form, not of fact: the old title sentence about the lesson's numbers is
replaced by the story opening (each number is still taught in its own scene); the old worked example's invented amounts were
replaced by new invented amounts that run the same formula and ratio (row for old scene 6 above); the old Ordinance or Law quiz
became a three-card swipe; and Value Reporting moved from an acted-out option to the reveal of scene 11.
