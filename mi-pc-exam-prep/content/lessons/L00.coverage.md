**Counting rule.** A fact counts as covered only where every learner meets it: narration beats, a term card's fields, or an
always-seen activity field (a choice's reveal and its right option's win text and outcome, quiz and swipe whys and reveals,
explore spots and reveals). A wrong option's beats or fail text are seen only by learners who pick that option, so they never
count on their own; below they are listed as extra places, marked "(wrong option, extra)". The right option's outcome beats
are skipped when the learner presses Skip; its win text and the reveal still show, so every fact in a right option's beats
is also in its win text or the reveal. On Skip the narrator also reads that win text aloud (for a quiz, its why clip),
before the reveal plays, so a learner who skips hears it as well as seeing it.

## Added in the new lesson (all sourced; see L00.trace.md)

- Cut score defined as a term, with the 111-right and 39-to-spare bar and the ride height-line analogy (trace rows 4-5, 59).

- In Michigan, no exam until the registered course is complete (trace row 14, MCL 500.1204(1),(2)).
- A real estate license earns no waiver; a producer license held within the past 12 months is on the waiver list (trace row 17, reg-013).
- Exam trap: 20 hours is a single-line course; 24 hours is CE for licensed agents (trace row 19, reg-012).
- "Producer" means agent; DIFS is Michigan's insurance department; "the director" is the insurance director (trace rows 60-62).
- PSI test site in Grand Rapids (trace row 35).
- Voicemail and email don't count for rescheduling; a no-show forfeits the fee (trace row 26).
- Lacking proper ID forfeits the fee (trace row 42).
- In Michigan, using notes during the licensing exam is a ground on which the director may refuse a license (trace row 39, MCL 500.1239(2), hook mn-mn_reg-12 watch-out).
- Ten minutes on one item is time for 12 items at the planned pace (trace row 53, arithmetic).
- The tutorial's sample questions let you practice (trace row 47).
