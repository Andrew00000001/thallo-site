## New facts added (all sourced in the trace)
- Twisting is a kind of misrepresentation; cash to switch, with nothing misleading said, is rebating, not twisting (scene 2 reveal; scene 4 options[0] fail). CP15, RF L279, L350, mn_reg-15 watch_out. Trace rows 2.6, 2.7, 4.3.
- A gift above the $50 merchandise limit is a rebate (scene 4 options[1]). RF L350, L354, reg-039. Trace row 4.4.
- Premiums in personal checking, even with a ledger, are commingled (scene 6 `exam`). reg-049. Trace row 6.6.
- A spoken OK is not the written authorization needed to hold a return premium (scene 7 options[1]). RF L388, reg-051. Trace row 7.3.
- Lender coercion is "banned" rather than called an unfair trade practice, because MCL 500.2077 sits outside sections 2001-2050 (scene 5 reveal). RF L264, L321. Trace row 5.8.
- Concrete PAPER example: Sam's homeowners policy is covered, Tony's business policy is not (scene 13 beat 1). RF L426. Trace row 13.2.
- From the first version, kept: spoken defamation counts too (mn-16 `watch_out`); a lender may reasonably approve the insurer the borrower picks (RF L321); a practitioner's public-media ads don't make it a runner (RF L409); the full lists of statements and authorized agencies (RF L410-411).
- Removed: the flood option in scene 4 (and with it the HO 00 03 05 11 citation). The flood promise survives as scene 2 cards[2], whose premise ("It excludes flood") comes from the CP15 drill.
