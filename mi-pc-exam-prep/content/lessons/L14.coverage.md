## Added in the new lesson (all sourced; see L14.trace.md)

- PIP pays the injured person's own bills even when they caused the crash; as a rule, your own policy pays first (choice 2;
  trace rows 6-9, cp_personal-12 drill, F:139).
- Medical has no 3-year cutoff; the other three benefits stop after 3 years (scene 3 reveal; trace row 18, mn_auto-07 watch_out).
- Work loss is cut 15% because the benefit isn't taxed (scene 4; trace row 14, mn_auto-08 unpack).
- What QHC is, and that Medicare A and B counts as QHC; the opt-out needs proof of the household's coverage (scenes 5-6;
  trace rows 26, 31, 32). An excluded named insured needs QHC other than Medicare (scene 5 beat 2; trace row 65, F:96;
  added in the cross-lesson audit).
- PPI does not pay for your own car; Michigan limited collision would not pay a driver over 50% at fault, broad form would
  (scenes 7, 8; trace rows 40-41, F:163, F:254-257, auto-048).
- A neighbor's mailbox and a Detroit storefront are PPI losses (scene 8 cards 1 and 6; trace rows 44-45, auto-032).
- The PPI rhyme hook mn_auto-12 (scene 7), the "DID it happen?" part of hook mn_auto-15 (scenes 9, 11) and the
  "three-grand small-claims fix" part of hook mn_auto-17 (scene 10).
- A plain meaning of tort liability, legal responsibility for harm you cause (scene 9 beat 0; trace row 48), and residual
  liability shown doing its job in a story (scene 9 beat 2; trace row 52).
- All five kinds of tort claims that survive: intentional harm and a nonresident's economic loss (threshold injury) are named
  in scene 9 beat 0 and its gray beat-0 labels; the other three are columns 1-3 (trace row 49, F:170-175; added in the
  cross-lesson audit).
- The mini-tort is brought in small claims court when possible, is claimed from the at-fault driver, and needs an insured
  damaged vehicle (scenes 8, 10; trace rows 47, 59).
- A three-way sort of PIP, PPI and residual liability from the six cp_personal-12 drills (scene 12; trace rows 62-63).
