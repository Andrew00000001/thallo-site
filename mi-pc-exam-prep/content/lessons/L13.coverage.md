## Added in the new lesson (all sourced; see L13.trace.md)

- Coverage F pays a guest hurt on the insured location with permission (trace row 5; prop-052).
- Section II is liability (trace row 3; mn_prop-14 watch_out).
- Claim expenses' lost pay is for time spent helping with the defense (trace row 14; mn_prop-19 hook and unpack).
- The Damage to Property of Others age trap: 13 or older AND intentional, so a 9-year-old's intentional damage and a
  15-year-old's accident can still be paid (trace rows 22-23; mn_prop-19 watch_out).
- Damage to Property of Others excludes business-related damage (trace row 59; prop-055, mn_prop-19 unpack).
- Damage to Property of Others excludes damage from motor vehicles, and the lesson's list is called "Some catches", so it
  doesn't read as complete (trace rows 60, 75; prop-055, mn_prop-19 unpack).
- The watercraft exclusion applies to Coverages E and F, and the Watercraft endorsement makes E and F apply to the described
  boat (trace rows 25, 28, 30; prop-gap-17).
- "Declared at inception" means declared when the policy began (trace row 61).
- Open perils means any cause of loss not excluded (trace row 63; core_facts, IRMI).
- Without the endorsement, HO-3 personal property is paid at ACV (trace row 36).
- Section II excludes business liability, with a few exceptions (trace row 41).
- The $2,000 business threshold is the 2011 forms' figure (trace row 67; prop-038).
- The Personal Injury endorsement excludes knowingly false statements (trace row 65; mn_prop-24).
- Innocent co-insured defined in plain English (trace row 47).
- Some states' versions require a domestic-abuse showing; Michigan's does not (trace row 53).
- Subrogation explained in plain English (trace row 55; core_facts).
- Michigan's own suit minimum is 1 year, so the 2 years comes from the 2011 policy (trace row 57; prop-009, prop-gap-15).
