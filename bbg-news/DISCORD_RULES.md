# #submit-clips rules (copy this into a pinned message)

📌 **HOW TO GET YOUR CLIP ON BBG NEWS**

1. Post your Battle Bears Gold clip here as a **video file** (MP4 or MOV, at least 3 seconds). Free Discord uploads max out at 20 MB, so trim it to the best 10–30 seconds.
2. Want an Instagram tag? Add `ig: @yourhandle` to your message.
3. The bot replies to your clip. React 👍 **on the bot's reply** to confirm it's your own gameplay and that BBG News may post it on Instagram with credit.
4. If your clip is picked, it goes up on the BBG News Instagram and the bot replies with the link.

Only post your own recordings. Instagram may block clips with copyrighted music. Want a posted clip taken down? Just ask here.
BBG News is an unofficial fan page, not affiliated with SkyVu.
